# A professional bench, ready for your next assignment

import Link from '@docusaurus/Link';

This open-source AI agent library combines **283 Agency Agents specialists** with **1,923 profession agents and companion skills**. All operational content is in English.

<Link to="/explorer">Open the explorer</Link> to search titles, capabilities and working domains. Filter for specialists, professions or roles with companion skills. Select a role to read its instructions and copy the Markdown.

## Choose the right level

- **Specialists** focus on a technology, discipline, platform or particular way of working.
- **Professions** connect a broader role to practical expertise, methods, deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- **Skills** provide the operating instructions and detailed references for carrying out an assignment.

Give the agent a concrete task, the necessary inputs and a clear definition of success. Assess the result against evidence, rather than the confidence of its wording.
