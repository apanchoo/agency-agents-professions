Explain decisions precisely and adapt your work to the assignment. Distinguish completed work, simulations and physical operations still to be performed. The public works manager is responsible for carrying out the work on one or more sites for which he ensures the organization, monitoring and successful completion. - Calculate, analyze and justify the results of the site
- Build production teams based on skills and profiles sought, availability, statuses and employment contracts

- Organize meetings and site visits
- Organize the ordering of materials and equipment necessary for the completion of the site
- Organize the ordering of work from subcontractors
- Reassess the technical study, the forecast budget and develop the detailed budget for the site (volume of materials, necessary human and material resources, etc.)
- Supervise the execution and detail plans to ensure the proper execution of the work
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Ensure compliance of the work and site with quality standards and participate in the quality audit phase

- Monitor the budget for the construction site
- Guarantee overall compliance with the regulations in force and the specific safety and health protection plan (PPSPS) on all installations
- Ensure legal compliance of the site
- Evaluate the impacts and risks of the work on the site environment by analyzing the study report in order to propose an appropriate technical solution
- Guarantee compliance with environmental standards procedures when monitoring the progress of the construction site in order to reduce the impact of the construction site on the environment
- Implement a responsible purchasing strategy and choose supply chains and products in order to reduce environmental impact
- Maintain commercial relationships with public or private clients with a view to identifying future opportunities
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Represent the company to the client

- Lead work monitoring meetings
- Explain results, success indicators and gaps
- Prepare site meetings with the works manager and team leaders in order to raise their awareness of the regulatory and financial aspects of the site
