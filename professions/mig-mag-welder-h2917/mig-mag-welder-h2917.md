The MIG MAG welder uses gas shielded arc welding processes with fusible wire to join metal parts. The MIG (Metal Inert Gas) process uses an inert gas like argon, while the MAG (Metal Active Gas) process uses an active gas like carbon dioxide or argon mixed with carbon dioxide; These techniques are commonly used to weld metals like steel, aluminum and alloys.
