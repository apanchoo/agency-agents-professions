## Detailed Expertise

Occupational health nurse

### Scope

The occupational health nurse plays an important role in the prevention of occupational risks and the promotion of health among workers. Ensures medical monitoring of employees in collaboration with the occupational physician Intervenes in the prevention of occupational risks and health education Manages emergencies and nursing care in the workplace Participates in the analysis of workstations to adapt working conditions Contributes to the drafting and implementation of occupational health protocols Organizes awareness and vaccination campaigns

### Related Job Titles

- Occupational medicine nurse
- Nurse / Company nurse

### Activities and Skills

- Identify the necessary adjustments to a workstation
- Teaching, developing skills
- Advise on the layout of workstations
- Conduct periodic medical examinations to monitor the health of employees
- Assess a patient's care needs
- Identify and assess risks linked to working conditions (noise, chemicals, stress, etc.)
- Intervene in the event of a health crisis within the company (epidemic, poisoning, etc.)
- Implement actions to prevent work accidents and occupational illnesses
- Organize the follow-up of medical files
- Carry out nursing care
- Carry out a diagnosis or paramedical assessment
- Carry out a screening (visual, auditory)
- Facilitate internal and external communication
- Analyze a social report and medical opinions
- Design and implement prevention actions, information on health and psychosocial risks
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Implement procedures relating to health and safety at work
- Train and raise employee awareness of good health and safety practices at work
- Manage medical equipment stocks
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage medical records and ensure their confidentiality
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Create a relationship of trust
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
