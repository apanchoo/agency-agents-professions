The geologist studies the composition, structure, history and formation processes of soils, rocks and other terrestrial materials. Analyzes rock formations to determine their composition, structure, and evolution Conducts field studies to collect geological data Interprets geophysical data to identify natural resources or anticipate environmental risks Collaborates with engineers to evaluate the stability of soils in construction or drilling projects Participates in the writing of scientific reports and the publication of research results
