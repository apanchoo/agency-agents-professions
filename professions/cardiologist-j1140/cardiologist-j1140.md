The cardiologist is the expert on the heart and blood vessels, he ensures the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases. Diagnoses and treats cardiovascular diseases Performs specific tests such as echocardiography or electrocardiogram Prescribes drug treatments or lifestyle modifications to prevent heart disease Intervenes urgently for cases of heart attacks or other cardiovascular complications Collaborates with other specialists for multidisciplinary patient care
