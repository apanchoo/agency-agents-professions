The airport operations supervisor, a key role in ensuring the fluidity and safety of airport operations. Coordinates and supervises passenger boarding and disembarkation operations. Ensures compliance with safety procedures and regulations in force. Manages incidents and emergency situations in collaboration with ground teams. Ensures liaison between the different airport services to optimize passenger and baggage flows. Monitors the quality of services provided to passengers and suggests improvements.
