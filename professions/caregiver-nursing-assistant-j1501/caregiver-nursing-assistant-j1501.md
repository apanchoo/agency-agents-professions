## Detailed Expertise

Caregiver / Nursing assistant

### Scope

The caregiver provides support to patients by offering local care and personalized support, under the supervision of the nurse. Ensures daily care, comfort and well-being of patients Provides acute care if necessary in accordance with the protocol or medical prescription Provides preventive care and health education Maintains a clean and secure environment for the patient Ensures the link between patient, family and multidisciplinary team

### Related Job Titles

- Nursing assistant / Geriatric nursing assistant

### Activities and Skills

- Accompany and support patients for a medical intervention or diagnostic examination
- Inform and advise the patient or supported person in managing their environment
- Maintain and promote the person's social connection
- Accompany the patient or resident during meals as needed
- Assist the nurse when providing care
- Distribute meals and snacks to the patient or resident according to needs or medical prescription
- Assess patient pain and adjust care
- Handle emergency situations and provide first aid if necessary
- Search, process and transmit information to ensure continuity of care and activities
- Enter the care provided in the medical file of the patient or the person accompanied
- Carry out or control hygiene and comfort care and provide assistance to the patient
- Monitor a person's health
- Ensure patient care instructions are clearly understood
- Identify, implement prevention actions and assess risks
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Sort and direct waste according to its future treatment or recovery
- Help promote preventive health practices
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Follow cleaning and disinfection protocols
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital tools
