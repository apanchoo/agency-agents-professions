The municipal police officer is a local agent responsible for ensuring respect for security, tranquility and the rules of daily life in his municipality; it intervenes 24/7 close to residents by patrolling by car, on foot or by bike, to ensure security, calm neighborhoods and ensure respect for the sharing of public space.
