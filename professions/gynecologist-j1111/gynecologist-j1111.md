### Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Ensure medical follow-up of patients after surgery to check convalescence and treat potential complications
- Diagnose and treat vaginal infections, sexually transmitted diseases (STDs), ovarian cysts, uterine fibroids, and gynecological cancers
- Diagnose the causes of infertility and offer treatment or refer to medically assisted procreation techniques (MAP)
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Carry out regular consultations for gynecological follow-up
- Inform and advise women experiencing voluntary termination of pregnancy (IVG)
- Inform and advise women undergoing medical termination of pregnancy (IMG)
- Inform patients about gynecological disease prevention, safe sexual practices, and healthy lifestyle habits
- Implement a therapeutic project
- Providing advice and treatment for managing menopausal symptoms, such as hot flashes and hormonal changes
- Carry out pregnancy monitoring
- Managing childbirth, including natural births and cesarean sections
- Suggest and prescribe appropriate contraceptive methods to patients
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Perform pelvic exams, cervical-vaginal smears, pelvic ultrasounds and colposcopies to diagnose gynecological disorders
- Perform obstetric ultrasounds
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Carry out a surgical procedure
- Monitor the health of mother and child
- Update the patient's medical record
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Ensure liaison between medical teams
- Respect the confidentiality of information
