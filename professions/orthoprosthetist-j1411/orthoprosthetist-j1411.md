- Teaching, developing skills

- Innovate in prosthetics techniques
- Analyze a medical prescription

- Design limb orthoses

- Cut, shape the materials and assemble the elements of the equipment (orthosis, prosthesis, etc.)
- Take an impression (prosthesis molding, orthosis)
- Analyze specific prosthetic needs
- Try the device on the person
- Proceed with the definitive installation of a prosthesis or the replacement of an orthosis

- Maintain prosthesis manufacturing equipment in good condition
- Control the quality of the materials used
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Carry out financial transactions in cash, bank cards
- Arguing about technical choices before medical authorities
- Collaborate with medical teams for personalized solutions
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Act quickly in the event of equipment faults
