## Detailed Expertise

Chief of municipal police

### Scope

The municipal police chief orchestrates public security and peace within the municipality; he directs and coordinates the municipal police service, organizes the means necessary for surveillance, prevention and repression of offenses; its role is also to develop a close relationship with the population. Directs and coordinates the activities of the municipal police Ensures the application of municipal decrees and laws in force Plans and supervises interventions in the field Manages the human and material resources of the service Develops delinquency prevention strategies Collaborates with other security and emergency services

### Related Job Titles

- Head of municipal police department

### Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Liaise with emergency services
- Implement and monitor the activity of the municipal police department
- Optimize resources for maximum security coverage
- Provide mediation
- Supervise the continuing training of municipal police officers
- Participate in official community ceremonies
- Organize surveillance patrols
- Carry out remote monitoring
- Monitor municipal property (buildings, schools, public transport, parks, cemeteries, etc.), events and public places
- Manage capture and impoundment operations for stray or dangerous animals
- Implement crime prevention strategies
- Identify and report anomalies concerning roads, public lighting, faulty signs
- Supervise activities related to the application of rules relating to port, transport, place of detention of weapons, shooting training and handling of weapons
- Supervise a vehicle removal operation
- Securing people, places and property with a video protection system
- Secure pedestrian crossings near schools
- Prepare periodic safety reports
- Establish an execution report
- Manage budgets allocated to municipal security
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Report an infraction
- Draft legal documents and procedures
- Coordinate security interventions
- Manage vehicle impoundment
- Prevent disturbances to public peace by islanding, patrolling, and resolving conflicts
- Carry out a public order mission
- Participate in defining the community's orientations in terms of prevention and public safety
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Manage found items
- Analyze feedback to improve practices
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Coordinate with the Urban Supervision Center (CSU)
- Encourage the use of digital technologies in surveillance
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Handle emergency or unforeseen situations calmly and efficiently
- Maintain regulatory monitoring in matters of public safety
