The Aircraft Area Coordinator orchestrates ground operations to guarantee flight safety and efficiency. Supervises and coordinates runway activities for the arrival and departure of aircraft Distributes the work to be done by taking part in certain specific tasks Ensures liaison between ground teams and cabin crew Ensures compliance with the loading plan established by the weight and balance technician (loading baggage in containers and in bulk, and cargo) Ensures compliance with security procedures and airport regulations Manages emergency situations and unforeseen events affecting flight operations Is in contact with a centralized coordination technician to inform them in real time of malfunctions that could impact the scheduled flight departure time

### Activities and Skills

- Coordinate activities in the aircraft area
- Facilitate communication between track personnel and pilots
- Optimize the use of resources on the track
- Organize crew rotations according to regulations
- Plan and adapt, if necessary, the team's activity, assign tasks
- Plan the allocation of equipment and teams
- Prepare the material (beacons, holds) and equipment (passenger ramp, etc.) depending on the type of device and loading
- Install and remove means of access to the aircraft (hand-operated or self-propelled)
- Install and remove baggage belts (conveyor belt) / on bulk aircraft
- Evaluate the compliance of security procedures
- Implement runway-aircraft gesture communication
- Installing and removing power cables
- Carry out aircraft water and toilet emptying operations
- Anticipate what to do in abnormal and emergency situations
- Promote safe work practices on the track
- Preventing workplace accidents
- Preventing aeronautical risks
- Check the security of aircraft taxiing or parking areas
- Analyze incident reports for continuous improvement
- Control the quality of service provided by the team
- Carry out the pushback, towing and conveying of the aircraft
- Carry out maneuvers for hooking/unhooking aircraft from the towing vehicle and transporting aircraft to the taxiway
- Monitor the supply of fuel and provisions
- Collaborate with the centralized coordination technician, in order to inform him in real time of malfunctions that could impact the scheduled flight departure time
- Optimize loading and unloading procedures
- Organize operations in the field of hotel equipment (catering)
- Ensure compliance with the loading plan established by the weight and balance technician: loading of baggage (in containers and in bulk) and freight
- Supervise the loading of a plane
- Transmit to the captain a final validated state of charge
- Send the modified loading plan to the weight and balance technician at the end of the operation
- Check compliance with the aircraft loading plan
- Write a report, an activity report
- Evaluate the performance of ground operations
- Organize technical flight preparation operations
- Ensure an interface role between the different departments of the company
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Monitor the implementation of current procedures, safety and security rules during the implementation of operations and activities
- Guarantee the proper use of equipment around the aircraft
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events
- Build your professional project in the field of avionics
