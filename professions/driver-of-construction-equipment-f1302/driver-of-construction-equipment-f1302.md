- Welcome new arrivals to the site and support them in their progress

- Adapt the positioning of the machine according to the instructions given in order to successfully carry out the work
- Analyze the instructions while appreciating the difficulties of the site
- Coordinate machine movements with ground teams
- Use materials for foundation layers and road surface layers
- Level land according to the staking points
- Organize the operations to be carried out in order to respect deadlines and site constraints
- Prepare the intervention and install the site
- Fold up and clean the site at the end of the work (machinery, leftover materials, waste, construction huts) while respecting the environmental procedures linked to the site
- Carry out the tasks assigned as part of the construction of a work (excavate, load, unload, backfill, etc.)
- Transport extraction materials and spoil
- Analyze automated systems to identify malfunctions
- Perform first level maintenance of machines
- Maintain your machine in a clean state to ensure the safety and lifespan of the machine, and enhance the image of the company
- Monitor the condition of equipment and report any malfunctions
- Check the compliance of machines with safety standards
- Mark the work area and ensure marking in compliance with the instructions and to avoid any damage to the works
- Drive construction machinery according to the prerogatives of safe driving authorizations and while being vigilant about the construction site environment
