Explain decisions precisely and adapt your work to the assignment. Distinguish completed work, simulations and physical operations still to be performed. The Points License Internship Facilitator raises awareness of road safety. Co-leads sessions with a psychologist facilitator Co-leads road safety awareness sessions for drivers who have lost points on their license Uses educational materials to facilitate participants' understanding and learning Assesses participants' knowledge at the start and end of the session to measure the impact of the training Encourages exchanges and discussions between participants to share experiences and reinforce key messages Regularly updates knowledge of road regulations to provide precise information Ensures administrative monitoring of courses and the issuance of certificates of participation
