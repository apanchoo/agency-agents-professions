## Detailed Expertise

Construction ironworker

### Scope

The Ironworker, working in tandem with the formworker, constructs the iron structure of a building or structure in order to guarantee its stability. Shapes and assembles metal reinforcements necessary for construction to reinforce the reinforced concrete Prepares the trellises and props to form the formwork Respects safety standards and regulations in force Works in collaboration with other teams on the site

### Related Job Titles

- Fabricator-assembler-scrapworker
- Reinforcer-fastener / Reinforcer-fastener in construction
- Concrete reinforcement worker
- Welded mesh ironworker/assembler

### Activities and Skills

- Coordinate work with other trades
- Read and interpret reinforcement plans and slips
- Identify the technical characteristics of a work
- Identify the different elements and reinforcements on a reinforcement plan in order to adapt the prefabricated reinforcements
- Assembling raw scrap bars
- Pouring concrete works
- Position work markers on a construction site
- Record site measurements and site characteristics
- Cut, cut materials
- Close a construction site and clear the work area (machinery, materials, waste, etc.)
- Casing concrete works
- Check the strength of the formwork and the correct positioning of the reinforcements
- Cut, adjust and weld the bars, mesh and metal parts used to form the formwork
- Install metal structural elements
- Insert the iron reinforcement inside the formwork to prepare for pouring the concrete
- Prepare the intervention and install the site
- Carry out fixing operations
- Select the metals needed for the construction site
- Clean construction site tools and equipment
- Put in place protections to secure the site
- Control the quality of the work carried out
- Load, unload, handle products
- Plan the quantity of concrete, wood, reinforcing iron and other materials in order to successfully complete the work
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Report on your activity
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
