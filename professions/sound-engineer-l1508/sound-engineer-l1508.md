The sound engineer is a specialist in acoustics and audio technologies, who designs, captures and shapes sound, serving creation and production, particularly within the framework of phonographic publishing projects. Designs and produces sound recordings for various audiovisual and phonographic productions Mixes and edits soundtracks to ensure the best audio quality Collaborates with directors, producers as well as artists and artistic directors to create the desired soundscape Manages and maintains audio equipment to ensure optimal operation May supervise a technical team during large events or productions Provides technical training for assistants and new team members
