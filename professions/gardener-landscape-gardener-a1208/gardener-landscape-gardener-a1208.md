The landscape gardener maintains gardens or green spaces, to beautify the environment and create a functional and welcoming atmosphere. Designs and lays out gardens and green spaces according to the client's needs and site constraints Maintains plantings, lawns and outdoor equipment Carries out pruning, mowing, weeding and treatment of plants Installs irrigation systems and ensures their operation to optimize water consumption Can participate in the creation of settings for special events The gardener can work in creation, or he designs private and business gardens, parks and spaces public green spaces, according to a designer's plans. He ensures soil preparation, turns the earth, provides fertilizer. He carries out seedlings, sets up lawns, plants, trees and shrubs. He can create terraces, paths, low walls, and carry out carpentry work to structure the space. He can also install the most suitable lighting. The gardener can work in maintenance, or he ensures the good growth of landscaping plants thanks to his regular interventions in gardens and parks, on green roofs and walls. He intervenes regularly to ensure that the plants are not victims of diseases or predators. He watches over plant growth and supervises automatic watering. He weeds, using methods that respect biodiversity. He mows, digs the flowerbeds, collects leaves, prunes shrubs and flowerbeds
