A heritage curator is a professional specialized in the protection, management and enhancement of cultural heritage, whether tangible (monuments, sites, objects, works of art, archives, collections) or intangible (traditions, know-how, languages, music, festivals, etc.). Protects and conserves cultural property (objects, monuments, archives) for future generations Studies and assesses works and sites to determine their history, authenticity and condition Manages collections by organizing the acquisition, classification and cataloging of pieces Implements preventive conservation actions and supervises necessary restorations Promotes heritage through exhibitions, visits and mediation actions with the public Coordinates heritage projects in compliance with regulations
