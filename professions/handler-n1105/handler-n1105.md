Loads and unloads goods in storage areas and means of transport Sorts and classifies products according to warehouse or customer specifications Moves or stores goods, possibly using suitable equipment (example: manual or electrically assisted pallet jack) Follows safety standards and operational procedures to prevent accidents and damage to goods May participate in stock inventory and report any discrepancies May carry out conditioning and packaging tasks for products before shipment
