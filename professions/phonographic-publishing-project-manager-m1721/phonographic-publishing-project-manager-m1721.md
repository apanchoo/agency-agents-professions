Pilots the marketing, communication and commercial strategy of a musical project from the post-production phase until its distribution on the market Is responsible for the promotion of the artistic project, the management of the artist's image, and the overall coherence of the promotion and distribution actions, while respecting the allocated budget Defines and implements the marketing strategy of a project in consultation with the artist, the artistic direction and the management of the label Pilots the commercial launch actions (release date, formats, partners, etc.) and coordinates all internal and external stakeholders (promotion, distribution, media relations, artistic direction) Supervises the production of promotional content (clips, visuals, teasers) and monitors performance indicators (listenings, sales), and adjusts the strategy if necessary Represents the project and advises the artist on the consistency of its image and the positioning of its releases Manages the marketing and promotional budget, ensuring the balance between artistic ambitions and economic constraints and can contribute to the valorization of the catalog
