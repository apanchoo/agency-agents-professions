## Detailed Expertise

Lifeguard

### Scope

The lifeguard ensures the safety and well-being of swimmers in various aquatic environments. Conducts swimming learning sessions for all ages Conducts aquatic sports activity sessions Monitors the pools and ensures the safety of swimmers according to current regulations Intervenes in the event of an emergency and performs the necessary first aid Controls water quality and maintains facilities in good condition Informs and raises public awareness of water safety rules

### Related Job Titles

- Sports instructor / Sports instructor in swimming
- Swimming instructor

### Activities and Skills

- Help participants to assess their sporting level and their progress
- Design the program of activities according to the specificities of the public
- Explain the conditions for practicing a sporting activity
- Organize learn-to-swim sessions
- Inform users about good swimming practices
- Maintain team cohesion and motivation
- Adapt a course or an educational sequence according to the level and progress of the learners
- Teaching, developing skills
- Identify areas of individual and collective progress for practitioners
- Transmit theoretical bases, techniques and gestures by combining several teaching methods
- Respond quickly in the event of a water emergency
- Coordinate aquatic activities during events
- Carry out continuous monitoring of game, animation and learning techniques
- Check the pH and chlorine of the water regularly
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Promote safety rules in the use of aquatic facilities
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Check availability of emergency equipment
- Ensure compliance with health and safety standards
- Adapt rescue actions to the Surveillance and Rescue Organization Plan during an incident
- Evaluate weather conditions for safety
- Evaluate personal protective equipment
- Participate in the development and modification of the Surveillance and Rescue Organization Plan
- Monitor aquatic pools or a swimming area
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Inform the public about the practice of the sporting discipline and the methods of organizing sessions
- Design an educational project
- Carry out an assessment of the educational or sports activities project and propose changes
- Communicate effectively with pool staff
- Working in a group, in a network
- Managing conflicts between pool users
- Transmit the group operating rules
- Regularly train first aid actions as a team in order to improve the rescue chain during an incident
