- Carry out a postural study to assemble a personalized bike adapted to the customer's morphology and practice

- Assemble the elements of a hydraulic or mechanical braking system

- Install accessories

- Report the customer's measurements on a dimension sheet to adapt the cycle to the customer
- Replace defective elements of the electrically assisted bicycle (battery, sensors, connectors, etc.)
- Carry out a diagnosis on the mechanical elements of the cycle
- Carry out a technical diagnosis on the electrical part of the electrically assisted bicycle
- Check and adjust the wheels (sail, jump, offset)
- Check the state of charge of the electric batteries
- Check the quality of cycles before delivery
- Contribute to inventory management of the cycle store
- Conclude and invoice the sale of the cycle or electrically assisted bicycle
- Plan an after-sales cycle intervention by measuring its level of technicality
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Greet the customer face to face to create a climate of trust favorable to sales
- Provide maintenance advice to the customer
- Complete the main sale by offering cycle or cyclist equipment and accessories
- Conduct an interview to identify the customer's needs and purchase cycle motivation
- Argument the technical characteristics of electrically assisted cycles and bicycles
- Organize and arrange the cycle sales area
- Create a personalized restitution of the cycle
- Write a product sheet to liven up and update the store's website
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
- Use digital communication tools
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of new cycle technologies
