Ensures the publication of instrumental scores and educational works (new releases and reprints) within the allotted deadlines and budgets Completes the preparation of manuscripts transmitted by the editorial director Chooses subcontractors (engraver, illustrator, graphic designer) and designs or has graphic charters produced then has them validated by the editorial director and the author Manages the corrections until the final proof based on his remarks, those of the authors and collection directors, ensures compliance with the company's musical copying charter and the playability of the scores Provides all the elements (files and technical information) to the manufacturing department, prepares the multimedia files for their online publication Contributes to the promotion of the works (writing the back cover, preparation of extracts, from the cover to web format)
