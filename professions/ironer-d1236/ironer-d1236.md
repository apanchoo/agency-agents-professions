- Ironing textiles (clothes, linen)

- Control the application of quality procedures
- Identify a stain or dirt

- Manage the stock of supplies necessary for ironing (water, starch)
- Carry out collection and delivery of items to customers
- Return the items to the customer after checking cleaning, ironing
