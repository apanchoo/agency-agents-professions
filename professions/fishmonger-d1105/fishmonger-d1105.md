The fishmonger is a seafood artisan who selects and prepares the best aquatic products for his customers. Selects and purchases fish, crustaceans and molluscs from local suppliers or fishermen Prepares seafood for sale, cleaning, filleting and presenting it in an attractive manner Advises customers on product freshness and best preparation methods Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards throughout the product handling process May make special preparations or ready meals to meet specific customer requests Manages inventory and supply to ensure the availability of products while minimizing losses
