Explain decisions precisely and adapt your work to the assignment. Distinguish completed work, simulations and physical operations still to be performed. The delicatessen-caterer, an artisan of taste who values culinary traditions. Prepares and sells charcuterie products and ready-made meals Ensures stock management and orders for raw materials Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards Advises customers on products and their preparation Can organize receptions and develop tailor-made menus Develops innovative recipes to diversify the offer
