# Professional pleasure sailor

## Scope

The professional pleasure sailor is very versatile because he can fulfill different functions on deck (sailor) or on the engine (mechanic). Sails on recreational boats for professional use and ensures the safety of passengers Maintains the boat and checks the proper functioning of equipment Can train new crew members and share navigation knowledge Implements excursions for passengers Can participate in sailing competitions

## Related Job Titles

- Professional pleasure boat crew
- Sailor on yacht

## Activities and Skills

- Train new crew members in navigation procedures
- Use navigation equipment to optimize routes (GPS, mobile applications)
- Regularly check the condition of safety equipment
- Carry out minor repairs when necessary
- Maintain nautical equipment
- Maintain a boat
- Check the operation of a safety device
- Ensure the monitoring of stocks of provisions and fuel
- Supervise the loading and unloading of boats
- Monitor the course of a boat
- Carry out docking or undocking maneuvers for the boat
- Sailing on a motor pleasure yacht
- Sailing on a sailing pleasure yacht
- Participate in maritime navigation operations and update nautical charts and documents
- Prepare and operate the ship's equipment (anchors, cables, ropes and rigging, etc.) during departure or docking maneuvers
- Carry out the delivery of a sailing pleasure vessel
- Participate in a maritime safeguarding action or mission
- Communicate with port authorities
- Manage passenger and crew safety
- Comply with maritime regulations
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
