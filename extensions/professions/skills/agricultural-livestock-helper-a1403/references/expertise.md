# Agricultural Livestock Helper

## Scope

Provides assistance to the breeder in daily activities with the animals (distribution of food, assistance with milking, egg collection, etc.) Participates in the care and ensures the well-being of the animals. Can lead a herd. Can carry out sorting and packaging operations for livestock products.

## Activities and Skills

- Provide preventive or curative care to animals
- Respect animal welfare
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Take the animal out to an outdoor area
- Identify a sick animal and inform the stakeholders concerned (owner, veterinarian, etc.)
- Driving a herd to pasture
- Identify an animal by marking
- Prepare milking (grouping and preparing animals)
- Grouping animals together
- Carry out the milking
- Carry out shearing operations and transfer the wool to the storage area for processing and packaging
- Harvest the product of breeding
- Adjusting the hydraulic systems of agricultural machines
- Distribute the animals in breeding premises or outdoors
- Select a breeding male
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Maintain the structural elements of a building (fences, storage areas, etc.)
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Packaging products
- Manage food stocks and distribute food
- Sort, store products, raw materials
- Identify the presence of pests and note the nature and extent of the damage
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
