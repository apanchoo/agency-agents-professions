# Director of medical biology analysis laboratory

## Scope

The Director of the medical biology analysis laboratory plays a crucial role in the diagnosis and monitoring of pathologies through precise and rigorous analyses. Supervises and ensures the quality of biological and medical analyzes Manages the laboratory team and coordinates daily activities Ensures compliance of laboratory practices with regulatory standards Develops and implements laboratory protocols to optimize procedures Collaborates with other healthcare professionals to improve diagnostic processes Participates in research and studies to advance in the field of medical biology

## Related Job Titles

- Deputy director of the medical biology analysis laboratory
- Assistant director of medical biology analysis laboratory

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Provide technical support to staff
- Build a continuing education plan for technical staff
- Teaching, developing skills
- Act quickly in case of abnormal results
- Analyze biological samples
- Identify the use of new digital tools and instill new working methods
- Carry out the biological analysis or check the conformity of application of the procedures for carrying out the analyzes by the technicians
- Take a biological sample
- Update the patient's medical record
- Write the report of the biological analysis
- Design analysis protocols
- Determine the methods of sampling and biological analysis according to medical prescription
- Check the conformity of laboratory equipment
- Supervise sample collection and preparation
- Manage stocks of reagents and consumables
- Carry out operations related to human resources management
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Carry out internal audits
- Collaborate with teams to optimize processes
- Define the policy and general orientations of an organization
- Communicate results to stakeholders
- Ensure the confidentiality of analysis results
- Respect laboratory safety and hygiene standards
