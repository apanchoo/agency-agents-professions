# Professional Risk Prevention Speaker - IPRP

## Scope

The Professional Risk Prevention Practitioner - IPRP ensures the safety and health at work of employees. Evaluates professional risks and proposes prevention measures Advises and trains employees and management on safety practices Conducts safety audits and writes detailed reports Participates in the development and implementation of prevention plans Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams of occupational health services to optimize working conditions Ensures regulatory and technical monitoring in matters of safety and health at work

## Related Job Titles

- Occupational health assistant

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Communicate effectively with teams and management
- Identify the necessary adjustments to a workstation
- Implement the transformation actions necessary for changes
- Helping companies draft the Single Professional Risk Assessment Document (DUERP)
- Business advisor
- Train employees in occupational safety and health standards
- Implement communication actions
- Write the content of a communication support
- Work in collaboration with staff representatives, CHSCT (Hygiene, Safety and Working Conditions Committees), and other bodies
- Analyze a situation and produce a diagnosis
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Evaluate the effectiveness of the prevention measures put in place
- Train and inform employees to make them aware of risk prevention
- Identify and assess professional risks in the company
- Promote workplace safety practices
- Carry out regular visits to premises and workshops to identify potential risks and assess working conditions
- Monitor compliance with safety standards
- Study working conditions to propose ergonomic improvements
- Suggest improvements to optimize processes
- Carry out an audit
- Write a report, an activity report
- Evaluate the nuisance factors in the school or professional environment and propose improvements
- Identify and identify health and environmental risks
- Implement safety performance indicators
- Use work organization methods
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate the results of the studies to the occupational physician
- Participate in multidisciplinary meetings
- Maintain regulatory and technical monitoring
- Plan interventions in the event of an accident or incident
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
