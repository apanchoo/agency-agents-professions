# Demographer

## Scope

The demographer studies human populations in all their aspects, size, structure, geographical distribution, evolution over time, and their movements such as births, deaths, migrations, etc. using statistical methods and data from various sources to analyze and interpret these factors. Analyzes statistical data to study the structure and dynamics of populations Develops predictive models on the evolution of populations Collaborates with public organizations for urban and social planning Conducts impact studies on public policies Participates in demographic research projects Presents and explains the results of studies to non-specialists
