# Armed forces helicopter pilot

## Scope

The armed forces helicopter pilot is responsible for operating helicopters for military or security missions. Is assigned to combat, search and rescue, troop or equipment transport, or reconnaissance missions. Works in difficult and sometimes dangerous conditions Is able to make rapid and precise decisions under pressure Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out his profession

## Related Job Titles

- High-level expert helicopter pilot
- Maneuver and assault helicopter specialist
- Destruction support helicopter specialist
- Reconnaissance and attack helicopter specialist
- Specialist helicopter pilot

## Activities and Skills

- Use your weaponry and perform combat shots
- Maintain staffing equipment
- Anticipate a danger or constraint
- Control the aircraft
- Assess threats and adapt responses
- Managing stress and reactions in combat situations
- Managing a crisis situation
- Implement strict security measures
- Perform combat air missions
- Carry out a mission in instrument flight mode
- Manage on-board equipment and systems constituting a cockpit
- Master aeronautical phraseology
- Implement the onboard armament of an aircraft
- Implement air navigation procedures
- Implement reactions to external threats on board an aircraft
- Pilot an aircraft and use onboard subsystems
- Prepare for an air mission
- Prepare a report on the evolution of a situation
- Know the issues and challenges of security and defense
- Communicate effectively with other members of the unit
- Communicate by radio means in combat situations
- Act in a context of high responsibility and high pressure
- Understand, acquire and maintain the operational fundamentals of the combatant
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Managing stressful work situations
- Managing an emergency situation
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Search for information
- Respect the engagement procedures
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
- Prepare yourself physically and get tougher

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
