# Team leader of rail network operating agents

## Scope

The Rail Network Operator Team Manager manages the safety and efficiency of rail operations. Supervises and coordinates the activities of driving agents to guarantee the safety and punctuality of trains Ensures continuing training of driving agents to maintain and improve their skills Controls compliance with procedures and regulations in force in the railway sector Optimizes work schedules according to operational needs and staff availability Manages emergency situations in collaboration with other security services Participates in the continuous improvement of processes and work practices within the team

## Related Job Titles

- Rail network traction manager
