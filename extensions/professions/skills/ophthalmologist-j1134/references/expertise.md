# Ophthalmologist

## Scope

The ophthalmologist, a doctor specializing in the care and treatment of the eyes and vision. Diagnoses and treats vision diseases and disorders Prescribes and fits glasses and contact lenses Performs eye exams to assess eye health Performs surgical procedures to correct visual defects Counsels patients on preventive eye care Collaborates with other healthcare professionals to provide comprehensive care

## Related Job Titles

- Ophthalmologist

## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Diagnose eye diseases and disorders
- Perform tests to assess vision, intraocular pressure and other eye parameters
- Perform visual exams to detect vision abnormalities
- Manage pre- and post-operative care to optimize patient recovery
- Interpret the results of eye exams and medical imaging
- Prescribe medical treatments and corrective devices (glasses, contact lenses)
- Carry out a surgical procedure
- Conduct study and research work
- Update the patient's medical record
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Communicate with patients and their families

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate leadership
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of optometry equipment
- Use of lasers in ophthalmology

## Areas of expertise

- Ophthalmology
- Eye pressure measurement techniques
- Eye microsurgery
- Medical imaging
- Post-operative follow-up
- Use of specialized instruments (ophthalmoscope, tonometer, slit lamp)
- Eye pathologies
