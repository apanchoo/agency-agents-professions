# Support Specialist

## Scope

The IT Support Specialist is responsible for guaranteeing the fluidity and efficiency of the company's IT systems. Oversees and coordinates technical support activities for internal users Manages incidents and support requests to ensure rapid and efficient resolution Provides training and support to users to optimize their use of IT resources Maintains track of trouble tickets and updates technical documentation and internal procedures Collaborates with IT teams to continually improve IT systems and services Analyzes incident trends to suggest improvements and prevent future problems
