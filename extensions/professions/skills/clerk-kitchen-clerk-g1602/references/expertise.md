# Clerk / Kitchen clerk

## Scope

The Kitchen Assistant, an essential player in the preparation of dishes and production in the kitchen. Assists cooks and chefs in the preparation of dishes Prepares the ingredients necessary for preparing recipes Maintains the cleanliness of work surfaces, kitchen utensils and premises Participates in the reception and storage of goods Applies hygiene and food safety standards Participates in sending dishes during service

## Related Job Titles

- Kitchen agent
- Kitchen help
- Rotating kitchen clerk
- Pastry clerk / Restaurant pastry clerk

## Activities and Skills

- Cook meat, fish or vegetables
- Cooking sauces to accompany the products
- Prepare dishes for service
- Cut and trim vegetables, fruits and condiments
- Peel vegetables and fruits
- Handle kitchen equipment
- Prepare kitchen utensils
- Prepare and make doughs, mixtures and creams for desserts
- Prepare meats and fish
- Prepare your intervention on construction sites or production sites
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Optimize the use of food resources
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Participate in an inventory
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Apply eco-gestures in your practice
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Present and decorate the preparations according to the instructions
- Maintain effective communication with management
- Integrate into the team and show cooperation
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Carry out activities according to the stages and time allocated

## Professional Approach

- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
