## Scope

Le Cidrier, artisan of tradition and innovation, designs and produces unique ciders using traditional or innovative methods. Controls the quality of the apples and other ingredients used Monitors the fermentation and maturation processes Ensures the bottling and packaging of the cider Develops new recipes to diversify the cider offering Can market and promote its products to consumers and professionals Can grow and produce its own fruit
