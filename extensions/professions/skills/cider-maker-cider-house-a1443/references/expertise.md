# Cider maker / Cider house

## Scope

Le Cidrier, artisan of tradition and innovation, designs and produces unique ciders using traditional or innovative methods. Controls the quality of the apples and other ingredients used Monitors the fermentation and maturation processes Ensures the bottling and packaging of the cider Develops new recipes to diversify the cider offering Can market and promote its products to consumers and professionals Can grow and produce its own fruit

## Related Job Titles

- Vintage boiler
- Cider factory worker

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Carry out chemical control tests during the fermentation process and issue recommendations (additions of ingredients)
- Adjust the dosage of alcohol ingredients
- Mix raw materials
- Carry out and follow the steps of producing an alcohol
- Develop new recipes and flavors by testing different combinations of ingredients
- Develop a blend or a vintage
- Managing the temperature of fermentation tanks
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions
- Drawing off a product after fermentation
- Monitor the condition of a plantation
- Using presses to extract juice from fruits
- Use and adjust agri-food machines or equipment
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Check product storage conditions
- Load, unload, handle products
- Carry out the placing in barrels or bottles, the capping, the labeling
- Organize order processing
- Plan production based on customer orders
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Order products and raw materials from different suppliers and farmers in your region
- Negotiate contracts with suppliers and distributors
- Managing production waste in an environmentally friendly way
- Implement sustainable production practices
- Marketing food products
- Develop innovative sales techniques
- Implement direct-to-consumer strategies
- Promote products to customers
- Promote hygiene and cleanliness in the workshop
- Use digital tools
