# Childcare worker

## Scope

A childcare worker specializes in the care and support provided to newborns, children and their parents. Monitors the health of the child and their general well-being Organizes the care of children according to medical requirements or the guidelines of the structure Evaluates the physical and psychological development of the child, as well as their state of health Supports parents and the family in the exercise of their parental functions by helping them to carry out care, prevention and education activities Implements health promotion activities in a public health and child protection approach Can coordinate a team (childcare assistants, nursing assistants, maternal assistants, etc.), and manage a reception structure

## Related Job Titles

- Childcare nurse
- Childcare nurse
- Child care worker in early childhood care structure
- Childcare worker responsible for nursery
- Child care worker coordinator / Child care worker early childhood coordinator
- Hospital childcare worker
- Health and inclusive reception representative (RSAI)

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Supporting parents in their educational and parental role
- Support educational teams through advice, awareness and training
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Support parents in the first maternal or paternal gestures
- Analyze medical test results
- Contribute to the development and monitoring of protocols (health protocols, adaptation measures, etc.)
- Promote the inclusive reception of children with disabilities, chronic illnesses or special needs
- Prepare and give a medical prescription to the child
- Collect information on the child's living environment and state of health
- Inform parents about how a childcare structure works
- Carry out nursing care
- Carry out care for a newborn and birth examinations
- Monitoring a child's health
- Take a biological sample
- Use specific software
- Prepare, install a medical-technical device
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Managing an activity
- Facilitate coordination with partners (PMI, health professionals, medico-social services, local stakeholders)
- Inform parents about the organization of the structure and present the program of activities to the children
- Participate in a multidisciplinary project
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
