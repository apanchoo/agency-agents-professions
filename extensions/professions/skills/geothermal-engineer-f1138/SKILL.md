## Scope

The Geothermal Engineer transforms the natural heat of the basement into renewable energy, offering an ecological and sustainable alternative to meet energy needs. Studies the thermal properties of soils to determine their geothermal potential Designs and supervises the installation of geothermal systems for heating and electricity production Conducts risk analyzes and environmental impact studies for geothermal projects Collaborates with engineers, scientists and technicians to optimize the exploitation of geothermal resources Ensures compliance of projects with current regulations and standards Conducts technological and scientific monitoring to stay at the forefront of innovations in the field of geothermal energy

## Skills to Apply

- Analyze a region's resources and evaluate potential geothermal sites
- Define and coordinate the implementation of research methods and processes, prospecting, soil and subsoil studies
- Determine the quantity of exploitable energy in the subsoil
- Carry out technical and financial feasibility studies
- Analyze a situation and produce a diagnosis
- Plan the development phases of geothermal projects
- Define risk prevention measures
- Design and manage a project
