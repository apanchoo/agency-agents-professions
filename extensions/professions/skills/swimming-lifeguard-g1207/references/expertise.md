# Swimming lifeguard

## Scope

The swimming supervisor ensures the safety of bathers in the water but also around the supervised area (beach, body of water, swimming pool). Prepares the swimming area before the arrival of users, supervises swimming and enforces safety and hygiene rules Administers first aid in the event of drowning or injury Conducts risk prevention activities for the public, advice and information for users Unlike the lifeguard, the swimming supervisor does not have the right to teach

## Related Job Titles

- Lifeguard / Aquatic lifeguard

## Activities and Skills

- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Apply first aid techniques in an emergency
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Observe facts, events, behaviors
- Monitor equipment or sports equipment
- Define risk prevention measures
- Evaluate security risks in different situations
- Implement emergency alert procedures and other predefined measures in the event of an incident
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Know how to install simple evacuation or emergency devices
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Check availability of emergency equipment
- Check the operation of safety equipment
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Participate in the development and modification of the Surveillance and Rescue Organization Plan
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Identify the customer's expectations and inform them about the possible activity(ies) according to their level and organizational arrangements
- Inform the public about the practice of the sporting discipline and the methods of organizing sessions
- Develop and manage interpersonal relationships
- Raise public awareness of regulations
- Trigger the emergency alert in the event of an accident, and apply backup actions
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Demonstrate agility in your action depending on the context
- Keep up to date with knowledge of safety, risk management and first aid

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show responsiveness
