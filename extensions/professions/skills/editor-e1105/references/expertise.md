# Editor

## Scope

The publisher participates in the implementation of the editorial program for its scope in relation with the author and the reader-correctors. Researches and suggests authors and illustrators Participates in the negotiation of contracts Supervises the production of works in compliance with budgets and planning, ensuring their quality and accessibility Completes the finalization of the texts, in close contact with the author Participates in decision-making concerning the physical or digital production of the works and the choice of illustrations Writes the back covers and sales pitches for the works Supervises the distribution and promotion of the works

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Support an author in the development and improvement of his text
- Collect and prepare texts, illustrations
- Design the model, layout, text integration and image processing
- Define the editorial policy (collections, publishing projects, editorial program) and its implementation
- Determine technical solutions and manufacturing methods
- Create book presentation materials (cover, brochure, poster, web pages)
- Write an article, editorial content
- Write a reading note
- Understand the uses, limits and legal issues of AI/AGI in the processing and generation of content
- Organize the different stages of producing a work
- Coordinate the creation and manufacturing of a work
- Analyze the literary quality and coherence of a manuscript
- Validate the texts, the Good to Print
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Manage copyright and intellectual property
- Write a contract
- Know and take environmental issues into account in your editorial practices
- Define and implement the policy and actions for promotion and dissemination of works
- Negotiate contracts and manage exploitation rights
- Search for authors and select works, texts, manuscripts
- Direct one or more collections (novels, scientific works, etc.)
- Develop innovative content strategies
- Evaluate the implementation of a project, an action plan
- Monitor publishing projects with contributors, authors, illustrators, printers, proofreaders
- Communicate in English orally and in writing
- Control the texts, proofs and structure of books, works and magazines
- Use digital tools
- Organize and develop your professional network
