# Heating engineer

## Scope

The heating engineer carries out the commissioning and troubleshooting of heating installations (building boilers, heat pumps, steam or hot water production in industrial environments, industrial ovens, cogeneration equipment, etc.), ventilation and air conditioning. Installs, repairs and maintains heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) systems Diagnoses faults, carries out necessary repairs on boilers, other heating, ventilation, air conditioning (HVAC) equipment Performs routine checks to ensure optimal operation of installations Advises customers on energy saving solutions, decarbonization and new HVAC technologies Ensures compliance of installations with safety and environmental standards
