# Chromo line manager in the textile industry

## Scope

The Chromo Line Manager in the textile industry supervises and coordinates dyeing and printing operations on fabrics. Supervises and coordinates production activity on a chromo line in the textile industry Controls the quality of textile products and ensures compliance with standards Optimizes machine settings to improve productivity and reduce costs Trains and supervises production line operators Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of line equipment Collaborates with other services (maintenance, quality, R&D) to continually improve processes

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate personnel interventions on machines
- Ensure the training of new operators
- Analyze a production document
- Clearly communicate production objectives
- Optimize an industrial process
- Mount/dismount the cylinders, frames, etc. on the printing machine, frame the pattern and feed the machine with colors
- Position and secure the rollers on the machine, center the fabric and adjust the tension when feeding
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Adjust production machine parameters
- Monitor machine measurement data during printing
- Select tools (squeegees, rollers, etc.) and printing frames and determine product needs (colors, etc.)
- Collaborate with teams to improve processes
- Control the quality and conformity of a deliverable
- Identify anomalies, manufacturing defects
- Respect quality and safety standards
- Identify equipment and material needs
- Implement performance improvement solutions
- Explain technical choices to customers
- Act quickly in the event of a malfunction
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
