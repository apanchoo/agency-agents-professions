# Production manager in the wood industry

## Scope

The Production Manager in the wood industry organizes manufacturing according to the company's industrial policy and in order to achieve defined objectives. Supervises and optimizes production processes to guarantee the quality of finished products Coordinates workshop teams Analyzes production performance and proposes technical or organizational adjustments Implements continuous improvement strategies to reduce costs and increase productivity Ensures the maintenance and proper functioning of production equipment
