# Production manager in the wood industry

## Scope

The Production Manager in the wood industry organizes manufacturing according to the company's industrial policy and in order to achieve defined objectives. Supervises and optimizes production processes to guarantee the quality of finished products Coordinates workshop teams Analyzes production performance and proposes technical or organizational adjustments Implements continuous improvement strategies to reduce costs and increase productivity Ensures the maintenance and proper functioning of production equipment

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Implement human resources management actions
- Check the feasibility of a production
- Develop and plan a production and operating program
- Plan production, estimate equipment, materials and labor requirements, in order to respect cost constraints and completion deadlines
- Identify manufacturing operations from the manufacturing file
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Monitor the supply of machines, facilities and the flow of materials or products
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
