# Photographic laboratory technician

## Scope

The photographic laboratory technician is a professional combining passion and art of photographic development. Processes and develops photographic films to produce high quality images Operates with specialized equipment for enlarging and printing photographs Ensures the maintenance and proper functioning of photographic developing devices and products Performs careful retouching to improve the aesthetics and artistic effect of images with software Manages stock and archiving with classification of negatives and developed photographs Can advise on best practices for shooting and developing in store
