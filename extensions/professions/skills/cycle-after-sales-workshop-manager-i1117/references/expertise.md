# Cycle after-sales workshop manager

## Scope

The cycle after-sales workshop manager is a key professional in the management of cycle maintenance and repair within a workshop. Supervises and coordinates workshop activities in cycle repair and maintenance Ensures the quality of interventions and compliance with delivery times Manages spare parts stocks and orders the necessary supplies Trains and supervises workshop technicians by planning tasks Develops strategies to improve workshop efficiency Maintains effective communication with customers to ensure optimal after-sales service

## Related Job Titles

- Bicycle after-sales workshop manager
- Bike workshop manager
- Cycle and motorcycle workshop manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Conduct annual individual and professional interviews
- Coordinate workshop activities
- Identify the human and material resources to carry out a technical intervention
- Distribute the workload and tasks to be performed among team members
- Supervise and supervise the team of technicians
- Ensure continued training of teams
- Identify gaps between actual and required skills
- Maintain technical documents and workshop procedures up to date
- Carry out a postural study to assemble a personalized bike adapted to the customer's morphology and practice
- Use inventory management software
- Plan interventions for the after-sales cycle workshop
- Assemble the peripheral elements (saddle, handlebars, handlebar tape, visual and audible safety elements)
- Assemble the elements of the cycle transmission (derailleur, crankset, chain, speed systems, etc.)
- Ensure the maintenance of workshop tools and equipment
- Organize maintenance operations
- Replace defective elements of the electrically assisted bicycle (battery, sensors, connectors, etc.)
- Carry out a diagnosis on the mechanical elements of the cycle
- Control the quality of interventions and parts used
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Check the quality of cycles before delivery
- Define supply needs
- Manage inventory of spare parts and workshop tools
- Apply labor law rules
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Welcome and advise customers on necessary interventions
- Greet customers in the workshop
- Provide maintenance advice to the customer
- Create a personalized restitution of the cycle to the customer
- Improve customer satisfaction and manage complaints
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Implement corrective actions in terms of cycle workshop repair methods
- Monitor the activity of employees in the cycle after-sales workshop
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
- Respect delivery times
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of new cycle technologies
