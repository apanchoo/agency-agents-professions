# Manager of vehicle rental agency

## Scope

The vehicle rental agency manager supervises rental vehicle management and marketing teams. Manages the fleet of vehicles available for rental by analyzing dashboards for monitoring Optimizes customer satisfaction by developing quality procedures, processes complaints from suppliers and customers Supervises the daily operations of the agency, including the management of reservations and vehicle returns Develops commercial strategies proposing a dynamic pricing policy to increase the profitability of the agency Organizes the activities of the rental sector (counter, telephone, fleet monitoring, etc.) Participates in the functional organization of the agency and rental locations
