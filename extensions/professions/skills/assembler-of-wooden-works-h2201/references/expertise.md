# Assembler of wooden works

## Scope

The Wooden Works Assembler assembles various wooden elements and associated materials for the creation or repair of structures and works. Uses manual and electric tools to cut, shape and adjust pieces of wood Respects plans and technical specifications to guarantee the quality and conformity of works Performs quality checks to ensure the solidity and durability of assemblies May specialize in the repair or restoration of wooden pieces and old or damaged structures
