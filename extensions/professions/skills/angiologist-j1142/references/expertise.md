# Angiologist

## Scope

An angiologist is a doctor specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of disorders of the blood and lymphatic vessels. Diagnoses and treats diseases of the blood and lymphatic vessels Performs Doppler examinations to assess blood circulation Prescribes drug treatments or appropriate preventive measures Regularly monitors patients with chronic vascular diseases Collaborates with other specialists for multidisciplinary care

## Related Job Titles

- Angeiologist

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze medical test results
- Diagnose diseases of blood and lymphatic vessels
- Assess patients’ cardiovascular risks
- Managing medical emergencies
- Interpret vascular imaging results
- Organize patient monitoring
- Prescribe and adjust anticoagulant treatments
- Preventing thromboembolic complications
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Perform arterial and venous Doppler ultrasound examinations
- Collaborate with clinical research teams
- Participate in clinical research projects
- Advise patients on the prevention of vascular diseases
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage medical records and ensure their confidentiality
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Regularly update your knowledge
