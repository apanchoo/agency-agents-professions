# Project Manager “Sustainable Cities and Territories”

## Scope

The “Sustainable Cities and Territories” project manager contributes to the emergence of a new way of designing, building and managing the city, based on the 4 pillars of the sustainable city, namely Sobriety, Resilience, Inclusion and Creativity. Coordinates, at regional and departmental level, national measures in favor of sustainable cities Co-constructs the approach with the services concerned
