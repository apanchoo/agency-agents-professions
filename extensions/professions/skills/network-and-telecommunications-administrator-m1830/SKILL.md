## Scope

The Network-Telecoms Administrator ensures the fluidity and security of digital communications, being able to be at the heart of connected territories to strengthen links between communities and propel innovation at the heart of our regions. Manages and supervises the daily operations necessary for the proper functioning of hardware or software elements (tools, networks, databases, messaging, etc.) Ensures the consistency, accessibility and security of information Installs and configures network and telecom equipment according to the needs of the company Monitors and maintains the performance and security of networks Resolves problems and provides technical support to users Collaborates with IT teams to optimize network infrastructures
