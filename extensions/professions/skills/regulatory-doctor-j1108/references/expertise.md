# Regulatory doctor

## Scope

The Regulatory Physician, an essential pillar in the management of medical emergencies. Responds to emergency calls and assesses the severity of situations to direct patients to appropriate structures Coordinates emergency resources and monitors interventions in real time Participates in the continuing training of medical and paramedical teams Ensures the regulation of hospital beds to optimize patient reception Contributes to the continuous improvement of emergency protocols May be involved in research and development missions in emergency medicine

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the intervention of multidisciplinary teams
- Coordinate emergency response teams
- Teaching, developing skills
- Train new operators on medical regulation procedures
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Managing stressful situations among patients
- Identify the patient's request and search for additional information in the medical file
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Receive calls in the medical emergency control center by assessing the degree of urgency
- Monitor the evolution of patients' health status
- Process medical information for rapid decision-making
- Create procedures for collecting medical information
- Update the patient's medical record
- Analyze emergency call trends to improve protocols
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Manage medical databases
- Coordinate an assistance and rescue operation for people
- Optimize control processes to reduce response times
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
