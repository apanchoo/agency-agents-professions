# Natural spaces maintenance worker

## Scope

The natural area maintenance worker plays an important role in the preservation and development of natural areas, preserving biodiversity and ecosystems. Maintains natural spaces Carries out development work on natural environments Participates in the development of areas accessible to the public Carries out regular interventions to prevent the degradation of natural sites

## Related Job Titles

- Agent for the development of hiking, equestrian and mountain bike trails
- Agent for development of hedges and ditches
- Nature maintenance agent
- Rural space maintenance agent
- Natural spaces maintenance agent
- River maintenance agent
- Coastal nature maintenance agent
- Nature and biodiversity maintenance agent
- Environmental Technical Agent (ATE)
- Ecological engineering worker

## Activities and Skills

- Pruning the branches of a tree
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Create and maintain leads
- Carry out plant maintenance
- Maintain an outdoor space
- Install fences, barriers or gates
- Install a natural space protection element
- Materialize the location of the site using benchmarks
- Install and maintain outdoor furniture or signage
- Carry out development and maintenance work on a natural area
- Carry out the installation and maintenance of nets and retaining walls to stabilize the dunes
- Carry out the ecological restoration of natural environments (moorland, marshes, ponds, etc.)
- Carry out trail opening and development operations for the public
- Manufacture and install outdoor facilities (benches, tables, walkways, observatories)
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain waste collection equipment
- Driving a vehicle requiring authorization
- Apply current environmental standards
- Analyze the health of trees and plants
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
