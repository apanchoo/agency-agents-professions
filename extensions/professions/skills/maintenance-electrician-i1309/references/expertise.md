# Maintenance electrician

## Scope

The Electrician diagnoses electrical breakdowns on various equipment and installations based on diagrams or layout plans, according to safety rules and regulations. Installs networks and equipment by checking the conformity of the assembly and identifying non-conformities Ensures preventive maintenance of electrical installations and takes corrective actions for each malfunction Writes intervention reports after inspection and commissioning of equipment
