# Forklift driver

## Scope

The forklift driver contributes to and ensures the management of goods flows and logistics through the movement of materials or products for a company; depending on the size of the company and the missions entrusted, he: Operates and manipulates handling carts to load, unload or move goods Ensures the storage and preparation of orders in compliance with safety procedures Controls the quality and quantity of products received and shipped Contributes to stock monitoring and participates in inventories Participates in the organization of storage space to facilitate access and circulation of products Maintains his equipment in good condition and reports any anomalies technical

## Activities and Skills

- Organize and optimize the physical storage of products and materials within its scope of activity
- Organize storage in a storage space
- Carry out first level maintenance on your handling trolley
- Ensuring cargo safety during transportation
- Communicate effectively with the team on security protocols
- Detect risks and alert in the event of an incident, accident and anomaly
- Be vigilant in relation to other people and your environment (traffic and activity)
- Assess the risks associated with storage activities
- Ensure that equipment is in good working order
- Report any anomaly observed on equipment or the working environment
- Work safely, for yourself and in relation to others
- Check the status of loads, identify anomalies and transmit them to the relevant department
- Apply the standards in force for the handling of dangerous or specific products
- Apply the standards in force for the unpackaging and removal of dangerous or perishable products
- Monitor and control the loads handled according to the specificities of the machine (distribution, weight, etc.)
- Ensure compliance of an operation
- Load or unload means of transport
- Drive a forklift while respecting safety standards
- Track inventory via computerized systems
- Store products
- Participate in an inventory
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Store equipment in dedicated spaces
- Repackage the goods
- Carry out storage and retrieval operations
- Receive and ship orders
- Check the conformity of deliveries
- Know the pictograms on products and materials and apply the associated handling rules
- Contribute to order preparation
- Optimize a goods loading plan
- Process orders and prepare shipments
- Coordinate with other departments to optimize logistics processes
- Communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders
- Know and interpret the safety pictograms on products and materials and deduce the associated usage precautions
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
