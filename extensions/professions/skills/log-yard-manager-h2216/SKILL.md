## Scope

The Log Yard Manager plays a crucial role in the management and optimization of wood stocks. Supervises the reception and storage of logs in the park Organizes the layout of the park to optimize space and access to different types of wood Controls the quality of stored wood and manages inventories Coordinates work teams Collaborates with suppliers and customers to adjust wood flows according to needs Ensures the traceability of wood and respects environmental standards
