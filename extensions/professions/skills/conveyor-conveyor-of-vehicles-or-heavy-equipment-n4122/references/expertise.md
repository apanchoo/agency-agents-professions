# Conveyor / Conveyor of vehicles or heavy equipment

## Scope

The vehicle or heavy equipment conveyor transports vehicles (buses, boats, etc.) or heavy machinery (construction equipment, agricultural machinery, industrial machines, etc.) from one location to another or to customers in complete safety. Checks the conformity of vehicles or machines to be delivered with orders Manages the documents necessary for transport and delivery Respects predefined routes and delivery times Maintains effective communication with transport coordinators and customers Ensures basic maintenance of the vehicle before and after transport May be required to participate in exceptional convoys

## Related Job Titles

- Conveyor driver / Vehicle conveyor driver
- Conveyor / Boat conveyor

## Activities and Skills

- Apply emergency procedures in the event of an accident
- Use navigation equipment to optimize routes (GPS, mobile applications)
- Carry out hitching operations
- Ensure first level maintenance of the vehicle
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Clean and maintain hitch equipment and vehicles
- Ensuring cargo safety during transportation
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Unload the machines from the machine carrier to transfer them to the site while respecting the safety instructions
- Organize alternative routes in the event of disruptions
- Driving a heavy goods vehicle
- Drive an exceptional convoy vehicle
- Moving and storing vehicles in a storage yard
- Determine a route based on delays and traffic characteristics
- Prepare the vehicle, mark the route and plan for hazards
- Manage the necessary administrative documents
- Respect planned schedules despite unforeseen circumstances
- Write a report, an activity report
- Update delivery or collection tracking data on digital or handwritten media
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Communicate effectively with the coordination center
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Respect the highway code
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Respect road safety rules
- Report any anomaly or damage
