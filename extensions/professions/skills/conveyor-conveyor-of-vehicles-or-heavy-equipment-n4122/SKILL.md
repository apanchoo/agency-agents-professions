## Scope

The vehicle or heavy equipment conveyor transports vehicles (buses, boats, etc.) or heavy machinery (construction equipment, agricultural machinery, industrial machines, etc.) from one location to another or to customers in complete safety. Checks the conformity of vehicles or machines to be delivered with orders Manages the documents necessary for transport and delivery Respects predefined routes and delivery times Maintains effective communication with transport coordinators and customers Ensures basic maintenance of the vehicle before and after transport May be required to participate in exceptional convoys
