# Industrial design project manager in mechanics

## Scope

The Mechanical Industrial Design Project Manager manages the development of mechanical products. Directs and coordinates mechanical design projects from study, choice of materials to completion Ensures liaison between the different technical departments during prototyping and testing and Interacts with customers to define their needs and project specifications Optimizes technical solutions taking into account cost, quality and deadline constraints Supervises design teams and subcontractors to guarantee compliance and compliance of products with standards and deadlines Carry out technical and financial monitoring of projects to ensure their proper execution
