# Advertising assistant

## Scope

The Advertising Assistant plays a key role in the coordination and implementation of advertising campaigns. Ensures the work of monitoring the creation of campaigns Maintains liaison between the agency and clients to understand their needs Coordinates the work stages with the teams and service providers concerned Monitors the production of advertising materials, in compliance with the defined schedule Collects and analyzes campaign performance data Can participate in the writing of creative content

## Related Job Titles

- Assistant advertising manager
- Agency Advertising Assistant

## Activities and Skills

- Lead briefing or debrief times
- Coordinate teams for the production of advertising content
- Implement project monitoring and evaluation tools
- Adapt campaigns to brand objectives
- Design and create graphic and visual elements
- Manage relations with media, partners and other departments
- Supervise the graphic design of advertising materials
- Act proactively in the face of emerging trends
- Analyze user needs for creating multimedia content
- Analyze statistical data for specific projects
- Analyze risky situations and propose solutions
- Provide and update activity monitoring tools (production dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Control the quality and conformity of a deliverable
- Manage a schedule
- Plan the distribution schedule for advertising campaigns
- Readjust the schedule according to contingencies and priorities
- Write a report, an activity report
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Analyze the impact of campaigns on the target audience
- Ensuring competitive intelligence in the media sector
- Prepare or establish commercial proposals, write price offers, develop quotes
- Research and analyze information on a product, a brand (situation, market, previous advertisements, etc.)
- Monitor and analyze the results of a communication campaign
- Ensure an oral and written presentation of your activity in front of a client, employees, your team in order to generate support
- Collaborate with marketing teams
- Organize follow-up meetings with clients
- Multitask and adapt to rapid changes
- Manage conflicts and negotiate solutions
- Manage a documentary database
- Meet deadlines and specifications for multimedia projects
