## Scope

The vehicle glazing repair and installation technician carries out all common and complex glazing repair and replacement interventions on all types of vehicles. Removal and installation of jointed or glued windshields Disassembly and reassembly of side windows, rear windows, panoramic roof and other glazing Repair of all types of windshields Installation of emergency protection Removal and installation of electronic safety and comfort equipment linked to glazing (e.g. reversing camera) and recalibration Welcomes, advises customers and makes additional sales in connection with the intervention

## Skills to Apply

- Prepare and manage your outdoor vehicle glass repair intervention
- Remove and install removable glazing from a vehicle
- Removing and reinstalling non-removable glazing from a vehicle
- Identify and analyze the repairability of vehicle glass
- Repair vehicle windows while respecting safety standards
- Repairing an impact on a window
- Checking the tightness of a vehicle's non-removable windows
- Establish a quote, an invoice in accordance with the insurer guarantee
