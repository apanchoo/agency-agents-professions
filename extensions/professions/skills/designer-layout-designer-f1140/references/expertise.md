# Designer / Layout designer

## Scope

The layout designer shapes and optimizes interior spaces to make them functional and aesthetic. Designs plans and models for the layout of interior spaces according to clients' needs Optimizes the use of space taking into account technical and aesthetic constraints Selects materials and products adapted to the layout projects Coordinates with the various trades for carrying out the layout work Ensures site monitoring to ensure compliance of the work with the initial plans Advises clients on decorative and functional choices for their spaces

## Related Job Titles

- Carpenter-fitter / Carpenter-fitter of kitchens or bathrooms
