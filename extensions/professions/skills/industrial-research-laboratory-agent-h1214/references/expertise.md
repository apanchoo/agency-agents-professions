# Industrial research laboratory agent

## Scope

The industrial research laboratory agent carries out analyzes and experiments for the development of products and processes. Carries out experiments and analyzes in accordance with established protocols Ensures the maintenance and calibration of laboratory equipment Collaborates with research and development teams to optimize processes Complies with safety standards and regulations in force in the laboratory Monitors and manages stocks of consumables in conjunction with the purchasing department Participates in laboratory cleaning and waste management

## Related Job Titles

- Laboratory assistant in the pharmaceutical industry

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze laboratory experiment results
- Analyze samples of materials or products
- Collect samples for analysis
- Design theoretical models (calculation, simulation, modeling)
- Design or improve tests and trials relating to manufacturing processes
- Make prototypes
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Check data compliance
- Create a technical drawing, plan, or map
- Document a material achievement
- Interpret analysis results for industrial applications
- Update a file, a database
- Carry out statistical analysis or modeling of data
- Write and develop instructions and technical sheets
- Use laboratory data management software
- Create simple assemblies based on a sketch, drawing or description
- Perform and verify general or applied mathematics calculations
- Identify anomalies in test results
- Master the characteristics of a product or material
- Collect data and record them in laboratory notebooks, databases, registers, reports
- Carry out measurements and analyses, identify non-conformities and make technical adjustments
- Ensure cleanliness and order in the work space
- Check the conformity of laboratory equipment
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Carry out cleaning and adjustment operations on materials and equipment
- Monitor laboratory environmental conditions
- Ensure regular calibration of instruments
- Check the condition of a laboratory sample
- Detect non-conformities in materials or products, as well as materials and the working environment, and carry out first-level maintenance
- Participate in research and development projects
- Define supply needs
- Manage inventory
- Managing laboratory waste in an environmentally friendly way
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Act quickly in the event of an incident
- Ensuring the confidentiality of research information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
