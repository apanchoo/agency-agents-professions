# Manager / Project manager in land use planning

## Scope

The Land Use Planning Project Manager manages the planning and implementation of projects aimed at optimizing the use of urban and rural spaces. Develops development projects to meet the sustainable development needs of urban and rural areas Coordinates with various stakeholders to ensure compliance of projects with local and national regulations Conducts environmental impact studies to ensure a balance between economic development and the well-being of the population Proposes innovative solutions to integrate necessary infrastructure without compromising the aesthetics of the landscape Supervises the implementation of projects and monitors progress and necessary adjustments Participates in public meetings to inform and involve local communities in development projects
