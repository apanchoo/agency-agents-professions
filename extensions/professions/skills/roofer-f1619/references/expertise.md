# Roofer

## Scope

The roofer is a craftsman specializing in the installation, repair and renovation of roofs on new construction sites or renovation of residential, commercial or industrial buildings. Carries out the choice of materials, the preparation and installation of roofing elements Installation of waterproof and insulating roofing, installs rainwater evacuation systems and specific elements: roof windows, ornamental elements Can install solar or photovoltaic panels Can work on old and heritage buildings often classified or registered in the register of historic monuments
