# Chef / Head of food products department

## Scope

The Food Products Department Manager supervises the sales flow of products in his department to satisfy customers. Manages the supply and assortment of products on the shelf, ensuring stock availability Optimizes the presentation of products through events and promotions, ensuring their good visibility Controls the quality and freshness of food products Supervises and trains the department's sales team Analyzes the department's performance indicators to adjust commercial strategies Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards
