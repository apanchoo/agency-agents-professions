# Snow grower

## Scope

The Nivoculteur manages the snow level to improve the quality of the slopes and the ski offering in a ski area while respecting the environment and water and energy resources. Produces artificial snow on the ski slopes according to weather conditions and the resort's needs Controls its installation and adjusts the equipment to optimize snow production Evaluates, plans and supervises snow production to ensure the quality of the ski activity Collaborates with other resort services to evaluate and plan snow production Performs or participates in preventive and corrective maintenance of snow production installations May participate in training new employees in snowmaking techniques Travels to a ski area by snowmobile
