# Drinking water production plant manager

## Scope

A drinking water production plant manager is a professional responsible for managing and supervising all of the processes necessary for the production of drinking water. Supervises the production and distribution of drinking water while adhering to quality and safety standards Manages plant facilities and equipment to ensure proper operation and maintenance Optimizes water treatment processes to improve efficiency and reduce costs Ensures plant regulatory compliance with environmental and health laws Leads and trains a team of technicians and operators to ensure efficient operation of the plant Analyzes and resolves technical issues to minimize service interruptions

## Related Job Titles

- Drinking water station manager
- Wastewater treatment plant manager

- Coordinate personnel interventions on machines
- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Promote and measure team performance
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Organize the work of a team
- Train in technical and regulatory developments
- Analyze solid, liquid, gaseous samples (water, sludge, waste) and record the results
- Define eco-industrial intervention procedures
- Prescribe technical solutions for operating new sites, improving equipment or networks
- Measure the pressure, the level of water tables or collection wells and check their condition
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Coordinate field interventions
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Plan the shutdown of equipment
- Monitor equipment performance indicators
- Check the operation of equipment and installations, identify anomalies and preventive or corrective actions
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Perform tests and analyzes to verify compliance with quality standards
- Identify non-conformities
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Manage inventory
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Manage production and operations management
- Supervise the daily production of drinking water
- Monitor factory performance indicators
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Document the procedures and interventions carried out
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage on-site emergencies and incidents
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
