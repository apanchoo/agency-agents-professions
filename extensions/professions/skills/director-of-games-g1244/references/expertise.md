# Director of games

## Scope

The Gaming Director oversees operations related to table games and slot machines. Manages and trains teams of dealers and room staff Controls the application of game rules and security procedures Monitors the fairness and sincerity of games and compliance with regulations Collaborates with security services to prevent fraud

## Related Job Titles

- Director of Slot Machines (MAS)
- Deputy Director / Deputy Director of Games
- Member of the games management committee

## Activities and Skills

- Liven up the game table
- Lead briefing or debrief times
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Implement company guidelines and policy
- Plan and organize the activity of staff under their responsibility
- Recruit and train new staff members
- Train staff on the different gaming tables
- Train employees on anti-money laundering regulations
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Observe and spot changes in player behavior
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Check the operation of play equipment
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Oversee daily operations
- Ensure the accuracy of counting operations, payment of winnings and collection of lost bets
- Organize and plan an activity
- Plan work schedules based on peak activity
- Argue in the event of disputes or inspections
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Monitor compliance with gaming rules and procedures
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Technically assist the games staff during incidents in the running of the games, disputes, customer complaints
- Inform, inform, guide a person on the services offered
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Respond to requests for information
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Communicate effectively with the team and clients
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Arguing to resolve conflicts or misunderstandings
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Intervene in the event of an incident or emergency
