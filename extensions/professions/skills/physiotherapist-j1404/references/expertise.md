# Physiotherapist

## Scope

The physiotherapist plays an essential role in the physical rehabilitation and well-being of patients. Assesses a patient's motor, sensory and cognitive abilities Rehabilitates and rehabilitates the person's motor deficiencies and functional limitations according to medical prescription Uses manual or instrumental techniques to improve mobility and reduce impairments Designs and implements personalized rehabilitation programs for each patient Collaborates with other healthcare professionals to ensure comprehensive patient care May manage a practice

## Related Job Titles

- Chiropractor physiotherapist
- Osteopathic physiotherapist
- Masseur / Masseuse physiotherapist
- Rehabilitative physiotherapist masseuse

## Activities and Skills

- Help optimize physical preparation sessions before, during and after the competition
- Teaching, developing skills
- Offer advice on well-being and lifestyle
- Apply manual mobilization techniques
- Design a therapeutic project
- Assess the patient's physical condition and identify dysfunctions
- Demonstrate dexterity and precision in manipulating patients' muscles and joints
- Identify the patient's needs and exchange information (current treatments, medical history, x-rays, etc.)
- Master pain management techniques and relief methods
- Master therapeutic massage and joint mobilization techniques
- Master the techniques of functional rehabilitation, rehabilitation, and corrective movements
- Modify treatments based on changing patient needs
- Organize the physiotherapy session and adapt it
- Install medical equipment
- Providing paramedical care
- Carry out the physiotherapy massage diagnosis based on muscle strength and posture tests
- Use physiotherapy equipment such as ultrasound, electrostimulators and other devices
- Implement communication actions
- Monitor the progress of treatments and adjust if necessary
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Detect contraindications following fractures, bone infections and refer the patient to a doctor, psychologist, etc.
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Write a report, an activity report
- Write a summary note
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Ensure the accuracy and relevance of information recorded in medical records
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
