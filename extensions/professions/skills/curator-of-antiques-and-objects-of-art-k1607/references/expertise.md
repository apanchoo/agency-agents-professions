# Curator of antiques and objects of art

## Scope

The curator of antiquities and works of art (CAOA) has the mission of identifying, safeguarding and promoting movable objects of heritage interest in the departmental territory, under the heading of Historical Monuments. Organizes the monitoring and preservation of protected movable heritage Is in charge of the legal protection of movable heritage Participates in the implementation of restoration actions (advice, preparation of files, etc.) Documents and catalogs the furniture belonging to historic monuments Collaborates with researchers and experts for in-depth studies
