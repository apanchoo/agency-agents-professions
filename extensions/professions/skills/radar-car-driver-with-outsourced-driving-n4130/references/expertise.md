# Radar car driver with outsourced driving

## Scope

Outsourced Driving Radar Car Driver plays a crucial role in road safety and speed control of drivers. Drives a vehicle equipped with an automatic speed measurement system Monitors and records vehicle speed violations on various roads Performs basic maintenance of the vehicle and on-board equipment Follows pre-established routes and safety instructions Collaborates with authorities to report collected data Participates in road safety awareness missions
