# Systems and network architect

## Scope

The Systems and Network Architect designs and supervises the architecture of computer systems and networks to optimize performance and security. Designs the architecture of computer systems and networks according to the needs of the company Ensures the implementation and deployment of technical solutions Optimizes the performance and security of systems and networks Coordinates projects for the integration of new technologies Supervises the maintenance and evolution of IT infrastructures Collaborates with development and security teams to guarantee the consistency of systems
