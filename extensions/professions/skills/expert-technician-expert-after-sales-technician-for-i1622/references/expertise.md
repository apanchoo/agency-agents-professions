# Expert technician / Expert after-sales technician for road transport vehicles

## Scope

The Expert After-Sales Technician for road transport vehicles is the technical referent for all those involved in automobile after-sales, in the workshop and at customer reception. Works on all types of utility and industrial vehicles, including vehicles with the latest technologies (all gas and electrical systems) Ensures the diagnosis and restoration of conformity of a set of systems Works in particular on complex breakdowns of thermal engines (diesel, gas and alternative fuels), injection, supercharging and depollution systems, multiplexed systems, comfort, safety and ground connection systems, mechanical systems (kinematic chains, braking, suspensions, etc.), constituent elements of the traction chain of an EV/VH, equipment components of utility and industrial vehicles Provides support to the after-sales customer advisor to provide his technical expertise Implements technical training sequences for workshop employees
