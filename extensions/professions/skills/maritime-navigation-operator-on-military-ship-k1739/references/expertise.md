# Maritime navigation operator on military ship

## Scope

The maritime navigation operator on a military ship carries out the basic tasks required within the bridge team of all types of State military ships. Participates, from the bridge, in the operational activity of the vessel Keeps nautical documentation up to date Contributes to nautical monitoring

## Related Job Titles

- Navigation operator
- Operator on nautical watch
- Navigation Technician
- Nautical watch technician

## Activities and Skills

- Use an information system
- Use rigging and maneuvering gear
- Maintain staffing equipment
- Anticipate an event, a situation
- Managing stress and reactions in combat situations
- Implement engineering safety measures
- Apply coastal and offshore or river navigation techniques
- Understand and take into account marine meteorological phenomena
- Contribute to nautical monitoring
- Use navigation methods
- Implement a nautical craft
- Participate in maritime navigation operations and update nautical charts and documents
- Participate, from the bridge, in the operational activity of the ship
- Use navigation equipment
- Using transmission equipment on a military ship
- Ensure proper maintenance and updating of nautical documents
- Communicate effectively with other members of the unit
- Communicate in English orally and in writing
- Implement transmission procedures
- Act in a context of high responsibility and high pressure
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Ensure the application of security rules
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
