# Mover

## Scope

The mover ensures the transport, using a suitable vehicle over all distances, of furniture or all objects on behalf of individuals or companies. Packs, assembles and dismantles furniture and secures items that may be fragile and valuable, loading and unloading them into the vehicle Ensures the handling of sometimes heavy items with appropriate equipment to prevent the risk of injury Organizes and optimizes space in the truck or crates for safe and efficient transportation Coordinates with clients to plan the details of the move and meet their specific needs
