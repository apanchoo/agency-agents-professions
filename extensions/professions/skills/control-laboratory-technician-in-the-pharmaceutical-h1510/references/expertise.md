# Control laboratory technician in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Control Laboratory Technician carries out analyzes and tests to guarantee the quality and conformity of products to specified standards. Performs physicochemical/biological/microbiological/and radioactivity analyzes to control the quality of raw materials and finished products Controls the quality of raw materials and products at different stages of production Interprets test results and writes detailed reports Ensures the calibration and regular maintenance of laboratory equipment Participates in the continuous improvement of testing procedures and safety protocols Collaborates with production teams to resolve quality problems Ensures compliance with safety standards and hygiene in the laboratory
