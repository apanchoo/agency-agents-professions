# Passementier / Passementière

## Scope

The Passementier in the textile industry manufactures and assembles decorative trims for textile applications. Designs and creates textile ornaments such as braids, fringes, and trimmings Uses traditional and modern techniques to create high-quality products Collaborates with designers and fashion houses to develop personalized products Ensures the maintenance and adjustment of looms and other equipment May participate in the restoration of ancient textiles and heritage conservation Manages stocks of raw materials and supervises the production chain

## Activities and Skills

- Train new employees in trade techniques
- Innovate in weaving techniques to improve quality
- Adapting trimmings techniques to modern machines
- Assemble the parts and sub-assemblies of textile articles by stitching according to the technical sheets
- Carry out fixing operations, installation of iron-on adhesive, reinforcing element on textile articles
- Carry out filling operations (feathers, wadding, etc.) on textile items
- Carry out filling operations (hemming, topstitching, etc.) on textile items
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Use and adjust textile devices and equipment
- Maintain cleanliness of the textile workspace
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Analyze the quality of textile raw materials
- Check the conformity of the materials used
- Control the quality of finished products
- Supervise the production process
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Manage stocks of textile raw materials
- Document manufacturing procedures
