# Sports doctor

## Scope

The Sports Doctor is a professional in the health and performance of athletes Evaluates and monitors the state of health of athletes, in prevention, treatment or rehabilitation of injuries Offers rehabilitation programs adapted to sports injuries Collaborates with other specialists to offer complete monitoring at the nutritional and physiotherapeutic level Carries out sports medicine interventions and emergency care at competition or training sites Advises athletes on best practices for their health and performance Participates in research and for education on topics related to sports medicine

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Educate athletes on appropriate nutrition and lifestyle
- Teaching, developing skills
- Accompany the patient in rehabilitation exercises
- Ensure medical follow-up of patients after surgery to check convalescence and treat potential complications
- Design and carry out rehabilitation care
- Design the rehabilitation program (readaptation to daily life actions, relearning spatial landmarks) and adapt it according to the person's condition
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Evaluate the physical condition and sporting abilities of patients
- Managing medical emergencies
- Master the techniques of functional rehabilitation, rehabilitation, and corrective movements
- Implement a therapeutic project
- Implement medical monitoring protocols
- Organize adapted physical rehabilitation programs
- Perform medical expertise
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Carry out a medical diagnosis
- Monitor the evolution of injuries and adjust treatments
- Regularly monitor the health of athletes
- Promote public health programs
- Conduct study and research work
- Update the patient's medical record
- Advise on sports injury prevention techniques
- Promote safe sports practices
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage medical databases
- Communicate diagnoses clearly to patients
- Participate in public health programs
