# Commercial advisor / Bank sales advisor

## Scope

The Bank Commercial Advisor works with clients by combining financial expertise and commercial skills. Analyzes customers' financial needs and offers products and services adapted to their needs Evaluates and develops a portfolio of individual or professional customers by offering personalized solutions Negotiates financial conditions and supports customers in their efforts for better optimization Ensures compliance with the bank's compliance and risk procedures by ensuring regular monitoring

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Negotiate financing terms with banks
- Analyze a client's financial situation: checking their income, debts, legal arrangements and repayment capacity
- Create a credit file
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop a customer and prospect portfolio
- Develop a network of business providers
- Develop and adapt a commercial proposal
- Check data compliance
