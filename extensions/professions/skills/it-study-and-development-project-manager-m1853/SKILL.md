Analyzes client needs to deliver tailored IT solutions Coordinates development teams to ensure quality and on-time deliverables Oversees technical design and application development Ensures communication between project stakeholders to maintain a common vision Manages project budget and resources to optimize costs and efficiency Evaluates and integrates new technologies to improve development processes
