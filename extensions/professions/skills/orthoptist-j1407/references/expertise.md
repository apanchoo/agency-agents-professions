# Orthoptist

## Scope

The Orthoptist is a professional in the diagnosis of visual disorders and rehabilitation. Performs visual assessments and screens for abnormalities in binocular vision and eye motor skills Offers visual rehabilitation exercises adapted to the needs of patients Collaborates closely with ophthalmologists for the monitoring and treatment of patients Uses specialized equipment to assess ocular health Can intervene in specialized contexts such as low vision Contributes to the education of patients on ocular health and the prevention of visual disorders

## Related Job Titles

- Orthoptist rehabilitation / Orthoptist rehabilitation in low vision

## Activities and Skills

- Educate the patient on practices promoting better vision
- Teaching, developing skills
- Act quickly if the patient's vision deteriorates
- Identify the type of dysfunction (strabismus, non-convergence), the difficulties (headaches, double vision) and the patient's environment
- Design and carry out rehabilitation care
- Design the program for the orthoptic session and adapt the exercises according to the patient's reactions
- Design the rehabilitation or orthoptic rehabilitation project and decide on the intervention conditions according to the patient's situation
- Determine the need for orthoptic rehabilitation based on tests and inform the patient
- Perform tests to assess vision, intraocular pressure and other eye parameters
- Evaluate residual visual capacity to adapt exercises
- Perform visual exams to detect vision abnormalities
- Implement a care process
- Organize the follow-up of medical files
- Carry out visual examinations
- Carry out the end of rehabilitation assessment with the person and exchange information with the doctor, teacher, etc.
- Monitor the evolution of the patient's visual condition
- Conduct study and research work
- Maintain medical records up to date
- Process patient monitoring data
- Guide the patient in the use of special optical devices
- Develop visual fatigue prevention programs
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Clearly communicate progress to stakeholders
- Explain in a clear and educational manner
- Analyze visual posture and suggest corrections
- Document interventions and anomalies encountered
- Respect hospital safety and hygiene standards

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
