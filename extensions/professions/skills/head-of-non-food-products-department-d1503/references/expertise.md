# Head of non-food products department

## Scope

The Non-food Products Department Manager manages and optimizes sales within a store. Organizes the product assortment according to trends and consumer demand Supervises and trains the sales team to ensure quality customer service Analyzes sales performance and adjusts commercial strategies accordingly Ensures the implementation of attractive promotions and special commercial operations Ensures compliance with merchandising and product presentation standards Collaborates with suppliers to ensure continuous and appropriate supply by anticipating needs
