# Breeder of pets

## Scope

The Pet Breeder essentially raises and cares for purebred dogs and cats with a view to reselling them. Selects and reproduces breeds according to specific criteria Provides a balanced diet adapted to the needs of each animal Ensures veterinary monitoring and the necessary preventive care Advises clients on the care and maintenance of acquired animals Can participate in animal exhibitions or competitions

## Related Job Titles

- Cat breeder
- Dog breeder
- Kennel Boy/Girl

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in sports competitions, animal competitions or exhibitions
- Administer veterinary products
- Determine food rations according to the physical and physiological needs of the animals
- Educate/train animals
- Teach an animal behaviors adapted to different situations
- Schedule animal vaccinations
- Prepare and distribute food rations to animals
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Monitor an animal's pregnancy status
- Monitor daily animal behavior
- Document genetic lines for traceability
- Manage individual animal monitoring files
- Build a Whelping Box
- Identify the needs for renewal or diversification of species on a farm
- Identify an animal by marking
- Plan reproductive cycles
- Select a breeding male
- Check the geographic or genetic origins of an animal
- Clean equipment and breeding areas
- Manage stocks of food and other supplies
- Ensuring the safety and welfare of animals during transport
- Schedule veterinary check-up visits for animals
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Carry out accounting operations
- Advise clients on proper animal care
- Provide information on animal nutrition, exercise and general well-being
- Inform the client about the breeds and lifestyle of animals
- Marketing a livestock product
- Develop partnerships with food and equipment suppliers
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Regularly update your knowledge on the breeds and specificities of animals
