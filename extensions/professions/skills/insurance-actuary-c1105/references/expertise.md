# Insurance actuary

## Scope

The insurance actuary analyzes financial and demographic risks in order to design forecasting models. Develops insurance products by calculating the necessary premiums and provisions Collaborates with risk management teams to optimize insurance strategies Conducts statistical studies to adjust insurance policies based on market trends Advises management on the financial and actuarial implications of different insurance policies Participates in bringing insurance products into compliance with current legislation
