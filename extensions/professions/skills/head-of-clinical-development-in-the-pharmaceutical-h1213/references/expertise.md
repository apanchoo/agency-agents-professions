# Head of clinical development in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Clinical Development Manager coordinates clinical studies and ensures the compliance of research projects. Coordinates the health product development plan with all associated clinical studies Guarantees the implementation of clinical study protocols in compliance with regulatory and scientific compliance Provides medical expertise on projects Liaises with health authorities and ethical committees for all compliance issues Analyzes and interprets clinical trial data to derive valid conclusions Manages the budget and resources allocated to clinical development projects

## Related Job Titles

- Director of Clinical Research
- Director of clinical studies
- Head Clinical Development
