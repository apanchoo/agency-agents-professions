# Home automation engineer

## Scope

The Home Automation expert, an automation expert, helps improve the comfort or security of the home. Designs and installs equipment automation systems Programs and configures connected devices to automate daily tasks Ensures the maintenance and updating of installed systems Advises customers on the technological solutions best suited to their needs Performs diagnostics to optimize the performance of installations Can train users in the use of home automation equipment
