# Log vehicle driver

## Scope

The log vehicle driver uses a road combination (road tractor and semi-trailer or truck and trailer) and an incorporated crane to load logs and transport them to companies using wood. Drives and maneuvers heavy vehicles intended for the transport of logs Ensures the loading and unloading of logs in complete safety Ensures compliance with traffic rules and safety standards specific to the transport of wood Maintains the log vehicle, checks its proper functioning and reports any anomalies Plans routes by optimizing journeys to reduce costs and transport time Can participate in the management of wood stocks and planning deliveries

## Related Job Titles

- Driver of logging vehicles and mounted cranes
- Log driver

## Activities and Skills

- Record activities in a log
- Use navigation equipment (professional GPS, mobile applications)
- Ensure cleaning of equipment and workplace
- Perform first-level preventive and corrective maintenance on its equipment
- Carry out vehicle safety checks
- Prepare the machine for maintenance
- Carry out technical cleaning
- Anticipate what to do in abnormal and emergency situations
- Apply safety techniques in a forest environment
- Ensuring safety on the construction site
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Check the quantity and condition of the goods
- Prepare, secure and monitor cargo during loading and unloading operations
- Analyze the characteristics of the wood to be transported
- Securing large logs
- Load and unload log stocks with its road package
- Drive your road vehicle on roads, forest tracks and other access roads
- Move large items according to quality and safety standards
- Untie, detach large logs
- Sling, guide the movement of large elements
- Organize vehicle loading according to the tour plan
- Check that your load complies with current legislation
- Carry out administrative tasks (delivery slip, waybill, etc.) related to loading or delivery
- Complete transport documents
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Respect the specific rules relating to forestry sites
- Use the safety equipment provided in the event of an emergency
- Check the safety systems of a handling machine
- Maintain a high level of customer service
- Implement product and container recovery procedures
- Adopt professional communication with customers, law enforcement, emergency services and road users
- Report to the operator on his activity
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Establish traffic rules on a construction site and its surroundings
- Manage breaks and driving times in accordance with regulations
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Respect the highway code
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
