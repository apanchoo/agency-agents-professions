# Recycling agent

## Scope

The recycling center agent welcomes and guides users on how to deposit waste on the site. Ensures the monitoring, management and operation of the recycling center Prepares the opening and closing of the site Regulates the entry and exit flow of users of the site Controls unloading Manages the monitoring of the rotation of skips Ensures the security of the site

## Related Job Titles

- Reception and reception agent for recycling center
- Waste Control Agent
- Waste disposal technical agent
- Delegated management agent / Delegated waste management agent
- Recycling center guard

## Activities and Skills

- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Anticipate the rotation schedule for skips and other containers
- Estimate volumes contributed by users
- Evaluate the filling of skips by optimizing their volumes in order to avoid overflows
- Ensure the correct allocation of waste and correct any errors
- Check the general condition of the site
- Manage site openings and closings
- Maintain cleanliness of the site
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Control user access to the site
- Guarantee the quality of sorting to avoid any refusal by buyers
- Identify deposits that could be intended for reuse
- Optimize the filling and rotation of skips
- Monitor the quality of waste sorting
- Receive, monitor quantities and control waste from plants and bio-waste collections
- Coordinate evacuations with different service providers
- Perform daily activity reports
- Complete all documents related to the activity and waste monitoring
- Receive service providers during the removal of skips
- Promote recycling initiatives to users
- Sort and direct waste according to its future treatment or recovery
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Raise public awareness
- Manage incidents and apply internal regulations
- Provide all documents and information relating to the activity and operation of the recycling center
- Report any incident, accident and anomaly to your superiors according to the procedures in force
