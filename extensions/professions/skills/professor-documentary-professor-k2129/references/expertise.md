# Professor / Documentary professor

## Scope

An expert in the field of information sciences, documentation and communication, the librarian teacher contributes to teaching and systems enabling the acquisition of information culture and mastery by all students. Proposes to the head of the establishment and implements a documentation policy adapted to the contexts and contributes to the definition of the digital aspect of the establishment project Ensures the management of a documentation and information center service Teaches (information-documentation sciences) Participates in the management of multidisciplinary and cultural educational activities

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Organize time, space, and operation of the CDI
- Support the learner's realization of personal and professional projects
- Welcome and support students in the documentation and information center (CDI)
- Contribute to the pre- and post-baccalaureate training continuum
- Contribute to the cultural, social and civic education of the student
- Contribute to the construction of citizenship
- Teaching, developing skills
- Embody, bring to life and transmit the values of the Republic
- Implement projects that develop a taste for reading
- Implement teaching in information documentation (pedagogy and didactics of information documentation)
- Implement media and information education
- Implement teaching by including it in a training course and contribute to disciplinary and specialty teaching as part of cooperation with the teaching and educational team
- Chair or participate in an examination board
- Use digital educational tools
- Create, manage and animate the physical and digital documentary collection
- Enrich a database
- Define the strategic documentary orientations of the establishment and specific to the Documentation and Information Center (CDI)
- Manage a service
- Implement an acquisition policy
- Implement an educational project for the CDI which is integrated into the establishment project
- Welcoming students to the CDI in conjunction with the students' different living and working places (classrooms, study rooms, boarding schools)
- Initiate or participate in multidisciplinary and educational activities, in partnership with the teaching and educational team
- Participate in the activities of the teaching team
- Participate in documentary networks and lead, where applicable, the RENADOC network
- Work in partnership with stakeholders in the economic and cultural environment and possibly in a network
- Ensure monitoring for the teaching and educational team
- Update a catalogographic, bibliographic and siteographic database
