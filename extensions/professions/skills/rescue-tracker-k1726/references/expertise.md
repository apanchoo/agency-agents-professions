# Rescue tracker

## Scope

The First Aid Tracker ensures the safety and well-being of skiers. Ensures the security of ski slopes (protective devices, markings, signage, etc.) Ensures the surveillance and safety of skiers and informs them about risks and conditions Intervenes quickly in the event of an accident to provide first aid as a team Participates in preventing avalanche risks and securing risk areas Can supervise a team

## Related Job Titles

- Avalanche dog handler
- Track safety manager
- Avalancher shooter

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate a team of rescuers
- Regularly evaluate the skills of rescue teams
- Optimize access routes for emergency services
- Provide first aid
- Organize mountain rescue operations
- Manage communications
- Ensure technological monitoring of new security devices
- Ensure the maintenance of emergency equipment and runway protection equipment
- Receive alerts (phone calls, alarms) and trigger appropriate rescue and assistance interventions
- Follow emergency evacuation procedures
- Ensure the security of the slopes: signage, protection devices
- Check the operation of a safety device
- Study snow and snowpack characteristics and assess avalanche risks
- Assess the situation, the risks and determine assistance and rescue actions for people
- Evaluate security equipment needs
- Trigger avalanche shots (if enabled)
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Drive specialized vehicles to access intervention sites
- Document incidents and write reports
- Evaluate weather conditions for safety
- Carry out searches for missing persons
- Carry out operations in difficult to access areas
- Use tracking equipment to find victims
- Communicate and develop a helping relationship
- Raising awareness about mountain safety
- Using English in a professional context
- Inform visitors about the conditions and safety of their activity
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
