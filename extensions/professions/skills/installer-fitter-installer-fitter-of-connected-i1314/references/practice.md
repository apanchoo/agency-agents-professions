# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Collect symptoms, history and architecture of the equipment.
2. Prioritize possible causes and discriminating controls.
3. Prepare for intervention and recurrence prevention.
4. Define recommissioning and maintenance indicators.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Mechanical and Fluidic Technology 1
- Mechanical and Fluidic Technology 3
- Mechanical and Fluidic Technology 2
- Optical fibers and propagation
- Network and telecom technologies
- Introduction to computer networks

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Install a system as a team
- Carry out and check the connection of equipment
- Manage security solutions for the needs of a client's IT network
- Deploy operator infrastructure and customer services

## Deliverables

- Hierarchical diagnosis
- Range of intervention
- Maintenance plan and return to service sheet

## Acceptance Criteria

- A proposed cause has proof or a check to be carried out
- The consignment conditions are identified
- The return to expected operation is verifiable

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
