# Designer / Digital designer in jewelry

## Scope

Provides by computer-aided design (CAD) the virtual representation of a technical object, integrating the technical constraints of the different manufacturing processes and respecting the function of the product, while conforming to the expected aesthetics Studies and prepares the digital part model Produces or has produced a draft of the part model Checks the deviations between the designed parts and the expected, analyzes them and carries out a reverse design if necessary

## Related Job Titles

- CAD designer in jewelry and jewelry
- 3D jewelry designer/designer

## Activities and Skills

- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Create a first digital representation of the piece respecting the creative intention and feasibility constraints
- Track Part Print
- Appropriate the aesthetic intention of the creator from various media (gouache / 3D / physics)
- Validate the volumes and aesthetics of part drafts
- Analyze and control the gaps between the drawn parts and the expected
- Guarantee the feasibility of the product in collaboration with the manufacturing workshops and technical services
- Integrate ergonomics, integrity and repairability criteria into its design
- Conduct feasibility studies
- Model a model in CAD
- Create the prototype and definition file
- Carry out technical or technological monitoring to anticipate developments
- Design and produce the computer files necessary for rapid prototyping of models (resins, wax, metal, etc.)
- Study and interpret the drawing of a part
- Master digital design software: CREO, Rhino&Rhino Gold, Grasshopper
- Produce the plans and diagrams necessary to create a technical file
- Carry out a reverse design if necessary
- Understanding the jewelry making process
- Control the quality of finished products
- Readjust the schedule according to contingencies and priorities
- Based on visuals, make the various adjustments with the client and finalize the part model
- Analyze and interpret customer demand and needs (internal/external) based on the information provided
- Analyze the customer order, write the specifications, develop the costing of the work
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Work in collaboration with the workshop
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Organize and archive your files securely

## Professional Approach

- Show curiosity, open-mindedness
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
