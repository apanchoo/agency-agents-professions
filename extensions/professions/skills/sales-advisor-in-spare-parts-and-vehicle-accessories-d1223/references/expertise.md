# Sales advisor in spare parts and vehicle accessories

## Scope

The sales advisor in spare parts and vehicle accessories can specialize in the marketing of parts and accessories for private vehicles, industrial vehicles or motorcycles. Welcomes the customer and advises them from a technical point of view on their choice, use or assembly of products and accessories suitable for vehicles, particularly in areas of complex technology. Purchases and markets spare parts, products and services. Handles customer and supplier complaints. Promotes the company's products and services.

## Related Job Titles

- Automotive accessories trader
- Automotive accessories salesperson

- Receive and check the conformity of an order for spare parts and vehicle accessories
- Prepare and ship an order for spare parts and automotive accessories

- Provide technical advice to the customer on spare parts and vehicle accessories

- Identify customer needs to complement the main sale of spare parts and vehicle accessories
- Arrange the sales area for spare parts and vehicle accessories
- Conclude the sale of spare parts and vehicle accessories
