# Psychologist

## Scope

The psychologist provides psychological support to individuals. Evaluates and diagnoses psychological and behavioral disorders Proposes therapies adapted to individual needs Supports patients in their personal and professional development Carries out interventions in schools, hospitals or businesses Participates in research to improve psychological practices Collaborates with other health professionals for overall patient care

## Related Job Titles

- Neuropsychologist
- Psychology Practitioner
- Research psychologist
- Clinical psychologist
- Psychologist-psychotherapist
- Psychotherapist

## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Lead a psychological support unit
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Determine the need for psychological support, decide on arrangements with the person or refer them to a doctor, social service, paramedical staff
- Interpreting non-verbal behaviors in a therapeutic context
- Practice an individual, group or family psychotherapy session
- Carry out psychological tests, evaluate the person's profile and establish the psychological assessment
- Prevent psychosocial risks
- Carry out neuropsychological assessments and propose a retraining program (memory workshop, cognitive rehabilitation of daily living activities, etc.)
- Provide psychological support
- Monitor the progress of patients' therapy
- Analyze the life context of a person or family
- Regularly assess the needs of the person or family
- Update a file, a database
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Carry out legal expertise actions
- Develop and unite a network (health professionals, health authorities, patient associations, etc.)
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Guarantee the confidentiality of information
- Act quickly in an emergency situation
- Structuring, synthesizing information
- Use digital tools
- Regularly update your knowledge

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
