# Buyer seller / Buyer seller of gold, precious metals

## Scope

The Buyer-seller of gold and precious metals is an expert in the precious metals market. Evaluates, negotiates these raw materials according to market fluctuations with customers and suppliers Ensures the sale of precious metals respecting authenticity standards through traceability of transactions Expertise and advises customers on the value, characteristics and investments of precious metals Manages stocks of precious metals and ensures their security May participate in trade fairs and exhibitions specializing in precious metals
