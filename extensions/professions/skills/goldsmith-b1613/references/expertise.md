# Goldsmith

## Scope

The goldsmith ensures the assembly of small series individual pieces of goldsmithing and the goldsmith restorer carries out the repair of pieces of goldsmithing using fundamental goldsmithing techniques, relying on technical expertise allowing a methodology adapted to all forms. Can braze, weld, chisel, dent, adjust (goldsmith) Assembles the whole and sub-assembly by brazing and tin welding (goldsmith) Creates the necessary tools for simple or complex parts (goldsmith restorer)

## Related Job Titles

- Goldsmith restorer

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Create drawings or plans of missing or specific parts
- Create plans and assembly range
- Analyze the product manufacturing sheet to meet customer expectations (product range, expected level of finish, expected production times, etc.)
- Record the start and end of a step on a digital production order
- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Model shapes to be made on models or prototypes
- Ensure surface preparation such as adjustments or chiseling
- Define the intervention steps for shaping or decorating an object
- Identify defects in a part during adjustment, dent removal or polishing
- Create specific pieces of goldsmithing with given instructions or independently
- Organize your space and workstation
- Organize your space and workstation, following the rules of cleanliness and safety
- Ensure first-level maintenance of your work tools
- Ensure repairs such as solder pits or deformations linked to welding
- Carry out repair operations
- Carry out restorations with a view to resembling the initial pieces
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Control the quality of parts
- Control assemblies for high-level rendering
- Detect defects, nuances, alloy of materials and determine the constraints of the part
- Carry out checks at the end of the project, ensuring compliance with the specifications
- Participate in the development of a technical quote
- Send information to your manager
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Apply safety rules in practices and tasks
- Identify the characteristics of the product in order to mobilize the best possible preparation or assembly techniques
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
- Mobilize your concentration throughout a process
