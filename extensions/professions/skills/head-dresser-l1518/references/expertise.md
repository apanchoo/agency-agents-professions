# Head dresser

## Scope

The Head Dresser supervises and coordinates the dressing of the artists, ensuring the management of costumes and accessories to guarantee their availability and good condition throughout the performances. Coordinates the dressing of performers before and during performances or filming Manages inventory of costumes and props, and supervises their maintenance May make on-site costume adjustments and modifications Ensures consistency of clothing style throughout production

## Related Job Titles

- Head dresser / Head dresser for entertainment and audiovisual
- Dresser

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate the activity of a team
- Organize the distribution of tasks for the preparation of accessories
- Handle rapid scene changes
- Follow staging guidelines
- Write and develop instructions and technical sheets
- Decorate costumes using trompe l'oeil techniques
- Organize the necessary fittings and adjustments
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Check the costumes and carry out alterations and finishes
- Ensure monitoring of the maintenance of costumes and their accessories (small sewing, ironing, stain removal)
- Supervise the preparation of costumes for shows
- Ensure that the standards of costumes are checked before their release on stage
- Ensure compliance with health and safety standards
- Ensure costume management during productions
- Maintain an accurate inventory of costumes and props
- Organize the storage of products in reserve
- Ensure punctual service according to agreed times
- Respect the schedule established in collaboration with all production stakeholders
- Readjust the schedule according to contingencies and priorities
- Ensure the supply, preparation and distribution of costumes
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Using English in a professional context
- Document work procedures
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
- Use digital tools
- Memorize the roles of the shows as well as the costumes and accessories associated with them

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
