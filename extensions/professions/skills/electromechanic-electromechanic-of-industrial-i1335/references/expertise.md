# Electromechanic / Electromechanic of industrial equipment

## Scope

The Industrial Equipment Electromechanic ensures the maintenance and repair of electromechanical equipment in a production environment. Ensures the installation, monitoring, maintenance and troubleshooting of industrial equipment Controls and optimizes the operation of rotating machines and operating equipment Performs precise diagnostics to prevent breakdowns and guarantee the continuity of production Participates in the improvement of manufacturing processes and the optimization of machine performance Collaborates with production teams to ensure effective preventive maintenance Complies with current safety standards and environmental regulations

## Activities and Skills

- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Support the appropriation of a tool by its users
- Train users in basic maintenance procedures
- Troubleshoot advanced technical issues
- Analyze maintenance data
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Collaborate with suppliers for product quality
- Make adjustments to automated systems to optimize their operation
- Make adjustments to industrial equipment
- Make hydraulic, electrical and mechanical connections for equipment and accessories
- Check the conformity of automated installations according to safety standards
- Diagnose breakdowns in electromechanical systems and propose solutions
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Identify faulty components and parts
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Use testing devices (pressure, flow, vibration, etc.) and electrical measuring devices
- Analyze machine performance to propose improvements
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Ensure the application and compliance with hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental (waste management, noise pollution, etc.) rules on the site
- Manage stocks of maintenance equipment and spare parts
- Write a report, an activity report
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Collaborate with teams to improve quality
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- React effectively in the event of a technical incident
