# Technical-commercial

## Scope

The technical sales representative advises and sells specific products or services to customers. Identifies customer needs and offers appropriate technical and commercial solutions Negotiates the commercial and technical conditions of contracts Ensures technical and commercial monitoring of projects Develops and retains the customer portfolio Conducts technological and competitive monitoring Can coordinate a sales or technical team depending on the structure of the company
