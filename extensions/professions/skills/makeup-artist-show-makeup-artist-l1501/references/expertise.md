# Makeup artist / Show makeup artist

## Scope

The show makeup artist is an artist who transforms and enhances faces for each performance. Designs and creates makeup for singers, musicians, actors, models, TV presenters, journalists for shows, concerts, fashion shows, filming, according to production and broadcast requirements. Collaborates with directors and costume designers to create appropriate makeup Uses a variety of techniques to create special effects, such as scarring or aging Maintains and organizes makeup equipment, ensuring cleanliness and hygiene May train and supervise assistants to prepare actors' makeup for large productions
