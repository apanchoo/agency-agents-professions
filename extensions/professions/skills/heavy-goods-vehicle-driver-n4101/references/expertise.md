# Heavy goods vehicle driver

## Scope

The heavy goods vehicle driver safely drives a heavy group vehicle (carrier vehicle) requiring C or C1 licenses, autonomously and optimized in the commercial context of the company, to transport goods from a collection point to a delivery point over a short or long distance. Participates in the protection of the environment by adopting the principles of eco-driving Respects deadlines, the regulations in force particularly in terms of driving and rest times, road regulations, safety and company procedures Prepares and organizes its mission Checks the operating state of the vehicle and its equipment, the presence and validity of mandatory documents and reports any anomaly Can load and unload goods or supervise handling operations while ensuring their safety and integrity Completes or arranges for transportation documents to be completed Maintains regular communication with the operations department to report any incidents Represents the company's brand image in its relationships with shippers and recipients
