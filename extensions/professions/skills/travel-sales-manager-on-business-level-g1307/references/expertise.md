# Travel sales manager on business level

## Scope

The Business Travel Sales Manager is a reference partner for the sale of business travel solutions. Manages and develops a portfolio of corporate clients for their business travel needs Designs tailor-made solutions to optimize business travel and associated costs Ensures commercial monitoring and customer satisfaction by offering tailored services Negotiates with travel service providers to obtain the best conditions Uses advanced technological tools for managing reservations and itineraries Can manage a team and train new employees in the specifics of business travel

## Related Job Titles

- Corporate travel service manager
