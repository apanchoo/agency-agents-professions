## Scope

The cabin mechanic's mission is to carry out maintenance operations relating to the layout of aircraft cabins (galleys, water circuits, cabin lighting, emergency circuits, upholstery, equipment, toilets, etc.); he works for an aeronautical equipment manufacturer, or on behalf of an airline or an aircraft maintenance company. Guarantees the comfort and safety of passengers, and therefore plays a major role in the airline's brand image Installs the commercial dressing elements of a cabin Carry out periodic inspection visits (transit, pre-flight, weekly, etc.) Ensure the cleanliness of the cabin (seats, carpets, toilets, equipment, etc.) Report any worn or damaged parts and replace them Check the presence of safety elements in cabin (fire extinguishers, oxygen mask, etc.) Ensure compliance of an intervention with the regulatory standards in force Strictly respects the safety standards and aeronautical regulations in force

## Skills to Apply

- Check the conformity of equipment installed on the aircraft

- Maintain and check on-board electrical and electronic circuits
- Identify faulty aircraft components
- Carry out inspections of aircraft structures and equipment during stopovers
- Check cabin equipment and repair it
- Advise management on equipment investments
- Train new technicians in procedures
