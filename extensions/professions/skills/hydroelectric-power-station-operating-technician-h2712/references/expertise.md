# Hydroelectric power station operating technician

## Scope

The Hydroelectric Power Plant Operations Technician ensures the management and maintenance of renewable energy production facilities. Supervises and controls hydroelectric installations to ensure their operation Adjusts the parameters of production equipment according to needs and available resources Carries out preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Monitors performance indicators of installations and proposes improvements to increase efficiency Ensures compliance of operations with environmental and safety standards Can intervene on one or more production sites
