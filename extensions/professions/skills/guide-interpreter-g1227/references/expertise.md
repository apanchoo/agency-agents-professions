# Guide-interpreter

## Scope

At the service of customers, the guide-interpreter transmits his knowledge in the form of an animated historical and cultural journey. Welcomes and accompanies groups of tourists or individual visitors Interprets the cultural, historical and natural heritage during guided tours Adapts his speech according to the audience and the language of the visitors Organizes and plans visit itineraries according to the expectations and constraints of the group Can manage the logistical aspects of the visits, including reservations and transport

## Related Job Titles

- National interpreter guide
- Regional guide-interpreter

## Activities and Skills

- Train junior guides on guiding best practices
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Assist tourists in case of specific needs
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Use group management software to organize visits
- Ensuring the safety of visitors during excursions
- Optimize routes for specific needs (accessibility)
- Establish an authorization or reservation request
- Ensure monitoring of current regulations
- Monitor the application of environmental standards
- Accompany tourists while respecting ecological standards
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Monitor and regulate the flow of visitors in restricted areas
- Welcome and inform tourists about historic sites
- Provide relevant cultural and historical information
- Provide personalized recommendations for additional visits
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Negotiate with suppliers for tour services
- Organize personalized tourist tours
- Analyze tourism trends to offer innovative tours
- Develop and manage relationships or partnerships
- Manage tour itineraries to optimize time
- Promote responsible and respectful tourism in order to protect natural and cultural sites
- Present the geographical, historical and cultural particularities of the places
- Communicate clearly in multiple languages with tourists
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Express yourself with politeness and courtesy
- Raise awareness of local customs and traditions
- Collect information on the duration, number of participants, and specific features of the tourist support service
- Carry out documentary research
- Carry out documentary monitoring
- Quickly resolve logistical issues arising during visits
- Use audiovisual equipment to enhance the visiting experience
- Regularly update your knowledge
- Collect feedback from participants to improve future visits
