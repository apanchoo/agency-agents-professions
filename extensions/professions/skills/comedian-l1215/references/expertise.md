# Comedian

## Scope

The Comedian, an artist who brightens everyday life with his talent and originality. Creates and performs skits, funny stories or monologues to entertain an audience Participates in live shows, television shows or special events Writes and refines their own lines to develop a unique and captivating style Collaborates with other artists and entertainment professionals May host workshops or classes to share their art and train future comedians

## Related Job Titles

- Comic

## Activities and Skills

- Maintain optimal physical condition for performance
- Contribute to promotional actions in the media and on social networks
- Schedule show and tour dates
- Write sketches, texts or humorous columns
- Interact with the audience live
- Master your gestures and body expression
- Put on a show
- Modify the spectacular or comic effects of the number according to the reactions of the audience
- Parody a character or situation
- Practice vocal exercises
- Renew yourself to keep your writing original and current
- Use voice, tone and body language to reinforce humor
- Adapt humorous content depending on the context
- Manage technical or artistic unforeseen events during shows
- Collaborate with other authors or directors
- Create smooth transitions between jokes
- Use storytelling techniques to captivate the audience
- Continually develop your joke repertoire
- Memorize information
- Organize and develop your professional network
