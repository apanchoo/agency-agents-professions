# Leatherworker

## Scope

The leatherworker mainly designs, manufactures and repairs small leather goods and custom-made items using different types of leather and other materials. Designs and produces leather items such as bags, belts, wallets Selects quality materials and cuts the leather pieces according to the patterns Assembles the leather pieces using specific techniques such as sewing or gluing Apply manual finishes to improve the aesthetics and durability of the products Can personalize items at the customer's request

## Related Job Titles

- Leather craftsman
- Bootmaker
- Belt / Belt maker
- Custom shoe manufacturer
- Sheath
- Glovemaker
- Malletier / Malletière
- Art leatherworker
- Parurier

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Design new models and accessories for a collection
- Design and make patterns for leather items
- Determine preparation or finishing interventions depending on the materials, products (hides, leather, etc.) and defects identified
- Adjust the thickness of skins and leathers depending on the final use of the item
- Assemble (gluing, sewing) the parts of the item (stem, bellows) manually or using a machine
- Assemble the parts of a prototype by stitching or gluing
- Choosing the right tools for leatherwork or saddlery
- Decorate rooms or items
- Prepare the machines and tools necessary for production
- Cover rigid objects or supports with leather or other soft materials
- Create leather items at the customer's request
- Carry out assembly operations by nailing, stapling, sewing, on leather and skin parts
- Carry out assembly/assembly operations on shoes
- Carry out material cutting operations (template positioning, etc.) manually or using a machine
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Select the raw materials to mobilize
- Using Leather Dyeing Techniques
- Ensure the maintenance of workshop tools and equipment
- Restoring leather goods
- Ensure compliance of finished products with specifications
- Control the quality of treated hides and skins
- Control the quality of finished products
- Check the conformity of parts and shaped articles (color appearance, regularity of seams, etc.) and carry out corrective actions
- Define supply needs
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Define with the customer the characteristics of the item to be made according to their wishes and needs and inform them of the conditions of production
- Provide quality service
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Evaluate production costs and define sales prices
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Negotiate with suppliers of leather and materials
- Ensure that activities are carried out in compliance with quality, cost and deadline objectives
- Collaborate with other artisans for specific projects
