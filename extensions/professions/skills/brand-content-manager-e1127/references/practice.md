# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Identify audiences, information objective and editorial line.
2. Cross-reference sources and organize information.
3. Produce the requested content or documentary device.
4. Check accuracy, rights, readability and distribution.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Fundamental Marketing, Brand Strategy
- Image and sound
- Market research, Marketing research
- Storytelling (narrative communication)
- Content management
- Communication and marketing strategies

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Implement a digital communications strategy
- Carry out a study on a brand
- Create for a visual communication campaign
- Produce multimedia content

## Deliverables

- Editorial or documentary brief
- Content or ranking system
- Source verification grid

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every verifiable statement has a source
- Rights and credits are identified
- The format meets the uses of the target audience

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
