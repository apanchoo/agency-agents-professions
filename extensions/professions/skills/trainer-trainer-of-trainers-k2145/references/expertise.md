# Trainer / Trainer of trainers

## Scope

The Trainer of Trainers ensures the development of the teaching skills of training facilitators. Designs and runs training sessions for trainers Adapts teaching methods and tools to the specific needs of trainers Evaluates the skills acquired by trainers at the end of the sessions Supports trainers in putting their new skills into practice Regularly updating training programs to ensure their relevance Ensures educational monitoring to integrate innovations into training practices

## Related Job Titles

- Facilitator/Trainer facilitator
- Trainer / Trainer of training facilitators

## Activities and Skills

- Provide constructive and appreciative feedback
- Supervise teams of trainers
- Encourage critical thinking
- Facilitate interactive training sessions
- Strengthen trainers’ project management skills
- Promote and share good practices
- Ensure post-training follow-up to ensure effective application of lessons
- Design training modules
- Design training programs adapted to specific needs
- Design educational supports and tools (teaching materials, educational software, exam subject)
- Advise trainers on best teaching practices and teaching tools
- Contribute to the development of skills in an informal setting
- Correct assessments (exercises, practical work, exams)
- Define educational progression
- Define a learning objective and the program of educational activities
- Teaching, developing skills
- Integrate different teaching approaches (face-to-face, distance learning, e-learning, blended learning)
- Promote innovative teaching methods
- Support trainers in preparing their own training sessions
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Use varied teaching methods to promote learning (practical cases, workshops, simulations)
- Use digital educational tools
- Ensure educational monitoring to stay up to date on teaching methods
- Ensure training monitoring and trace it in the educational monitoring tools available
- Evaluate the effectiveness of the teaching methods used
- Evaluate the effectiveness of training programs
- Evaluate the skills acquired by trainees
- Implement a training evaluation process
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Lead a themed workshop
- Stay informed of new educational trends and technological developments
- Use digital tools
- Participate in continuing education sessions and professional conferences
