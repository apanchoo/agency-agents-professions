# Audiovisual equipment operation and maintenance technician

## Scope

The Audiovisual Equipment Operations and Maintenance Technician provides maintenance, repair and troubleshooting of equipment used in professional contexts such as recording studios, performance halls and production facilities. Diagnoses and resolves breakdowns and malfunctions Carries out preventive and corrective maintenance interventions for users, ensuring proper operation Configures and installs audiovisual systems according to needs Can coordinate a technical team during complex installations

## Related Job Titles

- Professional audio-video maintenance technician
- Sound system maintenance technician
- Sound-video technician

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Train users in the operation of audio-video equipment
- Define the technical characteristics of the product
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Promote the use of emerging technologies
- Define an installation procedure or electronic system configuration according to the customer environment
- Adapting electronic equipment to new needs
- Configure the installation and carry out its adjustment
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Use and adjust sound and light, cinematographic, audiovisual machines or equipment
- Connect electronic equipment
- Carry out an electrical wiring operation
- Determine the causes of malfunctions
- Identify faulty components and parts
- Locate the breakdown or failure through checks, tests and measurements
- Plan maintenance interventions according to priorities
- Carry out a technical diagnosis
- Anticipate cybersecurity risks
- Define interventions on equipment according to the technical file
- Guarantee the proper functioning, availability and performance of connected equipment under your responsibility
- Check the assembly and wiring (value of components, electrical continuity, etc.)
- Verify by simulation, tests, trials, calculations, the functionalities and characteristics of the electrical or electronic system
- Analyze the quality of a film stock
- Sort and direct waste according to its future treatment or recovery
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Explain and argue an intervention or technical solution to the client
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Adjust your intervention on equipment or a system according to the risks
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital tools
- Analyze customer feedback to improve after-sales service
