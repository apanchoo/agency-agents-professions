# TMA Project Manager - Third Party Application Maintenance

## Scope

The TMA Project Manager - Third Party Application Maintenance plays a crucial role in the management and optimization of a company's IT systems. Supervises and coordinates IT application maintenance activities Liaises between users, technical teams and external service providers Analyzes customer needs and proposes tailored solutions to improve existing systems Plans and tracks software updates and bug fixes Manages the budget and resources allocated to maintenance projects Ensures compliance of maintenance practices with safety and quality standards
