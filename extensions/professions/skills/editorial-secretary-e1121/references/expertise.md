# Editorial secretary

## Scope

The editorial secretary is an essential link in the editorial production chain which takes care of the quality of the content, before publication. Assists editors and journalists in documentary research and verification of information Checks and corrects editorial and visual content Formats and enhances articles and other texts before publication Manages deadlines and priorities to ensure the punctuality of publications Can contribute to the writing of content according to the needs of the editorial team

## Related Job Titles

- Editorial assistant
- Editorial secretary in Computer Aided Publication -DTP-
- General Secretary / General Editorial Secretary

- Adapt content and communications to different media and platforms
- Ensure consistency of editorial content
- Collaborate with other departments for unified communication
- Collect and prepare texts, illustrations
- Contribute to the writing of editorial reports
- Create different reading levels on the page (hooks, inter-titles, etc.)
- Make the necessary arbitrations between editorial pages and advertising pages
- Prioritize and organize information within a page, consistent with the model
- Prepare and follow the digital railway
- Create a layout model
- Write a press article
- Analyze the structure and content of texts
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Ensure regulatory compliance of technical documents and publications
- Use word processing and layout software
- Check the quality of content before publication
- Correct spelling, grammar, syntax and typographical errors
- Ensure compliance with an editorial line
- Organize meetings and write reports
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Respect publication deadlines
- Respect ethical standards in publishing content
- Apply eco-gestures in your practice
- Analyze the potential and expectations of the public
- Optimize the natural referencing (SEO) of online articles
- Monitor publishing projects with contributors, authors, illustrators, printers, proofreaders
- Correct and format a document
- Identify sources and locations of information
- Identify emerging topics likely to be the subject of editorial treatment
- Create or modify the layout of a document
- Carry out documentary research
- Rewrite content or change text style
- Know how to manage several projects or activities simultaneously
- Supervise the finalization of documents before publication
- Select illustrations for an article
