# Merchant Navy deckhand

## Scope

The merchant navy sailor monitors, operates and maintains the equipment (on board or operating machines, etc.) and the cargo of ships during maritime crossings, according to maritime transport regulations, rules for the safety of goods and people and service requirements. Carries out painting, greasing and cleaning work on handling and rescue equipment Watches loading and unloading operations of goods and passengers under the authority of the ship's officers Watches the bridge in shifts (periods of 4 hours) under the authority of the officer of the watch With a qualification as bridge watchman (helmsman), can also hold the helm Can carry out ship delivery, leasing, passenger transport (charter and large practice) pleasure craft) Can carry out mooring operations Can coordinate the activity of a team
