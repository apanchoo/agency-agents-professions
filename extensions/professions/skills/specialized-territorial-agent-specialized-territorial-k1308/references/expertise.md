# Specialized territorial agent / Specialized territorial agent for nursery schools (ATSEM)

## Scope

Assists teaching staff in welcoming children and leading educational activities. Helps children carry out everyday activities during school or extracurricular time. Carries out the preparation of equipment, storage and maintenance of the children's living environment.

## Related Job Titles

- Specialized agent / Specialized nursery school agent (ASEM)
- Specialized agent / Specialist agent for primary schools
- Maternal help / School nursery help
- Territorial Assistant / Territorial Teaching Assistant

## Activities and Skills

- Welcoming children
- Organize fun and educational activities during the journey
- Supporting the development, autonomy and awakening of the child
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Adapt your practices to support children with disabilities
- Contribute to a person's psychological well-being
- Observe the child's behavior and development and identify possible difficulties
- Provide the child with elements of comfort and reassurance
- Prepare and give a meal or drink to the child
- Analyze risky situations and propose solutions
- Use specific software
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Maintain, clean a product or equipment
- Handle cleaning and maintenance products
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Define supply needs
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Organize the selective sorting of waste
- Build lasting professional relationships with customers and suppliers
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Inform parents about the organization of the structure and present the program of activities to the children
- Relay information
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Understand and respect the rules of collective life
- Memorize information

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be open to changes
- Listen, show empathy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
