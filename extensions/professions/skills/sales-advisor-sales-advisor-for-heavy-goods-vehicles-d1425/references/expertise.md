# Sales advisor / Sales advisor for heavy goods vehicles

## Scope

The heavy goods vehicle sales advisor is a professional specializing in the sale of trucks, buses and other types of heavy utility vehicles. Advises customers, whether individuals, businesses or organizations, in the selection of heavy-duty vehicles suited to their needs Conducts vehicle demonstrations and tests to facilitate customer decision-making Negotiates terms of sale and establishes contracts in accordance with company policies Follows market trends to offer competitive and innovative products May coordinate with after-sales services to ensure customer satisfaction Develops a professional network to increase sales opportunities
