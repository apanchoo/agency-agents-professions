## Scope

The Social Contributions Controller regulates and supervises contributions. Controls companies to ensure compliance with social declarations Carries out audits to detect fraud or errors in payments Informs and advises companies on social legislation while participating in the implementation of social security policy Writes detailed reports following the controls and audits carried out Can intervene in support during legal proceedings linked to social contributions
