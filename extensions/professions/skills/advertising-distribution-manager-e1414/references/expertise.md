# Advertising distribution manager

## Scope

The Distribution and Campaign Management Manager is responsible for setting up, monitoring and optimizing advertising campaigns. Sets up and configures advertising campaigns Monitors and optimizes campaign performance Coordinates service providers and advertisers Manages incidents and corrects anomalies

- Advise clients on optimal advertising space
- Negotiate with customers in case of disagreements
- Ensure technological monitoring in the field of multimedia
- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Interpret advertising campaign results
- Return campaign results to a client
- Correct broadcast technical anomalies
- Negotiate the conditions for purchasing advertising space
- Manage budgets allocated to advertising campaigns
- Ensure campaigns comply with advertising regulations
- Analyze customer needs to offer appropriate services
- Analyze the return on investment of advertising campaigns
- Analyze advertising market trends

- Plan and manage advertising campaigns

- Ensure monitoring of performance indicators (KPI)

- Develop digital marketing strategies
- Manage advertising inventories
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Convince, negotiate
