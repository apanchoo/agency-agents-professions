## Scope

The industrial modeler transforms a stylist's idea or a collection drawing into a concrete prototype of clothing, ready to be industrialized. Carries out the technical and qualitative development of articles by modifying or creating model patterns based on specifications, a sketch Works in close collaboration with the design, production and quality control teams Intervenes in compliance with manufacturing standards in standardization of sizes, morphological adjustments Analyzes the technical, economic and environmental constraints of production in terms of quality, productivity, deadlines, sustainability
