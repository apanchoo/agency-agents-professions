# Military Special Forces Fighter

## Scope

The military special forces fighter is a highly skilled military member trained to conduct high-complexity, high-intensity special operations. Develop sufficient physical and mental fitness to carry out your profession. Work in hostile environments and to achieve specific tactical objectives, such as reconnaissance, surveillance, sabotage, release of hostages, the fight against terrorism and unconventional warfare. Carry out “commando” type interventions, behind the enemy’s rear or in areas of insecurity
