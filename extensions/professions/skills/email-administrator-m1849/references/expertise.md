# Email Administrator

## Scope

The Email Administrator plays a crucial role in managing and securing a company's electronic communications. Manages and administers messaging systems to ensure the smooth flow of internal and external communications Ensures the security of information exchange by messaging to protect data against unauthorized access Implements data backup and recovery policies to prevent loss of information Performs regular updates of messaging software to ensure their effectiveness and security Provides technical support to users to resolve messaging-related issues Analyzes and optimizes the performance of messaging systems to improve business productivity

## Related Job Titles

- Email Administrator

## Activities and Skills

- Train users to use messaging tools effectively
- Administer an information system
- Analyze the performance of an information system
- Analyze email usage trends to predict future needs
- Ensure preventive maintenance of messaging systems to avoid breakdowns
- Manage IT security
- Manage user access rights
- Update email systems to ensure compatibility with new software
- Suggest improvements to increase system efficiency
- Write and develop instructions and technical sheets
- Diagnose and resolve technical messaging issues
- Determine the causes of malfunctions
- Carry out remote maintenance interventions
- Lead risk management
- Monitor email system security to prevent intrusions
- Communicate with internal and external contacts
