## Scope

The market gardener is a farmer specializing in the cultivation of vegetables and aromatic plants intended for food. Cultivates a variety of vegetables according to the seasons, the region, and production objectives Supervises and coordinates planting, irrigation and harvesting activities Ensures the management of crops in the open field and in greenhouses in compliance with environmental standards Optimizes yields while preserving the quality of the harvested products Packages and markets the harvested products Can participate in organic and innovative crop development projects

- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Store a product
- Marketing the products of a farm
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Train new employees in trade techniques
