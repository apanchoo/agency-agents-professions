# Camera Machinist

## Scope

A camera operator is a cinema or audiovisual technician who takes care of everything that moves and stabilizes the camera during a shoot. Installs and adjusts the shooting equipment according to the instructions of the director of photography Ensures first-level maintenance of the equipment used Participates in the installation of lighting and sets Works closely with the technical team to coordinate camera movements Ensures compliance with safety instructions on the film set May drive special vehicles for moving shots
