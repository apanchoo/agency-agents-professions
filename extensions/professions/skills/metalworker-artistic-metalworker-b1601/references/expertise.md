# Metalworker / Artistic metalworker

## Scope

The artistic metalworker creates works individually or in small series, repairs large decorative and utilitarian pieces (monumental statuary, stair rails, ornamental grilles, etc.) or small ones (statues, pieces of goldwork, knives, weapons, keys, old locks, etc.); To do this, he uses different metals (steel, copper, brass, bronze, tin, etc.), and various artisanal metalworking processes (machining, forming, molding, etc.), according to health and safety rules. Can carry out the restoration of old metal objects Can pass on their know-how and participate in awareness-raising activities for their profession Can manage a structure
