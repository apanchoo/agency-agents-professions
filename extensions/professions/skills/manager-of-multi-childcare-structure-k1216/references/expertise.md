# Manager of multi-childcare structure

## Scope

The head of a multi-childcare structure manages, coordinates and implements the educational project of a structure welcoming children under 6 years old, ensuring their well-being, compliance with safety standards and the development of staff skills. Manages and coordinates the activities of a reception structure for young children Ensures compliance with safety and hygiene standards Supervises and trains staff to guarantee a stimulating and secure environment for children Develops educational programs adapted to the different ages of children Maintains effective communication with parents to involve them in the development of their children Supervises the administrative and budgetary management of the structure

## Related Job Titles

- Child care worker responsible for nursery
- Childcare worker responsible for daycare
- Childcare worker responsible for early childhood care structure
- Childcare worker assistant nursery manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate multidisciplinary teams
- Maintain team cohesion and motivation
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Distribute the workload and tasks to be performed among team members
- Promote and share good practices
- Manage human resources
- Supporting parents in their educational and parental role
- Coordinate the educational activities of the establishment
- Train staff in child education and care methods
- Supporting the development, autonomy and awakening of the child
- Support hygiene, nutrition and development of children under 3 years old
- Create and implement conditions necessary for the development, health, safety and well-being and autonomy of young children
- Observe the child's behavior and development and identify possible difficulties
- Monitoring a child's health
- Provide and update activity monitoring tools (production dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Define risk prevention measures
- Foster a safe learning environment
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Optimize the use of material and financial resources
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Train staff in cleaning waste recycling practices
- Promote eco-responsibility in the center's activities
- Have an overview of the operation of the establishment
- Design an educational project
- Contribute to service improvement projects
- Evaluate and improve activity programs
- Train and supervise establishment staff
- Manage the resources of a child care service or establishment
- Arguing to defend a project or idea
- Inform parents about the organization of the structure and present the program of activities to the children
- Maintain effective communication with parents and educational staff
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Ensure follow-up of educational guidelines
- Ensure legal and regulatory monitoring
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
