# Preparer-corrector / Preparer-corrector in the graphics industry

## Scope

The preparer-corrector in the graphics industry ensures the conformity and quality of documents before they are printed. Checks and corrects spelling, grammar and syntax errors in texts Creates or modifies the layout of a document Respects the quality standards and procedures in force in the graphics industry Manages production deadlines to guarantee the punctuality of publications Collaborates with graphic designers, editors and printers to adjust and optimize files Uses specialized software to correct and adjust files
