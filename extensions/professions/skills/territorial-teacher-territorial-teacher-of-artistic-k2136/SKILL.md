## Scope

The Art Education Teacher is a creator and transmitter of artistic knowledge, including drawing, design, illustration and digital tools. Teaches various artistic techniques and art theories Develops students' creativity and technical skills Prepares and leads practical and theoretical workshops Evaluates students' work and progress Organizes and supervises exhibitions of student work Participates in the cultural and educational life of the establishment

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Facilitate cultural and artistic activities
- Lead artistic practice workshops
- Encourage creativity and innovation
- Maintain an environment conducive to artistic learning
- Advise learners to develop their artistic abilities
- Define a learning objective and the program of educational activities
- Evaluate student progress and adjust teaching methods accordingly
- Organize workshops, practical work and case studies to put theoretical lessons into practice
