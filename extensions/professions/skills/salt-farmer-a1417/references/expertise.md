# Salt farmer

## Scope

Carries out salt production and harvesting operations according to operating objectives, health and safety rules and environmental standards Ensures the maintenance of salt sites by regulating water levels and participates in the preservation of salt marshes and the protection of coastal ecosystems Can harvest samphire Can coordinate a team or manage a salt farm Can offer activities to discover salt marshes and their exploitation

## Related Job Titles

- Saludier / Paludière
- Salt producer
- Salicorniere
- Saltworks
- Saunier / Sauniere

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Take a sample
- Process collected meteorological information
- Carry out the manual picking of the samphire
- Master the characteristics of a product or material
- Prepare materials for work
- Perform a salinity control test
- Harvesting fleur de sel or salt
- Adjust the level of a body of water
- Immerse salt pans
- Carry out the ecological restoration of natural environments (moorland, marshes, ponds, etc.)
- Repairing natural damage
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Supervise operations, actions
- Check the salt water level
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Packaging products
- Manage inventory
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Store a product
- Organize order processing
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Carry out accounting operations
- Offer additional products
- Marketing extraction products
- Close a sale
- Develop tourism marketing strategies
- Negotiate optimal purchasing conditions
- Organize personalized tourist tours
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital communication tools
