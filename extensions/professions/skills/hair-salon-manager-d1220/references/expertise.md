# Hair salon manager

## Scope

The hair salon manager orchestrates the management and running of his establishment with passion and expertise. Directs and supervises the daily activity of the salon, ensuring quality customer service Manages appointments, employee schedules and cash register operations Recruits, trains and motivates a team of hairdressers and hair technicians Ensures compliance with hygiene and safety standards within the salon Develops marketing strategies to attract and retain customers Manages inventory of hair products and equipment, optimizing costs and resources

## Related Job Titles

- Head hairdresser
- Technician manager / Hair salon technician manager
- Hair salon manager

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Encourage creativity and innovation
- Establish sales objectives for the team
- Maintain effective communication within the team
- Provide training on new hairdressing techniques
- Teaching, developing skills
- Organize continuing training for staff
- Carry out hairdressing services
- Organize special events (open days, etc.)
- Use salon management software
- Regularly check the condition of hairdressing equipment
- Supervise the hygiene and cleanliness of the salon
- Manage inventory of hair products and accessories
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Negotiate with suppliers for product purchases
- Optimize payment and invoicing processes
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Ensure compliance with environmental standards
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Greet the customer and install them
- Identify the expected hairstyle and advise the client
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Analyze hairdressing market trends
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Evaluate and improve the services offered by the salon
- Implement strategies to increase customer satisfaction
- Sell a service or product
