# Textile processing machine operator

## Scope

The Textile Processing Machine Operator controls machines intended for textile processing operations, aimed at improving their appearance, feel or functional properties. Carries out dyeing, printing, priming or finishing operations, following chemical, mechanical or thermal processes Ensures the transformation of raw textiles into finished or semi-finished products Ensures that technical and aesthetic requirements are met

## Activities and Skills

- Train operators on new machines
- Organize the flow of products on a production line
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Load and unload the textile ream
- Monitor industrial dyeing/coloring results and make adjustments
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine
- Position and secure the rollers on the machine, center the fabric and adjust the tension when feeding
- Take note of the manufacturing order before the start of production
- Prepare colorings
- Use a tool, machine, equipment, installation
- Check the conformity of the parts in relation to the manufacturing order
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Perform first-level preventive and corrective maintenance on its equipment
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Monitor the flow and progress of products on a production line
- Monitor the operation of a machine and intervene if necessary
- Checking the winding of the fabric on a roller
- Check the conformity of materials and colors during production
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Manage stocks of raw materials and consumables
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events in production
- Meet production deadlines to meet customer requests
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
