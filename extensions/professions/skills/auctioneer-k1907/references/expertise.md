# Auctioneer

## Scope

The auctioneer, expert in evaluation and auction, has an important role in the art and antiques market. Evaluates goods and art objects to determine their market value Organizes and conducts public auctions Writes sales catalogs and promotes items for sale Advises sellers on the best sales strategies Ensures legal compliance of all transactions May specialize in a particular type of object, such as art, antiques or real estate

## Related Job Titles

- Auctioneer / Judicial auctioneer

## Activities and Skills

- Write sales catalogs detailing objects
- Conduct research on the history of certain works
- Analyze the risks associated with purchasing works of art
- Ensuring the security of works or valuable objects during exhibitions
- Evaluate the risks of deterioration of works during exhibitions
- Complete the necessary formalities for legal procedures with competent services
- Prepare and manage online auctions
- Prepare necessary documents for art exports
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Liaise with art restorers
- Managing the financial aspects of art sales
- Analyze the authenticity and provenance of works of art
- Advisor on preventive conservation of collections
- Estimate the value of a good or a product
- Ensure compliance of transactions with legislation
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Defend the interests of a natural or legal person
- Negotiate insurance for high-value works
- Draft legal documents and procedures
- Ensure the legal security of a decision or a project
- Complete a transaction
- Negotiate optimal purchasing conditions
- Promote art objects to potential buyers
- Promote special events or in-store promotions
- Receive a client affairs management mandate
- Monitor art market trends and adjust strategies
- Maintain relationships with artistic institutions
- Serve and execute court decisions and notarial documents
- Documenting art transactions and acquisitions
