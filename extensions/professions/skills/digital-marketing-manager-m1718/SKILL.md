## Scope

The Digital Marketing Manager implements online marketing strategies to increase visibility and engagement around a product, brand or company across various digital channels Designs and implements digital marketing operations to optimize online visibility and engagement Analyzes the performance of digital campaigns and proposes improvements based on the data collected Collaborates with content, design and development teams to create coherent and effective campaigns Manages digital marketing budgets and optimizes spending to maximize ROI Stays up to date with the latest digital marketing trends and emerging technologies

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.
