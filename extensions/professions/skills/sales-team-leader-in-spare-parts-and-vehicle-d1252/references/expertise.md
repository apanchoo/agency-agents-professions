# Sales team leader in spare parts and vehicle accessories

## Scope

The sales team leader for spare parts and vehicle accessories supervises a team dedicated to the marketing of spare parts and accessories. Provides technical support to its employees, contributes to the development of their skills and participates in the organization of sales areas and the store Supervises the flow of products (reception, storage, shipping), updates activity dashboards and organizes inventories Manages customer and supplier complaints according to the directives of its hierarchy Also ensures the sale and advice of parts and accessories at the different points of contact (workshop, store, boutique or remotely)
