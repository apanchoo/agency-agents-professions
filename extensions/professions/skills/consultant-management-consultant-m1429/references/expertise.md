# Consultant / Management consultant

## Scope

The management consultant analyzes a company's practices to propose strategies aimed at improving its efficiency and performance. Advises companies on strategy and organization Optimizes processes to improve operational efficiency Supports changes and transformation of structures Analyzes data to propose innovative solutions Trains teams in new methods and tools Can specialize in a specific sector or function

## Related Job Titles

- Performance management consultant
- Senior management consultant
- Engineer / Management consulting engineer
