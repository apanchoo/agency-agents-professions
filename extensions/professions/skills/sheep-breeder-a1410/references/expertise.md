# Sheep breeder

## Scope

The Sheep Breeder ensures the well-being, health and reproduction of his flock. Raises and cares for sheep for the production of wool, meat and milk Manages pastures to ensure adequate nutrition and a healthy environment for the animals Monitors animal health and administers necessary treatments Participates in the reproduction of flocks to ensure the sustainability of the livestock market Markets livestock products Maintains and improves breeding facilities to optimize the living conditions of sheep
