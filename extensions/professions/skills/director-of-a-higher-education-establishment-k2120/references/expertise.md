# Director of a higher education establishment

## Scope

The Director of the higher education establishment defines the educational and administrative strategy to follow to promote academic excellence. Defines the strategic orientations of the establishment and ensures their implementation Supervises the administrative, financial and educational management of the establishment Ensures the representation of the establishment towards external partners and official bodies Promotes the teaching programs and research projects of the establishment Supervises and leads the teaching and administrative teams Ensures the continuous improvement of the quality of teaching and services offered to students

## Related Job Titles

- Director of the Health Executive Training Institute
- Director of a nursing training institute (IFSI)
