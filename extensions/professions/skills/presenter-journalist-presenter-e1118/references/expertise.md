# Presenter / Journalist presenter

## Scope

The presenter journalist is responsible for processing and broadcasting the various facets of the news on air. Prepares and presents television or radio news Analyzes and selects relevant information for broadcast Interviews guests and leads discussions on current topics Collaborates with an editorial team to develop program segments May work in the field to cover live events Liaises with viewers or listeners via social media or other interactive platforms

## Related Job Titles

- Presenter journalist / Radio presenter journalist
- Presenter journalist / TV presenter journalist
