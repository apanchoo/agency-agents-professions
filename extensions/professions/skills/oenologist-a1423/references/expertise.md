# Oenologist

## Scope

The oenologist, an expert in the wine industry, combines his passion for science and his taste for wine to develop quality wines. Analyzes the chemical and sensory characteristics of wines Advises winegrowers at all stages of production and participates in research and development to improve manufacturing processes Ensures quality control throughout the production process Can represent a winery or brand during tastings or professional events

## Related Job Titles

- Consulting oenologist
- Oenologist in research development

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the activities of a cellar team
- Train and develop team skills
- Advising clients on food and drink pairings
- Advise winegrowers on vine cultivation techniques (choice of grape varieties, terroir, treatments to be applied, etc.)
- Conduct research to improve winemaking and vine growing techniques
- Perform chemical and microbiological analyzes of grapes, musts and wines
- Propose innovations in wine packaging
- Carry out chemical control tests during the fermentation process and issue recommendations (additions of ingredients)
- Process collected meteorological information
- Plan grape harvest operations
- Analyze the characteristics of the soil and terroir to determine the types of cultivation and varieties of vines suitable
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Carry out winemaking operations (assembly, measurement of alcohol level, filtration, etc.)
- Supervise the aging of wines in vats or barrels, controlling temperature, humidity and aeration conditions
- Monitor the growth of the vines throughout the season and identify intervention needs (pruning, treatment, etc.)
- Use and adjust agri-food machines or equipment
- Control the operation of winemaking equipment and monitor maintenance
- Ensure the traceability of production batches
- Control the health quality of products
- Check product storage conditions
- Check the organoleptic characteristics (successions of sensations, olfactory pyramid, balance, stability, etc.) of the final product
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Conditioning products before a fermentation operation
- Evaluate the cellar’s supply needs
- Carry out the placing in barrels or bottles, the capping, the labeling
- Supervise the packaging of wine in bottles
- Establish production and incident reports
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Optimize the energy consumption of systems
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Develop sustainable development plans for the winery
- Evaluate the environmental impact of winemaking practices
- Analyze wine market trends
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Organize wine tasting events
- Using English in a professional context
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Meet production deadlines to meet customer requests

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Show curiosity, open-mindedness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
