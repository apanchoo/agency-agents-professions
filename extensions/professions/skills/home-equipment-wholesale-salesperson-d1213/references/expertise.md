# Home equipment wholesale salesperson

## Scope

The home equipment wholesaler is a professional in home design and decoration. Manages inventory and product procurement to ensure item availability Negotiates with suppliers to obtain the best prices and conditions Ensures attractive presentation of products in store Processes orders, deliveries and returns of merchandise Maintains up-to-date knowledge of new products and market trends
