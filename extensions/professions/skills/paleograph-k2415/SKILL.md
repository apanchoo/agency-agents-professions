## Scope

Analyzes and interprets ancient manuscripts to decipher their contents Contributes to the conservation of historical documents by establishing preservation standards Collaborates with historians and archaeologists to contextualize discoveries of ancient texts Participates in the writing of scientific publications to share discoveries with the academic community Teaches paleography techniques at academic institutions Uses modern technologies for the analysis and digitization of ancient texts
