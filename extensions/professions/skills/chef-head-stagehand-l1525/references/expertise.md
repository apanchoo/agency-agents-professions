# Chef / Head stagehand

## Scope

The Head Stagehand is in charge of supervising the team of stagehands as well as coordinating and managing the stage space. Supervises the installation of stage equipment according to artistic directives Coordinates the technical team for scene changes during shows Ensures the maintenance and safety of equipment used on stage Participates in the design of stage devices to optimize performances Trains new technicians in procedures and techniques specific to the theater or event Ensures compliance with safety standards and regulations in force during assembly and dismantling
