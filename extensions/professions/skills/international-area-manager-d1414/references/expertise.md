# International area manager

## Scope

The international area manager orchestrates the development and management of the company's activities on an international scale. Defines the commercial and development strategy in assigned areas Coordinates activities between different departments and subsidiaries abroad Analyzes international markets to identify new growth opportunities Negotiates contracts and strategic partnerships with local players Oversees compliance of operations with international regulations Optimizes processes to improve efficiency and reduce costs

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate internal and external communication
- Analyze and process information for anticipation purposes
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Analyze the quality of a product
- Analyze the quality of a service
- Optimize international logistics flows
- Track orders, invoicing
- Prepare detailed reports for management
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Develop budgets for international projects
- Ensure traceability and security of information data
- Integrate regulatory norms and international standards
- Implement compliance procedures
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Manage local and international partnerships
- Ensure interface with health authorities and health professionals in the countries concerned
- Ensure international competitive intelligence
- Identify and adapt the products or range of products to be marketed according to the market and local legislation
- Negotiate price levels and reimbursement amounts in each country
- Organize exports: transport and distribution, establishment of local marketing structures
- Plan international marketing campaigns
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Develop and coordinate the operational deployment of the strategy of its scope (Europe/world)
- Establish strategic partnerships
- Implement the promotional strategy through the use of promotional campaigns, tools and services
- Participate in the analysis of results and profitability in relation to objectives
- Communicate, read and write technical documents, reports, notes in English
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
