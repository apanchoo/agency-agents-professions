# Auto Mechanic

## Scope

The automobile mechanic carries out all preventive and corrective maintenance interventions on thermal, electric and hybrid vehicles. Carry out periodic maintenance operations on the vehicle, including air conditioning Carry out a first level diagnosis, particularly on the mechanical systems Carry out replacement or restoration operations in accordance with the procedures Bring the injection, depollution or supercharging systems into conformity on a petrol or diesel engine of a thermal or hybrid vehicle Comply with health, safety and environmental rules

## Activities and Skills

- Update on-board vehicle software
- Use automotive diagnostic software
- Apply standards relating to the handling of refrigerants
- Install additional equipment (comfort devices, protection, tinted films, hitch, etc.) on a private or industrial motor vehicle
- Ensure the maintenance of workshop tools and equipment
- Check and replace the elements of the braking system (classic, ABS, ESP)
- Check and adjust the running gear
- Checking the state of charge and aging of a vehicle's traction battery
- Check the technical conformity of a vehicle
- Visually check the charging elements of an electric and/or hybrid vehicle
- Replace and repair tires
- Replace the transmission components (clutch, gearbox)
- Replace a starting and service battery (<60 volts DC)
- Perform maintenance on clutch systems
- Perform maintenance on steering systems
- Carry out maintenance on the petrol or diesel depollution systems of a thermal engine
- Carry out maintenance on an engine's gasoline or diesel supercharging systems
- Carry out maintenance on the petrol or diesel injection systems of a thermal engine
- Carry out maintenance on the air conditioning circuit
- Carry out the diagnosis of the running gear
- Carry out the diagnosis of the air conditioning circuit
- Carry out diagnostics of the various components (or systems) of the vehicle: engine, steering, braking, clutch, transmissions or even air conditioning
- Carry out maintenance operations on the ignition, air, fuel and engine exhaust circuits
- Carry out maintenance operations on lubrication circuits
- Carry out maintenance operations on tires, shock absorbers and suspensions
- Carry out maintenance operations on electrical, safety and comfort elements of old and historic vehicles
- Carry out maintenance operations on the braking system
- Carry out maintenance operations on the transmission system (clutch, gearbox, transmission chain)
- Monitor risk areas
- Apply standards relating to the prevention of electrical risks
- Driving a light vehicle
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Advise customers on necessary maintenance and repair options
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
