# Crane operator Bridge operator / Port bridge crane operator

## Scope

The port bridge crane operator plays a crucial role in handling and moving heavy loads in port areas. Operates lifting cranes to move containers and other heavy loads Provides first-level maintenance of lifting equipment Controls the stability of loads and secures work areas Coordinates with ground crews for precise placement of loads Meets strict safety standards to prevent accidents May participate in the training of new operators

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Communicate with ground teams to coordinate movements
- Document lifting operations for site reports
- Ensure first level maintenance of the crane
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Analyze the work environment to anticipate risks
- Check the operating status of the equipment
- Check the stability of the ground before positioning the crane
- Manage crane movements and lifting operations
- Maneuver the crane to move heavy loads
- Process load weight and dimensions information
- Plan lifting operations according to the schedule
- Collaborate with other operators to optimize working time
- Communicate instructions clearly
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Follow safety instructions during lifting operations
- Respect environmental standards when moving loads
