## Scope

The weaver is an artistic craftsman, who makes fabrics, clothing or accessories at the customer's request (these are unique pieces) or for commercial purposes. Produces clothing fabrics, furnishing textiles, decorative textiles, carpets, etc. Creates the pattern on which he will base himself to create his piece Chooses the fibers, wools and colors Weaves the different pieces of the model Makes the finishes (borders, seams, hems, etc.) and is concerned with the quality of the finished product.

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Innovate in weaving techniques to improve quality
- Manually make a template, a pattern
- Distinguish between textile raw materials and their properties
- Manufacture textiles for several purposes: furniture, decoration, carpets, etc.
- Manufacture fabrics and fabric items (clothing, accessories, etc.)
- Shaping pieces of art
- Assemble the parts and sub-assemblies of textile articles by stitching according to the technical sheets
- Make fabric accessories, embroidery
