# Electric vehicle battery maintenance technician

## Scope

The Electric Vehicle Battery Maintenance Expert Technician carries out preventive and corrective upkeep and maintenance operations specific to vehicle batteries with partial or total electric propulsion, and is responsible for the application of safety rules at the workstation and masters intervention protocols specific to batteries, on technical and organizational aspects. Carries out operations to verify the physical integrity of the battery (watertightness, degradation, etc.) and its traceability of the battery Carries out operations to establish diagnostics Carries out operations for removing and refitting the battery Carries out operations for securing, conditioning and storing the battery Carries out operations for sorting and safely storing the electronic and electrical components of a vehicle with partial or total electric propulsion
