# Family employee

## Scope

The Family Employee, an essential pillar for the daily well-being of families. Accompanies children in their daily activities and leisure activities Assists people experiencing loss of autonomy in the actions of daily life Maintains the person's living environment Can support people in their daily administrative procedures Ensures shopping management, prepares daily meals

## Related Job Titles

- Home help
- Family help
- Domestic employee
- Housekeeper
- Family employee / Family domestic worker

## Activities and Skills

- Organize recreational activities for children
- Provide emotional support to those supported and those around them
- Encourage independence in daily activities
- Carry out actions to develop or maintain social or family ties
- Accompany the child in his daily activities
- Helping people in their living environment
- Ensure a presence and a safe environment for those accompanied
- Carry out errands for one person
- Maintain household linen and clothing
- Feeding pets
- Schedule and coordinate medical appointments
- Walking pets
- Prepare meals
- Check that the person is taking medication according to the medical prescription
- Prepare balanced meals while respecting specific diets
- Change the sheets, make the bed (clean, re-lay) and replace the bathroom linen
- Dust floors, carpets, furniture, objects and ventilate, deodorize rooms
- Maintain laundry
- Maintain the living spaces and laundry of the person being accompanied
- Maintain a surface, a floor
- Inform the person of anomalies observed on devices or furniture
- Washing windows or other surfaces
- Ensure cleanliness and order in living spaces
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Apply eco-gestures in your practice
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Promote communication between different stakeholders
- Maintain effective communication with all family members
- Report on your activity
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach
