# Staffer Stuqueur / Staffer Stuqueuse

## Scope

A craftsman in detail and finishing, the plasterer shapes or restores interior or exterior decorative elements, in staff or stucco. Works for new constructions or renovations Restores staff elements in historic buildings Collaborates with architects and decorators to create tailor-made elements Installs creations on sites, ensuring their fixation and aesthetic integrity

## Related Job Titles

- Staffer
- Show decor staffer
- Ornamentalist staffer
- Stucco maker / Stucateuse

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Draw new patterns
- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Make an aesthetic or artistic appreciation
- Sculpt decorative elements
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Draw plans or sketches of a work
- Design castings and moldings of raw materials
- Mix raw materials
- Study the assembly diagram and assemble the supports
- Prepare the surfaces (degreasing, masking, sanding, etc.) depending on the type of support
- Cover a support with a decorative covering
- Create architectural ornaments
- Create curved, arched elements
- Make and smooth the joints
- Carry out a sanding, polishing, smoothing operation
- Assemble a decor
- Design an architectural or development project
- Coat the support (smoothing)
- Assemble or dismantle scaffolding
- Make coatings
- Act quickly in the event of a technical problem
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Restore elements in staff
- Check the quality of the support provided
- Manage material stock
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Explain the choice of materials and techniques
- Contract a service
- Prepare or establish commercial proposals, write price offers, develop quotes
- Collaborate with teams on renovation projects
- Communicate effectively with the team and clients
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Follow safety instructions on site
- Process project information
- Develop your technical skills
