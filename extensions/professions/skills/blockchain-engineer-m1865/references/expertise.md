# Blockchain engineer

## Scope

The Blockchain Engineer plays a role in developing and securing digital transactions and networks against cyber threats. Designs and develops blockchain architectures tailored to customer needs Implements security solutions for transactions and data exchanges Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to integrate blockchain into various projects Conducts security audits to prevent the risk of cyberattacks Stays up to date with technological developments to continually improve the proposed solutions Can lead projects and coordinate development teams specialized in blockchain
