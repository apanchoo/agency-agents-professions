# Biostatistician

## Scope

The Biostatistician contributes to the design and development of biostatistical methodologies used in pre-clinical, clinical or epidemiological studies. Analyzes numerical data relating to biology to extract useful information and interpret it, in order to help the research team make decisions Can collaborate with private or public research teams in the context of technology transfer or research and development projects

## Related Job Titles

- Biostatistician / Biostatistician data manager
- Senior biostatistician
- Biostatistician engineer
- Responsible biostatistician
- Statistician in clinical development

- Present and explain scientific advances and research work

- Choose a statistical model and define the biostatistical methodology for pre-clinical, clinical or epidemiological studies
- Design and coordinate a program, a research project
- Conduct study and research work

- Coordinate the progress of a study
- Determine experimental protocols
- Establish a study or research report

- Establish an experimental trial plan: methodology, evaluation criteria, hypothesis testing, randomization plans
- Participate in the development of the clinical trial protocol and define the appropriate methodology
- Participate in the drafting and validation of the protocol
- Carry out tests, experiments
- Carry out specific studies
- Write the statistical part of study reports
- Adapt statistical data processing tools

- Design a statistical analysis plan
