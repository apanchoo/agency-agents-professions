# Territorial development manager

## Scope

The Territorial Development Manager drives and coordinates projects aimed at revitalizing the territories. Develops and implements local development strategies by making a diagnosis Coordinates economic, social and environmental projects between the different stakeholders in the territory Analyzes the strengths and weaknesses of the territory, proposes innovative solutions in a sustainable economic development approach Ensures the monitoring and evaluation of actions carried out to enable attractiveness and quality of life Can represent the community during meetings and professional events
