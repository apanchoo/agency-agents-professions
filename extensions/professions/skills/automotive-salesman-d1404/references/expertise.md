# Automotive salesman

## Scope

The car seller, within the framework of guidelines set by the hierarchy, carries out all the activities contributing to the act of selling new and used vehicles. Welcomes customers and prospects, physically or remotely, and identifies their requests Proceeds with the sale of vehicles, mobility solutions and peripheral products Estimates used vehicles as part of the trade-in Offers financing solutions and services (insurance, extended warranty, maintenance contract) Monitors customer files

## Related Job Titles

- Commercial / Automotive sales
- Commercial advisor / Automotive sales advisor
- Automotive Sales Technician
- Salesperson of new vehicles
- Used vehicle salesperson
- Salesperson in the used vehicle sector
- Salesperson in the new vehicle sector

## Activities and Skills

- Inform and use a customer and prospect database
- Write a report, an activity report
- Advise on financing options
- Ensure compliance of business practices
- Greet the customer face to face to create a climate of trust favorable to sales
- Welcome and inform the customer in a virtual space
- Conduct an interview to identify the customer's vehicle purchase motivations
- Advise the customer on good practices for using an electric or hybrid vehicle
- Provide vehicle purchasing advice tailored to the customer's needs and expectations
- Arguing the technical characteristics of a motor vehicle
- Conclude a vehicle sale by obtaining the customer's agreement
- Build, present and negotiate a global vehicle purchase offer tailored to the customer's needs
- Develop and propose a vehicle financing offer tailored to the customer's needs
- Develop and propose a range of peripheral products and services adapted to customer needs
- Estimate the trade-in value of a used vehicle
- Implement and monitor prospecting and customer loyalty plans
- Organize and carry out a personalized delivery of the vehicle
- Prospect a customer in the field or by telephone to develop vehicle sales
- Present and justify a trade-in price for a used vehicle
- Present a vehicle adapted to the customer in a static or dynamic way to highlight the qualities of the vehicle
- Carry out commercial monitoring of developments in the automotive market
- Connect the customer with a disability with the appropriate contacts to study the vehicle’s arrangements
