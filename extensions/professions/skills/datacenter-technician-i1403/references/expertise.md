# Datacenter Technician

## Scope

The Datacenter Technician ensures the proper functioning of the IT equipment and physical infrastructure of a data center. Performs first-level service and support services and interfaces with the customer Ensures operational building management, systems monitoring and incident management to ensure continuity of service Is responsible for monitoring maintenance operations Participates in the planning and optimization of data center space and resources Collaborates with IT teams to improve the security and efficiency of systems

## Related Job Titles

- Data Technician
- Datacenter maintenance technician

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze the performance of an information system
- Configure network and telecom equipment
- Install or configure a network server
- Provide and update activity monitoring tools (production dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Carry out pre-wiring testing of a computer installation
- Receive computer pre-cabling
- Install patches
- Install active equipment mixing low and high current (antenna, telecom bay, 48V rectifier, etc.)
- Put into production and de-provision IT racks
- Set up, assemble, disassemble IT equipment
- Carry out the electrical wiring for connection to a bay (electrical)
- Carry out network cabling of computer or office equipment
- Ensure compliance of installations with current standards
- Carry out IT equipment changes such as hot disk swaps or memory sticks
- Manage incidents and technical problems
- Plan and monitor maintenance operations
- Carry out corrective and preventive maintenance interventions on IT equipment, at a level of complexity requiring training in the specificities of the equipment
- Carry out simple maintenance interventions on IT equipment (procedures not requiring complete dismantling)
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Check the operation of a safety device
- Evaluate security risks in different situations
- Manage server air conditioning
- Comply with an information security policy
- Monitor the operation of computer and telecom networks
- Check network cabling
- Check the quality and reliability of the installation
- Prepare an accident and incident report
- Open, track and close a ticket (intervention report)
- Optimize the energy consumption of systems
- Ensure the quality of relationships with customers
- Handle customer requests and complaints according to the established procedure and by proposing appropriate solutions
- Ensure customer satisfaction
- Communicate effectively with interdependent teams
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Respect quality standards
- Respect safety rules

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
