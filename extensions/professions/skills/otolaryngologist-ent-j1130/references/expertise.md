# Otolaryngologist (ENT)

## Scope

The Otolaryngologist (ENT) diagnoses and treats disorders of the ear, nose and throat Performs hearing and inner ear examinations Prescribes and advises patients on appropriate drug or surgical treatments as well as prevention ENT Follows patients in consultation for follow-up care on the face and neck area May perform minor or major surgical procedures

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Advise on suitable hearing aids
- Teaching, developing skills
- Analyze medical test results to establish a diagnosis
- Ensure follow-up of patients hospitalized for ENT interventions
- Ensure medical follow-up of a patient
- Diagnose specific ENT pathologies
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Carry out post-operative ENT follow-ups
- Inform, support a patient
- Interpret ENT examination results
- Implement a therapeutic project
- Prescribe appropriate treatments
- Carry out the necessary examinations depending on the diagnosis (radiography, biological samples, etc.)
- Carry out medical examinations
- Prepare patients before surgical procedures
- Perform audiograms to assess hearing
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Carry out a screening (visual, auditory)
- Conduct study and research work
- Update the patient's medical record
- Analyze hearing test results to adapt the fitting
- Carry out maintenance of the hearing aid according to the person's request (discomfort, whistling, hearing changes, etc.)
- Use specialized ENT equipment with precision
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage medical databases
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Organize awareness campaigns on hearing health
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of advances in ENT

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
