# Director of sports equipment

## Scope

The sports facilities director organizes the activity and manages the operation of one or more facilities (aquatic centers, stadiums, ice rinks, fitness centers, golf courses, etc.) in its human, commercial, technical and financial dimensions, with an objective of quality and economic profitability and taking into account the policy of the sports department. Manages daily operations and ensures maintenance of sports facilities Supervises and trains staff to guarantee quality service Ensures user safety and compliance with security standards Optimizes financial and material resources to improve the services offered Promotes events and activities to increase public participation
