# Sports instructor / Sports health instructor

## Scope

Designs, conducts and evaluates a sports program adapted to the state of health for practitioners who do not have moderate or severe functional limitations Contributes, depending on the case, to improving the well-being or state of health of the practitioner Is a specialist in the supervision of people with particular health problems linked in particular to aging or a disability, by contributing to the fight against loss of autonomy and a sedentary lifestyle Has knowledge of the limitations and contraindications induced in terms of physical activities in link with the state of health of practitioners, which is necessary for practitioners to practice safely

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Adapt the material or equipment according to the supervised audiences
- Adapt the route to the practitioner
- Design and lead sports activities
- Establish appropriate physical activity programs
- Explain the conditions for practicing a sporting activity
- Present the exercise to practitioners and provide technical support
- Evaluate the starting level of the practitioner
- Transmit theoretical bases, techniques and gestures by combining several teaching methods
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Monitor equipment or sports equipment
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Manage a schedule
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Evaluate personal protective equipment
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Identify the customer's expectations and inform them about the possible activity(ies) according to their level and organizational arrangements
- Build lasting professional relationships with customers and suppliers
- Develop study monitoring tools
- Organize physical and sporting activities as part of health-related policy projects
- Promote healthy sport and club projects to practitioners and outside
- Arouse the interest and motivation of the practitioner by giving meaning to the proposed sporting exercises
- Network and cooperate with diverse professional profiles

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
