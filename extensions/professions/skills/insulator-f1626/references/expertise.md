# Insulator

## Scope

The Insulator is a professional specializing in the thermal and acoustic insulation of buildings or industrial installations. Installs and fixes insulating materials to prevent heat transfer Uses heat-insulating techniques to improve the energy efficiency of systems Makes precise measurements and cuts to adapt insulating materials to the surfaces to be treated Ensures the maintenance and repair of existing insulation to ensure their effectiveness Collaborates with other trades to coordinate work on construction sites Can also carry out noise insulation

## Related Job Titles

- Thermal and acoustic insulation installer
- Insulation installer / Insulation installer on pipes and devices
- Sheet metal worker-insulator
