# Hospital medical representative

## Scope

At the intersection of health and communication, the hospital medical representative forms the link between pharmaceutical laboratories and the hospital environment. Monitors the evolution of ethical provisions and regulations in force and respects their application Ensures the traceability of information and promotion actions through the development of reports and reports Informs and trains healthcare professionals on new pharmaceutical products and medical advances Maintains an ongoing professional relationship with hospital staff to ensure good cooperation Establishes and implements a sectoral action plan Maintains multidisciplinary relationships as part of the improvement and optimization of the care pathway
