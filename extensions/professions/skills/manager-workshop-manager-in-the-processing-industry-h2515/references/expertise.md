# Manager / Workshop manager in the processing industry

## Scope

The Workshop Manager in the processing industry supervises activities and production objectives in terms of quantity and quality within a workshop. Optimizes production processes, management of raw material stock to improve efficiency and reduce costs Ensures compliance with quality standards and control procedures by implementing corrective actions Ensures the maintenance of production equipment and coordinates with the maintenance department Applies and enforces safety, hygiene and environmental rules Supervises and leads a team of operators, technicians and island managers
