# Lawyer

## Scope

The lawyer guarantees legal and regulatory interests. Advises and assists clients or the company on various legal issues Drafts and validates contracts, agreements and other legal documents Represents the company or clients during disputes or negotiations Ensures compliance with legal and regulatory standards in force Analyzes legal risks linked to the activities of the company or clients Conducts legal monitoring to anticipate legislative and regulatory developments
