# Chef/Head of fresh produce department

## Scope

The fresh produce department manager manages and highlights the products in his department to optimize sales and satisfy customers. Supervises the supply and management of stocks of fresh products Optimizes the presentation of products and ensures their rotation to guarantee their freshness Manages the department team, plans schedules and supervises the training of new employees Analyzes sales indicators to adjust orders and maximize profitability Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards Develops promotion and marketing strategies to attract and retain customers

## Related Job Titles

- Chef / Head of catering products department
- Chef / Head of frozen food department
- Fresh produce manager
- Fresh produce department manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Supervise and lead the department team (planning, distribution of tasks, motivation)
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Manage your team using performance indicators
- Schedule training sessions for employees
- Regularly check department equipment to prevent breakdowns
- Check the state of conservation of a perishable product
- Control the shelving of items
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Remove non-compliant products
- Monitor the quality of products received to ensure their freshness
- Manage the supply and orders of fresh products based on sales and seasonality
- Identify and manage unsold items
- Organize a physical inventory
- Supervise inventory management
- Contribute to human resources management
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Ensure compliance with health standards
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Ensure compliance with product presentation standards
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Innovate in product presentation to increase sales
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Negotiate with suppliers to obtain better conditions
- Adapt the department's offering to customer expectations and consumer trends
- Analyze performance indicators after each marketing action in order to improve commercial operations and develop turnover
- Organize the layout of the department to optimize attractiveness and the customer journey
- Monitor sales indicators
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
