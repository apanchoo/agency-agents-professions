# Prison guard

## Scope

The Prison Officer plays a crucial role in managing security and order within prison establishments. Monitors the behavior of inmates and intervenes when necessary Accompanies and supervises inmates during movements within or outside the establishment Participates in the social reintegration of inmates by helping them follow educational or vocational programs Writes reports on incidents or specific behaviors of inmates Controls communications between inmates and the outside world to prevent escapes or illegal activities

## Related Job Titles

- First prison guard
- Brigadier supervisor / Prison brigadier supervisor

## Activities and Skills

- Communicate effectively with inmates
- Assist inmates in times of medical or personal need
- Promote the rehabilitation and education of prisoners
- Train new guards in prison procedures and policies
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Controlling inmate cells
- Carry out remote monitoring
- Receive alerts (phone calls, alarms) and trigger appropriate rescue and assistance interventions
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Check the operation of safety equipment
- Organize and plan an activity
- Write detailed reports on incidents and daily activities
- Control or apprehend a person
- Carry out surveillance rounds
- Managing the behavior of prisoners on a daily basis
- Monitor access to restricted areas to prevent escapes
- Ensure the safety of property and people in the establishment
- Implement appropriate disciplinary measures
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Transmit and explain to people the social and civic rules during daily life activities
- Use digital tools
- Ensure the protection of sensitive data and information
- Participate in continuing training to improve your skills

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Listen, show empathy
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

## Professional techniques

- Suicide prevention in prisons
- Mediation techniques in prison
