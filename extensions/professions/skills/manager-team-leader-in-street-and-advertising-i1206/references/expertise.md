# Manager / Team leader in street and advertising furniture

## Scope

The Street and Advertising Furniture Team Leader manages the installation, upkeep and maintenance of urban equipment and advertising media installed in public spaces. Organizes workstations and distributes activities within the team Supervises and coordinates installation, cleaning and maintenance activities for equipment (bus shelters, benches, billboards, etc.) Manages the supply and stock of necessary equipment Ensures compliance with safety standards on construction sites Collaborates with municipal services and advertising companies to coordinate maintenance operations Trains and supervises a team of specialized agents
