# Locker room manager

## Scope

The locker room manager manages the security of customers' personal belongings. Manages the reception of customers and the receipt of clothing and personal items Ensures the safety and integrity of items deposited Organizes storage space to optimize storage and quick access to personal effects Ensures the cleanliness and order of the locker room Resolves possible conflicts or complaints related to loss or exchange of items
