# Trainer driving motorized and lifting equipment

## Scope

The Trainer in driving motorized and lifting equipment provides training for future drivers of machines such as cranes, forklifts, aerial work platforms, excavators, and other handling equipment. Provides theoretical and practical courses on the safe operation of motorized and lifting equipment Assesses learners' skills and adapts training programs accordingly Monitors learners' progress and provides constructive feedback Maintains up-to-date knowledge of safety regulations and technical innovations in the field Prepares learners to obtain the necessary certifications to operate motorized equipment Contributes to the continuous improvement of the teaching methods used in training

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Advise companies and drivers on good safety and machine maintenance practices
- Supervise driving sessions on different types of machinery (forklifts, cranes, nacelles, etc.)
- Teach the mechanical operation of different types of machines
- Train users in the safe operation of lifting equipment
- Keep training materials and equipment up to date
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Monitor compliance with safety standards
- Evaluate the skills acquired by trainees
