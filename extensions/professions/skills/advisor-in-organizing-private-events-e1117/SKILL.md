## Scope

This events professional designs and organizes events for individuals, from A to Z. Coordinates all aspects of the event, from venue selection to supplier management Proposes creative and personalized concepts adapted to clients' needs and budgets Ensures logistics management on the day of the event to ensure a smooth running Negotiates with providers to obtain the best rates while maintaining quality Provides post-event follow-up to ensure client satisfaction and collect feedback

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Design the modalities of the event according to the objectives and the budget, by providing expertise and creativity on the means and tools to be mobilized
- Guarantee compliance with safety and quality requirements relating to the entire event, from its preparation to its closing
- Plan the different tasks necessary to carry out the event
- Supervise the event to ensure the smooth running of the schedule
- Coordinate the stages of organizing the event and its animation, ensuring compliance with deadlines and contractual obligations
- Organize a retroplan
- Control the execution and costs of a service
- Select and supervise the service providers who will intervene at the event
