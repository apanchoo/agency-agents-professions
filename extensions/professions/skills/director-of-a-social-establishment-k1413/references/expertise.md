# Director of a social establishment

## Scope

The Director of a social establishment ensures the management and improvement of social services intended for varied populations. Directs and coordinates the activities of the establishment in accordance with the social policies and regulations in force. Develops strategies to improve the quality of services offered. Supervises staff and manages the human resources of the establishment. Ensures financial management and budgetary control of the structure. Establishes partnerships with local and national organizations to optimize resources. Ensures respect for the rights and well-being of those welcomed.

## Related Job Titles

- Director of social accommodation
- Director of specialized reception center
- Director of accommodation and social rehabilitation structure
- Director of a Company or Work Support Service (ESAT)
- Director of youth protection establishment
