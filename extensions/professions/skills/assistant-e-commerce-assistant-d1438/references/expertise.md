# Assistant / E-commerce assistant

## Scope

The E-commerce Assistant strategically contributes to the activity of online platforms by ensuring the management and optimization of online sales. Manages the online product catalog, ensuring product sheets and stocks are updated Optimizes product visibility on the e-commerce platform and on search engines by increasing site traffic Analyzes sales performance and suggests improvements to increase turnover Collaborates with marketing teams to develop promotional strategies adapted to the web Provides customer service for online orders, returns and complaints, as well as the user experience and facilitates Internet user navigation Participates in monitoring competitive to stay informed of the latest e-commerce trends
