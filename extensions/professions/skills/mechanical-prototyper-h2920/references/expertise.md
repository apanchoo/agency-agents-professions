# Mechanical prototyper

## Scope

The Mechanical Prototypist designs and produces prototypes of mechanical parts to test their operation. Works closely with engineers to adjust designs based on test feedback Operates on machine tools such as milling machines or 3D printers to create components Uses CAD software to model parts before manufacturing May participate in improving manufacturing processes by providing technical expertise
