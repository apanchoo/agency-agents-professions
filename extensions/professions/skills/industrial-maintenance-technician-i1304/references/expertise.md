# Industrial maintenance technician

## Scope

The Industrial Maintenance Technician carries out preventive and corrective maintenance, and contributes to the performance and safety of industrial equipment. Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of industrial machines and equipment Detects and diagnoses breakdowns and carries out necessary repairs Optimizes maintenance processes to improve equipment reliability Intervenes urgently on equipment to minimize production stoppages Writes maintenance reports and updates technical documentation Trains and assists operators for better use of machines

## Activities and Skills

- Support the appropriation of a tool by its users
- Train users in basic maintenance procedures
- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Troubleshoot advanced technical issues
- Analyze maintenance data
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Identify technical solutions for improving equipment and installations
- Make adjustments to industrial equipment
- Check the conformity of automated installations according to safety standards
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Carry out preventive, systematic or conditional maintenance operations
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Analyze electronic signals to ensure system reliability
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Use testing devices (pressure, flow, vibration, etc.) and electrical measuring devices
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Ensure the application and respect of hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental rules (waste management, noise pollution, etc.)
- Manage stocks of maintenance equipment and spare parts
- Produce inspection and diagnostic reports for anomalies or risks and transmit them to the production, maintenance and security departments.
- Collaborate with teams to resolve technical issues
- Promote sustainable development practices in production
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- React effectively in the event of a technical incident
