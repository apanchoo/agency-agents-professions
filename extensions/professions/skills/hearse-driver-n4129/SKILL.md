## Scope

The Hearse Driver plays an essential role in funeral ceremonies, ensuring solemn and respectful transportation. Drives the hearse during funeral ceremonies, respecting the planned routes and schedules Ensures the vehicle is in good condition and is prepared before each ceremony Assists families during this period of mourning by offering a respectful and professional service Coordinates with other funeral professionals to ensure the smooth running of the ceremonies Respects road regulations and protocols specific to funeral services May be asked to help with the handling of coffins and the organization of ceremonial spaces

## Skills to Apply

- Use equipment specific to funeral transport

- Managing stress and emotions during sensitive ceremonies
- Ensure discretion and respect towards bereaved families
- Respect the highway code
