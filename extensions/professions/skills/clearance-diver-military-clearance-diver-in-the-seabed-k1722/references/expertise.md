# Clearance diver / Military clearance diver in the seabed

## Scope

The military clearance diver is a highly trained personnel who operates underwater to search, identify and neutralize explosive devices detected at sea and in ports. Participates in public service missions requiring underwater intervention for the security of ports, waterways and strategic maritime areas Implements diving techniques Intervenes with explosives and munitions in an underwater environment Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out his profession

## Related Job Titles

- High-level expert in human diving and underwater intervention
- Expert in human diving and underwater intervention
- Technician in human diving and underwater intervention
- Clearance diver technician responsible for underwater mine clearance
- Clearance diver technician responsible for underwater engineering

- Plan and coordinate mine clearance operations
- Prepare and plan mine clearance operations and exercises

- Maintain a technological watch on maritime innovations

- Implement air diving techniques up to 35m

- Carry out underwater work (welding, cutting, clearing of port accesses, etc.)

- Maintain and repair diving and mine clearance equipment

- Anticipate a danger or constraint
- Implement strict security measures
