# Clothing retoucher

## Scope

The clothing retoucher, a detail artisan who enhances clothes. Makes adjustments and alterations to garments according to customer needs Repairs defects and damage to garments to extend their life Customizes garments based on specific requests Advises customers on possible modifications and necessary maintenance Manages inventory of sewing materials and supplies Maintains a clean and organized work space to ensure quality of service

## Related Job Titles

- Assayer-retoucher
- Clothing repairer
- Retoucher / Retoucher in confection
- Leather retoucher
- Sales retoucher

## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Create a technical drawing, plan, or map
- Assemble and fix the different elements (lining, collar, sleeve, etc.) by sewing
- Choose sewing techniques adapted to the materials
- Make a textile item
- Make adjustments on sewing machines
- Insert the tightening elements (elastic, etc.), reinforcement and protection (elbow pad, etc.) on the item
- Mark alterations and repairs during fitting
- Make accessories (bags, hats, etc.) from a pattern or model
- Make themed costumes from a pattern or model
- Carry out filling operations (hemming, topstitching, etc.) on textile items
- Make formal wear from a pattern or model
- Select the raw materials to mobilize
- Detach a product
- Identify alterations and repairs to be made on the garment and inform the customer about the conditions
- Ironing textiles (clothes, linen)
- Touch up or make changes to fabric
- Alter or make changes to a garment
- Carry out a recoloring operation
- Control the quality and conformity of a deliverable
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Schedule alterations based on client priorities
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Carry out accounting operations
- Master cleaning or repair techniques to delay the obsolescence of a product or object
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect delivery times
- Use digital tools
