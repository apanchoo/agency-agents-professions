# Barber

## Scope

A barber is a professional who takes care of men's beards and hair. Performs haircuts, beard trims and shaves according to the latest trends or customer preferences Advises customers on hair and beard care to maintain their style between visits Creates a welcoming and relaxed atmosphere to provide an enjoyable experience Maintains a clean and hygienic work environment in accordance with health standards May sell hair and beard care products tailored to customer needs

## Related Job Titles

- Barber hairdresser

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure the training of new employees
- Teaching, developing skills
- Carry out shaping, styling, fixing the hair (curling, blow-drying, etc.)
- Prepare and apply coloring and bleaching products (highlights, etc.)
- Carry out hairdressing services
- Carry out hair diagnosis, washing preparations and shampoo
- Make a cut or hairstyle
- Promote the establishment on social networks
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Manage inventory of hair products and accessories
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Ensure compliance with health standards
- Greet the customer and install them
- Advise on suitable hair care products
- Identify the expected hairstyle and advise the client
- Show the client their hairstyle from different angles to get their opinion
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Adapt cutting techniques to current trends
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Hair product inventory management
- Characteristics of hair products and accessories
- Use of natural and organic products

## Areas of expertise

- Men's hairstyle
- Knowledge of current trends
- Hair diagnosis
- Customer relationship management (CRM)
- Welcoming and advising customers
- Continuing education in hairdressing and barbering
- Marketing and promotion of the hair salon
- Using salon management software

## Standards and processes

- Health and safety standards
- Development of customer loyalty strategies

## Professional techniques

- Handling cutting tools (cutting scissors, notchers, etc.)
- Knowledge of beard styles
- Relaxation and well-being techniques
- Haircut techniques
- Bleaching and hair coloring techniques (contrast, tone on tone, oxidation, etc.)
- Drying techniques
- Scalp massage techniques
- Shaving techniques and trimming mustaches and beards

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Self-employed
- Fine motor activities (dexterity, etc.)
- In contact with the public
- Wearing professional attire or uniform
- Daytime work
- Work on Saturday
