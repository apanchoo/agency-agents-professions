# Winder-assembler / Electrical winder-assembler

## Scope

The Electrical Assembler-Winder, an essential professional in the manufacturing and repair of electrical components. Produces electrical windings for motors, transformers and other devices Ensures the maintenance and repair of existing windings Controls the quality and conformity of the windings produced Uses specific tools and machines for electrical winding Complies with safety standards and technical procedures in force Can advise on technical improvements and innovations in the field of winding

## Related Job Titles

- Winding assembler of large electrical equipment
- Winder fitter

## Activities and Skills

- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Optimize winding processes
- Binding coil heads to hold cables (hooping)
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Prepare and carry out the winding using a mandrel or template, ensuring that it runs smoothly
- Carry out varnishing and steaming of the coil against corrosion
- Select the tools and materials according to the characteristics of the coil
- Organize your space and workstation
- Assemble the elements of the electrical or electronic product (components, wiring)
- Connect coils (to each other, on terminal board) with wires or bars
- Reassemble an entire electric motor or device and make adjustments
- Manufacture or repair an electric coil for integration into a static machine (transformer, etc.)
- Identify the characteristics of equipment
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Carry out corrective actions or winding/unwinding repairs
- Check the conformity of the coil (inductance, electrical continuity, static and dynamic balance, etc.)
- Detect an abnormal situation and inform
- Be vigilant
- Observe safety instructions when using machines
- Manage inventory of materials for winding
- Arguing technical choices during meetings
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Communicate technical issues effectively
- Develop team spirit
- Foster a collaborative work environment
