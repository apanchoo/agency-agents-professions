# Cheese maker-ripener

## Scope

The cheesemaker, an artisan of taste, values cheese-making heritage and artisanal production methods. Manufactures and matures cheeses in compliance with hygiene and food safety rules Packages cheeses and manages stocks according to storage conditions Advises customers on the choice of cheeses and pairings with other products Participates in the creation of new cheeses and the improvement of existing recipes Can sell complementary dairy products and organize tastings Can manage their own dairy farming
