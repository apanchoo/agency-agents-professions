# Republican Guard

## Scope

The Republican Guard is a gendarme who has chosen to join this prestigious unit that the Republican Guard represents; he protects institutions and high-ranking personalities with excellence and rigor within an elite unit. Ensures security in national palaces (example: Élysée Palace, Senate, National Assembly, Palace of Justice, etc.) Ensures the security of the Presidency of the Republic, its ministers and the highest authorities, whether they are French public figures or foreign personalities visiting the Head of State. Participates in official events, including military honorary missions.

## Activities and Skills

- Carry out physical preparation exercises
- For Republican Cavalier Guards, take care and manage your animal
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Have a sense of honor
- Have attention to detail and a taste for excellence (essential for honor services)
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Carry out public order missions (reinforcement in certain risky operations, escorts of detainees, surveillance patrols, road safety missions, supervision of public events, etc.)
- Ensure missions to maintain security at national palaces
- Carry out long-term missions (for example in Guyana, fight against gold panning)
- Direct or carry out military or public security operations
- Be vigilant in all circumstances, use firearms if necessary, and adopt strict security measures
- Participate in and secure official and protocol visits
- Protecting national palaces and the people who work there
- Securing French embassies abroad
- Welcoming foreign Heads of State and senior state officials
- Carry out public relations missions (civil or private events, in France or abroad)
- Ensure missions of military honors in national palaces, such as special ceremonies within the Elysée, the Senate or Matignon for example
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Demonstrate endurance and composure
- Be patient and keep your cool
- Respect strict military protocols
- Processing sensitive information
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
- Follow the sniper training provided by the GIGN
- Use your weaponry and perform precision shooting
