# Notary clerk

## Scope

The notary clerk is a legal collaborator responsible for assisting the notary in the preparation, drafting, instruction and finalization of deeds. Creates and examines files until they are signed and closed Draws up simple or complex legal documents Carries out preliminary and subsequent administrative formalities Carries out the necessary legal research Supports clients in their procedures and ensures follow-up Organizes appointments and prepares signatures Ensures the conformity of documents and compliance with applicable rules
