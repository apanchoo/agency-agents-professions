# Geophysicist

## Scope

The Geophysicist, an expert in Earth sciences, studies the internal and external physical characteristics of the earth and other planets and plays a crucial role in understanding geological phenomena. Conducts physical observations and measurements Analyzes and interprets geophysical data to map underground structures Designs and implements seismic studies to explore natural resources Collaborates with engineers and scientists to assess seismic and geological risks Uses specialized software to model geophysical phenomena Participates in research projects to develop new exploration methods Ensures compliance with environmental regulations when conducting geophysical studies

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate teams on geophysical research projects
- Contribute to scientific publications
- Ensure national and international regulatory and scientific monitoring
- Collect, test, analyze samples, materials
- Design theoretical, predictive geological models of soils and subsoils (calculation, simulation, modeling)
- Define and coordinate the implementation of research methods and processes, prospecting, soil and subsoil studies
- Develop new methods to study the properties and mechanisms of the subsoil
- Manage research and development projects in geophysics
- Carry out studies of foundations, settlement or stability of land (sliding, erosion, resistance)
- Use geophysical instruments to collect data in the field (seismograph, gravimeters, magnetometer, etc.)
- Analyze a situation and produce a diagnosis
- Create technical documentation
- Interpret geological surveys of soils, subsoils, rocks and study the characteristics (extents, compositions, geometry, etc.)
- Carry out surveys, drilling, soil and subsoil pollution
- Define risk prevention measures
- Evaluate seismic risks for construction projects
- Organize and plan an activity
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Develop strategies to minimize the environmental impact of projects
- Carry out an environmental impact study
- Develop partnerships with local businesses or institutions
- Communicate in English orally and in writing
- Participate in scientific conferences
- Present research results within the scientific community or to the public or clients
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Structuring, synthesizing information
- Use digital tools
