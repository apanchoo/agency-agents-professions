# Landscape team leader

## Scope

The landscaping team leader coordinates teams on maintenance and green space development sites to ensure the success of landscape projects. Organizes workstations and distributes activities on development or maintenance sites Selects the plants and materials suitable for each project Ensures the availability of materials and supplies for sites Raises awareness among clients or teams about the implementation of ecological solutions to maintain gardens and green spaces Can design landscaping according to client needs and site characteristics

## Related Job Titles

- Green space maintenance team leader
- Landscape Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the activity of a team
- Carry out administrative monitoring (clocking, etc.) and define schedules
- Plan the allocation of equipment and teams
- Distribute workstations
- Ensure the integration of new recruits (presentation to teams, safety training, etc.)
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Complete control and intervention documents
- Plan crop rotations to maintain soil fertility
- Choose plants adapted to the soil and local climate
- Install a water recovery and irrigation system
- Participate in the preparation of plants in the greenhouse (sowing, cuttings)
- Plant a tree, a plant, a vegetable
- Prune the trees
- Develop and maintain a green space
- Design gardens that guarantee biodiversity
- Determine the location of flowerbeds, design exterior layouts and green spaces
- Provide technical support to site teams
- Collaborate with teams for development projects
- Coordinate landscaping work, greening roofs and terraces
- Install street furniture
- Install a lighting system
- Plan site operations
- Carry out landscape masonry works (slabs, paving, low walls, stairs, etc.)
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Organize appropriate measures in the event of a breakdown, incident, fall or weather change
- Monitor compliance with safety standards
- Manage the stock of materials necessary for the team's activity and place orders with suppliers
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Analyze the health of trees and plants
- Analyze the quality of soil and plants
- Managing plant waste in an ecological way
- Managing natural resources sustainably
- Promote the use of local plant varieties
- Analyze client needs for landscape design
- Communicate effectively with clients on projects
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Respect safety standards
- Use digital tools
