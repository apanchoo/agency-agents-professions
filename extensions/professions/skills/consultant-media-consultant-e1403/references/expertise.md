# Consultant / Media consultant

## Scope

The Media Consultant is a strategic expert in optimizing advertising and media campaigns. Analyzes client needs and proposes tailored media strategies Evaluates campaign effectiveness and adjusts strategies accordingly Collaborates with creative and technical teams to design integrated campaigns Advises clients on best practices in content and social media engagement Stays up to date with market trends and technological innovations in media May train and supervise other consultants or team members on media best practices
