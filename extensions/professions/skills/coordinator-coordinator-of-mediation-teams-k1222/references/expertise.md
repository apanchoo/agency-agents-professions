# Coordinator / Coordinator of mediation teams

## Scope

The Mediation Team Coordinator supervises, leads and organizes the work of the teams of mediators in the field for an active local presence and to ensure a strengthening of social and cultural cohesion. Supervises, coordinates and organizes the intervention methods of mediators Manages the schedules and distribution of interventions according to needs Assesses mediation needs and proposes adapted solutions (workshops, events, etc.) Ensures social monitoring, in conjunction with local partners
