# Pulmonologist

## Scope

The pulmonologist is a specialist in the respiratory tract, he diagnoses and offers treatments for pulmonary diseases. Diagnoses and treats diseases of the lungs and respiratory tract Performs tests such as spirometry to assess respiratory function Prescribes and adjusts treatments for conditions such as asthma, COPD or pneumonia Interprets chest x-rays and other imaging studies Collaborates with other specialists on complex cases requiring a multidisciplinary approach Follows patients long-term to manage chronic respiratory diseases
