# Public lighting installer

## Scope

The Public Lighting Installer plays an essential role in the illumination and safety of public spaces. Installs and maintains public lighting systems to guarantee the safety and comfort of users Carry out fault diagnoses and carry out necessary repairs Controls and ensures compliance of installations with current safety standards Participates in the implementation of new intelligent urban lighting projects in order to contribute to the preservation of the environment

## Related Job Titles

- Public lighting electrician
- Public lighting operator

- Promote the use of new lighting technologies contributing to the preservation of the environment
- Adapt to technological developments in the sector
- Dismantle electrical cables
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Process technical information to improve operations
- Hanging road equipment at height
- Coordinate interventions on public roads with municipal services
- Disable and log an electrical system
- Install ducts and wiring
- Install festive lighting systems or to highlight heritage (churches, cultural or historical buildings)
- Install lighting equipment
- Install an electrical pole, pylon or mast
- Install a lighting system
- Install an electrical system

- Prepare the intervention and install the site
- Carry out a cable pulling operation
- Act quickly in the event of a breakdown to minimize interruptions
- Ensure preventive maintenance of lighting systems
- Check the conformity of public lighting installations
- Maintain electrical installations and equipment
- Find the origin of breakdowns and identify repair solutions
- Check the quality of electrical installations
- Plan emergency response
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Manage the stock of public lighting equipment
