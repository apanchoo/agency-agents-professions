# Music teacher

## Scope

The Music Teacher, an artist and teacher who awakens and develops musical talents, he gives his lessons in schools, conservatories, music schools, or in private lessons. Teaches various musical instruments and theories according to students' level and needs Conducts concert bands and prepares musicians for concerts Integrates technology, such as Computer-Assisted Music (CAM), into music instruction Organizes and participates in recitals and performances to promote learning Adapts teaching methods based on students' progress and interests Contributes to the cultural education of students by exposing them to various musical genres

## Activities and Skills

- Promote the importance of regular practice
- Preparing students for musical competitions
- Support personal expression in a collective setting
- Accompany and support people in their actions and their development
- Teach students to play a musical instrument or sing, guiding them in the development of their technique and musical interpretation
- Design teaching programs adapted to the levels and needs of students
- Advise learners to develop their artistic abilities
- Define a learning objective and the program of educational activities
- Encourage students to participate in competitions and shows to expose them to performance experiences
- Teach fundamental music concepts such as music theory, sheet music reading, rhythm, and harmony
- Teaching, developing skills
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Evaluate student progress and adjust teaching methods accordingly
- Explain styles, genres, and history of music to provide a comprehensive understanding to learners
- Making it easier to learn to read sheet music
- Organize knowledge and skills assessments, exams
- Organize the educational space
- Complete school assessment materials
- Supervise rehearsals and performances to improve student competence and confidence
- Monitor the acquisition of basic techniques
- Organize auditions and concerts
- Prepare and organize concerts, auditions, and end-of-year exams
- Adapting musical works
- Decipher an artistic work: script, musical score, choreography
- Encourage musical creativity in students
- Put on a show
- Orchestrate a musical work
- Organize instrument discovery workshops
- Using music software for teaching
- Perform adjustments or first level maintenance of musical instruments
- Respect safety standards during events
- Schedule a singing tour/concert
- Establish contacts between students and professionals during artistic events
- Promote listening and harmony within a musical group
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Stimulate interest in various musical genres
- Acting in the face of unforeseen events during performances
- Promote discipline and attendance during classes
- Use digital tools
