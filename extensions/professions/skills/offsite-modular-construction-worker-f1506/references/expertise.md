# Offsite Modular Construction Worker

## Scope

The off-site modular construction worker specializes in building modules or recycling and transforming containers into habitats, for example. Assembles prefabricated elements in the workshop according to plans and technical specifications Uses specific tools and machines for cutting and assembling materials Controls the quality of the assembled elements and makes the necessary adjustments Collaborates with other professionals to integrate the different systems (electrical, plumbing, etc.) May participate in transporting the modules to the site and finalizing the construction

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate work with other trades
- Analyze construction plans to prepare for welding operations
- Identify the materials and the various appropriate welds according to the instructions, technical documents, plans, etc.
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Apply anti-corrosion treatments to metal parts
- Assemble structural finishing elements
- Assemble elements of metal structures
- Assemble the heavy supporting structures of a work
- Break up a container using cutting tools to transform its structure
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Report cutting and assembly marks on construction parts
- Carry out assembly and assembly work
- Cut, cut materials
- Use and adjust a tool, machine, construction equipment
- Assemble wooden structures
- Assemble metal elements
- Fixing joinery elements
- Install thermal or acoustic insulation systems
- Connect, manage a technical network
- Carry out fixing operations
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Control the quality of welds and assemblies
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Check the assembly and installation of elements and sub-assemblies by checking compliance with the technical file
- Check the finishes
- Load, unload, handle products
- Promote the reuse or recycling of recovered materials
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Report on your activity
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
