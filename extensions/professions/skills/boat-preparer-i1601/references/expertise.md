# Boat preparer

## Scope

The boat preparer is responsible for preparing and maintaining boats before their use, including checking the equipment. Prepares and maintains boats before launching or delivery to customers Performs mechanical, electrical and hull repairs to ensure the proper functioning of the boats Installs and checks navigation and safety equipment Carry out the rigging and fittings of sailboats to optimize their performance Controls the compliance of boats with safety and environmental standards Advises customers on the maintenance and optimal use of their boat
