# Import-export transit coordinator

## Scope

The Import/Export Transit Coordinator effectively orchestrates international freight transport operations. Organizes and plans transport routes based on customer requirements and international regulations Negotiates and contracts with carriers and logistics service providers Ensures shipment tracking and compliance of export and import documents Optimizes costs and transit times to ensure customer satisfaction Resolves problems and disputes that may arise during the different stages of transit Maintains fluid and regular communication with all actors involved in the supply chain

## Activities and Skills

- Update a file, a database
- Implement technological solutions for cargo tracking
- Optimize processes to reduce environmental risks
- Strengthen security measures for the transport of sensitive goods
- Continuously improve logistics processes
- Control the quality of service provided by the team
- Design a goods delivery plan
- Organize the national and international transport chain
- Monitor the delivery of goods, inform the customer about the progress of the service and present solutions in the event of anomalies
- Prepare, monitor or control invoicing for transit or customs operations: supplier payment, customs taxes, Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB)
- Prepare and control documents related to the international movement of goods (customs invoice, Single Administrative Declaration -DAU-, letter of credit, etc.)
- Manage necessary documents for international transit
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Negotiate contracts with logistics service providers
- Handle litigation files
- Collaborate with partners to improve sustainability
- Evaluate the environmental impact of logistics activities
- Analyze customer demand and identify the technology(s) to use as well as the different process steps
- Analyze market trends to anticipate needs
- Codify the goods based on the description provided by the customer
- Negotiate transport arrangements
- Innovate in transportation methods for greater efficiency
- Maintain clear and professional communication
- Manage transport and order tracking documents
- Use digital tools
