# Head of distribution sector

## Scope

A distribution sector manager is responsible for developing sales and visibility of a company's products in a given geographic area, mainly in large-scale distribution. Supervises and coordinates the activities of the various store departments Develops strategies to achieve sales and operational efficiency objectives Optimizes the implementation of products on the shelves (merchandising, planograms) Negotiates with department managers or store directors Ensures training and development of the teams under his responsibility

- Manager / Group leader in mass distribution
- Bazaar sector manager
- Head of commercial sector

- Head of non-food sector

- Hypermarket or supermarket sector manager
- Director of hypermarket divisions
- Hypermarket branch manager
- Cultural space manager in mass distribution

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the activities of teams at different points of sale
- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Regularly evaluate employee performance and provide constructive feedback
- Facilitate communication between different departments
- Provide an advisory role in operational marketing to your contacts (shelf placement, product quality) in order to optimize sales
- Train and inform store teams about products and new products
- Guarantee the referencing, availability and renewal of products, in compliance with commercial agreements
- Plan and supervise regular inventories to maintain stock accuracy
- Supervise inventory management
- Manage department or service budgets
- Check the correct application of commercial agreements established by headquarters in the field
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Negotiate with suppliers to optimize costs and product quality
- Optimize product layout to maximize visual appeal and sales
- Organize marketing actions (promotions, special operations, events, etc.) in the various points of sale in its network
- Promote a corporate culture focused on customer service and performance
- Develop strategic partnerships to expand the market
- Develop strategies to achieve sales objectives
- Develop strategies for managing peak activity
- Develop turnover and volumes in your geographic sector
- Establish clear and measurable sales objectives for the team
- Manage the daily operations of a distribution sector
- Optimize merchandising according to planograms and national recommendations
- Organize and plan visiting tours in your sector
- Represent the brand's image and values in the field
- Monitor the achievement of the objectives of the points of sale in your network
- Build a lasting relationship of trust with store teams
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Carry out competitive and sectoral monitoring
