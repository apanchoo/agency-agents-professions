# Assistant / Family life assistant

## Scope

The family life assistant supports people in need of help at home in their daily lives, offering support and companionship. Contributes to the well-being of any person (family, child, elderly person, adult or child with a disability, illness or convalescence) in carrying out daily life activities Promotes the autonomy of the people supported in respecting their lifestyle habits at home Maintains a secure and comfortable environment for the people supported and their families or those around them

## Related Job Titles

- Home Usability Assistant
- Employee working at home with adults

## Activities and Skills

- Organize entertainment activities according to the participants and the context
- Support the appropriation of a current digital or digital tool by its users
- Provide emotional support to those supported and those around them
- Encourage independence in daily activities
- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Maintain and promote the person's social connection
- Accompanying a person while traveling
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Help someone get dressed
- Ensure a presence and a safe environment for those accompanied
- Carry out errands for one person
- Encourage the person to maintain appropriate physical activity
- Prepare, help with meals and encourage handling of cutlery
- Ensure personal hygiene
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Maintain the living spaces and laundry of the person being accompanied
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Prevent risks and the safety of the person accompanied
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Communicate with those close to the person being supported
- Give relevant information depending on the context (orally, in writing via documentation, signage, etc.)
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Adapt your behavior and your professional practice to situations
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
