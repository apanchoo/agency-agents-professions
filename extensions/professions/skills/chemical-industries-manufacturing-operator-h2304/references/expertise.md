# Chemical industries manufacturing operator

## Scope

The Chemical Industries Manufacturing Operator is involved in the transformation and production of chemical substances. Monitors and regulates a chemical or pharmaceutical processing facility from a remote control room Controls the quality of products during production and adjusts parameters if necessary according to the manufacturing processes Takes samples of materials and products for analysis and control Ensures first level maintenance of equipment and monitors operating parameters Complies with strict safety and environmental standards Writes detailed production reports
