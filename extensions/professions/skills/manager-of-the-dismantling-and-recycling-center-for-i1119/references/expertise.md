# Manager of the dismantling and recycling center for end-of-life vehicles - ELVs

## Scope

The ELV dismantling and recycling center manager (end of life vehicles) is responsible for all management and organizational activities for the ELV dismantling and recycling activity. Manages the activity and ensures overall monitoring of the structure's commercial performance Identifies material recovery outlets and optimizes the management and sale of reused parts Handles all customer or supplier complaints and disputes Manages the organization of employees' work and carries out managerial activities Ensures compliance with regulations relating to the ELV dismantling and recycling activity

## Related Job Titles

- Automotive recycling center manager
- ELV center manager
