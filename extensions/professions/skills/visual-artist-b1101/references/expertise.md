# Visual Artist

## Scope

The visual artist designs, creates and produces works of visual art using various techniques and materials. Develops a personal artistic approach and expresses an aesthetic, conceptual or symbolic vision intended to be exhibited, disseminated or sold Conceives and develops artistic projects by defining an intention, a theme and a creative approach Creates plastic works by experimenting with different materials, supports and techniques Exhibits and disseminates his creations to the public, galleries, cultural institutions or during artistic events Manages the professional aspects of his activity (artistic files, communication, search for financing, sales) Participates in cultural life by collaborating with other artists, cultural structures or by carrying out artistic mediation actions
