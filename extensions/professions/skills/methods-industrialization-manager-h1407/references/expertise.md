# Methods-industrialization manager

## Scope

The Methods-Industrialization Manager defines the means and implements best practices to ensure the industrialization of new products. Supervises the continuous improvement of manufacturing processes, methods and tools by optimizing production flows Collaborates with production, quality and maintenance teams by carrying out manufacturing tests for operational efficiency Trains teams of technicians in handling production lines Ensures technological and regulatory monitoring
