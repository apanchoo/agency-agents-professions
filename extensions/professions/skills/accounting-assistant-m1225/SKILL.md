## Scope

The Accounting Assistant participates in the daily financial management of companies. Ensures the processing of invoices and the preparation of payments Controls and records the company's revenues and expenses Participates in the preparation of balance sheets and income statements Assists the accounting team in the management of tax and social files Can manage the relationship with suppliers for financial aspects Contributes to the optimization of accounting processes to improve efficiency
