# Accounting assistant

## Scope

The Accounting Assistant participates in the daily financial management of companies. Ensures the processing of invoices and the preparation of payments Controls and records the company's revenues and expenses Participates in the preparation of balance sheets and income statements Assists the accounting team in the management of tax and social files Can manage the relationship with suppliers for financial aspects Contributes to the optimization of accounting processes to improve efficiency

## Related Job Titles

- Accounting assistant biller

## Activities and Skills

- Develop a recipe book
- Use accounting software
- Check stock status
- Ensure tax and regulatory obligations
- Codify an invoice
- Collect the information necessary for establishing payroll (hours worked, absences, leave, bonuses, etc.)
- Track orders, invoicing
- Manage expense reports: collect and check paper and/or electronic supporting documents, record or validate entries
- Manage electronic payments
- Participate in the preparation of accounting statements
- Prepare and declare VAT
- Process invoices and payments
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Prepare tax and social security declarations
- Negotiate payment terms with suppliers
- Monitor and adjust operational expenses
- Analyze accounting documents for verification
- Ensure follow-up of customer and supplier accounts
- Record business revenue
- Establish cash flow reporting
- Prepare a bank reconciliation statement
- Manage accounting and financial documents
- Manage invoices and collections in conjunction with accounting, legal and litigation departments
- Maintain tax records up to date
- Carry out accounting operations
- Process invoices and bank reconciliations
- Manage financial disputes
