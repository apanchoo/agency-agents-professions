# Washing station manager

## Scope

The Washing Station Manager is responsible for the management and proper functioning of a washing station. Supervises and coordinates vehicle cleaning activities with a view to profitability Ensures the maintenance and proper functioning of station equipment Manages stocks of cleaning and maintenance products Trains and supervises car wash staff Optimizes processes to improve quality of service and customer satisfaction Ensures compliance with safety, hygiene and environmental standards
