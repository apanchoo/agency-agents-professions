# Language reader in higher education

## Scope

The Language Reader in Higher Education shares his linguistic knowledge with students. Teaches a foreign language to higher education students Prepares and adapts educational materials adapted to the level of the students Evaluates the linguistic skills of the students through examinations and continuous assessments Participates in the development of study programs in collaboration with other teachers and professors Organizes and leads activities which reinforce the practice of the language among the students Contributes to the promotion of cultural and linguistic diversity within the establishment

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead discussion sessions in a foreign language
- Help classroom teachers in the preparation and management of language courses
- Assist foreign language teachers in teaching their mother tongue
- Co-facilitate courses, seminars and conversation workshops to encourage oral practice and active listening among students
- Design and prepare courses, tutorials or practical work
- Correct assessments (exercises, practical work, exams)
- Correct homework and provide advice for improvement
- Develop an educational approach for teaching fundamental knowledge
- Establish intercultural relationships
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Evaluate students' oral and written comprehension
- Explain linguistic and cultural concepts in a clear and accessible way
- Facilitate discussions and debates in a foreign language to encourage practical and contextual use of language skills
- Introduce students to the culture, practices and traditions of the region or country whose language they are learning
- Organize the educational space
- Provide academic support (completion/correction of exercises, homework, etc.)
- Using interactive and immersive methods to improve students' language skills
- Using multimedia supports to enrich teaching
- Organize cultural events
- Stimulate student engagement through interactive initiatives
- Stimulate interest in language through creative projects
- Prepare educational projects and activities with teachers and supervise them
- Encourage the use of language in varied contexts
- Encourage peer collaboration for learning
- Facilitate language practice workshops
- Promote the use of language in everyday life
- Raise awareness of cultural diversity and interculturality
- Adapt training methods to specific needs
- Provide constructive feedback to participants after activities
