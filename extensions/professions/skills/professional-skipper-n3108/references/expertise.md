# Professional Skipper

## Scope

The professional Skipper pilots, manages a boat and ensures the transport of passengers on sailing pleasure vessels. Plans trips and adapts trajectories according to weather conditions Masters navigation techniques and uses on-board instruments Ensures regular maintenance of the boat and ensures its good working condition Welcomes passengers on board and transmits instructions to them Apply safety protocols and manages emergency situations Ensures administrative management of trips, budget management and ensures compliance with maritime regulations Can coordinate and supervise the activities of a crew and distribute tasks

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Facilitate communication between passengers and crew
- Organize the work of a team
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Use navigation equipment to optimize routes (GPS, mobile applications)
- Ensure basic maintenance of navigation equipment
- Carry out minor repairs at sea
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain a boat
- Define supply needs
- Manage inventory of on-board supplies
- Manage provisions and stocks on board
- Monitor the course of a boat
- Carry out docking or undocking maneuvers for the boat
- Navigate while respecting maritime safety rules
- Sailing on a sailing pleasure yacht
- Participate in maritime navigation operations and update nautical charts and documents
- Prepare and operate the ship's equipment (anchors, cables, ropes and rigging, etc.) during departure or docking maneuvers
- Carry out the delivery of a sailing pleasure vessel
- Carry out maneuvers for entering and exiting ports, anchoring, changing sails, etc.
- Transport passengers
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Help the customer to settle in and explain the safety rules
- Inform passengers in the event of a deviation from the route
- Promote a pleasant atmosphere on board
- Communicate and cooperate with maritime authorities
- Promote ecological practices among passengers such as waste sorting
- Using English in a professional context
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Adapt routes according to weather conditions
- Managing an emergency situation
- Inform passengers of safety procedures on board
