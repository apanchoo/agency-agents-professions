# Professional photographer

## Scope

The photographer takes photos and intervenes throughout the creation cycle of his photos, according to the visual needs of the client or the project. Takes shots in the studio or outdoors after identifying the location while organizing its logistics Processes and retouches the images to obtain the desired quality and effect Adapts its shooting techniques according to technological developments and trends by controlling lighting Manages the archiving of images and the maintenance of photographic equipment Multiplies its shots and acts on technical adjustments in response to conditions and unforeseen events in the field
