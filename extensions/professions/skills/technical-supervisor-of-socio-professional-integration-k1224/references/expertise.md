# Technical supervisor of socio-professional integration

## Scope

The technical socio-professional integration supervisor provides supervision and socio-professional support for employees being integrated into an integration structure producing goods or services. Organizes and manages all or part of a production activity in compliance with the structure's sustainable development approach Supervises the team of employees in integration, taking into account their specificities, in particular possible disability situations in an inclusive approach Implements the conditions necessary for their integration into the structure Participates in close collaboration with internal stakeholders in reception, socio-professional support and monitoring the path of employees in integration Promotes the acquisition of professional skills and behaviors in a production situation Evaluates acquired skills throughout the integration process and formalizes the results in order to allow traceability of the acquisition of professional skills and behaviors
