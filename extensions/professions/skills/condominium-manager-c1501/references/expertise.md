# Condominium manager

## Scope

The co-ownership manager administers one or more real estate site(s) and implements the technical, administrative, financial and accounting means to conserve or improve the real estate assets according to the regulations in force. Ensures the monitoring of decisions taken at the general meeting and implements the corresponding actions. Supervises the upkeep and maintenance of common areas and coordinates the intervention of service providers and companies. Manages unpaid debts and implements recovery procedures by monitoring the co-ownership's forecast budget Responds to requests and complaints from co-owners and complies with safety and accessibility standards
