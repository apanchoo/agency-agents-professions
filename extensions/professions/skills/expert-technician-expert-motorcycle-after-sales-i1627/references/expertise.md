# Expert technician / Expert motorcycle after-sales technician

## Scope

The Motorcycle After-Sales Expert Technician, technical referent within the workshop, carries out inspection, diagnosis and repair operations on all types of motorized two-wheel vehicles. Carries out diagnostics and complex repair of motorcycles Supports the receiver in his activity of welcoming customers and returning vehicles, and provides his technical expertise Plans interventions and organizes his activity and his expertise (particularly in the context of electronic diagnostics) Advises customers of the motorcycle workshop Can set up technical training sequences for workshop employees

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Identify the human and material resources to carry out a technical intervention
- Provide technical support to workshop collaborators
- Tutor young people in training
- Interpret electrical diagrams
- Ensure the maintenance of workshop tools and equipment
- Carry out periodic maintenance of motorcycles
- Carry out the diagnosis and re-compliance of motorcycle gearboxes
- Carry out the diagnosis and restoration of compliance of the thermal engine standby devices (STOP AND START)
- Carry out the diagnosis and restoration of conformity of heat engines and their peripheral circuits
- Carry out the diagnosis and re-compliance of motorcycle injection and depollution systems
- Carry out the diagnosis and re-compliance of motorcycle controlled suspension systems
- Carry out the diagnosis and re-compliance of mechanical transmissions of motorcycles
- Carry out the diagnosis and restoration of conformity of the elements of the traction chain of an electric or hybrid vehicle (excluding traction battery)
- Carry out the diagnosis and restoration of conformity of the electronic gasoline injection circuit of motorcycles
- Carry out the diagnosis and re-compliance of the motorcycle braking system
- Carry out the diagnosis and bring the cycle part of the motorcycle into conformity
- Carry out the diagnosis and bring the active safety systems of motorcycles (ADAS) into compliance
- Estimate the costs and deadlines for an activity or service
- Welcome the motorcycle workshop customer
- Provide technical advice to the customer with a view to selling additional services or complementary services
- Advise customers on the maintenance of their motorcycle
- Carry out a personalized return of the motorcycle to the customer
- Explain and argue an intervention or technical solution to the client
- Formalize the diagnostic results (via the diagnostic sheet, a checklist, etc.)
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
