## Scope

Carries out diagnostics and complex repair of motorcycles Supports the receiver in his activity of welcoming customers and returning vehicles, and provides his technical expertise Plans interventions and organizes his activity and his expertise (particularly in the context of electronic diagnostics) Advises customers of the motorcycle workshop Can set up technical training sequences for workshop employees
