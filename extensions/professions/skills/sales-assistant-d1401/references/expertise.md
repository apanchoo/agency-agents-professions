# Sales assistant

## Scope

The sales assistant manages the intermediary commercial activity between the company's customers and sales representatives. Manages the tracking of orders and deliveries Ensures communication and customer follow-up to respond to specific requests and resolve problems Participates in the drafting of commercial proposals Updates the customer database and sales statistics May contribute to the organization of commercial events and customer loyalty

## Related Job Titles

- Assistant/Commercial management assistant
- Commercial management assistant
- Administrative and commercial assistant
- Sales assistant / Sales assistant and calls for tenders
- Sales assistant / Sedentary sales assistant

## Activities and Skills

- Optimize inventory and resource management
- Organize commercial events
- Write product sheets or sales pitches
- Feed and update the customer and prospect database, classify and archive files and documents
- Update and close customer files
- Complete and update activity monitoring and traceability tools (dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Managing crises and business emergencies
- Process orders and prepare shipments
- Ensure customer relations: commercial monitoring, incident management (exchanges, returns, credits), follow-up for recovery
- Create, open accounts for new customers (commercial proposals, quotes, order/contract, information necessary for delivery and invoicing)
- Track orders, invoicing
- Establish sales and activity reports
- Manage sales from order to delivery, ensuring agreed deadlines and compliance of contractual conditions with internal procedures
- Organize meetings and write reports
- Ensure relationships and monitoring with the various project stakeholders (subcontractors, suppliers, end customers, communities, operators)
- Prepare commercial files before sales contacts and meetings
- Manage budgets allocated to commercial activities and projects
- Manage invoices and collections in conjunction with accounting, legal and litigation departments
- Ensure compliance of business practices
- Collect and analyze data relating to goods, international trade flows, and other aspects of customs operations
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Welcome face to face
- Analyze customer needs
- Collaborate with internal teams to respond to customer requests
- Manage incoming contacts (calls, emails, letters) and forward them to the right person
- Handle special customer requests
- Ensure all outgoing contacts for customers, non-customers, prospects, partners
- Ensure post-sales follow-up
- Provide commercial support: customer loyalty campaign
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Carry out commercial and competitive monitoring (customer portals, public and private market platforms)
- Read and understand activity dashboards and indicators
- Optimize the customer journey
- Optimize sales processes
- Participate in trade shows and conferences
- Participate in the development of a technical quote
- Schedule and monitor actions to be taken following contacts with customers or prospects
- Prepare or establish commercial proposals, write price offers, develop quotes
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Respond to a call for tenders
- Monitor deliveries, settle complaints related to after-sales service, in liaison with the sales, purchasing, production, technical, logistics departments
- Define and prioritize priorities based on set objectives
- Develop digital marketing strategies
- Develop and analyze sales statistics
- Implement commercial performance indicators
- Collaborate with the sales team to achieve common goals
- Develop team spirit
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Participate in cross-functional projects and provide technical skills and customer knowledge
- Plan and organize meetings
- Ensure legal and regulatory monitoring
- Respect delivery times
