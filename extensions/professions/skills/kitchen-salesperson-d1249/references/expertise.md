# Kitchen salesperson

## Scope

The kitchen seller is an expert in interior design, and enhances each kitchen project thanks to an approach combining listening, creativity and technical mastery. Advises customers and assesses their kitchen needs and expectations Designs personalized kitchen plans using specialized software Presents and sells solutions adapted to customer budgets and tastes Follows up on orders and coordinates with delivery and installation teams Manages product stocks and equipment orders

## Related Job Titles

- Kitchen designer

## Activities and Skills

- Create 2D/3D plans adapted to the project
- Create kitchen and bathroom plans
- Use computer-aided design (CAD) software
- Manage any after-sales service or post-installation adjustments
- Define supply needs
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Organize order processing
- Proceed to cashout
- Create a credit file
- Carry out accounting operations
- Establish a sales contract
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Analyze customer needs, tastes and constraints
- Order furniture, equipment and accessories from suppliers
- Advise clients on materials, technical solutions and styles
- Present the design solutions and material choices
- Solve any problems related to design or installation
- Argument the advantages of the products and configurations proposed
- Provide after-sales service
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Provide detailed quotes and cost explanations
- Negotiate commercial terms
- Participate in setting up the showroom and commercial operations
- Present and promote a product or service
- Collaborate with installers, suppliers and other services
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Respect safety standards
- Use digital tools
- Follow the evolution of decoration trends and household appliances

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of precise measuring tools

## Areas of expertise

- Technical standards related to electricity, plumbing and ergonomics
- Knowledge of ventilation systems
- Commercial argument
- Interior decoration
- Costing and calculation
- 3D design and planning software
- Customer relations and technical advice
- Knowledge of different cooking styles

## Professional techniques

- Technical drawing techniques
- Material cutting techniques
- Oral, written and digital communication techniques
- Adaptation of designs according to demand
- Merchandising techniques
