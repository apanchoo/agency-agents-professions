# Director of outdoor hotels

## Scope

The Outdoor Hotel Director defines the development strategy of his establishment and ensures its implementation. Manages the daily operation of the site, including reservations, reception, accommodation, catering and leisure activities. Ensures the quality of services offered to customers and ensures their satisfaction Supervises and coordinates the work of teams on the site Develops and implements marketing strategies to promote the site and the service offering Controls budgets and optimizes operating costs Ensures compliance with safety and hygiene standards
