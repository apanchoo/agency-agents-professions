# Salesperson in personal accessories

## Scope

The personal accessories seller, expert in fashion and comfort items, guides and advises his customers with passion and expertise. Welcomes and advises customers on the products best suited to their needs and desires Presents and highlights the items, explaining the characteristics and advantages of each product Manages sales transactions, ensuring customer satisfaction at each stage Participates in inventory management and the organization of promotions and special events
