# Mascot-character

## Scope

The mascot-character enlivens and brightens up events and leisure parks. Animates and interacts with the public to create a festive and welcoming atmosphere Wears character costumes for thematic performances during special events Participates in the design and implementation of shows or activities for various audiences Ensures compliance with safety and behavior guidelines in costume May participate in photo sessions or meetings with visitors to reinforce the immersive experience Contributes to the promotion of the image of the company or park through quality performances

- Christmas character
