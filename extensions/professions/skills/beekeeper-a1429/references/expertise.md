# Beekeeper

## Scope

An artisan of nature, the beekeeper raises bees to produce honey, wax, royal jelly and other bee products. Regularly inspects hives to ensure the health and protection of bees against diseases or predators Harvests hive products and markets them, promoting their origin and their specificities Participates in the preservation of local bee species and the biodiversity of its environment Can offer educational tours to raise public awareness of the importance of bees in the ecosystem

## Related Job Titles

- Bee breeder

## Activities and Skills

- Implement and promote sustainable beekeeping practices
- Administer veterinary products
- Regularly check the health of bees or the presence of parasites and take preventive or curative measures
- Handle animals
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Analyze bee behavior to prevent colony swarming
- Create, design new products or product improvements
- Plan honey harvest times
- Apply bee breeding techniques
- Extract honey from frames according to hygiene and quality rules
- Make objects or goods from products from the hive
- Harvest the product of breeding
- Use specific beekeeping equipment
- Optimize the location of hives for the season and plan their rotations according to flowering
- Manufacture or repair hives, frames and other beekeeping equipment
- Clean and disinfect hives and equipment to prevent disease
- Organize storage in a storage space
- Handle hives while respecting safety rules
- Respect honey quality standards
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Packaging products
- Manage merchandise inventory
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Carry out accounting operations
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Offer additional products
- Marketing the products of a farm
- Offer a tasting to customers
- Present and promote a product or service
- Manage production and operations management
- Collaborate with farmers and establish pollination contracts
- Organize workshops, visits or conferences to raise public awareness of the importance of bees and beekeeping
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Act quickly in the event of a hive attack
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
