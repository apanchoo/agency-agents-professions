# Manager of café, bar-brewery

## Scope

The Café Manager, bar-brewery supervises daily operations, manages staff, ensures customer satisfaction and ensures the profitability of his establishment. Manages the daily life of the café, bar or brasserie, ensuring quality service of drinks and culinary preparations Supervises the team, trains staff and manages schedules Ensures compliance with hygiene and safety standards Optimizes stock and supply management to meet customer needs
