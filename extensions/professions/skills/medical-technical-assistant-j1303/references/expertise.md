# Medical-technical assistant

## Scope

The medical-technical assistant is a healthcare professional who works closely with doctors, nurses and other members of the medical team. Welcomes and directs patients in a medical office or health facility Manages appointments, telephone calls and patient medical records Assists physicians during consultations and medical examinations Prepares and organizes consulting rooms and ensures their cleanliness Performs administrative tasks such as billing and filing documents May attend specific medical-technical procedures and manage medical equipment

## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Assist medical staff during consultations
- Coordinate the activities of the medical office
- Handle minor emergencies while waiting for medical personnel
- Seat the patient for consultation or examination
- Prepare radiology accessories and develop images
- Carry out a screening (visual, auditory)
- Receive the medical-technical elements and inform the practitioner and the patient with a view to the intervention
- Making adjustments to a respiratory assistance device
- Install medical equipment at home (medical bed, respiratory assistance device, etc.) and explain how it works
- Prepare the equipment and products according to the practitioner's instructions and the nature of the intervention
- Prepare, install a medical-technical device
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Prepare treatment rooms and equipment necessary for interventions
- Establish elements of an order
- Organize appointments and the agenda of a healthcare professional
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Greet, orient and provide information to a patient
- Install a person, a client
- Relay information
- Collect data from a medical-technical device
- Respect the confidentiality of information
