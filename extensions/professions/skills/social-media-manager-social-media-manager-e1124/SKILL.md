## Scope

Develops and implements the social media communications strategy Manages and leads online communities to increase company visibility Creates and optimizes content for different social platforms Analyzes campaign performance and adjusts strategies accordingly Collaborates with other departments to ensure consistency of communication May train and supervise a team of community managers
