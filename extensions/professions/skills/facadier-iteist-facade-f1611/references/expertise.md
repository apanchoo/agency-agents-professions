# Façadier/Iteist Facade

## Scope

The Façadier itéiste, a professional essential to the aesthetics and protection of buildings, applies coatings and coverings to protect and decorate facades. Carries out thermal insulation from the outside to improve comfort and reduce the energy consumption of buildings Carries out renovation work to renovate and preserve the integrity of facades Ensures compliance of the work with safety and environmental standards Advises clients on the best solutions for their facade projects Can specialize in specific techniques such as plastering or sandblasting
