# Laminating machine driver

## Scope

The Laminating Machine Operator defines and adjusts the machine parameters to ensure a quality finish for printed products. Operates and monitors laminating machines to apply a protective or decorative film to printed products Makes fine adjustments to ensure quality and conformance of finishes Controls the quality of finished products and makes adjustments as necessary Maintains machines in good operating condition, performing maintenance and minor repairs Manages inventory of laminating materials and places orders according to production needs Collaborates with other members of the production team to ensure maximum efficiency and meet deadlines
