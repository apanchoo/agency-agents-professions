# Driver of road transport of live animals

## Scope

The road transport driver of live animals safely drives a heavy group vehicle requiring category C and/or CE licenses, independently to ensure the movement of live animals from a place of departure to a place of destination. By adopting the principles of eco-driving, it contributes to the protection of the environment Respects the regulations in force in particular relating to the protection of animals during transport and related operations, in terms of driving and rest times, road regulations and safety Ensures safe and comfortable transport for the animals Checks the operating condition of the vehicle and its equipment, the presence and validity of mandatory documents and reports any anomaly Completes or has the transport documents completed Maintains hygiene and safety conditions health safety according to the type of animal transport Maintains regular communication with the operations department to, among other things, report any incident Transmits technical information and quality indicators to clients (slaughterhouses, others) Represents the company's brand image

## Related Job Titles

- Animal taxi driver
- Animal transport driver

## Activities and Skills

- Know and evaluate the animals' reception conditions and environment
- Use electronic management systems
- Use navigation equipment (professional GPS, mobile applications)
- Use driving aids
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Carry out vehicle safety checks
- Anticipate what to do in abnormal and emergency situations
- Apply safety procedures in case of emergency
- Ensuring the safety and welfare of animals during transport
- Driving a heavy goods vehicle
- Monitor animal comfort throughout transport
- Determine a route based on delays and traffic characteristics
- Clean and disinfect the vehicle according to the type of animal transported
- Take charge of a vehicle with its equipment and accessories
- Carry out coupling operations for an articulated vehicle
- Complete transport documents
- Apply current European legislation regarding the transport of live animals
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Evacuate animals, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Adopt professional communication with customers, law enforcement, emergency services and road users
- Report technical information and quality indicators to clients (slaughterhouses, others)
- Report to the operator on his activity
- Represent the company and its brand image to clients and clients
- Manage breaks and driving times in accordance with regulations
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Respect the highway code
- Respect road safety rules
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
