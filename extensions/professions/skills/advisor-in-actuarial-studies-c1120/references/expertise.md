# Advisor in actuarial studies

## Scope

The Actuarial Studies Advisor analyzes statistical data to evaluate financial and actuarial risks. Participates in the design of insurance products and pricing Collaborates with risk management teams to optimize strategies Conducts actuarial studies to support the company's strategic decisions Ensures the updating of actuarial models according to market developments Presents the results of the studies and recommendations to stakeholders
