# Researcher in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The research manager, a key player in scientific and technological advancement. Conducts research and development projects in various scientific fields Analyzes and interprets data from experiments Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to innovate and solve complex problems Writes research reports and scientific publications Presents research results at conferences or to stakeholders Ensures scientific and technological monitoring to stay at the forefront of your field

## Related Job Titles

- Officer / Applied Research Officer
- Researcher in pharmaceutical sciences
- Scientific research pharmacist

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Collaborate with international teams
- Present and explain scientific advances and research work
- Write a scientific publication
- Analyze experiment results
- Design theoretical models (calculation, simulation, modeling)
- Design and coordinate a program, a research project
- Conduct study and research work
- Develop innovative research strategies
- Establish a study or research report
- Optimize experimental protocols
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Check data compliance
- Determine and develop methods of research, collection and analysis of data
- Analyze industrial and institutional needs and contract research services
- Participate in defining the schedule, overall budget and deadlines
- Apply pharmaceutical regulations
- Search for financing, investors
- Supervise and control the progress and progress of scientific experiments and observations
- Provide scientific support to researchers, institutions, companies
- Communicate research results to a non-specialist audience
- Organize a documentary base
- Use digital tools
- Regularly update your knowledge
- Building a professional network in research

## Professional Approach

- Show curiosity, open-mindedness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
