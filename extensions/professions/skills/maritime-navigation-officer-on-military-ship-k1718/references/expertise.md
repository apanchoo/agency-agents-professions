# Maritime navigation officer on military ship

## Scope

The maritime navigation officer on a military ship is a qualified professional who has in-depth expertise in navigation, maritime safety and regulations. Ensures or participates in the operation of a maritime or river navigation vessel Implements the principles of coastal and offshore or river navigation Uses rigging and maneuvering equipment Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out his profession

## Related Job Titles

- High-level expert in maneuvering and piloting a ship
- Expert in nautical driving
- Expert in maneuvering and piloting a ship
- Technician in navigation and nautical monitoring
- Underwater navigation technician

## Activities and Skills

- Perform maintenance and repair of nautical equipment
- Anticipate a danger or constraint
- Maneuver a footbridge
- Have in-depth knowledge of navigation rules and maritime regulations
- Understand and take into account marine meteorological phenomena
- Drive navigation
- Know the different types of military boats and their specificities
- Carry out docking or undocking maneuvers for the boat
- Be able to pilot different types of boats
- Guarantee the safety of the vessel during navigation
- Managing emergency situations at sea
- Master navigation techniques (cartography, piloting, marine weather)
- Implement rigging and maneuvering equipment
- Implement the principles of coastal and offshore or river navigation
- Knowing how to react following combat damage
- Use navigation equipment
- Using transmission equipment on a military ship
- Ensure proper maintenance and updating of nautical documents
- Advise and support in maritime law
- Communicate in English orally and in writing
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Managing stressful work situations
