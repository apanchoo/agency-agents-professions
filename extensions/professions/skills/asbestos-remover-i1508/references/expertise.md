# Asbestos remover

## Scope

The Asbestos Remover specializes in the identification, encapsulation and safe removal of asbestos from buildings, indoors and outdoors. Prepares the site and secures the work area to avoid contamination Removes asbestos following strict procedures to minimize health risks Packages asbestos waste for transport to specialized treatment sites Monitors post-intervention air quality to ensure the absence of asbestos fibers Writes detailed reports on interventions and measures taken
