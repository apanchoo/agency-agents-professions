# Expert in automobile damage insurance

## Scope

The Motor Damage Insurance Expert is responsible for assessing damage following accidents or disasters and protecting financial interests. Assesses material damage to vehicles Writes detailed expert reports for insurance companies to help them determine the amount of compensation to be paid Negotiates with the parties involved to establish responsibilities and compensation Can intervene in disputes between policyholders and insurance companies

## Related Job Titles

- Automobile insurance expert
- Automobile insurance expert

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze and solve a common or complex problem
- Inspect damaged vehicles to assess the extent of damage, determine causes and estimate repair costs
- Use software specific to automotive expertise
- Arrange appointments for vehicle inspections
- Write clear and detailed expert reports
- Analyze automobile claims files
- Negotiate compensation with the parties involved
- Apply insurance rules and regulations
- Provide technical advice to insurers and policyholders on repair procedures and associated costs
- Monitor the progress of repairs to ensure they are carried out in accordance with established standards and specifications
- Communicate effectively with repair shops
- Promote communication between different stakeholders
- Negotiate with repairers, insurers and policyholders to find solutions acceptable to all parties
- Integrate digital tools for file tracking
- Respect deadlines for processing files
- Respect ethical and confidentiality standards
- Building your professional network in the insurance sector
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of new insurance regulations
