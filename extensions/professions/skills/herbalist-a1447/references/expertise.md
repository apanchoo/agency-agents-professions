# Herbalist

## Scope

The Herbalist advises and sells medicinal or aromatic plants, herbs and derived products, used for therapeutic or well-being purposes. Prepares and sells infusions, decoctions, tinctures, essential oils, balms and other herbal preparations Advises customers on plants and their use according to their request and informs them of possible contraindications Manages stocks of raw materials and preparations Organizes product presentation workshops and training on the benefits of plants Maintains up-to-date knowledge of the properties of plants and regulatory developments Can cultivate and harvest plants in compliance with natural cycles

## Related Job Titles

- Farmer/Peasant herbalist
- Phytotherapist
