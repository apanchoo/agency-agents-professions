# Holiday club presenter

## Scope

The holiday club host creates a festive and friendly atmosphere for holidaymakers. Organizes and leads recreational and fun activities adapted to different audiences Ensures the safety and well-being of participants during the activities Promotes interaction and cohesion between vacationers Adapts activities according to the age groups and interests of the participants Can specialize in a type of activity (artistic, sporting) or for a specific audience (children) Can participate in promoting the club's activities to vacationers

## Related Job Titles

- Holiday village facilitator
- Camping facilitator
- Facilitator in outdoor hospitality
