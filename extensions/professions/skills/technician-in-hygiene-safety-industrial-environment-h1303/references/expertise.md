# Technician in Hygiene, Safety, Industrial Environment (HSE)

## Scope

Plays a crucial role in preserving health and safety at work while ensuring protection of the environment. Assesses and analyzes occupational and environmental hazards in the industry Implements safety policies and accident prevention programs Conducts regular safety audits and inspections to ensure compliance with applicable standards Trains staff in safety practices and emergency procedures Manages safety and environmental documentation, including incident reports and permits Collaborates with regulatory authorities and certification bodies to maintain necessary accreditations

## Activities and Skills

- Organize training on safety equipment
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Issue activity authorizations and determine their execution methods (wearing special equipment, authorization, fire permit, etc.)
- Carry out readings, measurements, samples, identify deviations (rate, threshold, etc.) and recommend corrective measures
- Analyze a malfunction or non-compliance
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Collaborate with teams to strengthen safety culture
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Train and inform employees to make them aware of risk prevention
- Implement emergency alert procedures and other predefined measures in the event of an incident
- Optimize processes to reduce environmental risks
- Propose solutions to improve safety at work
- Recommend risk management methods and tools
- Carry out or participate in carrying out risk studies (identification, census, evaluation, etc.)
- Check the operation of monitoring and treatment installations for discharges (water, smoke, etc.) or waste
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Monitor the application of current safety standards
- Communicate a diagnosis of environmental or health risks
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Carry out an environmental impact study
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Build a professional project focused on environmental safety
