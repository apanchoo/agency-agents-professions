## Scope

The Grooming Machine Operator shapes and maintains the ski slopes to guarantee the safety and enjoyment of skiers. Checks the condition of the grooming machine Prepares and maintains the ski slopes by operating a grooming machine Controls and adjusts the parameters of the machine for optimal grooming Produces daily reports on the condition of the slopes and the interventions carried out May participate in the training of new grooming machine drivers

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Complete the intervention documents
- Use GPS guidance technologies for work precision
- Groom the slopes using the necessary accessories (cutter, blade, etc.)
- Perform first level maintenance of machines
- Check the operating status of a machine
- Maneuver the machine according to the terrain (nature of the ground, height difference, risks, etc.)
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Manage technical unforeseen events and adjust plans accordingly
