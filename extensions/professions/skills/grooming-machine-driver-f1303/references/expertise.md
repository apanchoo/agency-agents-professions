# Grooming machine driver

## Scope

The Grooming Machine Operator shapes and maintains the ski slopes to guarantee the safety and enjoyment of skiers. Checks the condition of the grooming machine Prepares and maintains the ski slopes by operating a grooming machine Controls and adjusts the parameters of the machine for optimal grooming Produces daily reports on the condition of the slopes and the interventions carried out May participate in the training of new grooming machine drivers

## Related Job Titles

- Snow groomer driver

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in regular team meetings
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Complete the intervention documents
- Use GPS guidance technologies for work precision
- Manufacture modules if necessary in compliance with current standards (freestyle)
- Establish a damage plan based on needs and weather conditions
- Evaluate the condition of the snow and terrain
- Organize the operations to be carried out in order to respect deadlines and site constraints
- Groom the slopes using the necessary accessories (cutter, blade, etc.)
- Ensure preventive maintenance of machines
- Perform first level maintenance of machines
- Check the operating status of a machine
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Carry out a visual inspection of the machine to check its working condition
- Ensure visibility to avoid any accidents during interventions
- Maneuver the machine according to the terrain (nature of the ground, height difference, risks, etc.)
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Participate in emergency relief interventions
- Monitor weather conditions to plan activities
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Adapt working methods to weather conditions
- Manage technical unforeseen events and adjust plans accordingly
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Use digital tools
