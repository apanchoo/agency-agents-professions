# Ironer

## Scope

The Ironer is a professional manual providing the final touch on the neat and clean appearance of the garment. Treats and irons clothing and textiles to remove creases Uses various types of ironing equipment to treat different fabrics Controls the quality of textiles after treatment to ensure their conformity Can carry out touch-ups and minor finishes on textiles during ironing Organizes their work space to optimize the flow of textiles to be treated Respects safety and hygiene standards in the handling of ironing equipment

## Related Job Titles

- Ironer / First hand ironer
- Ironer / Second hand ironer

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze the materials to understand the expected level of finish
- Visually check the finish of clothes after ironing
- Distinguish between textile raw materials and their properties
- Implement product quality control procedures
- Prepare an item for ironing
- Carry out filling operations (hemming, topstitching, etc.) on textile items
- Check the costumes and carry out alterations and finishes
- Ensure routine maintenance or cleaning of equipment after use under the required health and safety conditions
- Check the cleanliness of the laundry
- Maintain ironing equipment in good working order
- Ironing textiles (clothes, linen)
- Check the conformity of the finishes and carry out re-mesh repairs, sewing repairs, etc.
- Practice safe handling gestures and postures
- Analyze the quality of textiles for optimal ironing
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Identify a stain or dirt
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Pack ironed clothes for delivery to customer
- Manage the stock of supplies necessary for ironing (water, starch)
- Carry out collection and delivery of items to customers
- Return the items to the customer after checking cleaning, ironing
- Communicate with customers to understand their specific expectations
