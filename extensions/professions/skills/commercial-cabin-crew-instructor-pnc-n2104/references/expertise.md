# Commercial Cabin Crew Instructor (PNC)

## Scope

The Commercial Flight Personnel Instructor (PNC) plays an essential role in the training and maintenance of the skills of air crews. Ensures initial and continuing training of commercial cabin crew according to aviation safety standards Evaluates the practical and theoretical skills of crew members Updates training programs according to regulatory developments and new procedures Organizes and supervises flight simulation sessions for cabin crew Contributes to the continuous improvement of safety and emergency procedures on board May participate in the selection of new cabin crew members

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Provide continuing training on safety equipment
- Provide ground courses for recurrent and initial training
- Provide regulatory training for the exercise of the cabin crew profession
- Teach the mechanical operation of different types of machines
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, the characteristics of the service equipment
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, the checks to be carried out before and after the flight
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, the operations to be carried out on board the aircraft
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, safety procedures
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, customer service procedures and formalities
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, to identify and report malfunctions and anomalies in aircraft cabin equipment
- Teach, depending on the type of aircraft, the appropriate procedures (first aid, reminder of safety measures) to passengers in the event of need or difficulty
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Train staff in the use of equipment
- Train new employees in safety procedures
- Train new employees in operational procedures
- Train trainees on operations inside aircraft cabins
- Train users in the use of fire extinguishers
- Ensure compliance with the methodology and training conditions in work and tutoring situations
- Develop your crisis management and communication skills
- Produce an air flight report
- Maintain regulatory monitoring of aviation safety
- Raise awareness and train staff in safety and prevention instructions
- Ensure the demonstration of safety instructions
- Be able to justify 3000 flight hours
- Stay informed of new educational trends and technological developments
