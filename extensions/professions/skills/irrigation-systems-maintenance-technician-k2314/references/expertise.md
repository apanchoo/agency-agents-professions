# Irrigation systems maintenance technician

## Scope

The irrigation system maintenance technician ensures the efficiency and durability of irrigation systems. Installs and commissions irrigation systems for agriculture and green spaces Carry out fault diagnoses and carry out the necessary repairs to ensure the proper functioning of the installations Carry out regular checks and preventive maintenance of equipment to prevent malfunctions Advise customers on the optimal use of systems and on available technological innovations Can participate in the design and improvement of irrigation systems to specifically meet the needs of users Ensures technological monitoring to stay at the forefront of developments in the field field of irrigation

## Related Job Titles

- Irrigation systems installation technician

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Adapt irrigation systems to the specificities of the land
- Analyze and interpret the results of permanent sensors placed on water networks
- Analyze water quality in relation to chemical and biochemical quality benchmarks
- Assemble pipes by pointing, screwing, bolting
- Install automated irrigation systems
- Adjust operating or water sampling equipment
- Use a self-guided camera
- Install operating or water sampling equipment
- Diagnose the modernization work of a sanitation network
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain pipes
- Implement drinking water pumping and storage techniques
- Implement chemical water treatment techniques
- Identify leaks and obstructions in a sanitation network
- Carry out renovation, rehabilitation or repair work on obsolete networks
- Carry out remote monitoring and regulation of operating equipment (automates, valves, pumps, etc.) and the flow of rain, floods, etc. remotely
- Carry out hydraulic cleaning (sluice boat, sluice wagon, etc.)
- Carry out manual cleaning
- Carry out mechanized, hydrodynamic, hydrocleaning
- Control a network or operating equipment
- Check a pipe
- Control a maintenance operation
- Check the tightness of a pipe
- Analyze process quality
- Ensure compliance of maintenance operations
- Carry out efficiency audits of irrigation systems
- Manage inventory
- Carry out order management (placing, tracking, reception)
- Write a report, an activity report
- Optimize irrigation settings for water efficiency
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Provide expertise in the field of wastewater purification or sanitation
- Evaluate the implementation of a project, an action plan
- Apply regulations specifying sanitation standards
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Use digital communication tools
- Acquire new skills independently to adapt to developments and apply this new knowledge
