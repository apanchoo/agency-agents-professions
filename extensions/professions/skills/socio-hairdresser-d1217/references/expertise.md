# Socio-hairdresser

## Scope

The Socio-hairdresser / The Socio-hairdresser, a professional in beauty and social well-being. Performs cuts and hair care adapted to the social situation of clients Listens and provides moral support to clients in precarious or isolated situations Offers hairdressing workshops in social or medico-social structures Adapts hairdressing techniques to the specific needs of vulnerable groups Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to offer an integrated service Participates in community development projects through hairdressing

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Providing aesthetic care to people who are vulnerable, suffering or in social reintegration
- Carry out shaping, styling, fixing the hair (curling, blow-drying, etc.)
- Prepare and apply coloring and bleaching products (highlights, etc.)
- Carry out hairdressing services
- Carry out hair diagnosis, washing preparations and shampoo
- Make a cut or hairstyle
- Use hair products tailored to specific client needs
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Greet the customer and install them
- Advise on styles suited to the client's body shape and complexion
- Provide quality service
- Manage customer expectations during peak periods
- Identify the expected hairstyle and advise the client
- Show the client their hairstyle from different angles to get their opinion
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Promote hair care products suitable for each hair type
- Sell a service or product
- Adapt cutting techniques to current trends
- Respect hygiene and health safety standards

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
