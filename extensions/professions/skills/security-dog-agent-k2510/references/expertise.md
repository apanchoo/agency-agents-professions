# Security dog agent

## Scope

The security dog officer ensures the protection of property and people thanks to his canine companion. Monitors sites and protects property and people using a trained dog Intervenes in emergency or alert situations to prevent intrusions or malicious acts Controls access to sites and ensures the security of the premises Carries out security patrols to prevent any suspicious activity Can participate in search and rescue missions with his dog Maintains his dog in physical and technical condition to guarantee their operational efficiency
