# Sculptor

## Scope

The sculptor is an artist who creates works in volume by shaping materials such as stone, wood, metal or clay. Designs artistic works in three dimensions Shapes the material using manual or mechanical tools Makes models or preparatory sketches Works shapes, textures and volumes with precision Restores or maintains existing sculptures
