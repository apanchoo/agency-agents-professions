# Social mediator

## Scope

The social mediator facilitates access to services and rights, resolves misunderstandings between people and institutions and helps prevent and resolve conflicts. Monitors and ensures an active social presence in public spaces Intervenes in conflict situations to ease tensions Collaborates with social, educational and access to rights services to develop the inclusion of people Organizes and participates in events aimed at improving neighborhood life and strengthening social ties Informs and directs people in precarious or excluded situations about the resources and services available
