# Communications officer

## Scope

The Communications Manager is a key player in the strategy of promoting a company's brand image. Manages the relationship with the media and other communication partners Implements and evaluates the company's internal and external communication strategy Writes and distributes content, guarantees the company's digital visibility Regularly organizes events external and internal to the organization

- Internal communications manager
- Online communications manager
- Scientific communication officer
- Social communications manager
- Web communications manager
