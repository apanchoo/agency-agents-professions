# Performing artist

## Scope

The performing artist represents, sings, plays or performs a work in public or in the studio, seeking the adequacy between artistic intentions and production requirements. Performs artistic works in the recording studio or on stage Adapts their interpretation to the style, artistic direction and production constraints Collaborates with other artists and technicians in staging performances Participates in regular rehearsals to perfect their craft May participate in the creation of new artistic works Manages their career and public image, often with the help of agents or managers
