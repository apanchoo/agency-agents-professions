# Centralized station operator in cement plant

## Scope

The cement plant centralized station operator regulates the operation of the cement plant equipment from the control room. Controls production parameters with computerized systems verifying the conformity of finished products and raw materials Controls ovens, grinders, silos and other production machines and corrects anomalies on equipment by ensuring continuous maintenance Applies and respects safety, quality and environmental procedures in force in the industry Collaborates with maintenance technicians, production managers and other operators
