# Mechatronician

## Scope

The mechatronics expert, expert in technological convergence, combines mechanics, electronics and IT to design and maintain the intelligent systems of tomorrow. Designs, integrates and maintains mechatronic systems Develops, tests and optimizes intelligent devices Ensures their proper functioning Ensures their technological development

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Train users in the correct use of equipment
- Design mechatronic systems integrating mechanics, electronics and IT
- Develop strategies for solving complex problems
- Study and identify developments (functionality, profitability, quality, etc.) of components, products, equipment
- Follow technological developments in the sector
- Program microcontrollers and automatons in suitable languages
- Calculate and define the powers, quantities, physical constraints of components, subassemblies, assemblies
- Define and analyze system requirements
- Train teams in specific procedures
- Implement the safety devices required by regulatory requirements, to achieve the operating and performance characteristics of the need or specifications
- Master the processes and calculation methods specific to your field
- Propose solutions to improve the efficiency of installations
- Test prototypes to validate their conformity and performance
- Configure sensors and actuators for specific applications
- Make precise adjustments to mechanical components
- Make adjustments to automated systems to optimize their operation
- Carry out the development of automated systems
- Assemble the elements of the electrical or electronic product (components, wiring)
- Mount electronic components or incorporate electronic cards into devices
- Analyze a malfunction or non-compliance
- Diagnose, prevent and locate malfunctions, remedy them or recommend alternative solutions
- Troubleshoot mechatronic equipment using advanced diagnostic tools
- Write technical reports and maintenance procedures
- Check the conformity of the installation of electrical and electronic components
- Optimizing industrial processes to improve efficiency and sustainability
- Design and propose technical solutions responding to the study request
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Adapting existing solutions to new industrial contexts
- Comply with current safety standards and regulations
