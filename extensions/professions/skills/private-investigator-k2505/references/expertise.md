# Private investigator

## Scope

The private investigator intervenes on behalf of natural or legal persons, through investigations and surveillance, in order to collect information of all kinds, search for property and debtors, and gather evidence in the civil and commercial fields. Discreetly observes and follows individuals to obtain evidence of specific behaviors or activities Conducts background checks for businesses or individuals Collaborates with appropriate authorities in criminal investigations Works with attorneys to gather evidence for civil or criminal litigation May testify as an expert in court
