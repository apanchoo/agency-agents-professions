# Plasterer

## Scope

The plasterer intervenes in new construction or renovation to create interior spaces and improve the thermal and acoustic insulation of rooms. Installation of drywall and false ceilings Makes the joints between the plates to obtain a smooth surface ready for finishing Adapts the installation techniques to the specificities of the buildings to guarantee the quality and durability of the installations

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Ensure the finishing of surfaces and angles
- Make and smooth the joints
- Select materials according to needs
- Use a tool, machine, equipment, installation
- Adjust and fix plasterboard to metal or wooden structures
- Apply coatings, joint tapes
- Collaborate with teams to coordinate work
- Cut the plasterboard (placo) according to the measurements
- Set up a construction site
- Install thermal or acoustic insulation systems
- Install partitions or false ceilings
- Assemble or dismantle scaffolding
- Install simple electrical appliances
- Install suspended ceilings
- Trace the partitions to visualize the work to be done by checking the condition of the surfaces
- Clean the intervention site and store the tools
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Check the alignment and plumbness of the mounted structures
- Load, unload, handle products
- Organize and plan an activity
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Interpret technical information and construction plans
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
