# Conveyor / Cash conveyor

## Scope

The mission of the cash conveyor is to transport and secure funds and valuables to or from customers. Ensures his safety, that of his teammates and funds transported in an armored or unmarked vehicle by applying security procedures and instructions Maintains constant communications with the control center and with the teams on site Can coordinate operations

## Related Job Titles

- Conveyor / Conveyor of funds and values
- Conveyor / Conveyor guard
- Conductor conveyor / Fund conductor conveyor
- Messenger conveyor
- Exceptional convoy motorcycle guide
