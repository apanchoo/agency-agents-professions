# Rope Technician

## Scope

The rope technician is a specialist who masters all the techniques of working at height and on ropes: Under the responsibility of the supervisor, he works on construction, cleaning, inspection or maintenance sites of buildings, civil engineering or in natural environments, at work stations inaccessible by other means. Installs all the equipment and safety devices for work at height Ensures the planned work is carried out using personal protective equipment against falls from height Uses complex techniques for access to height, in particular by means of ropes Must know how to participate in rescue or evacuation operations at height in all configurations Strictly complies with the legal, regulatory and normative requirements for work at height Participates in the construction, maintenance and inspection of structures at height or access difficult, such as buildings, bridges, structures in a natural environment, industrial installations, wind turbines, etc.

## Related Job Titles

- Specialist in work at height in buildings
- Specialist in construction rope work
- Technician in difficult access work
- Rope work technician

## Activities and Skills

- Organize your work to reduce professional wear and tear and musculoskeletal disorders
- Move and position a light load in all directions using PPE
- Install workstations at height and on ropes in any situation
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Move on ropes in all directions, ergonomically to reduce the risk of professional wear and tear
- Use personal protective equipment against falls from height in accordance with their recommended use
- Fix, place, install elements, works
- Carry out repair operations on building envelopes and structures with difficult access
- Carry out maintenance or cleaning work on buildings, infrastructure or equipment
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Carry out visual inspections of structures at height or with difficult access
- Washing windows or other surfaces
- Carry out maintenance work at height or difficult to access
- Continuously monitor the applicability of prevention means, and report to the supervisor
- Perform a height rescue to bring a victim to safety in any situation
- Identify a working situation at height and understand the associated theoretical concepts (fall factor, air draft, impact force, pendulum effect, etc.)
- Install anchoring devices in accordance with their instructions for use
- Permanently respect established safety rules
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Check the operation of safety equipment
- Participate in a process of continuous improvement of prevention means, via a critical analysis of your activity
- Evaluate weather conditions for safety
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Apply the instructions given by your supervisor
- Take a critical look at the site documents made available
- Know the hierarchical chain and its role within it
- Trigger the emergency alert in the event of an accident, and apply backup actions
- Respect procedures, operating methods and instructions
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Use digital communication tools
