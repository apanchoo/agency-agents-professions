# Assistant to the hotel and catering management

## Scope

The Executive Assistant in the hotel and catering industry plays a key role in the daily management and smooth running of hotel and catering establishments. Assists the director in the operational management of the hotel and/or restaurant establishment Supervises the daily activities of the hotel and/or restaurant, guaranteeing quality service Coordinates and plans events and seminars, ensuring compliance with the establishment's standards Manages reservations, welcoming customers and monitoring their satisfaction Participates in human resources management, including recruitment, training and evaluation of staff Contributes to the development of commercial and marketing strategies to promote the establishment

- Assistant manager in hotel and catering
- Assistant / Assistant manager in catering
