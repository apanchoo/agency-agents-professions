# Gardener / Landscape gardener in sports field

## Scope

The sports field landscape gardener is involved in the development, maintenance and renovation of grassy or synthetic sports surfaces, under the responsibility of a site manager. Prepares and levels the ground using suitable machinery Installs the natural grass, synthetic or stabilized covering and carries out the marking of the grounds Carry out peripheral development work such as the installation of borders, fences or small masonry works Ensures the specific maintenance of the sports fields, mowing, aeration, sanding, fertilization and treatments adapted according to the state of the soil and lawns Intervenes to restore the front surfaces, during and after sporting events in order to guarantee their quality and safety Manages watering systems, programming, commissioning, shutdown and routine maintenance operations
