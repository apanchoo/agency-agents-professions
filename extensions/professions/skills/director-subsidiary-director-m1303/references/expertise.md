# Director/Subsidiary Director

## Scope

The Subsidiary Director is responsible for opening and managing a subsidiary in another region or another country, than the headquarters of a company. Directs and coordinates all activities of the subsidiary Defines commercial and operational strategies adapted to the local context Supervises financial management and budgetary control Manages human resources, including recruitment, training and skills development Develops reporting tools and remains in permanent contact with the company's headquarters to align strategic objectives

## Related Job Titles

- Director of a group of subsidiaries

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Promote the company culture and values within the subsidiary
- Supervise and manage organizational, technical, human, financial, logistical and communication resources to achieve objectives
- Supporting change among employees
- Design and implement a skills development plan
- Design and manage a human resources management policy
- Implement the transformation actions necessary for changes
- Participate in the implementation of HR projects facilitating inclusion, equality and quality of life at work within the structure
- Analyze the company's needs in training, recruitment and professional mobility
- Implement communication actions
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Supervise tax compliance of foreign subsidiaries
- Plan the management of premises and equipment
- Evaluate and manage the risks linked to the subsidiary's activities
- Managing a crisis situation
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Define the financial strategy of a structure
- Define and deploy a sustainable development policy
- Develop and manage international relations
- Analyze market trends to anticipate changes
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Develop a commercial strategy
- Implement sales strategies
- Design management tools, indicators, dashboards
- Design and manage a project
- Direct the day-to-day operations of a subsidiary
- Determine performance objectives, track achievements and identify corrective actions
- Develop a business development strategy
- Evaluate the social and environmental consequences of your decisions and act consistently with the company's values
- Identify potential public and private partners in a territory
- Implement effective reporting systems
- Oversee the implementation of business strategies
- Defend a project in front of a steering committee, collaborators or partners
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Make a decision and explain it
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Report development projects to management and partners
