# Category Manager Responsible (CATMAN)

## Scope

The Responsible Category Manager (CATMAN) is the person responsible for a product category who manages the offer, prices and promotion by integrating issues of economic performance, customer satisfaction and social and environmental responsibility. Analyzes category performance (sales, margins, rotations, CSR impacts) Defines the category strategy in line with customer expectations and responsible commitments Selects assortments favoring sustainable, ethical and market-adapted products Optimizes prices and promotions to reconcile profitability and accessibility Collaborates with suppliers to improve innovation, quality and environmental impact Manages merchandising and product promotion in store or online
