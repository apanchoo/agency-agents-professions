# Cutter in the textile and soft materials industry

## Scope

The Cutter in the textile and soft materials industry cuts, manually or using traditional or digital machines, textiles, leathers, foams or other soft materials, according to templates or cutting plans. Contributes to the essential stage of manufacturing clothing, textile items, leather goods, saddlery or furnishings Controls the quality of raw materials and cuts to ensure compliance with standards Collaborates with design and production teams to adjust cuts as needed

## Activities and Skills

- Train new operators in specific cutting techniques
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Plan cutting operations according to the production schedule
- Analyze production specifications for material cutting
- Read and interpret technical sheets, cutting plans or templates
- Mark the location of mounting notches on cut parts
- Mark a mark on a material
- Carry out manual cutting of parts for prototypes or small series
- Quilting a material for mass cutting
- Adjust parameters of automatic cutting machines
- Select the tools (saw blades, knives, etc.), mount them and adjust them (position, tension, etc.)
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Identify anomalies, manufacturing defects
- Group the cut parts by size, model, color, etc. for assembly/assembly of items
- Optimize the arrangement of patterns on materials to minimize waste
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
