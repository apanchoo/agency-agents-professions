# Humanitarian Logistics Project Coordinator

## Scope

The humanitarian logistics project coordinator coordinates and optimizes the management of product flows on an operational site, for all movements of entry, exit and handling of goods (including their packaging) and ensures that they are in constant communication with other departments of the company or actors in the supply chain, in relation to quality of service and traceability issues. Plans and coordinates goods receipt, storage and delivery operations Optimizes costs and deadlines for logistics operations by implementing performance indicators Supervises and trains operational teams to ensure the efficiency of logistics processes Ensures compliance of logistics operations with current regulations Collaborates with suppliers and customers to improve quality of service and resolve logistics problems Implements technological solutions to automate and improve logistics processes
