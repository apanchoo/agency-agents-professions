# Specialist in tools, operating systems, networks and telecoms

## Scope

The Tools, Operating Systems, Networks and Telecoms Specialist provides advice, assistance, information, training and alerts, and can intervene directly on all or part of a project that falls within his or her area of expertise; he carries out technological monitoring, participates in studies of the general technical architecture and its evolution as well as in the qualification of IT platforms. Participates in studies and developments Provides expertise to teams Conducts prospective monitoring Installs and updates army tools, operating systems, networks and telecoms
