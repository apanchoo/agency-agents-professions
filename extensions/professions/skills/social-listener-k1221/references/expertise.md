# Social listener

## Scope

The Social Listener provides reception, information and guidance for people in precarious situations. Receives requests by telephone or in person Helps identify needs to direct people to the appropriate services Ensures regular follow-up of cases to guarantee continued assistance Updates and provides detailed and confidential documentation of cases treated

## Related Job Titles

- Social Listener Coordinator
- Referent social listener

## Activities and Skills

- Welcoming, reassuring, guiding, informing a person and those around them
- Discuss with the person their feelings (discomfort, satisfaction, etc.) and refer them to the professional concerned (doctor, social service assistant, etc.)
- Assess a person's specific needs
- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Guide, support a person or family in their efforts
- Carry out specific social monitoring actions
- Refer to the social network or to accommodation structures appropriate to the person's situation
- Determine the need for psychological support, decide on arrangements with the person or refer them to a doctor, social service, paramedical staff
- Receive phone calls from people in distress, victims, etc. and offer them a listening service
- Emotionally supporting individuals in difficulty
- Regularly assess the needs of the person or family
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Manage crisis situations with tact and diplomacy
- Document individual interactions and progress
- Manage users' social files
- Write a report, an activity report
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Manage telephone reception
- Optimize the reception of different audiences
- Mobilize local resources for social projects
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Communicate and develop a helping relationship
- Actively listen to the needs and concerns of individuals
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Coordinate with other services for social assistance
- Working in a group, in a network
- Work, interact remotely
- Ensure the confidentiality of exchanges and information
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Use digital tools
