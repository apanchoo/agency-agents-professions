## Scope

The Geomatician is an expert in geographic information systems, ensures and coordinates the development and management of the geographic information system. Offers decision-making and action support tools in different areas of activity (Planning, Biodiversity, telecommunications, Transport and logistics, Energy, etc.) through the management and analysis of spatial data Organizes the acquisition, structuring, referencing of spatial and geographic data Sets up, develops and administers thematic databases (entry, integration and exploitation of data) evaluates the availability, timeliness and quality of data Product cartographic supports and statistics in response to needs through the identification and evaluation of cartographic needs, data integration and management of the geographic information system and processing (formatting) of geographic data Analysis, interpretation of geographic data to support decision-making by superposition and combination of data Designs and manages geographic information systems (GIS) adapted to user needs Develops spatial analysis tools to optimize the use of spatial and geographic data
