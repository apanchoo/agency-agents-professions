## Scope

The Travel Ticket Agent advises, makes reservations, issues transport tickets and is responsible for organizing trips for individuals or companies. Organizes and manages ticket reservations for individual or group trips Monitors customer files and updates travel information Negotiates with suppliers to obtain the best travel conditions Advises customers on travel options and assists them in choosing itineraries Manages changes, cancellations and refunds of reservations Maintains a watch on developments in the travel sector to offer the most suitable solutions
