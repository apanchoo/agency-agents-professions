# Buyer

## Scope

The Buyer plays a key role through organization and rigor in optimizing costs and product quality for companies. Negotiates the best prices with suppliers while ensuring the quality of products and services Analyzes the market to identify the best purchasing opportunities Manages relationships with suppliers to ensure effective and sustainable collaboration Participates in defining the company's purchasing strategy taking into account ethical and responsible purchasing issues Ensures order tracking and delivery compliance
