# 2D 3D Animator - animated films

## Scope

The 2D-3D animator is an image professional who brings characters, objects and environments to life in an animated film, using digital techniques in two dimensions (2D) or three dimensions (3D). Prepares and produces the animation of films in the shapes and movements of the characters, elements of the shot or digital visual effects Can produce special effects VFX - Visual effects in 3D Can coordinate a team Can supervise a phase of the creation chain of an animated film (pre-production, production or post-production)
