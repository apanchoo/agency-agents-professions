# Fighter in a land environment

## Scope

The land combatant is a soldier who participates in military operations to defend national interests and who can be deployed abroad for certain missions. Is specialized in combat operations on land, on foot or in vehicle Works in a team using a variety of weapons and equipment, Understands, acquires and maintains the operational fundamentals of the combatant Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to exercise his profession

- Implement staffing equipment
- Use hand-to-hand combat techniques adapted to high-intensity combat
- Use your weaponry and perform combat shots
- Maintain staffing equipment
- Managing stress and reactions in combat situations
- Implement security measures
- Communicate effectively with other members of the unit
- Communicate effectively by radio means
- Understand, acquire and maintain the operational fundamentals of the combatant
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Search for information
- Respect the engagement procedures
- Move quickly and silently in the field
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
