# Precast agent in the concrete industry

## Scope

The prefabrication agent in the concrete industry produces tailor-made concrete products (panels, slabs, stairs, walls, special parts for sewerage networks, etc.) which will then be installed on construction sites. Prepares the molds to pour concrete according to the customer's technical specifications Monitors the parameters of the casting machine to guarantee the quality of the concrete Unmolds the manufactured parts, verifies their conformity and carries out finishing touches on the concrete parts Handles and moves the manufactured products Ensures first level maintenance of production equipment to prevent breakdowns

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Collect samples for analysis
- Identify the configuration and sizing of a mold, the type of reinforcement, the pouring and unmolding method depending on the part to be manufactured
- Identify the different elements and reinforcements on a reinforcement plan in order to adapt the prefabricated reinforcements
- Pour the concrete into the mold or prestressing bench
- Prepare the equipment (cutting of steel wires and strands, etc.) and the material necessary to produce the product (prestressed, concrete part)
- Making steel reinforcements
- Carry out pouring and molding of raw materials
- Create prestressing parts using a prestressing bench (pre-slabs, beams, hollow core slabs, pre-lintels)
- Produce prestressing parts using molds
- Carry out surface treatment work on the mold (brushing, pleating, sanding, bush hammering, leveling)
- Carry out the finishing of a product, an article, a work
- Create the constituent elements of a mold (subassembly of metal, wood, PVC, reinforcement) in preparation for pouring the concrete
- Uncover the concrete part and dismantle the mold
- Assemble the parts of a structure on a construction site
- Position the steels, reinforcements, inserts, reservations, etc. in the mold or the prestressing bench.
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Evaluate a risky situation
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Visually check the quality of the manufactured part and carry out dimensional checks using suitable equipment
- Load, unload, handle products
- Identify a product
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Carry out product labeling
- Store a product
- Establish elements of an order
- Write a report, an activity report
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
