# Director of credit organization

## Scope

The director of a credit organization manages risks and supervises the credit granting processes while ensuring the profitability of the organization. Assesses customer creditworthiness and risks associated with loans to minimize financial losses Develops credit strategies in collaboration with other departments to support overall business objectives Implements a fraud detection approach and monitors the quality of the credit portfolio Analyzes market trends to adapt credit offerings to customers' evolving needs Provides training and development of teams to improve credit performance and expertise
