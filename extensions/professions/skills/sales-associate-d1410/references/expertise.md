# Sales associate

## Scope

The Sales Representative effectively boosts the company's offering to loyal customers or prospects. Develops and maintains relationships to maximize sales and customer satisfaction Identifies customer needs and proposes tailored solutions to improve their experience Negotiates contracts and commercial terms to optimize financial results Participates in increasing the company's market share Monitors sales and performance indicators to adjust commercial strategies Participates in trade shows and events to promote the company's products and services Collaborates with internal teams to ensure consistency of offerings and quality of customer service

## Activities and Skills

- Carry out quantitative and qualitative monitoring of prospecting actions: type of contact, profile of the interlocutor, nature of the exchanges (possibly via CRM/GRC software)
- Monitor sales figures and carry out associated reporting
- Apply and enforce legislation in the commercial services of one or more entities
- Manage complaints as well as commercial disputes
- Negotiate a contract
- Follow a contract, check compliance with contractual clauses
- Analyze customer needs to offer appropriate services
- Ensure the quality of relationships with customers
- Collaborate with internal teams to respond to customer requests
- Detect the needs and issues of prospects or customers in connection with the company's service or product offering
- Analyze calls for tenders and assess the company's ability to respond to them
- Design and promote a commercial offer
- Conduct commercial negotiations: prices, deadlines, promotions and conclude the sale (purchase orders, sales contracts, etc.)
- Create a list of prospects and design a prospecting plan: who to approach when and how
- Develop strategies to improve customer experience
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop a customer and prospect portfolio
- Perform a demonstration in front of a client or audience
- Get in touch with prospects via all possible and relevant media (telephone calls, trade shows, fairs, commercial partners, via professional social networks, etc.)
- Establish a prospecting tour plan (targeting, contacts, preparation of technical files)
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and adjust services
- Raise customer needs internally to adapt the offer to emerging issues
- Identify the strengths and weaknesses of the offer in contact with reality, to adapt the offer to the reality on the ground
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Implement targeted marketing campaigns
- Participate in operational marketing actions: hold a stand at trade shows, fairs, sporting events, carry out promotional operations in the field
- Participate in the precise definition and updating of the profile of prospects to be approached with regard to the sector of activity and the company profile
- Monitor market trends to stay competitive
- Ensure monitoring of performance indicators (KPI)
- Control the profitability of contracts and missions (particularly concerns salespeople selling staff delegation)
- Develop digital marketing strategies
- Carry out regular monitoring of commercial activity: number of customers met, commercial proposals written, presented, sold, follow-ups carried out, etc.
- Communicate effectively with the team and clients
- Convince yourself of the relevance of the products and services developed by the company
- Develop and manage a network of commercial partners likely to promote contact with prospects
