# Sports instructor

## Scope

Depending on his training, teaches and supervises sports and physical activities in one or more disciplines, according to the expectations of his audience (leisure, initiation, competition). Ensures the safety of people and compliance with regulations Depending on the size of the club, can participate in the management of the structure, the development of the activity, the organization of events, the management of partnerships Can carry out their activity in several clubs or sports structures.
