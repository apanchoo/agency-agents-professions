# Industrial cheese maker

## Scope

The industrial cheesemaker specializes in the production of cheese within a large dairy industry from cow's, goat's or sheep's milk according to food safety instructions and rules. Monitors the reception and preparation of milk Carries out cheese manufacturing operations (curdling, molding, salting, pressing and draining) Manages the different stages of refining depending on the types of cheese produced Carry out quality controls at different stages of manufacturing, refining and packaging Ensures cleaning, upkeep and maintenance of production equipment

## Related Job Titles

- Machine driver in the cheese industry

- Molding a cheese

- Select the raw materials to mobilize
- Check the temperature and hygrometry of cellars or storage rooms
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Identify the cause of an anomaly
- Monitor the supply of machines, facilities and the flow of materials or products
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Controlling the maturation of a cheese
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Ensure the availability of products and inputs
- Establish production and incident reports
