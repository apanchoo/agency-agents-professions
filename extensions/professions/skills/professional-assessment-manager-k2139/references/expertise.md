# Professional assessment manager

## Scope

The Professional Assessment Manager supports professionals in their career direction and progression. Evaluates professional and personal skills Proposes areas for improvement and development Organizes and leads individual or collective review sessions Writes detailed reports on skills and development prospects Advises on training or positions adapted to client profiles Assists clients in defining their professional project

## Related Job Titles

- Professional assessment speaker
- Professional assessment practitioner
- Professional assessment manager

## Activities and Skills

- Evaluate skills and detect potential
- Evaluate professional skills in a work context
- Organize skills development workshops
- Support the development and implementation of a career guidance project
- Support job search and reclassification
- Help candidates define or clarify their professional or training project
- Help professionals become aware of their strengths and areas for improvement
- Design an orientation program
- Define skills development needs
- Study the opportunities and constraints linked to the planned projects (labor market, available training)
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Identify the actions to be taken to achieve professional objectives (training, VAE, retraining)
- Conduct in-depth interviews to identify candidate skills, transferable skills and motivations
- Implement assessment tools (psychotechnical tests, personality questionnaires, practical exercises)
- Propose training, orientation and support actions adapted to the person's profile
- Propose professional retraining solutions
- Prepare and organize information actions on the labor market
- Present this summary document to the candidate and support them in their post-assessment procedures
- Present the objectives, steps and methods of the skills assessment
- Receive and listen to candidates to understand their professional background and their expectations
- Carry out assessment or professional guidance services (investigation, evaluation, taking tests, etc.) and report the results
- Carry out a skills assessment
- Write a final report covering the assessment process, the results of the evaluations and the established professional project
- Support learners in building their professional project
- Use various interview techniques (active listening, open questioning, reformulation)
- Design an information program
- Carry out psychotechnical tests
- Prepare detailed reports on training activities
- Inform people about training offers and services
- Implement career monitoring indicators
- Develop professional networks to facilitate employment

## Professional Approach
