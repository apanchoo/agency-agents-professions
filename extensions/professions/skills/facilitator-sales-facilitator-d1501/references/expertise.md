# Facilitator / Sales facilitator

## Scope

A sales facilitator is a professional responsible for boosting and promoting the sales of a product or range of products. Organizes and supervises in-store promotional operations Leads and trains teams in sales techniques and new products Monitors sales objectives and suggests improvement strategies Maintains good relationships with customers and improves the purchasing experience Analyzes sales performance and adjusts marketing actions accordingly

## Related Job Titles

- Sales Promotion Agent
- Network presenter
- Facilitator / Sales facilitator
- Sales facilitator
- Facilitator-demonstrator
- Demonstrator

## Activities and Skills

- Support points of sale to help them optimize their results thanks to field observations
- Lead a sales team
- Collaborate with teams to achieve sales objectives
- Train field teams on the specificities of products and marketing actions
- Motivate and train the team to improve customer service
- Organize and participate in various entertainment events (breakfasts, trade fairs, conferences, etc.) and various commercial meetings
- Assess business risks
- Ensure compliance of business practices
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Supervise the application of the commercial policy and the partnership or franchise contract
- Adapt a sales strategy to a target or a product
- Analyze market trends to anticipate customer needs
- Attend the launch of different products as well as new points of sale
- Monitor sales objectives with the team
- Develop action plans to achieve sales objectives
- Implement local marketing strategies
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Participate in the deployment of the partner network and the development of the company's turnover
- Present and promote a product or service
- Relay your company's communication to the points of sale in your network
- Relay marketing operations (point-of-sale advertisements, promotions, campaigns, etc.)
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Ensure that you broadcast a good image of your company to the points of sale in your network
- Analyze performance indicators after each marketing action in order to improve commercial operations and develop turnover
- Develop and manage a network of commercial partners likely to promote contact with prospects
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
