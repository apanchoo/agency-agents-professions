# Refiner / Reamender

## Scope

The Repairer, an essential craftsman in the preservation of fishing nets. Maintains and repairs damaged fishing nets to ensure their functionality Advises fishermen on best net maintenance practices May participate in the manufacturing of new nets according to specific needs

## Related Job Titles

- Fishing net repairer

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Analyze the product manufacturing sheet to meet customer expectations (product range, expected level of finish, expected production times, etc.)
- Adapt working methods according to orders
- Inspect threads for signs of wear and make preventive repairs
- Participate in the manufacture of new nets according to the needs of fishermen
- Repair and maintain fishing nets according to plans or traditional methods
- Select materials taking into account their environmental impact
- Assemble different elements of the fishing net (floats, weights, ropes)
- Install the nets in their environment of use (on fishing vessels, in sports facilities, etc.)
- Maintain fishing equipment in good condition
- Clean and maintain nets to extend their lifespan
- Carry out the remeshing of a net ensuring minimum or maximum compliance with the size of the mesh
- Use specific tools and different knotting techniques to secure the stitches
- Manage supply orders and raw material inventory
- Manage stocks of materials and tools necessary for repairing nets
- Process customer orders and organize production priorities
- Advise fishermen on the choice of materials and net maintenance practices
- Collaborate with other professionals for specific projects
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
