# Reader-corrector

## Scope

The reader-corrector carries out copy preparation work, guaranteeing the coherence and meaning of the texts as well as the quality and accessibility of the works entrusted to him. Performs critical reading of manuscripts Prepares the text for composition (coherence and accuracy of information) Makes proposals for stylistic improvement Exchanges and formulates critical feedback to the editor or author Guarantees the quality of spelling and typographical correction of works Checks the translation of certain texts
