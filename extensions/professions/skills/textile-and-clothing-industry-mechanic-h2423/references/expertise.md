# Textile and clothing industry mechanic

## Scope

Qualified technician in industrial sewing who is proficient in the use of various sewing machines and textile production equipment. Reads and interprets sewing patterns and technical sheets Adjusts and maintains industrial sewing machines, such as overlockers, stitchers, or embroidery machines Assembles pieces of fabric Performs finishing touches such as fitting buttons, zippers, bias binding, and other decorative or functional elements Controls the quality and conformity of the products manufactured

## Related Job Titles

- Manufacturing agent in the textile industry
- Production agent in the textile industry
- Clothing industry mechanic
- Mechanic
- Whole piece mechanic in the clothing industry
- Serial mechanic in the clothing industry

## Activities and Skills

- Develop your skills in preventive maintenance
- Assemble the parts and sub-assemblies of textile articles by stitching according to the technical sheets
- Position the parts according to the marks for assembly/assembly of the item
- Carry out assembly and assembly work
- Adjust production machines to optimize product quality
- Adjust production machine parameters
- Check the assembly of a piece of clothing
- Make corrections or repairs to a work
- Check and maintain the cleanliness of production and control equipment
- Evaluate the risks associated with the use of certain machines
- Observe safety instructions when using machines
- Monitor the proper functioning of machines
- Analyze machine performance to propose improvements
- Ensure the conformity of finished products
- Check the stitching layout and tension, make the necessary repairs and remove non-compliant items
- Manage spare parts inventory
- Develop partnerships with suppliers
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Collaborate with teams to optimize processes
- Arguing technical choices during meetings
- Arguing for the adoption of new technologies
- Communicate effectively with supervisors about progress and issues
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
