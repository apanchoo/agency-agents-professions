# Driving school management assistant

## Scope

The Driving School Management Assistant plays a key role in the coordination and administration of the activities of a driving school, thanks to its collaboration with the various stakeholders of a driving school (teachers, trainers, educational directors or even unit managers). Ensures the reception and information of the customers of a driving school Creates the administrative and financial files of candidates on dedicated platforms and software Supports the administrative process of candidates for driving license exams Organizes and supervises the schedules of training and exam sessions Ensures compliance with the regulations in force in the field of driving. Physically accompanies candidates for driving license exams
