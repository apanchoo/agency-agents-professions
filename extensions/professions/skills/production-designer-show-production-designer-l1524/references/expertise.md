# Production designer / Show production designer

## Scope

The production designer creates and arranges stage spaces in conjunction with the director or director. Designs and defines the artistic line of the sets based on the script and artistic direction Collaborates closely with the director and technicians to adapt the sets to technical and artistic constraints Supervises the construction and installation of set elements at the location of the performance or filming Ensures management of the budget allocated to the sets and negotiates with suppliers Can produce models or 3D plans to preview the sets Ensures compliance with safety standards in construction and use decorations

## Related Job Titles

- Production designer / Audiovisual production designer

- Coordinate the technical teams on set

- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Design visual communication supports
- Define the artistic line of a decor
- Arguing design choices in front of production
- Ensure technical or artistic monitoring in order to be a force for proposals in terms of innovation
- Maintain constant technological and artistic monitoring
- Create a sketch or model of sets and accessories
- Document set assembly and dismantling procedures
- Use 3D design software for sets
- Adapt the settings according to the scenario and its possible modifications
- Assemble a decor
- Choose suitable materials for decorations
- Compose film sets and shots
- Design the layout of performance stages (theatre, television sets, cinema)
- Implement decorative elements
- Optimize stage space for different configurations
- Plan the assembly and dismantling of sets
- Ensure compliance of sets with artistic guidelines
- Ensure maintenance of the sets during the show
- Resolve technical issues related to scenery
- Ensure compliance of decors with safety standards
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Supervise the construction of sets
- Ensure safety at the filming or performance location
- Build a network of reliable suppliers
- Evaluate the costs and time required for building sets
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Promote the reuse or recycling of recovered materials
- Integrate eco-design principles into processes
- Provide solutions to different artistic and technical requests
- Create a quote
- Negotiate with suppliers of accessories and materials
- Arguing design choices in meetings
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
