## Scope

The radiation protection specialist within the Ministry of the Armed Forces is responsible for the implementation and monitoring of protection measures against ionizing radiation within military units and installations under the Ministry. Ensures the safety of personnel and the environment by applying current regulations Identifies the risks linked to ionizing radiation Implements preventive measures to protect personnel and the environment Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out their profession
