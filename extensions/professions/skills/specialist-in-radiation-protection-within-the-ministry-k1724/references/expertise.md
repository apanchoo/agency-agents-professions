# Specialist in radiation protection within the Ministry of the Armed Forces

## Scope

The radiation protection specialist within the Ministry of the Armed Forces is responsible for the implementation and monitoring of protection measures against ionizing radiation within military units and installations under the Ministry. Ensures the safety of personnel and the environment by applying current regulations Identifies the risks linked to ionizing radiation Implements preventive measures to protect personnel and the environment Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out their profession

## Related Job Titles

- High-level expert in radiological protection
- Specialist in conveying nuclear weapon elements
- Specialist in radiological protection
- Radiation protection specialist
- Specialist in transport of nuclear weapon elements

## Activities and Skills

- Working with civil and military authorities
- Participate in the design and implementation of radiation protection devices
- Design procedures, protocols, operating methods
- Ensure the maintenance and repair of measurement and protection equipment against radiation
- Maintain and repair safety equipment
- Implement prevention and protection measures
- Plan and coordinate security operations
- Respect the safety rules and procedures in force
- Analyze and assess potential risks for forces, installations or sensitive equipment
- Identify risks linked to ionizing radiation
- Implement preventive measures to protect staff and the environment
- Organize and coordinate radiation protection interventions within your unit
- Communicate with supervisory authorities
- Communicate with the different stakeholders about the risks linked to radiation
- Communicate about the protective measures to be adopted
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Managing stressful work situations
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Update your knowledge of new threats
- Keep informed of the latest advances in radiation protection
