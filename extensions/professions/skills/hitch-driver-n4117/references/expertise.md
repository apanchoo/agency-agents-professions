# Hitch driver

## Scope

The driver combines his passion for horses with the use of traditional transport in line with societal and environmental developments. Drives teams of one or more horses to transport people, goods or equipment Maintains equipment and ensures the well-being of horses Ensures the safety of passengers and horses during journeys Can work in agriculture, forestry, community maintenance, school transport or tourism Can participate in events and ceremonies, offering a unique transport service Can carry out guided tours and comment on tourist routes

## Related Job Titles

- Coachman / Coachwoman
- Equestrian tourism driver
- Longshoreman leader

## Activities and Skills

- Accompany groups on guided tours or outdoor outings
- Promote good environmental practices
- Provide routine care for equines
- Educating equines with a view to carrying out equestrian activities
- Harness the horses and hitch the vehicle
- Respect animal welfare
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Monitor animal health before and after runs
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Clean and maintain hitch equipment and vehicles
- Ensure customer safety by following appropriate procedures
- Communicate safety instructions clearly
- Plan routes and activities taking into account weather and terrain conditions
- Adapt driving according to the type of coupling and the activities to be carried out
- Ensuring the safety and comfort of passengers during transport
- Select and prepare horses according to the type of carriage and the activity to be carried out
- Check the equipment (harness, bridle, etc.) to ensure that it is correctly adjusted
- Respect scheduled times and routes
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Identify a tourist route
- Raise visitor awareness about environmental protection
- Using English in a professional context
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Respect road safety rules

## Professional Approach
