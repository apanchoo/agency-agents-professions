# Systems and networks engineer for connected territories

## Scope

The Connected Territories Systems and Networks Engineer is a pillar of urban innovation, working for robust and intelligent digital infrastructures that transform our sites and cities into safer, more efficient and more connected spaces for all citizens. Develops the digital infrastructures of connected territories Designs digital architectures and deploys networks adapted to the needs of these territories Ensures the management and maintenance of existing infrastructures Ensures the security and integrity of data on the networks Optimizes the performance of systems to guarantee fluid connectivity

- Advise development, production and user teams
- Use technological monitoring (materials, new standards, regulatory constraints)
- Improve an information system
- Analyze the performance of an information system
- Allocate network logistics and hardware resources
- Design computer networks

- Deploy wireless network solutions
- Determine technical choices of software and hardware architecture and select technologies, hardware, software, configurations
