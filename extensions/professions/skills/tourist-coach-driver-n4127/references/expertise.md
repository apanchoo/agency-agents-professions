# Tourist coach driver

## Scope

The tourist driver leads and accompanies groups on tourist tours. Ensures the safety and comfort of passengers throughout the journey Assists the group leader and may be required to provide brief explanations of the interest of the route Manages the logistical aspects of the excursions, such as schedules, itineraries and customs formalities May make reservations for attractions or restaurants Ensures the maintenance and cleanliness of the vehicle used

## Related Job Titles

- Accompanying driver / Tourist escort driver
- Grand touring coach driver

## Activities and Skills

- Use on-board communications equipment and audiovisual equipment
- Perform first level interview
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Clearly communicate safety and evacuation instructions from the coach
- Clearly present the safety equipment on board the vehicle and their operation
- Check passenger baggage labeling
- Loading, unloading baggage
- Adapt driving to road conditions
- Ensure the safety of passengers during boarding and disembarkation
- Determine an itinerary based on deadlines, traffic characteristics and the tourist circuit to be carried out
- Take charge of a vehicle with its equipment and accessories
- Transporting passengers
- Use modern navigation equipment adapted to the vehicle
- Make payments on behalf of the company during the trip and keep the supporting documents
- Managing currencies during an international trip or tour
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the proper completion of services ordered from service providers
- Check the presence of the necessary documents for the trip or tour
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Create and personalize a tour at the customer's request
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Communicate effectively with passengers and/or their attendants
- Promote ecological practices among passengers such as waste sorting
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Respect the rules relating to driving times, rest and amplitude applicable to touring drivers
- Strictly respect scheduled times while ensuring safety
- Take regulatory obligations into account when organizing the route and inform customers thereof
- Check the presence of the necessary documents on board the vehicle

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Listen, show empathy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
