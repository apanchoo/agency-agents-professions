# Director of the work environment

## Scope

The Work Environment Director ensures the management and optimization of workspaces to improve well-being and productivity. Defines and implements the workspace layout strategy based on employee needs and company objectives Oversees the maintenance and improvement of facilities to ensure a safe and pleasant working environment Collaborates with different departments to identify their specific needs and adapt spaces accordingly Manages the budget allocated to work environments and optimizes costs while maintaining high quality Integrates ecological and sustainable solutions in the management of workspaces to promote environmental responsibility Analyzes regularly the use of spaces to propose continuous and innovative improvements

## Related Job Titles

- Responsible for work environments

## Activities and Skills

- Arrange a position and working conditions
- Foster an inclusive work environment
- Identify the necessary adjustments to a workstation
- Optimize the ergonomics of workstations to improve health, safety and performance
- Promote an inclusive corporate culture
- Prevent and limit the risks generated by digital technology on health, well-being and the environment
- Advise on the adaptation of workstations for employees with disabilities or medical restrictions
- Identify and assess risks linked to working conditions (noise, chemicals, stress, etc.)
- Work in collaboration with staff representatives, CHSCT (Hygiene, Safety and Working Conditions Committees), and other bodies
- Implement technological solutions to improve efficiency
- Integrate ergonomics, integrity and repairability criteria into its design
- Analyze working conditions and propose improvements
- Create or modify diagrams, drawings, plans of works, installations, developments according to standards and changes in constraints
- Carry out the technical layout of a work space
- Adapt living spaces to specific needs
- Design interior designs with project managers
- Create ergonomic workspaces adapted to the needs of employees, with an emphasis on mobility and flexibility
- Determine renovation or development work on a structure
- Optimize the use of spaces
- Define risk prevention measures
- Identify the means of protection/prevention of risks on site
- Carry out regular visits to premises and workshops to identify potential risks and assess working conditions
- Oversee the acquisition and distribution of office supplies, furniture and other necessary resources
- Supervise regular maintenance of equipment
- Detect non-conformities in materials or products, as well as materials and the working environment, and carry out first-level maintenance
- Study working conditions to propose ergonomic improvements
- Analyze office supply needs and manage supplies
- Participate in defining the schedule, overall budget and deadlines
- Ensure the interface with the various stakeholders in the work project (client, public administration, design office, architectural firms, control bodies, etc.)
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage service contracts and suppliers
- Receive and study the elements of the technical file (architect's plans, study report, forecast budget)
- Supervise the maintenance, safety and cleanliness of buildings and work spaces
- Ensure regulatory compliance of installations
- Monitor regulatory developments in CSR
- Evaluate the environmental impact of purchases
- Evaluate the environmental impact of waste treatment methods
- Evaluate the environmental impact of equipment
- Integrate ecological solutions into projects
- Analyze development needs and propose solutions
- Collaborate with other professionals for specific projects
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Apply ergonomic rules to prevent injuries
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
