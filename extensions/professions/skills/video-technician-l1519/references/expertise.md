# Video technician

## Scope

The Video Technician is a versatile and essential professional who installs, implements and operates the equipment of the audiovisual production chain: multi-camera production control rooms, broadcast control room, reporting and set cameras, media servers, sound recording and mixing equipment, satellite reception station, mixing consoles, audio and video post-production stations. Manages the technical implementation and operational aspects during audiovisual productions for the capture and retransmission of events, for production, broadcast and editing Ensures the installation and adjustment of video equipment before filming Operates the equipment during recordings, ensuring image quality Performs preventive and corrective maintenance of video equipment Collaborates with production teams to plan specific technical needs Can carry out simple video editing and retouching Can contribute to the archiving and management of video content produced

## Related Job Titles

- Title bench technician

- Configure network and telecom equipment
- Manage video archives and their indexing
- Collect and qualify data, signals, measurements, images, transmitted or emitted
- Propose technical solutions to improve video capture
- Setting up cameras
- Install stage lighting equipment
- Wiring equipment
- Control the audio and video quality of live productions
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Analyze technical needs for video installations

- Monitor live video feed
- Check network cabling
- Check images for compliance with broadcast standards
- Correct technical defects in a video recording
- Ensure the loading and unloading of recording media, their packaging
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
