# Operator of automatic binding machines

## Scope

The Automatic Binding Machine Operator is in charge of the production line of documents to be bound. Operates and monitors automatic binding machines to assemble books and documents Adjusts the machines according to the specifications of the work to be performed Controls the quality of finished products to ensure their compliance with standards Performs first-level maintenance of the machines to ensure their proper functioning May coordinate a team of technicians to optimize the production flow Ensures compliance with safety and hygiene standards throughout the binding process
