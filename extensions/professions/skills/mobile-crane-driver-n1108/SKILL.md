## Scope

The mobile crane driver, a key role in lifting and handling operations on construction sites. Operates mobile cranes to lift, move and position heavy loads Performs first level maintenance of the crane and verifies its proper operation before each use Meets strict safety standards to prevent accidents on site Collaborates with site teams to plan lifting operations Adapts lifting techniques according to the specifics of loads and site conditions May train and supervise other less experienced crane operators
