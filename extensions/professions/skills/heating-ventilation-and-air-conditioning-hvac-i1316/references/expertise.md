# Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC) Maintenance Technician

## Scope

The HVAC Maintenance Technician provides preventive and curative maintenance of heating, ventilation and air conditioning equipment in buildings to ensure comfort and air quality. Controls, monitors and regularly maintains heating, ventilation and air conditioning equipment Detects, diagnoses breakdowns and carries out necessary repairs Advises customers on best practices for the use and maintenance of systems Optimizes the performance of installations to ensure their energy efficiency Can intervene urgently to restore the functionality of equipment Can manage the maintenance of specific infrastructures, such as data centers, in order to ensure optimal conditions of temperature, humidity and air quality, necessary for the operation of IT equipment

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate technical teams for interventions
- Train customers on the optimal use of heating systems
- Troubleshoot advanced technical issues
- Analyze maintenance data
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Install heating control systems
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Install air filtration systems
- Implement regulation devices for thermal installations
- Connect devices to the electrical panel
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Change or repair a defective part or assembly
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Manage maintenance files
- Refurbish air filtration systems
- Carry out troubleshooting of refrigeration or air conditioning installations
- Carry out pre-settings and commissioning of the heating installation (electrical circuits, burners, hydraulic components, regulation systems, etc.)
- Repair faulty installations based on a diagnosis
- Manage emergencies and service calls
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Check availability of emergency equipment
- Check pressures and temperatures, heat exchanges, etc. and adjust the settings
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Ensure the application and respect of hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental rules (waste management, noise pollution, etc.)

- Open, track and close a ticket (intervention report)

- Carry out a technical assistance service
- Propose solutions to improve energy efficiency
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- React effectively in the event of a technical incident
