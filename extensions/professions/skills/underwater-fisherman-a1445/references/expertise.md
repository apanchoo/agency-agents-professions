# Underwater fisherman

## Scope

The Underwater Fisherman dives underwater to capture aquatic species using specific techniques Identifies and selects fishing areas rich in resources Uses suitable diving equipment for deep interventions Complies with environmental regulations for sustainable fishing Can process and prepare marine products on board the vessel Manages the maintenance of diving and fishing equipment

## Related Job Titles

- Corailleur / Corailleuse
- Sea urchin
- Diving fisherman
- Underwater tubist

## Activities and Skills

- Collect or take samples of fauna or flora from the marine environment (corals, plants, etc.)
- Collect and record fishing catch data
- Plan underwater fishing trips
- Capturing specific marine species
- Cook shellfish and crustaceans while respecting the nature of the product
- Prepare fish for storage
- Harvesting seafood
- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions
- Use diving equipment (wetsuit, fins, snorkel) and maintain it after each dive
- Carry out basic maintenance of the boat
- Maintain fishing equipment in good condition
- Load, unload, handle products
- Packaging products
- Sorting fish products
- Navigate precisely to intervention locations
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Managing natural resources sustainably
- Observe the marine ecosystem to choose the best fishing spots
- Use environmentally friendly fishing techniques
- Marketing harvested or fished products
- Sell products directly to consumers or to a network of local merchants or restaurateurs
- Collaborate with other underwater fishermen
- Communicate effectively with crew in the event of an emergency
- Communicate underwater via signals
- Adapt fishing techniques to marine conditions
- Comply with maritime regulations
- Respect safety standards during interventions

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be open to changes
- Show perseverance

## Products, tools and materials

- Characteristics of seafood products

## Areas of expertise

- Regulated fishing zones
- Fisheries resource management

## Standards and processes

- Health standards
- Nautical chart reading
- Safety and diving rules
- Local fishing regulations
- Diving Equipment Maintenance Procedures
- Storage arrangements for food products

## Professional techniques

- Snorkeling

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
