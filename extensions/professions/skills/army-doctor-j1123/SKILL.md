## Scope

The Army Doctor has a crucial role in measuring the state of health and well-being of soldiers in all situations. Provides medical care and emergencies for military personnel in barracks and on operations Conducts prevention and public health programs within the armed forces by assessing needs Participates in the medical training of military personnel Manages medical resources and health equipment in the military environment Anticipates risks, dangers and threats in order to preserve the operational capacity of the military Collaborates with other specialists for the medical monitoring of troops May participate in humanitarian or peacekeeping missions under the aegis of international organizations
