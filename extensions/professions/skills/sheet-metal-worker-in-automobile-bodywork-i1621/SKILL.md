## Scope

The automotive bodywork worker carries out work on the removable and non-removable elements of vehicles, particularly for the reshaping of bodywork elements. Replaces, adjusts and regulates all types of elements: removable, welded, glued or crimped Carries out the replacement or repair of glazing Carries out upholstery work related to the bodywork (seats and various trims) Masters straightening and welding techniques to guarantee the quality of the repairs Uses the appropriate technical documentation for each intervention Ensures the layout and maintenance of his workstation and tools Follows the company's quality procedures to ensure compliance of repairs Establishes workshop documents for monitoring repairs

## Skills to Apply

- Assemble the sheets by welding, riveting and gluing
- Install and remove removable elements
- Examine glazing to determine whether repairability is possible, and repair it using methods and products appropriate to the nature of the glazing
- Straighten an aluminum bodywork element
- Repairing impacts and cracks in glazing
- Apply standards relating to the prevention of electrical risks
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
