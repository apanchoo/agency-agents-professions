# Health Insurance Delegate (DAM)

## Scope

Its objective is to provide better care while spending better! The Health Insurance Delegate (DAM) is at the service of health professionals and establishments, to enable them to promote medical control of expenses, by sharing useful information and supporting their application. Contributes to the implementation of medical control programs built with the aim of limiting the evolution of health expenditure Relays the measures taken in favor of public health, in particular via prevention campaigns Develops professional cooperation useful for controlling the care system between health professionals, self-employed people and/or health establishments Participates in improving the efficiency of the health system

## Related Job Titles

- Health Insurance Delegate - DAM

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure the promotion of good medical practices resulting from the recommendations of health authorities (HAS, ANSM, learned societies, etc.)
- Supporting health personnel and users towards practices promoting the development of prevention
- Ensure regular visits to health professionals (doctors, pharmacists, paramedics, health establishments, etc.)
- Advise on regulatory and technical aspects
- Contribute to information and promotion actions for health services and access to care
- Disseminate to health professionals the guidelines for good medical practices, treatment conditions and conventional devices
- Edit concrete elements reflecting the practice of the health professional, allowing him to position himself in relation to his colleagues
- Induce an evolution in the practice of health professionals towards better compliance with standards and recommendations
- Raising awareness about mental health and wellbeing
- Promote public health programs
- Write public health reports
- Write a report, an activity report
- Apply current legislation and regulations
- Analyze public health data
- Evaluate the impact of public health interventions
- Implement public health policies
- Take charge of public health cases
- Advise on the terms of coverage by health insurance or mutual insurance companies
- Support health professionals and users towards improving the relevance of care
- Ensure the interface between health professionals and CPAM services
- Inform and raise public awareness about health behaviors and practices to prevent diseases
- Raise awareness among different stakeholders about public health policy, prevention and health promotion
- Maintain regulatory and technical monitoring
