## Scope

The Cooper, traditional craftsman and partner of winegrowers, shapes wooden barrels intended for the production and conservation of wines and alcohols. Selects and prepares the wood necessary for the construction of the barrels Makes and repairs barrels, barrels and other wooden containers according to the required specifications Ensures the watertightness of the containers by making the necessary adjustments Can customize the barrels according to specific customer requests

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Cut the staves (staves) to precise dimensions
- Proceed with assembling and heating the barrels
- Select the trees or wood needed to make the barrels
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Checking the tightness of a cooperage product
- Carry out packing and packaging of barrels for shipping
- Analyze customer needs to offer appropriate services
- Establish partnerships with local and international winegrowers
