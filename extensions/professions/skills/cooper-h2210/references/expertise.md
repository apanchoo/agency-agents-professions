# Cooper

## Scope

The Cooper, traditional craftsman and partner of winegrowers, shapes wooden barrels intended for the production and conservation of wines and alcohols. Selects and prepares the wood necessary for the construction of the barrels Makes and repairs barrels, barrels and other wooden containers according to the required specifications Ensures the watertightness of the containers by making the necessary adjustments Can customize the barrels according to specific customer requests

## Related Job Titles

- Production agent in cooperage
- Fustier / Fustière
- Cooper / Wooden cooper
- Cooper / Manufacturing cooper
- Repairing cooper
