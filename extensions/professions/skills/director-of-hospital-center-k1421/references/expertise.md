# Director of hospital center

## Scope

The hospital center director supervises the management and strategic development of health establishments. Directs and coordinates all services of a hospital establishment Defines and implements the establishment's policy in collaboration with medical and administrative authorities Optimizes the management of human, financial and material resources Ensures the quality and safety of care offered to patients Develops partnerships and innovative projects to improve the healthcare offering Represents the hospital in external relations and negotiations with partners

## Related Job Titles

- Director of regional hospital center
- Director of specialized hospital center
- Director of University Hospital Center (CHU)
- Director of public hospital
- Director of thermal hospital
- Deputy director / Deputy director of hospital center
- Deputy director / Deputy director of regional hospital center
- Deputy Director of a specialized hospital center

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate the intervention of teams of specialists
- Coordinate the intervention of multidisciplinary teams
- Coordinate activities between different departments
- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Evaluate staff skills and performance
- Optimize resource management
- Ensure personnel management
- Design and manage a human resources management policy
- Manage the daily operations of a hospital center
- Organize the coordination of health professionals (internal and external) working in the structure
- Taking into account innovations in medico-social care
- Determine a communication and promotion strategy for the establishment
- Manage public relations
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Assess medical equipment needs
- Secure equipment and plan procedures to ensure data security
- Design strategies to improve the quality of services
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Master hospital circuits and financing, the public procurement code and tender procedures
- Supervise compliance with health and safety standards
- Promote sustainable health practices within the hospital
- Have an overview of the operation of the establishment
- Design management tools, indicators, dashboards
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Develop and manage relationships or partnerships
- Develop a business development strategy
- Optimize a company's costs and expenses
- Optimize hospital processes to improve efficiency
- Organize and manage the prevention and treatment of incidents and malfunctions
- Communicate with health authorities
- Raise team awareness of the issues of Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR)
- Act quickly in the event of a critical failure to minimize the impact on hospital services
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Respect hospital safety and hygiene standards
