# Interior designer

## Scope

Designs and arranges interior spaces according to their use and customer requests. Develops projects taking into account technical and budgetary constraints in order to create functional, aesthetic and comfortable spaces. Plans and coordinates the work.

## Related Job Titles

- Decorator Architect
- Decorator assistant / Interior decorator assistant
- Interior designer/designer
- Architectural Advisor
- Interior decorator
- Environmental designer
