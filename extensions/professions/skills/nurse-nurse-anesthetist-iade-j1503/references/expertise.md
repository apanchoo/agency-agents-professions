# Nurse / Nurse anesthetist (IADE)

## Scope

Works within multidisciplinary teams, in collaboration and under the responsibility of intensivist anesthetists. Performs specific care and technical procedures in the areas of anesthesia, emergency, resuscitation and pain management. Supports the well-being, monitoring and safety of the patient from the start of the surgical procedure until the recovery phase. May contribute to training and research in his area of ​​expertise.

## Related Job Titles

- Qualified Nurse Anesthetist / State Qualified Nurse Anesthetist (IADE)

## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Assess patient pain and adjust care
- Participate in a surgical procedure
- Providing paramedical care
- Prepare a patient for a medical procedure
- Carry out nursing care
- Perform or participate in the performance of locoregional anesthesia according to medical protocol
- Monitor the patient's clinical condition and transmit information to the medical team
- Monitor a person's health
- Monitor the patient during the awakening phase and adapt the treatment according to needs
- Check the conditions of the intervention
- Complete technical documents during a surgical procedure and transmit them to the relevant contact
- Prepare the anesthesia site according to the operating program and the patient's needs
- Prepare anesthetic products according to the instructions of the anesthetist and resuscitator
- Check the operation of biomedical and monitoring devices
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Dispose of sensitive waste
- Provide technical assistance to the medical team
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Managing an emergency situation
- Collect data from a medical-technical device
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate self-control
- Show perseverance
- Show responsiveness

## Areas of expertise

- Use of patient management software
- Characteristics of the operating room
- Painless childbirth techniques
- Pharmacology
- Intensive care and resuscitation
- Use of medical software
- Methods for identifying signs and degree of pain
- Post-Interventional Care Room (SSPI)

## Standards and processes

- Vigilance circuit
- Anesthesia Site Check Procedures
- Pain management
- Infection prevention
