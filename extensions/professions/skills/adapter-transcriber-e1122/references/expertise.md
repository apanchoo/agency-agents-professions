# Adapter Transcriber

## Scope

The Adapting Transcriber is an expert in transcribing, orally and in writing, dialogues or documents to make information accessible to all. Provides real-time transcription services for debates, conferences, meetings or interviews requiring immediate accessibility Adapts specialized documents for accessible formats, such as Braille or Complétée Spoken French (LPC) Ensures the accuracy and confidentiality of information transmitted Collaborates with teams to ensure the accuracy of transcriptions May specialize in specific areas such as medical or legal
