# Boat broker

## Scope

The boat broker is an independent commercial intermediary, specializing in the sale, purchase and rental of pleasure boats and yachts, who connects a boat owner wishing to rent or sell his property, with potential clients or buyers. Evaluates boats and advises customers on the best rental, purchase or sale options Organizes boat visits and sometimes sea trials Accompanies customers during visits to provide them with advice on the option that best meets their needs/expectations Negotiates and finalizes boat and yacht sale transactions and ensures administrative and legal follow-up of transactions Develops a network of contacts in the nautical sector Can manage the rental of yachts for specific events or periods
