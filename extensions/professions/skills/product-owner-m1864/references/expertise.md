# Product Owner

## Scope

The Product Owner, a key player in project management, manages the development of a product using an agile method. Intermediate between users and development teams maximizing product value Prioritizes and manages the product backlog to meet user needs Collaborates closely with the development team to ensure feature delivery Analyzes user feedback and adjusts possible improvements Ensures communication between stakeholders to ensure alignment of objectives Participates in defining acceptance criteria for deliverables and ensures they are met
