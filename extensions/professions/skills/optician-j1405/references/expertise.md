# Optician

## Scope

The optician is a vision professional who provides optical correction equipment to people with visual disorders. Advises customers in the choice of frames, glasses and contact lenses adapted to their needs Measures visual acuity and carries out the adjustment and fitting of glasses to ensure optimal comfort Performs vision checks to determine the necessary optical corrections Manages orders for optical products and monitors stocks Supervises and trains less experienced staff within the store
