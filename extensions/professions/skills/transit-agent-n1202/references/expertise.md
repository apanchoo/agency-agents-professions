# Transit Agent

## Scope

The transit agent manages international goods transport operations (import and export). Organizes and supervises the transport of goods internationally Coordinates all stages of the transport service with the various players in maritime, air and land transport Ensures compliance with customs and security regulations Manages the hazards that may arise during transit Manages the documentation necessary for the transit of goods Contributes to guaranteeing a service that meets the objectives in terms of quality of service, customer satisfaction and profitability.

## Activities and Skills

- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Check data compliance
- Provide activity traceability tools
- Complete and maintain activity monitoring tools (dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Assess the risks associated with the transport of specific goods
- Identify incidents at the different stages, impacting deadlines and compliance of transit operations
- Coordinate and control associated services (customs, storage, packaging, handling, etc.)
- Identify and control storage areas for goods in transit
- Optimize a goods loading plan
- Contribute to the development of a transport plan in an international context
- Coordinate transport operations for specific products
- Create and verify freight transport documents (Air way, Bill of Landing, etc.)
- Make a freight reservation in different modes of transport
- Monitor the delivery of goods, inform the customer about the progress of the service and present solutions in the event of anomalies
- Process delivery information and adjust plans accordingly
- Check documents related to the international movement of goods (customs invoice, documentary credit, etc.)
- Manage transport and operations monitoring documents
- Prepare, monitor or control invoicing for transit or customs operations: supplier payment, customs taxes, Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB)
- Apply the defined framework to reduce the environmental impact during services
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Analyze and interpret customer demand and needs (internal/external) based on the information provided
- Apply a price list
- Calculate the margins, profitability of a product or activity
- Collect a customer's opinion and comments
- Communicate, read and write technical documents, reports, notes in English
- Interact on a daily basis with the network of agents or correspondents
- Participate in the circulation and sharing of information within the team
- Managing emergencies and unforeseen events in transit
- Maintain regulatory and technical monitoring
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
