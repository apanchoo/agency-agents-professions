# Health mediator

## Scope

The health mediator closely supports people in vulnerable situations to facilitate their health journey, he also raises awareness among health system stakeholders about the public's obstacles to accessing it. Ensures the interface between the public and the health system Supports people in their journey of access to rights, prevention and care Mobilizes stakeholders in the health system and raises their awareness of the specificities of the public Promotes the development of individual and collective power to act Alerts on dysfunctions encountered in access to the health system

## Related Job Titles

- Community health mediator
- Mediator in intercultural health
- Peer health mediator

## Activities and Skills

- Develop and manage a network of partners and service providers
- Get in touch with the population and promote initiatives (consultation meetings, consultations, etc.)
- Promote exchange relationships and a climate of cooperation
- Regulate exchanges between participants in a debate
- Accompany and support people in their actions and their development
- Welcoming, reassuring, guiding, informing a person and those around them
- Ensure an interface function between the public and the health system
- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Promote the development of the power to act, through individual or collective projects
- Maintain and facilitate the inclusion of supported people to avoid social isolation
- Mobilize your lived experience with your peers and professionals
- Participate in the design and monitoring of an individualized project
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Facilitate access to care for vulnerable populations
- Develop communication strategies adapted to different audiences
- Promote public health programs
- Analyze the life context of a person or family
- Facilitate access to support systems
- Inform the public about available health resources
- Develop partnerships with local actors
- Implement educational activities as part of projects in the social, medico-social and health sector
- Know how to work cross-functionally
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Identify and understand emotions in yourself and others
- Inform and raise public awareness about health behaviors and practices to prevent diseases
- Organize your work within a multi-professional team
- Adapt your behavior and your professional practice to situations
- Promote values of respect and tolerance
- Respect the rules of confidentiality, ethics and impartiality
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Use communication tools adapted to the needs of the person
- Participate in continuing education sessions and professional conferences
