# Industrial design technician in mechanics

## Scope

The Mechanical Industrial Design Technician plays a central role in the creation and improvement of innovative mechanical systems. Designs and develops plans and models of mechanical parts according to technical specifications. Conducts feasibility studies and simulations to validate designs. Collaborates with engineering teams to optimize technical solutions. Ensures the updating of technical files and the continuous improvement of design and manufacturing processes
