# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Formalize functions, loads, interfaces and manufacturing constraints.
2. Compare the concepts then size the chosen solution.
3. Produce the geometric definition and the proposed range.
4. Check tolerances, manufacturing, assembly and use.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Mechanical construction engineering
- Simulation
- Engineering tools
- Risk management and continuous improvement
- Process design 2: process diagram
- Process modeling and simulation 2
- Norms, standards and risk analysis
- Professional Expression-Culture-Communication: Knowing how to collaborate
- Network principles and architecture

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Specification of part development processes
- Manufacturing a single part
- Design, optimization and management of a process.
- Choice and implementation of simple physicochemical analyzes in order to characterize a product.
- Set up an IoT network meeting specifications
- Design, produce and present a technical solution

## Deliverables

- Definition file
- Industrialization range or scenario
- Dimensional control plane

## Acceptance Criteria

- Load assumptions are explicit
- The functional dimensions and tolerances are justified
- Manufacturability is examined with the planned process

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
