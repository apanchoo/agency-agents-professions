# Designer-illustrator

## Scope

The Designer-Illustrator designs and creates original graphic works that translate ideas, stories or concepts. Creates illustrations for a variety of media and media, such as books, magazines, advertisements, and websites Collaborates with clients or publishers to understand their needs and adapts their artistic style accordingly Uses drawing and image editing software to refine their designs Participates in exhibitions or trade shows to promote their work and meet potential clients Manages the copyrights of their works to protect their work and negotiate publishing or reproduction contracts May specialize in a genre or field particular such as comics, caricatures or visual communication
