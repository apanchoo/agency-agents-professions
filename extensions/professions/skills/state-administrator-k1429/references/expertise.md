# State Administrator

## Scope

The State administrator is a senior civil servant belonging to a senior management body of the State civil service, with an interministerial vocation, and reports to the Prime Minister. Carries out missions of design, implementation and evaluation of public policies Ensures senior functions of management, supervision, expertise and control Can carry out these missions in all State services and its public establishments, and in advisory functions to the Government

## Related Job Titles

- State Representative

## Activities and Skills

- Working at the interface of several fields of public policy
- Advise political power on methods of action in crisis management
- Ensure interface with partner administrations
- Prepare and write administrative and budgetary documents
- Prepare draft laws or regulations for the Government and develop presentation documents for Parliament
- Design strategic orientations consistent with other central administration directorates and other ministries
- Monitoring the performance of public finances
- Evaluate the economic impact of new policies
- Negotiate with institutional partners and other ministries
- Manage the overall strategy of the administration in line with government guidelines
- Improve the strategic management of a structure
- Coordinate cross-functional strategic files
- Discerning the issues
- Define the organization's strategic objectives
- Determine the conditions of feasibility of political objectives in relation with national and territorial actors
- Establish forecasts, evaluations, recommendations, perspectives
- Establish an international strategic diagnosis
- Being able to anticipate and decipher changes in the environment, activity and organization
- Evaluate the financial impact of strategic decisions
- Interpret analysis results for strategic decisions
- Mobilize a strategic vision and anticipation
- Carry out a strategic diagnosis

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate leadership
- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility

## Areas of expertise

- Public policies

- International and geopolitical relations
- Monitoring developments in government policies
- Negotiation with government entities
- Intergovernmental communication

- Political communication
