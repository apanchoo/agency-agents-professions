# Debt recovery officer

## Scope

The debt recovery officer plays a crucial role in financial management by ensuring that debts are paid on time. Manages and monitors customer debt recovery files Negotiates payment terms with debtors Follows up on payments and follows up with late customers Writes monitoring and activity reports for management May initiate legal proceedings in the event of non-payment Collaborates with accounting departments to adjust entries and provisions

## Related Job Titles

- Debt collector

## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Update a file, a database
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Ensure customer relations: commercial monitoring, incident management (exchanges, returns, credits), follow-up for recovery
- Record payments in management systems
- Analyze customer accounts to identify late or unpaid receivables
- Define suitable payment terms
- Establish contact with debtors and follow up with them if necessary
- Evaluate the financial risks of recovery files
- Monitor recovery procedures
- Process payment deadline requests
- Apply current legislation and regulations
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Initiate legal proceedings if necessary in coordination with the legal department
- Implement and follow recovery procedures in the event of non-payment
- Handle litigation files
- Propose solutions adapted to the financial situation of the debtor
- Collaborate with teams to optimize processes
- Convince, negotiate
- Create a relationship of trust
- Negotiate payment terms or repayment plans
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
