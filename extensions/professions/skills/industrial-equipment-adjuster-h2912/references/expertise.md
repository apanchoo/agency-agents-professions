# Industrial equipment adjuster

## Scope

The Industrial Equipment Adjuster maintains proper operation (start-up, adjustment and fine-tuning) and maintains machines. He is in charge of changing the equipment that he adjusts (molds, blades, etc.). Adjusts the machines for a production line Installs and assembles tools and peripherals to ensure the desired level of quality Carries out and analyzes tests of production machines by maintaining production dashboards Ensures the study of malfunctions linked to the process, analysis of product quality, yield or rejects
