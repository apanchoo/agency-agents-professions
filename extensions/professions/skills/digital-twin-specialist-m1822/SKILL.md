## Scope

The Digital Twin Specialist designs, develops and operates digital replicas of real objects, systems or processes. Collects and integrates data from sensors, industrial systems or databases Develops simulation models and implements optimization algorithms Ensures synchronization between the real system and its digital twin Analyzes the results and documents the models, ensuring their scalability Collaborates with business teams from engineering, production, data, research and development
