# Manager of gîte and guest rooms

## Scope

The gîte and guesthouse manager ensures reception, logistics, organization of additional services to offer optimal accommodation and stay conditions to his clients Manages and supervises the maintenance of one or more gîtes to guarantee a quality welcome Coordinates reservations and optimizes the occupancy of gîtes Ensures a warm and professional welcome to guests Develops local partnerships to enrich the visitor experience Can offer and organize tourist activities and recreational Manages the budget and administrative aspects of the lodges

## Related Job Titles

- Director of tourist lodges
- Operator of guest rooms
- Rural lodge operator
- Mountain refuge guardian
