# Floor clerk

## Scope

The Housekeeper ensures cleanliness and order in the hotel rooms, thus contributing to the comfort of customers. Cleans and tidies hotel rooms and common areas according to establishment standards Changes sheets, makes beds and replaces towels and consumables Checks the condition of room equipment and reports any anomalies to maintenance Respects hygiene and safety rules and uses specific cleaning products Can manage found objects and return them to reception Ensures discreet and efficient service, contributing to customer satisfaction and comfort

## Related Job Titles

- Clerk / Floor clerk
- Hotel housekeeper
- Housekeeping employee in outdoor hotels
- Employee / Housekeeper in the hotel industry
- Butler in hospitality
- Valet / Maid
