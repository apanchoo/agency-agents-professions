# Remote insurance claims and damage expert

## Scope

The remote insurance claims and damage expert manages claims files to guarantee fair and rapid compensation. Evaluates claims reports and associated damages Analyzes files to determine responsibilities and proposes compensation solutions adapted to clients Negotiates claims settlements with the parties involved by ensuring administrative and technical follow-up of files Maintains clear and professional communication with all stakeholders

## Related Job Titles

- Expert in damage insurance
- Expert in insurance claims settlement

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Update a file, a database
- Check the guarantees taken out in the event of a disaster

- Write a report, an activity report
- Analyze the origin, cause and circumstances of a disaster
- Evaluate and manage a disaster
- Evaluate the amount of damage
- Expertize bodily, material and moral damages

- Deploy compensation procedures for policyholders and victims

- Assist employees at the commercial or technical level
- Ensure follow-up of customer files
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Develop and adapt a commercial proposal
- Conduct customer satisfaction surveys
