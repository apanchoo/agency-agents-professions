# Pipefitter

## Scope

The Pipefitter manufactures and maintains/repairs piping elements intended for transporting gas or fluids between industrial equipment, ensures their assembly and maintenance on site. Analyzes the technical documents and identifies the operations to be carried out Prepares the components, cuts and shapes the piping assemblies by bending, folding, etc. Assembles the piping elements according to different processes then assembly on site Control (visual, tightness, ultrasound, etc.) and carries out the commissioning tests Ensures the maintenance and repair of the piping elements to be reworked (corrosion) Writes the intervention traceability documents
