# Career management advisor

## Scope

The Career Management Advisor enables the optimization of talents within the company. Identifies and evaluates employee skills Implements skills development strategies adapted to the needs of the company and the employee Designs and implements personalized training plans Supports employees in their professional development Advises management on human resources management strategies Monitors legal and regulatory developments in HR matters
