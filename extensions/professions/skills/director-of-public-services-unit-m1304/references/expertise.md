# Director of public services unit

## Scope

The Public Services Unit Director plans, organizes, directs and controls the activities of the unit to ensure that services are provided and guarantee public satisfaction. Directs and coordinates the activities of the public services unit Implements the strategy and general orientations of the structure Develops local strategies to improve the quality of services offered Supervises the staff and manages the human resources of the unit Establishes partnerships with external organizations to strengthen the quality of services Manages the budget and financial resources of the unit
