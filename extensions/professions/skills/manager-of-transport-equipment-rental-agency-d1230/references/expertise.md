# Manager of transport equipment rental agency

## Scope

The transport equipment rental agency manager manages and develops the activity of his agency. Manages the vehicle fleet and ensures their optimal maintenance in compliance with safety standards and regulations in force Develops customers and maintains commercial relationships by ensuring the monitoring of contracts Supervises the daily operations of the agency and coordinates the team Optimizes costs and maximizes the profitability of the agency Analyzes market trends to adjust the service offering
