# Show rider

## Scope

The Show Rider is an equestrian artist who captivates and enchants his audience. Performs dressage acts and equestrian shows in front of an audience Maintains a relationship of complicity and respect with the horses Participates in the daily training of horses to develop their skills and well-being Ensures the safety of animals and spectators during performances Collaborates with other artists and technicians to create captivating shows May participate in the management and maintenance of equestrian equipment and facilities

## Related Job Titles

- Stunt rider
- Vaulting rider
- Squire

## Activities and Skills

- Perform equestrian aerobatics exercises
- Respect performance times
- Periodically evaluate the skills of animals and riders
- Carry out physical preparation exercises
- Adapt the choreographies according to the animals' abilities
- Know and evaluate the animals' reception conditions and environment
- Check the health of the animals before and after the show
- Educate/train animals
- Maintain good physical condition to withstand physical efforts
- Handle animals
- Implement education or training actions for animals during daily activities
- Providing physical and aesthetic care to animals
- Promote productions to the media and the public
- Prepare an artistic event
- Design and produce a visual number (technical, artistic aspects)
- Perform regular reps to ensure smooth performance
- Perform a choreography
- Put on a show
- Modify the spectacular or comic effects of the number according to the reactions of the audience
- Ensure the safety of the public and animals during the show
- Manage technical or artistic unforeseen events during shows
- Ensure that shows comply with local regulations
- Monitor weather conditions for outdoor shows
- Collaborate with other artists for interdisciplinary projects
- Communicate with the technical team for specific show needs
- Involve an audience
- Respect safety standards
