# Intimacy Coordinator

## Scope

The intimacy coordinator coordinates the teams involved during the preparation and filming of intimacy scenes, and enforces the framework within which these scenes are prepared and filmed. Identifies, analyzes and organizes the intimate scenes appearing in the script Collaborates closely with the actors, the director, the producer and the technicians concerned Ensures that the framework in which the intimate scenes are filmed (filming protocol, nudity clauses) complies with what has been previously established Alerts those responsible in the event of a breach observed during the filming of these scenes Checks the conformity of the rushes of the intimate scenes and establishes reports for the production Can support the actors who wish, after filming and in post-production

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead briefing or debrief times
- Clearly communicate artistic expectations to your team
- Manage interactions between the different technicians on set
- Mobilize individually and collectively around the objectives
- Ensuring quality of life at work
- Support actors after filming, in post-production or during the film promotion period
- Carry out mediation work to reach agreements reconciling consent and the needs of the narrative
- Identify uncomfortable situations or facts likely to contravene the consent of artists, technicians
- Use relaxation techniques to reduce stress
- Carry out rehearsal sessions for the choreography of intimate scenes to clarify the role of each member
- Manage the coordination of activities on a film set
- Organize time for discussion and work with the technicians involved in the project
- Participate in the filming of intimate scenes to ensure compliance with filming protocol and nudity clauses
- Perform a scenario review to identify all intimate scenes
- Carry out a risk analysis for each intimate scene
- Write the filming protocol, to be attached to the daily duty sheet
- Analyze the rushes of the intimate scenes to check their compliance with the expectations established before filming
- Establish reports for production to report on the progress and compliance of intimate scenes
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Participate in the drafting of nudity clauses for intimate scenes (level of nudity, nature of intimate acts)
- Control the progress of a film shoot or performance
- Liaise with stage technicians
- Show tact and diplomacy
- Anticipate the needs and difficulties of organizing the activity
- Managing internal conflicts
- Respect safety guidelines on film sets
