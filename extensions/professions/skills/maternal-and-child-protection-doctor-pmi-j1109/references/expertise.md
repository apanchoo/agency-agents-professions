# Maternal and Child Protection Doctor -PMI-

## Scope

The Maternal and Child Protection Doctor -PMI- plays a crucial role in monitoring and promoting the health of children and mothers. Ensures medical monitoring of children and mothers Coordinates prevention and screening actions for disorders in children Participates in training and awareness-raising actions on child and maternal health Collaborates with other health professionals and institutions to optimize care Intervenes in crisis situations for the protection of the child and mother Conducts epidemiological studies to improve public health practices

## Related Job Titles

- Maternal and Child Protection Coordinating Doctor -PMI-
- Health and early childhood services doctor

## Activities and Skills

- Support people in implementing the nutritional program
- Supporting parents in their educational and parental role
- Advising parents on child nutrition
- Teaching, developing skills
- Support parents in the first maternal or paternal gestures
- Greet patients and assess their general well-being
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Determine the need for additional medical examination and refer the person to a treating physician or specialist
- Identify the patient's needs and exchange information (current treatments, medical history, x-rays, etc.)
- Organize vaccination campaigns
- Schedule health checkups
- Prevent the risks of mistreatment, illiteracy, domestic risks
- Monitor the evolution of patients' health status
- Update the patient's medical record
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage medical records and ensure their confidentiality
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Help promote preventive health practices
- Participate in a multidisciplinary project

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
