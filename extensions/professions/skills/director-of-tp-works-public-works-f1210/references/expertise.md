# Director of TP works - Public Works

## Scope

The public works director coordinates projects and budgets for major construction projects. Repairs, manages and directs the project in compliance with the budget and all rules relating to the safety of people and the environment Ensures the proper application of the contract, coordinates the drafting of complaints to ensure the economic performance of the project Gives directives to the works managers who ensure the execution of the works both on a technical and financial level Follows the completion of the project, ensures budgetary monitoring and reports to its management Supervises the teams and participates in the development of human resources: recruitment of supervisory staff, training and operational management human resources (leave, absences, etc.) Leads the Management Committee and communicates on key information Represents the company and manages customer relations

## Related Job Titles

- Head of public works sector

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate one or more teams
- Evaluate the performance of the teams under his responsibility
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Design and implement a skills development plan
- Manage human resources
- Strengthen internal communication on projects
- Evaluate the skills of its employees and propose training actions
- Propose areas of skills development based on identified needs
- Create technical documentation
- Update a file, a database
- Provide technical support to site teams
- Close the site
- Identify and select the necessary resources (human and material) for the successful completion of the project
- Deliver the sites to the client in compliance with the costs and deadlines provided for in the specifications
- Manage the execution of work
- Plan the work phases
- Project and organize the activity of all stakeholders over the entire duration of the project
- Propose the organization of the site internally and with possible partners (project organization chart, etc.)
- Ensuring safety on construction sites
- Managing a crisis situation
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Lead meetings with the various trades to ensure the proper progress of the project and compliance with the budget
- Monitor the project budget
- Guarantee the overall economic performance of the project with management bodies
- Readjust the budget according to the vagaries of the project or specific requests from the client
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Ensure compliance with environmental standards
- Follow a contract, check compliance with contractual clauses
- Ensure follow-up of customer relations during and after the work
- Guarantee with stakeholders the proper application of contracts and amendments linked to the work, resolve disputes
- Represent the company or its group to the client and defend its interests
- Supervise after-sales service and monitoring of associated services where applicable (maintenance)
- Estimate the cost of work and construction sites
- Prepare and present application files for calls for tenders
- Direct public works operations
- Develop and manage a network of partner organizations
- Carry out reporting and ensure that turnover and performance objectives are achieved
- Participate in investment choices in conjunction with the agency director and support services (price studies, equipment, purchasing): selection of suppliers or service providers, purchases of large equipment or materials, etc.
- Monitor activity indicators for all projects under your responsibility: current business volume, monitoring of customer and supplier invoicing, etc.
- Arguing decisions with all types of interlocutors
- Lead decision-making bodies by communicating key information
- Carry out public communications if necessary (round table, press, collective information, etc.)
- Raise team awareness of the issues of Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR)
- Ensure technological and regulatory monitoring
