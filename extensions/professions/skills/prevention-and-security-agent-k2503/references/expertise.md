# Prevention and security agent

## Scope

The prevention and security agent ensures the protection of property and people. Monitors and controls access to sites to guarantee the safety of goods and people Intervenes in emergency situations and manages security incidents Carries out rounds of prevention and detection of anomalies Informs and alerts the competent services (firefighters, law enforcement) in the event of a serious threat Ensures compliance with security rules within establishments Manages the use of monitoring and alarm equipment
