# Theater usher

## Scope

The Theater Operator contributes to providing a positive experience to the public through a quality welcome in entertainment spaces. Welcomes and checks tickets at the entrance to the rooms Directs spectators to their seats Ensures the safety and comfort of the public during events Follows procedures in the event of an emergency or necessary evacuation May manage the sale of programs or promotional items

## Related Job Titles

- Performance hall control agent
- Usher opener / Concert hall usher

## Activities and Skills

- Assisting people in difficulty
- Apply first aid techniques in an emergency
- Ensure the cleanliness of common areas
- Troubleshoot minor seating or equipment issues
- Check the operation of a safety device
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Check the operation of the room equipment
- Check the installation and public safety conditions (harness, etc.) and lock the security systems (bars, doors, etc.)
- Prepare the room before spectators arrive
- Report any anomaly or incident
- Respect working hours according to show programs
- Use electronic ticketing systems and manage transactions
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Screening people or objects using screening devices
- Supervise the application of ticket control procedures and room access devices
- Monitor and regulate the flow of visitors in restricted areas
- Monitor spectator behavior
- Greet spectators and guide them to their seats
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Answer audience questions about the show
- Offer programs or promotional items
- Communicate effectively with the team to ensure consistency of work
- Communicate program delays or changes
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Apply accessibility rules for people with disabilities
- Ensure a neat presentation
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Managing a conflict situation
- Intervene quickly in the event of an incident or spectator needs

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be open to changes
- Listen, show empathy

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of audiovisual equipment

## Areas of expertise

- Emergency and first aid procedures

## Standards and processes

- Local regulations for performance halls
- Reading seating plan
- Safety standards for live performances
- Conditions for booking services

## Professional techniques

- Stress management in a noisy environment
- Monitoring and reporting room anomalies
- Oral, written and digital communication techniques
- Conflict resolution techniques
- Preparation and organization of performance spaces
- Room emergency devices
- Interpersonal communication techniques

## Working Conditions

- People with disabilities
- In contact with the public
- Prolonged standing
- Work in staggered hours
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Leisure, cultural or tourist structure
