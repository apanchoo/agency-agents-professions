# Journalist

## Scope

The journalist provides multi-media and multi-channel reporting depending on the title for which he works. Writes articles, reports, interviews or commentary on current events Verifies the accuracy of information and meets publication deadlines Adapts content for different media outlets such as print, radio, television and the web Collaborates with photographers, videographers and other journalists to enrich the content of reports Participates in press conferences and events to gather first-hand information Maintains a network of contacts to quickly access reliable information
