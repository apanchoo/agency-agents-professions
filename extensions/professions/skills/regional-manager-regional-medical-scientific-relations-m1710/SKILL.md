Ensures liaison between researchers and health professionals to optimize scientific exchanges Leads a network of experts (scientific, medical, institutional) Contributes to the implementation of regional scientific communication strategies Organizes and participates in conferences, scientific meetings Supports research and development initiatives at the regional level Provides up-to-date medical and scientific information to health professionals Ensures compliance with ethical and regulatory standards in all interactions
