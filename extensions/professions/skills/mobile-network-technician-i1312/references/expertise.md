# Mobile network technician

## Scope

The Mobile Network Technician Installs mobile telecommunications networks to provide network coverage and connectivity to end users. Performs electrical wiring and connections and configuration of telecommunications equipment Monitors network operation and resolves technical problems to ensure uninterrupted service May update systems and software to improve network performance

## Related Job Titles

- Telecommunications Technical Agent
- Low current technician
- Mobile network deployment technician
- 4G-5G mobile network technician
- 4G - 5G cable network installation technician
- Telecommunications and radio installation technician
- GSM network technician

## Activities and Skills

- Organize a technical assistance service
- Monitor technological developments in information and telecommunications systems and propose technical solutions
- Ensure specific network connection operations (specific coverage, events, etc.)
- Configure or configure telecommunications equipment, low currents
- Diagnose malfunctions on network installations
- Sizing, planning and monitoring the capacity of the telecom network and its interconnections
- Install and configure network software
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Complete the intervention documents
- Update and upgrade telecommunications or low current equipment
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Assemble the elements of the electrical or electronic product (components, wiring)
- Connect electronic equipment
- Connect a junction box
- Wiring equipment
- Disable part of a network before intervention
- Identify installation or wiring modifications
- Install ducts and wiring
- Install the equipment on site and connect it to external networks
- Commission the network and check the installation
- Connect an electrical cabinet to equipment in domestic or tertiary premises
- Make hydraulic, electrical and mechanical connections for equipment and accessories
- Carry out network cabling of computer or office equipment
- Carry out a cable pulling operation
- Perform software update
- Check the conformity of the installation on site, help desk, hot line
- Control a network or operating equipment
- Check safety equipment or materials (PPE, harness, etc.)
- Identify and assess potential risks linked to the environment (variation of climatic conditions)
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Monitor the operation of computer and telecom networks
- Establish an intervention report on internal IT tool or customer tool
- Identify the characteristics of a site
- Respect the planned intervention time
- Determine solutions and technical recommendations and communicate them to customers
- Adapt working methods to the level of risks in electrical environments
- Adapt working methods to the level of risks of working at height
- Continuously develop your skills, particularly in emerging technologies

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility
