## Scope

The Mobile Network Technician Installs mobile telecommunications networks to provide network coverage and connectivity to end users. Performs electrical wiring and connections and configuration of telecommunications equipment Monitors network operation and resolves technical problems to ensure uninterrupted service May update systems and software to improve network performance

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Configure or configure telecommunications equipment, low currents
- Update and upgrade telecommunications or low current equipment
- Install the equipment on site and connect it to external networks
- Make hydraulic, electrical and mechanical connections for equipment and accessories
- Check the conformity of the installation on site, help desk, hot line
- Monitor the operation of computer and telecom networks
- Determine solutions and technical recommendations and communicate them to customers
- Organize a technical assistance service
