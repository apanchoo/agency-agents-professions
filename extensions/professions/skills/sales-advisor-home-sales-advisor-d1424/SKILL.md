Organizes sales meetings at customer sites to present products Offers personalized advice to meet specific customer needs Takes orders and manages financial transactions Ensures customer follow-up to ensure customer satisfaction and loyalty Can manage customer returns and complaints Develops a network of customers through word of mouth and recommendations
