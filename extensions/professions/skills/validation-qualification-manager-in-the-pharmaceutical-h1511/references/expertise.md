# Validation-qualification manager in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Validation-Qualification Manager supervises and carries out the validation and qualification processes of systems, equipment, and procedures to guarantee their compliance with standards. Validates and qualifies processes and equipment according to current standards Carry out tests and analyzes to ensure product conformity Proposes and implements preventive or corrective quality actions Participates in the implementation of the qualification strategy Collaborates with development teams to identify and correct anomalies Ensures regulatory monitoring to stay informed of developments in standards Participates in the continuous improvement of validation processes
