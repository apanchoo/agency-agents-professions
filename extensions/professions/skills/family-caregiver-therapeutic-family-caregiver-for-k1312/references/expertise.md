# Family caregiver / Therapeutic family caregiver for adults

## Scope

The therapeutic family caregiver for adults is a professional who welcomes adults in a fragile or disabled situation into their home, often as a result of psychological disorders, thus offering them a stable and secure environment. Welcomes adults requiring psychological or physical support in a family environment Ensures the daily well-being of the adults welcomed by ensuring their comfort and safety Organizes adapted therapeutic activities to promote personal and social development Collaborates with health professionals to develop and follow personalized care plans Maintains regular communication with the families of those welcomed to keep them informed of developments Manages the administrative aspects related to therapeutic family reception
