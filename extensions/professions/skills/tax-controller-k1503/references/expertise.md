# Tax Controller

## Scope

The Tax Controller ensures the management and regulation of public finances. Checks the compliance of tax declarations with the legislation On-site inspection of the accounts of companies and individuals Writes control reports and suggests tax corrections Participates in the fight against tax fraud Ensures the follow-up of litigation files Informs and advises taxpayers on tax legislation
