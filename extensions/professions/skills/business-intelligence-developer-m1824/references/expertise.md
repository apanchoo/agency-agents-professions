# Business Intelligence Developer

## Scope

The Business Intelligence Developer designs, develops and maintains analytical solutions and reporting tools to help businesses make decisions. Designs and develops reporting and data analysis solutions Optimizes and maintains existing decision-making systems Collaborates with business teams to understand their needs and provide appropriate solutions Ensures the quality of data and its integrity in the reports generated Trains users in the tools and solutions developed Can participate in technology monitoring and the continuous improvement of BI practices
