# Moving Team Leader

## Scope

The moving team leader ensures the transport, using a suitable vehicle over all distances, of furniture or all objects on behalf of individuals or companies. Manages the team of movers and distributes tasks Packs, assembles and dismantles furniture and secures items that may be fragile and valuable, loading and unloading them into the vehicle Ensures the handling of sometimes heavy items with appropriate equipment to prevent the risk of injury Organizes and optimizes space in the truck or crates for safe and efficient transportation Coordinates with customers to plan the details of the move and meet their specific needs May drive heavy trucks and manage the logistical aspects of long-distance transport
