# Production operator in the wood industry

## Scope

The Production Operator in the wood industry carries out manual or semi-automatic operations depending on the production chain process. Monitors the supply and flow of machines and ensures the proper circulation of products Controls the quality of wooden products and ensures compliance with safety standards Performs adjustments and first-level maintenance on production machines Carries out, mechanically or manually, the handling and storage of finished or semi-finished products according to categories Respects environmental procedures for the management of wood waste and recycling

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Record activities in a log
- Organize your space and workstation in line with a production program
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Start or stop a machine, a line
- Manufacture, shape products
- Shape wooden elements on a traditional machine (flat top, flying top, scroll top, etc.) by manually guiding the tool or part
- Shape wooden elements on a numerically controlled or automated machine
- Identify manufacturing operations from the manufacturing file
- Carry out sawing and cutting operations
- Adjust equipment (autoclaves, ovens, etc.) for wood treatment (drying, preservation, etc.) and monitor their operation
- Monitor the flow of wood on the production line
- Use and adjust machines or equipment elements of the wood industry
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Check safety elements on production equipment (machine tool, assembly line, etc.)
- Identify the risks of breakdowns, incidents or accidents
- Practice safe handling gestures and postures
- Monitor the supply of machines, facilities and the flow of materials or products
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Visually control the quality of products during production
- Remove non-compliant products
- Load, unload, handle products
- Move and store products and materials according to quality and safety standards
- Check raw material stocks
- Write a report, an activity report
- Protect the environment by reducing production waste
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Alert the maintenance department
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
