# Educator of young children

## Scope

The educator of young children ensures that educational continuity is maintained while respecting the family, social and cultural environment. Supports children in their psychomotor and socio-emotional development Designs and implements educational activities promoting children's development Ensures the safety and well-being of children within the structure Collaborates with parents and other professionals to monitor the child's progress Participates in the management and organization of the reception structure Can coordinate an early childhood team or a relay of childminders

## Related Job Titles

- Educator / Educator of young children early childhood coordination
- Educator of young children and animation of childminder relays
- Early Childhood Educator Deputy Head of Early Childhood Care Structure
- Educator of young children responsible for early childhood care structure

## Activities and Skills

- Welcoming the child's family
- Provide supervision for the child or a group of children
- Organize and lead fun and awareness activities
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Liaise with families
- Develop an educational project
- Encourage independence in daily activities
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Adapt your practices to support children with disabilities
- Apply first aid techniques in an emergency
- Create and implement conditions necessary for the development, health, safety and well-being and autonomy of young children
- Evaluate the child's autonomy and physical, cognitive and relational abilities in their daily life
- Managing children’s rest and sleep times
- Establish a relationship of trust with the child and support them in their development, in conjunction with their family
- Observe the child's behavior and development and identify possible difficulties
- Inform parents about how a childcare structure works
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Managing child safety during activities
- Complete administrative and regulatory formalities
- Design and conduct educational action within a multidisciplinary and multiprofessional team
- Contribute to the understanding and evolution of the educational and social field of early childhood
- Develop a parenting support project within the educational project
- Develop and implement a social, educational and pedagogical project for young children
- Implement educational activities as part of projects in the social, medico-social and health sector
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate and develop a helping relationship
- Promote communication between different stakeholders
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing a conflict situation
- Maintain a clean and welcoming environment
- Promote values of respect and tolerance
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be open to changes
- Listen, show empathy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Emergency and first aid procedures
- Knowledge of children's rights
- Stages of child development
- Child and adolescent psychology

## Standards and processes

- First aid suitable for children

## Professional techniques

- Group management of children
- Monitoring children's behavior
- Group facilitation techniques
- Game-based learning methods
- Planning educational activities

## Working Conditions

- Children under 3 years old
- Children under 3 years old with disabilities
- Children aged 3 to 6
- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Public sector employee
- In a noisy environment
- Prolonged standing
- Work in staggered hours
- Daytime work
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity
- Association
- Crèche, daycare
