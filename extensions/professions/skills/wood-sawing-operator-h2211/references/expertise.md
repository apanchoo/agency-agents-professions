# Wood sawing operator

## Scope

The wood sawing operator is a specialist in wood cutting. Transforms logs into lumber according to required specifications Controls the quality of the wood and ensures compliance with safety standards Performs first-level maintenance of sawing equipment

## Related Job Titles

- Edger
- Unwinder / Unwinder in primary wood processing
- Debarker / Sawmill debarker
- Splitter / Staves splitter
- Marker / Sawmill marker
- Primary lumber processing operator
- Trimmer
- Sawyer / Wood sawyer
- Sawyer / Long sawyer
- Sawyer / Resumption sawyer
- Sawyer / Head sawyer
- Tracer-debiter / Tracer-debiter in primary wood processing
- Slicer / Wood slicer
- Chainsaw / Chainsaw in primary wood processing

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure the training of new operators
- Adapt working methods according to orders
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Eliminate foreign bodies (metallic elements, etc.), debark and cut logs (transformation into logs, ridges, etc.)
- Splitting a stave log
- Position and maintain the work material on the machine
- Adjust, operate an automated sawing line
- Adjust, operate a debarker
- Follow sawing instructions specific to wood types
- Select the tools (saw blades, knives, etc.), mount them and adjust them (position, tension, etc.)
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Clean the work area, in accordance with the instructions, in order to guarantee the continuity of activities in the area at the end of the intervention
- Troubleshoot technical issues
- Monitor the proper functioning of machines
- Check the humidity of the wood before sawing
- Check the quality of wood and cuts
- Manage stocks of wood and necessary materials
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Receive logs (classification, sorting, etc.) and store them according to species and use
- Transport logs
- Write a report, an activity report
- Optimize the material yield of a log
- Apply ergonomic rules to prevent injuries
- Meet production deadlines to meet customer requests
- Respect safety and hygiene standards
- Participate in continuing education sessions
