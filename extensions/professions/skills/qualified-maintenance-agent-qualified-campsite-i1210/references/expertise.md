# Qualified maintenance agent / Qualified campsite maintenance agent

## Scope

The qualified campsite maintenance agent maintains the infrastructure of a campsite in good condition to ensure the comfort and safety of campers. Ensures second-level maintenance and intervenes in one or more technical areas (electricity, plumbing, heating-ventilation-air conditioning, aquatic spaces, etc.) Guarantees the optimal operation and safety of the installations, ensuring the necessary repairs and operations Prepares the installations and carries out renovation and preventive maintenance work in low season Responds to the needs for urgent interventions, ensures troubleshooting and monitors customer relations in high season

## Related Job Titles

- Qualified maintenance agent / Qualified maintenance agent in outdoor hotels

## Activities and Skills

- Update technical documentation and records (playgrounds, gas, laundry, etc.)
- Monitor water quality and adjust parameters
- Maintain a green space
- Install and maintain outdoor furniture or signage
- Carry out painting or finishing work (carpentry, plasterboard, floor covering, etc.)
- Carry out an installation (electrical, plumbing, etc.)
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Observe a breakdown in an installation (lighting, heating, sanitary facilities)
- Diagnose a breakdown or malfunctions (electrical, mechanical, automation, hydraulic, etc.)
- Carry out routine repair work in plumbing, painting, electricity
- Maintain the batteries of electric equipment and vehicles
- Carry out the wintering and de-wintering of pools and aquatic spaces
- Carry out a complex maintenance intervention in your area of expertise (electricity, plumbing, swimming pool, green spaces)
- Repair and replace swimming pool equipment
- Check the proper functioning of the aquatic area equipment
- Ensure customer safety by following appropriate procedures
- Monitor the operation of sanitation systems and intervene in the event of an anomaly
- Monitor heating and cooling systems to ensure they are functioning
- Regularly inspect building structures to identify repair needs
- Apply current standards (accessibility, environment, etc.)
- Manage the consumption of energy and consumables in an eco-responsible manner
- Manage waste and recycling in an environmentally friendly way
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Regularly check the installations and detect risky situations (lack of maintenance, dangerous behavior, etc.)
- Coordinate interventions with external service providers
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Report on your activity
- Adapt your organization and activity to peaks in traffic, seasonal and territorial constraints
- Manage unforeseen events, hazards and last minute changes
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Respect the establishment’s quality standards
