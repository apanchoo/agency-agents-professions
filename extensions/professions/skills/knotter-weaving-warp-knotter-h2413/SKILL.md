## Scope

The Weaving Warp Knotter repairs and knots the threads of a weaving warp to ensure the continuity of the textile manufacturing process. Intervenes on broken threads by making solid knots and checks the strength and regularity of the knots to avoid irregularities in the fabric Ensures the continuity of the threads on the loom to avoid production interruptions Adapts the knotting techniques to the different types of threads and weaves using automatic or manual knotters Collaborates with other technicians to optimize the adjustment of the weaving machines Cleans his workstation and maintains the tools knotting to ensure efficient production
