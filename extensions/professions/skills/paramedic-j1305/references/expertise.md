# Paramedic

## Scope

The paramedic plays a crucial role in medical transport services, providing rapid and competent assistance. Transports patients to healthcare facilities while respecting safety regulations and medical emergencies Ensures the comfort and safety of patients during transport Performs first aid monitoring and assistance of patients during transport Manages medical equipment and ensures their proper functioning Communicates with medical personnel and emergency services (SMUR) to ensure effective care Maintains transport records and intervention reports up to date

## Related Job Titles

- Paramedic / Emergency paramedic
- Chief paramedic
- Hospital paramedic
- Medical Vehicle Driver
- Driver of Light Medical Vehicle (VSL)
- Ambulance driver
- Ambulance driver / Emergency services ambulance driver
- Hospital ambulance driver

## Activities and Skills

- Contribute to a person's physical well-being
- Contribute to a person's psychological well-being
- Encourage patients to express their concerns and questions
- Quickly assess patients' medical needs, monitor them and report changes and developments
- Provide appropriate professional actions or those dictated by the doctor
- Prepare, install a medical-technical device
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Keep the medical vehicle clean
- Check the operation of the medical vehicle equipment, identify malfunctions
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Take into account the expiration dates of supplies
- Install the patient in the medical vehicle
- Stretch a patient
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Respect scheduled times and routes
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Communicate with those close to the person being transported
- Exchange information on medical surveillance with hospital staff
- Establish a professional relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being transported
- Respect health safety protocols

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
