# Criminal investigation technician

## Scope

The Criminal Investigation Technician plays a crucial role in resolving criminal cases; To do this, he carries out scientific and technical analyzes on evidence and clues that he has collected to solve criminal cases. Analyzes crime scenes to collect and preserve evidence Uses scientific techniques to examine collected evidence Works closely with law enforcement to interpret investigative data Writes detailed reports on analysis results to support forensic investigations May testify as an expert at trials to explain technical evidence Participates in continuing education to stay current on technological and methodological advances

## Related Job Titles

- Technician of the technical and scientific police (TPTS)
- Criminal research and identification technician

## Areas of expertise

- Biological analysis (blood analyses, various secretions such as saliva and semen, hair, genetic fingerprinting, etc.)
- Analysis of documents and traces (papillary marks, writings or false documents)
- Analysis of narcotics (chemical substances and samples collected during a seizure or at the scene of a crime)
- Analysis of explosives and flammable liquids
- Analysis of digital technologies (study of telephones, computers, GPS, video images, etc.)
- Evidence Management
- Fingerprinting techniques
- Physics - chemistry analyzes (traces of paint, gunshot residue, glass pieces, soil samples, etc.)
- Criminal psychology
- Toxicology analysis (search for toxic substances in biological environments)
- Knowledge of current criminal trends
- Ballistic analysis (study of weapons, ammunition, and shooting trajectories found at the crime scene)
- Behavioral analysis of suspects
