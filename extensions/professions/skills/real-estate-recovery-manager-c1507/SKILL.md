# Real estate recovery manager

## Scope

The Real Estate Recovery Officer is involved in the recovery of unpaid debts from defaulting tenants, co-owners or buyers to obtain payment of the sums due. Ensures the follow-up of recovery files and the implementation of pre-litigation and litigation procedures Collaborates closely with legal services to prepare litigation files Negotiates repayment plans with debtor tenants or owners Keeps recovery files up to date and writes activity reports

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Write a report, an activity report
- Apply appropriate recovery procedures
- Manage invoices and collections in conjunction with accounting, legal and litigation departments
- Collect rent
- Supervise debt recovery procedures
- Establish amicable and forced recovery procedures for taxpayers in arrears
- Implement and follow recovery procedures in the event of non-payment
- Respect the confidentiality of information
