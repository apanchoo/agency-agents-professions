# Ethics-deontology-compliance manager in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Ethics-Deontology-Compliance Manager ensures the application of ethical and legal standards within the company. Ensures compliance of company practices with legal and ethical standards Develops and implements compliance policies Organizes training on compliance issues for employees Conducts regular audits to ensure compliance with guidelines Advises management on compliance and ethics issues Manages incidents of non-compliance and proposes corrective solutions
