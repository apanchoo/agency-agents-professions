# Responsible for waste treatment

## Scope

The waste treatment manager plays a role in the sustainable management of resources. Designs and implements waste treatment and recovery processes Optimizes waste management systems to reduce environmental impact Supervises operations at treatment sites and ensures compliance with environmental standards Develops innovative solutions for recycling and reuse of materials Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to improve waste management practices Conducts environmental impact studies and proposes continuous improvements

## Related Job Titles

- Engineer in waste treatment and recovery
- Waste treatment technician

## Activities and Skills

- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Design procedures, protocols, operating methods
- Define eco-industrial intervention procedures
- Develop materials recycling strategies
- Plan preventive maintenance operations
- Prescribe technical solutions for operating new sites, improving equipment or networks
- Master waste treatment processes (incineration, composting, recycling, methanization, etc.)
- Master energy recovery technologies
- Supervise the maintenance of waste treatment equipment
- Assess the risks associated with processing operations
- Check the operation of equipment and installations, identify anomalies and preventive or corrective actions
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Carry out a technical audit
- Perform daily activity reports
- Develop strategies to reduce waste and optimize recycling
- Ensure regulatory compliance of installations
- Ensure compliance with environmental standards
- Write regulatory compliance reports
- Apply standards related to material recycling
- Assess the environmental impact of waste management activities
- Evaluate the environmental impact of waste treatment methods
- Manage green waste according to environmental standards
- Supervise waste treatment operations
- Coordinate actions with local authorities
- Provide expertise in the field of waste treatment and recovery
- Manage environmental projects, from design to implementation
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Coordinate with municipal services for waste disposal
- Apply safety measures and environmental standards
- Ensure regulatory and legislative monitoring
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
