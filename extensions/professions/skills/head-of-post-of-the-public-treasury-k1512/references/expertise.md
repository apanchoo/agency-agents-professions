# Head of post of the Public Treasury

## Scope

The Head of the Public Treasury post ensures financial and accounting management within the administration. Supervises the collection and management of public funds and expenditure Ensures the keeping of public accounts and the control of the regularity of financial operations Advises local authorities on financial and tax management Participates in the development of budgets and their monitoring Can represent the administration in various meetings and commissions Manages and develops the human resources of its service

## Related Job Titles

- Head of general treasury department

## Activities and Skills

- Providing technical and strategic expertise to public decision-makers and businesses
- Advise an organization, a structure
- Oversee the preparation and consolidation of financial reports
- Implement financial management tools
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Raise awareness about the rational use of public funds
- Apply appropriate recovery procedures
- Control the regularity of public accounts
- Manage funds deposited in Treasury coffers
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Evaluate financial risks
- Prepare financial and activity reports
- Carry out public revenue collection operations and manage funds deposited with the State
- Verify and control the financial management of public funds of the State, local authorities and public establishments
- Ensure compliance of tax operations
- Seek an amicable solution to prevent unpaid debts or initiate a pre-litigation procedure
- Handle litigation files
- Check compliance with regulations and use of public funds
- Control public expenditure and revenue
- Carry out expertise and advisory services
- Represent and enhance the company's image
- Establish agreements, conventions, contractual relationships on behalf of the State with local authorities, associations and local partners
- Establish financial performance indicators and adjust strategies accordingly
- Coordinate with other financial services
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Monitor and update technical, economic, regulatory information, etc.

## Professional Approach
