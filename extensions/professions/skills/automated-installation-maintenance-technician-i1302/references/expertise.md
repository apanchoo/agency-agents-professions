# Automated installation maintenance technician

## Scope

The Automated Installations Maintenance Technician is essential to guarantee the operation and reliability of a site's automated equipment while respecting safety standards and regulations in force. Regularly controls, monitors and maintains equipment Detects, diagnoses faults and makes necessary repairs Responds quickly to resolve first-level faults or calls upon specialized technicians Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to continually improve maintenance processes May participate in the installation and commissioning of new equipment

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Troubleshoot advanced technical issues
- Analyze maintenance data
- Identify technical solutions for improving equipment and installations
- Make adjustments to automated systems to optimize their operation
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Take measurements (atmospheric, sound, etc.) and carry out monitoring
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Analyze automated systems to identify malfunctions
- Check the conformity of automated installations according to safety standards
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Carry out preventive, systematic or conditional maintenance operations
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Repair the installation by replacing and reconditioning electrical, electronic, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic devices
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Use testing devices (pressure, flow, vibration, etc.) and electrical measuring devices
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Ensure the application and respect of hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental rules (waste management, noise pollution, etc.)
- Manage stocks of maintenance equipment and spare parts
- Produce inspection and diagnostic reports for anomalies or risks and transmit them to the production, maintenance and security departments.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Act quickly in the event of a breakdown to minimize production stoppages
