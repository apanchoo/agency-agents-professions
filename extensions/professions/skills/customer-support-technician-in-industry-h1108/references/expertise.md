# Customer support technician in industry

## Scope

The customer support technician provides technical assistance, problem solving and quality after-sales service, by working on industrial equipment or systems. Supports customers in getting started with products Provides instructions for use and technical advice Collaborates with internal teams and suppliers to resolve complex problems Performs preventive and corrective maintenance interventions Applies safety and regulatory standards Manages intervention priorities

## Related Job Titles

- Technical support manager
- Technical support advisor
- Technical representative / Technical user representative
- Technical support technician

## Activities and Skills

- Develop your technical support skills
- Support customers in using a product or equipment
- Create technical documentation
- Maintain a database of customer interactions
- Make technical documents available to customers
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Use specific software
- Collaborate with suppliers for product quality
- Analyze customer technical needs
- Carry out a technical diagnosis
- Collaborate with teams to improve facility security
- Collaborate with teams to ensure continuity of production
- Handle emergency calls with priority and accuracy
- Develop partnerships with suppliers
- Collaborate with teams to resolve technical issues
- Communicate with customers for specific requests
- Advise customers on necessary maintenance and repair options
- Collaborate with other team members to improve service quality
- Carry out technical monitoring of products and their evolution with customers
- Collaborate with other technicians to optimize work processes
- Collaborate with a project team
- Communicate instructions clearly
- Clearly communicate technical procedures to customers
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Working in a group, in a network
- Respect the standards and regulations in force in the industry
- Use digital tools
- Master elearning, assistance and customer technical support techniques

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
