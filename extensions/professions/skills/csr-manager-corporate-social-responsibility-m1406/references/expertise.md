# CSR Manager (Corporate Social Responsibility)

## Scope

The CSR manager develops, implements and supervises the company's social responsibility strategy in accordance with international regulations and standards. Participates in the company's strategic bodies Sets, in collaboration with management, the trajectories to integrate social responsibility into all decisions Collaborates with the different departments to integrate sustainable practices into processes Collects and analyzes the company's sustainability performance data Communicates to stakeholders and investors the company's developments in terms of social and environmental responsibility

## Related Job Titles

- Director CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility)
- Head of CSR department - Corporate Social Responsibility
- Sustainable development manager
- Sustainable development and CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) manager
