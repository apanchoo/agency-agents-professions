# Vehicle after-sales technical manager

## Scope

The Vehicle After-sales Technical Manager is responsible for all after-sales management and organization activities at a dealership. Carries out the management of the activity and ensures the overall monitoring of the commercial performance of the structure Ensures the management of the sector or one or more poles of the after-sales activity Supervises under his responsibility one or more supervisors Optimizes the organization of the workshop in order to improve efficiency and intervention times

## Related Job Titles

- Bodywork division manager
- Automotive technical team manager
- Automotive after-sales technical manager
- Motorcycle after-sales technical manager
- After-sales technical manager for road transport vehicles

## Activities and Skills

- Conduct annual individual and professional interviews
- Monitor the activity of the vehicle after-sales workshop by analyzing the results obtained (margin level, indicator, ratio)
- Recruit a collaborator
- Identify gaps between actual and required skills
- Use sales and activity analysis tools
- Optimize repair processes to reduce intervention time
- Manage the equipment and tools of the vehicle after-sales service workshop
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Manage an activity quality approach
- Organize and control a supply
- Organize inventories to optimize stock management
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Analyze the results obtained, the different margin levels and the reference indicators and ratios
- Manage and monitor the budget
- Track vehicle maintenance activity using dashboards
- Apply labor law rules
- Enforce legislation in vehicle maintenance operations
- Manage customer complaints and provide after-sales service
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Implement measures to improve customer satisfaction
- Choose and use analysis tools (prospecting, results, penetration, margins, etc.)
- Build and implement a corrective action plan to monitor the maintenance activity of the vehicle after-sales workshop
- Implement the change management project internally and externally
- Managing internal conflicts
- Respect production and repair deadlines
- Dealing with conflict situations between colleagues
