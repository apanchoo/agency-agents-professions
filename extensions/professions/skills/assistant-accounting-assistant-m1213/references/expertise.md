# Assistant / Accounting assistant

## Scope

The accounting assistant supports a company's financial operations by entering accounting entries, managing invoices, and tracking payments. Manages daily accounting operations Prepares invoices, tax returns and balance sheets Follows up on payments and collections Controls financial flows and prepares budgets Collaborates with auditors for annual audits Provides administrative support for financial matters

## Related Job Titles

- Accounting management agent
- Assistant / Accounting and budget assistant
- Accounting office assistant
- Assistant / Management assistant in accounting
- Administrative assistant / Administrative and accounting assistant
- Accounting Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Codify an invoice
- Collect the information necessary for establishing payroll (hours worked, absences, leave, bonuses, etc.)
- Record accounting operations in the account books (purchases, sales, receipts, payments, etc.)
- Manage expense reports: collect and check paper and/or electronic supporting documents, record or validate entries
- Participate in the preparation of accounting statements
- Proceed to cashout
- Prepare and declare VAT
- Process invoices and payments
- Record business revenue
- Prepare a bank reconciliation statement
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of accounting regulations
- Carry out accounting operations
- Process invoices and bank reconciliations
- Check the recording of banking transactions and regulatory accounting documents
- Respond to requests for information
- Guarantee the confidentiality of information
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Archive files and reference documents
- Prepare accounting documents
- Process and transmit encrypted or coded information
- Use digital tools
