# Tourism consultant

## Scope

The tourism consultant advises clients on destinations, itineraries and services to optimize their trips. Analyzes market trends and consumer behaviors Develops tourism development strategies adapted to the specific needs of destinations or businesses Proposes innovative solutions to improve the tourist experience and increase visitor satisfaction Collaborates with local stakeholders to promote tourist attractions and activities Organizes and participates in events and trade fairs to represent and promote destinations Conducts feasibility studies and profitability analyzes for new tourism projects
