# Nephrologist

## Scope

The nephrologist is a kidney specialist, he diagnoses and offers treatments for kidney diseases. Diagnoses and treats kidney diseases Monitors the development of kidney pathologies in patients Performs dialysis and supervises dialysis treatments Collaborates with other specialists on complex cases requiring a multidisciplinary approach Participates in research to improve treatments for kidney diseases

## Related Job Titles

- Nephrologist

## Activities and Skills

- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams for patient monitoring
- Advise patients on kidney disease prevention
- Educate patients about renal replacement therapy
- Teaching, developing skills
- Analyze complex medical images (MRI, scanner)
- Analyze medical test results to establish a diagnosis
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Optimizing treatment protocols in nephrology
- Caring for chronic kidney disease
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Monitor the evolution of patients' renal pathologies
- Monitor a person's health
- Conduct study and research work
- Update the patient's medical record
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage medical databases

## Professional Approach
