# Responsible for developing advertising assets

## Scope

The Advertising Asset Development Manager implements and deploys a poster site asset policy, on a regional or national scale. Identifies and analyzes market opportunities for advertising spaces Negotiates, contracts and monitors leases, supervising administrative monitoring Develops partnerships with public and private lessors Monitors and evaluates the performance of advertising spaces acquired and optimized Coordinates and supervises the teams involved in carrying out a display campaign
