# Director of prison integration and probation services (DSPIP)

## Scope

The Director of penitentiary integration and probation services mainly ensures the supervision, coordination and operation of penitentiary integration and probation services (SPIP), these services are the heart of the system for preventing recidivism and for the integration of people placed under the custody of the justice system (detainees or suspended sentences), he can also work within the interregional directorates of penitentiary services, at the national evaluation center (CNE), at the National School of Administration prison (ENAP), the Prison Employment Service (SEP) and the central administration of the Ministry of Justice but also in centers for modified sentences and in semi-release centers. Supervises and coordinates the activities of the integration and probation services Develops social reintegration strategies for prisoners Ensures monitoring and evaluation of probation programs Manages the human and material resources of the service Maintains collaboration with judicial institutions and other social organizations Ensures compliance with regulations and the rights of prisoners
