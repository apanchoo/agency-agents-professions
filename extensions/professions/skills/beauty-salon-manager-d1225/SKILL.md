## Scope

Directs and coordinates the activities of the institute to ensure optimal quality of service Supervises and trains a team of beauty professionals to guarantee high-level services Develops commercial strategies to attract and retain customers Ensures the daily management of the institute, including financial and administrative aspects Ensures the application of hygiene and safety standards for the well-being of customers and staff Innovates by offering new aesthetic services and treatments adapted to current trends
