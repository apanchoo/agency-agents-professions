# Cutting workshop team leader

## Scope

The Team Leader in the cutting workshop orchestrates cutting operations with precision and efficiency. Supervises and coordinates fabric cutting activities according to orders and technical specifications Optimizes the use of materials and reduces waste by efficiently planning cuts Ensures the training and maintenance of the skills of its team to guarantee quality and productivity Controls the conformity of the cuts made with respect to quality and precision requirements Manages the supply of materials and tools necessary for the cutting workshop Maintains a safe working environment that complies with current standards

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Communicate effectively with teams and management
- Supervise the activity of a team
- Provide technical training for teams
- Shape fabric, leather on cutting table and adjust positioning (alignment)
- Mark the location of mounting notches on cut parts
- Carry out processing operations on skins
- Carry out manual cutting of parts for prototypes or small series
- Select the tools (saw blades, knives, etc.), mount them and adjust them (position, tension, etc.)
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Ensure regulatory compliance of production processes
- Control the quality of products during production
- Train teams to current environmental standards
- Identify anomalies, manufacturing defects
- Monitor inventory and report needs
- Sort rolls of fabric, skins, etc. according to their characteristics (color, size, defect, etc.) and form batches to cut
- Process order and production information
- Analyze production performance and propose improvements
- Establish performance indicators for quality monitoring
- Implement strategies to achieve production goals
- Optimize organizational performance
- Argument and defend production processes to management
- Encourage collaboration between team members
- Promote sustainable development practices in production
- Managing internal conflicts
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events in production

## Professional Approach
