# Public works team leader

## Scope

The public works team leader carries out his job in the field, under the responsibility of the site manager and organizes the daily tasks of his team on a site or part of the site. Coordinates and controls the work of the team for which he is responsible Prepares and carries out (or has carried out) the site according to the constraints and obstacles of the terrain Monitors the progress of the work and checks its quality Assists in the reception of the work at the end of the site and ensures the restoration of the surroundings Supervises and leads the team from a technical and organizational point of view and monitors the working climate and the transmission of know-how

- Black Products Team Leader

## Activities and Skills

- Check the application of the work plan
- Coordinate the activity of a team
- Coordinate work with other trades
- Carry out administrative monitoring (clocking, etc.) and define schedules
- Evaluate team performance
- Organize and control team activity according to priorities and contingencies
- Participate in the creation of teams and distribute work according to the skills and authorizations of each person
- Anticipate recruitment needs in conjunction with the works manager
- Use modeling and simulation software
- Analyze technical incidents and propose improvements
- Establish the schedule and establish the methods of execution of the site
- Analyze and follow the execution plans transmitted by the works manager (plans, rates, delivery deadlines, budget)
- Provide elements participating in the establishment of as-built plans
- Provide technical support to site teams
- Ensure the restoration of the site
- Check the progress of the work
- Define and ensure the correct location of the construction site in the work area according to the execution plans (ground marking, tracing of the work, identification of networks, marking)
- Carry out acceptance of work with the client
- Regularly carry out site reviews with the works manager in order to monitor the overall progress of the site
- Organize and adapt construction sites taking into account the work environment (team, materials, equipment, weather, etc.)
- Organize the operations to be carried out in order to respect deadlines and site constraints
- Participate in the evaluation of the means to be implemented for the execution of the work (men, machines, tools, supplies)
- Prepare the intervention and install the site
- Respect the planned schedule in the execution of tasks
- Prepare and transmit daily site reports to the site manager
- Ensure compliance with implementation or construction techniques specific to the site (civil engineering, VRD, roads, railways, etc.)
- Supervise the withdrawal of the site at the end of the work and the restoration of the premises
- Ensuring safety on the construction site
- Implement security devices, arrange access routes
- Monitor compliance with safety standards
- Check that the equipment and materials used have been checked and that the workers are wearing safety equipment
- Check the conformity of execution of the works
- Define supply needs
- Manage the stock of materials necessary for the team's activity and place orders with suppliers
- Receive materials in order to organize production
- Carry out reporting of your activity
- Choose equipment and materials taking into account environmental requirements
- Promote the reuse or recycling of recovered materials
- Identify tasks that could have an environmental impact and propose preventive measures
- Supervise the evacuation and recycling of waste from the site
- Assist the client in site reception
- Attend site meetings
- Describe a problem relating to your activity and its practical solutions
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Report on your activity
- Transmit the rules and instructions to your team and ensure their understanding
- Act quickly in the event of an incident
- Alert those concerned by malfunctions, risks or non-compliances
- Managing internal conflicts
- Plan and adapt your activity by regulating that of others
- Propose an adjustment of resources (human and technical)
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Use digital communication tools
