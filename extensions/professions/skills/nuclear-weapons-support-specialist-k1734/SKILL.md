# Nuclear weapons support specialist

## Scope

The nuclear weapons support specialist is involved in the maintenance and security of nuclear weapon systems. Provide technical support for the operation and maintenance of the deterrent weapon system and associated transport Manage the configuration of equipment and documentation in force Develop guidelines for maintenance operations and analyze technical facts. Participate in staff training and implementation of safety procedures. Develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.
