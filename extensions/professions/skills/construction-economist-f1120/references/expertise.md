# Construction economist

## Scope

The Construction Economist plays a key role in cost control, planning and monitoring of construction projects. Evaluates the costs of construction and renovation projects Optimizes budgets by proposing the best options for materials and techniques Collaborates with architects and engineers to ensure the economic and technical feasibility of projects Advises clients on the financial aspects of projects Monitors the evolution of costs during the execution of the work Participates in the drafting of calls for tenders

## Related Job Titles

- Construction design technician
- Construction design technician in climate and energy engineering
- Construction price studies technician
