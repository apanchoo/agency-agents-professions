# Agricultural aid in crop production

## Scope

Agricultural aid in crop production provides assistance to farmers in farm management. Participates in the preparation of soils and the planting of various crops Ensures routine maintenance of crops by controlling growth and applying the necessary treatments Carries out the manual harvest of cereals, vegetables and plants Participates in sorting and packaging operations of harvested products Performs maintenance on tools, equipment and installations

- Store a product
- Sort the harvested plants according to their future use
