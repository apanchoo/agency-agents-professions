## Scope

The Quality Safety Environment Manager -QSE- in industry ensures the continuous optimization of processes to guarantee quality, safety and respect for the environment. Defines and implements the company's QSE policy in accordance with regulations and standards in force Conducts internal and external audits to ensure compliance with QSE standards Trains and raises staff awareness of QSE practices to promote a culture of safety, environmental respect as well as the prevention of professional risks Analyzes incidents and non-conformities to determine the causes and implement corrective actions Monitors QSE performance indicators and suggests improvements to optimize processes Collaborates with the various departments to integrate QSE requirements into all company activities
