# Assistant / Stylist assistant

## Scope

In support of the creative team, the stylist assistant monitors and provides assistance at each stage of conceptualization. Assists the stylist in trend research Creates trend and collection repositories Participates in the selection of fabrics and materials for new creations Collaborates in carrying out tests and monitoring manufacturing prototypes Contributes to the coordination between the different departments (production, artistic direction, etc.) May be required to manage relationships with suppliers and external partners
