# Traffic manager

## Scope

The Traffic manager is in charge of programming and technical management of spaces and online advertising campaigns. Defines and implements traffic strategies to maximize website visibility Analyzes campaign performance and adjusts strategies in real time Collaborates with marketing teams to align campaigns with business objectives Uses analytics tools to monitor traffic and identify opportunities for improvement Manages advertising campaign budget to optimize ROI Stays up to date with the latest trends and technologies in traffic management
