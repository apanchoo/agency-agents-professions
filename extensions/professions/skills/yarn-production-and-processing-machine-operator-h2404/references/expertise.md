# Yarn production and processing machine operator

## Scope

The Yarn Production and Transformation Machine Operator is involved in spinning, texturing, stretching, twisting or yarn conditioning operations. Controls production parameters, adjusts equipment settings and optimizes their operation according to technical specifications Monitors production operations, identifies anomalies and intervenes in the event of a malfunction Monitors the quality of the yarns produced and ensures their compliance with production requirements Performs first-level maintenance of machines to guarantee their proper functioning and applies safety instructions
