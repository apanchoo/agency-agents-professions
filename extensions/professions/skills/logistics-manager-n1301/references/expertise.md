# Logistics manager

## Scope

The Logistics Manager coordinates and optimizes the management of product flows on an operational site, for all movements of entry, exit and handling of goods (including their packaging) and ensures constant communication with other departments of the company or actors in the supply chain, in relation to quality of service and traceability issues. Plans and coordinates goods reception, storage and delivery operations Contributes to optimizing operational costs and deadlines by coordinating operational performance Supervises, manages locally and trains operational teams to guarantee the efficiency of logistics processes Ensures compliance of logistics operations with current regulations Implements technological solutions to automate and improve logistics processes
