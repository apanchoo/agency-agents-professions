# Leather colorist

## Scope

The leather colorist applies chemicals to dye the leather, either on the surface or deep. Develops colors, generally from customer samples Develops the mixture of dyes and chemicals necessary for the industrial production of coloring on leather Controls the result obtained, and if necessary adjusts the color Complies with environmental and safety standards in the treatment of hides

## Related Job Titles

- Applicator in tannery and tannery
- Leather finishing colorist
- Leather dyeing colorist

## Activities and Skills

- Tutor young people in training
- Mix colors to obtain precise shades
- Provide quality and production monitoring support
- Design procedures, protocols, operating methods
- Determine preparation or finishing interventions depending on the materials, products (hides, leather, etc.) and defects identified
- Develop a coloring formula, based on indications provided by the research and development department (in structures where it exists)
- Perform and verify general or applied mathematics calculations
- Adjust settings in case of printing defects or color deviations
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Ensure the deep dyeing of the leather, using a fuller
- Ensure the finishing of the leather: apply several layers of primer to the surface in order to standardize the color, improve the visual appearance of the leather and give it its surface properties
- Unload a machine at the end of production
- Do color tests with a manual spray gun
- Select raw materials based on the color of the support used and the compatibilities between pigments and dyes
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Monitor for wear or failure
- Establish operating procedures specific to carrying out operations
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Check the conformity of achievements, make corrections
- Detect defects on items
- Carry out the required checks in the light cabin (cabin equipped with standardized lighting)
- Carry out measurements on a spectrocolorimeter
- Carry out the color conformity check
- Validate color
- Optimize production flows
- Meet deadlines and deadlines
- Communicate effectively with customers to understand their needs
- Relay information
- Report on your activity
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Alert of a malfunction or risky situation
- Read, write, count
- Respect procedures, operating methods and instructions
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Respect safety rules
