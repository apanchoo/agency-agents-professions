# Cycle store manager

## Scope

The cycle store manager supervises the work of a team made up of cycle technical advisors and cycle mechanics (workshop/store). Develops the budget for the store and the cycle workshop, organizes inventories, and carries out stock management Is in permanent contact with the suppliers of parts, equipment (cycle and cyclist) and the sales representatives of the cycle manufacturers Organizes and supervises the establishment of the store Takes care of customer complaints and is also responsible for guarantee follow-ups Also offers the customer financing and insurance solutions for the cycle Participates in responses to calls for tenders within the framework of fleet deployments cycles for private and public companies

## Activities and Skills

- Support employees in achieving objectives
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Conduct annual individual and professional interviews
- Organize work within store teams or cycle maintenance sites
- Monitor the activity of cycle store employees
- Recruit a collaborator
- Supporting a new arrival employee when they take up their position
- Co-build the skills development plan with the employee
- Update the technical and commercial documentation of the store or cycle maintenance site
- Manage equipment and materials at cycle maintenance sites
- Contribute to the construction of an activity quality approach to increase customer or supplier satisfaction
- Organize inventories to optimize stock management
- Conclude and invoice the sale of the cycle or electrically assisted bicycle
- Build a corrective action plan for the sales and maintenance activity of cycles and cycle products
- Establish a maintenance plan, an inventory and purchase renewal proposals
- Contribute to the development and monitoring of the cycle store budget
- Apply labor law rules
- Enforce legislation in sales and maintenance operations of cycles and cycle products
- Respect the general conditions of sale
- Process cycle or cycle product warranties
- Handle disputes and customer or supplier complaints related to the sale of cycles and cycle products
- Promote soft mobility and eco-responsible transport solutions
- Greet the customer in a positive manner, on site and remotely
- Complete the main sale by offering equipment and accessories to the customer
- Actively listen to the information communicated by the customer, on the telephone or in the sales area
- Analyze the needs of the cycle store customer
- Bring technical and commercial expertise to responses to calls for tenders
- Argument the technical characteristics of electrically assisted cycles and bicycles
- Define a commercial action plan for cycle stores
- Organize and arrange the cycle sales area
- Propose financing and insurance solutions to customers of the cycle store
- Write a product sheet to liven up and update the store's website
- Analyze the results (margin level, indicators, ratios) of the sales and maintenance activity of the cycles and products of the cycle
- Define a change management strategy
- Implement the change management project internally and externally
- Deal with conflict situations, in order to move towards a peaceful climate
