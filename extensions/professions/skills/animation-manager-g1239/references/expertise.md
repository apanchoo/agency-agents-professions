# Animation manager

## Scope

The entertainment manager is in charge of organizing and implementing fun and educational activities for all ages. Designs and implements animation programs adapted to different audiences Supervises and coordinates the team of facilitators to ensure the smooth running of activities Ensures administrative and budgetary management of animation projects Develops partnerships and local networks to enrich the range of activities Ensures compliance with safety standards and the quality of the services provided Can participate directly in the animation of specific activities
