# Manufacturing technician in the food industry

## Scope

The Food Industry Manufacturing Technician ensures the quality and safety of food products throughout the production chain. Supervises and optimizes food production processes Controls the compliance of raw materials and finished products with food safety standards Participates in the continuous improvement of manufacturing processes to increase productivity and reduce costs Conducts regular tests and analyzes to prevent risks of contamination or non-compliance Ensures training and compliance with procedures by production staff

## Related Job Titles

- Technician responsible for manufacturing in the food industry

## Activities and Skills

- Provide technical support to quality, maintenance, methods, research and development services
- Teaching, developing skills
- Collect samples for analysis
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Plan and interpret the realization of measurements, analyzes and results
- Collaborate with suppliers for product quality
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Ensure compliance of equipment with current standards
- Check the proper functioning and cleanliness of equipment
- Diagnose the nature and origin of incidents and implement corrective measures
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Act quickly if non-compliance is detected
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Analyze the quality of a product according to current regulations and specifications
- Control the quality of products during production
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Identify anomalies or malfunctions in production
- Implement corrective actions in the event of non-compliance
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Ensures the application of good hygiene practices by staff (wearing clothing, hand washing, etc.)
- Check the conformity of product packaging (tightness, weight, temperature, etc.)
- Check the organoleptic characteristics (successions of sensations, olfactory pyramid, balance, stability, etc.) of the final product
- Complete the conformity control, traceability and quality monitoring documents
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure compliance with safety and hygiene standards
- Monitor regulatory developments in food safety
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Analyze machine performance indicators
- Collaborate with teams to optimize processes
- Arguing for the adoption of new production practices
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Ensure technological and regulatory monitoring
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
