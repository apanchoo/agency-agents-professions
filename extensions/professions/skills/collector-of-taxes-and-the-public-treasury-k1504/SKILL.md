## Scope

The Collector of Taxes and the Public Treasury is important for the management of public finances. Collects taxes on behalf of the State and local authorities Manages public funds and ensures their proper allocation Controls expenditure and revenue operations within public services Participates in the development of budgets and their monitoring Ensures the keeping of public accounts and their compliance with accounting rules Can advise communities on their financial management

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Support taxpayers by providing them with information on their tax rights and obligations
- Community advisor
- Control the regularity and compliance of payments made by public administrations
- Raise awareness about the rational use of public funds
- Control the regularity of public accounts
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Supervise the execution of state and local government expenditures
- Supervise debt recovery procedures
