# Telesecretary

## Scope

The Telesecretary, a professional essential to the management of communications and agendas, ensures quality customer service and contributes to the professional image of the company and its clients, liberal professions and businesses. Manages incoming and outgoing telephone calls on behalf of one or more companies Ensures appointment scheduling and management of medical and other diaries Writes and sends emails and administrative documents May perform data entry and database updating tasks

## Related Job Titles

- Telephone secretary

## Activities and Skills

- Supporting a new arrival employee when they take up their position
- Transcribe voice messages to text
- Update a file, a database
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Use appointment scheduling software
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Manage electronic diaries
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Detect emergency situations and manage them according to customer instructions
- Manage telephone reception
- Identify and process a customer request
- Complete customer documents
- Respect response and processing times
- Answer phone calls, emails, chat and inform customers
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Ensure an interface role between the different departments of the company
- Collaborate with other remote secretaries to manage workflows
- Guarantee the confidentiality of information
- Write professional messages and emails
- Know how to interact remotely with digital communication tools (messaging, videoconferencing)
- Using English in a professional context
- Archive files and reference documents
- Arguing to resolve conflicts or misunderstandings
- Classify documents
- Follow standardized response procedures
- Use digital tools
