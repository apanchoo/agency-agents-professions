# Ski lift driver

## Scope

The ski lift operator plays a crucial role in the operation and safety of cable transport installations. Supervises and controls the operation of ski lifts and associated equipment Ensures the safety of users when boarding, disembarking and during transport Carries out first-level preventive and corrective maintenance operations Responds to emergency or degraded situations Informs and assists users on how to use the facilities Can coordinate a team and train new employees

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Communicate with teams in the field to coordinate operations
- Train new employees in operational procedures
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Use specific software
- Adapt operations according to weather conditions
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Intervene quickly in the event of a malfunction or degraded situation
- Maintain boarding/disembarkation areas in good condition
- Check the operation of a safety device
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Implement emergency alert procedures and other predefined measures in the event of an incident
- Organize appropriate measures in the event of a breakdown, incident, fall or weather change
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Monitor the proper functioning of equipment during the operation
- Manage passenger flows to optimize waiting times
- Ensure the safety of passengers during boarding and disembarkation
- Operate the ski lift systems, monitor the passage of cabins, poles and the flow of passengers
- Transport passengers
- Ensure the traceability of interventions
- Write incident reports
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Control access to ski lifts
- Coordinate with emergency services in the event of an accident
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Respond to requests for information
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Communicate effectively with passengers and/or their attendants
- Inform passengers of safety instructions
- Relay information
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
