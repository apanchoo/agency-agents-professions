# Interpreter

## Scope

The Interpreter orally translates a speech or exchanges, from the language of the speaker into the language of the listener. Provides translation during conferences, meetings or events, respecting the nuances and intentions of the speaker Facilitates liaison between people who do not share the same language Adapts their working method according to the context and the type of interpretation required Can specialize in a specific field such as legal, medical or technical

## Related Job Titles

- Business interpreter
- Conference interpreter
- Liaison interpreter
- Sign language interpreter
- Interpreter-translator

- Coordinate your interventions with other interpreters
- Accurately interpret cultural nuances
- Master the translation of technical terms in specialized fields
- Master the conventions and nuances specific to each language
- Translate speech in real time at international conferences
- Simultaneously translate the words of an interlocutor
- Develop specialized glossaries for translation or interpreting projects
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Conduct research and prepare to handle complex topics
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Establish the terms of a service
- Carry out an assessment of a service with the client
- Communicate deadlines and costs to clients
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Write one or more foreign languages
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Adapt the working method according to the type of meeting (conference, meeting, liaison interview)
- Respect ethical and confidentiality standards
- Develop your skills in rare languages
- Mobilize your concentration throughout a process
- Organize and develop your professional network
- Know how to manage mental fatigue during long work sessions
- Follow and remember fast-paced speeches
