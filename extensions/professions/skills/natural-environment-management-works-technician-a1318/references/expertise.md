# Natural environment management works technician

## Scope

The natural environment management works technician carries out management operations to maintain the ecological functionalities of spaces and natural heritage and/or restores the ecosystems of degraded environments in their environment (wetlands, forests, meadows, coastlines, geological heritage, etc.) within the framework of specific projects or the management plan of a protected natural area. Implements work to maintain the ecological functionalities of its environment Implements projects and ecological restoration work for natural spaces and ecosystems Ensures planning and site monitoring of its environment As a field player within protected natural spaces, is required to participate in naturalist monitoring (measurement, monitoring) of natural spaces
