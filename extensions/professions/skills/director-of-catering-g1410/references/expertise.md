# Director of Catering

## Scope

The Catering Director ensures the good management and quality of all catering services (fast and traditional restaurants) to guarantee a remarkable culinary experience. Supervises and coordinates the activities of catering staff to ensure quality service Develops commercial and marketing strategies to increase the profitability of establishments Manages budgets, purchases and stocks of food and beverages Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards Develops innovative menus in collaboration with chefs Manages relationships with suppliers and negotiates contracts to obtain the best products

## Related Job Titles

- Catering manager
- Restaurateur caterer
