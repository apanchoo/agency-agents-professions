# Responsible for the execution of public expenditure

## Scope

The Public Expenditure Execution Officer is important for the financial management of public funds, he acts for the general directorates, services and public establishments of the Ministry or other administrations (ordinary, investment expenditure, European funds, public contracts, civil servants' remuneration and pensions, etc.). Ensures the management and monitoring of public expenditure to ensure their compliance with laws and regulations Controls financial commitments and ensures their regularization Participates in the development of budgets and forecasting expenditure Collaborates with different services and organizations to optimize expenditure processes Conducts internal audits to ensure the transparency and efficiency of expenditure Proposes improvements to public financial management procedures
