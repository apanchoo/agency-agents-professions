# Social development officer

## Scope

The Social Development Officer implements social policies and services to improve the living conditions of local populations Identifies the social needs and expectations of vulnerable and disadvantaged people Develops and implements social development projects in collaboration with local partners Promotes the social and professional integration of individuals through various programs and initiatives Coordinates actions with public institutions, associations and economic actors Evaluates the impact of actions carried out on the ground and adjusts local development strategies accordingly Organizes events and meetings to raise awareness and mobilize around social issues

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Implement and lead socio-cultural actions
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate local community initiatives
- Facilitate and create the conditions for cooperation
- Implement collaborative work tools and methods
- Implement the transformation actions necessary for changes
- Analyze the social and medico-social needs of individuals
- Advise an organization, a structure
- Develop projects contributing to the development of practitioners in an educational, care, health and inclusion dynamic
- Implement social reintegration initiatives
- Plan and organize community events for social cohesion
- Carry out public relations, dissemination and promotion of information actions
- Carry out public satisfaction studies
- Find solutions using problem-solving methods
- Design and implement prevention actions, information on health and psychosocial risks
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Write, launch and manage a call for projects
