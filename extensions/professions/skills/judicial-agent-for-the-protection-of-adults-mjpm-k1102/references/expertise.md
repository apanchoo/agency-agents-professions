# Judicial agent for the protection of adults (MJPM)

## Scope

The Judicial Agent for the Protection of Adults ensures the protection of vulnerable adults in the exercise of their rights while preserving their autonomy. Represents and defends the interests of protected adults before institutions and courts Manages the property and income of protected persons while ensuring their material and financial well-being Ensures legal and administrative monitoring of cases in conjunction with various social and legal professionals Intervenes regularly within the framework of different protection measures if necessary Collaborates with social, medical services and families for comprehensive care of vulnerable persons Informs and advises protected adults on their rights and administrative procedures
