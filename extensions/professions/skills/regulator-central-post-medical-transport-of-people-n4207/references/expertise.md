# Regulator central post medical transport of people

## Scope

The central medical transport station controller receives calls and requests for medical transport by ambulance from injured, sick, elderly or disabled people, and ensures efficient and secure coordination of medical transport. Coordinates medical transport operations to guarantee a rapid response adapted to the needs of patients Manages communications between the different actors in medical transport to optimize travel Ensures the monitoring of vehicles and personnel on mission to maintain the quality of service Analyzes emergency situations and makes appropriate decisions for patient safety Plans itineraries taking into account time and distance constraints Ensures compliance with safety standards and medical protocols during transport

## Activities and Skills

- Communicate with teams in the field to coordinate operations
- Coordinate the activity of a team
- Participate in regular team meetings
- Evaluate staff training needs
- Train new employees in operational procedures
- Use medical transport management software
- Deploy and implement palliative measures in the event of a disrupted situation (passenger information, replacement transport, staff safety, etc.)
- Manage priorities in a crisis situation
- Monitor the condition of the transport equipment used
- Check the validity of qualification of means and people (authorization, authorization, approval)
- Assign a vehicle fleet
- Analyze transport flows in real time
- Ensure the traceability of transport in the company's information system until the end of patient care
- Liaise with customers for transportation services
- Controlling a transport operation
- Manage ambulance schedules, based on medical prescriptions and emergency requests
- Inform, where applicable, the regulating doctor, medical services and families of the transport service and, where applicable, of the health condition of the person transported
- Organize alternative routes in the event of disruptions
- Organize a transport logistics operation
- Plan and distribute transport requests based on available ambulances and ambulance crews
- Plan medical transport: ambulance or Light Medical Vehicle (VSL)
- Plan, coordinate and optimize rounds within the framework of compliance with legal and conventional rules on ambulance workers' working hours
- Collect and record information or requests concerning transport services and their specificities
- Design the driver assignment schedule on the network or intervention area
- Perform daily activity reports
- Manage on-board documents and administrative formalities
- Technically assist drivers (route modifications, delays) in the event of a breakdown or accident
- Prioritize requests based on the degree of urgency and operational constraints
- Manage a vehicle fleet
- Work in interaction with the paramedics to whom all useful information is transmitted on the conditions of care of the person to be transported
- Optimize routes based on emergencies
