# Chef / Head waiter

## Scope

The head waiter orchestrates table service within the restaurant. Organizes and prepares the setting up of his row before the service Leads and coordinates the activities of his team of servers Welcomes and advises customers with professionalism Takes orders and ensures their proper execution in the kitchen Ensures that table service runs smoothly Checks customer satisfaction and manages any complaints

## Related Job Titles

- Demi-chief / Demi-chief of row

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Ensure a debriefing with the team
- Pay attention to the successful integration of each new employee
- Carry out the setup of the room and the office
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Participate in the management of food and beverage stocks
- Participate in an inventory
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Manage a cash register
- Proceed to cashout
- Enter an order on computer
- Handle complaints professionally
- Assist and help people while waiting for help
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Greet the customer and install them
- Advise the customer on their choice of drinks or dishes according to their tastes
- Serving dishes at the table
- Guarantee customer satisfaction
- Manage customer feedback to improve services
- Take a customer order
- Offer additional products
- Inform the customer about the composition of the products, allergens, origin
- Ensure careful presentation of dishes
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Express yourself with politeness and courtesy
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate leadership
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
