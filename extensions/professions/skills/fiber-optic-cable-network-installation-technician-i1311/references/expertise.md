# Fiber optic cable network installation technician

## Scope

The fiber optic cable network installation technician deploys fiber optic communications networks generally in the public domain. Installs the optical fiber in accordance with the ordering parties' operator specifications Ensures quality control of the optical link Analyzes malfunctions and writes measurement and acceptance specifications Collaborates with project teams to plan and execute network deployments

## Related Job Titles

- Fiber optic installer
- Telecom deployment technician
- Fiber optic technician

## Activities and Skills

- Configure or configure telecommunications equipment, low currents
- Read and interpret an instruction sheet and an execution plan
- Complete the intervention documents
- Update and upgrade telecommunications or low current equipment
- Assemble the elements of a machine or equipment
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Carry out optical fiber welding operations
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Select the hardware based on the diagram, wiring plan or device power
- Connect electronic equipment
- Connect a junction box
- Install ducts and wiring
- Install and connect fiber optic cables
- Install the equipment on site and connect it to external networks
- Install an internet connection
- Carry out and interpret optical network measurements (reflectometry, photometry, etc.)
- Make hydraulic, electrical and mechanical connections for equipment and accessories
- Test optical connections to ensure they are working properly
- Change or repair a defective part or assembly
- Diagnose malfunctions on a fiber optic network
- Diagnose, prevent and locate malfunctions, remedy them or recommend alternative solutions
- Check the conformity of the installation on site, help desk, hot line
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Check the assembly and wiring (value of components, electrical continuity, etc.)
- Prepare site reports with the works manager or the client
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Respect safety rules
