# Seller of stationery and creative hobbies

## Scope

The seller of stationery and creative hobbies promotes its product expertise in order to guide customers towards the tools and materials best suited to their projects. Offers suitable products (school supplies, fine stationery, artistic materials, creative hobbies) Gives technical advice on the use of materials (paints, papers, tools, accessories) Produces seasonal or thematic highlights (back to school, scrapbooking, parties, bullet journal, etc.) Participates in setting up creative workshops or product demonstrations

## Related Job Titles

- Seller of stationery bookstore items
- Stationery seller
- Supervisory salesperson
- Art supplies salesperson
- Salesperson in creative hobbies

## Activities and Skills

- Explain the operation and maintenance of specialized machines (laminators, paper cutters, Cricut/Silhouette type cutting machines)
- Disable anti-theft for an item
- Ensure cleanliness and order in the sales area
- Carry out the shelving
- Restock the shelves and ensure the proper presentation of items
- Monitor sales figures and carry out associated reporting
- Proceed to cashout
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Manage merchandise returns
- Build customer loyalty by offering offers and personalized support
- Inform about promotions, new products and services offered
- Recommend the appropriate tools (scissors, cutters, technical markers, brushes, punches, stamps) according to customer projects
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Adapt a sales strategy to a target or a product
- Demonstrate the use of creative products by carrying out small events or workshops in store
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Highlight products in windows and in stores
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Participate in merchandising and point-of-sale animation operations
- Carry out a commercial animation
- Follow creative market trends (new materials, emerging techniques, leading brands)
- Develop in-depth product knowledge (materials, styles, trends)
- Identify the different types of paper, weights, textures and uses (scrapbooking, watercolor, calligraphy, etc.)
