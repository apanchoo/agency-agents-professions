# Goldsmith Assembler

## Scope

The goldsmith assembler ensures the assembly of small or large series parts, relying on technical mastery in welding and brazing. Proceeds with the assembly of assemblies and sub-assemblies by brazing and tin welding Can work on various pieces of goldsmith's work
