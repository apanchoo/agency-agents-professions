# Expert surveyor

## Scope

An expert in measurement, land and law, the surveyor carries out legal and technical missions in land, town planning and regional development in order to guarantee a sustainable living environment. Produces topographical plans and documents to demarcate, represent and evaluate land properties (cadastre, survey documents, geographical databases, etc.) Contributes to updating and conservation of data relating to land ownership Carry out procedures related to requests for urban planning authorization (urban planning certificate, planning permit, building permit, etc.) Provides advice and support missions for a clientele of individuals, local authorities or businesses (developers, construction companies, promoters, etc.) Can ensure the management and development of real estate or land assets

## Related Job Titles

- Land surveyor
- Engineer / Surveyor
- Topographic engineer

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the intervention of multidisciplinary teams
- Supervise a team of topographic surveyor technicians
- Advise an organization, a structure
- Contribute to improving the local living environment
- Carry out advisory missions to communities, developers or building owners
- Inventory construction constraints (regulatory, technical, environmental)
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Draw up topographical plans at different scales (mass, division, street body, execution and proofing plans for works, etc.)
- Write the file of the works executed (compliance of the implementations) and the technical reports
- Carry out precision measurements (metrology) and control structures (bridges, buildings, etc.)
- Carry out and record metric surveys of topographical elements (land leveling, distances, natural reliefs, settlements)
- Identify land characteristics from topographical surveys
- Carry out land studies or documents and establish architectural plans (plot division, land development, etc.)
- Carry out a topographic survey of land (measurements, digital models, etc.)
- Use and adjust geology and topography machines or equipment
- Check the conformity of the implementation of an achievement
- Check compliance of projects with existing planning rules and easements
- Perform daily activity reports
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Evaluate the land value of properties by integrating environmental issues (risks, mobility, etc.)
- Apply current environmental standards
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Monitor legislative and regulatory developments
- Advise on legal and technical aspects of land transactions
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Propose solutions to prevent and resolve land disputes and neighborhood conflicts
- Encourage and support local initiatives in terms of sustainable development and improvement of the living environment
- Promote sustainable practices in the sober land management of development projects
- Assist and advise elected officials in their development choices (town centers, VRD, green spaces, etc.)
- Design and manage a project
- Coordinate land development and optimization projects
- Identify potential public and private partners in a territory
- Raise team awareness of the issues of Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR)
