# Marina Agent

## Scope

The marina agent ensures the management and security of the installations within the port area. Manages berth reservations (annual and transit) via port management tools and the reception of boaters Ensures the safety of people, boats and port installations Coordinates the movements of boats and optimizes the use of mooring spaces Ensures basic maintenance and the proper functioning of port equipment Conducts regular rounds to check the condition of the installations and report anomalies Applies port and environmental regulations (waste collection, fuel) Maintains relationships with sea rescue services in the event of an emergency
