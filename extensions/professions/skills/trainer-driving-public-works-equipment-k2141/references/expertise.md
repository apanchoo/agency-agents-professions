# Trainer driving public works equipment

## Scope

The public works equipment driving trainer provides training for qualified operators. Provides theoretical and practical courses on the operation and maintenance of public works machinery Ensures the safety of learners by supervising practical exercises in the field Adapts training programs according to technological developments in machinery Evaluates the skills acquired by learners and their progress Prepares learners to obtain the necessary certifications to operate public works machinery Maintains up-to-date knowledge of safety regulations and new machines

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Adapt teaching methods to the needs of learners
- Advise companies and drivers on good safety and machine maintenance practices
- Correct assessments (exercises, practical work, exams)
- Determine the abilities of candidates to drive vehicles
- Teach the driving and handling of different types of construction equipment such as bulldozers, excavators, cranes, loaders, and compactors
- Teach the mechanical operation of different types of machines
- Teaching, developing skills
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Keep training materials and equipment up to date
- Implement practical assessments
- Organize practical training sessions
- Organize and supervise skills tests
- Prepare trainees for emergency situations
- Monitor learner progress
- Transmit theoretical bases, techniques and gestures by combining several teaching methods
- Transmit knowledge related to safety rules, machine operation, basic maintenance, as well as advanced driving and operating techniques
- Using simulators in training
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Carry out routine maintenance of machines (1st level: small repairs, troubleshooting)
- Check the compliance of machines with safety standards
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Supervise security at the training site
- Evaluate the skills acquired by trainees
- Evaluate learner progress
- Supervise practical driving exercises
- Ensure post-training follow-up of trainees
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Manage conflicts in the event of a dispute with a customer
- Respect safety rules
- Report any mechanical anomaly detected
- Monitor regulatory developments
- Regularly update your knowledge
- Maintain knowledge of machine safety up to date
- Follow the necessary training and certifications
