# Singer

## Scope

The Singer expresses emotions and stories through his voice, thus captivating his audience. Performs musical works solo or in a group in front of an audience or in the studio Works on your voice regularly to improve your vocal technique and interpretation Participates in rehearsals and recording sessions to prepare your performances Collaborates with other musicians, conductors and technicians to harmonize performances May participate in the creation of new musical compositions or adaptations
