# Eco-industrial site manager

## Scope

The eco-industrial site manager manages the activities of a site by adopting ecological and sustainable practices. Organizes, coordinates and controls means and processes with the aim of operating water treatment facilities (drinking, waste) Supervises daily waste treatment and recovery operations Ensures site compliance with environmental regulations Optimizes processes to maximize efficiency and reduce costs Supervises teams and coordinates activities between different departments Develops strategies to improve the sustainability and environmental impact of the site Maintains relationships with local authorities, customers and suppliers
