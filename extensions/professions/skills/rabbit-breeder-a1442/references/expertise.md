# Rabbit breeder

## Scope

The rabbit breeder raises and cares for rabbits to ensure their well-being and optimize production, while adopting sustainable agricultural breeding practices. Manages the reproduction of rabbits to maintain and improve bloodlines and according to production requirements Monitors the growth and development of rabbits Markets rabbits and derived products such as meat and fur Maintains breeding facilities in good condition Respects safety and hygiene standards to prevent disease
