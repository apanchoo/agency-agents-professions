# Bookseller

## Scope

The bookseller transmits his passion for books by guiding each person towards the reading that really suits them. Advises customers on book choices and new literary releases Manages book stocks and places orders with publishers Organizes cultural events such as meetings with authors or signing sessions Maintains the organization and attractive presentation of the bookstore May manage the financial and administrative aspects of the bookstore

## Related Job Titles

- Bookstore salesperson
- Book seller

## Activities and Skills

- Manage relations with media, partners and other departments
- Organize attractive and coherent presentation spaces
- Participate in book fairs and fairs
- Promote local literature
- Promote works via windows, social networks or events
- Use specific software
- Anticipate delivery times to ensure stock availability
- Define supply needs
- Receive and ship orders
- Proceed to cashout
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Welcome and inform customers about the works and services offered
- Advise readers on works suited to their tastes
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Host meetings, signings or cultural events in bookstores
- Classify and shelve the works according to standards and themes
- Order books from publishers and distributors
- Know publisher catalogs, literary trends and new releases
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Negotiate preferential rates with publishers
- Present and promote a product or service
- Follow current literary trends
- Select books and cultural products to reference
- Analyze book sales performance
- Develop and manage relationships or partnerships
