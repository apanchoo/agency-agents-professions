# Art mover

## Scope

At the heart of cultural and artistic exchanges, the art mover is an expert professional, whose mission is to move works of art of all formats, under the most advanced security conditions. Moves works safely Provides tailor-made packaging Works in close collaboration with owners, museums and cultural institutions Can carry out administrative procedures related to the movement of works of art

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Adapt your vehicle driving to avoid any shock, friction, vibration or impact
- Attend auctions in the sales rooms (set-up, installation and removal)
- Ensuring secure transport of works of art by truck, utility vehicle or car
- Create tailor-made protective boxes
- Manage administrative and customs formalities, essential for moving works of art around the world
- Implement specific packaging types and methods
- Obtain the necessary authorizations for “cultural property”
- Wear white gloves before handling high-value items
- Take into account humidity, temperature and atmospheric pressure when transporting works of art and paintings by plane, boat or land vehicle
- Take into account the dimensions of the work for successful delivery, storage and moving
- Protect the painting or art object with several layers of materials (crystal paper, foam, wooden box, armored cardboard, etc.)
- Provide insulation leaving the work of art free from possible chemical reactions during removal
- Identify sensitive areas of works that require reinforcement or special care
- Strengthen packaging to facilitate handling
- Carry out the move of valuable material goods (precious objects, works of art, furniture, objects, etc.)
- Carry out administrative procedures for specific temporary exit procedures for National Treasures
- Carry out Fine Arts formalities, in coordination with the Ministry of Culture and the Customs service
- Use specific handling equipment for moving precious or rare objects
- Collaborate with artists or managers to identify points of vigilance when moving the work
- Coordinate with security teams for the convoy
