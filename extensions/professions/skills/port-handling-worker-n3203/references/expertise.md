# Port handling worker

## Scope

The Port Handling Worker plays a key role in the efficient operation of ports, ensuring smooth loading and unloading operations. Loads and unloads goods from ships using various lifting equipment Ensures the storage and securing of cargo on board ships Controls and reports anomalies on the packages handled Participates in the maintenance of handling equipment and port equipment Follows strict safety instructions to prevent accidents Can coordinate teams during port handling operations

## Activities and Skills

- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Materialize the handling area to work safely while respecting the instructions during work on site
- Optimize storage spaces
- Organize and optimize the physical storage of products and materials within its scope of activity
- Carry out a maintenance operation
- Check the operation of devices, carry out first-level maintenance or inform the manager, the maintenance department in the event of malfunctions
- Supervise the smooth running of port operations
- Check the securing of the load
- Load, unload, handle products
- Check the conformity of transported goods
- Move and store products and materials according to quality and safety standards
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Handle material vertically at height, using winches or pulleys
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Supervise the loading and unloading of boats
- Use handling equipment safely
- Control and supervise the carrying out of dock operations (loading and unloading of goods, shipments)
- Coordinate loading and unloading operations
- Optimize loading space to secure transport
- Organize port handling operations
- Select and position the equipment and slings, winches, etc. according to the nature of the cargo
- Comply with national and international transport regulations
- Plan daily port activities
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
