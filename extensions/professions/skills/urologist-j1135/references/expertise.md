# Urologist

## Scope

The urologist is the urinary tract specialist, diagnosing infections of the urinary system Treats diseases of the kidneys, bladder, urethra and genitals Performs surgical procedures to correct or improve urological conditions Performs imaging examinations and functional tests to assess the urological health of patients Advises and prescribes medications to treat infections and disease prevention Collaborates with other specialists to provide comprehensive, multidisciplinary care
