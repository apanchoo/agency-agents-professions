# Responsible for military aircraft guidance

## Scope

The military aircraft guidance officer ensures maneuvers of planes and helicopters before and after each flight, on the ground and at sea, to support aircraft entering and exiting parking lots. Carry out ground guidance, assistance and parking operations for passing aircraft Maneuver aircraft on the take-off point of a ship Ensure the safety of personnel during ground maneuvers Develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out their profession

- Expert in the implementation of onboard aircraft on flight decks and hangars

- Aeromobility hold operational crew operator
- Air surface integration specialist
- Technician responsible for organizing the handling of aircraft on board the flight deck and hangars
