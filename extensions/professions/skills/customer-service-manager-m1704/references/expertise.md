# Customer service manager

## Scope

The Customer Service Manager plays an essential role in customer loyalty and satisfaction, by ensuring optimal relationship management. Manages and develops customer relationships to ensure customer satisfaction and loyalty Supervises and trains customer service teams to ensure quality service Analyzes customer feedback and complaints to improve services Collaborates with other departments to improve the overall customer experience Uses CRM tools to track and analyze customer interactions
