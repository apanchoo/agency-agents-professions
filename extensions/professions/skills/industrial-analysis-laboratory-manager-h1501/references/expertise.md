# Industrial analysis laboratory manager

## Scope

The Industrial Analysis Laboratory Manager supervises laboratory activities to ensure the quality of analyzes and the conformity of industrial products. Organizes measurement activities and interprets analysis results Develops and validates analysis methods in accordance with current standards Manages the laboratory team, training and evaluating technical staff Writes detailed reports and monitors the application of safety procedures, hygiene rules, quality and environment Collaborates with other departments to improve production processes during malfunctions
