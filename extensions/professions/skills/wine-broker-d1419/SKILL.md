## Scope

The Wine Broker is an intermediary specializing in the sale of wines between producers (winemakers) and professional buyers (traders, distributors, restaurateurs). Negotiates prices and conditions for purchasing wines from producers Liaises between wine producers and potential buyers Advises customers on wine choices based on their needs and preferences Organizes tastings to present products to customers Follows wine market trends to offer the best products May participate in the promotion of selected wines during specialized events

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.
