# Head bartender

## Scope

The head bartender passionately orchestrates the preparation of cocktails and entertainment in an elegant and friendly setting. Trains and supervises a team of bartenders Designs and prepares classic and innovative cocktails Ensures customer satisfaction and experience Manages the supply and inventory of drinks and accessories Maintains a clean and organized work environment May organize events and special promotions at the bar

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Evaluate the skills of your employees and ensure annual interviews
- Recruit and integrate a person
- Train new employees in operational procedures
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Create, organize or coordinate an event
- Organize cocktail tastings
- Create cocktail recipes with or without alcohol
- Innovate with new mixology techniques
- Optimize the bar layout
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Monitor the quality of drinks served
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Define supply needs
- Organize and supervise the supply and logistics of an event
- Manage a cash register
- Proceed to cashout
- Carry out invoicing
- Enter an order on computer
- Monitor the operating budget of the entrusted area
- Apply current regulations for bars and restaurants
- Manage complaints as well as commercial disputes
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Ensure the safety of property and people in the establishment
- Greet the customer and install them
- Advise the customer on their choice of drinks or dishes according to their tastes
- Create a welcoming and friendly atmosphere at the bar
- Take a customer order
- Offer additional products
- Inform the customer about the composition of the products, allergens, origin
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
