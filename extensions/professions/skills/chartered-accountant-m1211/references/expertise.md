# Chartered accountant

## Scope

The Chartered Accountant is responsible for financial management and guarantees the reliability of a company's accounts. Ensures the maintenance, monitoring and closing of annual accounts Advises companies on tax and social aspects Optimizes the financial management of structures Carry out audits and detailed financial analyzes Supervises compliance of accounting practices with current standards

## Related Job Titles

- Accounting Collaborator
