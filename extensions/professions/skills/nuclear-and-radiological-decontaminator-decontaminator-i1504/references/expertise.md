# Nuclear and radiological decontaminator / decontaminator

## Scope

The Nuclear and Radiological Decontaminator is responsible for decontaminating areas and equipment exposed to radioactive substances. Ensures the decontamination of nuclear or radiological sites and equipment following strict safety protocols Controls radiation and contamination levels to ensure the safety of intervention areas Participates in the preparation and implementation of emergency plans in the event of a radiological or nuclear incident Carries out cleaning and decontamination operations using specialized equipment Performs measurements and analyzes to evaluate the effectiveness of decontamination procedures May train and supervise other technicians on safety procedures and decontamination
