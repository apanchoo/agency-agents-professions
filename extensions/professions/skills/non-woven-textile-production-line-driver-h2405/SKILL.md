## Scope

The Nonwoven Textile Production Line Manager manages all the operations necessary for the transformation of raw materials into nonwoven textile products obtained without weaving or knitting. Supervises machines and manufacturing processes Ensures first-level maintenance of equipment to prevent breakdowns and failures Respects and enforces quality, safety and performance standards on the production line

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine
- Adjust machine and equipment parameters
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Control the quality of products during production
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Train new operators on the equipment
