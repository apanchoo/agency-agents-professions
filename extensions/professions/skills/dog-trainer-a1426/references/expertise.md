# Dog trainer

## Scope

The dog trainer, passionate about animal behavior, guides dog owners in the education of their canine companions to promote a harmonious relationship. Evaluates the behavior of dogs and establishes an appropriate education program Advises owners on education and training methods May participate in the breeding and training of guide dogs for visually impaired people

## Related Job Titles

- Dog trainer
- Guide dog trainer

## Activities and Skills

- Advise the dog's owner and teach him to better understand his animal and acquire authority
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Training dogs for competitions or specific activities such as agility
- Evaluate the dog's concentration, its attention, and teach it to respond to commands
- Evaluate the behavior of dogs and establish a suitable education program
- Regularly evaluate the dog's progress and adjust the education program
- Training dogs to support people with disabilities
- Teach an animal behaviors adapted to different situations
- Handle animals
- Organize and lead individual or group dog training sessions
- Respect animal welfare
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Identify a sick animal and inform the stakeholders concerned (owner, veterinarian, etc.)
- Maintain a clean and secure environment
- Prepare detailed reports on animal progress
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Welcome, inform and guide customers

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Listen, show empathy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
