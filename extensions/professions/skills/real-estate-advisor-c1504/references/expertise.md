# Real estate advisor

## Scope

The Real Estate Advisor carries out real estate and/or land transactions (rental, sale) and advises clients on the possibilities of acquisition, rental, sale according to real estate legislation. May be specialized in a field (commercial real estate, commerce, etc.) May manage a real estate agency or a network of agencies
