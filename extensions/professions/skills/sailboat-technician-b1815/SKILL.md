## Scope

Based on a descriptive plan, the sailboat technician independently carries out the manufacturing operations for sails, awnings and hoods (assembly, gluing, sewing and finishing of new or repaired sails); all these operations are carried out on modern or sometimes traditional materials. Carries out manufacturing and maintenance operations for sails (sails for sailboats, kitesurf sails, etc.) and awnings in compliance with the rules of quality, hygiene, safety, health and environmental protection. Takes measurements and outlines the work manually. Traces and cuts parts and carries out the assembly, gluing, seams and finishing touches necessary for the making or repair of sails, awnings and hoods. Implements assembly techniques for different traditional and more technical materials Uses different technical sewing equipment May also be required to carry out seamanship operations on exotic and synthetic fibers Installs and repairs the rigging of sailboats

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Assemble the sail parts by sewing or welding
- Choosing the right materials for making sails
- Carry out assembly operations by nailing, stapling, sewing, on leather and skin parts
- Carry out material cutting operations (template positioning, etc.) manually or using a machine
- Ensure the maintenance of workshop tools and equipment
- Make adjustments to the sails based on customer feedback
- Optimize manufacturing processes to reduce costs
- Advise customers on the choice of sails suited to their use
