# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Describe the expressed need, the public and the territory.
2. Analyze resources, actors and conditions of participation.
3. Co-construct a proposal for support or action.
4. Plan for evaluation, relays and confidentiality.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Modalities for supporting people in the social, medico-social and health fields
- Territorial health and social action policies
- Continuous improvement and quality approach

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Define the management and general organization methods of a health and/or medico-social structure
- Analyze the operation of a project carried out in a health and/or medico-social structure

## Deliverables

- Situation diagnosis
- Action project and partnerships
- Evaluation grid and relay

## Acceptance Criteria

- Needs are distinguished from interpretations
- Public participation is planned
- Sensitive information is limited to authorized use

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
