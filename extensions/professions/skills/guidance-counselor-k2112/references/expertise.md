# Guidance counselor

## Scope

The Guidance Advisor guides and supports individuals in their educational and professional journey, promoting opportunities suited to their profiles. Supports people in defining their professional or educational project Informs about the different sectors, training and professions Carries out skills assessments or personalized assessments Offers career discovery workshops Refers to structures or professionals according to specific needs Assists in preparing for interviews and writing CVs

## Activities and Skills

- Organize skills development workshops
- Support individuals in their educational and professional choices
- Helping individuals locate useful information
- Welcoming, reassuring, guiding, informing a person and those around them
- Analyze the orientation needs of individuals
- Design an orientation program
- Advise at each key stage (choice of options, applications, etc.) in the process of building the professional project
- Determine transferable skills with regard to a professional target
- Intervene in the reorientation phases in the event of difficulties or failure
- Carry out career guidance actions
- Conduct different types of interviews to support people in developing their career guidance project
- Propose training, orientation and support actions adapted to the person's profile
- Prepare and organize information actions on the labor market
- Carry out assessment or professional guidance services (investigation, evaluation, taking tests, etc.) and report the results
- Carry out a skills assessment
- Conduct an interview, an interview, an audition
- Use skills assessment tools
- Apply legal provisions regarding training
- Inform, inform, guide a person on the services offered
- Refer a person to bridging partners
- Inform about job market trends
- Develop partnerships with other educational structures
- Promote interaction between peers for the exchange of experiences
- Participate in multidisciplinary meetings
- Monitor new orientation methods
- Document case studies in guidance
- Inform about existing rights and validation systems
- Structuring, synthesizing information
- Use digital tools
- Ensure the confidentiality of shared information
