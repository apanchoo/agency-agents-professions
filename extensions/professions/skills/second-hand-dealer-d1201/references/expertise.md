# Second-hand dealer

## Scope

The second-hand dealer, passionate about history, discovers hidden treasures. Seeks and acquires antique or used items with added value Estimates the value of items for purchase and sale Organizes and manages stalls at flea markets or flea markets Sometimes restores items to increase their value Advises customers on the history and value of items May specialize in one type of item such as books, furniture, or other antiques

## Related Job Titles

- Antique dealer
- Bookseller
- Antiques salesman

## Activities and Skills

- Organize events such as fairs or markets
- Promote a work
- Analyze the state of conservation of objects
- Create technical documentation
- Update a file, a database
- Restoring old furniture while respecting original techniques
- Apply light restoration techniques to improve the appearance of objects
- Restore old books while respecting their integrity
- Restoring art objects intended for resale
- Restoring old leather or fabric bindings
- Ensuring the security of works or valuable objects during exhibitions
- Regularly evaluate inventory to identify unsold items
- Track orders, invoicing
- Maintain accurate records of transactions
- Proceed to cashout
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Develop partnerships with suppliers
- Analyze the authenticity and provenance of works of art
- Study and evaluate works, pieces, collectibles
- Appraise, value an art object
- Locate copies, reproductions of works of art
- Carry out accounting operations
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Advise clients on the history of objects
- Identify and process a customer request
- Determining fair sales prices
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Negotiate purchase prices for collectibles
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Prepare items for sale
- Present and promote a product or service
- Search for art, old or second-hand objects from professionals or individuals
- Know how to estimate the value of objects and recognize rare or authentic pieces
- Monitor collectibles market trends
- Sell or rent products or services
- Use social networks
- Have a good general knowledge of the different periods, styles and materials of ancient objects
