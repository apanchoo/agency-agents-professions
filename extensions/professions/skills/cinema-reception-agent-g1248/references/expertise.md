# Cinema reception agent

## Scope

The multi-purpose cinema agent carries out reception, ticketing, confectionery sales and animation of spaces within the cinema. Welcomes, informs, advises and directs spectators Sells tickets and products, in compliance with collection procedures Stocks the sales areas, ensuring the availability of products Ensures or controls the maintenance of rooms and common areas between screenings in order to guarantee hygiene and safety conditions to the public

- Film agent
- Versatile agent / Versatile cinema agent
