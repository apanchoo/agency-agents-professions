# Perfumery salesperson

## Scope

The perfume salesman embodies elegance and know-how in the field of fragrances and cosmetics. Advises customers and helps them choose from a variety of perfumes and cosmetic products Performs personalized demonstrations to highlight product characteristics Maintains the attractive presentation of the point of sale and ensures cleanliness of the spaces May organize events or special promotions to attract and retain customers

## Related Job Titles

- Perfumery Advisor
- Advisor in perfumery and cosmetics
- Cosmetics salesperson

## Activities and Skills

- Disable anti-theft for an item
- Define supply needs
- Manage the stock of equipment and cosmetic products
- Proceed to cashout
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Analyze customer needs to propose a complete routine (perfume, care)
- Advise customers on perfumes, treatments and cosmetics adapted to their needs
- Discover fragrances by offering tests and demonstrations
- Build customer loyalty by offering offers and personalized support
- Identify the olfactory preferences and expectations of each customer
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Highlight products in windows and in stores
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Participate in the implementation of commercial events or olfactory workshops
- Develop your knowledge of brands, olfactory families and new products

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Fragrance characteristics
- Hygiene and perfumery products

## Areas of expertise

- Knowledge of fashion trends
- Commercial argument
- Characteristics of faces and skin
- Customer relationship management (CRM)

## Professional techniques

- Customer feedback management
- Business Negotiation Techniques
- Sales techniques
- Merchandising techniques
- Inventory management
- Customer complaints management techniques

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Individual customers
- In contact with the public
- Prolonged standing
- Daytime work
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity
- Local commerce
