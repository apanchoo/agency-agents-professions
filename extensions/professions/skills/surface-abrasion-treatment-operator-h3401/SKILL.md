# Surface abrasion treatment operator

## Scope

The Surface Abrasion Treatment Operator carries out abrasion finishing operations aimed at reducing the roughness and roughness of machined, cast or forged industrial parts, in compliance with safety rules and production requirements (quality, deadlines). Prepares the parts to be treated (washing, degreasing, stripping) Adjusts the equipment and carries out the operations in accordance with the defined process Controls the appearance of the treated surfaces and carries out the necessary touch-ups Ensures first-level upkeep and maintenance of the machines Completes the production monitoring documents

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Provide quality and production monitoring support
- Abrade the surface, check its condition (roughness, appearance) and carry out any touch-ups
- Identify the completion/finishing operations, tools/abrasives and surface condition based on technical documents, sheets, etc.
- Adjust and mount tools and abrasives (grinding wheels, belts, felts, etc.), positioning and holding the part and abrasion parameters
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Check the abrasive for wear and change it
- Adapt, adjust an article or production according to need
- Identify manufacturing operations from the manufacturing file
