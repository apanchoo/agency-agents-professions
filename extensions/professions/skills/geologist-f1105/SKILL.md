# Geologist

## Scope

The geologist studies the composition, structure, history and formation processes of soils, rocks and other terrestrial materials. Analyzes rock formations to determine their composition, structure, and evolution Conducts field studies to collect geological data Interprets geophysical data to identify natural resources or anticipate environmental risks Collaborates with engineers to evaluate the stability of soils in construction or drilling projects Participates in the writing of scientific reports and the publication of research results

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Design theoretical, predictive geological models of soils and subsoils (calculation, simulation, modeling)
- Define and coordinate the implementation of research methods and processes, prospecting, soil and subsoil studies
- Carry out studies of foundations, settlement or stability of land (sliding, erosion, resistance)
- Map data
- Interpret geological surveys of soils, subsoils, rocks and study the characteristics (extents, compositions, geometry, etc.)
- Carry out surveys, drilling, soil and subsoil pollution
- Define risk prevention measures
- Evaluate the quality of mineral resources
