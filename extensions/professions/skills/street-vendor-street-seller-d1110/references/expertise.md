# Street vendor / Street seller

## Scope

The traveling salesman, a dynamic profession in direct contact with customers. Travels through different locations to offer products for sale Adapts its offering according to the audience and location of sale Manages financial transactions and ensures the smooth running of sales Can offer food or non-food products as needed Maintains an attractive and organized display to attract customers Respects hygiene and safety standards when selling food products

## Related Job Titles

- Barbapapiste

## Activities and Skills

- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Ensure cleanliness and order in the sales area
- Ensure compliance with health and safety standards
- Follow up on orders with suppliers
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Monitor stock levels and place orders accordingly
- Transport and install your equipment
- Manage a cash register
- Proceed to cashout
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Know how to calculate margins and determine sales prices
- Know local laws and regulations
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Obtain the necessary authorizations to practice
- Identify and process a customer request
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Ensure communication with customers and partners
- Choosing the best locations to sell
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Negotiate optimal purchasing conditions
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Present and promote a product or service
- Carry out a commercial animation
- Convince, negotiate
- Listen to customer needs
- Demonstrate commercial acumen and interpersonal skills
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy

## Products, tools and materials

- Inventory and order management

## Areas of expertise

- Stock and inventory management
- Knowledge of consumer trends

## Standards and processes

- Collection procedures
- Hygiene and cleanliness standards

## Professional techniques

- Outdoor Sales Techniques
- Mobile cash register management

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Self-employed
- Business travel
- In contact with the public
- Outdoors
- Prolonged standing
- Work independently and in isolated situations
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity
