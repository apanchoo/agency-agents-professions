# Model maker

## Scope

The pattern maker develops patterns for unique products or those intended to be created in small series. Interprets the stylist's sketches to create patterns adapted to customer needs Produces clothing prototypes to validate cuts and shapes Makes adjustments to prototypes based on feedback Participates in the development of technical files intended for production

## Related Job Titles

- Fashion designer

## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate cross-team collaboration for product development
- Building a fashion design portfolio
- Make an aesthetic or artistic appreciation
- Adapt a pattern or template from the sketch
- Act quickly to resolve design issues
- Collect, test, analyze samples, materials
- Identify the particularities of model execution
- Edit an existing pattern
- Cast a canvas on a mannequin from a sketch or figurine
- Make adjustments to prototypes
- Make prototypes
- Make the pattern of clothing items
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Make a sketch, a part drawing
- Write and develop instructions and technical sheets
- Adapt clothing models to current trends
- Analyze the materials to understand the expected level of finish
- Detect assembly difficulties, non-conformities (aesthetics, falling clothing, fit) and provide technical solutions
- Produce lingerie, seaside lingerie or corsetry items individually or in small batches
- Carry out fittings on models
- Carry out filling operations (hemming, topstitching, etc.) on textile items
- Carry out complex manufacturing projects that may involve precious materials
- Use manual sewing techniques for delicate finishes
- Control the quality of finishes on haute couture clothing
- Control the quality of raw materials used
- Check the finishes
- Visually inspect finished products for defects
- Organize and plan an activity
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage relationships with customers and suppliers
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Encourage the use of eco-responsible products
- Promote the use of recycled or eco-friendly materials
- Analyze customer needs for a tailor-made tailoring project
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Follow developments in the fashion market
- Respect collection production deadlines
- Communicate design changes clearly
- Present an article or prototype for validation
- Work in collaboration with the workshop
