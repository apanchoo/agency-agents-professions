# Tiler

## Scope

Expert in laying tiling, earthenware or mosaic, the Tiler combines technique and creativity to dress the floors and walls of a building under construction or renovation. Prepares surfaces and lays tiles on floors and walls Cuts and adjusts materials according to plans or measurements to fit each space Makes joints between tiles for an aesthetic and waterproof finish Advises clients on the choice of materials and designs
