# Technical projection manager

## Scope

The Technical Projection Manager supervises all technical aspects related to cinema projection. Manage a team of projectionists and train new technicians Coordinate equipment maintenance interventions and plan technical shutdowns Ensure technical quality and guarantee compliance with projection and safety standards Verify the reception and integration of content while optimizing operating costs Write activity reports and keep technical documentation up to date

## Related Job Titles

- Cinematographer / Projection cinematographer
- Projectionist
