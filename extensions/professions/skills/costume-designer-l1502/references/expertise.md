# Costume designer

## Scope

The Costume Designer creates visual identities through costumes and takes care of the staging of characters for theater, cinema and television. Designs and creates costumes adapted to the needs of artistic productions while respecting the guidelines of the script and the eras represented Collaborates closely with directors, directors and set designers to harmonize the costumes with the entire production Manages the dressing of artists before and during performances or filming Ensures the maintenance and repair of costumes throughout their use May supervise a team of dressers and seamstresses for the creation of costumes Participates in the search for fabrics and accessories necessary to make the costumes
