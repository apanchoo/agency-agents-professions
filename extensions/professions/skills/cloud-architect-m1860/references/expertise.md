# Cloud Architect

## Scope

The cloud architect plays a key role in the digital transformation of companies, orchestrating the infrastructure of online services. Designs and develops cloud computing architectures tailored to business needs Optimizes costs and performance of cloud infrastructures Ensures security and compliance of systems in the cloud Collaborates with development teams to integrate cloud solutions Manages the migration of applications and data to the cloud Monitors and analyzes the effectiveness of deployed cloud services
