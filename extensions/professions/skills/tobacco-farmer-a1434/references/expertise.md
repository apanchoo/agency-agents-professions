# Tobacco farmer

## Scope

The Tabaculteur manages the different aspects of tobacco production and processing. Cultivates and maintains tobacco plants while respecting growth cycles and agricultural standards Monitors the condition of the crops and applies the necessary phytosanitary treatments Harvests the tobacco at maturity and ensures the drying and fermentation processes Prepares the tobacco leaves for sale or subsequent processing Manages agricultural equipment and maintains the farm's infrastructure Sells the tobacco produced to manufacturers

## Related Job Titles

- Tobacco farmer
- Tobacco planter
- Tobacco producer

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Promote the implementation of sustainable practices and innovations on farms
- Train employees in new cultivation methods
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Provide and update activity monitoring tools (production dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Plan a sowing, processing or harvesting operation on an operating site
- Analyze the quality of the soil and plants
- Apply phytosanitary treatments adapted according to regulations
- Conduct and control a fermentation process
- Carry out agricultural operations (sowing, harvesting)
- Position the harvested plants in the dryers and monitor the drying phases
- Prepare soils, plantations (spreading, sowing, etc.)
- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions
- Adjusting irrigation and watering equipment
- Monitor the condition of a plantation
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Ensure monitoring of current environmental standards
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Packaging products
- Store products
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Sorting plants
- Organize order processing
- Track orders, invoicing
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Optimize the use of water resources
- Negotiate contracts with suppliers and distributors
- Managing plant waste in an ecological way
- Marketing the products of a farm
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Adapt strategies to market developments
- Manage production and operations management
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
