# Media library director

## Scope

The media library director manages, organizes and develops a documentary resource center within an educational, university, cultural, administrative or private establishment. Defines the documentary strategy, supervises the team, guarantees access to reliable and relevant information, and ensures the adaptation of services to the needs of the public Supervises the management, enrichment and promotion of collections (printed and digital) Supervises and coordinates the documentary team by distributing tasks and supporting the development of skills Ensures access, dissemination and mediation of information to different audiences Develops partnerships and sets up innovative projects promoting education for media and information
