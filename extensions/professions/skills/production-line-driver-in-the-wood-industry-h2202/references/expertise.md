# Production line driver in the wood industry

## Scope

The production line operator operates one or more machines, automated or semi-automated, intended for machining operations on wooden works. Supervises and controls the production process on the machines Ensures first-level maintenance of production equipment Optimizes settings to guarantee the quality and conformity of finished products Coordinates the activity of operators on the line in order to respect production objectives Controls the quality of raw materials and products during manufacturing Respects and enforces safety standards and operational procedures
