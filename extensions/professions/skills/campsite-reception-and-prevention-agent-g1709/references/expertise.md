# Campsite reception and prevention agent

## Scope

The campsite reception and prevention agent welcomes customers upon their arrival and accompanies them to their pitch or rental accommodation. Ensures the smooth flow of arrivals and departures, compliance with installation and safety rules and the cleanliness of locations Communicates information and responds to initial requests from vacationers Ensures permanent contact with the reception team Can support reception depending on peaks of activity Can collaborate in the organization of events

## Related Job Titles

- Reception and prevention agent in outdoor hospitality
- Versatile reception agent in outdoor hospitality
- Field reception agent
- Customer installation manager
- Location referent

## Activities and Skills

- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Use specific equipment for accessibility for people with reduced mobility (PRM)
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Monitor the premises and property and enforce the internal regulations by residents, tenants, visitors
- Ensure customer safety by following appropriate procedures
- Check compliance of the site, installations and equipment with regulatory standards
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Detect an abnormal situation and inform
- Ensure compliance of maintenance operations
- Carry out the inventory of fixtures and the customer's entry and exit inventory
- Encourage eco-friendly actions and reduce the environmental footprint
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Greet customers upon arrival and manage their departure
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Inform customers about security rules and available services
- Present the site map and locations to newcomers
- Respond to guests' requests for information and needs during their stay
- Collaborate with internal services (management, reception, technical service) and external services (fire brigade, gendarmerie, etc.) in the event of danger
- Communicate in English orally and in writing
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Organize your work within a multi-professional team
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Raise customer awareness about eco-responsible actions (sorting, water, waste) and site preservation
- Enforce the rules of hygiene, safety, accessibility and environmental protection
- Manage unforeseen events, hazards and last minute changes
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the establishment’s quality standards
- Use digital tools
