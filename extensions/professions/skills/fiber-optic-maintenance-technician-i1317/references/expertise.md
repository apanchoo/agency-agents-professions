# Fiber optic maintenance technician

## Scope

The Fiber Optic Maintenance Technician guarantees the performance and reliability of communications networks. Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance operations on fiber optic networks Diagnoses and resolves technical problems Carries out interventions on customer sites (end users) to repair or replace defective elements

## Related Job Titles

- Fiber optic maintenance manager
- Optical fiber expert
- Cable fiber optic communication network maintenance technician
- Technician in maintenance and after-sales service for fiber optic networks

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Evaluate the timetable feasibility and confirm the intervention
- Determine technical solutions and recommendations
- Carry out performance tests on fiber optic networks
- Update maintenance log
- Configure or configure telecommunications equipment, low currents
- Complete the intervention documents
- Update and upgrade telecommunications or low current equipment
- Propose continuous technical improvements
- Take measurements for the creation of the optical equipment and establish the work execution sheet
- Carry out optical fiber welding operations
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Connect electronic equipment
- Connect a junction box
- Install and connect fiber optic cables
- Install the equipment on site and connect it to external networks
- Connecting low voltage elements
- Carry out and interpret optical network measurements (reflectometry, photometry, etc.)
- Test optical connections to ensure they are working properly
- Diagnose malfunctions on a fiber optic network
- Troubleshoot and repair fiber optic cables
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Perform preventive maintenance on fiber optic equipment
- Identify the characteristics of equipment
- Carry out preventive, systematic or conditional maintenance operations
- Repair faulty installations based on a diagnosis
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Check equipment or material
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Check the operation of safety equipment
- Establish the intervention report
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Determine solutions and technical recommendations and communicate them to customers
- Identify solutions and courses of action based on the established diagnosis
- Reformulate the solutions implemented in appropriate language
- Arguing to resolve conflicts or misunderstandings

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
