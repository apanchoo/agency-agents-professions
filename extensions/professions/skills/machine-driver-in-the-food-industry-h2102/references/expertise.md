# Machine driver in the food industry

## Scope

The Food Industry Machine Operator operates one or more manufacturing or packaging machines in the food industry in compliance with food hygiene and safety standards. Ensures the placement of raw materials and controls the regular supply of the line Performs adjustment of machines according to production specifications Monitors production parameters and adjusts processes to guarantee conformity and product quality Performs first level maintenance operations and reports technical anomalies to the relevant departments Performs product quality checks throughout the production process
