# Demolisher / Wrecker

## Scope

Dismantles or demolishes constructions or structures while respecting safety rules. Performs selective sorting of materials with a view to their recycling or revalorization. Can use mini-machines: chainsaw, chisel, jackhammer, mini-excavator, mini-loader.

- Demolition Contractor
- Demolition Operator
- Demolition worker
- Deconstruction Preparer
- Demolition Preparer
- Core sawyer / Concrete core sawyer
