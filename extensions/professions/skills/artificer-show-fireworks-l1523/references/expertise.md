# Artificer / Show fireworks

## Scope

The Show Artificer designs and produces pyrotechnic shows while respecting safety standards. Chooses the materials and products adapted to each type of show Ensures the installation and proper functioning of the firing devices Coordinates the technical team for the assembly and dismantling of the installations Performs preliminary tests to guarantee the quality and safety of the show Can design pyrotechnic choreographies in synchronization with music or other show elements

- Pyrotechnician
