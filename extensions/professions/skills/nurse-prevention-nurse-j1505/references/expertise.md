# Nurse / Prevention nurse

## Scope

The Prevention Nurse plays a crucial role in promoting health and preventing disease within communities. Carries out prevention and health education actions with different audiences Participates in vaccination and disease screening campaigns Collaborates with other health professionals to develop prevention programs Intervenes in school, professional or community settings to raise awareness of health issues Can participate in the training and supervision of health personnel or trainees

## Related Job Titles

- Preventive health nurse

## Activities and Skills

- Identify the necessary adjustments to a workstation
- Advise, support a person
- Teaching, developing skills
- Offer advice on well-being and lifestyle
- Observe the state of mental and physical health of the person accompanied
- Offer personalized advice on nutrition, physical exercise, stress management
- Organize vaccination campaigns
- Organize and administer vaccination and screening programs to detect diseases early
- Carry out a screening (visual, auditory)
- Raise awareness of mental and physical well-being
- Monitor the health status of populations at risk and intervene to limit the spread of diseases
- Facilitate internal and external communication
- Promote public health programs
- Design and implement prevention actions, information on health and psychosocial risks
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Ensure the implementation of solutions for identifying and preventing risks to public health
- Write a report, an activity report
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Identify health or environmental risk factors
- Work with other health professionals and community organizations to develop prevention programs
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Inform and raise public awareness about health behaviors and practices to prevent diseases
- Participate in multidisciplinary meetings
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
