# Press publishing manager

## Scope

The press publishing manager organizes and coordinates editorial production, defines the digital publishing strategy and projects, and develops brand visibility on the internet. Supervises the editorial line and ensures the consistency of published content Coordinates editorial teams and optimizes work processes Develops strategies to increase the reach and impact of publications Ensures the quality and veracity of the information disseminated Manages relationships with authors, journalists and other contributors Analyzes market trends to adapt content to readers' expectations
