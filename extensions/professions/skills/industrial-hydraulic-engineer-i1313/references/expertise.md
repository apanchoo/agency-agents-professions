# Industrial hydraulic engineer

## Scope

The Industrial Hydraulician, an expert in hydraulic systems, plays a crucial role in optimizing industrial processes. Designs and develops hydraulic systems for various industrial applications Installs and commissions hydraulic equipment in accordance with safety standards Carries out preventive and corrective maintenance of hydraulic installations Optimizes the performance of the systems to guarantee their efficiency and reliability Diagnoses and resolves technical problems related to hydraulic systems Provides user training on the operation and maintenance of the equipment

## Related Job Titles

- Industrial hydraulician / Industrial installation hydraulician
- Hydraulic engineer
- Oil-hydraulic systems maintainer

- Mechanotrician
- Industrial hydraulic fitter
- Oil-hydraulic systems operator
- Oleohydraulic systems installation or maintenance technician

## Activities and Skills

- Define the technical characteristics of the product
- Encourage responsible innovation
- Calculate thermal, hydraulic or aeraulic sizing
- Develop standardized maintenance procedures
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Sizing a hydraulic network
- Install and adjust hydraulic power elements (cylinders, motors, etc.)
- Make hydraulic, electrical and mechanical connections for equipment and accessories
- Analyze the maintenance needs of hydraulic systems
- Check the conformity of hydraulic installations
- Check hydraulic circuits and exchange components
- Diagnose breakdowns in hydraulic systems and propose solutions
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Carry out hydraulic cleaning (sluice boat, sluice wagon, etc.)
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Repair the installation by replacing and reconditioning electrical, electronic, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic devices
- Troubleshoot technical issues
- Build a preventative maintenance plan
- Evaluate equipment risks
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Optimize assembly processes for hydraulic systems
- Ensure the application and compliance with hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental (waste management, noise pollution, etc.) rules on the site
- Explain technical choices to customers
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Apply a safety instruction
- React effectively in the event of a technical incident
