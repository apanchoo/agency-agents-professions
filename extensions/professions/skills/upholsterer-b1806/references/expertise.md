# Upholsterer

## Scope

The upholsterer carries out by hand and machine the manufacturing operations (upholstery, covering, etc.) and renovation of seat bases, mattresses, furnishing elements (curtains, curtains, hangings, etc.) individually or in small series. Can repair seat frames, install curtains, stretched ceilings, wall canvases for clients. Can coordinate a team and manage a structure
