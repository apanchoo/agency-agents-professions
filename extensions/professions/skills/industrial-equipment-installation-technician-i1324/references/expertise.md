# Industrial equipment installation technician

## Scope

The Industrial Equipment Installation Technician is responsible for the installation and commissioning of equipment and machines used in various industrial sectors. Installs and configures industrial equipment according to technical specifications Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of machines Optimizes processes to improve equipment efficiency Trains users in basic operations and maintenance of equipment Respects and applies current safety and environmental standards

## Activities and Skills

- Encourage innovation and adoption of new technologies
- Support customers in using a product or equipment
- Troubleshoot advanced technical issues
- Analyze maintenance data
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Manage technical documentation for equipment
- Make adjustments to industrial equipment
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Install an electrical system
- Commission the network and check the installation
- Make hydraulic, electrical and mechanical connections for equipment and accessories
- Ensure compliance of installations with safety standards
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Carry out preventive, systematic or conditional maintenance operations
- Repair faulty installations based on a diagnosis
- Check the quality of electrical installations
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Follow a maintenance or technical support operation
- Use testing devices (pressure, flow, vibration, etc.) and electrical measuring devices
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Ensure the application and respect of hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental rules (waste management, noise pollution, etc.)
- Manage stocks of maintenance equipment and spare parts
- Collaborate with teams to resolve technical issues
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- React effectively in the event of a technical incident
