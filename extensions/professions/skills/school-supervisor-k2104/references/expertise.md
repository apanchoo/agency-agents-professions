# School supervisor

## Scope

The school supervisor's mission is to maintain order and the safety of students. He ensures supervision during class hours and recess He ensures compliance with internal regulations He intervenes in the event of conflict or problem between students He organizes and monitors extracurricular activities He participates in the organization of exams and competitions He contributes to supervising students during school outings

## Related Job Titles

- Educational assistant
- Educational framework / Educational framework of private education
- Boarding school master/mistress
- Examination invigilator
- Day school supervisor
- Boarding school supervisor

## Activities and Skills

- Support and ensure the safety of students during extracurricular activities
- Assist the CPE (Principal Education Advisor)
- Ensuring the safety of students during interclasses
- Monitor students in common areas, constantly, during exams and outings
- Encourage student participation in school activities
- Facilitate and create the conditions for cooperation
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Supporting disabled students
- Provide mediation
- Actively listen and diagnose to build an adapted and personalized response in a responsive manner
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Assist educational staff during emergencies
- Analyze and solve a common or complex problem
- Organize security awareness events
- Document incidents and write reports
- Control access to school facilities
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Working in a group, in a network
- Understand and respect the rules of collective life
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing conflicts between students
- Promote respect for the rules of the establishment
- Monitor punctuality, student attendance at classes, check supporting documents
- Use digital tools
- Ensure the application of evacuation procedures
