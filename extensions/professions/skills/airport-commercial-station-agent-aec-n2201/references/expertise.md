# Airport Commercial Station Agent (AEC)

## Scope

The Airport Station Agent ensures the smooth running of ground operations at airports. Welcomes and informs passengers, manages check-ins and boarding Ensures coordination between the various airport services to optimize passenger and baggage flows Controls travel documents and ensures regulatory compliance of procedures Manages emergency situations or last-minute changes to minimize impacts on passengers Supervises loading and unloading operations of baggage and goods May participate in the training of new agents and the continuous improvement of stopover processes

## Related Job Titles

- Airport stopover agent
- Airport Stopover Commercial Services Agent (ASC)
- Agent
- Airport handling agent
- Air transport station agent

## Activities and Skills

- Maintain documents relating to stopover operations up to date
- Check baggage or objects, in compliance with regulations
- Supervise the loading and unloading of baggage
- Analyze feedback to improve processes
- Carry out check-in opening and boarding operations
- Driving passengers on an airport runway using a minibus or car
- Carry out boarding and disembarkation operations (on-track bus reservation, passenger support, boarding pass management, etc.)
- Proceed to cashout
- Check payment methods and keep supporting documents
- Apply customs regulations
- Apply the legal framework for air transport and air navigation
- Closing a flight (closing doors, closing documents, etc.)
- Inform passengers of flight changes and manage complaints
- Control access on board and check tickets
- Manage passenger flows while respecting schedules
- Ensure compliance with environmental standards in stopover operations
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Identify and process a customer request
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Improve quality of service
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Optimize the organization of boarding to reduce delays
- Ensure communication between cabin crew and ground services
- Facilitate inter-departmental communication for better coordination
- Working in a group, in a network
- Ensure monitoring of airport security and safety procedures
- Collect flight information
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Integrate digital technologies to optimize stopover processes
- Negotiate in the event of conflict to defuse tensions
- Use digital tools
