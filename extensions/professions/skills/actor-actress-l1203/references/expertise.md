# Actor / Actress

## Scope

Interprets roles, characters in cinematographic works, audiovisual productions (television films, series, radio serials, advertising films, etc.), shows (plays, musicals, etc.), according to the artistic intentions of a director, a director, etc. and the requirements of filming and programming. Can act as an extra or silhouette. Can perform voice dubbing.
