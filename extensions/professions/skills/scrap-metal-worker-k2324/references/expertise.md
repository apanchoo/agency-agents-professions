# Scrap metal worker

## Scope

The Scrap Metal Dealer specializes in the collection, sorting, processing and resale of ferrous and non-ferrous metals (steel, aluminum, copper, etc.) and thus participates in the management of industrial and domestic waste and the preservation of the environment. Recovers or buys metals from individuals, companies, construction sites or recycling centers Separates the different types of metals to facilitate their recycling Resells the sorted metals to manufacturers or recycling centers Complies with environmental standards and legal obligations linked to this activity

## Related Job Titles

- Scrap metal worker
- Scrap metal worker-metal salvager
- Metal scavenger

## Activities and Skills

- Collect household or industrial waste
- Cutting metal
- Separate the different types of ferrous and non-ferrous metals
- Set up a physical storage space
- Maintain a tool or equipment
- Load, unload, handle products
- Store a product
- Organize tours to collect or purchase batches of metals
- Prepare the tour and the necessary equipment
- Transport materials
- Track orders, invoicing
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Apply current environmental standards
- Apply standards related to material recycling
- Control the quality of sorted materials
- Promote the reuse or recycling of recovered materials
- Prepare recovered metals (cutting, compacting, grinding) to facilitate their recycling
- Recycle metal products and materials
- Recover equipment or products at the end of their life
- Sort products, parts, materials and direct them to recovery or elimination channels
- Negotiate commercial terms
- Sell a service or product
- Negotiate with local partners
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect safety rules
