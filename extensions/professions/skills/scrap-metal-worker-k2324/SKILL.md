## Scope

The Scrap Metal Dealer specializes in the collection, sorting, processing and resale of ferrous and non-ferrous metals (steel, aluminum, copper, etc.) and thus participates in the management of industrial and domestic waste and the preservation of the environment. Recovers or buys metals from individuals, companies, construction sites or recycling centers Separates the different types of metals to facilitate their recycling Resells the sorted metals to manufacturers or recycling centers Complies with environmental standards and legal obligations linked to this activity
