# Automotive painter

## Scope

Carries out all specific painting operations on a vehicle, including colorimetry activities. Prepares the surfaces to be painted and carries out touch-ups and finishes on the vehicles Applies layers of paint and varnish to protect and beautify the vehicles Determines the colors and prepares the products Makes a paint connection Repairs a composite element Manages the stocks of paint and necessary consumables Respects environmental and safety standards in the handling of products

## Related Job Titles

- Bodywork painter
- Automotive body painter
- Painter fitter / Painter fitter in bodywork
- Retoucher painter / Bodywork retoucher painter

## Activities and Skills

- Train new employees on painting techniques
- Analyze the color of a vehicle's paint
- Use inventory management software
- Using software to create paint shades
- Apply painting and finishing products
- Apply and dry the repair products to a composite material support
- Apply a resin/canvas or mat to repair a composite material
- Sand surfaces to prepare for paint application
- Protect unpainted surfaces
- Prepare the color and finishing products for the painting operation
- Prepare surfaces by laying putty and applying primer
- Make a paint connection
- Use paint spraying equipment
- Repairing a composite material by welding
- Monitor risk areas
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Ensure the proper functioning of ventilation systems
- Check the conformity of materials and colors during production
- Check the quality of the paint applied
- Visually control the different stages of the manufacturing process
- Manage stocks of paints and consumables
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Advise customers on necessary technical painting interventions
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Document painting procedures for future reference
- Prioritize tasks based on emergencies and needs
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
- Respect production and repair deadlines
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
