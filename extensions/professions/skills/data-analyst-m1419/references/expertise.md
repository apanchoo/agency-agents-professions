# Data analyst

## Scope

The Data Analyst transforms data into statistical and technical information. Collects analytical data to extract trends and forecasts to solve problems Collaborates with technical and business teams to understand data needs Presents the results of analyzes in the form of reports or understandable visualizations Ensures the quality and integrity of the data used in analyzes through technological monitoring of advances in tools Participates in data science and artificial intelligence projects to improve processes
