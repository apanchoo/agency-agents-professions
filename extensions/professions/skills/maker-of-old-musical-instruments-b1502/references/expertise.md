# Maker of old musical instruments

## Scope

The maker of antique musical instruments is a craftsman specializing in the manufacture and restoration of historic musical instruments, using traditional techniques and materials. Restores and preserves antique musical instruments while respecting original techniques Manufactures identical replacement parts to maintain the authenticity of the instruments Adapts antique instruments to modern needs while preserving their historical integrity Advises musicians on the maintenance and appropriate use of antique instruments May perform evaluations and appraisals for collectible instruments Participates in research and historical reconstruction projects related to early music
