# Bioproduction Technician

## Scope

The Bioproduction Technician intervenes throughout an industrial process specializing in biological production generally in industries such as pharmacy, biotechnology, agri-food or cosmetics. Ensures the preparation of cell cultures, fermentations or biochemical reactions Operates and monitors production and packaging equipment Conducts tests and analyzes to ensure that products meet quality and safety standards Performs maintenance and sterilization of laboratory and production equipment Documents procedures and ensures traceability of production operations Follows strict protocols to ensure the quality and safety of biological products

## Activities and Skills

- Propose innovative solutions to solve technical problems
- Carry out technical or technological monitoring to anticipate developments
- Ensure the traceability of pharmaceutical production batches
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Start or stop a machine, a line
- Participate in the design, manufacturing and quality control of drugs in the pharmaceutical industry
- Sterilize media and buffers
- Adjust production machine parameters
- Use specific equipment (fermenters, bioreactors)
- Perform first-level preventive and corrective maintenance on its equipment
- Manage unforeseen events and propose adapted solutions
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Check pressures and temperatures, heat exchanges, etc. and adjust the settings
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Ensure product traceability throughout production
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Control the traceability of cleaning and sterilization operations
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Prepare media, buffer solutions, and cleaning solutions and check the conformity of all solutions
- Apply eco-gestures in your practice
- Propose solutions to reduce the ecological footprint
- Collaborate with other technicians to optimize work processes
- Participate in multidisciplinary meetings
- Respect procedures, operating methods and instructions
- Follow cleaning and disinfection protocols
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Comply with current pharmaceutical regulations
