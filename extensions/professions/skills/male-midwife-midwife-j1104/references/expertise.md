# Male midwife / Midwife

## Scope

With good listening skills, this essential healthcare professional supports pregnant women throughout their pregnancy. Ensures medical monitoring of the pregnancy and carries out the necessary ultrasound scans Accompanies and advises pregnant women on good health and nutrition practices Prepares and assists women during childbirth Carry out postnatal monitoring of mothers and newborns Can carry out home births Organizes birth and parenthood preparation sessions

## Related Job Titles

- Midwifery practitioner

## Activities and Skills

- Encourage the patient to express her expectations and concerns
- Accompany the patient during labor
- Design and implement a post-natal rehabilitation program with the patient
- Advise the mother and father on the first maternal or paternal actions, specific care and recommend other medical or psychological follow-ups
- Assessing fetal well-being during labor
- Managing stressful situations among patients
- Manage obstetric emergencies while waiting for the doctor to arrive
- Identify the patient's request and search for additional information in the medical file
- Informing and advising women in situations of birth under X
- Inform and advise women experiencing voluntary termination of pregnancy (IVG)
- Inform and advise women undergoing medical termination of pregnancy (IMG)
- Inform and advise women in medically assisted procreation situations
- Carry out pregnancy monitoring
- Carry out a birth or assist the obstetrician
- Promote reproductive health practices to patients
- Prepare the parturient for delivery and monitor fetal heart rate, cervical dilation, clinical status, etc.
- Take biological samples (blood test, smears, etc.)
- Perform obstetric ultrasounds
- Perform postpartum care and newborn birth examination
- Update the patient's medical record
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Greet, orient and provide information to a patient
- Facilitate childbirth or parenthood preparation sessions
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Organize maternal and child health workshops
- Participate in a multidisciplinary project

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
