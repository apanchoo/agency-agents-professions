# Assembler of wooden structures

## Scope

The Wood Structure Assembler is a craftsman in sustainable and ecological construction. Assembles and installs wooden structures for various constructions such as houses, chalets and other buildings Uses specific techniques for the assembly of wooden and composite structures while respecting plans and safety standards Participates in the preparation of sites and the installation of the necessary foundations Collaborates with other trades on construction projects Makes adjustments and finishes to guarantee the quality and durability of installations

## Related Job Titles

- Assembler of wooden chalets
- Assembler of wooden frame houses
- Assembler in wood and composite structures
- Installer of industrial frames

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate work with other trades
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Establish the schedule and establish the methods of execution of the site
- Assemble the heavy supporting structures of a work
- Develop solutions to solve complex assembly problems
- Drill, screw and clip the different elements of the structure in order to prepare the roofing maneuver
- Report cutting and assembly marks on construction parts
- Use hand tools to adjust pieces of wood
- Use hand and portable tools (hammers, chasers, planes, cutter meters, etc.)
- Adjust the assembly of the structural parts
- Close a construction site and clear the work area (machinery, materials, waste, etc.)
- Carry out acceptance of work with the client
- Fix, place, install elements, works
- Assemble or dismantle scaffolding
- Install a prefabricated wooden staircase
- Prepare the execution of the site
- Make adjustments to structures to ensure their stability
- Carry out fixing operations
- Carry out on-site installation, installation and final assembly of a completed structure (frame, base, etc.)
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Clean the intervention site and store the tools
- Replace structural elements to repair or renovate a structure
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Install security or signaling elements
- Permanently respect established safety rules
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Control the quality of wood used for construction
- Check the assembly and installation of elements and sub-assemblies by checking compliance with the technical file
- Load, unload, handle products
- Sling, lift and distribute a load respecting the planned locations
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Handle material vertically at height, using winches or pulleys
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Prepare site reports with the works manager or the client
- Inform the customer about the conditions for completing an order
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency
- Use digital tools
