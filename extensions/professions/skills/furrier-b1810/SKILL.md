## Scope

The furrier is an artisan who creates luxury clothing and accessories by hand and machine, often unique pieces. Selects and prepares skins for making fur clothing and accessories Cuts and assembles fur pieces according to precise patterns Performs hand finishing to ensure the quality and aesthetics of the products Restores and repairs fur items to extend their life Advises customers on the care of fur products Can create personalized designs at the request of customers

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Analyze model specifications for pattern creation
- Assemble fur pieces with precision
- Select naturally tanned, dyed or shaved skins for making
- Use fur-specific cutting techniques
- Repair damaged fur pieces
- Install security or signaling elements
- Control the quality of furs and finishes
- Manage the supply of skins and furs
