# Geographer

## Scope

The Geographer analyzes and interprets geographical data, natural phenomena and their impact on the lives of populations. Conducts field studies to collect geographic and environmental data Uses mapping and GIS-Geographic Information System tools to create detailed maps Collaborates with urban planners and decision-makers to provide analyzes that influence territorial planning Participates in research projects to develop new methods of spatial analysis Guides the choice of major development projects to better exploit the territory's natural resources
