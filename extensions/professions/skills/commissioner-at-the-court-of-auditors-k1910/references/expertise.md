# Commissioner at the Court of Auditors

## Scope

The Commissioner of the Court of Auditors ensures the sound management of public finances. Controls the execution of financial laws and ensures the regularity of public accounts Writes reports on the financial management of public bodies Participates in audit missions on the operations of the State and communities Proposes recommendations to improve the management and efficiency of public expenditure Contributes to jurisdictional procedures in the event of faulty management Ensures an information mission to Parliament and the government

## Related Job Titles

- Advisor to the Court of Auditors

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in the training of public managers and the dissemination of good practices in budgetary and accounting management
- Conduct an interview, an interview, an audition
- Check data compliance
- Complete the necessary formalities for legal procedures with competent services
- Prepare financial management reports
- Write clear and detailed expert reports
- Analyze elements necessary for an audit
- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Control the regularity of public accounts
- Carry out a financial audit
- Carry out an accounting and financial analysis
- Verify and control the financial management of public funds of the State, local authorities and public establishments
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Defend the public interest
- Ensure the legal security of a decision or a project
- Check compliance with regulations and use of public funds
- Collecting evidence for a legal case
- Organize survey activities
- Conduct in-depth investigations in the event of suspicion of fraud or mismanagement
- Advising public authorities on public finance management
- Contribute to the drafting of the Annual Public Report of the Court of Auditors, which is presented to the President of the Republic and to Parliament
- Monitoring the performance of public finances
- Make recommendations to improve financial management practices
- Examine and verify the accounts of the various public administrations, local authorities, public establishments, and subsidized organizations
- Guarantee the financial integrity and efficiency of public administrations
- Conduct special surveys at the request of public institutions (government, Parliament) on specific subjects related to public finances
- Carry out financial audits to assess the effectiveness, efficiency and economy of public expenditure
- Write detailed control and audit reports presenting the conclusions, recommendations and possible irregularities observed
- Propose improvements to accounting procedures
- Suggest improvements for financial processes
- Make a decision and explain it
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing a conflict situation
