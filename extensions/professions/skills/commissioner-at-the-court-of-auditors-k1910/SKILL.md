## Scope

The Commissioner of the Court of Auditors ensures the sound management of public finances. Controls the execution of financial laws and ensures the regularity of public accounts Writes reports on the financial management of public bodies Participates in audit missions on the operations of the State and communities Proposes recommendations to improve the management and efficiency of public expenditure Contributes to jurisdictional procedures in the event of faulty management Ensures an information mission to Parliament and the government
