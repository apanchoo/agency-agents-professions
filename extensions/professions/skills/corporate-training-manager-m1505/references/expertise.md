# Corporate training manager

## Scope

The Corporate Training Officer, a key role for the development of internal skills. Identifies training needs within the company Designs and implements appropriate training programs Organizes and coordinates training sessions Evaluates the effectiveness of the training provided Ensures skills are updated in line with developments in the sector Can collaborate with external training organizations to enrich the training offer

## Related Job Titles

- Corporate training advisor

## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate professional development workshops
- Monitor the evolution of employee skills
- Supporting digital, demographic and organizational transitions in the world of work
- Analyze the company's needs in training, recruitment and professional mobility
- Ensure the training of new employees
- Ensure administrative follow-up of training
- Design training repositories
- Design training engineering and educational sequences
- Build a continuing professional development plan
- Coordinate external stakeholders for training
- Teaching, developing skills
- Manage material resources for training
- Organize internal training
- Propose innovative training solutions
- Ensure regulatory compliance of training
- Design a training evaluation process
- Prepare detailed reports on training activities
- Evaluate the effectiveness of training programs
- Evaluate the quality and conformity of training
- Write a report, an activity report
- Negotiate with educational content providers
- Identify grant opportunities for training
- Develop partnerships with training organizations
- Optimize the costs of training actions
- Facilitate communication between departments
- Optimize the use of educational technologies
