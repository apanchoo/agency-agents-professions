# Maker of keyboard instruments

## Scope

The keyboard instrument maker is a professional whose main activity is the preparation, maintenance and repair of instruments, some requiring specific know-how and tools such as the organ or the accordion. Advises customers on the choice and maintenance of their instruments Participates in the artisanal or semi-industrial manufacture of instruments and their components Tunes the instruments to guarantee their sound quality Can personalize the instruments according to the musicians' specifications
