# Management Advisor

## Scope

Organized and rigorous, you have a key role in optimizing the financial performance of companies. Analyzes financial statements to identify areas for improvement Proposes strategies to reduce costs and increase revenue Assists clients in financial and budget planning Provides advice on tax and regulatory compliance Develops financial forecasts and profitability models Supports companies in transformation and strategic change processes
