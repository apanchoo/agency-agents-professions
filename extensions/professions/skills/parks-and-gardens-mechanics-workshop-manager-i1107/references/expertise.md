# Parks and gardens mechanics workshop manager

## Scope

Job where your technical expertise and leadership will guarantee the reliability and performance of valuable equipment for green spaces. Supervises and coordinates the activities of a workshop dedicated to the maintenance and repair of park and garden equipment such as mowers, chainsaws, hedge trimmers, etc. Ensures the management of the technical team, monitoring of interventions Ensures the good organization of the workshop to guarantee the quality and efficiency of the services Manages the stocks of spare parts Optimizes work processes to improve efficiency Ensures compliance with safety and environmental standards
