# Podiatrist

## Scope

The chiropodist-podiatrist performs procedures on morphostatic and skin disorders of the foot. Diagnoses and treats nail diseases of the foot Performs preventive and curative pedicure treatments Designs and adapts plantar orthoses to correct static and dynamic foot disorders Advises patients on hygiene and foot care Can carry out complete podiatric assessments Collaborates with other health professionals for multidisciplinary monitoring of patients

## Related Job Titles

- Posturopodist podiatrist pedicure
- Podiatrist

## Activities and Skills

- Encourage the patient to actively participate in their treatment
- Teaching, developing skills
- Analyze clinical data
- Analyze the patient's gait to adapt the treatment
- Identify medical history, footwear habits, examine and evaluate treatment (pedicure, podiatry)
- Advise on foot care practices
- Manage patient post-treatment follow-up
- Identify the patient's request (care, orthotics prescription, etc.) and create or complete the file (contact details, socio-administrative information, etc.)
- Analyze a patient's postural system and orthostatic balance disorders and implement corrections
- Carry out the podiatric examination, take the patient's impression and determine the necessary correction or compensation
- Propose innovative solutions for podiatric care
- Perform manicure and pedicure treatments
- Treat pedicural conditions, advise the patient on a type of shoe, preventative care or refer them to a practitioner
- Conduct study and research work
- Follow technological developments in podiatry
- Design orthopedic shoes
- Design limb orthoses
- Design orthopedic insoles
- Make the plantar fitting (insoles, orthonyxia, orthoplasty elements, etc.) and try it on with the patient
- Adapting personalized foot orthotics
- Organize your space and workstation
- Maintain podiatry equipment in working order
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Carry out financial transactions in cash, bank cards
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Participate in continuing education sessions
