# Mushroom grower

## Scope

The Mushroom Grower is an expert in mushroom cultivation. Grows various varieties indoors and outdoors depending on the season Controls environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, light) to optimize mushroom growth Harvests, packages and prepares mushrooms for sale or processing Manages inventory and supplies needed to grow mushrooms May sell mushrooms at local markets or to specialty distributors

## Related Job Titles

- Head of mushroom cultivation
- Mushroom picker
- Mushroom grower
- Mushroom worker
- Mushroom worker
- Mushroom collector
- Mushroom manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Train new employees in trade techniques
- Plan a sowing, processing or harvesting operation on an operating site
- Apply phytosanitary treatments adapted according to regulations
- Calibrate a product
- Prepare soils, plantations (spreading, sowing, etc.)
- Carry out product measurement records (humidity, temperature, etc.) and detect anomalies linked to its transformation
- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions
- Adjusting irrigation and watering equipment
- Monitor the condition of a plantation
- Select and prepare growing substrates
- Select mushroom varieties based on production objectives
- Optimize growing space to maximize production
- Install planting material
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain, clean a product or equipment
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Check product storage conditions
- Package products in crates or containers to facilitate transport without damaging them
- Store a product
- Organize order processing
- Track orders, invoicing
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Managing plant waste in an ecological way
- Implement green initiatives to reduce environmental impact
- Marketing the products of a farm
- Sell products on the market or for direct sale on the farm
- Establish partnerships with distributors
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital tools
