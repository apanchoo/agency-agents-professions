## Scope

The Textile Ream Driver controls a continuous processing machine intended to transform and enhance textiles. Prepares the installation according to the manufacturing order and adjusts the parameters Monitors the smooth running of operations and adjusts if necessary to guarantee the quality of the finished product Ensures routine maintenance of the machine Collaborates with the quality, maintenance and logistics teams to optimize production
