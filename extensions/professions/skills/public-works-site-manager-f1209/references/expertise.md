# Public works site manager

## Scope

The public works site manager, always present on the ground, coordinates and plans one or more sites (depending on the size of the project) for which he is responsible for execution. Controls and coordinates the work of the teams Keeps the work progress schedule up to date Manages all necessary human and material resources Participates in the preparation and budgetary monitoring of the site Can replace the works manager in certain tasks

## Related Job Titles

- Craftsman in VRD works (roads and various networks)
- Public works and roads site manager
- Head of public works section
- Earthworks and VRD contractor (roads and various networks)

## Activities and Skills

- Check the application of the work plan
- Coordinate one or more teams
- Carry out administrative monitoring (clocking, etc.) and define schedules
- Evaluate the skills and performance of your teams
- Manage team leaders
- Anticipate recruitment needs in conjunction with the works manager
- Adapt to the interlocutors to explain the expectations of the site (technique, deadlines, etc.)
- Ensure a good atmosphere within teams and manage conflicts
- Support and train in your field of activity
- Identify training needs
- Ensure compliance with the methodology and training conditions in work and tutoring situations
- Ensure the digital model is updated on the relevant aspects
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Use navigation equipment (professional GPS, mobile applications)
- Use drone piloting software
- Analyze and follow the execution plans transmitted by the works manager (plans, rates, delivery deadlines, budget)
- Monitor work progress schedules and make the necessary adjustments to meet deadlines and the committed budget
- Coordinate the restoration of the site
- Coordinate the withdrawal of all site equipment and devices
- Define and ensure the correct location of the construction site in the work area according to the execution plans (ground marking, tracing of the work, identification of networks, marking)
- Regularly carry out site reviews with the works manager in order to monitor the overall progress of the site
- Identify technical and material resources for a construction site
- Identify the activities and constraints of stakeholders by visualizing them and integrating them into the digital model
- Organize and adapt construction sites taking into account the work environment (team, materials, equipment, weather, etc.)
- Participate in the establishment of proofing plans
- Prepare the execution of the site
- Receive a work
- Alert and notify incidents (including those relating to construction site risks) to your hierarchy
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Define and implement security protocols
- Remove the team from real or perceived danger
- Check an excavation base, a trench or a riffle
- Check a specific authorization
- Supervise the conformity of the work carried out
- Define supply needs
- Manage supply orders and raw material inventory
- Organize the storage of materials, structural elements and prefabricated elements
- Write a report, an activity report
- Enter and regularly transmit site reports using digital tools
- Communicate daily expenses to the works manager
- Develop partnerships with suppliers
- Make regular updates (organization, expenses, progress, etc.) with the works manager to regulate the budget
- Monitor consumption and expenses at the end of construction for small construction sites
- Monitor the budget for the construction site
- Negotiate contracts with suppliers and distributors
- Evaluate the impacts and risks of the work on the site environment by analyzing the study report in order to propose an appropriate technical solution
- Guarantee compliance with environmental standards procedures when monitoring the progress of the construction site in order to reduce the impact of the construction site on the environment
- Implement a responsible purchasing strategy and choose supply chains and products in order to reduce environmental impact
- Organize and plan human and material resources using appropriate means in order to reduce the environmental impact and preserve the site environment
- Supervise the evacuation and recycling of waste from the site
- Coordinate the intervention of machines and construction equipment
- Set daily production objectives (quality, compliance with deadlines) with team leaders and help them in the event of incidents (moving machinery, rectifying measurements, etc.)
- Participate in the analysis of the causes and propose corrective action
- Manage the performance and profitability of an activity or project
- Propose adjustments to the construction manager to improve profitability, such as new processes or materials
- Describe a problem, answer questions (internal and external) and explain the proposed solutions in an understandable manner
- Maintain good relations with local residents and the various stakeholders present in the site environment (other trades, other construction companies)
- Manage teams by raising awareness of ecological transition issues in order to develop good practices on the site
- Make a decision and explain it
- Report on your activity
- Transmit information and ensure understanding by using appropriate language to internal and external stakeholders
- Apply data security and confidentiality rules
- Enforce the rules of the specific safety and health protection plan (PPSP)
- Propose an adjustment of resources (human and technical)
- Use digital tools
