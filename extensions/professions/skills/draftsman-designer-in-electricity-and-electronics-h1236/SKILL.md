## Scope

The electrical and electronic designer designs detailed plans and diagrams for complex electrical and electronic systems. Collaborates with engineers to define needs and constraints in pre-project Carry out simulations and tests to validate designs in digital form Updates plans based on feedback and writes technical documents based on developments Ensures compliance of projects with safety standards and regulations in force

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Establish and carry out test programs based on specifications and technical files
- Create and assemble models, prototypes, pre-series of electronic products
- Test and validate circuit functionalities
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Design printed and electrical circuit diagrams
- Use computer-aided design (CAD) software
- Use modeling and simulation software
- Calculate and define the powers, quantities, physical constraints of components, subassemblies, assemblies
