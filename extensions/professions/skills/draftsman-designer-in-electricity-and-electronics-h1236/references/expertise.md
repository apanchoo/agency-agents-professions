# Draftsman/designer in electricity and electronics

## Scope

The electrical and electronic designer designs detailed plans and diagrams for complex electrical and electronic systems. Collaborates with engineers to define needs and constraints in pre-project Carry out simulations and tests to validate designs in digital form Updates plans based on feedback and writes technical documents based on developments Ensures compliance of projects with safety standards and regulations in force

## Activities and Skills

- Determine the specifications and dimensions of parts, subassemblies or assemblies
- Write technical documents in compliance with current standards
- Establish and carry out test programs based on specifications and technical files
- Study and identify developments (functionality, profitability, quality, etc.) of components, products, equipment
- Conduct feasibility studies
- Create and assemble models, prototypes, pre-series of electronic products
- Test and validate circuit functionalities
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Design printed and electrical circuit diagrams
- Design production support
- Create a technical drawing, plan, or map
- Create technical documentation
- Read and use an electronic diagram
- Use computer-aided design (CAD) software
- Use modeling and simulation software
- Calculate and define the powers, quantities, physical constraints of components, subassemblies, assemblies
- Optimize the arrangement of components on an electrical circuit
- Optimize production costs through judicious technical choices
- Identify technical constraints
- Ensure product compliance with current standards
- Make technical adjustments in response to manufacturing issues
- Verify by simulation, tests, trials, calculations, the functionalities and characteristics of the electrical or electronic system
- Respect cost constraints and production deadlines
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
