# Close protection agent

## Scope

The close protection agent ensures the security and protection of people from various threats. Ensures the physical security of protected people Analyzes risks and plans secure travel Intervenes in the event of a direct threat to neutralize the dangers Maintains constant monitoring of the immediate environment Collaborates with law enforcement and other security services Can drive armored vehicles for secure travel

## Related Job Titles

- Private bodyguard

## Activities and Skills

- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Analyze locations to identify weak points and recommend security improvements
- Apply safety procedures in case of emergency
- Check the operation of a safety device
- Detect and locate any individual or behavior presenting a threat to security or safety
- Escort protected individuals during their daily trips and special events
- Assess potential threats and plan necessary protection measures
- Evaluate security risks in different situations
- Maintain constant attention and be able to respond quickly to any threats
- Maintain good physical condition in accordance with job requirements
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Work closely with security officials and law enforcement
- Write detailed reports on incidents and daily activities
- Control access and movement of people
- Control or apprehend a person
- Evacuate people, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Manage access to restricted areas
- Implement means of protecting people
- Protect people and property
- Check accreditations, entry fees, access permits
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Managing a conflict situation
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Ensure the protection of sensitive data and information

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
