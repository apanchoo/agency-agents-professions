# DevOps Engineer

## Scope

The DevOps Engineer orchestrates the integration and continuous deployment of IT applications, ensuring optimal collaboration between the development and operations teams. Designs and implements continuous integration and deployment pipelines to automate development processes Supervises collaboration between developers and operational teams to ensure high availability of services Optimizes system performance by integrating development and operations best practices Manages development, test and production environments to ensure consistency and quality of deployments Implements monitoring tools to monitor the health and performance of applications in real time Ensures the security of IT infrastructures by applying security policies rigorous security
