# Industrial seamstress / Industrial clothing seamstress

## Scope

The industrial clothing designer carries out assembly operations, assembling textile articles or flexible materials on a production line. Controls finished products for compliance with industry standards while respecting quality, productivity and safety requirements Uses industrial machines for sewing and finishing Involved in the mass manufacturing of clothing or technical textile articles Manages the inventory of materials and supplies necessary for production

## Activities and Skills

- Design and develop a model, a prototype
- Develop more efficient working methods
- Adapt patterns for specific sizes
- Assemble the pieces of clothing
- Assemble-assemble skirts/pants
- Choose sewing techniques adapted to the materials
- Assemble the parts and sub-assemblies of textile articles by stitching according to the technical sheets
- Position the parts according to the marks for assembly/assembly of the item
- Carry out buttonhole operations on textile items
- Carry out fixing operations, installation of iron-on adhesive, reinforcing element on textile articles
- Carry out filling operations (feathers, wadding, etc.) on textile items
- Carry out elastic installation operations on textile articles
- Carry out lining installation operations on textile items
- Carry out filling operations (hemming, topstitching, etc.) on textile items
- Select the materials (fabrics, threads, ornaments, etc.), colors and tools appropriate to the work
- Organize the workspace to optimize production flow
- Make corrections or repairs to a work
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Manage material stock
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
