## Scope

The ethnologist is a scientist specializing in cultural anthropology, who focuses specifically on the study of human cultures, norms, traditions, mores, social structures and beliefs of different human groups. Analyzes the social structures, cultural practices, and beliefs of the groups studied Conducts fieldwork to collect data, often through participatory observation or interviews Contributes to the preservation of cultural heritage by documenting endangered languages and traditions Participates in conferences and writes articles to share findings with the scientific community and the general public Collaborates with institutions, universities, and museums to develop research projects and exhibitions May teach at academic institutions and train students in ethnographic research methods

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Study group dynamics
- Documenting traditional practices
- Present and explain scientific advances and research work
- Conduct field research, observing, interviewing, and participating in the activities of the communities studied
- Interpret data in a scientific context and develop theories
- Observe cultural behaviors
- Live in prolonged immersion within a community or cultural group to better understand their practices, beliefs, and social organization
- Analyze ethnographic data
