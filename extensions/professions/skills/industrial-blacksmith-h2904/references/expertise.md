# Industrial blacksmith

## Scope

The Industrial Blacksmith manufactures metal parts using hot or cold deformation processes. Chooses the forging processes to be implemented according to the constraints of the specifications Carries out the assembly and adjustment of production tools, forging tools according to the operating modes Configures and or operates the automated forging lines (heaters, cold forming) Carries out deformation operations according to the forging specifications Controls the conformity of the parts and or identifies technical anomalies during manufacturing Carries out first-level upkeep and maintenance of the equipment
