# Surgeon

## Scope

The surgeon is a highly qualified healthcare professional with knowledge of the anatomy and functioning of the human body Performs surgical procedures to treat various illnesses and injuries Examines patients and determines the need for surgery Explains the medical procedure to patients before surgery and provides post-operative follow-up Works closely with a healthcare team to ensure comprehensive care Maintains current medical and surgical knowledge to apply the most advanced techniques May specialize in a specific area of surgery to provide highly specialized care

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams for patient monitoring
- Coordinate a team in the operating room
- Train new team members
- Teaching, developing skills
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Analyze post-operative results
- Ensuring the traceability of surgical procedures
- Ensure medical follow-up of a patient
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Inform, support a patient
- Master pain management techniques and relief methods
- Perform medical expertise
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Carry out a surgical procedure
- Monitor vital signs during the procedure
- Process medical information for rapid decision-making
- Maintain a watch on innovations in emergency medicine
- Update the patient's medical record
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Evaluate the risks associated with the surgical intervention
- Apply health safety protocols
- Arguing the choices of surgical techniques during clinical meetings
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Show tact and diplomacy
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data
- Demonstrate endurance and composure
- Managing stress and emergencies in the operating room
- Demonstrate good physical and mental resistance

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Areas of expertise

- Monitoring technological advances in medicine
- Interpretation of medical analyzes

- Medical sterilization protocols
- Knowledge of surgical pathologies
