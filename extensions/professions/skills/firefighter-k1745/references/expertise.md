# Firefighter

## Scope

A firefighter is a rescue and safety professional who helps and saves lives in emergency situations. Mainly intervenes in emergency situations related to people (fire, road accident, drowning, flood, industrial pollution, earthquake, etc.). Carries out awareness and prevention actions Can supervise a team

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Organize interventions on fires, accidents, rescue of victims
- Raise awareness of the causes and consequences of road accidents to avoid repeat offenses
- Carry out emergency care or organize arrangements for hospitalization, decontamination, etc. during accident situations, intoxication, etc.
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of fire safety innovations
- Analyze risks and malfunctions, room for improvement in security systems
- Design and implement awareness-raising actions
- Evaluate and combat fire risks, implement preventive measures
- Assess potential risks and make decisions to prevent accidents
- Prevent fires
- Carry out interventions to combat disasters (fires, collapses, floods, etc.) and dangerous, corrosive, toxic products
- Control the security systems of public establishments
- Transport the injured who require it to the nearest hospital
- Apply rescue and firefighting techniques and tactics during aircraft accidents or incidents
- Quickly assess the condition of a victim to determine the appropriate rescue actions
- Intervene in the event of vital risk (cardiac arrest, hemorrhage, loss of consciousness, trauma, etc.)
- Intervene in hostile or dangerous environments (fire, road accident, etc.)
- Intervene in domestic accidents (fall, burn, carbon monoxide poisoning, electrocution, etc.)
- Intervene during natural disasters (flood, forest fire, storm, earthquake, avalanche, heatwave, etc.)
- Provide assistance to people
- Regulate traffic if the situation requires it
- Stabilize a victim's condition while waiting for medical help to arrive
- Use tracking equipment to find victims
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Effectively communicate needs and emergencies
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
- Practice handling equipment (truck, fire hose, rams, etc.)
- Regularly train first aid actions as a team in order to improve the rescue chain during an incident
- Keep up to date with knowledge of safety, risk management and first aid
