# Safety engineer in the nuclear industry

## Scope

The Nuclear Industry Safety Engineer plays a crucial role in the protection and management of risks linked to nuclear activities. Evaluates and analyzes nuclear risks to guarantee the security of installations Develops and implements safety procedures in compliance with current regulations Conducts security audits to identify and correct vulnerabilities Collaborates with technical teams to optimize safety processes Participates in staff training on nuclear safety practices Ensures regulatory and technological monitoring to maintain security standards

## Related Job Titles

- Nuclear safety engineer
- Engineer Expert in nuclear safety
- Nuclear security engineer
- Manager of the nuclear industry safety department
