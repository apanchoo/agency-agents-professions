# Tram driver

## Scope

The tram driver, an essential role in ensuring urban mobility and the fluidity of public transport. Drives a tram according to safety rules and established schedules Ensures the transport of passengers in optimal conditions of comfort and safety Monitors the cleanliness and proper functioning of the equipment Informs passengers about routes, stops and connections Manages emergency situations and technical incidents that may arise en route Maintains the cleanliness and proper functioning of the rolling stock

## Related Job Titles

- Trolleybus driver
- Traminot / Tram employee

## Activities and Skills

- Check the operation of devices, carry out first-level maintenance or inform the manager, the maintenance department in the event of malfunctions
- Analyze traffic conditions and adapt speed
- Ensuring passenger safety when stopping and starting
- Driving a rail vehicle
- Prepare the vehicle, mark the route and plan for hazards
- Transport passengers
- Transporting people with reduced mobility
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure monitoring of current regulations
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Control access to the tram when getting on and off
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Ensure effective communication between drivers and the control center
- Communicate with the control unit in the event of anomalies
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Managing a conflict situation
- Comply with local regulations
- Respect safety standards when driving
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Adapt to climatic conditions
- Report any anomaly or damage

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of tram signaling systems

## Areas of expertise

- Knowledge of technological developments in transportation
- Operational and Passenger Information Support Systems (SAEIV)

## Standards and processes

- Regulation of passenger transport
- Respect of strict transport schedules
- Continuing training in transport safety
- Public transport emergency and safety procedures
- Vehicle on-board documents

## Professional techniques

- Incident reporting procedures
- Passenger communication techniques
- Economical and ecological driving techniques
