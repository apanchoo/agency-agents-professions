# Carpenter / Aluminum joiner

## Scope

Manufactures aluminum elements for home equipment, according to the rules of the art: windows, doors, verandas, bay windows, glass facades, guardrails, partitions, pergolas, gates, screens, etc. Carries out works by applying the rules and standards in force, from taking measurements to manufacturing. Depending on the size of the company, can install joinery. Works on a traditional machine tool and/or on a digital machine, with recyclable materials. Contributes to optimizing the energy performance of the building work and the comfort of the occupants through the quality of finish of the achievements.

- Aluminum joinery installer
- Assembler / Aluminum assembler
