## Scope

The Litigation and Collections Department Manager plays a crucial role in financial management and debt recovery. Supervises and coordinates debt collection activities Negotiates payment plans with debtors to minimize financial losses Ensures legal monitoring of litigation files Optimizes recovery procedures in collaboration with his team Analyzes non-payment files and proposes appropriate solutions Trains and supervises his team members to improve their efficiency
