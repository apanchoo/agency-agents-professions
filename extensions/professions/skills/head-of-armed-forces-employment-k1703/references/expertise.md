# Head of Armed Forces Employment

## Scope

The person responsible for the employment of the armed forces contributes to the design, planning, preparation and conduct of military operations and civil and military actions, in staff or combat units. Analyzes and evaluates operational or tactical information Plans and conducts military operations Makes rapid and precise decisions under pressure Develops sufficient physical and mental fitness to carry out his profession
