# School manager (high school, college, etc.)

## Scope

The school superintendent participates in the management and proper functioning of educational institutions. Supervises the maintenance of infrastructure and equipment Ensures administrative management of staff and students Coordinates with academic and administrative services Ensures compliance with safety and hygiene standards Can participate in the development of educational and teaching projects

## Related Job Titles

- University Intendant

## Activities and Skills

- Ensuring safety in an educational environment
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate general and technical services
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Communicate with local and national academic authorities
- Update a file, a database
- Use administrative management software
- Monitor the work (facilitation of weekly monitoring meetings, writing of reports, quality management, cost, deadline, planning, hazard management, contradictory findings)
- Ensure the maintenance of educational and administrative materials
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Implement safety and evacuation procedures in the event of an emergency
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Control the maintenance of the assets of a structure and organize interventions (maintenance, monitoring of works)
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Organize and control a supply
- Check the conformity of deliveries
- Track orders, invoicing
- Prepare financial management reports
- Prepare the constituent elements of payroll
- Carry out administrative management of personnel
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Manage the budgets allocated to the various departments of the establishment
- Oversee financial management
- Prepare the constituent elements of a mandate
- Ensure compliance with current procedures and regulations
- Organize the material and educational resources necessary for the operation of a school establishment
- Supervise the administrative management of the establishment
- Arguing and defending improvement projects for the establishment before decision-making bodies
