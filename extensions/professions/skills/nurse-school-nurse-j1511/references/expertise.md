# Nurse / School nurse

## Scope

The School Nurse plays a crucial role in maintaining the health and well-being of students within educational establishments. Provides first aid and medical emergencies within the establishment Contributes to prevention and health education campaigns Carries out individual health monitoring and periodic check-ups Collaborates with teachers and parents to monitor students Participates in the implementation of health-related educational projects Manages medical records and ensures the confidentiality of information

## Related Job Titles

- Specialized nurse / Nurse specialized in school health

## Activities and Skills

- Promote collaboration between health services and schools
- Advise learners in the organization of their personal work
- Advise, support a person
- Teaching, developing skills
- Intervene in the event of an accident or illness
- Carry out nursing care
- Carry out a diagnosis or paramedical assessment
- Monitor the health of students on a daily basis
- Design and implement prevention actions, information on health and psychosocial risks
- Building a safe school environment
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Write a report, an activity report
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Collaborate with teachers to integrate health into the curriculum
- Organize health awareness workshops
- Promote good hygiene practices among students
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Common pathologies
- Communication with parents about children's health
- Integration of new health technologies
- Psychological assessment of young people

- Emergency care protocols

## Professional techniques

- Communication techniques with parents
