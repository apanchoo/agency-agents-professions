# Port handling structure manager

## Scope

The Port Handling Structure Manager orchestrates the loading and unloading of goods. Supervises ship loading and unloading operations Coordinates handling teams to optimize logistics flows Ensures compliance with safety standards and regulations in force Manages schedules and staff assignments Optimizes port operation costs and deadlines Maintains effective communication with other port stakeholders
