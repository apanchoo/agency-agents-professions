# Accompanying driver / Accompanying driver for people with reduced mobility

## Scope

The driver accompanying people with reduced mobility ensures safe and adapted transport. Provides transport and support for people with reduced mobility from their homes to different locations Ensures the safety and comfort of passengers during the journey Adapts their conduct and attitude according to the specific needs of passengers Assists passengers when getting in and out of the vehicle and installs them in the vehicle Keeps the vehicle in good clean and working order

## Related Job Titles

- Accompanying driver / Driver accompanying people with reduced mobility

## Activities and Skills

- Apply emergency procedures in the event of an accident
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Ensure first level maintenance of the vehicle
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Managing stress in busy situations
- Adapt driving to the mobility situation of passengers
- Driving a road vehicle
- Determine a route based on delays and traffic characteristics
- Position and secure wheelchairs in the vehicle
- Position and secure passengers in the vehicle
- Take charge of a vehicle with its equipment and accessories
- Transporting people with disabilities and/or reduced mobility
- Use specific vehicle access equipment for people with reduced mobility (PRM)
- Ensure the comfort and safety of passengers
- Write a report, an activity report
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Prevent and act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Ensure communication with the operations center
- Choose communication methods adapted to the understanding and expression abilities of each person
- Communicate effectively with passengers and/or their attendants
- Inform passengers and provide assistance to people with reduced mobility
- Apply accessibility rules for people with disabilities
- Ensure confidentiality and discretion regarding passengers
- Handle emergency or unforeseen situations calmly and efficiently
- Managing a conflict situation
- Respect health and safety rules at work

- Demonstrate rigor and precision
