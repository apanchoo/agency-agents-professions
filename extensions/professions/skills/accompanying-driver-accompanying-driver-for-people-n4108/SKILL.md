## Scope

The driver accompanying people with reduced mobility ensures safe and adapted transport. Provides transport and support for people with reduced mobility from their homes to different locations Ensures the safety and comfort of passengers during the journey Adapts their conduct and attitude according to the specific needs of passengers Assists passengers when getting in and out of the vehicle and installs them in the vehicle Keeps the vehicle in good clean and working order

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Driving a road vehicle
- Position and secure passengers in the vehicle
- Take charge of a vehicle with its equipment and accessories
- Transporting people with disabilities and/or reduced mobility
- Ensure the comfort and safety of passengers
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure communication with the operations center
