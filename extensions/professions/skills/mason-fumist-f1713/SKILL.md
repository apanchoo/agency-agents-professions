## Scope

The smoke mason is specialized both in the construction of masonry structures (walls, chimneys) and in the installation, maintenance and repair of smoke evacuation systems (ducts, stoves, boilers). Builds and installs fireplaces and wood or pellet stoves Carries out masonry work specific to the installation of heating systems Ensures compliance of installations according to current safety standards Advises customers on the choice of materials and equipment Performs diagnostics and preventive maintenance Can restore or renovate old fireplaces
