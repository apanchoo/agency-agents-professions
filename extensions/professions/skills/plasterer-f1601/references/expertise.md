# Plasterer

## Scope

The Plasterer, involved in building finishing, applies plaster to walls and ceilings to smooth them and prepare them for painting or decoration. Collaborates with other trades to ensure consistency and quality of finishes Prepares surfaces, mixes and applies plasters according to project specifications Installs thermal and acoustic insulation systems to improve the comfort of homes Can create moldings and decorative ornaments in staff to beautify spaces Can restore plaster elements in historic buildings or monuments
