# Medical writer / Medical writer in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Medical Writer in the pharmaceutical industry specializes in the production of scientific documentation in collaboration with doctors and scientific teams. Writes preclinical and clinical study reports, publications or any other medical document Ensures regulatory compliance of documents produced Guarantees the quality of clinical documents according to international standards and internal procedures Participates in the company's scientific communication strategy Can lead internal and external training
