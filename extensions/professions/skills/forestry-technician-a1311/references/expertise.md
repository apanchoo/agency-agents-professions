# Forestry technician

## Scope

The forest technician, specialized in the management, maintenance and conservation of forests, implements sustainable forest management programs. Manages and monitors the forest in conjunction with other forestry professionals Plans and supervises reforestation and cutting operations Informs and raises public awareness of biodiversity Advises owners or elected officials on best practices for sustainable forest management Participates in the prevention and management of natural risks (fires, diseases) Collaborates with public and private organizations for the implementation of forestry projects
