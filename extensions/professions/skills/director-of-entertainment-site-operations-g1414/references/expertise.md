# Director of entertainment site operations

## Scope

The Operations Director organizes the activity and manages the operation of the structure in its technical, commercial, human and financial dimensions. Supervises and coordinates the daily activities of the site Develops and manages the budget to optimize the profitability of the site Ensures compliance with safety standards and local regulations for visitors and staff Participates in the marketing and promotion strategy of the site
