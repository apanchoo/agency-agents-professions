# Pharmacy technician

## Scope

The pharmacy technician, in close collaboration with the pharmacist, ensures the preparation, management and delivery of medications to patients. Prepares and dispenses medications under the supervision of a pharmacist Advises and informs customers on the use of pharmaceutical products and compatibility with others Manages drug stocks and places the necessary orders Ensures the monitoring of patient files and updating of information Participates in the production of magistral and medicinal preparations Ensures compliance with hygiene and safety standards in the work space

## Related Job Titles

- Pharmacy assistant
- Community pharmacy technician
- Hospital pharmacy technician

- Inform healthcare staff about pharmaceutical products
- Participate in continuing education to stay informed of new pharmaceutical developments
- Technically assist staff in the interpretation of prescriptions, dosage and control the methods of dispensing medications

- Evaluate the effectiveness of medical treatments
- Identify the request from the healthcare service or the patient
- Inform about the terms of prescription
- Monitor medication delivery in the prescription scheduler
- Monitor side effects of treatments
- Ensure, with health professionals, that medication and care prescriptions are properly adapted
- Contribute to clinical studies to test new drugs
- Update pharmaceutical records
- Prepare medications in different forms (pills, powders, liquids)
- Carry out dosages and samples of products
- Make pharmaceutical preparations
- Check medical prescriptions to avoid drug interactions
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Monitor medication storage conditions
- Propose health crisis management plans
- Control the delivery method of certain specific medications (narcotics, etc.)
- Carry out quality checks on medicines
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Packaging products
- Control the management of stocks of medicines and medical equipment
- Define supply needs
- Define storage conditions
- Manage medication returns
- Manage stocks of medicines and pharmaceutical products
- Follow up on orders and deliveries
- Organize vaccination campaigns in pharmacies
- Establish elements of an order
- Manage medical records and ensure their confidentiality
- Propose generic alternatives to reduce treatment costs
- Ensure compliance with health standards
- Providing advice adapted to the client’s morphology
- Analyze drug consumption trends
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Participate in medication management programs
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data
- Respect safety and hygiene standards
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of medical products
