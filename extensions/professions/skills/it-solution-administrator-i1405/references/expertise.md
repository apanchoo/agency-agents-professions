# IT solution administrator

## Scope

The IT Solution Administrator takes care of the management and optimization of office IT systems. Ensures the maintenance and updating of office systems Optimizes office processes and tools to improve productivity Manages access rights and security of office computer systems Provides technical support to users for office software and hardware Documents procedures and trains users in new office technologies Analyzes user needs to propose suitable IT solutions

## Related Job Titles

- Administrator / Office administrator
- Workstation Administrator

## Activities and Skills

- Provide staff training on data protection
- Train users in office tools
- Ensure technological monitoring of big data tools
- Analyze the performance of an information system
- Develop scripts to automate tasks
- Exploit Data Science or Artificial Intelligence solutions
- Integrate AI/Data Science tools
- Optimize data backup processes
- Create a standard configuration on a workstation
- Carry out network cabling of computer or office equipment
- Diagnose faults from a computer
- Diagnose technical malfunctions
- Check the operation of devices, carry out first-level maintenance or inform the manager, the maintenance department in the event of malfunctions
- Anticipate cybersecurity risks
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Ensure compliance with cybersecurity rules
- Provide advanced technical support
- Deploy alternative solutions in the event of a malfunction of IT or office equipment
- Handle technical support requests
- Arguing for the adoption of new technologies
- Communicate system updates clearly
- Raise awareness of data protection issues
- Optimize the use of office tools
- Comply with IT security standards
