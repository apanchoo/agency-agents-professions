# Track supervisor

## Scope

The runway supervisor orchestrates operations on airport runways to guarantee safety and efficiency, in close collaboration with the air navigation services (SNA), the Civil Aviation Directorate (DAC) and runway operators. Ensures safety supervision in the movement area (aprons and maneuvering areas) Checks and ensures that all conditions are met for optimal operation of the movement areas Trains employees requiring driving authorization to travel in the “airside” area Manages emergency situations and unforeseen events in collaboration with the security services Optimizes processes to improve the fluidity of operations on the runway

## Related Job Titles

- Air Cargo Operations Supervisor
- Supervisory Technician/Flight Supervisor Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Liaise with emergency services
- Ensure a smooth transition between teams
- Encourage teamwork for efficient service
- Optimize the use of resources on the track
- Train new employees on runway procedures
- Train all people involved in driving on apron and maneuvering areas (theoretical and practical) on a regular basis
- Adapt operations according to needs
- Manage or monitor the maintenance of your own equipment (vehicles, radios, small equipment)
- Anticipate what to do in abnormal and emergency situations
- Ensuring the safety of runway operations
- Coordinate operations on the runway
- Participate in the preparation of specific operations in the movement area (official events, etc.)
- Supervise the loading of a plane
- Ensure the monitoring and control of apron subcontracting activities (in particular cleaning operations)
- Document incidents and actions taken
- Write a report, an activity report
- Monitor compliance with security procedures
- Ensure inspections of aprons and trigger corrective actions if necessary
- Ensure visits to maneuvering areas (4 visits per day of 1.5 hours each plus exceptional visits) and initiate corrective actions if necessary
- Control the operation of installations in coordination with air navigation services (SNA)
- Control obstacles in the airfield area to ensure the safety of aircraft operating around the airfield and on the platform
- Carry out specific inspections on the apron upon request (before an official visit, after de-icing a parking lot)
- Recover objects or animals that have been struck by aircraft from the movement area
- Monitor weather conditions to adjust operations
- Evaluate runway operations performance
- Optimize workflows on the track
- Organize technical flight preparation operations
- Ensure an interface role between the different departments of the company
- Communicate effectively with airport authorities
- Facilitate collaboration between ground teams
- Relay information
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Using English in a professional context
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Update the compilation of work for the current week and distribute to the various entities concerned (internal and external)
- Resolve operational issues quickly
