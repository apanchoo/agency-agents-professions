# Building painter

## Scope

Prepares supports and carries out painting, finishing and decoration work indoors, on walls and ceilings. Works on new or renovation projects. Depending on the site, may have to carry out additional activities such as the installation or removal of elements (sanitary facilities, partitions, flexible floors) or exterior painting.
