# Telecoms manager

## Scope

The Telecoms Manager ensures the development and maintenance of telecommunications infrastructures to guarantee optimal connectivity. Supervises and coordinates telecoms network development projects Ensures the maintenance and optimization of existing infrastructures Manages relationships with suppliers and technological partners Ensures compliance of installations with current standards and regulations Directs technical teams and supports their professional development Analyzes the company's communication needs and offers adapted telecoms solutions
