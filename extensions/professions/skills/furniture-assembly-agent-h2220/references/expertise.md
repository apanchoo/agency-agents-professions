# Furniture assembly agent

## Scope

The Furniture Assembly Agent assembles all the constituent parts of a piece of furniture as well as auxiliary elements, such as feet and cushions. Controls the quality of parts and assemblies to ensure compliance with specifications Makes adjustments and finishes on finished products Follows safety and hygiene standards throughout the production process May operate specific machines for cutting and assembling wood or metal
