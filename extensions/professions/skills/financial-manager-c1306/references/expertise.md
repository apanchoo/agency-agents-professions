# Financial Manager

## Scope

The Financial Manager is responsible for managing financial resources for public procurement. Ensures the stability and growth of public financial resources Optimizes costs and maximizes the efficiency of public spending Verifies compliance with financial and accounting standards, as well as making recommendations for improvement Advises on best financial practices in the context of public procurement Manages financial flows by developing budgets and ensures compliance with financial regulations

## Related Job Titles

- Banking market manager

- Develop, monitor and manage a budget

- Carry out financial market operations
- Carry out an accounting and financial analysis
- Supervise financing and investment operations
- Check expenditure compliance with budget forecasts
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Negotiate a contract

- Optimize organizational performance

- Develop and manage interpersonal relationships

- Ensure regulatory and legislative monitoring
