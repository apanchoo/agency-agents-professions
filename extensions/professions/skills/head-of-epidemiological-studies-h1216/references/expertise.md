# Head of epidemiological studies

## Scope

The Head of Epidemiological Studies directs and analyzes research on diseases in populations. Designs and directs studies to analyze health and disease in specific populations Interprets epidemiological data and makes recommendations for public health policies Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to improve data collection and analysis methods Participates in the writing of scientific reports and the communication of results to the scientific community and the public Ensures the application of ethical and legal standards in the conduct of studies Designs the epidemiological study plan and validates study protocols epidemiological Implements, monitors and manages subcontracted studies
