# Printer / Textile printer

## Scope

The Textile Printer specializes in the application of patterns, designs, logos or colors on textile supports, using printing processes. Helps shape the visual identity of textiles by intervening in one of the key stages of finishing Controls the quality of prints and adjusts machine parameters Respects safety standards in the production workshop

- Assistant driver / Assistant driver of textile printing machine
- Chromo driver in the textile industry
- Textile printing machine operator
- Operator of rotary frame printing machines
- Driver of color jet or spray printing machines
- Driver of flocking machines in the textile industry
- Driver of screen printing machines in the textile industry
- Printer in the textile industry
- Printer / Flat frame printer
- Printer / Rotary frame printer
- Printer / Printer on fabrics
- Printer / Tabletop printer in the textile industry

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure the training of new operators
- Manually apply colors
- Print designs
- Mount/dismount the cylinders, frames, etc. on the printing machine, frame the pattern and feed the machine with colors
- Optimize printing processes to reduce waste
- Position and secure the rollers on the machine, center the fabric and adjust the tension when feeding
- Take note of the manufacturing order before the start of production
- Prepare printing formulas
- Prepare the inks and supports needed for printing
- Adjust textile printing machine parameters
- Monitor machine measurement data during printing
- Select tools (squeegees, rollers, etc.) and printing frames and determine product needs (colors, etc.)
- Check the conformity of the parts in relation to the manufacturing order
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Checking the winding of the fabric on a roller
- Check the conformity of the prints produced
- Control the quality and conformity of a deliverable
- Identify anomalies, manufacturing defects
- Check the conformity of the first parts produced with a view to launching production and adjust as necessary
- Manage stocks of raw materials and consumables
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Document printing procedures for future reference
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
