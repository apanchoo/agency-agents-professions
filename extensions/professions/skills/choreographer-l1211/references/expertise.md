# Choreographer

## Scope

The choreographer, creator of dances and emotions, prepares, directs and coordinates, directly or indirectly, the work of the team which contributes to the development and presentation of a show Designs and stages dance sequences for shows or productions Directs auditions to select the dancers Collaborates with dancers, composers and costume and set designers Rehearses and adjusts the choreographies with the dancers Supervises rehearsals and performances to ensure artistic quality May master specific dance techniques May have side hustles such as practicing dance as a performer, teacher or art therapist

## Related Job Titles

- Choreologist
- Dancer / Dancer choreographer

- Ballet Master/Mistress
- Dance notator
