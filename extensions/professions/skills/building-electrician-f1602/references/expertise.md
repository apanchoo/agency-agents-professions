# Building electrician

## Scope

The building electrician is responsible for the design, implementation, maintenance and optimization of a building's low-voltage electricity installation. Carries out installation and commissioning work on electrical equipment in buildings for domestic, tertiary or industrial use according to safety rules Cables and connects very low voltage (ELV) installations (telephony, IT, alarms, etc.) Carries out troubleshooting and maintenance work Carries out work for the installation of renewable energies (EnR) Manages power outages and coordinates interventions to restore them Controls compliance of installations with safety standards and regulations vigor Monitors energy performance indicators of the installation and proposes improvement solutions
