# Careful Groom

## Scope

Provides daily care to horses and maintains vigilance over their state of health. Maintains boxes, paddocks and work tracks on a daily basis. Can also handle horse transportation.

## Related Job Titles

- Stud Warrant Officer
- Head groom
- Groom
