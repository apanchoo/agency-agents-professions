# Versatile yachting steward

## Scope

The versatile yachting steward ensures the reception, service and care of passengers on board. Manages the maintenance of cabins and common areas Organizes and supervises meals and special events Ensures the safety and comfort of passengers during the trip Can coordinate and lead a team of stewards Maintains the quality and service standards demanded by customers

## Related Job Titles

- Pleasure boat steward / hostess
- Cruise Steward / Hostess
- Steward / Hostess of maritime transport
- Steward / Hostess on yacht

## Activities and Skills

- Promote available leisure activities
- Organize on-board catering services
- Train new crew members
- Adapt services according to specific needs
- Optimize the layout of common spaces
- Ensure the cleanliness and order of the cabins
- Develop strategies to manage stress during peak season
- Participate in regular safety exercises
- Contribute to the continuous improvement of services
- Manage inventory of on-board supplies
- Liaise with local service providers
- Resolve passenger issues and complaints
- Respect environmental standards in waste management
- Welcome passengers and assist them during the trip
- Using technologies to improve the passenger experience
- Communicate effectively with passengers and/or their attendants
- Encourage collaboration between all crew members
- Ensuring the safety of passengers in the event of an emergency
- Ensure compliance with security procedures
- Managing emergency situations on board
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Regularly evaluate procedures
