# Automotive construction assembly worker

## Scope

Automotive assembly workers install and assemble prefabricated motor vehicle parts and components. Inspect motor vehicles for defects. Test assembled equipment to ensure proper performance and compliance with quality standards

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Identify the adjustment and assembly phases of elements, systems, mechanical assemblies based on the technical file or model
- Inject a material (foam, insulation, resin, glue, etc.) during assembly/assembly
- Integrate the digital twin into industrial processes
- Identify and position electrical elements, electronic components in the assembly order or power the machine
- Carry out the finishing of a product, an article, a work
- Carry out the development of production machines and tools
- Adjust machine and equipment parameters
- Identify editing, assembly, retouching operations
- Install ducts and wiring
- Carry out assembly, assembly and disassembly operations
- Check, position and fix the parts and elements of the assembly (frames, clusters, templates, structures, etc.)
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Monitor the supply of machines, facilities and the flow of materials or products
- Monitor assembly of parts
- Check the assembly/assembly (appearance, parallelism, clearance, maintenance, operation, etc.) and make adjustments, retouches
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Identify non-conformities
- Remove non-compliant products
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Optimize cycle times and production rates to improve industrial performance
- Participate in collaborative work
- Respect safety standards and quality procedures
- Respect the rates imposed by the assembly line
- Comply with current safety standards and regulations
- Build your professional project in the automotive sector
