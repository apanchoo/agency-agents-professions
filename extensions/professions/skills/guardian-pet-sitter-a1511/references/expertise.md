# Guardian / Pet sitter

## Scope

The Pet Sitter takes care of pets in the absence of their owners. Monitors and cares for animals in their environment, at home or in a kennel Feeds animals and ensures that their specific dietary needs are met Maintains cleanliness of facilities and animal living spaces Observes animal behavior and reports any changes to the owner or veterinarian May administer treatments prescribed by a veterinarian

## Related Job Titles

- Pet sitting - Pet sitter
- Pet sitter
- Kennel guard

## Activities and Skills

- Welcoming and reassuring those welcomed
- Advise the owner on diet, exercise, environment, to maintain the health and well-being of the animal
- Administer veterinary products
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Handle animals
- Implement education or training actions for animals during daily activities
- Feed the animals respecting specific diets
- Optimize living spaces for animal comfort
- Organize fun activities for animals
- Walking pets
- Prepare and distribute food rations to animals
- Respect animal welfare
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Take the animal out to an outdoor area
- Identify a sick animal and inform the stakeholders concerned (owner, veterinarian, etc.)
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Clean animal enclosures
- Manage food stocks and distribute food
- Carry out invoicing
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Ensuring the safety of animals during walks
- Communicate with pet owners
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Explain procedures and answer questions from pet owners
