# Expert in communication and networks

## Scope

The Communication and Networks Expert orchestrates the digital communication strategy and ensures network management. Designs and deploys communication strategies adapted to the company's objectives Manages and optimizes internal and external communication networks Ensures the maintenance and security of communication systems Analyzes network performance and suggests improvements Trains teams in the use of new communication tools and technologies Conducts technological monitoring to stay at the forefront of innovations in communication and networks
