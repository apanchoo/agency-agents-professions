# Construction equipment workshop manager

## Scope

The construction equipment workshop manager coordinates and organizes the work of electricians and mechanics within the repair workshop while managing the monitoring of equipment maintenance on construction sites or construction sites. Plans and ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Manages stocks of spare parts and orders the necessary supplies Ensures training and compliance with safety standards by teams Optimizes processes to improve workshop efficiency Maintains detailed documentation on interventions and maintenance carried out
