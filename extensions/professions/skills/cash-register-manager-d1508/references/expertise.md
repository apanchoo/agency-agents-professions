# Cash register manager

## Scope

The Cashier Manager is a professional responsible for supervising and managing cashier-related operations in a business or brand. Supervises and coordinates cash register activity Ensures training and monitoring of cashiers Optimizes customer flows to reduce waiting Manages disputes and ensures quality customer service Controls cash funds and transactions May participate in the development of commercial strategies for the point of sale

## Related Job Titles

- Assistant Cashier Manager
- Head of cash register
- Head of cash register department
- Head of cash register sector
- Cash register manager
- Cashier line manager

## Activities and Skills

- Organize the work of a team
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Supervise the activity of a team
- Train and develop team skills
- Supporting a new arrival employee when they take up their position
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Optimize checkout layout to speed up service
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Check the compliance of cash transactions
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Organize order processing
- Close a fund
- Track orders, invoicing
- Manage cash transactions and operations
- Proceed to cashout
- Carry out monetary transactions between the safe and the cash registers
- Monitor cash transactions
- Ensure compliance with the labor code and the company's internal regulations (respect of schedules, absences, etc.)
- Manage conflicts in the event of a dispute with a customer
- Carry out a control of financial transactions
- Optimize visitor flow to reduce waiting times
- Provide after-sales service
- Develop promotions to increase sales
- Make refunds
- Offer promotions and discounts to build customer loyalty
- Present and promote a product or service
- Monitor performance indicators, analyze and correct discrepancies
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- React quickly in the event of a cash register malfunction
