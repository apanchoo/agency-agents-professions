## Scope

The recovery and revalorization sorting operator plays a crucial role in waste management and preservation of the environment. Separates and classifies recyclable materials according to their nature and quality Controls the quality of sorted materials and eliminates contaminants Uses specific sorting equipment to optimize the recovery process Complies with safety standards and environmental regulations in force Contributes to environmental awareness and education in communities Participates in the continuous improvement of sorting and recycling processes

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Optimize sorting processes to increase efficiency
- Apply standards related to material recycling
- Recycle wooden products and materials, pallets
- Recycle products and materials made of paper, cardboard, plastic, rubber, glass, etc.
- Recycle metal products and materials
- Recycle household appliance products and materials
- Sort products, parts, materials and direct them to recovery or elimination channels
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
