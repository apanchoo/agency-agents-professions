# Fire safety engineer

## Scope

The Fire Safety Engineer develops, implements and manages safety strategies in various environments. Acts to prevent accidents, minimize risks and ensure compliance with safety standards and regulations Conducts safety audits and suggests improvements by ensuring regulatory and technical monitoring in matters of fire safety Trains staff in fire prevention and emergency procedures Collaborates with rescue and emergency services to optimize interventions

## Related Job Titles

- Industrial prevention-safety engineer
- Industrial process safety engineer
- Industrial safety engineer
- Environmental safety engineer in industry

## Activities and Skills

- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of fire safety innovations
- Keep security records up to date
- Develop or apply a safety organization plan in the event of a fire or crisis situation
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Supervise checks and maintenance of technical fire safety installations
- Analyze risks in terms of personal protection and fire safety
- Analyze, prevent and anticipate risks in the work environment
- Communicate safety instructions clearly
- Check compliance of the site, installations and equipment with regulatory standards (ERP, hygiene, fire safety, PMR accessibility)
- Define and implement security protocols
- Develop risk prevention strategies (environmental, health, fire, etc.)
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Evaluate and combat fire risks, implement preventive measures
- Assess potential risks and make decisions to prevent accidents
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Prevent fires
- Carry out a technical audit
- Write security reports
- Prevent and act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Ensure safety rules
- Managing an emergency situation
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Comply with current safety and environmental standards
- Use digital communication tools
