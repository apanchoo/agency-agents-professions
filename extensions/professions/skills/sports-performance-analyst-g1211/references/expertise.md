# Sports performance analyst

## Scope

Sequences matches, sports meetings and training sessions in order to produce statistics and video montages to objectively measure the individual and collective performance of athletes or groups of athletes (player placements, fouls committed, strengths and weaknesses, etc.) Intervenes before, during and after the match and training according to the objectives: prepare for a match, make tactical choices, improve a game strategy during a match, develop future training programs, improve technical aspects and tactics of teams and athletes Has proven technical skills in the collection and analysis of data and relies on a very detailed understanding of the game. Is fully integrated into the sports staff in the same way as other specialists and often acts under the responsibility of the sports director or the manager/head coach

## Related Job Titles

- Performance accompanist
- Sports video analyst
- Performance data analyst

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze sports videos to determine sports goals
- Evaluate the physical and technical skills of athletes
- Select and install sports performance analysis tools
- Support sports and technical staff in coaching professional athletes
- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Carry out technical or technological monitoring to anticipate developments
- Analyze data with a view to proposing appropriate solutions
- Analyze sports performance through video sequences
- Collect statistical data
- Compare analysis results to previous results, minimum or maximum values and report
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Select the material or equipment according to the competition and constraints
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Implement decision support tools
- Collaborate in a group to carry out a project
- Transmit information to help with decision-making
- Use digital tools
