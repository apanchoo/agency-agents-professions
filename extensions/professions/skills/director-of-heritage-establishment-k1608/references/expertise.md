# Director of heritage establishment

## Scope

A director of a heritage establishment is responsible for the management, promotion and development of a site or institution linked to heritage (museum, historical monument, archaeological site, archive center, cultural heritage establishment, etc.). Ensures the preservation of collections, buildings or sites Defines and monitors restoration and conservation policies Seeks funding (subsidies, sponsorship, partnerships) Defines guidelines for exhibitions, mediation and cultural programming Promotes cultural accessibility (schools, remote audiences, inclusion) Supervises and coordinates teams (curators, mediators, technicians, administrative staff, etc.)
