# Thanatopractor

## Scope

The Thanatopractor, a professional essential to respecting the last tributes. Carries out all conservation care Responsible for the presentation and dressing of the deceased according to the family's wishes and funeral protocols Uses embalming techniques that comply with health standards Maintains a clean and safe working environment

## Related Job Titles

- Embalmer
- Responsible for mortuary care

## Activities and Skills

- Carry out transfer operations, body reception, preparation, assistance with autopsies
- Carry out mortuary care
- Complete an intervention report
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Handle dangerous or fragile products
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Drain, recover and condition toxic products, fluids and substances
- Disinfect and decontaminate sensitive premises
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Establish the terms and conditions for providing conservation or embalming care
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Dispose of sensitive waste
- Provide information on funeral care and administrative procedures
- Raise awareness of body preservation practices and the use of funeral equipment
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect hygiene and health safety standards

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
