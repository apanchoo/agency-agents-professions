## Scope

Industrial Electroplating treats the surface of metals by electrolysis by depositing metallic layers to protect them against corrosion. Prepares surfaces by degreasing, stripping or polishing the parts to be treated Performs electrolytic baths by precisely dosing the chemicals according to established formulas Monitors the quality of the metal deposit and makes the necessary adjustments Performs finishing, rinsing and drying of the treated parts Ensures the maintenance of the installations and control of the quality of the baths
