# Weather station operations manager

## Scope

The Weather Station Operations Manager manages and ensures the proper functioning of weather stations. Oversees the collection of weather data to ensure its accuracy and reliability Analyzes weather data to provide accurate forecasts Manages and trains the station team to optimize daily operations Provides preventive and corrective maintenance of station equipment Collaborates with national and international agencies for the exchange of weather information Participates in research projects to improve weather forecasting techniques
