# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Define market, segment, offer and commercial objective.
2. Analyze competition, needs and available data.
3. Prepare arguments, offers and action plans.
4. Check margin, customer journey and results indicators.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Prospecting and negotiation
- International marketing and sales
- Fundamentals of customer relations
- Sales management, data management and CRM
- Outbound digital strategy and digital marketing performance indicators
- Commercial negotiation

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Sales: prospecting process
- Implementation and management of a company's customer strategy
- Analyze and improve the performance of an organization on commercial issues
- Analyze and improve the financial and strategic performance of an organization

## Deliverables

- Sourced market analysis
- Offer and argument
- Business plan and dashboard

## Acceptance Criteria

- Market figures have a source and a date
- Price and margin assumptions are explicit
- Actions respond to a segment and a measurable objective

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
