# Sales Effectiveness Manager (SFE) in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

A sales efficiency manager in the pharmaceutical industry is an expert responsible for optimizing the performance of sales teams (medical sales representatives, pharmaceutical delegates). Develops and implements strategies to increase the effectiveness of sales forces Collaborates with marketing and product teams to align sales efforts with overall company objectives Defines and develops reporting and results analysis tools Participates in the definition of customer strategy and associated sales methods Analyzes market research and results
