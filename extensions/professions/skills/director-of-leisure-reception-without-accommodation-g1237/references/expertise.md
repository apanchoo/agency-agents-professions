# Director of leisure reception without accommodation

## Scope

The leisure center director builds the educational project for welcoming young children, organizes the implementation of activities, supervises the entertainment team and manages the leisure center. Designs and implements leisure activity projects Supervises and trains the entertainment team to guarantee a safe and stimulating environment Ensures the administrative and budgetary management of the structure Ensures the management of human resources and team supervision Develops partnerships with schools, local authorities and other institutions Leads the relationship with families Ensures compliance with safety standards and regulations in force
