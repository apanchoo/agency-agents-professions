# Nose

## Scope

The nose composes aromas and fragrances intended for food, cosmetic (shower gels, body milk, etc.) or perfumery products and carries out chemical and sensory (taste and olfactory) analysis. Intervenes according to manufacturing standards (toxicity, tolerance, etc.) and consumer expectations Can train in tasting and olfaction techniques Can organize sensory evaluation tests for consumer products
