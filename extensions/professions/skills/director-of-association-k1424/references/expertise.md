# Director of association

## Scope

The association director orchestrates the development and management of the organization to maximize its social impact. Defines the strategy and directions of the association in collaboration with the board of directors Supervises the administrative, financial and human management of the association Represents the association with partners, the media and during public events Mobilizes financial resources through grants, donations and partnerships Ensures the monitoring and evaluation of the association's projects and activities Promotes the development of synergies with other organizations to increase the effectiveness of the association
