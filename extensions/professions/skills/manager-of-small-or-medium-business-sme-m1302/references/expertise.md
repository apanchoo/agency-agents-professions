# Manager of Small or Medium Business -SME-

## Scope

The manager of a Small or Medium Enterprise -SME- orchestrates the development and daily management of his business. Directs and coordinates the company's activities to ensure its profitability and development Defines business strategies and supervises their implementation Manages the company's human, financial and material resources Ensures the company's compliance with current regulations Develops and maintains relationships with partners, suppliers and customers Identifies growth opportunities and potential risks for the company
