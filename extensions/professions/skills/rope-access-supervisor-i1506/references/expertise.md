# Rope access supervisor

## Scope

The rope access supervisor supervises work carried out at height or difficult to access: Mandatory on construction sites, he is the on-site guarantor of the safety of workers. Directs and coordinates the activities of his team on site during interventions at height and using ropes Ensures the safety of those involved under his responsibility Controls the conformity of installations, means of protection and equipment used Must know how to supervise and carry out rescue or evacuation operations at height Ensures compliance with legal, regulatory and normative requirements for work at height Can participate in production Participates in the construction, maintenance and inspection of structures at height or with difficult access, such as buildings, bridges, structures in the natural environment, industrial installations, wind turbines

## Related Job Titles

- Rope access site manager

- Rope rope team leader
- Team leader for difficult access works

- Ensure the integration of new recruits (presentation to teams, safety training, etc.)

- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Adapt working methods according to operational needs
- Set up complex moorings independently taking into account the instructions transmitted
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Check the progress of the work
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Apply preventive measures based on site documents
- Ensure a continuous presence on site during the work phase in order to guarantee the safety of those involved

- Ensure that the instructions are properly understood by the teams, in particular by carrying out “safety briefings”
- Ensure that the rescue procedure is functional, adapted to the intervention conditions and ready to be triggered if necessary
- Supervise the assembly and dismantling of access devices
- Optimize resource use to reduce waste
- Ensure the permanent application of preventive measures
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Promote the use of recycled or eco-friendly materials
- Analyze feedback to improve practices

- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Coordinate worker rescue operations in all situations
- Transmit the rules and instructions to your team and ensure their understanding
- Document the procedures and interventions carried out
- Enforce occupational safety and health rules and make each employee responsible
- Managing internal conflicts
- Specify the usable anchors defined in the site documents to stakeholders and ensure that they comply with regulations
