# Director / Editorial Director

## Scope

The Managing Editor leads editorial strategy and oversees content production. Defines the editorial line and ensures its respect within the editorial staff Supervises and coordinates the work of editors and journalists Ensures the quality and consistency of published content Develops strategies to increase audiences and public engagement Collaborates with other departments (marketing, sales) to align editorial objectives with the company's objectives May represent the editorial staff at events or in external media
