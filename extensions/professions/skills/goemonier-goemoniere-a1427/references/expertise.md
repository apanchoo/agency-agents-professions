# Goémonier / Goémonière

## Scope

The seaweed grower, exploiting marine resources, harvests algae intended for processing, in compliance with environmental regulations in order to contribute to the preservation of the marine ecosystem. Harvests algae at sea using specific tools on board a seaweed vessel Sorts and prepares the harvested algae for sale or processing Participates in the management and maintenance of harvesting equipment Can transform the algae into by-products according to market needs

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Communicate with other crew members to coordinate tasks
- Raise awareness of harvesting practices for a new crew
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Use navigation equipment to optimize routes (GPS, mobile applications)
- Develop more efficient harvesting methods
- Determine harvest areas based on seasons and algae growth cycles
- Harvest algae using specific equipment
- Use geolocation tools to identify harvest areas
- Maintain a boat
- Maintain fishing equipment in good condition
- Monitor the condition of the boat during navigation, detect anomalies (damage, etc.) and inform the boatswain or the officer of the watch
- Analyze water quality for seaweed harvest
- Load, unload, handle products
- Manage the stock of harvested seaweed to optimize conservation
- Transport a raw material
- Organize order processing
- Monitor the course of a boat
- Carry out docking or undocking maneuvers for the boat
- Carry out or control vessel maneuvers and fishing operations
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Process weather information to plan sea trips
- Marketing harvested or fished products
- Negotiate commercial terms
- Building a network of contacts for the sale of seaweed
- Optimize the use of maritime resources
- Act quickly in the event of adverse weather conditions
- Comply with maritime regulations
- Respect the harvest quotas imposed by regulations
