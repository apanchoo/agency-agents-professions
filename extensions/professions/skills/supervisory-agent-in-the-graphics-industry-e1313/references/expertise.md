# Supervisory agent in the graphics industry

## Scope

The Graphics Industry Supervisor supervises and coordinates the activities of the production team while respecting deadlines and quality standards, while optimizing work processes. Manages production schedules and delivery times Ensures compliance with safety and environmental standards in the workshop Controls the quality of printing and finishing jobs Optimizes production processes to improve efficiency Trains and supervises teams on new printing technologies and techniques
