# Manager of vehicle technical inspection centers

## Scope

The Vehicle Technical Inspection Center Manager monitors and organizes the work of the teams from one or more technical inspection centers. Manages and manages one or more technical control centers Supervises one or more teams of technical controllers Ensures the maintenance and proper functioning of the center's equipment Develops and builds customer loyalty by monitoring services Guarantees compliance of control operations with current safety standards May participate in training activities on new regulations

## Related Job Titles

- Manager of automotive technical inspection centers
- Manager of heavy goods vehicle technical inspection centers
