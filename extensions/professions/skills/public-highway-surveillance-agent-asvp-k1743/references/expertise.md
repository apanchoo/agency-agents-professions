# Public highway surveillance agent (ASVP)

## Scope

The public highway surveillance officer (ASVP) has two essential missions on public roads, enforcing parking and protecting pedestrians near schools and other public places. Enforces regulations relating to stopping and parking as well as the display of the insurance certificate Observes and reports violations of various codes, starting with the Highway Code Participates in prevention and protection missions on public roads

## Related Job Titles

- Agent of the specialized surveillance of the municipality
- Road parking agent
- Paid parking enforcement agent
- Public road surveillance agent
- School crossing agent

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure mediation between the different local actors in the event of conflict
- Master the computer tool
- Monitor the condition of equipment and report any malfunctions
- Secure pedestrian crossings near schools
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Apprehend the perpetrators in flagrante delicto
- Note the violation of the Insurance Code in the event of failure to affix a valid certificate to a vehicle
- Observe traffic violations relating to stopping or parking vehicles
- Record violations of orders concerning the circulation, stopping and parking of vehicles in station yards
- Request the removal of a vehicle in default of parking
- Prepare and submit reports
- Carry out all observations on the advertising policy, signs and pre-signs
- Search for and note violations of the rules relating to the fight against neighborhood noise
- Identify identities
- Record in writing violations of health regulations relating to roads and the cleanliness of public spaces
- Report an infraction
- Enter computer data relating to fixed fines
- Sanction infractions and assign appropriate penalties
- Monitor the cleanliness of the premises and illegal dumping
- Monitor suspicious activities and report anomalies
- Fines the stopping or parking of a vehicle in the way reserved for pedestrian traffic
- Verbalize an offense
- Help and communicate with users
- Analyze user requests and provide appropriate responses
- Participate in monitoring the smooth running of public events
- Provide assistance to people
- Communicate effectively by radio means
- Raise awareness among road users of road safety rules
- Alert the territorial authority of a risk relating to security and public order on public roads

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Be proactive
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show responsiveness
