# Railway maintenance technician

## Scope

The Railway Maintenance Technician ensures the maintenance, repair and monitoring of train equipment and other materials to guarantee the reliability of the railway network. Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of trains and railway equipment Diagnoses breakdowns and carries out necessary repairs Controls equipment compliance with safety standards Writes detailed maintenance reports Participates in the continuous improvement of maintenance processes Can participate in the installation of new equipment and its commissioning, while respecting safety standards and technical specifications

## Related Job Titles

- Railway equipment maintenance mechanic
- Rail transport maintenance mechanic
- Train maintenance technician
- Railway equipment technician

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate work with other trades
- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Train new technicians in procedures
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Ensure technological monitoring to improve maintenance processes
- Update maintenance log
- Analyze maintenance data
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Use specific software
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Act quickly in the event of a breakdown to minimize interruptions
- Change or repair a defective part or assembly
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Identify faulty components and parts
- Locate the breakdown or failure through checks, tests and measurements
- Plan maintenance interventions according to priorities
- Find the origin of breakdowns and identify repair solutions
- Carry out adjustments to industrial or operating equipment and check its operation
- Carry out a diagnosis based on observed malfunctions
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Control the operation of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Perform regular safety inspections
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Supervise maintenance operations
- Use testing devices (pressure, flow, vibration, etc.) and electrical measuring devices
- Check the operation of signaling equipment
- Organize and plan an activity
- Write a report, an activity report
- Sort products, parts, materials and direct them to recovery or elimination channels
- Respect response and processing times
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
