# Rail network operating agent

## Scope

Responsible for monitoring, operating and maintaining rail infrastructure, the rail network operating agent ensures the fluidity and safety of rail traffic. Carries out regular inspections of tracks, equipment and installations Intervenes quickly in the event of a malfunction or breakdown Regulates traffic to prevent delays and guarantee safety Controls signals and communication systems Coordinates interventions in the event of an incident or breakdown Ensures liaison between the different players in the railway network Ensures compliance with safety rules by all stakeholders

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Communicate with teams in the field to coordinate operations
- Coordinate communications between different units
- Facilitate collaboration between the different actors in the network
- Maintain good physical condition to withstand physical efforts
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Process complex technical information
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Diagnose malfunctions on railway equipment
- Carry out regular inspections of railway tracks, equipment and installations
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Intervene quickly in the event of a malfunction or breakdown on the rail network
- Adjust and control the installations
- Communicate effectively with the team on security protocols
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Follow safety protocols during inspections
- Perform manual switching operations on tracks
- Maneuver the opening and closing of level crossings
- Regulate and supervise the traffic of a transport network
- Monitor and announce the approach of rail traffic to work zones
- Ensure effective communication between drivers and the control center
- Liaise with local authorities
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events while respecting security protocols
- Identify malfunctions in the intervention area during damage or delay, report anomalies
- Comply with current rail safety standards
- Have the ability to work under pressure

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
