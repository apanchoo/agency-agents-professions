# Customs declarant

## Scope

The customs management technician carries out his activity within an RDE (registered customs representative) or with a transport agent or within an industrial or commercial company which has import and/or export activities; a key professional for the regulation of international trade, he carries out customs operations on behalf of industrial or commercial companies and participates in trade facilitation. Ensures the administrative processing of customs clearance operations Implements appropriate customs procedures and regimes Controls customs documents and ensures their compliance with imported or exported goods Prepares customs declarations Determines and calculates the duties and taxes payable Supports customers on customs regulations and procedures Communicates with customs authorities, carriers, customers Provides technical support during customs controls

## Related Job Titles

- Customs declaring agent
- Declarant helper / Customs declarant helper
- Customs declarant assistant
- Customs broker
- Administrative manager / Customs administrative manager
- Taxator

## Activities and Skills

- Check data compliance
- Master the dematerialized transport documentation processes
- Use digital tools and software specific to the field
- Contribute to customs risk management
- Regularly evaluate customs processes to identify possible improvements
- Monitor the delivery of goods, inform the customer about the progress of the service and present solutions in the event of anomalies
- Ensure archiving in accordance with current regulations
- Prepare, monitor or control invoicing for transit or customs operations: supplier payment, customs taxes, Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB)
- Carry out customs formalities
- Analyze customs documents to ensure compliance
- Interact with customs authorities in the event of disputes
- Master the regulations in force related to your sector of activity
- Participate in the management of various audits
- Integrate environmental impact reduction into practices and workflow
- Alert a client to the risks linked to declaration failures
- Inform a customer about the different formalities applicable to the transit of goods
- Guide a customer on special regimes and procedures and regimes appropriate to the goods
- Apply customs clearance procedures
- Provide information to optimize procedures
- Interact regularly with customs administration, carriers, customers, internal services, etc.
- Adapt your communication to your interlocutors
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events in the customs clearance process
- Integrate the monitoring elements at your disposal (regulatory standards) into your activities
- Integrate developments in the digital tools at your disposal for monitoring files

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Price search
- Special diets
- Computerized customs procedures such as Delta, Gamma, ICS2...
- Customs operations processing software
- Price research techniques, nomenclatures

## Standards and processes

- Community Customs Code/Union Customs Codes
- Customs document management
- Regulation of different modes of transport
- Customs regulations for import/export
- European and French customs regulations
- International trade regulations (Incoterms® in force etc.) and free trade agreements

## Professional techniques

- Principles and calculation of the customs declaration (species - origin - value)
- Pricing and taxation techniques for road, sea and air freight
- Status and certification (AEO, approved exporter, registered exporter, etc.)
- Application of regulatory texts

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- International zone
- In an industrial environment
- In the airport area
- In the port area
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity

## Sectors

- Logistics and transportation
