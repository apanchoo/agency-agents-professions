# Gerontologist

## Scope

The gerontologist, an expert in aging, improves the quality of life of the elderly. Assesses the overall needs of older people to propose adapted care plans Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to offer integrated and personalized care Informs and trains health professionals on the specificities of aging Participates in the development of public health policies aimed at promoting the well-being of seniors Ensures the monitoring and evaluation of care programs to guarantee their effectiveness

## Related Job Titles

- Gerontology care assistant
- Gerontology care assistant (ASG) in an institution

## Activities and Skills

- Supporting families in managing aging
- Advise professionals, institutions or communities on issues related to aging
- Train teams to address the issues of aging and support for seniors
- Supporting family and professional caregivers
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Design individualized or collective support projects
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Assess the overall needs of older people in their living environment
- Prevent loss of autonomy through adapted and targeted actions
- Monitor a person's health
- Collaborate with clinical research teams
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Managing emotional crisis situations
- Preventing risky behavior among seniors
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Create a relationship of trust
- Guarantee the confidentiality of information
- Inform about available community resources
- Implement actions to promote aging well
- Participate in public health programs
- Promoting healthy living practices among seniors
- Analyze the social, cultural and environmental impacts of aging
- Analyze the biological, psychological and social processes of aging

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Senior care networks
- Emergency and first aid procedures
- Psychology of aging
- Pathologies of aging
- Assessment of the needs of older people
- Organization of stimulating activities for seniors
- Basic knowledge of pathologies, disability and aging

## Standards and processes

- Raising awareness of the rights of older people

## Professional techniques

- Assistance techniques for the elderly
- Oral, written and digital communication techniques
- Communication techniques adapted to seniors
