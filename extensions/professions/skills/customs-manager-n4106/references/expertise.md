# Customs manager

## Scope

The customs manager carries out his activity within a RDE (Registered Customs Representative), a transport agent, or within an industrial or commercial company which has import and/or export activities; a key player within the international chain of circulation of goods, and at the center of the fluidity of trade, the customs manager supervises all customs formalities for import and export. Manages and optimizes customs processes Guarantees regulatory compliance of declarations and associated documents Advises internally or externally on customs regulations and import/export procedures Implements internal control procedures and guarantees their traceability Conducts customs audits Proposes corrective actions if necessary Analyzes customs risks and handles disputes Ensures and coordinates the maintenance of certifications Supervises the customs service teams and manages the service's human and material resources Analyzes performance of service Implements corrective actions to achieve the objectives set in terms of quality of service, customer satisfaction and profitability

## Related Job Titles

- Head of the customs department
- Customs Advisor
- Customs Coordinator
- Principal declarant
- Customs representative

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise a team of declarants and assist declarants
- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Regularly evaluate the technical skills of the team
- Enforce the implementation of regulatory developments
- Train teams in specific customs procedures
- Maintain team cohesion and motivation
- Supervise and evaluate team performance
- Advising a client on the international movement of goods
- Apply processing to data to analyze and interpret it (with a spreadsheet, processing software, a calculation query in a database, etc.)
- Check data compliance
- Develop the use of digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects, interfaces specific to the customs field)
- Facilitate and support the digitalization of processes and the use of digital tools
- Optimize dematerialized documentation processes
- Assess the risks associated with the transport of specific goods
- Identify non-compliance risks and anticipate their impacts
- Integrate crisis situations (geopolitics, health, etc.) into activity management
- Secure operations
- Coordinate audits, controls and certifications (AEO, etc.)
- Regularly evaluate customs processes to identify possible improvements
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Driving a light vehicle
- Complete administrative formalities with administrations and authorities (prefecture, embassy, ministry)
- Carry out customs formalities
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Check compliance of customs operations invoicing
- Supervise the completion of customs operations
- Guarantee compliance of customs operations with current legislation
- Manage disputes with customs administrations
- Ensure the smooth running of signatures, compliance with legal procedures and regulations
- Advise a client on a customs strategy to consider (special regimes, options to propose)
- Contribute to improving the service and offer
- Contribute to the management of a network of customs correspondents at the shipper or at the RDE (registered customs representative)
- Regularly ensure reporting activity on the operation of the service, on major developments, on customs flow
- Clearly communicate decisions, arbitrations and instructions
- Interact regularly with customs administration, carriers, customers, internal governance
- Represent the group to administrations, experts and partners
- Adapt your communication to your interlocutors
- Implement a monitoring system (monitoring sources and elements, prices, standards, environment, etc.)
- Build and maintain a professional network in the customs sector
- Build your professional project in the transport sector

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Demonstrate leadership
- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility

## Products, tools and materials

- Navigation and geolocation systems (professional GPS, mobile apps)

## Areas of expertise

- Price search
- Import-export transit of goods
- Customs operations processing software
- Special diets
- Computerized customs procedures such as Delta, Gamma, ICS2...
- Price research techniques, nomenclatures

## Standards and processes

- Customs risk analysis
- Customs regulations
- Customs compliance audit
- International trade regulations (Incoterms® in force etc.)
- Pricing and taxation of road, sea or air freight

## Professional techniques

- Principles and calculation of the customs declaration (species - origin - value)
- Community Customs Code, Union Customs Codes (UCC)
- Status and certification (AEO, approved exporter, registered exporter, etc.)

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Working with international organizations or partners
- International zone
- Business travel
- In an industrial environment
- In the airport area
- In the port area
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity

## Sectors

- Logistics and transportation
