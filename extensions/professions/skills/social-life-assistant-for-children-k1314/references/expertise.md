# Social life assistant for children

## Scope

The social life assistant for children is a professional who intervenes to help children who are fragile or disabled to carry out the actions of daily life. Ensures a caring and reassuring presence for children Contributes to the social and emotional development of children Accompanies and helps children in everyday activities Organizes and participates in educational and fun activities Works closely with families to guarantee consistent and appropriate care at home Ensures the hygiene and safety of children

## Related Job Titles

- Family educator
- Family worker / Family worker working with children
- Master / Mistress of children's home

## Activities and Skills

- Liaise with families
- Encourage children's autonomy through simple tasks
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Facilitate activities adapted to children to stimulate their intellectual and social development
- Create and implement conditions necessary for the development, health, safety and well-being and autonomy of young children
- Facilitate the administration of medical care, where appropriate
- Observe the child's behavior and development and identify possible difficulties
- Providing emotional support and encouraging children's expression to help them overcome difficult times
- Ensuring the safety of children and the environment in which they live
- Maintain a clean and secure environment
- Observe rules and protocols established for the well-being and safety of children
- Collaborate with education professionals
- Facilitate communication between the child and his peers
- Write reports on the progress and daily lives of children, and communicate this information with families and other professionals
- Alert of a malfunction or risky situation
- Manage crisis or emergency situations

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show perseverance
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Emergency and first aid procedures
- Knowledge of common allergies in children
- Specific nutritional needs of children
- Childhood pathologies
- Stages of child development
- Knowledge of the signs of child abuse
- Child and adolescent psychology
- Psychology of sensory impaired children
- Relationship with child or adolescent
- Mastery of social assistance systems

## Standards and processes

- Children's rights and protection of minors
- Health and safety standards
- Home Safety Rules

## Professional techniques

- Child development techniques
- Methods of nonverbal communication with children
- Positive reinforcement methods for children
