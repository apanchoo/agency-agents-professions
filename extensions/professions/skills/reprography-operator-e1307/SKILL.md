## Scope

A reprography operator is a professional responsible for reproducing, printing and finishing documents. Carries out all or part of the document reproduction operations (order taking, advice, duplication, shaping), based on indications given by the customer according to security rules and production requirements. Can produce or modify the formatting of documents, package the duplicated products for delivery, establish a quote and carry out collections in payment of orders. Parameterizes and controls the reprographic equipment according to the work to be carried out. Controls the quality of the documents produced and makes the necessary adjustments. Carries out the finishing operations. such as shaping, assembling, binding or laminating

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Check data compliance
- Shape duplicate documents manually or using a machine
- Prepare documents to be reproduced, select printing machines and configure them
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Monitor the operation of the printing machine (paper arrival, jam, printing, etc.)
- Check the conformity of the product to the order
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Complete a transaction
