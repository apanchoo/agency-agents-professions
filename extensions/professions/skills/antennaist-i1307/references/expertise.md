# Antennaist

## Scope

The Antenna Technician installs and adjusts individual or collective antennas or dishes allowing digital signals to be received and transmitted. Installs and orients terrestrial and satellite reception antennas according to safety standards and regulations in force Diagnoses and resolves reception problems Ensures the maintenance and updating of equipment Advises customers on the best reception solutions Can work in varied environments, from individual houses to large buildings

## Related Job Titles

- Parabolic and antenna installer
- Low current installation technician

- Configure or configure telecommunications equipment, low currents
- Update electronic system software to optimize their performance
- Complete the intervention documents
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Prepare materials for work
- Carry out assembly and assembly work
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Analyze the instructions while appreciating the difficulties of the site
- Connect electronic equipment
- Connect a junction box
- Hoist, fix and adjust a cable and its accessories
- Install antennas and satellite dishes on roofs, terraces, balconies or on masts and pylons
- Install the equipment on site and connect it to external networks
- Plan operations based on weather forecasts
- Carry out fixing operations
- Ensure preventive maintenance of reception equipment
- Change or repair a defective part or assembly
- Carry out maintenance work at height or difficult to access
- Repair and work on antennas
- Troubleshoot technical issues
- Check the conformity of the installation on site, help desk, hot line
- Put in place protections to secure the site
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Check the quality and reliability of the installation
- Carry out invoicing
- Write a report, an activity report

- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
