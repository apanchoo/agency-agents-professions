# IT Datacenter Supervision Engineer

## Scope

The IT Datacenter Supervision Engineer is responsible for monitoring, analyzing and managing performance, as well as the availability of the IT infrastructure (physical and virtual networks, machines, etc.) of the Datacenter, he is the guardian of the performance and security of critical infrastructures, where each decision taken ensures the continuity and efficiency of tomorrow's digital services. Uses monitoring and performance management tools to track key metrics of servers, storage systems, networks and applications, and to identify problems and trends Supervises the proper functioning of the Datacenter and coordinates intervention teams May be required to intervene on site to repair/correct failures on this infrastructure (intervention on servers in the racks) Can intervene on both Hardware (hardware) and Software (software)

## Related Job Titles

- IT support engineer
- Network Support Engineer
- IT systems manager

- Allocate and organize project resources according to needs and constraints
- Coordinate the intervention of teams of specialists
- Train users in new technologies
- Train teams of technicians in first-level maintenance operations for connected equipment
- Develop and implement a test plan
- Study and identify developments (functionality, profitability, quality, etc.) of components, products, equipment
- Monitor technological developments in information and telecommunications systems and propose technical solutions
- Direct write access to machines and servers to configure
- Remotely diagnose a computer hardware or software malfunction
- Deploy computer networks
- Develop scripts to automate tasks
- Facilitate access to digital technologies
- Install and commission hardware and software, in compliance with usage standards and electrical safety rules
- Integrate AI/Data Science tools
- Configure business software and interface it with other applications
- Propose solutions to optimize system security
- Provide and update activity monitoring tools (production dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Guarantee the proper functioning, availability and performance of a software solution
- Manage incidents and technical problems
- Locate the breakdown or failure through checks, tests and measurements
- Carry out preventive, systematic or conditional maintenance operations
- Guarantee the proper functioning, availability and performance of network elements or software under your responsibility
- Comply with an information security policy
- Check the quality and reliability of the installation
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Negotiate with IT and security solution providers
- Make the choice of production, processing internally or by subcontracting and check compliance
- Establish disaster recovery plans
- Carry out an internal performance audit of IT equipment
- Manage databases and ensure their integrity
- Analyze customer needs
- Inform and advise customers and users on the use and maintenance of equipment
- Design an indicator and organize its production
- Establish detailed reports on quality performance
- Establish a strategic diagnosis
- Communicate effectively with teams and clients
- Document technical procedures and configurations
