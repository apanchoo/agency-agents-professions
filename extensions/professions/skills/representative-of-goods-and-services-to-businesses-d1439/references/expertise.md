# Representative of goods and services to businesses

## Scope

The business goods and services representative ensures the sale of machines, equipment or services to businesses, while ensuring the commercial development of these products and customer satisfaction. Actively prospects new customers and expands existing portfolio Presents and promotes company products or services to maximize sales Negotiates terms of sale and contracts with business customers Follows up on orders and resolves issues to ensure customer satisfaction Analyzes market and competition to adapt sales strategies Participates in trade shows and network meetings to represent the company

## Related Job Titles

- Consumer goods representative for businesses
- Representative in capital goods for companies
- Representative in intermediate goods and raw materials for companies
- Business Services Representative
- Sales representative
