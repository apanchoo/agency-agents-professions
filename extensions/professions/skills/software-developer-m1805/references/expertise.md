# Software Developer

## Scope

The IT Developer designs and improves computer systems. Designs and develops computer applications according to user needs Participates in the analysis of customer needs and proposes appropriate technical solutions Writes technical documentation and user guides for the applications developed Ensures the maintenance and updating of systems to guarantee their efficiency and security Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to integrate new technologies and improve development processes Performs tests and validations to ensure the quality and performance of applications
