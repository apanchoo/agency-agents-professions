# Administrative secretary / Administrative secretary of local authority

## Scope

The administrative secretary of a local authority ensures the administrative, organizational and regulatory management of a local public structure (town hall, department, region, territorial public establishment). Ensures the processing, monitoring and classification of administrative files Writes letters, reports, orders and administrative acts Organizes meetings, commissions and deliberative bodies (convenings, agendas, minutes) Welcomes and informs users as well as institutional partners Controls the conformity of documents and ensures compliance with regulatory procedures
