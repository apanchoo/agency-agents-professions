# Facilitator-coordinator / Training facilitator-coordinator

## Scope

The Training Facilitator-Coordinator energizes and organizes the training courses. Designs and implements training programs adapted to the needs of learners Conducts face-to-face or remote training sessions Coordinates the activities of trainers and external speakers Evaluates the effectiveness of training and suggests improvements Ensures administrative and educational monitoring of training Develops partnerships with institutions or companies to enrich the training offer

## Related Job Titles

- Training officer in training organization
- Coordinating Trainer / Training Coordinating Trainer

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise teams of trainers
- Strengthen trainers’ project management skills
- Support the continuing professional development of trainers
- Adapt a course or an educational sequence according to the level and progress of the learners
- Ensure liaison between trainers and learners
- Choose learning methods (face-to-face, e-learning, blended learning, etc.) and appropriate teaching tools (digital media, videos, quizzes, serious games, etc.)
- Design training modules
- Design educational supports and tools (teaching materials, educational software, exam subject)
- Coordinate external stakeholders for training
- Correct assessments (exercises, practical work, exams)
- Define a learning objective and the program of educational activities
- Manage training materials
- Identify areas of individual and collective progress for practitioners
- Measuring the impact of a training action
- Optimize available educational resources
- Organize continuing education sessions
- Propose improvements to educational content
- Supervise the trainers' educational program
- Transmit theoretical bases, techniques and gestures by combining several teaching methods
- Ensure the quality and relevance of educational content, in line with academic standards and the needs of the job market
- Check prerequisites, select candidates for training
- Ensure educational monitoring to stay up to date on teaching methods
- Ensure training monitoring and trace it in the educational monitoring tools available
- Control the progress of an establishment training plan
- Evaluate the effectiveness of the teaching methods used
- Implement a training evaluation process
- Carry out an educational assessment of a training course
- Ensure administrative follow-up of trainees (certificates of participation, transmission of information to prefectures, etc.)
- Ensure post-training follow-up of trainees
- Document learner progress and difficulties
- Coordinate future actions with the various stakeholders (trainers, secretariat)
- Establish partnerships with other training institutions to exchange resources
- Be able to work in project mode
- Inform trainers of their intervention schedule
- Monitor schedules: trainee payroll sheets and trainer time tracking
- Facilitate training sessions
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Respect quality standards and regulations in force in training
