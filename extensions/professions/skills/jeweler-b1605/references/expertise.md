# Jeweler

## Scope

The jeweler creates and transforms precious materials to give birth to unique pieces that combine artisanal know-how and artistic sense. Brings an idea or a drawing to life to make a body ornament with your hands Makes jewelry in metal (precious or not), glass, textile, wood, leather or any other material Works the material using various techniques, adapted to the material used while respecting the client's specifications and quality criteria Can transform or repair a piece of jewelry using all appropriate techniques

## Related Job Titles

- Jeweler / Costume jeweler
- Model maker in jewelry, goldsmithing, jewelry
- Jewelry model maker
