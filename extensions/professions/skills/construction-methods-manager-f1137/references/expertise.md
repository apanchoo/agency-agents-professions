# Construction methods manager

## Scope

The construction methods manager formalizes the construction methods and optimizes construction processes and techniques in order to improve the efficiency and quality of projects. Analyzes the needs of the project and proposes suitable implementation methods Establishes detailed plans for the construction phases and determines the needs for equipment, personnel and materials. Coordinates the site teams and ensures that the work follows the defined methods Guarantees compliance with safety and quality standards throughout the project in collaboration with the site manager Proposes innovative solutions to reduce costs and completion times Intervenes in the event of technical difficulties to adjust the methods and resolve the problems encountered Can participate in the training of teams on the new methods and technologies adopted

## Related Job Titles

- Engineer / Construction methods engineer
- Engineer / Works engineer
- Construction methods technician

## Activities and Skills

- Allocate and organize project resources according to needs and constraints
- Coordinate work with other trades
- Implement collaborative work tools and methods
- Optimize material usage to reduce costs
- Ensure ongoing training of teams on new standards or procedures
- Train teams in new working methods
- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Carry out technological monitoring to follow the evolution of technologies and materials
- Inventory construction constraints (regulatory, technical, environmental)
- Carry out technical and financial feasibility studies
- Analyze data from a study or project
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Develop a technical master plan, define equipment needs
- Establish the schedule and establish the methods of execution of the site
- Select PPE adapted to the needs of the site and organize their periodic management and control, taking into account the manufacturers' recommendations
- Adapt working methods to the specificities of the materials
- Carry out room measurements and surface area calculations
- Select materials taking into account their environmental impact
- Analyze the risks linked to the construction site
- Ensure compliance with construction standards
- Control the quality of materials
- Ensure compliance of the work and site with quality standards and participate in the quality audit phase
- Estimate the duration of the work
- Estimate the costs and deadlines for an activity or service
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Receive and study the elements of the technical file (architect's plans, study report, forecast budget)
- Integrate ecological solutions into projects
- Assist the works manager in the response phases to calls for tenders (construction, rehabilitation, development)
- Evaluate the implementation of a project, an action plan
- Implement performance improvement solutions
- Optimize construction costs
- Propose solutions to improve energy efficiency
- Supervise and control the execution of studies, documents and detailed plans entrusted to design offices
- Inform your superiors of the results during the different stages of the study
- Organize coordination meetings with clients and teams
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Relay information
- Report on your activity
- Working in a group, in a network
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Document work procedures
- Maintain construction records up to date
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
