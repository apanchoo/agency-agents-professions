# Plastics engineer

## Scope

The Plasturgist transforms plastic materials into finished or semi-finished products according to the technical specifications of industrial sectors. Performs dimensional and visual conformity checks of products during production Operates on molding, extrusion or thermoforming machines to shape plastics Adjusts equipment by optimizing throughput and carries out first-level maintenance Respects safety standards and environmental protocols in the handling of plastic materials
