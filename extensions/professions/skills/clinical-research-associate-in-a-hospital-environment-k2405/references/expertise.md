# Clinical research associate in a hospital environment

## Scope

The Hospital Clinical Research Associate plays a crucial role in the development of new medical therapies. Coordinates and supervises clinical trials within hospital establishments Liaises between medical teams and sponsors of clinical studies Ensures compliance with research protocols and ethical standards Collects, analyzes and synthesizes data from clinical trials Contributes to the writing of study reports and the publication of results Participates in the training of hospital staff on good clinical practices
