# Household appliance maintenance technician

## Scope

The Household Appliance Maintenance Technician ensures the longevity and proper functioning of domestic equipment. Diagnoses faults and carries out necessary repairs on various household appliances (brown, white) and digital Carries out preventive maintenance operations to avoid malfunctions Advises customers on the optimal use and maintenance of their appliances Updates the software of electronic and digital devices, adjusts settings according to manufacturers' specifications Writes detailed service reports and manages spare parts stocks May install and commission new appliances at customers' premises
