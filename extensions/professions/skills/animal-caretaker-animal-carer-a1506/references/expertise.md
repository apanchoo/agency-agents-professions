# Animal caretaker / Animal carer

## Scope

The animal caregiver shares the animals' daily lives, takes care of their diet and cleans their living space to ensure their well-being. Prepares and distributes meals adapted to each species Cleans and maintains facilities, enclosures, cages or pools Monitors animal behavior and reports any anomalies to the veterinarian Raise public awareness of species protection and animal welfare

## Activities and Skills

- Ensuring the maintenance of the natural environment of animals
- Carry out post-operative monitoring of an animal
- Handle animals
- Implement education or training actions for animals during daily activities
- Feed the animals respecting specific diets
- Prepare and distribute food rations to animals
- Prepare the animal for medical care
- Respect animal welfare
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Identify a sick animal and inform the stakeholders concerned (owner, veterinarian, etc.)
- Observe animal behavior to detect anomalies
- Update a file, a database
- Carry out regular rounds to check the security of the enclosures
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Manage stocks of food and other supplies
- Carry out the transport or transfer of an animal
- Organize and plan an activity
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage waste and recycling in an environmentally friendly way
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Answer specific questions from tourists
- Lead a themed workshop
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Collect information about the animal (problems, behavior, history, current treatments, etc.) from its companion or in the medical file
- Respect safety instructions during activities
- Use digital communication tools
