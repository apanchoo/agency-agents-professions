## Scope

The CSR project manager implements and monitors the company's social responsibility policies. Leads and coordinates projects such as the reduction of CO2 emissions, waste management, or initiatives in favor of diversity and inclusion Monitors the progress of projects and provides reporting to measure the impacts and results obtained Informs and raises awareness among the various stakeholders and company staff on issues related to CSR Communicates on good practices and the company's achievements in terms of CSR

- Raise team awareness of the issues of Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR)
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Lead team meetings
- Communicate effectively with teams and management
- Coordinate multidisciplinary teams
