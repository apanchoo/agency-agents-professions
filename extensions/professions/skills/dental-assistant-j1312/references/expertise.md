# Dental assistant

## Scope

The dental assistant plays an important role in the daily operation of a dental office. Prepares the materials and instruments necessary for interventions Assists the dentist in dental care procedures Manages the reception and comfort of patients before, during and after care Ensures the sterilization and storage of dental equipment Can manage appointments and patient files Contributes to the administrative management of the dental office

## Related Job Titles

- Dental help

## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Give post-procedure advice on oral hygiene
- Seat the patient for consultation or examination
- Receive the medical-technical elements and inform the practitioner and the patient with a view to the intervention
- Prepare the equipment and products according to the practitioner's instructions and the nature of the intervention
- Prepare, install a medical-technical device
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Clean and sterilize dental instruments
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Prepare treatment rooms and equipment necessary for interventions
- Establish elements of an order
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Carry out invoicing
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Greet, orient and provide information to a patient
- Assist the dental practitioner (presentation of instruments) during the procedure
- Create a relationship of trust
- Relay information
- Act quickly in case of dental emergency
- Manage patient records
- Respect safety and hygiene standards
- Follow cleaning and disinfection protocols
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
