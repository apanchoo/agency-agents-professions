# Cultural development officer

## Scope

The Cultural Development Officer promotes and energizes local cultural heritage. Designs and implements cultural projects in collaboration with various local partners Promotes access for all audiences and generates interest in cultural programs Develops local and international professional partnerships and networks Ensures the promotion of cultural events to the media and the public Manages the budget and resources allocated to cultural projects Evaluates the impact and importance of cultural initiatives on the community

## Related Job Titles

- Cultural attaché
- Cultural developer

## Activities and Skills

- Organize cultural workshops
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Design the modalities of the event according to the objectives and the budget, by providing expertise and creativity on the means and tools to be mobilized
- Encourage community participation in projects
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Supervise and manage organizational, technical, human, financial, logistical and communication resources to achieve objectives
- Supervise the programming of classes and artistic activities
- Effectively communicate project objectives
- Design the program for a cultural season
- Contribute to promotional actions in the media and on social networks
- Create, organize or coordinate an event
- Manage relations with media, partners and other departments
- Optimize the visibility of publications on social networks
- Organize cultural events to strengthen social bonds
- Promote local cultural events
- Promote productions to the media and the public
- Design the layout of performance stages (theatre, television sets, cinema)
- Supervise the logistics of cultural events
- Participate in defining the schedule, overall budget and deadlines
- Write, launch and manage a call for projects
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Manage budgets allocated to cultural projects
- Analyze the cultural needs of the community
- Collaborate with institutional partners
- Design and manage a project
- Lead a development project
- Develop partnerships with local actors
- Evaluate the impact of cultural projects on the community
- Evaluate the implementation of a project, an action plan
- Search for financing, investors
- Defend a project in front of a steering committee, collaborators or partners
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Working in a group, in a network
- Network and cooperate with diverse professional profiles
- Develop your professional network in the cultural sector
