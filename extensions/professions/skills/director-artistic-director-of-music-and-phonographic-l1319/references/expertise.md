# Director / Artistic director of music and phonographic publishing

## Scope

The Artistic Director in music and phonographic publishing identifies talents, selects songs or musical projects, and guides artists in their artistic choices (style, arrangements, image, collaborations) during the creation and recording of music. Is responsible for the musical and visual identity, and the creative direction of a signed artist or a project Ensures the artistic coherence of the project, from its signature to its production in connection with marketing and promotion Detects, supports and guides the artist by supporting the creation of the identity and the coherence of the project Coordinates and supervises the creative process with internal and external stakeholders (songwriters, directors, musicians, studios, copyists, etc.) Proposes, chooses artistic collaborations (artists, arrangers, mixers, etc.) and manages production within budget and deadlines Monitors artistic trends and identifies new inspirations and new creative tools

## Related Job Titles

- Responsible for artistic direction of music and phonographic editions

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Guide the artist in his musical creation (choice of repertoire, etc.)
- Design the programming of an artistic project
- Have a great culture and musical knowledge
- Select a project consistent with an artistic policy
- Understand the uses, limits and legal issues of AI/AGI in the processing and generation of content
- Manage copyright and intellectual property
- Identify the authors or composers to sign
- Ensure the artistic coherence of the project
- Manage a project within budget and deadlines
- Manage a project and coordinate the stakeholders necessary for the production of a work (scheduling management, etc.)
- Conduct economic feasibility studies
- Promote a proposal, a project
- Using English in a professional context
- Ensure artistic monitoring
