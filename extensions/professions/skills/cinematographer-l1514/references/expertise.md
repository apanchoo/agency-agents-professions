# Cinematographer

## Scope

The Cinematographer is in charge of the image throughout a film or audiovisual program broadcast live or delayed, on a set, outdoors or in a studio. Ensures the technical management of shooting on film sets or on location Collaborates closely with the director to define the visual style of the production and the cutting Supervises the technical team of camera, lighting and machinery to guarantee the quality of the images Chooses equipment and configurations adapted to the specific needs of each project Can participate in post-production to ensure the consistency of the final image Manages the logistical aspects linked to his field to optimize the progress of filming

## Related Job Titles

- Cinematographer/Chief camera operator
- Cinematographer / Lighting director
- Cinematographer / Video cinematographer
- Video-image designer
- Director / Director of photography
