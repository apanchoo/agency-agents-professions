# Hydrotherapy Care Agent

## Scope

The hydrotherapy care agent is a professional essential to well-being and health in thermal establishments. Welcomes and accompanies spa guests in the various thermal facilities Carry out treatments prescribed by a specialist doctor for a spa visitor or a patient Ensures compliance with hygiene and safety rules specific to thermal environments Informs and advises spa guests on the procedures and benefits of the treatments Ensures the monitoring of spa guests' files and the good maintenance of treatment areas Can participate in promotion and development activities for the spa establishment

## Activities and Skills

- Accompany clients in the thermal facilities
- Build and implement a wellness massage session adapted to the client's needs
- Identify the client's needs and precautions or contraindications to massage
- Master therapeutic massage and joint mobilization techniques
- Perform hydrotherapy treatments by gargling, inhalation, irrigation, spraying, nebulization
- Practice hydrotherapy treatments using sprinkling or bathing techniques
- Practice hydrotherapy treatments using draining, relaxing, firming and stimulating manual techniques
- Perform facial and body treatments
- Prepare appliances, baths, showers
- Prepare the wrap products (algae, mud, strips, etc.) and accessories (blankets, spatulas, etc.)
- Adjust or control equipment, baths or treatment products (fluidity, temperature, etc.)
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Check the safety conditions of thermal equipment
- Control a thermal pool and products related to care (algae, sludge, etc.)
- Define supply needs
- Welcome the person, identify the appropriate care and present the modalities (expected results, duration of treatment)
- Install a person, a client
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Respect health and safety rules at work
