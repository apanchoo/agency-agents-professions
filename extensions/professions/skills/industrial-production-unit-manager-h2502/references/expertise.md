# Industrial production unit manager

## Scope

The Industrial Production Unit Manager organizes, optimizes and supervises manufacturing means and processes in order to ensure the production of goods or products while respecting the requirements of safety, environment, quality, costs, deadlines and quantity. Can manage the entire production process, from procurement to shipping of finished products. Can supervise a team or department and manage the budget.
