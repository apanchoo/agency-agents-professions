# Designer of tourism products

## Scope

The tourist product designer develops tailor-made or à la carte trips relying on his network of local correspondents. Analyzes market trends to anticipate travelers' needs Creates and develops innovative tourism products adapted to customer expectations Collaborates with local service providers to enrich the tourism offer Regularly evaluates the impact and satisfaction of the offers proposed Can specialize in a geographical area Can participate in trade fairs and events to represent and sell tourism products
