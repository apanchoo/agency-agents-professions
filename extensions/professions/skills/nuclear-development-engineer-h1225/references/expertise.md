# Nuclear development engineer

## Scope

The Nuclear Development Engineer designs, develops and optimizes systems and technologies related to nuclear energy. Designs and develops nuclear power plant projects while respecting safety standards Optimizes energy production processes to improve efficiency and reduce costs Conducts studies and simulations to test system reliability Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to integrate new technologies Ensures project compliance with environmental regulations Oversees the maintenance and optimization of existing facilities

## Related Job Titles

- Atomist
- Nuclear engineer (research and development)

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure continuing training of staff on new nuclear standards
- Innovate in the design of more efficient nuclear fuels
- Analyze the operating data of a nuclear installation
- Develop mobile applications for monitoring nuclear installations
- Design control systems for nuclear reactors
- Monitor and regulate energy production equipment requiring authorization to operate a nuclear power plant (SN2, SN3, etc.)
- Monitor environmental emissions from nuclear sites
- Assess the risks linked to radiation and implement measures
- Optimizing nuclear security processes
- Carry out simulations of nuclear accident scenarios
- Using drones to inspect inaccessible structures
- Conduct environmental compliance audits for nuclear facilities
- Contribute to environmental impact studies for nuclear projects
- Optimizing the energy efficiency of nuclear power plants
- Arguing for new nuclear technologies at conferences
- Communicate research results to a non-specialist audience
- Act quickly in the event of a cooling system failure
- Develop emergency protocols for nuclear incidents
- Respect strict safety standards in a nuclear environment
- Comply with international nuclear safety standards
- Process real-time reactor monitoring information
- Develop your skills in modeling nuclear reactions
