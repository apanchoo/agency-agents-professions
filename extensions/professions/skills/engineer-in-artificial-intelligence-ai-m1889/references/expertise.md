# Engineer in Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## Scope

The Artificial Intelligence (AI) Engineer specializes in technological innovation relating to Artificial Intelligence. Transforms complex data into intelligent solutions Designs and optimizes machine learning algorithms to solve specific problems Analyzes customer needs to create tailored AI solutions Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to integrate AI systems into existing applications Performs testing and tuning to optimize the performance of AI models Stays up to date with the latest technological advances in AI to continually improve projects Can manage AI projects, overseeing technical and strategic aspects
