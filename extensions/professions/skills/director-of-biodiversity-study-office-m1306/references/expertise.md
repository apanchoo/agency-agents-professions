# Director of biodiversity study office

## Scope

The director of the biodiversity study office contributes to the preservation of biodiversity, the sustainable management of resources and the transition of territories through the management of the study office dedicated to biodiversity; he exercises hierarchical and strategic responsibility for the design office in charge of guaranteeing the scientific quality, economic viability and environmental impact of the projects carried out; for this, he: Defines the strategy of the design office by setting the scientific and technical orientations, developing the range of services (impact studies, inventories, ecological monitoring, etc.) by understanding and analyzing customer requests Supervises the activities (study, etc.) to guarantee scientific and methodological rigor, the quality of the recommendations and compliance with deadlines and budgets Manages the operation of the establishment and supervises the teams Ensures economic and commercial development
