# General Secretary

## Scope

The Secretary General is a high-level executive who plays a central role in the administrative and strategic management of the organization, holding responsibility for a wide range of functions from coordinating internal activities to managing relationships with external stakeholders. Coordinates the different departments of the company to ensure the coherence of actions Supervises the preparation of strategic files and ensures their monitoring Participates in the definition of strategic orientations and their implementation Ensures liaison between the board of directors and the various stakeholders of the company Ensures compliance with the company's legal and regulatory obligations Can represent the company during official events or important negotiations
