## Scope

The Electrical Winder specializes in the manufacturing, assembly and repair of windings of copper wires or other conductive materials in electrical devices such as motors, transformers and generators. Manufactures electrical coils Repairs and replaces damaged coils in electrical equipment Controls the quality and conformity of the coils produced Assists in the design and development of new electrical coils Manages the inventory of materials necessary for winding Complies with safety standards and environmental regulations
