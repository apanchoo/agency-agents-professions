# Winding machine / Electrical winding machine

## Scope

The Electrical Winder specializes in the manufacturing, assembly and repair of windings of copper wires or other conductive materials in electrical devices such as motors, transformers and generators. Manufactures electrical coils Repairs and replaces damaged coils in electrical equipment Controls the quality and conformity of the coils produced Assists in the design and development of new electrical coils Manages the inventory of materials necessary for winding Complies with safety standards and environmental regulations

## Activities and Skills

- Optimize the workspace
- Binding coil heads to hold cables (hooping)
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Prepare and carry out the winding using a mandrel or template, ensuring that it runs smoothly
- Carry out varnishing and steaming of the coil against corrosion
- Carry out a winding operation
- Select the tools and materials according to the characteristics of the coil
- Process technical information to adjust parameters
- Connect coils (to each other, on terminal board) with wires or bars
- Manufacture or repair an electric coil for integration into a rotating machine (motor, alternator, etc.)
- Reassemble an entire electric motor or device and make adjustments
- Ensure preventive maintenance of winding machines
- Manufacture or repair an electric coil for integration into a static machine (transformer, etc.)
- Perform a technical diagnosis on electric motors or transformers
- Restoring an old electrical winding
- Troubleshoot technical issues
- Check the conformity of the coil (inductance, electrical continuity, static and dynamic balance, etc.)
- Observe safety instructions when using machines
- Communicate technical issues effectively
