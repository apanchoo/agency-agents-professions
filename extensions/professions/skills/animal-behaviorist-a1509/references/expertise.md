# Animal behaviorist

## Scope

The animal behaviorist, professional in the human-animal relationship, evaluates the behavior of animals to improve their well-being and their relationship with their owners. Assesses problematic behaviors in pets Proposes appropriate behavior modification plans Organizes direct work sessions with animals Advises owners on best behavioral practices Writes progress monitoring and evaluation reports May work in collaboration with veterinarians for specific cases

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Advise the owner on diet, exercise, environment, to maintain the health and well-being of the animal
- Advise owners on training methods
- Teaching, developing skills
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Organize collective or individual training sessions for animals and their owners
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Identify the reason for consultation, the degree of urgency and inform the animal owner of the treatment arrangements
- Teach an animal behaviors adapted to different situations
- Implement education or training actions for animals during daily activities
- Organize play and exercise sessions to stimulate the animals
- Propose improvements to the animals' living environment to promote positive behaviors
- Respect animal welfare
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Observe and report behavioral changes
- Observe animal behavior to detect anomalies
- Implement animal behavior modification techniques
- Managing stressful situations in animals
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Collaborate with veterinarians for behavioral care plans
- Intervene in case of aggressive or dangerous behavior
- Collect information about the animal (problems, behavior, history, current treatments, etc.) from its companion or in the medical file
- Build a professional network and collaborate with other animal specialists
