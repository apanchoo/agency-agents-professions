# Biller Secretary

## Scope

The billing secretary plays a key role in administrative management and invoicing within companies. Ensures the entry of invoices and the monitoring of payments Manages customer reminders and ensures compliance with payment deadlines Prepares and verifies the necessary accounting documents Participates in the preparation of periodic financial statements Maintains effective communication with customers for all invoicing questions Organizes and files administrative documents to ensure good management
