# Land surveyor

## Scope

The Land Surveyor is a professional responsible for mapping and managing land properties. Carries out measurements and topographic surveys to determine property boundaries Performs calculations for updating cadastral plans Ensures the conservation and updating of cadastral documents Can participate in land use planning projects Uses advanced technologies such as GPS and GIS for data accuracy

## Related Job Titles

- Land surveyor
- Land Registry Inspector
- Cadastre surveyor technician

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze the specificities of the land for development projects
- Analyze aerial images
- Analyze variability in geospatial measurements
- Analyze the survey data, transcribe them onto a computer, use satellite images to complete and refine them
- Map roads, residential areas, rivers, bodies of water
- Create a technical drawing, plan, or map
- Draw up topographical plans at different scales (mass, division, street body, execution and proofing plans for works, etc.)
- Perform and interpret calculations, geo-reference geographic coordinates
- Maintain technical documents and plans up to date
- Carry out the digital mapping of a cadastral plan and a Local Urban Planning Plan (PLU)
- Write the file of the works executed (compliance of the implementations) and the technical reports
- Draw topographic and hydrographic maps
- Collect data in the field from the existing establishment or the site to be developed
- Carry out and record metric surveys of topographical elements (land leveling, distances, natural reliefs, settlements)
- Inventory the easements relating to land
- Materialize the identification by guaranteeing the required precision, stipulated in the specifications
- Carry out topographical land surveys
- Carry out an interior and facade survey of an existing structure and determine the perimeter and surface area
- Ensure compliance of technical plans
- Check and analyze the anomaly noted
- Check the conformity of the implementation of an achievement
- Pilot a drone for data capture, topographical data, transport of loads, etc.
- Advise on legal and technical aspects of land transactions
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate results to stakeholders
- Manage technical documentation and project archives
- Note all the physical aspects, the specificities of the work and the environment
- Digitizing terrain data for a geographic information system
