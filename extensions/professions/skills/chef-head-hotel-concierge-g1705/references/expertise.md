# Chef / Head hotel concierge

## Scope

The Head Hotel Concierge plays a key role in guaranteeing a service that meets customer expectations as closely as possible. Coordinates and supervises concierge services to meet guest needs Handles special guest requests, such as restaurant reservations or tourist recommendations Liaises with other hotel departments to ensure smooth and efficient service May handle administrative tasks and key management Maintains up-to-date knowledge of local attractions and events to offer relevant advice Ensures guest satisfaction and handles potential complaints

## Related Job Titles

- First hotel concierge

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate hotel services for guests
- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Plan and organize the activity of staff under their responsibility
- Recruit and integrate a person
- Create, organize or coordinate an event
- Update customer information in the computer system
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Track reservations
- Liaise with other departments within the establishment
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Ensure management of room keys and common areas
- Monitor the safety of property and people within the hotel
- Greet customers upon arrival and manage their departure
- Anticipate the needs and expectations of loyal customers
- Communicate with customers for specific requests
- Advise and guide clients in their procedures
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Manage telephone reception
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Carry out shopping at the request of a customer or for the needs of the establishment
- Ensure monitoring of customer satisfaction
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Implement strategies to increase customer satisfaction
- Supervise baggage service, vehicle parking and organize baggage transport
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Respect health and safety rules at work

## Professional Approach
