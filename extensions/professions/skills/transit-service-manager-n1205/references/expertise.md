# Transit service manager

## Scope

The Transit Service Manager manages and coordinates international transport and logistics operations, supervises all stages of transit while being responsible for optimizing transport costs and times. Coordinates and optimizes transit operations to guarantee efficient and secure delivery Supervises the teams and manages the human and material resources of the service Negotiates and manages contracts with suppliers and logistics partners for all services Ensures compliance of transit operations with national and international regulations Analyzes the performance of the service and implements corrective actions if necessary Optimizes the activities of its service / department to achieve the objectives set in terms of quality of service, customer satisfaction and profitability

## Related Job Titles

- Head of transit group
- Head of transit department
- International transit service manager

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise a team of transit agents
- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Regularly evaluate the technical skills of the team
- Maintain team cohesion and motivation
- Supervise and evaluate team performance
- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Apply processing to data to analyze and interpret it (with a spreadsheet, processing software, a calculation query in a database, etc.)
- Check data compliance
- Optimize dematerialized transport documentation processes
- Anticipate alternative plans to respond in the event of hazards or emergencies
- Assess the risks associated with the transport of specific goods
- Integrate crisis situations (geopolitics, health, etc.) into activity management
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Organize the national and international transport chain
- Organize logistics activities taking into account available resources, budget and regulations
- Design a multimodal transport plan in an international context
- Monitor and control transport operations in an international context
- Control the monitoring of progress, cash advances and invoicing in connection with customs operations
- Check documents related to the international movement of goods (customs invoice, documentary credit, etc.)
- Prepare, monitor or control invoicing for transit or customs operations: supplier payment, customs taxes, Declaration of Exchange of Goods (DEB)
- Optimize transport and customs costs
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Establish and enforce a framework integrating regulatory and environmental developments
- Implement ecological solutions in logistics
- Integrate the reduction of environmental impact into practices and the progress of transit operations
- Calculate the margins, profitability of a product or activity
- Contribute to establishing the service offering
- Contribute to establishing pricing conditions
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Raise customer needs internally to adapt the offer to emerging issues
- Negotiate freight purchase rates and associated services
- Coordinate with other departments to optimize logistics processes
- Establish strategic partnerships
- Structuring and improving operating methods with agents and correspondents within the network
- Clearly communicate decisions, arbitrations and instructions
- Interact on a daily basis with the network of agents or correspondents
- Master business English in an international context
- Managing emergencies and unforeseen events in transit
- Integrate digital tools for file tracking
- Implement a monitoring system (sources and elements of price monitoring, standards, environment, etc.)

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service

- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility
