# High mountain guide

## Scope

A summit specialist, the High Mountain Guide accompanies and supervises clients during mountain excursions, particularly in alpine, glacial or technical environments. Organizes and plans ascents and descents according to weather conditions Proposes itineraries adapted to clients' abilities Teaches progression, belaying and survival techniques in the mountains Ensures compliance with safety and equipment standards Intervenes in the event of an accident or emergency situation Informs, advises and raises awareness about equipment, mountain ethics and environmental protection
