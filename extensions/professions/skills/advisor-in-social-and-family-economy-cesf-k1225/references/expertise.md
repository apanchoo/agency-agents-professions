# Advisor in social and family economy (CESF)

## Scope

The social and family economy advisor is a qualified social worker whose core business is based on expertise in the areas of daily life (housing, consumption, social and professional integration, as well as in the field of food and health). Assesses the social and financial situation of individuals and families Proposes adapted solutions to improve their living conditions Supports the implementation of their life projects Organizes workshops and training on education, health, parenting, budget management Collaborates with other professionals in the social sector to offer both personalized and global support Refers people to the appropriate services in the event of a specific need

## Related Job Titles

- Social advisor
