# Pet store salesperson

## Scope

The pet store salesperson, a professional passionate about animal welfare. He advises clients on the choice and care of animals. Sells animal products and accessories Ensures the well-being of animals in the store Manages stocks and orders for animal products Maintains the cleanliness of facilities Can participate in training on animal products and care

## Related Job Titles

- Aquarium consultant salesperson
- Animal seller
- Animal feed salesperson
- Pet store salesperson
- Animal product salesperson
- Animal seller

## Activities and Skills

- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Collaborate with suppliers for product quality
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Follow up on orders with suppliers
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Carry out the shelving
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Organize order processing
- Track orders, invoicing
- Proceed to cashout
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Inform the client about the breeds and lifestyle of animals
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Make product returns to distributors or suppliers
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Organize animal product demonstrations
- Present and promote a product or service

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be proactive
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
