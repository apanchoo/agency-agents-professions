# Cardiologist

## Scope

The cardiologist is the expert on the heart and blood vessels, he ensures the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases. Diagnoses and treats cardiovascular diseases Performs specific tests such as echocardiography or electrocardiogram Prescribes drug treatments or lifestyle modifications to prevent heart disease Intervenes urgently for cases of heart attacks or other cardiovascular complications Collaborates with other specialists for multidisciplinary patient care

## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Analyze complex medical images (MRI, scanner)
- Ensure medical follow-up of a patient
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Diagnose cardiovascular diseases based on clinical examination and additional examinations
- Evaluate the effectiveness of post-operative treatments
- Managing medical emergencies
- Identify the patient's needs and exchange information (current treatments, medical history, x-rays, etc.)
- Interpret electrocardiograms (ECG) and other functional explorations
- Modify treatments based on changing patient needs
- Perform medical expertise
- Carry out Electrocardiography (ECG) examinations using radiographic techniques
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out stress tests and interpret the results
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Raise awareness of good hygiene and health practices
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Conduct study and research work
- Update the patient's medical record
- Advise patients on the prevention of cardiovascular diseases
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage medical databases
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data
- Carry out documentary monitoring
- Regularly update your knowledge
