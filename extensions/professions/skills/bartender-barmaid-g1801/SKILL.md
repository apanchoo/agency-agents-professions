## Scope

The Bartender, creator of atmosphere and flavors, is at the heart of the entertainment of a bar. Welcomes, advises and builds customer loyalty by offering a personalized and attentive service Prepares and serves drinks, particularly cocktails, respecting recipes and hygiene standards Maintains the bar and its working environment to ensure a pleasant and clean atmosphere Manages the supply and stock of drinks to guarantee a varied and quality offering Can organize events and activities to energize the establishment and attract a diverse clientele Ensures the proper application of safety rules and legal standards in force in the sector

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Define supply needs
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Proceed to cashout
- Apply current regulations for bars and restaurants
- Create a welcoming and friendly atmosphere at the bar
- Maintain impeccable hygiene of the bar and equipment
- Participate in the organization of activities or events within the bar
