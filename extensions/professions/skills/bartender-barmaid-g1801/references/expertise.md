# Bartender / Barmaid

## Scope

The Bartender, creator of atmosphere and flavors, is at the heart of the entertainment of a bar. Welcomes, advises and builds customer loyalty by offering a personalized and attentive service Prepares and serves drinks, particularly cocktails, respecting recipes and hygiene standards Maintains the bar and its working environment to ensure a pleasant and clean atmosphere Manages the supply and stock of drinks to guarantee a varied and quality offering Can organize events and activities to energize the establishment and attract a diverse clientele Ensures the proper application of safety rules and legal standards in force in the sector

## Related Job Titles

- Bartender / Bartender expert in cocktails
- Bartender / Mixologist barmaid
- Bar clerk

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in the organization of activities or events within the bar
- Innovate with new mixology techniques
- Prepare the products necessary for the service
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Take an inventory of products or materials available in reserve
- Define supply needs
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Proceed to cashout
- Carry out invoicing
- Optimize resource use to reduce waste
- Apply current regulations for bars and restaurants
- Handle complaints professionally
- Apply eco-gestures in your practice
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Ensure the safety of property and people in the establishment
- Greet the customer and install them
- Advise the customer on their choice of drinks or dishes according to their tastes
- Create a welcoming and friendly atmosphere at the bar
- Inform the customer about the composition of the products, allergens, origin
- Carry out room service
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Express yourself with politeness and courtesy
- Maintain impeccable hygiene of the bar and equipment
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Respect safety rules
- Respect the establishment’s quality standards
