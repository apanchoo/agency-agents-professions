# Industrial security manager

## Scope

The industrial safety manager is responsible for risk prevention and the protection of people, the environment and installations in industrial environments. Assess potential risks and develop plans to mitigate them Design and implement preventive measures Apply safety and environmental regulations Raise awareness among managers and their teams about industrial safety issues

## Related Job Titles

- Industrial quality control engineer
- Manufacturing quality control engineer
- Industrial Quality Reliability Engineer
- Industrial quality methods engineer
- Industrial quality engineer
- Quality engineer / Quality engineer in industrial quality management
