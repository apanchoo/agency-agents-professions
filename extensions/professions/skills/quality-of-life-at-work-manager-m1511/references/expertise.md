# Quality of Life at Work Manager

## Scope

The Quality of Life at Work Manager ensures the continuous improvement of working conditions and the well-being of employees in line with the overall performance of the company. Evaluates and analyzes working conditions within the company Proposes and implements actions aimed at improving the quality of life at work Collaborates with the various departments to integrate quality of life at work into managerial practices Organizes awareness and training campaigns on well-being at work themes Monitors well-being indicators and suggests adjustments based on employee feedback
