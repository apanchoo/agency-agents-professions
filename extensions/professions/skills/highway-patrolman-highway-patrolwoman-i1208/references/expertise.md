# Highway patrolman / Highway patrolwoman

## Scope

The Highway Patroller ensures infrastructure maintenance and intervenes in the event of an incident to guarantee the safety and fluidity of traffic on the highways. Monitors and regulates motorway traffic by carrying out regular patrols and using signaling equipment Responds in the event of an incident (accident, breakdown, obstacles, etc.) to secure the area Communicates with emergency teams and traffic management services to coordinate the necessary interventions Performs maintenance of roads and rest areas Produces activity reports to document incidents and the measures taken Ensures first level maintenance of patrol and signaling equipment

## Related Job Titles

- Highway maintenance agent
- Highway worker

## Activities and Skills

- Train new patrol officers in safety and intervention procedures
- Apply emergency procedures in the event of an accident
- Install and maintain outdoor furniture or signage
- Carry out regular patrols
- Carry out the upkeep and maintenance of parking spaces and their equipment (toilets, trash cans, etc.)
- Manage incidents and technical problems
- Clean the roadsides and carry out routine maintenance work on the roadway and road equipment
- Plan pavement repair operations
- Assess the situation, the risks and determine assistance and rescue actions for people
- Evaluate security equipment needs
- Install security or signaling elements
- Secure the end-of-life vehicle by identifying the different types of risks
- Identify and report anomalies concerning roads, public lighting, faulty signs
- Monitor the condition of the road layout and its equipment and identify anomalies and risks
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Drive specialized vehicles to access intervention sites
- Evacuate people, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Inform and assist users of the road network in the event of breakdowns and accidents
- Communicate effectively with teams and the public
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Raise awareness among road users of road safety rules
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Coordinate emergency response
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Respect road safety rules
- Process traffic information in real time
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
