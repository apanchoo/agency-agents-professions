# Sales advisor / Sales advisor of leisure vehicles

## Scope

The Leisure Vehicle Sales Advisor supports and guides customers in choosing the vehicle that meets their needs. Welcomes and advises customers on the different models of leisure vehicles Offers financing solutions adapted to customer needs and ensures commercial follow-up Organizes and carries out vehicle demonstrations Negotiates conditions of sale and concludes contracts Follows customer files from order to delivery Can manage the organization of promotional events related to leisure vehicles
