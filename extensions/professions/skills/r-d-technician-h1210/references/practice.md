# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Define the measurand, range and expected decision.
2. Choose measurement chain, calibration and protocol.
3. Process observations and establish the uncertainty budget.
4. Compare to acceptance criterion and write report.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Chemical balances – Laboratory safety
- Metrology and quality 2
- Environmental measurement techniques
- Regulation and use of phytosanitary products
- Introduction to waste management
- Hygiene Quality Safety Environment
- Process design 2: process diagram
- Experiment plans
- Process modeling and simulation 1

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Draw and design an element necessary for a measurement campaign using specific software (CAD/CAD)
- Carry out chemical analyzes (acids-bases, complexation, precipitation) by applying good laboratory practices (GLP)
- Organize and implement an animal experimentation procedure within the framework of current regulations
- Cultivate cells in compliance with good laboratory practices
- Choice and implementation of simple physicochemical analyzes in order to characterize a product.
- Extrapolation of a process from the laboratory to the pilot scale (Part 2)

## Deliverables

- Metrological protocol
- Uncertainty calculation
- Test report with data

## Acceptance Criteria

- Units, standards and ambient conditions are plotted
- Outliers are not removed without justification
- Uncertainty is taken into account in the decision

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
