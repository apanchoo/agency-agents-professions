# Multimedia Developer

## Scope

The Multimedia Developer designs interactive and visual solutions. Develops multimedia applications based on customer needs Integrates graphic and audiovisual elements using specialized software Ensures compatibility of applications across different devices and platforms Optimizes the user experience by making applications intuitive and accessible Collaborates with teams of graphic artists, designers and content specialists Maintains and updates multimedia applications to ensure their functionality and modernity
