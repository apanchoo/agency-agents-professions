# Manufacturing control biologist in industry

## Scope

The industrial laboratory biologist is in charge of the control and quality assurance of industrial processes. Carries out controls and analyzes to guarantee the quality of products during manufacturing Ensures compliance of processes with regulatory standards for quality, safety and environment Documents results and writes detailed reports Collaborates with production teams to optimize procedures Scientific and technological monitoring of developments in biology and industrial production

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Train staff in new analysis methods
- Collect, test, analyze samples, materials
- Design experiments to test specific hypotheses
- Make samples of new products, record reactions and modify compositions
- Carry out technical or technological monitoring to anticipate developments
- Receive samples, or take samples on construction sites or production sites, strictly respecting operating procedures and protocols
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Check the conformity of an analysis result
- Interpret analysis results for industrial applications
- Design analysis protocols
- Organize the samples and analyzes to be carried out according to the construction site schedule or the production site schedule
- Prepare and check the conformity of products and measurement and analysis devices
- Ensure regular calibration of instruments
- Assess the risks associated with handling chemical substances
- Implement strict safety standards in the laboratory
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Ensure traceability of analyzed samples
- Document procedures and analysis results for audits
- Supervise quality control operations in the laboratory
- Write clear and detailed expert reports
- Ensure compliance with technical and environmental regulations
- Collaborate with teams to optimize processes
