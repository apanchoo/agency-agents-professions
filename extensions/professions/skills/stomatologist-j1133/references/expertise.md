# Stomatologist

## Scope

The Stomatologist is an oral health expert who diagnoses and treats diseases of the mouth and maxillofacial sphere. Performs surgical interventions to treat pathologies such as cysts, tumors or dental anomalies Prescribes medications for the treatment of dental conditions and ensures post-operative follow-up Collaborates with other health specialists for the overall care of patients Advises patients on good oral hygiene practices to prevent diseases Conducts research to improve treatment techniques and knowledge in the field of dentistry
