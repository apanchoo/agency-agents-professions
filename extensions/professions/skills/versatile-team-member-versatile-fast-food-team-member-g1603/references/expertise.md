# Versatile team member / Versatile fast food team member

## Scope

The versatile fast food team member works mainly in fast food restaurants, guaranteeing fast and quality service. Ensures the preparation and sale of food products according to hygiene standards Participates in inventories and stock monitoring Maintains the cleanliness of equipment and the workplace Can make deliveries within promised deadlines Provides prompt and courteous customer service Participates in the daily activity of the restaurant or point of sale
