# Solidarity Project Coordinator

## Scope

The solidarity project coordinator designs, organizes, implements and evaluates solidarity, social inclusion or development actions in response to identified needs. Ensures the planning of activities Coordinates the teams involved Manages relations with partners Monitors the impact of actions carried out Guarantees compliance of projects with regulatory and financial frameworks Writes monitoring reports Contributes to the mobilization of the resources necessary to finance activities

## Related Job Titles

- Solidarity mission manager
- International solidarity project manager
- Coordinator / Coordinator of humanitarian projects

## Activities and Skills

- Develop and manage a network of partners and service providers
- Managing a multicultural team
- Coordinate with professionals and develop the appropriate network of stakeholders
- Use project management software
- Establish the chronology of the work phases
- Update management processes, methods and tools
- Write clear and detailed expert reports
- Use project management tools for monitoring
- Carry out international expert missions
- Establish a response to a call for tenders
- Design and manage a project
- Establish strategic partnerships
- Optimize project management processes
- Organize the progress of the project and structure the different phases
- Manage a project (phases and team)
- Search for financing, investors
- Encourage participation in collective projects
- Connect the different actors of a project
- Raise awareness of a humanitarian cause and collect donations
- Network and cooperate with diverse professional profiles
- Incorporating agility principles into project management
- Participate in a humanitarian action or mission
- Comply with regulatory requirements related to your area of expertise
- Regularly update your knowledge
