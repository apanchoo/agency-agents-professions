# Maritime transport operations manager

## Scope

The maritime transport operations manager orchestrates from an administrative point of view the management of ships, transport operations and the management of maritime flows. Coordinates the activities of the various port services to optimize port calls Ensures compliance with maritime and security regulations Manages relationships with customers and partners for maritime freight Develops strategies to improve the profitability and efficiency of port operations

## Related Job Titles

- Chief / Maritime line manager
- Army port delivery platoon leader
- Director of maritime line
- Ferry station manager
- Container terminal operations manager
- Port terminal operations manager
- Ship operations manager

- Liaise between shipping agents and port operators
- Optimizing the use of energy resources in maritime operations
- Develop continuing education plans for personnel involved in maritime transport
- Manage human resources
- Check data compliance
- Coordinate the circuit of information on the functioning of a structure
- Organize maintenance operations for marina facilities
- Plan preventive maintenance of maritime equipment to avoid breakdowns
- Carry out safety audits for maritime equipment and infrastructure
- Assess the risks associated with different maritime routes and take appropriate measures
- Train staff in maritime safety procedures
- Organize awareness campaigns on maritime safety among staff
- Analyze operational data to identify areas for improvement
- Controlling a transport operation
- Coordinate port activities to optimize goods flows
- Organize ship-handling operations
- Organize ship refueling operations (fuel, food, equipment, etc.)
- Organize port handling operations
- Organize and plan the allocation of port or maritime equipment (gantry cranes, ships, etc.)
- Organize the national and international transport chain
- Plan or coordinate the activity of port teams or sedentary maritime personnel
- Control the availability of ships and port equipment
- Organize passenger boarding or disembarkation operations
- Edit a statistical activity monitoring report
- Establish performance reports for maritime operations
- Carry out administrative management of personnel
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Design a contract proposal
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Manage customer complaints in the event of disputes related to maritime transport
- Negotiate ocean freight contracts with customers and suppliers
- Negotiate a contract
- Ensure compliance with maritime safety standards during port operations
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Collaborate with environmental authorities to ensure the protection of marine ecosystems
- Monitor weather conditions to anticipate impacts on maritime operations
- Conduct market research to explore new opportunities in shipping
- Develop a commercial strategy
- Determine changes in the organization and operation of a structure (productivity, profitability, reliability of operations) and monitor their implementation
- Develop strategic partnerships with other companies in the maritime sector
- Manage emergencies and incidents at the port
