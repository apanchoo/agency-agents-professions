# Veterinary assistant

## Scope

The veterinary assistant, in direct contact with animals and their owners, assists and supports the veterinarian within the office or clinic. Manages appointments, medical records and ensures stock management of medications and equipment Welcomes and advises clients on basic care, preventive care and animal welfare Prepares animals, instruments and equipment necessary for interventions Assists the veterinarian during consultations, treatments and surgical interventions Cleans and disinfects equipment and cages May perform basic care and post-operative follow-ups under the supervision of the veterinarian

- Veterinary assistant

- Qualified Veterinary Assistant (AVQ)

## Activities and Skills

- Welcoming and reassuring those welcomed
- Advise the owner on diet, exercise, environment, to maintain the health and well-being of the animal
- Administer veterinary products
- Assist the veterinarian during examinations and surgical interventions
- Carry out post-operative monitoring of an animal
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Identify the reason for consultation, the degree of urgency and inform the animal owner of the treatment arrangements
- Handle animals
- Prepare the equipment and provide technical assistance to the veterinarian, the breeder, etc.
- Preparing an animal for surgery
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Sterilize and prepare surgical instruments
- Select the equipment and products and prepare the animal for grooming by disinfection, shower, etc.
- Update a file, a database
- Complete the animal's follow-up file and offer further care
- Use specific software
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Clean and disinfect equipment after each use
- Manage veterinary drug stocks
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Arrange scheduled appointments and veterinary emergencies
- Schedule veterinary check-up visits for animals
- Proceed to cashout
- Carry out invoicing
- Dispose of sensitive waste
- Greet clients and their animals at the clinic
- Communicate effectively with veterinarians
- Explain procedures and answer questions from pet owners
- Prepare accounting documents
- Collect information about the animal (problems, behavior, history, current treatments, etc.) from its companion or in the medical file
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
