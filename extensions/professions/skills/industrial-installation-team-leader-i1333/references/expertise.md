# Industrial installation team leader

## Scope

The Industrial Installation Team Leader coordinates the installation and proper operation of industrial equipment. Supervises and coordinates the installation of industrial machines and equipment Ensures training and monitoring of operators on new installations Optimizes processes to improve efficiency and reduce costs Controls the quality and conformity of installed installations Manages preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Can intervene in the event of a breakdown to quickly restore optimal operation

## Related Job Titles

- Installation supervisor, industrial maintenance
- Chief Mechanic / Chief Industrial Maintenance Mechanic
- Foreman / Forewoman in industrial installation

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Encourage continuous improvement in practices
- Distribute the workload and tasks to be performed among team members
- Supervise and evaluate team performance
- Promote and share good practices
- Ensure continuing training of the team on new technologies
- Train new employees on installation procedures
- Follow technological developments in the sector
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Define the location of industrial or operating equipment
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Optimize an industrial process
- Commission the network and check the installation
- Analyze installation plans and suggest improvements
- Ensure compliance of equipment with current standards
- Ensure monitoring of interventions carried out by equipment suppliers or maintenance providers
- Control the quality of the installations carried out
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Plan maintenance interventions according to priorities
- Carry out adjustments to industrial or operating equipment and check its operation
- Carry out an operating diagnosis and check machines, installations and equipment
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Supervise the installation of industrial machines
- Monitor equipment performance indicators
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Manage a schedule
- Organize and plan an activity
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Manage maintenance contracts
- Optimize the energy consumption of systems
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Implement environmental policies in the workshop
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Propose solutions to increase productivity
- Liaise with other departments to coordinate installation projects
- Communicate, read and write technical documents, reports, notes in English
- Conduct team meetings to motivate and inform
- Encourage collaboration between team members
- Maintain effective communication with management
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage on-site emergencies and incidents
- Promote the use of digital technologies to improve processes
