# River technician

## Scope

The river technician ensures the management and preservation of watercourses and their ecosystems. Monitors and maintains watercourses to preserve biodiversity and water quality Conducts studies to assess the state of aquatic ecosystems Participates in the design and implementation of restoration and development projects for aquatic environments Collaborates with local stakeholders and institutions for the sustainable management of water resources Raises public and professional awareness of environmental issues related to watercourses Writes technical reports and monitors environmental data
