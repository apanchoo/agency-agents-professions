# Neighborhood house manager

## Scope

The Neighborhood Center Manager coordinates socio-cultural activities, promotes social cohesion and manages inclusive and community projects, particularly in favor of professional integration. Coordinates community activities and projects within the neighborhood Manages the human and material resources of the neighborhood center Develops partnerships with local stakeholders to support neighborhood initiatives Ensures mediation and conflict management within the community Organizes events and workshops to promote integration and social cohesion Supervises the administrative and financial management of the structure
