## Scope

The manager of a protected natural site is responsible for the management of a protected area (park, nature reserve, etc.) on land or sea in order to ensure the preservation and enhancement of the natural heritage while ensuring, where applicable, its accessibility and enhancement. Develops management documents and the action program for a protected natural area on the different axes (works, education, conservation, monitoring, development) in line with ecological, regulatory and territorial issues. Coordinates and supervises its implementation and evaluates the management documents to ensure the conservation, restoration and sustainable development of the protected natural area. Coordinates the implementation of management documents Coordinates monitoring activities in connection with applicable environmental regulations Coordinates the development and implementation of conservation programs Coordinates the implementation of the public reception, education and awareness program Coordinates collaboration with local stakeholders to promote environmental protection and ensure the territorial anchoring of the protected site
