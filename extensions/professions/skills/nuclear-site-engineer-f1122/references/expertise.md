# Nuclear site engineer

## Scope

The Nuclear Site Engineer designs the structures that house the nuclear installations. Supervises and coordinates activities on site in compliance with nuclear safety standards Ensures compliance of work with technical plans and current regulations Manages site logistics to ensure efficient progress of work Collaborates with engineering teams to resolve technical issues and optimize processes Performs regular inspections to ensure the quality and safety of installations Communicates with stakeholders to maintain transparency on project progress
