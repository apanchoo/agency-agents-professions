# Bus driver

## Scope

The Bus Driver ensures safe and comfortable transportation of passengers across the city and intercity areas. Drives buses on predefined routes while respecting established schedules Ensures the safety of passengers during the journey Checks the technical condition of the vehicle before departure and reports any anomalies Sells and controls transport tickets Informs passengers about routes and stops Manages emergency situations and difficult passenger behavior Welcomes passengers Communicates with traffic regulations

- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Manage busy periods calmly

- Driving a road vehicle

- Transport passengers
- Proceed to cashout
- Use electronic ticketing systems and manage transactions
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Regulate the flow of passengers at entry and exit
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Provide quality service
- Inform passengers in the event of a deviation from the route
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Communicate with regulators to know the traffic situation
- Communicate with the center in the event of technical problems
- Communicate journey information effectively
- Manage passenger interactions professionally
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Document delays and the causes of these delays
- Encourage compliance with the rules of conduct on board
- Managing a conflict situation
- Comply with local transportation regulations
- Report any anomaly or incident during the journey
- Follow predefined routes while remaining flexible to changes
- Participate in continuing training to improve your skills
