# Civil engineer

## Scope

Supervises the development, construction and maintenance of infrastructure. Takes into account plans, drawings and technical specifications of projects. Ensures compliance of projects with current standards. Manages planning, cost estimation, coordination of work teams, resources, budgets and deadlines.

## Related Job Titles

- Civil engineering design engineer
- Civil engineering industrial building engineer
- Civil engineering engineer in works of art
- Civil engineer in hydraulic works
- Civil engineering works engineer
- VRD Engineer
- Industrial and mining engineer (IIM)
- Mining engineer

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate project teams to achieve objectives
- Manage succession and synchronization of companies on site
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Plan the optimal intervention of the different companies (fastest and least expensive construction possible)
- Assist the project owner
- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Coordinate studies with other technical design offices
- Inventory construction constraints (regulatory, technical, environmental)
- Create the file of works executed (DOE) necessary for their operation
- Establish detailed plans (section, elevation) of the project
- Develop a technical master plan, define equipment needs
- Participate in the design and implementation of building information modeling (BIM)
- Prepare the work execution file (technical processes, methods, plans, etc.)
- Ensure that the execution documents as well as the works carried out comply with the provisions of the studies carried out
- Design an architectural or development project
- Analyze basic tasks relating to execution studies and works
- Ensure the follow-up of reservations made upon receipt of the work until their lifting
- Determine the choice and nature of materials as well as the implementation conditions
- Monitor the work (facilitation of weekly monitoring meetings, writing of reports, quality management, cost, deadline, planning, hazard management, contradictory findings)
- Establish the forecast schedule for execution of work by batch or trade
- Bring structures into compliance with user protection systems
- Organize and conduct work, a construction site
- Organize operations prior to receipt of work
- Carry out diagnostics on the state of deterioration of old structures and develop maintenance or compliance recommendations
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Permanently respect established safety rules
- Control the quality and conformity of a building, an infrastructure
- Carry out technical consistency of the documents provided
- Proceed to examine any problems reported by the project owner during the perfect completion guarantee period
- Write the analysis report and propose the classification, highlighting the recommended solution
- Analyze the offers received and possible variations
- Determine the overall completion time of the work
- Estimate the duration of the work
- Establish a Business Consultation File (DCE)
- Examine and rate technical offers obtained as part of a call for tenders
- Launch and manage a call for tenders
- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Establish an overall forecast cost
- Check the monthly and final statement projects, establish the advance payment statements and the general statement
- Analyze the technical needs of a project
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Stop the estimated cost of work
- Negotiate technical or financial solutions with a client
- Ensure monitoring of specifications for each project
- Size the project
- Manage and analyze soil studies to determine the operating procedures necessary for costing
- Manage the performance and profitability of an activity or project
- Supervise and control the execution of studies, documents and detailed plans entrusted to design offices
- Develop team spirit
- Maintain clear and professional communication
- Enforce the rules of hygiene, safety, accessibility and environmental protection
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Structuring, synthesizing information
