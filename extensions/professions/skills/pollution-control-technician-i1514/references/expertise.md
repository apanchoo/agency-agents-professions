# Pollution Control Technician

## Scope

The Depollution Technician Identifies contaminated areas to determine the appropriate decontamination methods and techniques. Monitors air and water quality to ensure compliance with current environmental standards Manages and maintains equipment used for depollution, soil cleaning and groundwater Writes detailed reports on depollution procedures and the results obtained Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to develop innovative depollution solutions

## Related Job Titles

- Operator in pyrotechnic depollution
- Nuclear, Radiological, Bacteriological, Chemical (NRBC) depollution technician

## Activities and Skills

- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Dismantle contaminated equipment or materials
- Carry out the logistical preparation of intervention materials and equipment
- Use and adjust machines or equipment for combining liquid or chemical elements
- Carry out the assembly and installation of collective protection (screen, airlock, wall, etc.)
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Check a sensitive area before intervening
- Define and implement security protocols
- Demarcate a security perimeter
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Limit the risks of spreading pollution or accidents
- Record measurements and detect violations or exceeding of contamination, toxicity or pollution thresholds
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Define and deploy a sustainable development policy
- Dispose of sensitive waste
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Carry out an environmental diagnosis
- Recover and recondition polluting agents and contaminated materials
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Raise public awareness
- Managing an emergency situation
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
