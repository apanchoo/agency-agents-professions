# Quality control project manager

## Scope

The quality control project manager develops and implements quality strategies to achieve company objectives in customer satisfaction, regulatory compliance and operational performance. Works closely with teams involved in the project to define requirements, quality standards Plans quality control activities, monitors project progress and resolves quality issues Evaluates existing processes and suggests improvements to strengthen quality Establishes quality standards for the company's products and services Develops quality monitoring reports for the company identifying opportunities for improvement
