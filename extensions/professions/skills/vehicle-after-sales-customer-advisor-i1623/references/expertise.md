# Vehicle after-sales customer advisor

## Scope

The vehicle after-sales customer advisor embodies the link between the workshop and the professional or individual customers of a vehicle dealership. Welcomes and advises customers on after-sales services Transmits repair requests Plans interventions Ensures the return of vehicles to customers Also ensures the marketing of the workshop's products and services

## Related Job Titles

- Receptionist in automobile mechanic workshop
- Receptionist in motorcycle mechanic workshop

## Activities and Skills

- Support employees of the vehicle after-sales workshop
- Identify the human and material resources to carry out a technical intervention
- Plan a vehicle after-sales intervention by measuring its technical level
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Greet the customer on the phone or at the workshop reception
- Analyze the workshop customer's needs
- Provide technical advice to the customer with a view to selling additional services or complementary services
- Explain billing and interventions carried out on the vehicle to the customer
- Inform the customer about the technical interventions carried out by adapting their language
- Suggest an appointment to the workshop customer
- Propose an alternative mobility solution (courtesy vehicle, vehicle rental solution)
- Return the vehicle to the customer
- Apply a customer satisfaction approach
- Prepare a quote listing the work to be carried out on the vehicle
- Follow up with workshop customers by telephone, by email, face to face
- Monitor the progress of work in the vehicle after-sales workshop
- Communicate effectively with repair shops
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
- Regulate the workshop load plan in real time
- Use digital tools

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
