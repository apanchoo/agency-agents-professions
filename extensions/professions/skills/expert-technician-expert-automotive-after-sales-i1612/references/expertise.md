# Expert technician / Expert automotive after-sales technician

## Scope

As a technical referent, the expert technician works with various players in the automotive after-sales sector, for all types of light vehicles, including vehicles with the latest technologies (LPG / CNG, electric / hybrid), with approved or independent repairers, whether or not attached to a brand or manufacturer. It works in particular in companies regularly carrying out electrical and electronic diagnostic operations. Ensures the diagnosis and restoration of conformity of a set of systems with their proper functioning tests Intervenes on complex breakdowns of all types of engines and the constituent elements of the powertrain of a vehicle Provides support to the after-sales customer advisor through its technical expertise Sets up technical training sequences for workshop employees
