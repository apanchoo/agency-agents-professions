# Traffic and Air Defense Controller

## Scope

The Traffic and Air Defense Controller ensures the safety and efficiency of air traffic from a control entity. Provide air traffic services to military aircraft from takeoff to landing Implement radio navigation and radar systems Be able to detect, identify and track aircraft in the airspace Develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your job
