# Manager of collective investment undertaking in transferable securities -UCITS-

## Scope

The Manager of an undertaking for collective investment in transferable securities -UCITS- manages investment portfolios in transferable securities to optimize returns for clients. Manages portfolios and selects securities and investment products tailored to client needs Collaborates with financial analysts to produce reports and recommendations Communicates with clients to discuss strategies and performance
