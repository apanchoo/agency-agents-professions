# Driver of heavy handling equipment

## Scope

The Heavy Handling Equipment Driver plays a crucial role in handling and logistics operations. Operates heavy machinery to move large loads on various sites Ensures compliance with safety standards when driving and handling loads Performs regular checks on the condition of machinery to ensure their proper functioning Coordinates with ground teams to ensure efficient and safe handling May participate in first level maintenance of machinery to prevent breakdowns Ensures management of necessary transport and handling documents

## Related Job Titles

- Agent for maneuvering and driving heavy handling equipment

## Activities and Skills

- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Coordinate machine movements with ground teams
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Perform first level maintenance
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Anticipate a danger or constraint
- Check the operating status of a machine
- Inspect the site to identify obstacles
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Check the conformity of the transported load
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Plan the order of load movements
- Carry out lifting and moving load operations according to the instructions of the ground teams
- Operate a mobile bridge
- Maintain a record of operations carried out
- Estimate the costs and deadlines for an activity or service
- Communicate with the team via signals
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
