# Technical writing service manager

## Scope

The Technical Writing Service Manager supervises and coordinates the work of the technical writing team. Ensures the quality and consistency of produced documents Develops strategies to improve writing efficiency Collaborates with development and product teams to ensure the accuracy of manuals and guides Trains new technical writers on company standards and tools Manages the budget and resources of the technical writing department
