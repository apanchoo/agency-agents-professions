# Pharmacist

## Scope

The pharmacist is an essential player in the healthcare system, working in pharmacies, hospitals, industry, research or medical biology. Ensures the delivery and preparation of medications while supporting patients in their treatment Participates in prevention and ensures the security of medication care Guarantees the quality and safety of medications, from their design to their marketing in the industry Develops new treatments in pharmaceutical research Analysis of samples for the diagnosis and monitoring of patients in medical biology

## Related Job Titles

- Pharmacist / Biological pharmacist
- Pharmacist in the pharmaceutical industry
- Pharmacist in pharmacies
- Pharmacist responsible for BPDO - good practices for dispensing oxygen
- Research pharmacist
- Pharmacist wholesale distributor
- Hospital pharmacist

## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Adapt pharmaceutical care to the specific needs of each patient
- Ensure the safe dispensation of medications (including oxygen) and medical devices to patients
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Demonstrate teaching and advice with patients to promote the proper use of treatments
- Inform and advise patients on the proper use of medications, their dosages and side effects
- Carry out masterful and hospital preparations
- Raise awareness of the proper use of all medications and responsible self-medication
- Vaccinate and screen
- Raise public awareness of public health issues through campaigns
- Analyze health data to track changes in prescriptions and anticipate medical trends
- Anticipate seasonal variations and specific patient needs
- Train in technological innovations (AI, e-health)
- Ensure compliance of medications with current regulations
- Develop new galenic formulations and innovative therapeutic solutions
- Participate in the design, manufacturing and quality control of drugs in the pharmaceutical industry
- Ensure the maintenance of medical devices and preparation equipment
- Carry out regular checks to avoid breakdowns and guarantee equipment conformity
- Ensure the proper functioning of pharmaceutical production equipment in industry and wholesale distribution
- Ensure pharmacovigilance, proper use and the information produced
- Control the traceability of medicines to avoid falsification
- Identify and prevent medication errors (interactions, contraindications)
- Ensure compliance with health safety protocols
- Implement and follow quality assurance procedures
- Write and apply internal protocols to guarantee the safety of care
- Validate and control the proper conservation, provision and safety of medicines throughout the pharmaceutical chain
- Validate the results and guarantee the quality of medical biology examinations
- Check the compliance of medicines with manufacturing, distribution and dispensing standards
- Control the management of stocks of medicines and medical equipment
- Manage and train staff, distribute and supervise tasks, to ensure the fluidity of operations
- Plan and coordinate the activities of a pharmacy, a hospital department or a production site
- Avoid waste by monitoring stocks and prescriptions
- Optimize purchasing and budget management
- Optimize organizations, equipment and waste management while respecting regulations and the environment
- Search for suppliers and negotiate purchases of medicines and medical devices
- Apply current legislation and regulations
- Ensure compliance with ethical and legal rules in relationships with other healthcare stakeholders and professionals
- Develop marketing strategies adapted to patient needs and market developments
- Set up additional services (screening tests, personalized advice)
- Develop partnerships between wholesale distribution, industry and pharmacy and hospital dispensers
- Ensure comprehensive and fluid communication between the different stakeholders in the pharmaceutical chain
- Work as a team with doctors, nurses and other healthcare professionals
- Adapt the organization according to activity peaks to ensure continuity
- Update your knowledge by following continuing training (new molecules, new regulations, new technologies)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
