# Civil security crew member

## Scope

The civil security team member is a professional engaged in the protection of people and property, intervening on the front line during emergency or crisis situations. Intervenes quickly in the event of fire, accident or natural disaster Rescues victims by applying first aid measures Assesses potential risks to anticipate dangers Coordinates actions with other emergency services (firefighters, SAMU, police)

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate actions to be taken in an emergency situation
- Train new agents in procedures and regulations
- Apply emergency procedures in the event of an accident
- Carry out emergency care or organize arrangements for hospitalization, decontamination, etc. during accident situations, intoxication, etc.
- Supporting those supported in managing their stress and anxiety
- Communicate effectively about security risks
- Process complex information to make decisions
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Apply security protocols
- Liaise with other security services
- Ensuring the safety of property and people
- Check and use PPE and EPC
- Demarcate a security perimeter
- Determine security or rescue operations during accidents or disasters after assessing the situations and risks
- Develop action plans for critical situations
- Evaluate potential risks and propose preventive measures
- Evaluate potential risks on the tracks to prevent accidents
- Adapt intervention methods according to the specificities of each situation
- Assist and rescue military personnel, populations and nationals
- Collaborate with security and emergency services
- Evacuate animals, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Evacuate people, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Inform the competent authorities
- Intervene in hostile or dangerous environments (fire, road accident, etc.)
- Intervene during incidents linked to urban activities (fire in the street, road accident, air pollution, etc.)
- Intervene during natural disasters (flood, forest fire, storm, earthquake, avalanche, heatwave, etc.)
- Participate in simulation exercises to prepare for real-life situations
- Inform the public (complaints, information) and assist or rescue victims of accidents, attacks, disasters
- Stabilize a victim's condition while waiting for medical help to arrive
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Communicate effectively with the public
- Communicate effectively with supervisors about progress and issues
- Effectively communicate needs and emergencies
- Communicate instructions effectively
- Raise public awareness of prevention and safety measures
- Act quickly in the event of accidental pollution
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Document interventions and anomalies encountered
- Document procedures and results of operations
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
