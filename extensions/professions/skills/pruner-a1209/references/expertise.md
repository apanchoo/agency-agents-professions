# Pruner

## Scope

The pruner, specialist in pruning and maintaining trees, takes care of the environment and public safety. Prunes trees and shrubs according to aesthetic or safety needs Ensures the maintenance and care of trees to preserve their health and growth Uses climbing techniques to access branches Manages plant waste resulting from pruning and pruning He or she must work in a team with a colleague who remains on the ground to monitor the security perimeter and receive the cut branches.

## Activities and Skills

- Train new employees in trade techniques
- Analyze tree growth and health data
- Pruning the branches of a tree
- Position and adjust a felling machine
- Prune the trees
- Develop and maintain a natural space, a forest
- Determine felling or pruning according to the trajectory of tree fall, taking into account the environment and climatic conditions
- Evaluate the health of trees and determine which branches to cut
- Identify trees to be felled or pruned
- Perform basic maintenance of pruning equipment
- Check the conformity of the equipment used
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Analyze the risks linked to the construction site
- Check safety equipment or materials (PPE, harness, etc.)
- Install security or signaling elements
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Observe safety instructions when using machines
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Handle material vertically at height, using winches or pulleys
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage green waste according to environmental standards
- Managing plant waste in an ecological way
- Communicate with customers for specific requests
- Ensure communication with the ground team during operations at height
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Adapt working methods to the level of risks of working at height
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Install temporary devices to secure work areas at height
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital communication tools
