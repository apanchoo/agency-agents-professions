# Public health officer

## Scope

The Public Health Project Manager is responsible for designing, implementing and coordinating public health actions and programs; his role is crucial for improving the health and well-being of populations, preventing disease, promoting health and reducing health inequalities. Develops and implements public health policies in collaboration with local and national institutions Analyzes epidemiological data to guide prevention and health promotion actions Coordinates vaccination and disease screening programs Participates in the design and implementation of awareness campaigns on public health issues Collaborates with stakeholders in the health sector to develop effective strategies for managing health crises Evaluates the impact of public health programs and proposes improvements based on the results obtained
