# Software or application test and validation specialist

## Scope

The software or application test and validation specialist plays a crucial role in the development of reliable and efficient IT solutions. Designs and implements test plans to ensure software quality Executes manual and automated testing to identify bugs and functionality issues Collaborates with developers to resolve issues identified during testing Documents test results and prepares detailed reports for development teams Participates in continuous improvement of testing processes to increase test efficiency and coverage May specialize in specific areas such as security, performance, or user experience
