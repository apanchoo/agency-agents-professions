# Road passenger transport operations manager

## Scope

The Road Passenger Transport Operations Manager orchestrates the smooth running of daily transport operations. Supervises and coordinates passenger transport activities to guarantee an efficient and safe service Optimizes driver routes and schedules to improve productivity Ensures compliance with current transport and safety regulations Manages customer relations and resolves potential disputes or complaints Trains and supervises driver teams to maintain a high level of service Analyzes operational performance and proposes continuous improvements
