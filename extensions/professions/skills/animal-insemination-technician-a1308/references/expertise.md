# Animal insemination technician

## Scope

The Animal Insemination Technician carries out artificial insemination on different animals to improve the genetic performance of livestock. Advises breeders on best breeding practices and improvement strategies and the genetic management of breeding Follows safety and hygiene protocols during inseminations to guarantee animal health and the quality of interventions Manages the traceability of interventions and genetic data to ensure regulatory compliance Advises breeders on best breeding practices and genotyping Participates in the management of seed stocks and the planning of insemination sessions May be involved in research and the development of new insemination techniques

## Related Job Titles

- Artificial inseminator

- Monitor the pregnancy of an animal

- Optimize insemination protocols to improve success rates
- Organize the reproduction of an animal species
- Prepare the animals and carry out insemination
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Monitor the reproductive cycles of animals to plan inseminations
- Take samples of materials or products
- Take a biological sample
- Analyze a situation and produce a diagnosis
- Check data compliance
- Manage individual animal monitoring files
- Update a file, a database
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Select a breeding male
- Maintain insemination equipment in working order
- Analyze reproduction data to optimize practices
- Ensure traceability of interventions and results obtained
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Prepare an inspection and control report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Identify the request from the person responsible for the animal and determine the intervention methods (on-site care, transfer, etc.)
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Explain treatment choices to pet owners
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
