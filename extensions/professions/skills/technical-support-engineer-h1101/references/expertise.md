# Technical support engineer

## Scope

The Technical Support Engineer is a dedicated expert, ready to quickly resolve any technical issues encountered by customers to ensure optimal satisfaction. Provides technical support to customers and internal teams Resolves complex technical issues and provides appropriate solutions Optimizes processes and systems to improve efficiency Trains users on new technologies and procedures Collaborates with development teams to improve products Manages technical documentation and intervention reports

## Related Job Titles

- Technical support expert
- Technical support engineer
- Customer service engineer
- Technical support specialist

- Coordinate activities with other technical teams
- Facilitate cross-departmental collaboration for technical support
- Support customers in using a product or equipment
- Provide technical training for users
- Organize a technical assistance service
- Write technical documents in compliance with current standards
- Develop strategies for solving complex problems
- Manage IT security
- Make technical documents available to customers
- Use specific software
- Plan preventive maintenance operations
- Propose adapted technical solutions
- Analyze customer technical needs
- Check the conformity of sensitive equipment or installation
- Manage incidents and technical problems
- Carry out a technical diagnosis
- Transmit a complex technical problem to a specialist
- Conduct regular technical audits
- Promote the use of sustainable practices in technical assistance
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Manage customer feedback to improve services
- Analyze market trends to anticipate needs
- Carry out technical monitoring of products and their evolution with customers
- Maintain effective communication with customers
- Conduct customer satisfaction surveys
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Adapt your behavior to different people
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Master elearning, assistance and customer technical support techniques
