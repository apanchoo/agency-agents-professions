# Assistant / Dependency life assistant

## Scope

The dependency life assistant facilitates the daily life of vulnerable people by providing them with assistance in the essential acts of everyday life. Promotes the autonomy of the people supported in respecting their lifestyle Accompanies people in the actions of daily life and carrying out household work in order to maintain their autonomy Ensures an active listening role as well as a reassuring presence and thus contributes to improving the quality of life Can participate in coordination and liaison activities with other care or support services Maintains a secure and comfortable environment

## Related Job Titles

- Help for the elderly
- Assistant / Life support for elderly people
- Assistant / Life assistant for disabled people
- Family and health assistant
- Peer Helper

## Activities and Skills

- Design and lead relaxation or leisure activities
- Supporting the development of a person’s autonomy
- Welcoming, reassuring, guiding, informing a person and those around them
- Provide emotional support to those supported and those around them
- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Inform and advise the patient or supported person in managing their environment
- Maintain and facilitate the inclusion of supported people to avoid social isolation
- Carry out specific social monitoring actions
- Participate in the design and monitoring of an individualized project
- Supporting people in everyday activities
- Accompanying a person while traveling
- Helping people in their living environment
- Help someone get dressed
- Help prepare balanced meals
- Apply first aid techniques in an emergency
- Ensure a presence and a safe environment for those accompanied
- Dispense or administer medications
- Carry out errands for one person
- Encourage the person to maintain appropriate physical activity
- Managing the daily hygiene of a dependent person
- Prepare meals
- Prepare, help with meals and encourage handling of cutlery
- Raise awareness of good hygiene and health practices
- Monitor a person's health
- Ensure personal hygiene
- Check that the person is taking medication according to the medical prescription
- Complete a person's tracking documents
- Use specific software
- Adapt recipes for specific needs (allergies, diets, preferences)
- Handle medical equipment
- Use special equipment for mobility assistance
- Change the sheets, make the bed (clean, re-lay) and replace the bathroom linen
- Check the cleanliness of the laundry
- Maintain the living spaces and laundry of the person being accompanied
- Handle cleaning and maintenance products
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Make recommendations on living space arrangements for personal safety
- Maintain a clean and secure environment
- Prevent risks and the safety of the person accompanied
- Define supply needs
- Transporting people with reduced mobility
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Carry out shopping at the request of a customer or for the needs of the establishment
- Communicate with those close to the person being supported
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
