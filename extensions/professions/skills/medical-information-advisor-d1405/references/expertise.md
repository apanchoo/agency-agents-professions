# Medical Information Advisor

## Scope

The medical information advisor plays a crucial role in disseminating accurate and up-to-date medical information. Informs and advises healthcare professionals on the latest medical advances and pharmaceutical products Liaises between pharmaceutical companies and the medical profession to ensure effective communication Organizes and leads information meetings and training for healthcare professionals Monitors and analyzes healthcare market trends to adapt information strategies Complies with current regulations regarding the dissemination of medical information

## Related Job Titles

- Medical Information Attaché
- Medical advisor / Medical advisor in indoor environment (CMEI)
- Medical Information Delegate
- Dental delegate
- Veterinary delegate
- Medical information technician

- Inform healthcare staff about pharmaceutical products

- Adapt communication to different medical contacts
- Design communication and image supports
- Write a report, an activity report

- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Have in-depth knowledge of medical and pharmaceutical products
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Present and promote a product or service
- Monitor medical information market trends
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Facilitate communication between different players in the medical sector
- Organize informative meetings for medical staff
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data
- Monitor and update technical, economic, regulatory information, etc.
- Use digital tools
- Regularly update your knowledge
