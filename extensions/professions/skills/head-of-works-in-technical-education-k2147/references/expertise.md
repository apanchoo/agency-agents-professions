# Head of works in technical education

## Scope

The Technical Education Work Manager manages practical and technical activities within educational establishments. Coordinates and supervises the technical and practical activities of educational establishments Ensures liaison between teachers, students and other members of technical staff Plans and manages the material resources necessary for practical work Ensures compliance with safety and hygiene standards in workshops and laboratories Participates in the development of educational projects in collaboration with the teaching team May intervene in the training and evaluation of apprentices and students

## Related Job Titles

- Agricultural teaching instructor
- Instructor in apprentice training center
- Agricultural technical instructor
