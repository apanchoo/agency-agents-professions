# Oil and gas field operations manager

## Scope

The oilfield operations manager supervises and coordinates drilling and oil or gas extraction operations on a site. Supervises and coordinates operational teams Plans drilling and equipment maintenance activities Optimizes processes to improve operational efficiency while maintaining high standards for safety and quality Ensures compliance of activities with environmental regulations

## Related Job Titles

- Drilling site manager
- Oil and gas site manager
- Head of deposit
- Gas project manager
- Manager of oil and gas field
- Driller / Driller station manager
- Drilling Supervisor
- Field exploitation technician

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Communicate with teams in the field to coordinate operations
- Promote and measure team performance
- Managing multicultural teams
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Carry out technical or technological monitoring to anticipate developments
- Analyze data to make strategic decisions
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Develop and plan a production and operating program
- Develop a technical master plan, define equipment needs
- Plan production, estimate equipment, materials and labor requirements, in order to respect cost constraints and completion deadlines
- Propose continuous technical improvements
- Control drilling parameters in real time
- Direct drilling operations
- Follow the evolution of a deposit
- Ensure compliance with industry standards
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Ensure the safety of drilling operations on site
- Communicate safety instructions clearly
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Supervise operations, actions
- Supervise regular maintenance of equipment
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Analyze production data to improve yields
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Organize and plan an activity
- Prepare detailed reports for management
- Write security reports
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Monitor and control expenses related to drilling operations in accordance with the allocated budget
- Control and minimize the amount of water produced with oil to reduce environmental impacts and processing costs
- Evaluate the environmental impact of operating projects
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Adjust operating parameters
- Manage production and operations management
- Communicate, read and write technical documents, reports, notes in English
- Make a decision and explain it
- Act quickly in the event of an incident
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Build emergency response plans
- Document technical procedures for teams
- Make quick decisions under pressure
