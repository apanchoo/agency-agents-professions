# Driver of works in natural environments

## Scope

The natural environment works manager orchestrates the completion of work projects to preserve and restore ecosystems through the management (planning, organization and coordination) of all ecological projects under his responsibility Identifies and evaluates the environmental risks associated with the project (reduction of impact, anticipation of pollution risks, etc.) Recommends technical solutions adapted to ecological issues, the characteristics of the site and operational and regulatory constraints Plans and coordinates the different phases of the ecological project Organizes the work activity through and monitors the different phases of the ecological project Ensures the management of the material, human and financial resources necessary in order to respect the deadlines, costs and quality of services in relation to ecological aspects Supervises and coordinates the work in the field in relation to compliance with deadlines, ecological objectives, safety and regulatory rules Ensures the link with the stakeholders (teams, partners and the project management to guarantee the alignment of objectives) Responds to offers and quantifies the interventions, and ensures administrative and financial monitoring of the site until invoicing
