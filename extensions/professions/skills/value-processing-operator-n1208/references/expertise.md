# Value processing operator

## Scope

The value processing operator ensures the valuation and packaging operations of funds (money) and values (watchmaking, jewelry, jewelry, goldsmithing, precious metals) entrusted or ordered by customers: Valuates and packages funds and values Contributes to the management of funds and values stored in the strong room Ensures routine maintenance of counting, valuation and packaging machines Handles packages within the company's premises Reports anomalies

## Related Job Titles

- Vault or safe room agent
- Head of cash register team - counting
- Cash register or counting operator
- Responsible for strong room or safe
- Cashier service manager
- Metering department manager
