# Equine dentist

## Scope

The equine dentist combines medical expertise and passion for horses, to ensure their well-being and performance; he monitors and carries out routine dental care for horses; if he is also a veterinarian, he can carry out more complex treatments in equine dentistry (paragraph 11 of article L243-3 established by ordinance no. 2011-862 of July 22, 2011 delimits the field of expertise of the equine dentist, depending on whether he is a veterinarian or not).

## Related Job Titles

- Equine dental technician
- Equine dental technician

## Activities and Skills

- Support the client in choosing appropriate care
- Apply strict hygiene protocols
- Ensure medical monitoring of equines and adapt the care protocol
- Correct the wear of dental tables (eliminate enamel points and roughness or overhangs, etc.)
- Diagnose dental pathologies
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Establish a care program if necessary which will allow the horse to find a comfortable situation for eating and/or working
- Extract milk teeth, wolf or pig teeth, in accordance with article 3 of the decree of October 5, 2011
- Identify anomalies and conditions that require the intervention of a veterinarian
- Participate in the good health of equines by ensuring good functionality of their teeth
- Allow the horse to eat correctly (optimization of chewing) and to accept the bit
- Carry out an examination of the oral cavity
- Carry out routine care for equine teeth
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Select the equipment, products and carry out the treatments
- Ensure routine maintenance or cleaning of equipment after use under the required health and safety conditions
- Ensure compliance with health and safety standards
- Maintain administrative files up to date
- Arrange scheduled appointments and veterinary emergencies
- Make an appointment to work with a customer
- Carry out invoicing
- Optimize payment and invoicing processes
- Inform the client about the breeds and lifestyle of animals
- Design customer loyalty strategies
- Develop a customer and prospect portfolio
- Maintain a high level of customer service
- Communicate effectively with veterinarians
- Report on your activity
- Act quickly in case of dental emergency
