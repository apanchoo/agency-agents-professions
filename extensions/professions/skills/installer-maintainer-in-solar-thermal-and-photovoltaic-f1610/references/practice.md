# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Establish uses and consumption balance with their units.
2. Model heat flows and operating conditions.
3. Compare technical variants, costs and impacts.
4. Prepare the sizing, monitoring and verification of gains.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Solar thermal and photovoltaic
- Renewable energies
- Electric energy

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Commissioning and first level maintenance of an installation
- Management and maintenance of installations – implementation of measurement and counting plans for optimization purposes

## Deliverables

- Energy balance
- Comparison of solutions
- Measurement and monitoring plan

## Acceptance Criteria

- The perimeters and climatic conditions are explained
- Yields and conversion factors are sourced
- Estimated gains are separated from measured gains

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
