# Cinema hall manager

## Scope

The cinema lobby manager is responsible for the quality of reception and spectator satisfaction. Leads and coordinates the reception and entertainment teams Supervises ticketing and product sales activities Manages spectator flows to ensure the smooth running of screenings Participates in the administrative and accounting management of the establishment in conjunction with operations managers

## Related Job Titles

- Cinema theater team leader

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Distribute the workload and tasks to be performed among team members
- Supervise and support the reception and sales missions of the hall teams
- Pay attention to the successful integration of each new employee
- Support the public in the use of digital tools (online reservations, subscriptions)
- Ensure the training of new employees
- Design visual communication supports
- Inform and advise spectators on cinema offerings and organized events
- Implement communication actions
- Create an exhibition panel, display, stand
- Update the display, the information made available to the public
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Monitor the launch and smooth running of screenings and report any technical problems
- Check the cleanliness of a place
- Evaluate security risks in different situations
- Supervise operations, actions
- Train teams in quality and safety standards
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Manage a cash register
- Use electronic ticketing systems and manage transactions
- Ensure compliance with the labor code and the company's internal regulations (respect of schedules, absences, etc.)
- Supervise the application of ticket control procedures and room access devices
- Supervise spectator flows and regulate access to rooms
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Ensuring the safe reception of visitors
- Advisor in the context of a commercial relationship
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Make a decision and explain it
- Promote a proposal, a project
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Managing an emergency situation
