# Bioinformatician in studies, research and development

## Scope

The Bioinformatician in studies, research and development, analyzes biological data and develops IT tools for scientific research. Analyzes and interprets genomic and proteomic data for biomedical research Develops and uses algorithms and software to process complex biological data Collaborates with researchers and scientists to design experiments and studies Contributes to the publication of scientific discoveries and the writing of research reports Maintains technological intelligence to integrate the latest advances in bioinformatics May teach and train students or colleagues in bioinformatics techniques and tools
