# Sales Administration Assistant

## Scope

The sales administration assistant, an essential element who above all ensures customer satisfaction. Manages customer orders from receipt to delivery Ensures the monitoring of customer files and the updating of databases Coordinates with the various departments to ensure the fluidity of operations Processes complaints and returns in collaboration with the after-sales service Prepares the necessary commercial and administrative documents Can participate in the development of commercial offers and the negotiation of contracts

## Related Job Titles

- Export Sales Administration Assistant
- Sales assistant
- Sales Administration Technician
