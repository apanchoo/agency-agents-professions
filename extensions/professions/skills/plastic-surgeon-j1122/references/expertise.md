# Plastic surgeon

## Scope

The plastic surgeon enhances and reconstructs the bodily and aesthetic harmony of patients. Performs surgical procedures to correct or improve physical appearance but also repair and reconstruct Assesses patient needs and proposes appropriate surgical solutions Ensures post-operative follow-up to ensure optimal recovery Collaborates with other health specialists to provide comprehensive care Maintains up-to-date knowledge of the latest techniques and technologies in cosmetic surgery Can participate in conferences and the training of new surgeons

## Related Job Titles

- Reconstructive surgeon / Aesthetic and reconstructive surgeon

## Activities and Skills

- Collaborate with other specialists for complex cases
- Collaborate with other specialists for integrated care
- Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team in the operating room
- Analyze patient needs for plastic surgery
- Ensure medical follow-up of a patient
- Ensure medical follow-up of patients after surgery to check convalescence and treat potential complications
- Evaluate the effectiveness of post-operative treatments
- Demonstrate dexterity and precision in manipulating patients' muscles and joints
- Inform, support a patient
- Implement and adapt the anesthesia procedure according to the patient and the progress of the intervention
- Schedule health checkups
- Carry out psychological tests, evaluate the person's profile and establish the psychological assessment
- Prepare a patient for a medical procedure
- Prevent psychosocial risks
- Carry out a medical diagnosis
- Carry out a surgical procedure
- Coordinate with the surgical team for the preparation of interventions
- Monitor vital parameters during surgery
- Complete technical documents during a surgical procedure and transmit them to the relevant contact
- Complete an intervention report
- Evaluate the risks associated with the surgical intervention
- Optimize safety procedures during interventions
- Respect ethical and legal standards in surgery
- Manage customer follow-up after intervention
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Communicate clearly with nursing staff
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Facilitate communication between different players in the medical sector
- Organize scientific meetings to update knowledge
- Manage surgical emergencies and complications
- Maintain strict hygiene in the operating room
- Respect the confidentiality of patient or beneficiary information
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of medical products
- Participate in continuing education sessions and professional conferences
