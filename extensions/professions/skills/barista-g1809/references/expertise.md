# Barista

## Scope

Specialist in the preparation and serving of coffee-based drinks, this professional guarantees a quality product. Welcomes and advises customers on the choice of drinks and accompaniments Prepares and serves drinks according to quality standards May create personalized drinks to meet customer preferences

## Related Job Titles

- Waiter / Cafe waitress

## Activities and Skills

- Train new employees on coffee standards
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Organize coffee tasting workshops
- Promote the establishment on social networks
- Use management software to track sales
- Arguing over coffee and beverage choices
- Create cocktail recipes with or without alcohol
- Develop latte art techniques
- Optimize the use of coffee machines for better quality
- Prepare hot or cold drinks
- Ensure a welcoming atmosphere in the café
- Optimize the bar layout
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Wipe and put away dishes, glassware, kitchen utensils
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Monitor the quality of products served
- Manage inventory of materials and supplies
- Manage a cash register
- Plan work schedules based on peak activity
- Proceed to cashout
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Greet the customer and install them
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Take a customer order
- Carry out room service
- Adapt offers according to market trends
- Design promotions and special offers
- Negotiate with suppliers to optimize costs and product quality
- Establish partnerships with local producers
- Communicate effectively with the team to ensure consistency of work
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Monitor regulatory developments
- Develop expertise in coffee beans

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Have a sense of service
- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
