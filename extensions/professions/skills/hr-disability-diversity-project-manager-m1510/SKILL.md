## Scope

The HR Disability Diversity Project Manager participates in the prevention of discrimination and values and promotes inclusion within the company. Develops and implements diversity and inclusion policies Monitors diversity indicators and suggests improvements Organizes training and awareness-raising on disability and diversity Collaborates with different departments to integrate inclusive practices Advises management on best practices in diversity and inclusion Participates in external networks to discuss diversity practices

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Integrate an employee with a disability
- Participate in the implementation of HR projects facilitating inclusion, equality and quality of life at work within the structure
- Recruit and integrate a person
- Advise on the adaptation of workstations for employees with disabilities or medical restrictions
- Promoting equal opportunities and combating discrimination
- Analyze working conditions and propose improvements
- Design and implement awareness-raising actions
- Develop and manage a network of partner organizations
