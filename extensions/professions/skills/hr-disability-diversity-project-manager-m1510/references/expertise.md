# HR Disability Diversity Project Manager

## Scope

The HR Disability Diversity Project Manager participates in the prevention of discrimination and values and promotes inclusion within the company. Develops and implements diversity and inclusion policies Monitors diversity indicators and suggests improvements Organizes training and awareness-raising on disability and diversity Collaborates with different departments to integrate inclusive practices Advises management on best practices in diversity and inclusion Participates in external networks to discuss diversity practices

## Related Job Titles

- Diversity and equal opportunities manager
