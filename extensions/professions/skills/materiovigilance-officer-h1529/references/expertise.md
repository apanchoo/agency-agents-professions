# Materiovigilance Officer

## Scope

The Materiovigilance Officer is essential to the monitoring and safety of medical materials. Ensures the monitoring of incidents or risks linked to the use of medical devices Analyzes safety data and draws up materiovigilance reports based on its knowledge of regulations Collaborates with health authorities to bring products into compliance Participates in the training of healthcare professionals on the risks linked to medical devices and good practices Proposes improvements to devices based on feedback Maintains technical and regulatory documentation for products up to date
