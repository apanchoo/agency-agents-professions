# Supply chain manager in industry

## Scope

The Industrial Supply Chain Manager optimizes the supply chain, from the acquisition of raw materials to the delivery of finished products. Plans and coordinates production and distribution operations according to customer needs Optimizes costs and production times by managing material and human resources Ensures stock management and supervises inventories to avoid any shortage or overstock Collaborates closely with the different departments (purchasing, production, sales) to guarantee the efficiency of the supply chain Analyzes the performance of the supply chain and proposes improvements to increase productivity Ensures compliance with quality, safety and environmental standards in all stages of the supply chain
