# Pastry chef / Restaurant pastry chef

## Scope

The restaurant pastry chef participates in the design of the dessert menu with the chef. Creates the desserts on the menu in accordance with the culinary style of the establishment Participates in monitoring stocks of raw materials and defines the requirements for foodstuffs necessary for the production of desserts Respects hygiene and food safety standards Can participate in culinary competitions and represent the restaurant during gastronomic events

## Related Job Titles

- Chef-pastry chef
- Pastry chef
- Pastry chef / Restaurant pastry chef
- Pastry chef / Pastry cook
- Second / Second pastry

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Ensure continuing training for apprentice pastry chefs
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Help organize special events
- Test new baking techniques
- Plan production operations according to orders
- Adapt recipes for specific needs (allergies, diets, preferences)
- Design innovative recipes for desserts
- Make pastries
- Measure out culinary ingredients
- Carry out the assembly and decoration of pastry products
- Develop recipes
- Handle kitchen equipment
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Participate in the development of menus and menus with the restaurant or establishment manager
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Prepare ice cream and sorbets
- Prepare kitchen utensils
- Prepare dried or fresh fruits (cutting, peeling, rinsing, etc.)
- Prepare a pastry dough
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Select the raw materials to mobilize
- Analyze possible malfunctions present in the kitchen, and propose corrective measures to remedy them
- Carry out freshness control tests on food and preparations and take samples for the hygiene department
- Packaging products
- Define supply needs
- Manage the supply of raw materials needed in the kitchen to avoid surpluses or stock shortages
- Organize the storage of foodstuffs, sorting them by expiration dates for example
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Organize and plan an activity
- Order products and raw materials from different suppliers and farmers in your region
- Determine the cost costs of culinary preparations
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Fight against food waste
- Optimize ingredient usage to reduce costs
- Manage waste and recycling in an environmentally friendly way
- Communicate with customers for specific requests
- Communicate effectively with staff and customers
- Coordinate activities between the kitchen and the room
- Act quickly in the event of a technical problem or shortage
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Develop your skills by following continuing training
