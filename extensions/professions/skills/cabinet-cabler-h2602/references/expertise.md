# Cabinet cabler

## Scope

The Cabinet Cabler assembles electrical or electromechanical elements inside cabinets and equipment, according to precise technical drawings and current safety standards. Carries out functional tests to check electrical continuity and proper operation Installs the wiring and connection of elements according to technical specifications Reads and interprets electrical plans, wiring diagrams to mount electrical components in cabinets Ensures preventive and curative maintenance with adjustments prior to power-up
