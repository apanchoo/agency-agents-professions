# Support officer

## Scope

The assistance manager is the interface between the insured and the insurer, he identifies the customer's problem (accident, illness, death, etc.) and guides him towards an appropriate solution. Handles incoming calls to provide support and guidance Ensures timely and effective assistance to customers when needed Coordinates emergency services and contractors to resolve critical situations Tracks customer files to ensure complete resolution of issues Maintains clear and professional communication with all stakeholders

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Update a file, a database
- Monitor the conformity of services provided and compliance with commitments
- Organize assistance services while respecting procedures and contractual guarantees
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Manage incoming and outgoing phone calls
- Inform beneficiaries about the progress of their file and the solutions put in place
- Prioritize requests based on the degree of urgency and operational constraints
- Receive and qualify incoming calls from beneficiaries in emergency or need situations
- Answer phone calls, emails, chat and inform customers
- Follow customer files until resolution
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Coordinate the various stakeholders (providers, medical services, transporters, partners)
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Work in collaboration with internal teams to ensure continuity of service
- Working in a group, in a network
- Act quickly in an emergency situation
- Analyze the request in order to quickly identify the most suitable solution
