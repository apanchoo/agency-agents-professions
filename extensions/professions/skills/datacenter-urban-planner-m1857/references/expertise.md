# Datacenter Urban Planner

## Scope

The Datacenter Urban Planner is an architect of tomorrow's digital infrastructures, his expertise in energy planning and optimization shapes sustainable and efficient technological environments. Is responsible for the planning, design and optimization of data center infrastructures Manages the implementation of IT equipment and their physical connection to networks Is a referent on the layout of IT racks and the choice of related technologies Ensures that the installations are efficient, secure and complies with environmental and regulatory standards Collaborates closely with technical teams, architects, and engineers to meet operational and performance needs

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Integrate innovative technologies to improve data center performance
- Carry out studies on the organization and evolution of data center architectures and configurations
- Analyze operating and usage conditions and implement solutions for adjustment, evolution or migration of networks and resources
- Sizing, planning and monitoring the capacity of the telecom network and its interconnections
- Develop detailed plans for the layout of equipment and infrastructure
- Establish and update urbanization plans for computer rooms
- Establish the implementation plan
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Calculate thermal, hydraulic or aeraulic sizing
- Establish and update a guide to intervention procedures on Datacenter equipment
- Plan production, estimate equipment, materials and labor requirements, in order to respect cost constraints and completion deadlines
- Sizing an electrical, refrigeration or connector installation
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Design solutions to optimize space and energy efficiency
- Monitor the work (facilitation of weekly monitoring meetings, writing of reports, quality management, cost, deadline, planning, hazard management, contradictory findings)
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Identify and implement procedures guaranteeing the security of premises
- Comply with an information security policy
- Monitor power, cooling, and network connectivity
- Effectively manage resources to minimize costs and maximize efficiency
- Master the regulations in force related to your sector of activity
- Analyze and control energy consumption
- Implement solutions to improve the energy efficiency of networks
- Analyze customer needs by defining projects and proposing technical choices
- Propose and validate clients’ urbanization choices
- Ensure that activities are carried out in compliance with quality, cost and deadline objectives
- Manage data center construction and renovation projects
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Demonstrate commercial acumen and interpersonal skills
- Respect cost constraints and production deadlines
- Respect safety rules
- Adapt your activity to the use of robots, cobots, digital tools and interfaces
