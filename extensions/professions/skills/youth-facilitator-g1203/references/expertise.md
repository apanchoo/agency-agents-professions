# Youth facilitator

## Scope

The youth leader designs, leads and supervises a program of educational and fun activities for young people between 11 and 17 years old. Organizes and leads fun, sporting and educational activities adapted to the age of participants Encourages the participation and personal development of young people Ensures the safety and well-being of children and adolescents Promotes social integration and teamwork among young people Adapts activities according to the specific needs of groups Ensures liaison with parents and other educators for the monitoring of young people
