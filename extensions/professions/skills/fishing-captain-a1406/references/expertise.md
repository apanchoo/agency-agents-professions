# Fishing Captain

## Scope

The Fishing Captain directs and controls navigation at sea and fishing operations according to operating objectives, safety rules and maritime regulations on the environment and catch management. Controls the handling, processing and conservation of fishing products until their marketing. May direct a vessel and ensure the management of a crew.

## Related Job Titles

- Fishing Commander
- Fishing supervisor
- Fishing Lieutenant
- Fishing boss
- Fishing boss
- Second / Fishing second

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Organize the work of a team
- Recruit and integrate a person
- Train in technical and regulatory developments
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Collect and record fishing catch data
- Collect and qualify data, signals, measurements, images, transmitted or emitted
- Collect technical data and develop the route map for the crossing (times, distances, weather, etc.)
- Process collected meteorological information
- Plan fishing according to season and weather conditions
- Identify possible fishing spots
- Monitor a natural area to prevent the risk of accidents, pollution and fire
- Maintain a boat
- Maintain fishing equipment in good condition
- Evaluate a risky situation
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Manage fishing gear (trawls, nets, etc.) and participate in the preparation and storage of catches
- Supervise product storage
- Organize order processing
- Monitor the course of a boat
- Set the ship's course and parameters on the steering and control instruments
- Carry out docking or undocking maneuvers for the boat
- Prepare and operate the ship's equipment (anchors, cables, ropes and rigging, etc.) during departure or docking maneuvers
- Carry out or control vessel maneuvers and fishing operations
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage on-board documents and administrative formalities
- Carry out accounting operations
- Promote sustainable fishing practices
- Promote products to customers
- Order a ship
- Coordinate activities on board the ship
- Communicate with port authorities
- Make a decision and explain it
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Monitor regulatory developments
