# Biomedical equipment maintenance technician

## Scope

The Biomedical Equipment Maintenance Technician guarantees the reliability and safety of equipment, thus contributing to the quality of care. Installs, carries out preventive and corrective maintenance and ensures quality control of biomedical equipment Performs technical diagnostics and resolves breakdowns Updates medical equipment software and trains staff in their use Manages the stock of spare parts and orders the new necessary parts Documents interventions and keeps maintenance files up to date Participates in risk management and monitoring of materiovigilance files

## Related Job Titles

- Biomedical equipment maintenance electronician
- Medical equipment maintenance technician
- Medical analysis laboratory after-sales service technician

## Activities and Skills

- Train staff in the use of equipment
- Raise user awareness of the investment and operating costs of medical devices
- Install and commission hardware and software, in compliance with usage standards and electrical safety rules
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Model a system of connected equipment in its environment
- Participate in the implementation of telemedicine protocols
- Promote the use of emerging technologies
- Define an installation procedure or electronic system configuration according to the customer environment
- Establish recommendations and recommendations for use and commissioning of connected equipment
- Configure the installation and carry out its adjustment
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Ensure the maintenance of measuring equipment
- Ensure monitoring of interventions carried out by equipment suppliers or maintenance providers
- Change or repair a defective part or assembly
- Determine the causes of malfunctions
- Plan maintenance interventions according to priorities
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Control the operation of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Define interventions on equipment according to the technical file
- Guarantee the proper functioning, availability and performance of connected equipment under your responsibility
- Verify by simulation, tests, trials, calculations, the functionalities and characteristics of the electrical or electronic system
- Manage stocks of maintenance equipment and spare parts
- Ensure the traceability of interventions
- Organize and plan an activity
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Communicate effectively with suppliers
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Negotiate optimal purchasing conditions
- Establish a relationship of trust with the medical, nursing and paramedical profession
- Report on your activity
- Ensure technological and regulatory monitoring
- Document interventions and incident reports
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
