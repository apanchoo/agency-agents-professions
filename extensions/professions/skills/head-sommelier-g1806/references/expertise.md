# Head sommelier

## Scope

The Head Sommelier, expert in wines and spirits, plays a key role in the gastronomic experience. Selects and purchases wines and spirits for the establishment Advises customers on food and wine pairings Manages the wine cellar, ensuring optimal conservation of bottles Trains staff in wine knowledge Organizes tastings and wine events Participates in the development of menus in collaboration with the chef de cuisine

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Organize the work of a team
- Educate customers about the history of wines
- Train staff in wine presentation
- Innovate in wine preservation techniques
- Use inventory management software
- Adapt the wine offerings to the seasons
- Design the wine and drinks list according to the type of cuisine, geographical location
- Participate in wine tasting events
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Monitor wine storage conditions
- Supervise room service during events
- Analyze the characteristics of wines for advice
- Ensure the health compliance of stored wines
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Managing a wine cellar
- Organize a cellar according to the characteristics of the bottles
- Carry out order management (placing, tracking, reception)
- Evaluate and select wine suppliers
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Plan purchases according to seasonal events
- Select and buy wines from winegrowers, wholesalers, etc.
- Enforce alcohol consumption regulations
- Manage complaints as well as commercial disputes
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Serving wine
- Prepare the selected wine (warming, decanting, etc.), choose the appropriate glassware and serve the customer
- Inform customers about the different characteristics of wines or spirits (grape variety, provenance)
- Adapt offers to market trends
- Define product or service pricing
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Integrate organic wines into the offer
- Negotiate wine purchase prices with suppliers
- Organize wine tasting events
- Promote local wines to customers
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Establish partnerships with local and international winegrowers
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Keep your knowledge up to date on new wines
