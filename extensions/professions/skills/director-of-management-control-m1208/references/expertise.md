# Director of management control

## Scope

The director of management control, a key role in optimizing the financial performance of companies. Supervises the preparation of budgets and the monitoring of deviations from forecasts Analyzes financial performance and proposes corrective measures Optimizes costs and financial processes to improve efficiency Collaborates with the different departments to ensure the consistency of financial data Participates in the definition of financial objectives and the development of strategies to achieve them Ensures compliance of management practices with current regulations

## Related Job Titles

- Director of Budget Control
- Management control manager
- Manager of management control and budgets
- Manager of management control and accounting
- International management control manager
- Manager of the management control department

## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate interdepartmental communication
- Monitor the management information system
- Act quickly if financial anomalies are detected
- Write a report, an activity report
- Collaborate with teams to align budgets
- Develop strategies for financial risk management
- Comply with international financial reporting standards
- Carry out accounting operations
- Supervise an accounting close
- Process accounting data, particularly for internal audits
- Calculate the margins, profitability of a product or activity
- Design a forecast activity plan
- Monitor performance indicators, analyze and correct discrepancies
- Develop dashboards
- Evaluate the financial impact of strategic decisions
- Innovate in budgetary control methods
- Implement decision support tools
- Suggest improvements for financial processes
- Supervise the internal control of a structure
- Collaborate with external auditors

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
