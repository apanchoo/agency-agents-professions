# Leather and skins cleaner

## Scope

The leather and skins cleaner is an essential professional in preserving the quality and aesthetics of noble materials. Cleans and maintains leather and hide items using appropriate techniques and quality standards Restores damaged items to extend their life Applies specific treatments to revive the color and texture of leather Advises customers on the proper care of their leather items Manages inventory of cleaning and restoration products May customize leather items to specific customer requests

## Related Job Titles

- Reviver / Leather and skin reviver
- Leather dyer

## Activities and Skills

- Determine preparation or finishing interventions depending on the materials, products (hides, leather, etc.) and defects identified
- Detect assembly difficulties, non-conformities (aesthetics, falling clothing, fit) and provide technical solutions
- Examine leather and skin items to identify types of soiling and damaged areas
- Polish and wax items to restore their shine
- Apply conditioners to moisturize and nourish the leather
- Apply protective products to improve the durability and wear resistance of items
- Remove tissue and skin
- Determine cleaning methods based on fabric types (dry wash, laundry, ironing, etc.)
- Evaluate the general condition of items to determine necessary treatment
- Clean skins and leathers with specific products
- Carry out a recoloring operation
- Practice safe handling gestures and postures
- Control the quality of treated hides and skins
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Define supply needs
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Apply rules and protocols aimed at limiting the effects of the activity on the environment
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Identify and process a customer request
- Return the items to the customer after checking cleaning, ironing
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Create a quote
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Present costs and delivery times to a customer
- Respect delivery times
- Use digital tools
