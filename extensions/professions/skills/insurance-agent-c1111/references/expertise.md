# Insurance agent

## Scope

Represents one or more insurance companies as a salesperson. Presents and offers contracts to approached clients. Supports and advises its customers by offering them solutions adapted to their needs. Develop and retain a clientele of professionals or individuals. Works as an independent contractor. Does not manage insurance contracts or claims settlement.

## Related Job Titles

- Insurance intermediary agent(s)

## Activities and Skills

- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Update a file, a database
- Use CRM tools to manage customer interactions
- Estimate the value of a good or a product
- Evaluate disaster risks
- Apply and enforce legislation in the commercial services of one or more entities
- Manage complaints as well as commercial disputes
- Carry out insurance underwriting operations
- Present the clauses of a contract
- Analyze a client's risks and determine their need for insurance
- Ensure follow-up of customer files
- Advisor in the context of a commercial relationship
- Develop a network of business providers
- Use negotiation and sales techniques for insurance products
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Present and promote a product or service
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Sell insurance or savings products
- Convince, negotiate
- Demonstrate commercial acumen and interpersonal skills
- Use digital communication tools

## Professional Approach

- Show curiosity, open-mindedness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
