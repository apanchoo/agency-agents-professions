# Biogas engineer

## Scope

The Biogas Engineer specializes in the design, optimization and management of biogas production facilities which transform organic materials (agricultural waste, livestock effluent, household waste, etc.) into renewable energy (biogas). Studies and designs methanization units Improves the efficiency of biogas production by optimizing processes Treats and recovers inputs (organic waste) Ensures the proper functioning of installations Guarantees regulatory compliance with environmental and energy standards

## Related Job Titles

- Methanization Engineer
