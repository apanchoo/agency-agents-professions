# Judicial mediator

## Scope

The judicial mediator acts for the amicable resolution of conflicts. Facilitates communication and negotiates between parties in conflict to find an amicable solution Ensures the confidentiality of exchanges and preserves the integrity of shared information Drafts mediation agreements and ensures their understanding and acceptance by all parties Intervenes in various contexts such as family, professional or commercial to defuse tensions Organizes and leads mediation sessions while respecting ethical and professional rules May collaborate with lawyers, judges or other legal professionals to facilitate the mediation process
