# Director of drug promotional information in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Director of Drug Promotional Information orchestrates the communication and promotion strategy for pharmaceutical products. Defines and implements the communication strategy to promote medicines Supervises the creation of promotional and informative materials in compliance with regulations Collaborates with research and development teams to understand the products Ensures training and information for teams on product characteristics Analyzes market feedback to adjust promotion strategies Maintains regulatory and scientific monitoring to ensure compliance of actions
