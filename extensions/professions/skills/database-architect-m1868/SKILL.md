## Scope

Designs and implements database architectures tailored to business needs Ensures performance, security and data integrity Optimizes and updates data structures to improve system efficiency Guarantees organization, accessibility and protection of data Collaborates with development teams to integrate databases into applications Conducts regular audits to ensure compliance with standards and regulations Trains users in best data management practices
