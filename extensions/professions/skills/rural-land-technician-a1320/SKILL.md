## Scope

The Rural Land Technician specializes in the management and development of rural areas. Participates in the management of agricultural, forestry and natural lands, by monitoring plots, rural leases and usage rights. Contributes to the development and development of rural areas, by working on land development projects, land consolidation, creation of rural roads, etc. Provides expertise to local authorities, farmers, landowners and other stakeholders in the rural world on issues of land management and use Ensures compliance with regulations regarding rural land Plays a mediator role between the different stakeholders in the rural world (farmers, owners, communities, etc.) to facilitate the concerted management of rural areas Can examine administrative files related to land use
