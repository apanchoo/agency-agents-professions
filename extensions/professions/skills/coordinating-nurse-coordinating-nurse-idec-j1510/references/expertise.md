# Coordinating nurse / Coordinating nurse (IDEC)

## Scope

The nurse coordinator intervenes within a network of actors to ensure the continuity and quality of the care pathway of a patient or a supported person, under the responsibility of a hierarchical manager. Organizes the activity and manages the healthcare teams while respecting the establishment project Coordinates the continuity of care internally and externally Contributes to the implementation of personalized care projects and associated protocols Ensures liaison with patients and their entourage Organizes and coordinates the logistical management of medications, products and medical equipment Contributes to the administrative management of activities

## Related Job Titles

- Nurse coordinator / Nurse coordinator of home support services
- Nurse coordinator / Nurse coordinator of home nursing care
- Nurse coordinator / Oncology nurse coordinator
- Nurse coordinator / Nurse coordinator in healthcare establishment
- Coordinating nurse / Coordinating nurse in a medico-social establishment

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the intervention of multidisciplinary teams
- Carry out administrative monitoring (clocking, etc.) and define schedules
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Integrate feedback into practices
- Ensure the completeness of care files by the teams
- Provide technical support to staff
- Evaluate students
- Evaluate the practices of IDE and AS (by EPP: Professional Practical Evaluation)
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Ensuring inclusive care practices for minorities
- Implement actions to ensure continuity of hospital-home care
- Taking into account innovations in medico-social care
- Search, process and transmit information to ensure continuity of care and activities
- Monitor and evaluate the application of care protocols
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Control the management of stocks of medicines and medical equipment
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Encourage ecological practices in daily management
- Contribute to the evaluation of the quality of care
- Develop and manage relationships or partnerships
- Managing an activity
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Help promote preventive health practices
- Participate in multidisciplinary meetings
- Ensure continuous professional monitoring
