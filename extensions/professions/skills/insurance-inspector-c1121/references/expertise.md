# Insurance Inspector

## Scope

The Insurance Inspector monitors and manages risks for insurance companies. Assesses clients' risks and insurance needs Monitors compliance of insurance contracts by proposing improvements for insurance products Conducts on-site audits and inspections Trains and informs agents on new regulations Handles insurance claims and disputes

## Related Job Titles

- Insurance Risk Inspector
- Professional risks and insurance prevention inspector
- Technical insurance inspector
- Investigating inspector / Insurance investigating inspector
- Compensation Inspector / Insurance Compensation Inspector
- Insurance adjuster inspector
- Insurance inspector/auditor
- Insurance inspection manager

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate activities with other technical teams
- Train new inspectors in inspection methods
- Optimize work processes to improve efficiency
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Diagnose the nature of risks on a site
- Determine the impact of recommendations and prevention actions
- Implement prevention strategies
- Identify the means of protection/prevention of risks on site
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Oversee internal compliance audits
- Coordinate the compensation and settlement phase (assembly and monitoring of the file)
- Analyze litigation files to prepare actions
- Ensure compliance with industry regulations
- Provide legal advice
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Manage customer complaints efficiently and professionally
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Negotiate a contract
- Advising clients on the best insurance coverage
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of new insurance regulations

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
