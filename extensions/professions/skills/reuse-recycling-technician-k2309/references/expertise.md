# Reuse Recycling Technician

## Scope

The Reuse and Recycling Technician plays a crucial role in waste management and environmental preservation. Collects and sorts recyclable materials from various sources Dismantles household appliances to recover reusable components Controls the quality of recycled materials to ensure their reuse Educates communities and businesses about recycling practices Manages the logistics and storage of recycled materials

## Related Job Titles

- Household appliance recycling worker
- Recycler
- Rejuvenate / Resourcière

## Activities and Skills

- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Optimize disassembly processes for parts recovery
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain waste collection equipment
- Repair equipment, a machine, an installation
- Evaluate the quality of materials
- Apply standards related to material recycling
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Recycle wooden products and materials, pallets
- Recycle products and materials made of paper, cardboard, plastic, rubber, glass, etc.
- Recycle hospital products and materials
- Recycle IT products and materials
- Recycle metal products and materials
- Recycle household appliance products and materials
- Sort products, parts, materials and direct them to recovery or elimination channels
- Analyze recycling market trends to optimize operations
- Manage documents relating to recycling standards
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital communication tools

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Specificities of solvents, diluents
- Characteristics of ferrous metals (steel, cast iron, etc.)
- Characteristics of non-ferrous metals
- Specificities of refrigerants
- Characteristics of electronic components
- Use of manual tools
- Use of machine tools

## Areas of expertise

- Characteristics of vehicle parts to be recycled
- Energy optimization of electrical processes
- Management of recovery systems
- Environmental risk management

## Standards and processes

- Waste transformation processes
- Regulations on waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE)
- Environmental certification procedures
- Depollution processes

## Professional techniques

- Component Reuse Techniques

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Prolonged standing
