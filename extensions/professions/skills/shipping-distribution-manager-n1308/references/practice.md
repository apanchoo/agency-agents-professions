# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Describe flows, volumes, delivery times and constraints.
2. Compare modes, routes, capacities and costs.
3. Prepare transport plan or logistics organization.
4. Check feasibility, traceability and hazard management.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Customs and logistics
- Warehouse management: in-depth
- Warehouses and logistics platforms
- Distribution, transportation and Supply Chain
- Measuring performance throughout the Supply Chain
- Forecasting and distribution planning

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Optimization of a logistics chain in a sustainable approach
- Optimization of a supply chain in a connected environment
- Execution of a production schedule
- Supply Chain project as middle manager

## Deliverables

- Flow diagram
- Costed transport plan
- Monitoring table and hazard scenario

## Acceptance Criteria

- Capacities and time windows are compatible
- Cost assumptions are sourced
- Regulatory and traceability constraints are identified

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
