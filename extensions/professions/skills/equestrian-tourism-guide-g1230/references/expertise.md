# Equestrian tourism guide

## Scope

The equestrian tourism guide or guide (ATE) prepares and leads rides and horseback riding independently on identified routes and between known stage relays. Ensures the safety of participants and the well-being of the animals Organizes and plans itineraries according to the levels of participants Manages the maintenance of equestrian equipment and equipment Provides cultural and historical information on the places visited May offer and sell additional services related to equestrian tourism

## Related Job Titles

- Equestrian tourism guide

## Activities and Skills

- Explain the conditions for practicing a sporting activity
- Evaluate participants’ equestrian skills
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Determine food rations according to the physical and physiological needs of the animals
- Educating equines with a view to carrying out equestrian activities
- Manage the daily feeding of animals
- Handle emergency situations and provide first aid if necessary
- Providing care for animals
- Preparing an animal for hiking and caring for it
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Monitor animal health before and after runs
- Distribute the animals in breeding premises or outdoors
- Maintain equestrian equipment in good condition
- Monitor equipment or sports equipment
- Ensure the safety of participants during the hike and courses
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Ensuring the safety and welfare of animals during transport
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Promote environmentally friendly riding practices
- Manage customer feedback to improve services
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Inform the client about the breeds and lifestyle of animals
- Raise visitor awareness about environmental protection
- Adapt routes according to weather conditions
- Managing an emergency situation
- Collect information on the duration, number of participants, and specific features of the tourist support service
