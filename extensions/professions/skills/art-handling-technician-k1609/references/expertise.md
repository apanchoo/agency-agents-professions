# Art handling technician

## Scope

An art handling technician is a professional specialized in the handling, movement, installation and protection of works (paintings, sculptures, heritage objects, contemporary installations, etc.). Handles and moves works of art using appropriate techniques and equipment to guarantee their integrity Packs and conditions the works according to their nature and fragility to ensure their protection during transport or storage Installs and uninstalls the works in the exhibition spaces while respecting the technical and scenographic instructions Ensures the loading, transport and unloading of the works in coordination with the specialized logistics teams Carry out or participate in condition reports in order to check the state of conservation of the works beforehand and after handling

## Activities and Skills

- Design suitable packaging solutions
- Identify the materials, supports and techniques of the works
- Establish emergency protocols for damaged works
- Assess the risks associated with movement and handling
- Maintain good physical condition in accordance with job requirements
- Carry out condition reports before and after handling
- Manage artwork inventories
- Installing and uninstalling works on display
- Plan rotations of works for exhibitions
- Complete administrative and regulatory formalities
- Ensure the traceability of interventions
- Complete the conformity control, traceability and quality monitoring documents
- Advisor on preventive conservation of collections
- Respect curatorial and institutional instructions
- Respect specialized transport protocols
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Collaborate with restorers for the maintenance of works
- Raise public awareness
- Work in coordination with managers and curators
- Document interventions and anomalies encountered
- Document interventions and incident reports
- Managing an emergency situation
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency
- Report any anomaly or damage
