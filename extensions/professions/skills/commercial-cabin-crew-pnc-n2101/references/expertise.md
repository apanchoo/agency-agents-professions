# Commercial Cabin Crew (PNC)

## Scope

With excellent interpersonal skills, demonstrating good listening skills, self-control, adaptability and very good team spirit, the Cabin Crew Member plays an essential role in the safety and comfort of passengers on board aircraft. Ensures a safety, security and first aid mission because it ensures compliance with these rules in the cabin on board the aircraft and applies the associated procedures if necessary; Carries out a commercial mission because he must be attentive to the comfort of passengers by guaranteeing the quality of the service provided to them.
