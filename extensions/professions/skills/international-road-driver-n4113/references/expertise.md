# International road driver

## Scope

The International Truck Driver is essential for the transportation and distribution of goods across borders. Transports goods from one point to another internationally Ensures the management of transport documents and customs formalities Ensures compliance with the road regulations of each country crossed Controls the loading and unloading of goods Maintains the vehicle in good condition and reports any anomalies Can plan routes according to deadlines and customer requirements

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Use navigation equipment (professional GPS, mobile applications)
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Load, unload, handle products
- Optimize a goods loading plan
- Organize alternative routes in the event of disruptions
- Ensuring the safety of goods during transport
- Driving a heavy goods vehicle
- Driving a vehicle requiring authorization
- Organize optimized delivery routes
- Arrange a delivery
- Transport goods
- Transport materials
- Carry out customs formalities
- Write a report, an activity report
- Optimize fuel consumption
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Argue in the event of disputes or inspections
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Inform and assist users of the road network in the event of breakdowns and accidents
- Communicate orally with customers and authorities
- Maintain clear and professional communication
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Respect delivery times
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Report any anomaly or incident during the journey
- Process traffic information in real time
