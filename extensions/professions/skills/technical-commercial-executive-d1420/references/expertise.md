# Technical-commercial executive

## Scope

The Technical-Commercial Executive, an expert combining technical and commercial skills to boost sales. Develops and maintains relationships with customers to understand their technical and commercial needs Proposes solutions adapted to customer issues based on technical expertise Negotiates contracts and sales terms to maximize profits while satisfying customers Ensures project monitoring and customer satisfaction after the sale Analyzes market trends to adjust sales strategies Trains and supervises sales teams on technical aspects of products

## Related Job Titles

- Sales engineer
- Business engineer / BtoB business engineer
- Technical sales engineer
- Technical-sales engineer / Technical-sales engineer in IT
- Technical sales manager

## Activities and Skills

- Promote the company's products or services during interactions
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Propose adapted technical solutions
- Monitor sales figures and carry out associated reporting
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Negotiate service prices, contractual conditions and delivery times
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Analyze customer needs
- Advise its customers on the best applications and the technical and administrative constraints of the products or services offered
- Provide quality service
- Adapt a sales strategy to a target or a product
- Conduct commercial negotiations: prices, deadlines, promotions and conclude the sale (purchase orders, sales contracts, etc.)
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop modifications to meet the specifications, current regulations and the expectations of the parties concerned
- Create a quote
- Establish a prospecting tour plan (targeting, contacts, preparation of technical files)
- Maintain effective communication with customers
- Master the services offered by the company, as well as the legal and regulatory framework
- Implement the company's commercial strategy
- Prepare and present application files for calls for tenders
- Monitor, manage and develop your customer portfolio within the assigned scope (geographic, sectoral, etc.)
- Analyze sales data to identify growth opportunities
- Optimize a commercial activity
- Ensure the link between its customers and the company's support functions
- Participate in cross-functional projects and provide technical skills and customer knowledge
