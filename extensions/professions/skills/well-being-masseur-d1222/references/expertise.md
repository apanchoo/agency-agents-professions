# Well-being masseur

## Scope

Well-being massage is personalized support, through touch, using a set of manual techniques. Implements a well-being massage session Promotes, maintains and optimizes the overall well-being of the person Offers a variety of well-being massages Adapts the well-being massage to the client's expectations and needs Manages and develops his professional activity

## Related Job Titles

- Well-being massage practitioner

## Activities and Skills

- Design and lead relaxation or leisure activities
- Provide personalized advice on stress management, posture and lifestyle
- Offer advice on well-being and lifestyle
- Adapt massage techniques to the person's anatomy
- Conduct a preparatory welcome interview for the wellness massage session
- Build and implement a wellness massage session adapted to the client's needs
- Contribute to a person's physical well-being
- Contribute to a person's psychological well-being
- Explain the techniques used, collect feedback, and adapt sessions based on client feedback
- Identify the client's needs and precautions or contraindications to massage
- Master different massage techniques to meet the varied needs of clients
- Master touch massage and its techniques
- Carry out personal hygiene, comfort and prevention care
- Use soothing music, scented candles, and essential oils to create an atmosphere conducive to relaxation
- Develop a massage location adapted to the client and compliant with ethics
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Demonstrate self-control, know how to regulate your impulses and emotional reactions

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

- Fundamentals of anatomy and physiology applied to well-being massage
