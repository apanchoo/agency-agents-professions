# Health, Safety, Environment (HSE) manager in industry

## Scope

The Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Manager in industry is responsible for guaranteeing the safety of people, the protection of health at work and respect for the environment within an industrial site. Develops and implements the safety and hygiene policy in accordance with current regulations Monitors and evaluates occupational risks in the company to prevent accidents and occupational illnesses Organizes training and safety and hygiene awareness campaigns for employees Supervises the installation and maintenance of safety equipment Conducts regular audits and suggests improvements to optimize environmental management systems Collaborates with local authorities and regulatory bodies to ensure compliance with environmental standards
