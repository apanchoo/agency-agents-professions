# Fishmonger

## Scope

The fishmonger is a seafood artisan who selects and prepares the best aquatic products for his customers. Selects and purchases fish, crustaceans and molluscs from local suppliers or fishermen Prepares seafood for sale, cleaning, filleting and presenting it in an attractive manner Advises customers on product freshness and best preparation methods Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards throughout the product handling process May make special preparations or ready meals to meet specific customer requests Manages inventory and supply to ensure the availability of products while minimizing losses

## Related Job Titles

- Auction employee
- Tide employee
- Fishmonger employee
- Fishmonger-caterer

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Cut the fish according to specific requests
- Cleaning fish and shellfish
- Open shellfish and crustaceans
- Prepare and serve seafood platters
- Prepare fish for storage
- Sort the fish and prepare them
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Ensure the freshness of the products on display
- Control the quality of products received
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Manage the supply of fresh produce
- Manage seafood stocks
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Monitor the temperature of storage areas
- Prepare special orders for events
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Advise customers on product preparation
- Analyze customer consumption trends
- Collaborate with suppliers for the selection of raw materials
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Set up or refresh the stall (ice, decoration), arrange the products and display the prices
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Offer a tasting to customers
- Comply with maritime regulations
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
