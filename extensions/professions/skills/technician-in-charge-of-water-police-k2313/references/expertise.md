# Technician in charge of water police

## Scope

The technician responsible for water policing contributes to the preservation of aquatic environments and the balanced management of water resources. Controls water quality and monitors samples taken from natural environments Applies and enforces regulations relating to water and aquatic environments Carry out inspections and audits on industrial and agricultural sites Participates in the development of water management plans and the implementation of environmental projects Informs and raises awareness among local stakeholders and the general public on water management issues Writes activity reports and intervention reports

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate with local authorities on water management issues
- Supporting project leaders towards compliance with environmental regulations
- Raise awareness among other State administrations or operators for better integration of water-related issues into their missions
- Analyze environmental data
- Analyze and interpret the results of permanent sensors placed on water networks
- Analyze water quality in relation to chemical and biochemical quality benchmarks
- Adjust operating or water sampling equipment
- Install operating or water sampling equipment
- Identify leaks and obstructions in a sanitation network
- Carry out remote monitoring and regulation of operating equipment (automates, valves, pumps, etc.) and the flow of rain, floods, etc. remotely
- Control a network or operating equipment
- Check a pipe
- Preventing diseases through appropriate health measures
- Carry out periodic security audits
- Ensure compliance of maintenance operations
- Control water quality
- Write water quality reports
- Liaise with environmental authorities on regulatory matters
- Inspect authorized projects, sanction illegal work and, if necessary, propose prosecutions to the prefect or prosecutor
- Ensure compliance with technical and environmental regulations
- Conduct awareness campaigns on water conservation
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing emergencies in the event of water pollution
- Use digital communication tools
