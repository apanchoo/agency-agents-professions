## Scope

Sharpens and maintains cutting tools to ensure their long-lasting use Controls the quality of tools and makes the necessary adjustments for their proper functioning Uses sharpening machines to make precise adjustments Respects safety standards when handling cutting tools and maintenance procedures to prevent accidents Conducts regular inspections of tools and verifies their optimal quality using measuring instruments
