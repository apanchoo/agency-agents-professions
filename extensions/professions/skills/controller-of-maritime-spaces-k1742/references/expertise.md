# Controller of maritime spaces

## Scope

The controller of maritime spaces participates in the mission of maritime defense of the territory and the action of the State at sea by ensuring the surveillance, control and assistance of maritime traffic near the French coasts. Is responsible for ensuring compliance with regulations and laws in force in territorial and international waters Ensures the safety and security of ships, crews and passengers, as well as the protection of the marine environment Implements integrated coastal watch systems Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out their profession

## Related Job Titles

- Coastal surveillance operator and maritime sovereignty zones
- Coastal surveillance technician and maritime sovereignty zones

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze, prioritize and arbitrate
- Communicate effectively with teams to ensure coordination
- Coordinate aero-maritime resources for public service missions at sea
- Working with civil and military authorities
- Implement integrated coastal monitoring systems
- Anticipate an event, a situation
- Implement engineering safety measures
- Detect, identify and track aircraft in airspace
- Understand and take into account marine meteorological phenomena
- Use navigation equipment
- Ensure proper maintenance and updating of nautical documents
- Know maritime space management procedures
- Know airspace management procedures
- Advise and support in maritime law
- Advise and support coastal law
- Manage alarms and warnings
- Communicate in English orally and in writing
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Managing stressful work situations
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Search for information
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
