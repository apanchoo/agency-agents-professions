# Manager in energy production and distribution

## Scope

The Energy Production Distribution Operations Manager coordinates and manages the operation of energy production and distribution systems. Supervises all activities to ensure efficient production or distribution of energy, in compliance with safety and quality standards Develops operational strategies with teams for technical problem resolution Ensures monitoring of equipment maintenance and compliance Collaborates with other departments and partners to align production objectives with market needs

## Related Job Titles

- Head of workshop in petrochemical energy
- Head of sector in energy distribution production
- Energy transport network dispatcher
