# Head of sales promotion

## Scope

The sales promotion manager designs, plans and implements promotional actions intended to stimulate sales of a product or range. Develops the promotional strategy based on commercial objectives (turnover, market share, product launch) Designs promotional operations (discounts, special offers, packaging, competitions, in-store events) Coordinates implementation with internal teams (marketing, trade marketing, sales force) and external teams (agencies, distributors) Monitors and analyzes the performance of promotions (return on investment, sales volume, impact on margin) Optimizes campaigns based on results and returns land
