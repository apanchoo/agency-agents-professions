# Training rider

## Scope

The Training Rider works under the responsibility of a trainer and participates in the physical and mental preparation of racing horses (gallop or trot) for competitions. Rides the racing horses in training according to the trainer's instructions Adapts the work according to the level and needs of each horse Ensures daily care of the horses (grooming, feeding, monitoring their state of health) Participates in the maintenance of facilities and equipment (saddlery, stables, training tracks) Observes the behavior of the horses and reports any problems to the trainer Respects safety instructions and animal welfare Accompanies and/or possibly transports the horses at the races

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in horse training sessions
- Compete in competition and adapt actions according to the directives of the coach, sports director
- Define a training project for the equine, independently or collaboratively, depending on the discipline and the objectives to be achieved
- Establish conditions promoting sporting performance and injury prevention (nutrition, recovery, technical-tactical work outside training)
- Prepare the necessary equipment for equine training
- Carry out physical preparation exercises
- Provide routine care for equines
- Know and evaluate the animals' reception conditions and environment
- Managing the well-being of equines on a daily basis
- Preparing horses for training
- Prepare equines for competition or events
- Carry out the breaking operation of a young horse
- Monitor the diet of equines to ensure their well-being and health
- Know the equine performance analysis tools
- Observe horse behavior to detect problems
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Maintain equestrian equipment in good condition
- Handle a mechanical or thermal machine dedicated to the maintenance of boxes and litter boxes
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Load, unload, handle products
- Assist in organizing travel for competitions
- Ensuring the safety and welfare of animals during transport
- Plan travel for equestrian competitions
- Select the material or equipment according to the competition and constraints
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Guarantee the safety of equines, property and people
- Communicate effectively with trainers, jockeys and horse owners
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Demonstrate endurance and composure
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Build your professional project in the field of horse racing
- Demonstrate good physical and mental resistance
- Participate in continuing education sessions
