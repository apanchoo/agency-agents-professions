# Datacenter Cybersecurity Engineer

## Scope

The Datacenter Cybersecurity Engineer is passionate about protecting critical infrastructure and securing sensitive data, playing an essential role in ensuring the resilience and security of datacenters against the most sophisticated cyberthreats. Ensures the physical and environmental security of data centers as well as their networks Decides, implements and monitors security and protection measures for data stored in the Data center Manages access and identities Ensures the continuous improvement of IT security systems by monitoring weak points in the protection systems and evaluating risk factors Manages security incidents and conducts forensic analyzes
