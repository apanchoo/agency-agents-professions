# Aquaculture worker

## Scope

The aquaculture worker is a sea “handyman”, a lover of fish and shellfish. Participates in fishing or harvesting aquaculture products Ensures sorting, packaging and, sometimes, marketing of aquaculture products Maintains equipment and installations in good working order

## Related Job Titles

- Shellfish farming help
- Aquaculture breeding help
- Oyster farming help
- Shellfish worker
- Mussel worker
- Oyster worker

## Activities and Skills

- Train new employees on breeding techniques
- Manage the daily feeding of animals
- Optimizing breeding space for animal welfare
- Providing care for animals
- Take a sample
- Monitor water quality and adjust parameters
- Check the submerged breeding elements and carry out the necessary manipulations
- Master the characteristics of a product or material
- Prepare materials for work
- Harvest the product of breeding
- Harvesting seafood
- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions
- Monitor the growth of aquatic animals
- Adjust the level of a body of water
- Define the perimeter of an intervention zone
- Immerse a pool or pond
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Clean equipment and breeding areas
- Ensure compliance with health and safety standards
- Load, unload, handle products
- Packaging products
- Move and store products and materials according to quality and safety standards
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Store a product
- Arrange the transport of animals for sale or transfer
- Promote sustainable breeding practices
- Identify the presence of pests and note the nature and extent of the damage
- Monitor weather conditions that may affect livestock
- Use environmentally friendly fishing techniques
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Maintenance of aquaculture equipment
- Thermal regulation of pools
- Lifting equipment
- Navigation and geolocation systems (professional GPS, mobile apps)

## Areas of expertise

- Animal Physiology
- Breeding methods
- Animal nutrition

## Standards and processes

- Water quality monitoring
- Health standards
- Storage arrangements for food products

## Professional techniques

- Manual packaging techniques
- Water treatment techniques
- Filtration techniques
