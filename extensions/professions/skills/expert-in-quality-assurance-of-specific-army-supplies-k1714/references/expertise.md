# Expert in quality assurance of specific army supplies

## Scope

The expert in quality assurance of specific army supplies pilots, manages and coordinates expertise, studies, analyzes and ensures technical monitoring of the design, production and manufacturing in the field of clothing or food. Participates in the management of the field's policy Ensures the conformity of supplies or food as part of the supply quality assurance (AQF) process Uses the regulations and case law of public procurement
