# Community manager

## Scope

The virtual community facilitator energizes and moderates exchanges between users on digital platforms. Leads discussions within online communities to foster a positive and constructive atmosphere Creates and shares relevant and attractive content to maintain the interest of community members Monitors web trends and adapts community activities accordingly to stay up to date Manages crises and conflicts within the community to maintain a healthy and respectful environment
