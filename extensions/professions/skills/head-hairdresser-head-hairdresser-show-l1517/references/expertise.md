# Head hairdresser / Head hairdresser show

## Scope

The Head Hairdresser is an artist and technician who enhances appearances on stage. Designs and creates hairstyles adapted to the characters and the requirements of the shows Collaborates closely with directors, costume designers and makeup artists to harmonize looks according to projects Supervises a team of hairstylists to ensure consistency and quality of services Manages the inventory of hairstyling products, ensuring their quality and safety Stays at the forefront of hairstyling trends to offer innovative creations Can train apprentices and new team members in specific show techniques.
