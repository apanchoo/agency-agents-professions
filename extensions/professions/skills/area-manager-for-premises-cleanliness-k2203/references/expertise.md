# Area manager for premises cleanliness

## Scope

Guarantor of cleanliness and quality of services, the sector manager organizes, supervises and controls the teams in the field. Supervises and coordinates the cleaning teams to ensure the cleanliness of the premises Controls the quality of the work carried out and ensures compliance with hygiene standards Manages the schedules and assignments of cleaning staff Ensures the training and monitoring of new employees in cleaning techniques and standards Can produce quotes and conduct commercial negotiations
