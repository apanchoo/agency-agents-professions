# Workshop team leader in dismantling and recycling of ELVs (end of life vehicles)

## Scope

The ELV dismantling and recycling workshop manager carries out diagnostic and security operations for the sensitive components of an end-of-life vehicle (ELV). Provides technical support to his team and contributes to the development of skills Organizes and coordinates work according to the objectives set and the regulations in force Pilots the treatment of ELVs by prioritizing interventions according to commercial needs and fleet flows Can define or optimize dismantling procedures Also manages materials and parts from the workshop, ensuring the recovery of waste and elements intended for reuse, recycling or recovery

## Related Job Titles

- Head of automobile recycling dismantling workshop
- Workshop team leader in automobile recycling dismantling
- Team leader in automobile recycling dismantling
