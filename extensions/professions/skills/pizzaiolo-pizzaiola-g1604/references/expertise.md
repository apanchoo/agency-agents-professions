# Pizzaiolo / Pizzaiola

## Scope

The pizza chef prepares and cooks pizzas according to traditional recipes and the establishment's standards. Prepares the dough, assembles and cooks culinary products according to the rules of the art Manages stocks of ingredients and ensures the freshness of the products Informs about the composition of the prepared pizzas Can take customer orders and manage deliveries Can manage a fast food business specializing in pizzas

## Related Job Titles

- Pizza preparer-seller

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Adapt recipes for specific needs (allergies, diets, preferences)
- Bake a pizza in the oven
- Develop new recipes and flavors by testing different combinations of ingredients
- Form a pizza dough
- Manage cooking time for each pizza
- Maintain oven temperature consistently
- Handle kitchen equipment
- Mixing culinary products and ingredients
- Prepare pizzas while respecting hygiene standards
- Prepare dishes using local products
- Knead dough manually or mechanically
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Clean your workstation after use
- Check the quality of ingredients before use
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Organize order processing
- Perform daily activity reports
- Proceed to cashout
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Handle complaints professionally
- Apply eco-gestures in your practice
- Manage waste recycling after cleaning
- Communicate with customers for specific requests
- Take a customer order
- Give the order to the customer (plate service, take-out packaging, tray, etc.)
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and adjust services
- Optimize the assembly of ingredients for a neat presentation
- Present and decorate the preparations according to the instructions
- Sell a service or product
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
