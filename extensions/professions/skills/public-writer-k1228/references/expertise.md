# Public writer

## Scope

The Public Writer, a craftsman of words at the service of all. Writes administrative, personal or professional documents for individuals or businesses Assists clients in formulating their requests or correspondence Offers correction, revision and formatting services for texts Can lead writing or reading workshops Offers advice in written communication and structuring of documents Contributes to the preservation of access to information and written culture

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Support people in understanding and writing forms or written procedures
- Advise clients on the formulation, structure and presentation of their ideas
- Conduct an interview, an interview, an audition
- Promote your activity as an author and your work among professionals and the public (meetings, workshops, debates, etc.)
- Write correspondence on behalf of individuals or businesses
- Write communication materials on behalf of individuals or companies
- Design a writing project
- Promote a work
- Proofread and correct the proof before printing the work or document
- Write letters, administrative documents, stories or personal texts for clients
- Write administrative documents on behalf of individuals or businesses
- Manage copyright and intellectual property
- Find out about the administrative, legal or social rules necessary for drafting certain documents
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Adapt and prepare a piece of writing
- Adapt the tone and level of language according to the audience and the type of document
- Promote your services via communication media or an online presence
- Respond to requests within reasonable deadlines and manage several requests in parallel
- Use social networks
- Analyze the information provided in order to produce clear and precise text
- Ensure the confidentiality of exchanges and information
- Develop a general culture and advanced mastery of the French language
- Manage document writing deadlines
- Rewrite or correct documents to improve their style, consistency and spelling
- Structuring, synthesizing information
- Use digital tools
- Organize and develop your professional network
