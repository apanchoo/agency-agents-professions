# Educational Director

## Scope

The Educational Director manages the educational and teaching strategy of an institution. Defines educational guidelines and supervises their implementation Ensures the management and development of training programs Coordinates and leads the teaching teams Controls the quality of the teaching provided and its adequacy with the objectives set Participates in the promotion of the establishment and its academic influence May intervene in the administrative and financial management of the establishment

## Related Job Titles

- Educational Technical Director
- Training center manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate project teams to achieve objectives
- Evaluate the impact of training on performance
- Organize the work of a team
- Supervise the educational activities of a team
- Ensure liaison between trainers and learners
- Define skills development needs
- Define educational resources
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Promote the integration of new technologies into programs
- Organize and manage a training program
- Organize the implementation of educational orientation processes
- Implement communication actions
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Ensure training monitoring and trace it in the educational monitoring tools available
- Design a training evaluation process
- Control the administrative and financial compliance of training actions
- Implement a training evaluation process
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Implement marketing strategies to promote the establishment
- Design an educational project
- Define and prioritize priorities based on set objectives
- Develop partnerships with other educational structures
- Supervise the administrative and financial management of an establishment
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Present and promote training
- Adapt training methods to specific needs
