# Responsible for preventive conservation

## Scope

The Head of Preventive Conservation ensures the proper conservation and preservation of the collections Develops and implements safeguarding plans for the works, objects, archives concerned Coordinates and monitors conservation-restoration campaigns Collaborates with restorers and scientists to develop suitable conservation solutions

## Related Job Titles

- Heritage preservation officer
- Collection Manager

- Organize continuing training for staff
- Apply solutions for heritage conservation
- Conduct study and research work
- Implementing innovative technologies in conservation
- Analyze the state of conservation of objects
- Analyze climate data
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Develop appropriate conservation protocols
- Choosing the Appropriate Materials for Restoration
- Using sustainable materials in conservation
- Collaborate with art experts for restoration
- Optimize storage space for preservation
- Restoring an item of heritage or a collection
- Evaluate the risks linked to the exposure of objects
- Propose preventive measures for conservation
- Periodically evaluate conservation methods
- Negotiate with material suppliers
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Advisor on preventive conservation of collections
- Study and evaluate works, pieces, collectibles
- Manage budgets allocated to conservation
- Write detailed reports on the status of collections
- Ensure compliance with industry regulations
- Define priorities for preventive conservation interventions
- Negotiate partnerships with research institutions
- Identify heritage, collections and control the movement of works (inventory, entries, exits, etc.)
- Search for financing, investors
- Search for grants for conservation projects
- Adapting conservation strategies to new research
- Document the conservation techniques used
- Manage technical documentation and project archives
