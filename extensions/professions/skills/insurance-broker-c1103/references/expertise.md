# Insurance broker

## Scope

The Insurance Broker mediates between a client and the insurance company that meets their needs. Prospects the insurance market as an independent trader Negotiates contracts on behalf of policyholders and advises them in the event of a claim Monitors the profitability of its portfolio Develops and leads a team of employees

## Related Job Titles

- Insurance or reinsurance broker

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Ensure personnel management
- Design visual communication supports
- Develop the specifications for the risk to be covered or the coverage
- Monitor developments in the insurance market (players, products)
- Feed and update the customer and prospect database, classify and archive files and documents
- Advise on the choice of bank based on the rates and rewards offered (rate, insurance, maturity, etc.)
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Negotiate terms of collaboration with insurance companies
- Apply and enforce legislation in the commercial services of one or more entities
- Advising companies on contract management
- Negotiate with suppliers
- Negotiate the adjustment of the contract conditions until its subscription
- Present the clauses of a contract
- Represent the interests of your client when settling a claim
- Analyze members’ needs
- Analyze a client's risks and determine their need for insurance
- Advisor in the context of a commercial relationship
- Monitor the contract and ensure customer satisfaction is maintained
- Develop a customer and prospect portfolio
- Develop a network of business providers
- Develop modifications to meet the specifications, current regulations and the expectations of the parties concerned
- Manage and lead a network of business providers
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Use negotiation and sales techniques for insurance products
- Present a commercial offer to a customer
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Develop a business creation project
- Mobilize a strategic vision and anticipation
- Monitor the evolution of its customer portfolio (volume, specificities, profitability, etc.)
- Ensure the interface between the insured and the insurer after the occurrence of a loss
- Demonstrate diplomacy and strength of conviction
- Demonstrate commercial acumen and interpersonal skills
- Master business English in an international context
- Negotiate a proposal
- Maintain accurate and up-to-date documentation

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy

## Areas of expertise

- Characteristics of insurance products
- Customer relationship management (CRM)
- Natural disaster risk coverage
- Insurance market and players (companies, intermediaries, products and services offered, etc.)
- Mastery of IT (Internet, email) and office tools (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Insurance
- Characteristics of reinsurance products
- Health coverage, insurance
- Financial risk coverage

- Civil Liability insurance regulations
- Regulations on property and casualty insurance (Fire, Accidents, Miscellaneous Risks)
- Typology of environmental and health risks
- Regulation of industrial risk insurance
- Insurance law and regulation
- Home insurance regulations
- Automobile insurance regulations
