# Technical framework of the bank

## Scope

The bank's technical framework monitors financial markets and optimizes banking operations with technical specializations. Analyzes and evaluates financial risks and opportunities Advises on financial products and manages investments for clients Verifies compliance with regulations and internal standards Contributes to technical expertise of IT systems and banking data

## Related Job Titles

- Bank branch manager
- Bank office manager
- Periodic bank office manager
- Regional bank manager
- Bank branch group manager
