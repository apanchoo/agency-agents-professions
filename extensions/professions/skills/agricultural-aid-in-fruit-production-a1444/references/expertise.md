# Agricultural aid in fruit production

## Scope

Agricultural aid in fruit production contributes to the cultivation and maintenance of fruit trees on a farm. Carries out pruning and maintenance of fruit trees and plantation Contributes to soil preparation and irrigation of orchards Helps with the selection and picking of ripe fruits for sale or processing May participate in the direct sale of products in local markets
