# Ice cream parlor / Cooler

## Scope

An ice cream parlor is an artisan or professional who makes and sells ice cream, sorbets and ice cream. Designs and prepares ice creams, sorbets and other frozen desserts according to specific recipes and techniques Selects and measures the ingredients (fruit, milk, cream, sugar, etc.) for the production of frozen products Uses specific equipment for the production of ice cream such as turbines or ice cream makers Respects hygiene and food safety standards throughout the manufacturing process Can create and test new recipes to enrich the range of products offered Can sell its products directly to the consumer in a store or a kiosk

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Develop new recipes and flavors by testing different combinations of ingredients
- Optimize ice cream recipes to improve texture and taste
- Prepare the basic ingredients (milk, cream, sugar, fruit, flavorings)
- Know how to choose and measure basic ingredients and flavorings
- Know how to use and maintain ice cream machines, ice cream makers, blenders, etc.
- Use ice cream machines to produce different textures and types of ice cream: ice creams, sorbets, granitas
- Controlling the temperature of freezing equipment
- Define supply needs
- Manage raw material stocks
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Collaborate with suppliers for the selection of raw materials
- Design attractive presentations for frozen desserts
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
