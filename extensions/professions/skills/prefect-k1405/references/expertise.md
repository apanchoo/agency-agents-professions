# Prefect

## Scope

The Prefect, representative of the State, plays a crucial role in the management of departmental and regional affairs. Represents the government and ensures communication between the central administration and local authorities. Ensures the application of laws and regulations at the local level. Coordinates state services and mobilizes the necessary resources during crises or major events. Oversees the implementation of public policies in security, education, health and economic development. Organizes elections and ensures that they run smoothly. Contributes to regional development and strategic planning.

## Activities and Skills

- Lead a debate
- Optimize the management of public resources at the territorial level
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Lead social dialogue
- Implement the transformation actions necessary for changes
- Ensure mediation between the different local actors in the event of conflict
- Explaining a government policy to the authorities and the public
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Ensure official communication in the event of a crisis or major event
- Manage crisis situations and coordinate emergency responses
- Supervise operations, actions
- Carry out operations related to human resources management
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Control the a posteriori legality of acts issued by local authorities (marriages, elections, building permits)
- Define and deploy a sustainable development policy
- Encourage and support local initiatives in terms of sustainable development and improvement of the living environment
- Ensure safety, health and public peace and implement relief operations in the event of an incident or natural disaster
- Lead actions and coordinate the means of decentralized State services or services abroad
- Develop and manage international relations
- Evaluate a public policy
- Facilitate the implementation of public infrastructure projects
- Monitor developments in the situation in the district or country and inform the Government and the French authorities
- Coordinate state services in a region or department
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Define or promote a local development strategy
- Develop partnerships with local businesses or institutions
- Establish agreements, conventions, contractual relationships on behalf of the State with local authorities, associations and local partners
- Mobilize and unite local stakeholders around common projects
