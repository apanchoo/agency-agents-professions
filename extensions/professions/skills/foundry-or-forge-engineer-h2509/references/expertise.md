# Foundry or forge engineer

## Scope

The foundry or forging engineer specializes in the shaping of materials, he designs and produces mechanical parts and or assemblies comprising molded and/or forged structures. Studies the feasibility of a product based on customer specifications and takes into account the person-machine relationship Designs foundry or forging parts Organizes and implements processes and means of production Supervises the manufacturing and molding of products Controls the quality of parts resulting from casting and molding

## Related Job Titles

- Engineer in the shaping of metallic materials
- Foundry Engineer
- Forge engineer
- Engineer in production of foundry and forging products
- Metal materials engineer

## Activities and Skills

- Support teams at the technical and methodological level
- Study and identify developments (functionality, profitability, quality, etc.) of components, products, equipment
- Use modeling and simulation software
- Adapt, plan and coordinate the phases of the production process
- Set molding parameters
- Design foundry or forging parts
- Make technical adjustments on numerically controlled machines
- Carry out heat treatment operations
- Act quickly in the event of machine failure to minimize production stoppages
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Diagnose a problem, a failure, an anomaly
- Supervise the metal smelting process
- Analyze metal properties for foundry
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Check the conformity of the first products manufactured in relation to the expected technical characteristics
- Ensure the application and compliance with hygiene, safety (wearing a helmet, etc.) and environmental (waste management, noise pollution, etc.) rules on the site
- Write performance reports
- Design and deploy the deconstruction, decontamination or recycling of a facility
- Promote recycling practices in production processes
- Analyze customer demand and identify the technology(s) to use as well as the different process steps
- Collaborate with development teams to adjust offers
- Negotiate metal buying and selling prices
- Monitor performance indicators, analyze and correct discrepancies
- Supervise the operation of materials processing and transformation facilities (control, adjustment, etc.)
- Document manufacturing procedures
- Comply with current safety and quality standards
- Verify compliance with operational rules, standards and procedures
