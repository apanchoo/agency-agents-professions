# Washer / Window washer

## Scope

A window cleaner is a person responsible for cleaning glass surfaces (windows, display cases, bay windows). Cleans the windows and glazed surfaces of different buildings while respecting safety standards Uses specific equipment such as nacelles to access windows at height Ensures the cleanliness of window frames and sills for complete work Can lead a team of window cleaners for large-scale projects Respects hygiene rules and uses ecological cleaning products when possible Manages the organization of your schedule and routes to optimize your time and interventions

## Related Job Titles

- Washer / Walk-in window cleaner
