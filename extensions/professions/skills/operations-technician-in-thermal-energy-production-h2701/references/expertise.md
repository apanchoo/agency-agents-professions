# Operations technician in thermal energy production

## Scope

At the heart of the thermal production sector, the Energy Production Operations Technician plays an essential role in producing electricity safely and in real time. Drives and monitors the installations in order to carry out the production program Carries out preventive and corrective maintenance of the equipment Ensures compliance of operations with environmental and safety standards Can carry out rounds on the installations Can intervene in the field at the request of the control room to carry out maneuvers or checks on the installations

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze a situation and produce a diagnosis
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Demonstrate an appetite for using digital software and new technologies
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Optimize processes to increase energy production
- Ensure remote start-up of equipment (from the control room)
- Control energy production parameters
- Proceed to stop or start an energy production line
- Adjust production machine parameters
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Write technical reports and maintenance procedures
- Repair equipment, a machine, an installation
- Manage emergencies and critical outages
- Supervise the installations and take the necessary corrective actions in the event of a malfunction
- Carry out control rounds and verification of installations
- Monitor performance indicators, analyze and correct discrepancies
- Inform in real time of possible changes to the production program
- Report on your activity
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Ensure technological and regulatory monitoring
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
