# Production technician in the paper industry

## Scope

The Production Technician in the paper industry supervises and regulates the production of paper and its derivatives. Optimizes paper production processes by ensuring high quality paper Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Optimizes production costs and energy consumption Respects and enforces safety and environmental standards Trains and supervises operational teams on production lines

## Related Job Titles

- Manufacturing technician in the paper industry
- Visitor in the paper industry

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate personnel interventions on machines
- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Train new employees in operational procedures
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Control and adjust pulp and paper manufacturing equipment
- Carry out the change of covering (felts, canvases, boarding cables, etc.) on the installations
- Adjust production machine parameters
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Diagnose technical malfunctions
- Determine corrective measures
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Monitor the flow and progress of products on a production line
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Identify anomalies or malfunctions in production
- Optimize production processes to reduce waste
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Establish production and incident reports
- Write detailed reports on the checks carried out
- Propose solutions to reduce industrial waste
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Analyze production data and adjust processes
- Optimize production costs
- Meet deadlines and deadlines
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Propose solutions to optimize energy consumption
- Ensure compliance with safety and hygiene standards on the production site
- Document quality and safety procedures
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events in production

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of measuring equipment and devices (tensiometer, viscometer, scale, sensors, etc.)
- Types of paper produced

## Areas of expertise

- Adaptation to new technologies
- Optimization of raw material flows
- Chemistry and physics of paper/cardboard
- Problem Solving Techniques
- Handling gestures and postures
- Effective communication with other departments
- Industrial electricity

## Standards and processes

- Paper quality control techniques
- Drying procedures (temperature, duration, etc.)
- Environmental regulatory framework
- Papermaking processes

## Professional techniques

- Adjusting machines for production changes
- Preparation and adjustment of machines before production
- Optimization of water consumption
- Use of machines specific to paper production
- Monitoring of production parameters on control screens

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- In the workshop
- In line or production island
- Handling a dangerous machine, equipment or tool
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Repetitive work or imposed cadence
- Daytime work
- Shift work (2x8, 3x8, 5x8, etc.)
