# Responsible for drug promotional information in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Drug Promotional Information Officer plays a key role in the communication and promotion of pharmaceutical products. Informs and advises health professionals on medicines and their uses Organizes and leads information meetings for health professionals Ensures scientific and regulatory monitoring of the products for which he is responsible Participates in the communication strategy and product promotion campaigns Collaborates with marketing and sales teams to optimize the promotion of medicines Respects the ethical and regulatory standards in force in the pharmaceutical industry
