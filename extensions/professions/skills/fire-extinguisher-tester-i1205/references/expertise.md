# Fire extinguisher tester

## Scope

The Fire Extinguisher Installer ensures the safety of people and property by equipping buildings with fire prevention systems. Installs and checks automatic extinguishing systems according to safety standards Regularly monitors the condition and operation of installed equipment Informs and trains users on the handling of safety equipment Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of systems Writes and submits reports on compliance with safety standards Can advise on the purchase of new equipment adapted to the specificities of the sites
