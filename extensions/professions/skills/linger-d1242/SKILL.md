## Scope

The laundry worker is responsible for washing, ironing and storing linen to maintain the cleanliness and hygiene of textiles within establishments. Sorts and cleans the different types of fabrics according to cleaning techniques Controls every detail of the linen to put it back into circulation in perfect condition Carry out alterations and small repairs and practices the correct ironing methods on textiles Manage linen stocks and ensure the inventory of cleaning products and the ordering of supplies Ensure the transmission of information between the teams using the textile

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Distinguish between textile raw materials and their properties
- Prepare an item for ironing
- Determine cleaning methods based on fabric types (dry wash, laundry, ironing, etc.)
- Handle cleaning and maintenance products
- Sort laundry according to the type and color of the items, the nature of the textile and remove unwanted objects
- Practice safe handling gestures and postures
- Check the quality of ironing on different textiles
- Manage stocks of linen and cleaning products
