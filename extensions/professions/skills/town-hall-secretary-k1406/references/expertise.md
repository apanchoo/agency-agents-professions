# Town hall secretary

## Scope

The town hall secretary implements, under the directives of elected officials, the policies implemented by the municipal team. Organizes municipal services, develops the budget and manages human resources. Can intervene in specific areas of expertise: town planning, civil status, social assistance, elections. May supervise or organize one or more services of a local authority. Manages and monitors specific files for the public (daycare, library, canteen, school transport, etc.) Develops administrative and budgetary documents.

## Related Job Titles

- Civil status employee
- Secretary general / Secretary general of town hall

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Design and manage a human resources management policy
- Manage human resources
- Prepare and write administrative and budgetary documents
- Plan the management of premises and equipment
- Write minutes of municipal or regional council meetings
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Prepare and monitor budget execution
- Carry out accounting operations
- Ensure the implementation of municipal decisions regarding town planning
- Prepare complex files including a legal dimension
- Write, manage and monitor public contracts
- Check the consistency and compliance with the regulatory framework of administrative files
- Integrate climate, environmental, economic and social issues into decision-making and project prioritization
- Assist and advise elected officials in their development choices (town centers, VRD, green spaces, etc.)
- Document a public issue
- Develop and implement public service delegation agreements and monitor their implementation (advertising, candidates, contracts, services, etc.)
- Implement action by public authorities within the framework of cultural, sustainable development, housing, youth and transport projects
- Carry out a territorial diagnosis
- Translate a political orientation into operational measures
- Welcome and provide information to the local population
- Develop and run a reception system for the population
- Ensure management of general affairs
- Design and manage a project
- Develop and implement municipal council decisions
- Evaluate the implementation of a project, an action plan
- Supervise the management of the electoral list and ensure the organization and smooth running of elections
- Broadcast municipal or regional council meeting deliberations
- Report the action taken to the elected official exercising political responsibility
- Prepare and format the mayor's administrative acts and civil status documents
