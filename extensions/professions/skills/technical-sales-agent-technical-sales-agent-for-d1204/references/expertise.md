# Technical-sales agent / Technical-sales agent for vehicle rental

## Scope

The technical-commercial vehicle rental agent carries out activities aimed at renting vehicles to private or professional customers, within a rental agency and takes care of reservations by telephone, by email or via the website. He welcomes and advises customers, checks the vehicles and collects them. He carries out the delivery and return of the rented vehicle. He also monitors the condition of the fleet and contributes to supplying the rental station with vehicles. He plans and monitors small repairs on vehicles and organizes the vehicle preparation activity.

## Related Job Titles

- Vehicle rental counter agent
- Vehicle Rental Operations Agent
- Vehicle rental company
- Motor vehicle rental company

## Activities and Skills

- Check the condition of the rental vehicle (bodywork, interior condition, fuel level, mileage) upon departure and return of the vehicle
- Manage and optimize the vehicle rental agency's vehicle rotation schedule
- Identify and inform your hierarchy about the vehicle supply needs necessary for the proper functioning of the rental agency
- Organize and plan repairs of the rental agency's vehicles according to the human and material resources available
- Carry out charging of electric vehicles
- Carry out the delivery of a rental vehicle to a private or professional customer
- Record and track rental vehicle reservation requests face-to-face or remotely (telephone, email)
- Carry out invoicing
- Create a customer file in the event of a dispute
- Establish and edit a vehicle rental contract
- Inform the customer in the event of damage caused following the return of the vehicle to the rental agency
- Inform the customer about the insurance conditions, general and specific vehicle rental conditions
- Greet the customer remotely (telephone, internet) or at the rental agency counter
- Conduct an interview to identify the expectations and needs of the rental agency's customer
- Advise the rental agency's client on peripheral services and products
- Present the features and operation of the rental vehicle to the customer
- Complete a transaction
- Propose a vehicle rental solution that meets the customer's expectations and needs
- Handle customer complaints from the rental agency
- Manage conflict situations between customers and employees of the vehicle rental agency
- Comply with current safety standards and regulations
