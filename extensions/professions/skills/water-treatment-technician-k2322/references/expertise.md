# Water treatment technician

## Scope

The water treatment technician plays a role in the management and treatment of wastewater. Controls and monitors the operation of the installations and equipment of the treatment plant Performs regular analyzes of water quality and adjusts treatments accordingly Maintains and repairs equipment to ensure their proper functioning Writes detailed reports on operations and treatment results Ensures compliance with environmental and safety standards Participates in the continuous improvement of water treatment processes

## Related Job Titles

- Wastewater treatment plant technician

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze solid, liquid, gaseous samples (water, sludge, waste) and record the results
- Define eco-industrial intervention procedures
- Prescribe technical solutions for operating new sites, improving equipment or networks
- Analyze water quality in relation to chemical and biochemical quality benchmarks
- Monitor inputs (organic waste) and outputs (biogas, digestates)
- Monitor water quality and adjust parameters
- Measure the pressure, the level of water tables or collection wells and check their condition
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Supervise the different phases of wastewater treatment (pretreatment, biological treatment, physicochemical treatment)
- Intervene in the event of a major breakdown to restore operation
- Carry out cleaning and adjustment operations on materials and equipment
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Monitor equipment or phases of winding, purification
- Monitor the flow of wastewater in pipes
- Evaluate the risks associated with the use of certain machines
- Participate in the design of risk prevention plans
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Plan the shutdown of equipment
- Monitor operating parameters of water treatment equipment
- Check the operation of equipment and installations, identify anomalies and preventive or corrective actions
- Ensure compliance with quality standards
- Control water quality
- Identify non-conformities
- Define a storage area
- Manage chemical inventory for processing
- Write water quality reports
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure compliance with health standards
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Provide expertise in the field of wastewater purification or sanitation
- Provide expertise in the field of waste treatment and recovery
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
