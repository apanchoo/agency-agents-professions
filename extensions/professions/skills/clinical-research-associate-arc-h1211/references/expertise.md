# Clinical Research Associate (ARC)

## Scope

The Clinical Research Associate sets up and monitors the clinical studies of a project, guaranteeing the quality of the data collected and compliance with regulations among the investigators and the study team. Involved in all stages of clinical development Plays a crucial role in the successful implementation of clinical studies, ensuring that procedures are followed rigorously, data is collected accurately, and the safety of patients participating in the study is ensured

## Related Job Titles

- Clinical research assistant in industry
- Clinical research associate in industry
- Pharmaceutical clinical research associate in industry
- Clinical research officer
- Manager of clinical studies in industry
- Associate pharmacist / Clinical research pharmacist
