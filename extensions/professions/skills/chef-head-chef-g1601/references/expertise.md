# Chef / Head chef

## Scope

The chef supervises and coordinates all activities related to the establishment's cuisine. Manages the supply of products and stocks Supervises the kitchen team Ensures the quality of products and guarantees food safety Regularly designs new recipes in order to build customer loyalty

## Related Job Titles

- Chef / Cooking workshop manager
- Head chef
- Chef-caterer
- Kitchen Director

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Anticipate longer periods of work by recruiting seasonal and temporary workers
- Delegate, empower
- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Identify needs and define the training actions to be implemented in order to develop the skills of your teams in the kitchen
- Arrange a position and working conditions
- Recruit and integrate new cooks with the establishment manager and restaurant manager
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Help organize special events
- Develop innovative restoration techniques
- Prepare dishes for service
- Develop low-carbon menus (reduction in the proportion of animal proteins, promotion of plants and seasonal products)
- Taste culinary preparations, adjust their seasoning and pay attention to the aesthetics of the dishes before serving them
- Propose and design new dishes, as well as improvements to recipes already created
- Analyze possible malfunctions present in the kitchen, and propose corrective measures to remedy them
- Control the quality of the production of your teams, as well as the proper use of equipment and materials present in the kitchen
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Carry out freshness control tests on food and preparations and take samples for the hygiene department
- Check the quality of products by meeting directly with producers
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Take an inventory of products or materials available in reserve
- Define supply needs
- Manage the supply of raw materials needed in the kitchen to avoid surpluses or stock shortages
- Order products and raw materials from different suppliers and farmers in your region
- Fight against food waste
- Monitor kitchen operating costs
- Monitor the operating budget of the entrusted area
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Design menus, menus and define dish prices
- Implement the company's commercial strategy
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate leadership
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
