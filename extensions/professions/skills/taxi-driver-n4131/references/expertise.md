# Taxi driver

## Scope

The taxi driver, an essential professional for secure and comfortable travel. Transports passengers from one point to another safely Ensures the comfort and safety of customers during the journey Manages routes to avoid delays and optimize travel times Maintains the vehicle to ensure its proper functioning Can provide tourist or practical information to passengers Complies with road regulations and safety standards

## Related Job Titles

- Taxi driver

## Activities and Skills

- Apply emergency procedures in the event of an accident
- Use communications equipment
- Discreetly observe the status of passengers
- Use navigation equipment to optimize routes (GPS, mobile applications)
- Use electronic payment equipment
- Perform basic vehicle maintenance
- Evaluate vehicle maintenance needs
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Check equipment, materials and vehicle
- Check vehicle fluid levels regularly
- Managing stress in busy situations
- Check the operation of safety equipment
- Loading, unloading baggage
- Ensuring the safety and comfort of passengers during transport
- Driving a taxi vehicle
- Determine a route based on delays and traffic characteristics
- Transport passengers
- Issue proof of payment
- Create an invoice
- Manage electronic payments
- Proceed to cashout
- Ensure monitoring of current regulations
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Communicate effectively with the team and clients
- Adapt routes in real time
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Ensure the confidentiality of exchanges and information
- Ensure a neat presentation
- Document journeys and daily receipts
- Respect the highway code

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate self-control
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Taxi
- Ability to maintain vehicle cleanliness

## Areas of expertise

- Emergency and first aid procedures

## Standards and processes

- Principles of eco-driving
- Courtesy rules and protocols
- Taximeter
- Vehicle on-board documents
- Regulation of private public transport of people

## Professional techniques

- Fatigue reduction techniques
- Managing complex schedules and routes
- Alternative routes
- Obstacle avoidance techniques
- Eco-driving techniques
