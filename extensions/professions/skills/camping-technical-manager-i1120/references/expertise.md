# Camping technical manager

## Scope

The camping technical manager supervises and coordinates the general maintenance of the establishment. Supervises a team of permanent or seasonal maintenance agents Plans and manages technical interventions on all buildings, rental accommodation, leisure facilities and green spaces Supervises the preventive and curative maintenance of buildings and infrastructures Guarantees the safety, regulatory compliance and operation of the establishment's equipment in conjunction with other services and service providers

## Related Job Titles

- Maintenance and green spaces manager in outdoor hotels
- Technical Center Manager
- Technical manager in outdoor accommodation

## Activities and Skills

- Anticipate longer periods of work by recruiting seasonal and temporary workers
- Coordinate the activity and planning of your team in a seasonal or permanent context
- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Motivate and train the team to improve customer service
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Distribute workstations
- Recruit and integrate new arrivals
- Train staff in service standards
- Create files (calculation notes, specifications, summary reports), write technical documentation
- Implement Computer Aided Maintenance Management (CMMS) software in order to prepare the monitoring of interventions to be carried out
- Carry out development work on accommodation and common areas
- Define a development or works project according to technical constraints and site plans
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Ensure customer safety by following appropriate procedures
- Build a preventative maintenance plan
- Check compliance of the site, installations and equipment with regulatory standards
- Define risk prevention measures
- Control the quality of the work carried out
- Check the general condition of the installations, the supply of materials and the cleanliness of the premises
- Continuously manage stock (products, foodstuffs, equipment) according to seasonal needs
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Check the safety of installations, aquatic spaces, play areas and access areas (barriers, safety devices)
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Implement risk prevention measures (signs, instructions, displays, etc.)
- Inform customers about security rules and available services
- Respond to guests' requests for information and needs during their stay
- Define a green space management plan
- Manage relationships with control and inspection bodies
- Implement and apply the establishment's CSR policy
- Coordinate interventions with external service providers
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Propose solutions to optimize energy consumption
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Report on your activity
- Raise customer awareness about eco-responsible actions (sorting, water, waste) and site preservation
- Adapt your organization and activity to peaks in traffic, seasonal and territorial constraints
- Ensure technological and regulatory monitoring
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Enforce the rules of hygiene, safety, accessibility and environmental protection
- Manage unforeseen events, hazards and last minute changes
- Respect the establishment’s quality standards

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Demonstrate leadership
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
