## Scope

The Baggage Handler in a hotel establishment plays a key role in welcoming and supporting customers in their hotel experience. Welcomes guests upon arrival and assists them during their stay Transports guests' luggage from reception to their room Ensures proper management of luggage in case of temporary storage Informs guests about hotel services and assists them when necessary Maintains the entrance hall and passage areas tidy and clean May carry out errands or external services at the request of guests
