# Pastry chef

## Scope

The pastry chef creates desserts and pastries following recipes and traditions. Supervises and coordinates the pastry team Manages stocks of raw materials and places necessary orders Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards Develops new recipes and participates in menu development May carry out demonstrations and participate in professional competitions

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Plan and adapt, if necessary, the team's activity, assign tasks
- Supervise the activity of a team
- Train new employees in baking techniques
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Innovate in creating new recipes
- Test new baking techniques
- Plan production operations according to orders
- Make pastries
- Measure out culinary ingredients
- Carry out the assembly and decoration of pastry products
- Carry out the necessary finishing touches on the finished product
- Develop recipes
- Prepare dried or fresh fruits (cutting, peeling, rinsing, etc.)
- Prepare a pastry dough
- Adjust cooking machine settings
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Organize or monitor cooking during one or more manufacturing phases
- Supervise cleaning and disinfection operations
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Ensuring the traceability of food products
- Control the quality of finished products
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Identify and process a customer request
- Analyze market trends to anticipate needs
- Collaborate with suppliers for the selection of raw materials
- Optimize production costs
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
