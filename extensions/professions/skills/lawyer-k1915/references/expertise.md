# Lawyer

## Scope

A lawyer is a legal professional who advises, assists and represents his clients in court. Represents and defends clients before the courts Advises clients on legal matters to prevent disputes Drafts legal documents and contracts ensuring legal compliance Negotiates amicable agreements between conflicting parties Conducts in-depth research on legislation and case law Can specialize in a specific area of ​​law to offer in-depth expertise
