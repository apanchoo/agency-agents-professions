# Constable

## Scope

The gendarme is a career soldier who ensures public security, maintains order and enforces the law, and can carry out a very wide range of missions (fight against homicide, kidnapping, pimping, cybercrime, extortion, armed robbery for example) Ensures the safety of people and property Intervenes in road accidents to provide assistance and regulate traffic Carry out judicial investigations to prevent and repress crime Participate in territorial surveillance missions and protection of institutions Assists and protects populations during public events or natural disasters Carry out security checks and prevention operations in risk areas
