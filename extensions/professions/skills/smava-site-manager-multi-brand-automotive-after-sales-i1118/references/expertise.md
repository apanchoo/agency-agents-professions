# SMAVA site manager (multi-brand automotive after-sales service)

## Scope

The multi-brand automotive after-sales service site manager organizes and develops the activities of a rapid automobile repair service center, taking into account both the commercial strategy of the brand and the local market. Supervises the activity of employees for the workshop and store part and facilitates exchanges between workshop and store employees. Ensures activities relating to the management, organization, marketing of products and services of a rapid vehicle repair service center

## Related Job Titles

- Head of automobile repair center
- Auto center director
- Director of multi-brand automotive after-sales services site

## Activities and Skills

- Conduct annual individual and professional interviews
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Manage the organization of work for employees of a profit center
- Develop and monitor the skills development plan
- Manage staff representative bodies (irp)
- Recruit a collaborator
- Identify gaps between actual and required skills
- Use sales and activity analysis tools
- Manage site equipment and tools
- Manage an activity quality approach
- Organize inventories to optimize stock management
- Monitor sales figures and carry out associated reporting
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Analyze the results obtained, the different margin levels and the reference indicators and ratios
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Apply labor law rules
- Handle customer or supplier complaints related to the after-sales of vehicles and the sale of associated products and services
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Ensure communication with customers and partners
- Ensure monitoring of customer satisfaction
- Build a corrective action plan to monitor sales and site maintenance activity
- Develop and implement a customer prospecting and loyalty plan
- Measure the results of commercial actions
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Implement a commercial action plan to develop the site's commercial activity
- Participate in the development of pricing policy
- Choose and use analysis tools (prospecting, results, penetration, margins, etc.)
- Define a change management strategy
- Implement the change management project internally and externally
- Propose investments for the purchase or renewal of equipment
- Monitor the activity of the site's workshop and store by analyzing the results obtained (margin level, indicator, ratio)
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Dealing with conflict situations between colleagues
