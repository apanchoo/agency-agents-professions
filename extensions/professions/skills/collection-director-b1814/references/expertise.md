# Collection Director

## Scope

The collection director manages and coordinates the work between the studios, workshops and product managers, from creative work to presenting the collection to buyers. Analyzes market trends to anticipate developments in fashion Defines the creative and strategic directions of the collections Supervises and coordinates the work of the creative teams Collaborates closely with the marketing and production teams Ensures the consistency of the collection with the brand image Can participate in the promotion and communication of the collections
