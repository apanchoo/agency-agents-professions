# Drone pilot

## Scope

The drone pilot, a profession of the future that combines technology and expertise in aerial navigation. Pilots drones for aerial photography, surveillance or delivery Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment to ensure their proper functioning Plans missions based on objectives and weather conditions Analyzes data collected during flights to extract relevant information Complies with aviation regulations and strict safety standards

## Related Job Titles

- Drone Team Leader
- Dronist
- Professional drone remote pilot instructor
- Civil drone operator
- Professional drone remote pilot

## Activities and Skills

- Actively listen and diagnose to build an adapted and personalized response in a responsive manner
- Record video footage on digital media
- Framing shots
- Take photos
- Analyze aerial images
- Analyze data from a study or project
- Use drone piloting software
- Configure and configure an installation, a machine
- Identify the operations to be carried out based on diagnostics and data provided by a drone
- Carry out aircraft inspection or maintenance operations
- Monitor a space, premises, location, installation
- Ensuring the safety of drone flights in populated areas
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Carry out a visual inspection of the machine to check its working condition
- Pilot a drone, in compliance with protocols and regulations
- Write an event report after a drone flight
- Using drones to inspect inaccessible structures
- Apply the legal framework for air transport and air navigation
- Comply with local and international regulations regarding the use of drones
- Collaborate with ground teams for specific missions
- Communicate effectively with aviation regulatory authorities
- Act quickly in the event of a drone malfunction
- Evaluate the effects of topography and air currents on low altitude flight
- Prepare and plan flights based on mission objectives and weather conditions
- Structuring, synthesizing information

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
