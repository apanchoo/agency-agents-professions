# Animal trainer

## Scope

The animal trainer specializes in the education and training of animals for specific performances, shows or activities. Ensures the physical and mental well-being of the animals under his responsibility Collaborates with veterinarians to ensure appropriate nutrition and care Participates in species reproduction and conservation programs Advises animal owners on best care and education practices May carry out demonstrations and training for the public or professionals

## Related Job Titles

- Falconer
- Army Falconer

## Activities and Skills

- Select animals according to their behavior or temperament and the activity to be carried out
- Adapt the choreographies according to the animals' abilities
- Know and evaluate the animals' reception conditions and environment
- Educate/train animals
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Periodically evaluate the animals' skills to measure their progress
- Teach an animal behaviors adapted to different situations
- Implement education or training actions for animals during daily activities
- Organize play and exercise sessions to stimulate the animals
- Providing physical and aesthetic care to animals
- Prepare and distribute food rations to animals
- Respect animal welfare
- Prepare an artistic event
- Carry out event communication actions
- Identify a sick animal and inform the stakeholders concerned (owner, veterinarian, etc.)
- Observe animal behavior to detect anomalies
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Clean and maintain animal facilities and equipment
- Ensure the safety of animals and the public during presentations
- Manage stocks of food and other supplies
- Document training sessions for detailed monitoring
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Act quickly in an emergency to protect animals and people
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Involve an audience
- Record data relating to animal health and behavior
- Respect safety instructions during activities
- Respect safety standards
- Build a professional network and collaborate with other animal specialists
