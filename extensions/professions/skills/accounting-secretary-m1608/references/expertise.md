# Accounting secretary

## Scope

The accounting secretary, organized and rigorous, plays the key role in the administrative and accounting management of companies. Ensures account keeping and prepares management elements Carry out closing operations and prepare the tax package Manages cash flow and relations with banks Carry out administrative monitoring of staff Prepares and monitors financing files and grants Assists in the preparation of meetings and writes reports
