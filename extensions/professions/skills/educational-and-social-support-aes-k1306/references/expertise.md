# Educational and Social Support (AES)

## Scope

The educational and social support worker (AES) carries out daily social interventions aimed at supporting people with loss of autonomy or people with disabilities, whatever their origin or nature. Participates in the design, implementation and monitoring of the individualized project Helps and supports people in daily life activities (preparing meals, traveling, maintaining the living environment) and in essential actions (help with washing, eating, dressing) Contributes to maintaining and developing the autonomy of the person supported Promotes the social and relational life of the person supported and those around them (support in social, cultural, leisure activities, assistance with procedures administrative, access to rights, maintaining social ties) Ensures the link with the entourage of the people supported and all professionals

## Related Job Titles

- Educational and social companion / Educational and social companion at home
- Educational and social support / Educational and social support in collective structure
- Medical-psychological help
- Geriatrics assistant
- Gerontology assistant
- Social Life Assistant (AVS)

## Activities and Skills

- Organize entertainment activities according to the participants and the context
- Support the appropriation of a current digital or digital tool by its users
- Provide emotional support to those supported and those around them
- Co-construct and adapt the socio-educational intervention mode with the supported person
- Advise the person or family in organizing their daily life activities
- Contribute to maintaining and developing people’s autonomy
- Discuss with the person their feelings (discomfort, satisfaction, etc.) and refer them to the professional concerned (doctor, social service assistant, etc.)
- Encourage independence in daily activities
- Regularly evaluate the progress of the person being supported
- Guide, support a person or family in their efforts
- Maintain and promote the person's social connection
- Accompanying a person while traveling
- Ensure a presence and a safe environment for those accompanied
- Contribute to a person's physical well-being
- Contribute to a person's psychological well-being
- Encourage the person to maintain appropriate physical activity
- Prepare, help with meals and encourage handling of cutlery
- Monitor a person's health
- Use appropriate technical material aids
- Ensure personal hygiene
- Maintain the living spaces and laundry of the person being accompanied
- Prevent risks and the safety of the person accompanied
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Contribute to projects for continuous improvement of the quality of support
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Encourage eco-friendly actions and reduce the environmental footprint
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate with those close to the person being supported
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Report on your activity
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
