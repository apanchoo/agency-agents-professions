# Chef / Tourism Product Manager

## Scope

The tourism product manager is in charge of designing, developing and marketing travel and stay offers that are attractive to customers. Analyzes market trends to adapt offers Designs and develops innovative and competitive tourism products Collaborates with local and international partners to enrich offers Manages the complete life cycle of tourism products, from design to marketing Ensures the promotion of products through various communication channels Regularly evaluates the performance of tourism products to ensure their commercial success

## Related Job Titles

- Tourism Product Manager
- Director of tourism production
- Organizer / Travel organizer
- Tourism production department manager

- Define a communication strategy

- Promote the company's products or services during interactions
- Use sales and activity analysis tools

- Control the execution and costs of a service
- Develop strategies to reduce logistics costs
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Follow the steps of putting together a tourism product
- Negotiate preferential rates with service providers
- Manage local and international partnerships
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Analyze tourism market trends
- Develop a commercial strategy
- Evaluate the performance of commercial strategies
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Optimize travel costs by negotiating with service providers
- Promote products to customers
