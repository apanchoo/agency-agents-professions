# Project management assistant in architecture

## Scope

The architectural project management assistant participates in the design and monitoring of construction, renovation, landscaping and town planning projects. Produces technical drawings and digital models Participates in the development of administrative application files (building permits, demolition permits, etc.) Participates in coordination with the various project stakeholders (engineers, contractors) Contributes to the administrative and financial management of projects in relation with clients

## Related Job Titles

- Landscape Design Assistant
- Architect Collaborator

## Activities and Skills

- Communicate effectively with all stakeholders
- Coordinate work with other trades
- Contribute to the development of skills in an informal setting
- Participate in technical and financial feasibility studies
- Create a digital model of a BIM project
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Check data compliance
- Create a technical drawing, plan, or map
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Create or modify diagrams, drawings, plans of works, installations, developments according to standards and changes in constraints
- Select materials taking into account their environmental impact
- Propose adapted architectural solutions
- Monitor construction sites and ensure the progress of the various lots (structural work, technical trades, etc.)
- Ensure monitoring of current environmental standards
- Estimate the costs and deadlines for an activity or service
- Negotiate with suppliers and subcontractors
- Follow a contract, check compliance with contractual clauses
- Promote the use of recycled or eco-friendly materials
- Analyze customer needs
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Participate in the development of administrative files (building permits for example)
- Prepare and present application files for calls for tenders
- Attend site meetings
- Organize coordination meetings with clients and teams
- Using English in a professional context
- Ensure technological and regulatory monitoring
- Manage a documentary database
- Use digital tools
- Regularly update your knowledge
