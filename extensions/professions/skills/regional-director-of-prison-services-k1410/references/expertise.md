# Regional Director of Prison Services

## Scope

The Regional Director of Prison Services plays a crucial role in the management and supervision of prison establishments in his region. Supervises and coordinates the activities of penitentiary establishments in his region Ensures compliance with legal and ethical standards within the establishments Manages the human, financial and material resources of the penitentiary services Develops strategies to improve the social reintegration of prisoners Maintains security and order in the establishments under his responsibility Collaborates with judicial authorities and other government agencies
