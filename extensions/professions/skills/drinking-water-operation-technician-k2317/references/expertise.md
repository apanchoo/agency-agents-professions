# Drinking water operation technician

## Scope

The Drinking Water Operations Technician manages and monitors water systems, ensuring healthy and safe distribution. Monitors and maintains water treatment equipment to ensure proper operation Controls water quality and adjusts necessary treatments to meet health standards Manages incidents and emergencies that may affect water quality Performs regular analyzes to prevent contamination or technical problems Informs and trains staff in operating procedures and safety standards Participates in continuous process improvement to optimize water treatment efficiency

## Related Job Titles

- Head of drinking water operation

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Coordinate with local authorities on water management issues
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Define eco-industrial intervention procedures
- Prescribe technical solutions for operating new sites, improving equipment or networks
- Monitor water quality and adjust parameters
- Master water distribution systems, fluid mechanics and water treatment processes
- Master water treatment equipment and technologies (pumping systems, filters, softeners, etc.)
- Measure the pressure, the level of water tables or collection wells and check their condition
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Plan the shutdown of equipment
- Supervise the daily operation of water treatment facilities
- Monitor equipment performance indicators
- Check the operation of equipment and installations, identify anomalies and preventive or corrective actions
- Analyze the microbiological quality of water
- Identify non-conformities
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Carry out physicochemical and biological analyzes of water
- Manage inventory
- Implement water recovery and recycling systems
- Manage production and operations management
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital communication tools
