# Director of tourist accommodation structure

## Scope

The Director of tourist accommodation structure manages all activities relating to hotel services in tourist establishments as well as in leisure, attraction or cultural areas Supervises the daily management of the establishment(s), including reception, service and maintenance Ensures compliance of the installations with safety and hygiene standards Manages staff, recruits and trains new employees Optimizes the profitability of the structure and develops commercial strategies Develops partnerships and relationships with local tourism stakeholders Organizes and supervises customer events and activities
