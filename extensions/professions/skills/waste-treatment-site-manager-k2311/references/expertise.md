# Waste treatment site manager

## Scope

The waste treatment site manager is a professional responsible for the management, coordination and optimization of waste collection, treatment and recovery processes in compliance with current regulations. Oversees waste collection, treatment and valorization Optimizes sorting and recycling processes to improve efficiency Ensures compliance with environmental regulations Develops strategies to reduce waste generation at source Collaborates with various departments to implement sustainable waste management practices Trains staff in waste handling and reduction methods

## Related Job Titles

- Waste reuse site manager
- Waste manager
- Responsible for waste in industrial company

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Organize the work of a team
- Write and update procedures and guides on waste treatment
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Define eco-industrial intervention procedures
- Prescribe technical solutions for operating new sites, improving equipment or networks
- Monitor inputs (organic waste) and outputs (biogas, digestates)
- Develop and implement recycling and waste reduction programs
- Examine methods of waste treatment, waste management and determine the best way to deal with them
- Plan and coordinate waste treatment and disposal
- Supervise the maintenance of waste treatment equipment
- Check the operation of equipment and installations, identify anomalies and preventive or corrective actions
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Manage inventory
- Develop reports and statistics on waste and its treatment
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Conduct internal audits to evaluate waste management practices
- Coordinate recycling activities on site
- Assess the environmental impact of waste management activities
- Inform and train on new energy technologies
- Propose solutions to reduce industrial waste
- Ensure compliance with technical and environmental regulations
- Provide expertise in the field of waste treatment and recovery
- Coordinate with municipal services for waste disposal
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
