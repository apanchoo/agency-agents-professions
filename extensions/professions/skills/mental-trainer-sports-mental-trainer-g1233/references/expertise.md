# Mental trainer / Sports mental trainer

## Scope

The sports mental trainer plays a key role in optimizing the performance of athletes. Evaluates and questions the psychological needs of athletes to improve their performance Contributes to proposing, testing and implementing mental preparation strategies adapted to each athlete Supports athletes in managing stress and emotions Works in collaboration with coaches to integrate mental preparation into the training program and make the coach/trainee performance relationship more efficient Organizes individual or group mental coaching sessions Regularly monitors progress and adjusts mental preparation plans

## Related Job Titles

- Sports mental coach
- Responsible for mental preparation
