# Automatic engineer in design office

## Scope

The automation engineer in a design office carries out studies and developments of installations or automated industrial systems, on control-command type applications, low current supervision (programmable logic controllers, man-machine terminals) or high currents (power electronics). Carry out adjustments, adjustments or commissioning of installations Can modify equipment according to changing standards Can coordinate a team

- Design instructions, procedures, specifications, study reports based on the results of tests and trials
