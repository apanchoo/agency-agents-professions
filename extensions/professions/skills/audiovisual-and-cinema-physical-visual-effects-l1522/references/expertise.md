# Audiovisual and cinema physical visual effects operator

## Scope

The physical visual effects operator designs and produces special effects for cinema, television or live shows, outside of digital. Collaborates with directors and art directors to align effects with the project vision Ensures safety on set when handling physical special effects devices May supervise a team of technicians for large projects Stays up to date with the latest technology and techniques in the field of special effects

## Related Job Titles

- Special effects technician

## Activities and Skills

- Train assistants on special effects techniques
- Design special effects for cinematographic or audiovisual productions
- Design sets and show props
- Design innovative mechanical systems
- Build special effects prototypes for demonstration
- Setting fire to pyrotechnic materials
- Carry out tests, experiments
- Carry out preliminary tests to validate special effects before filming
- Create a sketch or model of sets and accessories
- Analyze scripts to identify special effects needs
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Document special effects procedures
- Design electrical systems
- Design, manufacture and implement physical special effects such as fire, explosions, mechanical stunts
- Adapting weapons for a show, a film
- Adapt the special effects according to the directives of the director and other collaborators
- Dismantle and store decorations correctly after use
- Handle dangerous or fragile products
- Manipulate and maintain specialized equipment to create physical effects (snow, rain, explosions, etc.)
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine
- Select materials taking into account their environmental impact
- Assemble a decor
- Implement decorative elements
- Act quickly in the event of a technical problem
- Ensure compliance of special effects with safety standards
- Check the similarity of sets and props from the beginning to the end of the filming or performance
- Make technical adjustments in response to manufacturing issues
- Ensure the safety of the film crew and actors while implementing physical special effects
- Ensuring safety when performing pyrotechnic special effects
- Supervise the installation of the equipment necessary for special effects on set
- Prepare and launch special effects
- Plan special effects sequences in collaboration with the director
- Optimize special effects creation processes to reduce costs
- Negotiate with suppliers
- Propose ecological alternatives for special effects
- Ensure the sustainability and recycling of the materials used
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of new special effects technologies

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate rigor and precision
