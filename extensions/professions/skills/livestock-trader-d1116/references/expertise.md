# Livestock trader

## Scope

The livestock trader is a player in the livestock trade. Negotiates the purchase and sale of livestock Ensures the transport and welfare of animals during travel Monitors the quality and conformity of animals purchased and sold Develops and maintains a network of customers and suppliers Monitors market trends and adjusts sales strategies accordingly Complies with current regulations regarding the animal trade

## Related Job Titles

- Chevillard
- Horse dealer
- Livestock merchant
- Horse dealer

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure regular visits to producers to check the condition of the livestock
- Know the dietary and care needs of animals
- Handle animals in compliance with EHS rules and animal regulations
- Promoting animal welfare during transactions
- Know the techniques for boarding and transporting animals while respecting their well-being
- Assess a health risk and implement emergency measures (vaccination, closure of establishment, slaughter of animals, etc.)
- Master animal quality criteria (weight, age, state of health, etc.)
- Ensuring the safety and welfare of animals during transport
- Arrange the transport of animals for sale or transfer
- Organize the transport of animals from the place of breeding to the place of sale or slaughter
- Arrange a delivery
- Manage administrative and financial documents related to transactions (contracts, invoices, etc.)
- Maintain records of business transactions
- Know the health and veterinary regulations in force
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Negotiate prices with breeders, buyers or processors
- Inform the client about the breeds and lifestyle of animals
- Analyze livestock market trends
- Build and maintain relationships with breeders, slaughterhouses, exporters, etc.
- Develop a customer and prospect portfolio
- Negotiate livestock purchase and sale prices
- Manage the necessary regulatory documents (health certificates, traceability documents)
- Know the livestock market, the different breeds of animals, their characteristics, their needs and their market values
- Know the different breeds of livestock (cattle, sheep, goats, etc.)
