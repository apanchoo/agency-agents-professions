# Social reception agent

## Scope

The social reception agent facilitates access to rights and services for people in difficulty or in precarious situations, within social assistance structures. Welcomes, collects the request and provides a first level of information to the public Refers to the services, social or administrative structures concerned Assists people in the creation of administrative or social files Organizes and leads prevention and information actions Can intervene in specific areas such as family planning or women's rights Contributes to the improvement of reception and information systems within its establishment

## Related Job Titles

- Reception and social information agent
- Family planning information officer
- Women's Rights Information Officer
- Youth information officer
- Legal and social information officer

## Activities and Skills

- Assisting people in difficulty
- Ensure the link between users and external resources
- Advise users on their social rights
- Assess a person's specific needs
- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Guide, support a person or family in their efforts
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Emotionally supporting individuals in difficulty
- Interact with external bodies such as the Labor Inspectorate, Social Security and public health services
- Plan awareness events
- Update the display, the information made available to the public
- Organize a reception area
- Guarantee administrative follow-up
- Facilitate access to support systems
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Analyze user needs
- Facilitate the social inclusion of people welcomed
- Optimize the reception of different audiences
- Refer a person to bridging partners
- Lead a themed workshop
- Create a relationship of trust
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Coordinate with other services for social assistance
- Ensure the confidentiality of exchanges and information
- Facilitate access to rights through clear explanations
- Maintain a clean and welcoming environment
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Use digital tools
