# E-commerce manager

## Scope

E-commerce is a key player in online sales which boosts exchanges on digital platforms. Develops and manages a customer portfolio on the internet Optimizes product visibility on online sales platforms Analyzes purchasing behavior and adapts sales strategies Collaborates with marketing teams to create targeted promotional campaigns Ensures order tracking and customer satisfaction Can manage complaints and returns effectively

## Related Job Titles

- Manager / E-commerce manager
- E-commercial
- Commercial web
- Web seller

## Activities and Skills

- Lead a network, a community
- Support customers in using a product or equipment
- Train new employees on e-commerce tools
- Create digital documents
- Conduct online surveys and polls
- Manage online complaints responsively and efficiently
- Monitor virtual stocks to adjust offers
- Follow up on orders and deliveries
- Administer an e-commerce sale
- Track orders, invoicing
- Carry out a control of financial transactions
- Check the conformity of the recording of financial transactions
- Manage customer feedback to improve services
- Identify and process a customer request
- Analyze purchasing behavior on an online platform
- Analyze sales data to predict trends
- Provide after-sales service on digital platforms
- Interact with customers via instant messaging platforms
- Track a web audience
- Develop and manage relationships or partnerships
- Implement a web Analytics solution
- Negotiate with partners and sponsors
- Optimize online payment processes
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Analyze online community exchanges
