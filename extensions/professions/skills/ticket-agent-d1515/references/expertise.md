# Ticket agent

## Scope

A ticketing agent is responsible for the sale and management of access tickets (tickets, places, reservations) for cultural, sporting events and shows. Welcomes and informs customers about events, prices and availability Sells tickets in accordance with cash register procedures and reservation rules Collects payments and manages transactions (cash, credit card, online) Manages reservations, modifications and cancellations according to established conditions Checks the validity of tickets and resolves any problems or complaints

## Related Job Titles

- Ticketing manager

## Activities and Skills

- Use ticketing software and dedicated IT tools
- Propose alternative solutions in the event of stock shortages
- Check the validity of tickets and fare documents
- Manage payments and financial transactions
- Manage customer expectations during peak periods
- Inform, inform, guide a person on the services offered
- Optimize the reception of different audiences
- Optimize visitor flow to reduce waiting times
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Contract a service
- Present and promote a product or service
- Collect a customer's opinion and comments
- Update information relating to events or services offered
