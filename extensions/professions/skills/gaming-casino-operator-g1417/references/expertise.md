The Casino Games Operator manages all casino activities, including administrative, financial and strategic aspects in order to offer quality service and retain customers. Leads a team and supervises the daily operations of the casino Ensures compliance with legal regulations and safety standards in all activities Analyzes market trends to adapt the casino's offers and services Drives the commercial strategy to optimize the structure's performance Maintains relationships with regulatory authorities and partners
