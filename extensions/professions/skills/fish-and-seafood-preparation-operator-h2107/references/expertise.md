# Fish and seafood preparation operator

## Scope

The Fish and Seafood Preparation Operator prepares, processes and packages seafood for consumption, guaranteeing freshness and quality. Controls product quality and ensures compliance with hygiene and quality standards throughout the transformation process Works on specific machines for cutting and packaging products Follows recipes and procedures for the preparation of produced products Ensures inventory monitoring and supply management Can participate in the creation of new products or the improvement of existing processes

## Related Job Titles

- Cutter / Fish cutter
- Threader
- Fish Canning Operator
- Fish production operator
- Fish production worker

## Activities and Skills

- Take samples of materials or products
- Adjust machine settings as needed
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Clean, scale, gut and fillet fish according to final product specifications
- Open shellfish and crustaceans
- Prepare materials for work
- Prepare fish for storage
- Prepare fish for canning
- Follow recipes and procedures for preparing elaborate products (marinating, smoking)
- Processing raw materials of animal origin
- Sort the fish and prepare them
- Perform first-level preventive and corrective maintenance on its equipment
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Ensure the traceability of production batches
- Check the state of conservation of a perishable product
- Control the quality of raw materials upon receipt
- Carry out freshness control tests on food and preparations and take samples for the hygiene department
- Check the conformity of product packaging (tightness, weight, temperature, etc.)
- Load, unload, handle products
- Move and store products and materials according to quality and safety standards
- Carry out fish packaging operations: washing, sorting, packaging, labeling
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Carry out packaging of fresh produce
- Monitor the temperature of storage areas
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Monitor production processes to ensure quality
- Report on your activity
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Respect procedures, operating methods and instructions
- Meet production deadlines to meet customer requests
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach
