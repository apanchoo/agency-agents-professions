# Agent for finishing colors and leather and skin effects

## Scope

The color and leather and skin effects finishing agent enhances and sublimates materials. Applies surface treatments to improve the aesthetics and durability of hides and skins Controls the quality of colors and effects applied Respects environmental and safety standards in the treatment of materials Collaborates with designers and craftsmen to develop new finishes Performs tests and adjustments to ensure conformity of the final results May supervise a team and manage the supply of necessary materials

## Related Job Titles

- Skin finishing technician

## Activities and Skills

- Adapt the formulation of the color of materials (fabric, skins, plastic) according to the evolution of the components
- Collect, test, analyze samples, materials
- Design the shade formulation
- Carry out tests, experiments
- Carry out print samples on materials
- Process experimental data to adjust formulations
- Maintain formulation technical files up to date
- Use specific formulation software
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Adapt finishing techniques to different types of leather
- Configure and configure an installation, a machine
- Monitor industrial dyeing/coloring results and make adjustments
- Identify the type of preparation to be carried out on the supports to adapt the operating method to the materials
- Carry out pre-treatment or material tinting operations
- Select the raw materials to mobilize
- Dye materials and materials
- Analyze a malfunction or non-compliance
- Control the quality of finished products
- Visually inspect finished products for defects
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Organize order processing
- Optimize finishing processes to reduce waste
- Develop ecological alternatives in formulations
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Respect production deadlines
- Collaborate with other technicians to optimize work processes
- Communicate effectively with the production team
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events in production
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
