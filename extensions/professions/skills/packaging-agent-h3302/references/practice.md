# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Describe product, expected protection and logistics circuit.
2. Compare materials, shapes, assemblies and end of life.
3. Prepare the industrialization concept and scenario.
4. Define performance tests, conformity and material optimization.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Distribution logistics and transport
- Production and warehouse management
- Packaging control

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Ensuring the quality of packaging in production
- Carry out standardized tests on packaging

## Deliverables

- Packaging specifications
- Concept and nomenclature
- Test plan and environmental comparison

## Acceptance Criteria

- Protection meets distribution constraints
- Dimensions and quantities are checked
- Environmental claims have an explicit method

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
