# Ironworker in metal structures

## Scope

The Metal Structure Assembler is a professional in the assembly of metal structures essential to many construction projects. Assembles and erects metal structures according to plans and technical specifications Installs metal elements on construction sites while respecting safety standards Uses specific tools and machines to assemble the metal Controls the quality of the assembled structures and makes the necessary adjustments Can collaborate with other trades on the construction site to ensure coordination of the work Can supervise a team of erectors and manage the supply of necessary equipment

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate work with other trades
- Coordinate a team on an assembly project
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Identify the materials and the various appropriate welds according to the instructions, technical documents, plans, etc.
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Assemble elements of metal structures
- Assemble the heavy supporting structures of a work
- Develop solutions to solve complex assembly problems
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Carry out drilling, punching, nibbling, notching, milling, tapping operations
- Close a construction site and clear the work area (machinery, materials, waste, etc.)
- Carry out acceptance of work with the client
- Fix the elements of a metal structure
- Install metal structural elements
- Mount special structures
- Assemble or dismantle scaffolding
- Prepare the execution of the site
- Carry out fixing operations
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Put in place protections to secure the site
- Permanently respect established safety rules
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Control the quality of welds and assemblies
- Supervise the installation of structures and check their compliance
- Load, unload, handle products
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Handle material vertically at height, using winches or pulleys
- Prepare site reports with the works manager or the client
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency
- Use digital tools
