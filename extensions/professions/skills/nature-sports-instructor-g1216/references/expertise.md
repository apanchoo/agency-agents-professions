# Nature sports instructor

## Scope

The Nature Sports Instructor supervises and leads outdoor sporting activities, often in natural environments (mountains, forests, rivers, etc.) Organizes and leads sessions or courses for different audiences (children, adults, groups) Ensures the safety of participants during sporting activities in a natural environment (hiking, climbing, mountain biking, canoeing, etc.) Prepares the equipment, assesses the weather conditions and adapts the activities accordingly Raises public awareness of respect for nature and good ecological practices

## Related Job Titles

- Hiking guide
- Nature hiking guide
- Sports tourism guide
- Nature sport educator
- Sports guide
- Piroguier / Piroguiere

- Accompany groups on guided tours or outdoor outings
- Provide supervision for the child or a group of children
- Explain the conditions for practicing a sporting activity
- Organize sporting activities in a natural environment
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Preparing an animal for hiking and caring for it
- Define a communication strategy
- Create multimedia communication supports
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Monitor equipment or sports equipment
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Define risk prevention measures
- Evaluate security risks in different situations
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Establish an authorization or reservation request
- Ensure compliance with safety rules for property and people in the natural environment, in compliance with current regulations
- Evaluate personal protective equipment
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Identify and process a customer request
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Design a tailor-made trip
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop tourism marketing strategies
- Negotiate commercial terms
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Write one or more foreign languages
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Adapting to different cultural contexts
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- View and analyze weather data
- Managing an emergency situation
- Collect information on the duration, number of participants, and specific features of the tourist support service
- Transmit the values linked to the sporting discipline
