# Traveling caterer

## Scope

A traveling restaurant owner prepares and sells food by traveling from one location to another, rather than in a fixed restaurant. Prepares and sells meals in a mobile and often outdoor environment Ensures rapid, quality service to a varied clientele Manages food and beverage stocks to avoid stock shortages Keeps equipment and workspace clean and in compliance with hygiene standards Develops an attractive culinary offer adapted to its clientele Can participate in local events and markets to diversify its points of sale
