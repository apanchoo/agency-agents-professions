# Motorcycle mechanic

## Scope

The motorcycle mechanic carries out all preventive and corrective maintenance interventions on all types of motorcycles (thermal, electric). Carries out checks and repairs on the mechanical systems of motorcycles Carries out routine and periodic maintenance on motorcycles Implements a first-level diagnostic method on mechanical systems Works in compliance with health, safety and environmental rules

## Related Job Titles

- Motorcycle mechanic
- Motorcycle-scooter mechanic
- Motorcycle repair mechanic
- Motorcycle Sales Mechanic
- Motorcyclist

## Activities and Skills

- Support the appropriation of a tool by its users
- Ensure the updating of on-board software in vehicles
- Install comfort and safety equipment and accessories on motorcycles
- Prepare and assemble new and used motorcycles
- Ensure the maintenance of workshop tools and equipment
- Change or repair engines, gearboxes, clutches, suspensions, starters
- Check the status of a utility battery (<60 volts DC)
- Checking the state of charge and aging of a vehicle's traction battery
- Check the technical conformity of a vehicle
- Visually check the charging elements of an electric and/or hybrid vehicle
- Maintain and check clutches and transmissions
- Maintain the mechanisms of a motorized vehicle
- Bring the electrical harnesses back into conformity
- Replace and repair tires
- Replace a starting and service battery (<60 volts DC)
- Carry out maintenance on the cycle part of the motorcycle (forks, paralever, suspensions and shock absorbers)
- Diagnose braking systems
- Carry out transmission diagnostics
- Carry out the diagnosis and maintenance of the cycle part of the motorcycle (forks, paralever, suspensions and shock absorbers)
- Carry out the diagnosis and maintenance of heat engines and their electrical circuits
- Carry out the diagnosis and restoration of conformity of heat engines and their peripheral circuits
- Carry out maintenance operations on the ignition, air, fuel and engine exhaust circuits
- Carry out maintenance operations on lubrication circuits
- Carry out maintenance operations on tires, shock absorbers and suspensions
- Carry out maintenance operations on electrical, safety and comfort elements of old and historic vehicles
- Carry out maintenance operations on the hydraulic and mechanical braking system
- Carry out maintenance operations on the transmission system (clutch, gearbox, transmission chain)
- Carry out routine maintenance operations (draining, replacing filters, etc.)
- Repair and replace defective parts
- Repair the ground connection and steering of mopeds, scooters and motorcycles
- Repair the thermal or electric motorization of mopeds, scooters and motorcycles
- Repair the transmission of mopeds, scooters and motorcycles
- Repairing the braking of mopeds, scooters and motorcycles
- Repairing safety equipment on mopeds, scooters and motorcycles
- Use diagnostic tools to identify motorcycle problems
- Check and replace brake systems and fluids
- Apply standards relating to the prevention of electrical risks
- Maintain sufficient stock for current operations
- Manage spare parts orders
- Carry out road tests to check the proper functioning of vehicles after intervention
- Estimate the costs and deadlines for an activity or service
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Ensure compliance with technical and environmental regulations
- Welcome and advise customers on necessary interventions
- Advise customers on necessary maintenance and repair options
- Complete a transaction
- Develop and adapt a commercial proposal
- Explain the diagnostics and repairs carried out
- Provide detailed quotes and cost explanations
- Present and promote a product or service
- Explain in a clear and educational manner
- Complete a repair order to ensure traceability of the work carried out
- Respect safety standards during interventions
