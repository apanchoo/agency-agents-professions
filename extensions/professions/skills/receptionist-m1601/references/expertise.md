# Receptionist

## Scope

Warm and professional, you are the first point of contact in many organizations. Welcomes, directs and informs the public on site or by telephone Manages the telephone switchboard, takes messages and transfers calls Registers visitors and issues access badges Organizes the agenda of appointments and meeting rooms Ensures the management of incoming and outgoing mail, packages May be required to manage basic administrative tasks

## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Monitor entrance security systems
- Analyze office supply needs and manage supplies
- Monitor deliveries and shipments
- Organize the meeting agenda
- Carries out administrative processing of files (typing letters, formatting documents, etc.)
- Liaise with maintenance departments
- Guarantee administrative follow-up
- Ensure the management of complaints and claims
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Provide general information about the company
- Manage telephone reception
- Manage incoming and outgoing phone calls
- Identify and process a customer request
- Contribute to the continuous improvement of reception procedures
- Maintain clear and professional communication
- Relay information
- Write professional messages and emails
- Ensure the confidentiality of exchanges and information
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Use digital tools

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
