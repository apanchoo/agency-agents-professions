# Switchboard operator

## Scope

The Switchboard Operator, an essential role in communication and the first impression of the company's customers. Answers incoming calls and directs callers to the appropriate departments Supports the management of telephone messages for the company's various departments May be required to perform simple administrative tasks such as making appointments or managing the calendar Maintains contact information and internal mailing lists up to date

## Related Job Titles

- Bilingual switchboard operator

## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Update the display, the information made available to the public
- Maintain an up-to-date contact database
- Update a file, a database
- Provide a daily report of calls handled
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Manage incoming and outgoing phone calls
- Identify and process a customer request
- Save precise messages for recipients
- Relay information
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Use digital tools

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
