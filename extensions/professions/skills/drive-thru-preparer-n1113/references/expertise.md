# Drive-thru preparer

## Scope

The Drive Picker prepares customer orders. Verifies drive-thru orders Prepares selected products accurately and quickly Ensures optimal packaging and organization of products for pickup Manages inventory and reports stock discrepancies Optimizes preparation routes to reduce customer waiting time Provides quality customer service when picking up orders

## Related Job Titles

- Drive-thru employee

## Activities and Skills

- Adapt working methods to changing demands
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Organize storage in a storage space
- Managing stress in busy situations
- Monitor the condition of material handling equipment
- Anticipate replenishment needs
- Load, unload, handle products
- Manage inventory
- Participate in an inventory
- Pick up the products according to the order preparation instructions and put together the packages, batches, etc.
- Use handling equipment safely
- Organize order processing
- Resolve minor logistical issues
- Use computer systems for order management
- Perform daily activity reports
- Inform the customer about the conditions for completing an order
- Manage merchandise returns
- Analyze customer needs
- Communicate effectively with the team to ensure consistency of work
- Working in a group, in a network
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Respect delivery times
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Follow safety procedures when handling products

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of drive-specific software
- Optical barcode reading devices (gun, flasher, etc.)
- Non-motorized handling equipment (pallet truck, hand truck, etc.)
- Food products

## Standards and processes

- Storage plan reading
- Monitoring product sorting procedures
- Adaptability to order changes
- Optimized storage techniques

## Professional techniques

- Planning collection routes

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Just in time
- Daytime work
- Work on Sunday
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity

## Sectors

- Industry - Food
- Logistics and transportation
