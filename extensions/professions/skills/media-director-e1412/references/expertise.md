# Media Director

## Scope

The Media Director builds a company's overall communications strategy across different media channels. Defines and implements media strategy in accordance with general company policy Negotiates and purchases advertising space across various media Optimizes advertising campaigns to ensure the best return on investment Works closely with marketing and communications teams to align objectives Analyzes campaign performance and adjusts strategies accordingly Supervises and trains a team of media professionals

## Related Job Titles

- Head of advertising space purchasing department
- Director of media purchasing
- Media manager
