# Installer of electric charging stations

## Scope

The installer of electric charging stations plays an essential role for sustainable mobility. Installs and configures charging stations for electric vehicles according to safety standards Performs diagnostics to ensure the proper functioning of installations Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Advises customers on the choice and optimal use of charging stations Can coordinate large-scale installation projects Respects environmental standards and contributes to the promotion of low-carbon mobility

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Train users to use charging stations
- Inform customers about the conditions of use of the equipment
- Follow technological developments in the sector
- Analyze terminal consumption data to optimize their use
- Interpret electrical diagrams
- Update technical documentation and intervention reports
- Define the layout of equipment elements
- Propose solutions to improve the efficiency of installations
- Adapt the installations to the specificities of customer sites
- Configure and configure an installation, a machine
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Install electric charging stations in accordance with safety standards
- Organize the installation schedule for charging stations
- Connect equipment connected to high current and low current
- Carry out preventive maintenance of charging stations
- Checking the operation of a charging station after installation
- Manage spare parts inventory for charging stations
- Manage stocks of necessary materials
- Plan interventions based on customer availability
- Advise on best practices in energy saving
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Monitor performance indicators of installed terminals
- Explain technical choices to customers
- Propose solutions to optimize energy consumption
- Inform users of the security rules to follow
- Comply with current safety and quality standards
- Respect environmental standards when installing terminals
