# Postman / Postwoman

## Scope

The Postman provides an essential connection between individuals and organizations. Delivers mail and packages to residential and commercial addresses according to a predefined route Ensures the collection of letters and packages to be placed in mailboxes or at the post office Ensures delivery times are respected and the quality of customer service Offers additional services such as the sale of stamps, registration of registered letters and management of complaints Maintains a good relationship with customers by providing precise information and answering their questions Makes rounds by vehicle, by bike or on foot, whatever the conditions weather

## Related Job Titles

- Messaging distributor
- Sector postman
- Distributor receiver / Distributor receiver for La Poste
