# Boilermaker

## Scope

The Boilermaker manufactures specific parts from metal plates (brass, copper, steel, stainless steel, aluminum, etc.) of different thicknesses which he cuts, shapes, welds, assembles and assembles. Prepares and installs the machine tools following the analysis of the technical file or the plan or 3D models Prepares the industrialization, the workstation and creates the templates Manufactures metal structures, sub-assemblies and assemblies from defined plans Ensures the maintenance of the manufactured metal parts and monitors conformity over time Controls the conformity and quality of the part or sub-assembly
