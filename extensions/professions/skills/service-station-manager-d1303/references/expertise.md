# Service station manager

## Scope

The Service Station Manager directs and organizes the operation of a service station whose activities are oriented around the store (sales space), the fuel distribution track, as well as ancillary services (washing station, vacuum cleaner, inflator, etc.). Ensures the optimization of the management of one or more points of sale Organizes the daily operation of the service station Manages the store's fuel and product stocks Monitors compliance with safety rules and procedures Supervises and coordinates the work of the team
