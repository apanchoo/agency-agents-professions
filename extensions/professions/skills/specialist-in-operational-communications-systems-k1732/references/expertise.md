# Specialist in operational communications systems

## Scope

The operational communications systems specialist participates in the implementation of communications systems in order to carry out operational missions. Is responsible for the administration, maintenance, security and logistics of communication systems used in military or security operations Ensures the cybersecurity of communication systems Designs, plans and conducts cyber operations for computer control Develop sufficient physical and mental condition to exercise one's profession

## Related Job Titles

- Advisor force employment and planning of operations in a cyber environment
- Advisor on frequencies and electromagnetic easements
- High-level satellite networks expert
- Expert in specific techniques of information and communication systems
- Tactical Data Links Network Expert
- Technical writer for aeronautical information and communication systems
- Head of information and communication systems entities
- Specialist in force employment and planning of operations in a cyber environment
- Specialist in charge of naval tactical information and communication systems

## Activities and Skills

- Advise the authorities
- Have a sense of observation and analysis
- Administer, operate and supervise telecommunications systems and associated equipment on the ground or embedded in a vehicle, building or aircraft
- Maintain the capabilities and operational performance of telecommunications systems and networks to guarantee connections between the different components of the force
- Implement telecommunications means
- Design, plan and conduct cyber operations for computer control
- Master operational communication tools and technologies
- Maintain an information system in operational condition
- Administer computer systems and networks securely
- Ensuring the cybersecurity of communications systems
- Ensure the security of the installations and the area concerned in order to guarantee a high level of security
- Manage risks related to operational communications systems
- Interpret and apply safety and environmental regulations
- Respect safety standards and procedures
- Secure computer systems and networks
- Deploy and implement telecommunications systems and networks (radio, microwave, tactical data links, IP networks, satellites) in hostile areas
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Implement tactical liaison and data systems
- Implement a rule, protocols and procedures for a domain
- Have the ability to work under pressure
