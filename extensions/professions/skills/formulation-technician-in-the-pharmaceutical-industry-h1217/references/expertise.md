# Formulation technician in the pharmaceutical industry

## Scope

The Formulation Technician develops and tests mixtures of substances for new products. Designs and develops new formulations for products Conducts laboratory testing to ensure product quality and conformity Optimizes existing formulations to improve performance or reduce costs Collaborates with production teams for formula scaling Documents formulation procedures and results to ensure traceability Follows safety and environmental standards in all formulation activities

## Related Job Titles

- Galenist in industry
- Formulation operator
- Galenist Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Provide training for new technicians
- Collect, test, analyze samples, materials
- Establish and carry out test programs based on specifications and technical files
- Process experimental data to adjust formulations
- Maintain formulation technical files up to date
- Use specific formulation software
- Act quickly in the event of detected non-compliance
- Ensure regular calibration of instruments
- Analyze the chemical components of a formulation
- Control the quality of the conservation conditions of raw materials
- Suggest improvements to optimize processes
- Write test reports
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Apply current environmental standards
- Develop ecological alternatives in formulations
- Protect the environment by reducing production waste
- Develop modifications to meet the specifications, current regulations and the expectations of the parties concerned
- Ensure monitoring of specifications for each project
- Plan and monitor laboratory maintenance operations
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate analysis results effectively
- Promote sustainable development practices in production
- Follow laboratory safety protocols
