# Sales and territory manager

## Scope

The commerce and territory manager designs or participates in the design of commercial (re)vitalization, territorial attractiveness and local development projects, and implements them according to the area of intervention. Defines the landscape of economic activity and the attractiveness of the area of intervention for the years to come Supports implementation projects, develops and leads the economic project of the territory, in a partnership mode Provides an expertise and advisory function in the revitalization and commercial development of the territory Markets the offer of services in the territory (availability of land, real estate, city center or town center incubator) Can coordinate a team Can intervene in the setting up and creation of territorial development projects within the framework of international cooperation or a European program

- Local or territorial development project manager
- Economic development project manager (employment, attractiveness, innovation)
- Coordinator of local or territorial development system
- City center manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Facilitate and create the conditions for cooperation
- Implement collaborative work tools and methods
- Working at the interface of several fields of public policy
- Support and advise traders, craftsmen and entrepreneurs in their projects of creation, takeover, development and modernization of activities
- Advise, support business creators
- Teaching, developing skills
- Inform and guide economic players towards existing aid, subsidies and support schemes
- Create, organize or coordinate an event
- Organize representation of the community to regional or national organizations
- Propose communication supports adapted to the desired audiences
- Strengthen the attractiveness of businesses by providing expertise in terms of dynamism and commercial animation
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Launch and manage a call for tenders
- Write, launch and manage a call for projects
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Create and decide on a financing file
- Ensure active monitoring of new digital tools for commercial establishments but also of new business models
- Design and manage commercial animation and promotion actions to enhance the image of the city center or the region and its economic offer
- Bringing together public and private interests around common objectives of commercial development and territorial attractiveness
- Search for target companies and build a pool of companies to prospect (national brands in particular) in order to encourage new establishments
- Lead and structure networks of local economic actors
- Provide assistance and advice to elected officials in matters of economic development
- Determine a prospecting strategy and carry out proactive and communication actions
- Provide technical arguments to arbitrate and make strategic choices consistent with the political orientations of elected officials
- Identify and mobilize funding dedicated to the revitalization of businesses, crafts and services
- Identify economic development opportunities and promote a local development strategy based on carrying out a territorial diagnosis
- Negotiate and communicate with the issues and stakeholders involved, facilitate the integration of the commercial system into municipal projects
- Facilitate communication and coordination between the different community services concerned by the projects
- Demonstrate commercial acumen and interpersonal skills
- Working in a group, in a network
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Identify and mobilize private and public strategic partners in economic real estate
