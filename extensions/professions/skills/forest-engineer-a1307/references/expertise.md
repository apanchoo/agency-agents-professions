# Forest engineer

## Scope

The Forest Engineer manages sustainable forest management and exploitation projects. Develops and implements cutting and reforestation plans Supervises reforestation, development and maintenance operations of areas Conducts environmental impact studies to protect ecosystems Advises on best practices in forest management and biodiversity conservation Collaborates with government and non-governmental organizations to implement environmental policies Leads teams in the field during maintenance work in forest areas

- Rural water and forest engineering engineer (IGREF)
