# Welding engineer

## Scope

The Welding Engineer designs and supervises the quality of welding processes to join metallic materials ensuring compliance with safety and performance standards. Verifies the technical feasibility of projects and determines the appropriate welding parameters according to specifications Defines the welding processes and positions for the manufacturing phase Conducts welding and product demonstrations by assisting workshop managers in welding operations Writes welding procedures and manufacturing processes in compliance with current regulatory standards

## Related Job Titles

- Welding Technologist

## Activities and Skills

- Provide technical support to quality, maintenance, methods, research and development services
- Develop your skills in advanced welding techniques
- Train new employees on MIG MAG welding techniques
- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Conduct tests, manufacturing trials and production (assembly, prototype, pre-series etc.)
- Analyze non-destructive testing (NDT) data
- Analyze construction plans to prepare for welding operations
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Choose the materials and prepare the necessary settings for the welding operation
- Produce welded assemblies, metal joinery, 2D and 3D for stainless steel, aluminum and steel
- Supervise welding operations while respecting safety standards
- Trace, transfer the cutting and assembly marks on the parts
- Use and adjust mechanical, metallurgy or plastics processing machines or equipment
- Use and adjust electrotechnical or robotic machines or equipment
- Analyze a malfunction or non-compliance
- Ensure preventive maintenance of welding equipment
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Check safety devices on welding equipment
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Follow a maintenance or technical support operation
- Analyze process quality
- Ensure compliance of maintenance operations
- Control the quality of welds and assemblies
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Participate in defining the schedule, overall budget and deadlines
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure relations and monitoring of the project with subcontractors
- Reduce costs, consumption, waste
- Coordinate teams to ensure continuous production
- Communicate, read and write technical documents, reports, notes in English
- Apply strict safety standards when welding
- Document welding procedures for each project
- Document welding procedures for continuing education
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
