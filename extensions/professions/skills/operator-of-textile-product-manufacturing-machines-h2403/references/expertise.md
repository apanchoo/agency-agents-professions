# Operator of textile product manufacturing machines

## Scope

The Textile Product Manufacturing Machine Operator ensures the operation of textile production equipment, whether automated or manual. Operates processing or manufacturing machines in compliance with safety standards Performs first-level maintenance of equipment and provides support for monitoring Controls machines within the framework of quality requirements and production deadlines

## Related Job Titles

- Hosier / Hosier for the textile industry
- Embroiderer on multi-head machine
- Weaving machine driver
- Knitting machine operator
- Driver of tufting machines in the textile industry
- Knitting loom driver
- Ribbon
- Weaver in the textile industry
- Weaver
- Knitter
- Tulliste

## Activities and Skills

- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Ensure the training of new operators
- Provide quality and production monitoring support
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Run a production line
- Take note of the manufacturing order before the start of production
- Carry out elastic installation operations on textile articles
- Carry out lining installation operations on textile items
- Check the conformity of the parts in relation to the manufacturing order
- Analyze a malfunction or non-compliance
- Ensure compliance of installations with safety standards
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Carry out an initial malfunction diagnosis and apply corrective measures
- Check and maintain the cleanliness of production and control equipment
- Ensure self-checks of parts in relation to the manufacturing sheet
- Monitor the proper functioning of machines
- Control the quality of products during production
- Identify anomalies, manufacturing defects
- Monitor compliance of procedures
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Check the stitching layout and tension, make the necessary repairs and remove non-compliant items
- Manage stocks of textile raw materials
- Optimize production flows
- Suggest improvements for production processes
- Monitor production processes to ensure quality
- Communicate effectively with the production team
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
