# Geriatrician

## Scope

The geriatrician, a doctor specializing in the care of the elderly, deals with the prevention, diagnosis, treatment and monitoring of diseases and conditions linked to aging. Assesses the overall needs of older adults to propose an adapted care plan Diagnoses and treats diseases specific to advanced age Monitors the evolution of patients' health and adjusts treatments accordingly Informs and supports patients' families on care and best health practices Contributes to research and the continuous improvement of geriatric practices

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Organize cognitive stimulation activities for seniors
- Teaching, developing skills
- Adapt treatments according to patient progress
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Contribute to a person's physical well-being
- Contribute to a person's psychological well-being
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Evaluate patients' motor, perceptual and cognitive abilities
- Managing medical emergencies
- Inform, support a patient
- Implement a therapeutic project
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Monitor the evolution of patients' health status
- Conduct study and research work
- Update the patient's medical record
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Preventing falls and accidents among the elderly
- Respect medical protocols and safety standards
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Manage medical databases
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data

## Professional Approach

- Show responsiveness
