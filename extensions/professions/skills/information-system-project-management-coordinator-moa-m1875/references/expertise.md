# Information System Project Management Coordinator (MOA SI)

## Scope

The Information System Project Management Coordinator (MOA SI) plays an important role in the design and optimization of information systems within companies. Defines user needs and develops functional specifications Ensures liaison between end users and technical teams to ensure that the solutions developed meet the requirements Supervises the implementation of the information system and coordinates the different phases of the project by measuring its progress Controls the quality of the work carried out and ensures compliance of the deliverables with the initial specifications Organizes user training and provides technical support after implementation of the system Evaluates the effectiveness of the information system and proposes actions corrective measures to optimize processes

## Related Job Titles

- Information systems project management assistant
- Information system coordinator
- Information systems user manager
