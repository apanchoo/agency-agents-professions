# Glass industry technician

## Scope

The Glass Industry Technician participates in the manufacturing, processing and processing of glass products. Controls ovens, forming machines and production lines Analyzes the quality of glass products, production results and determines corrective measures Participates in the continuous improvement of manufacturing techniques and product innovation Ensures preventive maintenance of production equipment Respects and enforces safety and hygiene standards in the workplace

## Related Job Titles

- Furnace loader / Furnace loader in glass production or processing
- Glass furnace driver
- Launcher / Manufacturing launcher in the glass industry
- Glass material production installation pilot
- Oven supervisor in glass production or processing

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Organize the work of a team
- Provide technical support to quality, maintenance, methods, research and development services
- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Create technical documentation
- Develop and plan a production and operating program
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Carry out dosages and samples of products
- Carry out the casting of the ovens
- Carry out the restart of the oven (burner, etc.) and depolluters
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Adjust molds and tools
- Transform a raw material
- Use and adjust glassmaking or ceramic machines or equipment
- Determine the causes of malfunctions
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Carry out soaking and soaking of ovens
- Carry out rinsing of ovens (removal of tips, etc.)
- Carry out a technical diagnosis
- Control the operation of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Define and implement security protocols
- Check the status of the material fusion/transformation installation
- Analyze process quality
- Check the physico-chemical conformity of materials or products
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Carry out palletizing
- Recycle and recover waste or products
- Manage production and operations management
- Relay information
- Respect and enforce safety and hygiene standards on the line
