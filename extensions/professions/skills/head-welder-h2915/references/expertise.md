# Head welder

## Scope

The Head Welder orchestrates welding operations with expertise and precision, guaranteeing the quality and safety of metal assemblies. Supervises and coordinates the team of welders to ensure the efficiency and quality of welding work Controls the conformity of welds carried out with technical standards and specifications Trains welders in new techniques and current safety standards Manages the supply of equipment and consumables for welding Optimizes welding processes to improve productivity and reduce costs Ensures preventive maintenance of welding equipment to avoid breakdowns and guarantee optimal use

## Related Job Titles

- Head of welding workshop
- Welding Team Leader
- Welding Coordinator

## Activities and Skills

- Check staff qualifications
- Coordinate the activity of a team
- Ensure continuing training of the team on new technologies
- Analyze construction plans to prepare for welding operations
- Apply treatments adapted to the materials
- Configure and configure an installation, a machine
- Prepare the work area and the means necessary for the activity
- Carry out heat treatment of welds (carbon steel, etc.)
- Supervise welding operations while respecting safety standards
- Ensure basic preventive maintenance of equipment
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Make corrections or repairs to a work
- Promote occupational health and safety procedures
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Control the quality and conformity of a deliverable
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Evaluate defects and carry out recovery or finishing operations (edge, deposit, surface, etc.)
- Control a supply
- Manage inventory of welding materials and consumables
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Relay information

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate leadership
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Characteristics of sheets according to their thickness
- Characteristics of stainless steel materials
- Characteristics of materials in refractory alloys, superalloys
- Use of portable power tools (electric saw, sander, etc.)
- Characteristics of nickel, titanium, magnesium materials
- Characteristics of ferrous metals (steel, cast iron, etc.)
- Characteristics of copper, nickel and their alloys materials
- Characteristics of coated steel materials (zinc plated, galvanized, leaded, aluminized, plastic coated)
- Characteristics of aluminum materials and its alloys

## Areas of expertise

- Inventory management of welding consumables
- Fundamentals of metallurgy

## Standards and processes

- Waste management and recycling
- European welding safety standards
- Compliance of welds, constructions, assemblies

## Professional techniques

- Soldering techniques
- Assessment of welder skills
- Adaptation of welding techniques to specific materials
- Thermal cutting techniques

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- In the workshop
- In line or production island
- Exposure to high temperatures
- Handling a dangerous machine, equipment or tool
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Daytime work
