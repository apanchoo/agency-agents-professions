## Scope

With a keen eye for details, the automotive paint preparer is responsible for preparing all vehicle surfaces before painting, an essential step to receive the paint in optimal conditions and guarantee the aesthetics of the vehicles. Sands and putty to correct imperfections Prepares surfaces Apply primers before painting Keeps workshop clean and organized to ensure a safe working environment

## Skills to Apply

- Apply primers
- Equip the workstation with the tools, supplies, materials adapted and verified according to the activity to be carried out
- Prepare surfaces before painting the vehicle
- Clean a surface to be treated
- Check the surface (deposit, appearance, thickness), identify non-conformities and carry out finishing touches
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Apply anti-corrosion treatments to protect vehicles
- Degrease surfaces to be painted
