# Ambulance auxiliary

## Scope

The ambulance auxiliary plays an important role in daily medical transport. Transports patients to their healthcare establishments, ensuring their safety and comfort during transport Ensures cleaning and disinfection of the medical transport vehicle (VSL or ambulance) after each intervention As a crew, intervenes in support of the state-certified ambulance driver Can assist medical staff and state-certified paramedics in transporting patients by ambulance Can carry out first aid procedures under the supervision of the state-certified paramedic in charge of the flight

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Ensure monitoring of patients' health status
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Maintain a clean and safe environment for patients
- Driving a light vehicle
- Participate in stretching patients
- Manage the necessary administrative documents
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Communicate with those close to the person being transported
- Establish a professional relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being transported
- Relay information
- Keep your knowledge and skills up to date

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate self-control
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Rescue and emergency care ambulance (ASSU)
- Category D vehicle (light medical vehicle -VSL- intended for transport in a seated position)

## Areas of expertise

- Emergency and first aid procedures
- Stress management in emergency situations
- Patient handling

## Standards and processes

- Hygiene and asepsis rules
- Procedures for controlling medical-technical materials and equipment

## Professional techniques

- Safe lifting techniques for patients
- Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) techniques
- Ability to work as a team in stressful situations

## Working Conditions

- Interim
- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Business travel
- In contact with the public
- Wearing hygiene equipment
- Work in staggered hours
- Daytime work
- Work weekends and public holidays

## Sectors

- Logistics and transportation
