# Production manager

## Scope

Sets up and supervises the logistical and technical organization of shows (concerts, plays, etc.), filming or recordings (television shows, feature films, documentaries, advertising films, models, etc.) according to safety regulations and production requirements. Coordinates the activities of different technical services. May participate in the installation of decorative elements and technical equipment.

## Related Job Titles

- Assistant manager
- Director/Cinema technical director
- Stage Manager
- Recording studio manager
- Stage manager / Tour manager
- Exterior manager
- Cinema logistics manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Recruit and integrate a person
- Request a filming or performance authorization
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Create a seating plan
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Develop a technical master plan, define equipment needs
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Implement decorative elements
- Install stage lighting equipment
- Install sound equipment
- Install stage props
- Select a location for an event
- Make corrections or repairs to a work
- Supervise the sending of rushes to the post-production laboratory
- Load, unload, handle products
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Organize and control a supply
- Supervise the delivery and installation of logistics and signage at the venue of a show
- Manage a schedule
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Carry out accounting operations
- Carry out financial monitoring
- Design and manage a project
- Control the progress of a film shoot or performance
- Request logistical support from the services concerned
- Managing an emergency situation
- Organize and develop your professional network

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Be proactive
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Characteristics of sound equipment
- Non-motorized handling equipment (pallet truck, hand truck, etc.)
- Characteristics of shooting equipment
- Types of audiovisual/cinematographic products (film, report, etc.)
- Characteristics of lighting equipment

## Areas of expertise

- Handling gestures and postures
- Computer-aided design or drawing software (CAD/CAD)

## Standards and processes

- Reading work plan, filming plan
- Safety rules
- Social legislation
- Safety and accessibility standards for spaces and establishments open to the public
- Fire safety regulations
- Reading seating plan
- Technical sheets for a show or filming

## Professional techniques

- Set assembly/disassembly techniques
- Hanging techniques

## Working Conditions

- Business travel

- Association
