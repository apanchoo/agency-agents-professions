# Manager / Team leader in industry

## Scope

The Industrial Team Leader organizes the activity of a production or maintenance team in an industrial environment. Ensures the proper execution of tasks, compliance with quality and safety standards and production deadlines Controls the operation of equipment and provides technical support to operators and drivers Optimizes production flows to achieve productivity objectives by monitoring storage and shipments of products Trains team members in procedures and equipment and intervenes on technical problems during operations
