# Core moulder / Core moulder machine

## Scope

The core molder makes sand molds individually or in series intended for the molding of molten metals, according to safety rules and production requirements (quality, deadlines, etc.). Can carry out simple model transformation interventions Can coordinate a team

## Related Job Titles

- Manufacturing agent in sand casting foundry
- Core molding team leader
- Machinist helps molder
- Molder / Pit foundry molder
- Molder / Moulder of large foundry parts in pit
- Molder / Hand moulder
- Molder / Plasterboard moulder
- Molder / Sand molder
- Core moulder / Hand core moulder
- Coreder
- Core operator
- Remoulding operator
- Plasterer in foundry
- Remoulder

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Modify or adapt the shape and dimensions of a model, a model plate, a core box
- Using additive manufacturing to prototype and shape products
- Read and interpret technical data and documents, if necessary in English
- Establish a manufacturing range
- Prepare for a production change
- Clear the imprint, check its dimensions, surface condition and carry out any touch-ups and finishes
- Apply a coating to the molding surfaces (impressions, cores, etc.)
- Identify the intervention phases
- Prepare sandy mixtures
- Fill the mold with sand, determine the position of the model and mold the impression, the casting and gas evacuation conduits
- Make the cores (filling the core boxes with sand, tamping, positioning of reinforcements, hardening treatment, etc.)
- Adjust the machine and assemble tools (turnstile, production line)
- Position and fix the elements and close the mold
- Check the conformity of production equipment
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Join a material optimization process
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Process production data for performance analysis
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Integrate into the team and show cooperation
- Integrate recommendations and adapt your professional behavior
