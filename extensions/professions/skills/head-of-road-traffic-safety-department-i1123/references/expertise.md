# Head of road traffic safety department

## Scope

The head of the road traffic safety department is responsible for the design, implementation and management of road safety policies in a given territory. Develops the road safety strategy by defining the orientations, action plans and objectives based on the analysis of accidents and local issues Pilots and coordinates prevention, awareness and road education actions aimed at all audiences Manages and leads the service teams by supervising agents, organizing work and developing skills Coordinates partners and institutional actors by working with communities, law enforcement, technical services and associations Ensures regulatory and budgetary monitoring of the service
