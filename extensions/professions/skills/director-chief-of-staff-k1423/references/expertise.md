# Director / Chief of Staff

## Scope

The cabinet director is the right arm of an elected official or a senior civil servant, ensuring the strategic and operational coordination of the cabinet of a decentralized service or a central administration department; he manages key projects and activity and supports management in its action by articulating the definition of strategic objectives and their implementation. Directs the structure's cabinet Contributes to the definition of strategic orientations and coordinates transversal strategic files Supervises the organization of decision-making bodies Supervises the structure's communication activity and external relations Supervises and, where necessary drafts, speeches and language elements Constitutes the privileged entry point for other State services
