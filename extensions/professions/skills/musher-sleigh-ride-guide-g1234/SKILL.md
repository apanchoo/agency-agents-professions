## Scope

The musher guides sleigh rides and leads dog teams for excursions in the natural environment. Ensures the safety and well-being of animals and participants during excursions Prepares itineraries according to weather conditions and the level of participants Maintains the sled equipment and equipment necessary for the activity Can participate in dog sledding competitions Raise participants' awareness of the environment and local culture

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Accompany groups on guided tours or outdoor outings
- Plan itineraries adapted to the abilities of the participants
- Train participants in basic mushing techniques
- Driving a dog team safely
- Preparing an animal for hiking and caring for it
- Maintaining sled equipment in good condition
- Ensure the safety of participants during the hike and courses
- Communicate instructions clearly to participants
