# Fiber optic operations manager

## Scope

The Fiber Optic Operations Manager must guarantee the performance of our communications networks, by managing the operation of the fiber optic network within its scope. Supervises and coordinates optical fiber deployment work Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of installations and equipment Manages technical teams and ensures ongoing training of employees Controls the quality of installations and compliance with current standards Develops strategies to improve network performance and coverage Collaborates with other services and suppliers to guarantee network efficiency

## Related Job Titles

- Telecom consultant
- Negotiator for fiber optic telecom sites
- Negotiator for mobile telecoms sites

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate team activities in the field
- Promote exchange relationships and a climate of cooperation
- Carry out monitoring of an intervention team
- Analyze operating data to improve service
- Analyze information relating to the progress of the intervention taking into account execution plans and installation diagrams
- Check the progress of the work
- Control the progress of work
- Organize site personnel by ensuring reception, relations with staff representatives, interventions in the event of incidents or accidents
- Organize the operations to be carried out in order to respect deadlines and site constraints
- Monitor all work, ensure compliance with quality and deadlines and control pre-established budgets
- Ensure basic preventive maintenance of equipment
- Check the conformity of installations
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Carry out a diagnosis based on observed malfunctions
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Control a maintenance operation
- Assess the risks associated with fiber projects
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Supervise maintenance operations
- Write a meeting report
- Write an evaluation report
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure relationships and monitoring with the various project stakeholders (subcontractors, suppliers, end customers, communities, operators)
- Record and process complaints from customers and beneficiaries
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Ensure monitoring of customer satisfaction
- Carry out technical monitoring of products and their evolution with customers
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Collaborate with other departments for efficiency
- Develop network expansion strategies
- Implement technical performance indicators
- Lead work monitoring meetings
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
