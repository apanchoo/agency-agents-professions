# Teacher of French as a Foreign Language (FLE)

## Scope

The French Foreign Language (FLE) Teacher ensures the learning and mastery of oral and written French to a non-French-speaking public, children or adults, at various levels of study. Teaches French grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation to non-French speaking students Prepares teaching materials adapted to students' levels and needs Evaluates student progress and adapts teaching methods accordingly Organizes and leads activities that promote the practice of French in a real context Collaborates with other teachers to exchange resources and teaching methods Can participate in conferences or training to stay up to date on FLE teaching methods

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Manage classroom behaviors to maintain an environment conducive to learning
- Help students understand and integrate into a French-speaking environment
- Design educational supports and tools (teaching materials, educational software, exam subject)
- Teaching, developing skills
- Organize activities that promote language learning
- Prepare students for French as a foreign language exams such as DELF, DALF or TCF
- Monitor student progress and provide constructive feedback
- Encourage personal expression through French
