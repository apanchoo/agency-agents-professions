# Industrial Quality Manager

## Scope

The Industrial Quality Manager ensures the excellence and conformity of processes and products in the industrial sector. Ensures the implementation and monitoring of quality management systems, in accordance with current standards and regulations Develops and implements quality control procedures Analyzes quality data and proposes prevention, correction and continuous improvement approaches Supervises internal and external audit plans (products, processes, system) Manages labeling and certification by applying quality procedures
