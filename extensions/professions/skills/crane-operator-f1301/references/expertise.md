# Crane operator

## Scope

The Crane Operator, a key player on a construction site, maneuvers the crane with precision to guarantee safety. Operates the crane to move heavy loads on construction sites Checks the condition of the crane before and after each use Coordinates its activity with ground teams Can install and dismantle construction site cranes

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze the instructions while appreciating the difficulties of the site
- Coordinate movements with other equipment on site
- Set up a construction site
- Participate in the assembly of the crane, its start-up and its adjustment
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Check the stability of the crane before use
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Carry out a visual inspection of the machine to check its working condition
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Sling, lift and distribute a load respecting the planned locations
- Lifting loads
- Lifting heavy products or equipment
- Operate a crane in out-of-service configuration
- Position the block of a crane
- Carry out unblocking and stabilization operations on a lifting machine
- Apply eco-driving principles
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Communicate with ground teams by radio
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Adapt working methods to weather conditions
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Follow safety instructions on site
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Use digital tools
- Maintain high concentration during operations
