# Drain / Drain tray

## Scope

The sewer worker, an essential profession for maintaining urban sanitation. Ensures the maintenance and repair of sewerage networks Regularly inspects sewers to prevent blockages and failures Uses specialized equipment to clean and unclog pipes Carries out emergency interventions in the event of flooding or overflows Collaborates with city services to coordinate work on infrastructure Respects safety and environmental protection standards during interventions

## Related Job Titles

- Head sewer
- Main drain

## Activities and Skills

- Read and interpret sanitation network diagrams
- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Analyze and interpret the results of permanent sensors placed on water networks
- Assemble pipes by pointing, screwing, bolting
- Prepare materials for work
- Adjust operating or water sampling equipment
- Use a tool, machine, equipment, installation
- Use a self-guided camera
- Install operating or water sampling equipment
- Make joints on pipes (Teflon, tow and paste, gas grease)
- Have a good understanding of pipe systems and sewer networks
- Maintain pipes
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Implement drinking water pumping and storage techniques
- Implement chemical water treatment techniques
- Identify leaks and obstructions in a sanitation network
- Make corrections or repairs to a work
- Carry out small repairs and welding work
- Carry out renovation, rehabilitation or repair work on obsolete networks
- Carry out remote monitoring and regulation of operating equipment (automates, valves, pumps, etc.) and the flow of rain, floods, etc. remotely
- Carry out hydraulic cleaning (sluice boat, sluice wagon, etc.)
- Carry out manual cleaning
- Carry out mechanized, hydrodynamic, hydrocleaning
- Check pumping devices and detect malfunctions
- Identify and manage risks related to exposure to chemicals and biological agents
- Check the tightness of a pipe
- Ensure compliance of maintenance operations
- Write a report, an activity report
- Provide expertise in the field of wastewater purification or sanitation
- Apply regulations specifying sanitation standards
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
