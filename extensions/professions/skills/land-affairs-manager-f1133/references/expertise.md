# Land affairs manager

## Scope

The Land Affairs Manager is responsible for the search and acquisition of land. Identifies and evaluates land suitable for development projects Negotiates acquisitions or leases with land owners Ensures compliance of transactions with local and national regulations Plans land use in consultation with urban planners and architects Manages financial aspects of land transactions Oversees feasibility studies and environmental assessments

## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate communication between stakeholders
- Analyze the specificities of the land for development projects
- Carry out technical and financial feasibility studies
- Update land and cadastral files
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Carry out the digital mapping of a cadastral plan and a Local Urban Planning Plan (PLU)
- Inventory the easements relating to land
- Carry out land studies or documents and establish architectural plans (plot division, land development, etc.)
- Collaborate with urban planners and architects
- Design a subdivision project for agricultural or building land
- Manage land use planning
- Optimize land space management
- Carry out surveys of the implementation of existing distribution networks (electrical, telecommunications, etc.)
- Evaluate the risks associated with land acquisitions
- Write a report, an activity report
- Proceed with the various administrative authorization requests
- Propose innovative solutions for land optimization
- Apply the legal framework for public procurement
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Deciding on the terms of a contract
- Ensure compliance of land operations with legislation
- Advise on legal and technical aspects of land transactions
- Formalize a transaction request
- Negotiate a contract
- Present the clauses of a contract
- Draft legal documents and procedures
- Follow acquisition and disposal procedures and draft documents
- Promote sustainable practices in the sober land management of development projects
- Assist and advise elected officials in their development choices (town centers, VRD, green spaces, etc.)
- Define and implement land procedures
- Manage relations with local authorities
- Coordinate land development and optimization projects
- Develop land investment strategies
- Manage the performance and profitability of an activity or project
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Working in a group, in a network
