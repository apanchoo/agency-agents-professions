# Financial economist

## Scope

The financial economist analyzes and interprets financial data to guide economic decisions. Analyzes financial markets and economic trends Assesses financial risks and proposes mitigation strategies Conducts economic feasibility studies for new projects Advises businesses or public agencies on economic policies Prepares detailed reports and economic forecasts Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to optimize financial performance

## Related Job Titles

- Market economist

## Activities and Skills

- Advise an organization, a structure
- Provide tax advice
- Write and publish sectoral situation notes or studies on large companies
- Integrate financial mathematical models into information systems
- Carry out technical and financial feasibility studies
- Create a forecast model
- Develop instruments to assess the fragility and sensitivity of the company
- Prepare detailed reports for management
- Analyze a client's financial situation: checking their income, debts, legal arrangements and repayment capacity
- Analyze a financial statement
- Design a public purchase offer
- Develop strategies for financial risk management
- Estimate the value of the company analyzed
- Establish a recommendation for the sale of financial assets
- Evaluate financial risks
- Listing a company on the stock market
- Carry out a financial audit
- Track financial performance
- Advising businesses on new tax regulations
- Propose risk coverage solutions for a company
- Analyze financial markets
- Analyze economic trends
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Carry out a market study, assess the potential of a product or service
- Develop an investment plan, a business plan
- Carry out financial analyzes and studies (market, risks, products, economic restructuring, etc.)
- Ensure regulatory and legislative monitoring
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Monitor legislative changes affecting taxation
