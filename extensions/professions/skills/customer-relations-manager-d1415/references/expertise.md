# Customer relations manager

## Scope

The Customer Relations Manager, a key role in maintaining effective communication and meeting customer needs. Manages and develops relationships with customers to ensure their satisfaction Handles customer requests and complaints Tracks performance indicators and suggests improvements Collaborates with internal teams to resolve customer issues Maintains databases up to date

## Related Job Titles

- Visitor Relations Officer
- After-sales service manager

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in regular team meetings
- Train new employees in customer service procedures
- Negotiate with customers in case of disagreements
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Act quickly in the event of urgent customer requests
- Analyze customer needs to offer appropriate services
- Detect the needs and issues of prospects or customers in connection with the company's service or product offering
- Identify and process a customer request
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Complete customer documents
- Answer phone calls, emails, chat and inform customers
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Analyze and identify trends and consumer preferences
- Analyze market trends to anticipate customer needs
- Ensure monitoring of customer satisfaction
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Make a call or telephone follow-up to customers or prospects
- Conduct customer satisfaction surveys
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Make quick decisions under pressure

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
