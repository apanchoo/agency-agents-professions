# Toll Agent

## Scope

The toll agent is responsible for the proper functioning of motorway tolls as well as the management of vehicle flows. Welcomes, directs and informs users (subscriptions, complaints, traffic status, etc.) Collects payments in cash, by bank card or via electronic systems Monitors, anticipates traffic and gives instructions based on events occurring at the toll station Provides coin collectors or supervisors

## Related Job Titles

- Toll collector
- toll operator

## Activities and Skills

- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Complete an intervention report
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance
- Ensuring cleanliness and order in the toll area
- Act quickly in the event of equipment breakdown
- Apply safety procedures and operating instructions
- Follow a maintenance or technical support operation
- Monitor vehicle flows and regulate toll traffic
- Control payment vouchers and electronic toll subscriptions
- Manage a cash register
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Control access and security at the toll booth
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Inform users about prices and payment terms
- Inform users about prices, routes and services available
- Optimize visitor flow to reduce waiting times
- Communicate with the maintenance team
- Transmit to the control station events related to tolls and motorway traffic
- Working in a group, in a network
- Inform the relevant departments of complaints, anomalies, malfunctions and damage

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials
