# Jeweler

## Scope

The Jeweler brings to life an idea or a drawing to create with his hands, a body ornament in precious metal intended to highlight a gem. Makes complex jewelry adorned with precious or fine stones Prepares and organizes the work of making a piece of jewelry by defining the means and processes to adopt Makes, transforms and repairs a piece of jewelry, using all appropriate techniques Ensures the quality of the work and the aesthetic rendering

## Related Job Titles

- Jeweler / Jeweler model maker

## Activities and Skills

- Design and develop a model, a prototype
- Make a mock-up of the part, using traditional mock-up techniques
- Record the start and end of a step on a digital production order
- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Determine and optimize operating times
- Analyze, prepare and organize the work of creating a piece of goldsmithing by planing based on the instructions (drawing or technical sheet)
- Define the intervention steps for shaping or decorating an object
- Measure the loss of precious metals at each stage of production
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Implement manufacturing techniques while respecting the specifications and the part production plan
- Prepare materials for work
- Make joints and entanglements
- Create mechanisms: clasps, clips, pin systems, springs
- Make different types of bezels in jewelry
- Carry out the stonework of a room: laying stone and updating
- Carry out the assembly and assembly of the jewelry while respecting the appropriate processes and techniques
- Organize your space and workstation
- Carry out jewelry repair operations
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Detect defects, nuances, alloy of materials and determine the constraints of the part
- Carry out checks at the end of the project, ensuring compliance with the specifications
- Manage stocks of raw materials and consumables
- Ensure the monitoring of its production using the appropriate administrative supports (police book, weight counter, etc.)
- Evaluate manufacturing costs
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Identify and process a customer request
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Communicate effectively with clients on projects
- Send information to your manager
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
- Consult and use work documentation on a digital platform
- Mobilize your concentration throughout a process
