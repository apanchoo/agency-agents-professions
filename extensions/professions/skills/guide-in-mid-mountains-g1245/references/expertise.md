# Guide in mid-mountains

## Scope

The Mid-Mountain Leader supervises and guides groups on hiking trips in the mountains on marked or unmarked trails (excluding glacial or climbing areas). Prepares the routes, manages the equipment and the organization of the stages Adapts the routes according to the level of the participants and the weather conditions to guarantee safety Transmits knowledge and supports the discovery of the fauna, flora and local heritage Raises awareness of the environment and good practices in the mountains

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Accompany groups on guided tours or outdoor outings
- Accompany hikes and teach the practice of mountain sports activities (snowshoeing, Nordic walking, trekking, trail running)
- Design and lead relaxation or leisure activities
- Plan itineraries adapted to the abilities of the participants
- Teaching, developing skills
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Managing first aid in the event of an accident in the mountains
- Navigate with maps and GPS in the mountains
- Ensure the safety of participants during the hike and courses
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Manage the material resources necessary for activities and projects
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Ensure compliance with safety rules for property and people in the natural environment, in compliance with current regulations
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Explain local biodiversity and its importance
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Adapting to different cultural contexts
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Adapt routes in real time
- View and analyze weather data
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing an emergency situation
- Collect information on the duration, number of participants, and specific features of the tourist support service
- Transmit the values linked to the sporting discipline
