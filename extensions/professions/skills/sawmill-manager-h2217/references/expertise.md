# Sawmill manager

## Scope

The Sawmill Manager organizes production, ensures daily management and compliance with safety and environmental standards within the sawmill. Supervises the production of wood and the processing of logs or logs Optimizes production processes to ensure efficiency and product quality Manages wood stocks and ensures the logistics necessary for continuous supply Develops strategies to improve profitability and reduce production costs Coaches and trains staff to optimize team skills and performance
