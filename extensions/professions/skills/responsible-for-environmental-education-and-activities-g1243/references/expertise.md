# Responsible for environmental education and activities

## Scope

The Environmental Education and Animation Manager raises awareness, supervises and supports different audiences in the discovery, understanding, transmission of knowledge of ecological issues, respect for natural heritage and biodiversity; To do this, he: Coordinates, pilots and develops educational programs focused on environmental education and biodiversity Designs projects and educational tools to raise awareness among people in line with the educational policies and ecological issues of the territory and its structure in collaboration with stakeholders in the territory (schools, communities and associations) Supports his team of educators and leads workshops and educational activities for different audiences Ensures the administrative and budgetary management of projects (research of funding, monitoring of partnerships) Develops awareness and communication campaigns and materials to facilitate understanding and commitment to environmental issues and biodiversity Evaluates the impact of environmental education programs and adjusts strategies based on feedback and changing needs
