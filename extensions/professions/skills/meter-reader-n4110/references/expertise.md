# Meter reader

## Scope

The Meter Reader is responsible for recording energy and water consumption. Reads water, gas or electricity meters in homes and businesses Records consumption data to ensure accurate billing Reports anomalies and meter malfunctions Ensures monitoring of equipment and its proper functioning Can intervene for initial diagnostics in the event of a technical problem Contributes to raising user awareness about energy savings
