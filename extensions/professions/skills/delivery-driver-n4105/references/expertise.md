# Delivery driver

## Scope

The Delivery Driver ensures the distribution and punctual or regular delivery of goods transport within a short radius of action, with a daily return to the vehicle's parking location. By adopting the principles of eco-driving, participates in the protection of the environment Takes care of packages, handles them and secures them Plans its mission to optimize travel times and respond to customer requests, uses digital tools to ensure traceability Ensures compliance with deadlines expected by the customer and the quality of service Respects the rules of courtesy while driving and adopts a commercial posture towards customers Controls the operating condition of the vehicle and its equipment, the presence and validity of mandatory documents and reports any anomaly Complies with road regulations, safety and company procedures May be required to make collections
