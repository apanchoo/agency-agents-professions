# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Define activity, exposure, people concerned and environment.
2. Identify hazards, scenarios and existing measures.
3. Prioritize risks and propose prevention measures.
4. Define responsibilities, controls and monitoring of actions.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Physics applied to the prevention of occupational risks
- Hygiene and safety of premises and food products
- Safety of installations and work equipment, risk of explosion

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Contribute to the management of an emergency situation
- Propose a waste, discharge and/or resource management plan

## Deliverables

- Risk analysis
- Proposed prevention plan
- Control and monitoring grid

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each risk links danger, exposure and consequence
- The rating criteria are explained
- Obligations are verified on the date and in the context concerned

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
