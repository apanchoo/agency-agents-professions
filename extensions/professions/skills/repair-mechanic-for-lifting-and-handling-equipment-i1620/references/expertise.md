# Repair mechanic for lifting and handling equipment

## Scope

The Lifting and Handling Equipment Repair Mechanic takes care of the maintenance and performance of industrial equipment. Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of lifting and handling equipment Diagnoses breakdowns and carries out necessary repairs Controls the operation of equipment after intervention Advises users on routine maintenance of equipment Can intervene urgently on complex breakdowns Manages the stock of spare parts necessary for repairs

## Related Job Titles

- Handling equipment maintenance agent
- Mechanic of equipment and motorized handling machines
- Mechanic of equipment and motorized handling machines
- Repair mechanic for lifting and handling equipment

## Activities and Skills

- Train users in the safe operation of lifting equipment
- Carry out operational tests
- Assemble and install elements or subassemblies of industrial equipment
- Adjust the parameters of handling machines to optimize their operation
- Analyze information relating to the progress of the intervention taking into account execution plans and installation diagrams
- Install optional or complementary equipment to adapt to construction machinery
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Change or repair a defective part or assembly
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Detect the origin of a breakdown (on site or remotely)
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Materialize the handling area to work safely while respecting the instructions during work on site
- Carry out cleaning and adjustment operations on materials and equipment
- Carry out a breakdown or installation malfunction diagnosis
- Carry out on-site repairs or towing a machine (construction machine, agricultural machine, etc.)
- Ensure regular calibration of instruments
- Optimize safety procedures for the use of lifting equipment
- Manage the inventory of spare parts for handling equipment
- Complete the intervention monitoring materials
- Integrate into the team and show cooperation
- Transmit information and ensure understanding by using appropriate language to internal and external stakeholders
- Respect procedures, operating methods and instructions
- Respect safety and hygiene standards
- Respect quality standards
- React effectively in the event of a technical incident
