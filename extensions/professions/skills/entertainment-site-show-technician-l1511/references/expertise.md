# Entertainment site show technician

## Scope

The entertainment site show technician masters the technical platforms in sound, lighting and special effects to create the worlds of artistic productions. Ensures the installation and proper functioning of stage and audiovisual equipment Participates in the design and creation of sets and special effects Operates the sound, light and video systems during the performances Collaborates closely with the artists to adapt the technical aspects to the needs of the shows

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate technical teams during events
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Add sounds (music, sound effects, jingle) to a scenario
- Broadcast a sound composition (soundtrack, music, sound effects)
- Conduct rehearsals with actors and comedians
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Develop a technical master plan, define equipment needs
- Adapt the technical installations to the specificities of the shows
- Determine and manage lighting
- Determine the type and location of sound equipment
- Carry out lighting tests before a performance
- Launch lighting effects during a show
- Control a light effects console
- Install stage lighting equipment
- Install sound equipment
- Optimize the acoustics of performance spaces
- Supervise the assembly and dismantling of stage structures
- Perform first level maintenance
- Carry out a maintenance operation
- Check the proper functioning of stage equipment
- Manage technical or artistic unforeseen events during shows
- Ensure the proper functioning of sound equipment
- Load, unload, handle products
- Place orders for lighting equipment, consumables and tools according to the needs of a show or filming
- Respect working hours according to show programs
- Ensuring the safety of visitors and staff
- Organize and develop your professional network

## Professional Approach
