## Scope

A diamond lapidary is an artisan who specializes in transforming rough diamonds into cut precious stones, ready for use in jewelry. Analyzes the raw precious or ornamental stone (with the exception of diamonds for the lapidary) in order to determine the part to be highlighted Cuts the stone so that it is as beautiful and clean as possible, while losing as little material as possible Also improves an already cut stone (purity, shape, size, etc.) The craftsman who carries out this work on the diamond is the diamond cutter. On precious, fine or ornamental colored stones, it is the lapidary

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Make an aesthetic or artistic appreciation
- Adapt the cutting technique to the type of stone
- Polish the stone to brighten its shine
- Resume the faceting of a stone in order to modify its dimensions (calibration, adjustment on work) or appearance
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Use and adjust craft machines or equipment
- Evaluate the cutting time and the difficulty of the work
- Visually appreciate symmetry, color, regularity
