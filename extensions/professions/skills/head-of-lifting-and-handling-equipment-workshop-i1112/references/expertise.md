# Head of lifting and handling equipment workshop

## Scope

The lifting and handling equipment workshop manager guarantees the proper coordination of the activities of a workshop dedicated to the maintenance and repair of lifting and handling equipment Supervises and coordinates the activities of technicians through a monitoring schedule according to procedures and safety standards Ensures training of teams and ensures compliance of maintenance and repair operations Optimizes work processes to improve the efficiency of the workshop Manages stocks of spare parts and orders the necessary supplies Maintains technical documentation up to date, accessible and tests the machines Analyzes recurring breakdowns to propose technical improvements and informs customers about the work carried out
