# Technical-sales / Technical-sales wood products

## Scope

The technical sales of wood products is the interface between the specificity of the wood material and the concrete needs of customers: a profession where expertise and human relations meet. Prospects a clientele and offers technical solutions adapted to their needs and requirements Negotiates the commercial conditions of the sale Participates and advises on the strategy of commercial activity in conjunction with management and production services Can coordinate a sales team and lead a network of salespeople
