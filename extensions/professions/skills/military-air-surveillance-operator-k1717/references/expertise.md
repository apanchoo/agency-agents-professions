# Military air surveillance operator

## Scope

The aerial surveillance operator is responsible for monitoring the airspace using detection and tracking systems, such as radars and thermal cameras as part of the permanent aviation security posture. Works in an air traffic control center or military command post to detect, identify and track aircraft in the airspace Is responsible for handling alarms and warnings, coordinating with other agencies and providing information to pilots and air traffic controllers Knows airspace management procedures Develops sufficient physical and mental fitness to perform the job
