# Urban cleanliness manager

## Scope

The urban cleanliness manager ensures that the cleanliness of public spaces is maintained and improved. Supervises urban cleaning teams Plans and coordinates cleaning operations Controls the quality of interventions and ensures compliance with hygiene standards Manages cleaning materials and equipment Develops strategies to optimize cleaning processes Collaborates with municipal services for cleanliness initiatives

- Organize cleaning tours

- Train staff in service and quality standards

- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Define maintenance needs
- Carry out cleaning and adjustment operations on materials and equipment
- Ensure the good condition of vehicles and cleaning equipment
- Design and implement awareness-raising actions
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Ensure compliance with safety and environmental standards
- Control the quality of the services provided

- Set up additional cleaning arrangements for public events or emergency responses
- Carry out order management (placing, tracking, reception)

- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Negotiate a contract
- Define and deploy a sustainable development policy
- Supervise the collection and management of urban waste
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Develop strategies to improve cleaning efficiency
- Develop action plans for special events or particular seasons
- Coordinate with municipal services for waste disposal
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
