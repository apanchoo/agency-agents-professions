## Scope

The Glass Industry Fusion Technician transforms raw materials into molten glass by controlling the fusion process in very high temperature furnaces. Controls production parameters and adjusts the melting processes to guarantee the quality of the final product Masters the chemical and mechanical aspect of the transformation of solid materials into a homogeneous viscous liquid intended for forming machines Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of melting equipment to minimize production stoppages Respects and applies safety standards and environmental regulations in force
