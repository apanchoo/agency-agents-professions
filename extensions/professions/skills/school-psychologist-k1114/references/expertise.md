# School psychologist

## Scope

A school psychologist supports students in their academic, emotional and social development. Supports students with academic or personal difficulties Helps manage stress, emotions, conflicts Helps identify disorders (dyslexia, ADHD, anxiety, behavioral disorders, etc.) Advises teachers on educational adaptations Can help with academic guidance according to the student's abilities and interests

## Related Job Titles

- National Educational Psychologist, Education Development Learning (EDA)

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise individual or group educational projects
- Participate in the implementation of educational projects
- Advise teachers on teaching approaches
- Analyze learning and behavioral difficulties
- Contribute to the educational inclusion of students with disabilities
- Evaluate the cognitive, emotional and social development of students
- Assess the psychological needs of students
- Identify the specific needs of students with special educational needs
- Intervene in crisis situations within the establishment
- Implement prevention actions (harassment, dropping out of school, violence)
- Direct students towards specialized structures or professionals if necessary
- Carry out psychological tests
- Conduct individual follow-up interviews
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Managing emotional crisis situations
- Write a report, an activity report
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Lead discussion groups or psychoeducational workshops
- Collaborate with parents to support children's education
- Collaborate with educational and multidisciplinary teams
- Create a relationship of trust
- Encourage the expression of emotions
- Guarantee the confidentiality of information
- Ensure continuous professional monitoring
- Managing an emergency situation

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
