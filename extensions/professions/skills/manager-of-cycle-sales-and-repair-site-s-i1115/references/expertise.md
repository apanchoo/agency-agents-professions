# Manager of cycle sales and repair site(s)

## Scope

The cycle sales and repair site manager monitors and organizes the work of teams from several cycle stores or cycle repair sites. Is in permanent contact with suppliers of parts, equipment (cycle and cyclist), sales representatives of cycle manufacturers and partners (specifiers, service providers, major account customers) Develops the budget for stores and/or cycle repair sites in its local network, organizes inventories, carries out stock management Monitors fleets of cycles in maintenance Follows the processing of customer complaints from its network and in this sense also guarantees follow-up of guarantees Develops responses to calls offers as part of the deployment of cycle parks for private and public companies

## Related Job Titles

- Manager of bicycle sales and repair sites

## Activities and Skills

- Conduct annual individual and professional interviews
- Conduct a recruitment interview
- Recruit a collaborator
- Monitor sales activity and cycle maintenance by analyzing the results obtained (margin level, indicator, ratio)
- Identify gaps between actual and required skills
- Use management software to track sales

- Optimize repair processes to reduce intervention time
- Manage equipment and materials at cycle maintenance sites
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Organize and control a supply
- Organize inventories to optimize stock management
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Build a corrective action plan for the sales and maintenance activity of cycles and cycle products
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Analyze the results obtained, the different margin levels and the reference indicators and ratios
- Monitor the budget of cycle stores and cycle maintenance sites
- Apply labor law rules
- Enforce legislation in sales and maintenance operations of cycles and cycle products
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Respect the general conditions of sale
- Process cycle or cycle product warranties
- Advise customers on necessary maintenance and repair options
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Define a commercial action plan for cycle stores
- Build customer loyalty through quality service

- Organize and arrange the cycle sales area

- Respond to a call for tenders
- Choose and use analysis tools (prospecting, results, penetration, margins, etc.)
- Define a change management strategy
- Implement the change management project internally and externally
- Implement a commercial action plan to develop the commercial activity of cycle stores
- Demonstrate commercial acumen and interpersonal skills
- Raise team awareness of the actions to be taken (marketing, margins, etc.)
