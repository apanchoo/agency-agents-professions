# Animal production technician

## Scope

Neither a farm worker nor an engineer, the animal production technician advises breeders on possible decisions likely to improve or optimize animal performance. Supervises and optimizes animal production processes to ensure product quality and quantity Controls the health and welfare of animals, paying attention to their diet and environment Manages animal reproduction and monitors the development of young Maintains animal production and health records in accordance with regulations Collaborates with veterinarians to prevent and treat animal diseases Participates in the research and implementation of new techniques to improve livestock efficiency
