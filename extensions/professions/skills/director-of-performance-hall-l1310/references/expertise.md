# Director of performance hall

## Scope

The director designs and develops the artistic and cultural, economic and social project of the company, where applicable within the framework of the policy defined by the supervisory authorities or the policy and commercial strategy defined by the corporate officer. Organizes and supervises the programming of shows and cultural events Manages the logistical and technical aspects of events Negotiates contracts with artists and companies Supervises the budget and financial resources of the venue Ensures compliance with safety and comfort standards for the public

## Related Job Titles

- Administrator / Theater administrator
- Director of choreographic center
- Circus director
- Festival Director
- Theater director
- Director of cultural enterprise
- Director of performing arts company
- Manager / Theater manager

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate activities related to public reception and security
- Supervise technical, administrative and artistic teams
- Define a communication strategy
- Develop the attractiveness of the place through communication and cultural action
- Manage public relations
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Oversee event promotion and marketing
- Resolve technical, organizational or artistic unforeseen circumstances
- Ensuring the safety of the public and artists
- Optimize the use of room spaces
- Plan the calendar of events and shows
- Supervise the delivery and installation of logistics and signage at the venue of a show
- Carry out operations related to human resources management
- Ensure compliance with ERP standards and live performance regulations
- Implement sustainable development initiatives
- Negotiate with service providers and suppliers
- Negotiate contracts, conventions and reception conditions for artists
- Optimize the operation of the venue (rental, ticketing, logistics, planning)
- Manage the overall strategy of the place and its cultural development
- Supervise the artistic programming consistent with the identity of the room
- Coordinate technical operations during events
- Establish partnerships with companies, communities and cultural institutions
- Organize and develop your professional network
