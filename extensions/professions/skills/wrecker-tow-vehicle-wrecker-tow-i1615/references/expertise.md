# Wrecker-tow / Vehicle wrecker-tow

## Scope

The vehicle towing company is an essential professional on the roads. Intervenes with professionals and individuals whose vehicle (car, motorcycle, truck, bus and coach) is in an accident or broken down Identifies and evaluates the problem encountered before deciding to tow or repair Ensures minor repairs (supply of fuel, tire replacement, etc.) at the site of the intervention while ensuring the safety of the intervention Tows the damaged vehicle to the appropriate garages or depots Manages the administrative formalities related to repair and towing of the vehicle, in conjunction with assistance companies, impounds, etc. Offers advice and assistance services to motorists in distress Maintains its recovery equipment in good working order

- Wrecker-tow truck / Wrecker-tow truck for road transport vehicles
