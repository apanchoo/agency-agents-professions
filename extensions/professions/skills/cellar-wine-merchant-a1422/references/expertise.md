# Cellar wine merchant

## Scope

The cellar manager participates in all stages of manufacturing, conservation and packaging of the wine, under the responsibility of the cellar master and in collaboration with the oenologist. Prepares the vats and receives the grapes during the harvest Performs all wine-making operations throughout the manufacturing process Bottling in compliance with hygiene and food safety rules Can participate in the blending of wines to produce the vintages

- Develop strategies to improve wine quality

- Carry out chemical control tests during the fermentation process and issue recommendations (additions of ingredients)
- Select a sample during production
- Analyze a production document
- Manage the production schedule
- Adjust the dosage of alcohol ingredients
- Conduct and control a fermentation process
- Carry out and follow the steps of producing an alcohol
- Develop a blend or a vintage
- Managing the temperature of fermentation tanks
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Prepare the cellars, clean and disinfect the vats
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Carry out winemaking operations (assembly, measurement of alcohol level, filtration, etc.)
- Drawing off a product after fermentation
- Supervise the aging of wines in vats or barrels, controlling temperature, humidity and aeration conditions
- Use and adjust agri-food machines or equipment
- Act quickly in the event of a technical problem
- Control the operation of winemaking equipment and monitor maintenance
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Optimize storage spaces
- Monitor wine storage conditions
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Analyze the quality of grapes or musts
- Control the clarity and limpidity of wines
- Check product storage conditions
- Ensure compliance with health and safety standards
- Monitor wine stock levels
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Carry out the placing in barrels or bottles, the capping, the labeling
- Receive and ship orders
- Receive the grapes and transport them to the vats
- Supervise the packaging of wine in bottles
- Complete the goods traceability tracking support
- Communicate effectively with suppliers
- Organize wine tasting events
