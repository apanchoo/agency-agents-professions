# Marine world researcher

## Scope

The marine world researcher dives into the heart of the oceans to unravel the mysteries of marine ecosystems, and contribute to the preservation of biodiversity and the anticipation of developments in this vital ecosystem for the planet.

## Related Job Titles

- Oceanologist

## Activities and Skills

- Contribute to scientific publications on climate
- Publish articles in specialized journals
- Design models based on oceanographic observations
- Conduct field missions to observe and study marine ecosystems
- Documenting underwater discoveries
- Develop mathematical models to predict changes in marine ecosystems
- Sampling organisms for taxonomic and genetic studies
- Study the interactions between marine species and their environment
- Observe the behavior of species in their natural habitat
- Participate in scientific expeditions
- Seek solutions for the conservation and restoration of ecosystems
- Take biological samples while respecting protocols
- Analyze oceanographic data as part of environmental studies
- Ensure computer modeling of biological processes in order to propose a model for observed biological phenomena
- Capturing specific marine species
- Develop marine monitoring strategies
- Analyze the microbiological quality of water
- Write reports on the state of marine areas
- Analyze the ecosystem to identify endangered species
- Analyze environmental data from marine areas
- Analyze environmental data to assess ecosystem health
- Analyze the impacts of underwater human activities
- Collaborate with environmental authorities to ensure the protection of marine ecosystems
- Make hypotheses about the evolution of the oceans and their biodiversity
- Establish marine forecasts and analyze propagation conditions in the marine environment
- Manage relationships with partners, investors, public bodies and regulatory authorities
- Interpret analysis results for strategic decisions
- Arguing for marine protection
- Liaise with academic authorities
- Communicate and cooperate with maritime authorities
- Communicate critical information to relevant authorities
- Educate the public on the importance of marine conservation
- Inform and raise public awareness of the impacts of climate change
- Organize your work within a multi-professional team
- Write scientific publications (posters, abstracts, articles, protocols, analysis plans, final reports) and format them
- Raise awareness of the importance of the ecological transition
- Collect biological data
