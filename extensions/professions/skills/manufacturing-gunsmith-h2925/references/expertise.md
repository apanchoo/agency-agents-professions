# Manufacturing gunsmith

## Scope

The Manufacturing Gunsmith designs and manufactures firearms, parts and gunsmithing accessories. Repairs and maintains firearms and related equipment by performing functional, safety and reliability tests Verifies compliance of weapons and ammunition with strict weapons manufacturing standards and regulations Operates machine tools to machine parts and assemble them Ensures regular maintenance of equipment and tools used in manufacturing Participates in the research and development of new models of weapons or accessories

## Related Job Titles

- Mechanic-armourer

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze weapon system malfunctions
- Carry out tests, experiments
- Follow technological developments in the field of gunsmithing
- Read and interpret an instruction sheet and an execution plan
- Design or develop manufacturing ranges
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Carry out the measurement and development of systems, mechanical equipment and production tools (mold, tools, etc.)
- Identify the adjustment and assembly phases of elements, systems, mechanical assemblies based on the technical file or model
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Finishing a part made of composite materials
- Make adjustments and finishes, assemble mechanical elements and subassemblies
- Use a tool, machine, equipment, installation
- Position and fix composite material parts together or with accessories
- Supervise the assembly of electronic components on weapon systems
- Check, position and fix the parts and elements of the assembly (frames, clusters, templates, structures, etc.)
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Repair equipment, a machine, an installation
- Define and implement security protocols
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Control the quality of manufactured weapons
- Detect defects, nuances, alloy of materials and determine the constraints of the part
- Check the conformity of the mounting and assembly
- Promote sustainability in product choice
