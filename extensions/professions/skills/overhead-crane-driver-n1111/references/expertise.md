# Overhead crane driver

## Scope

The Overhead Crane Driver, a key role in the management of lifting and handling operations. Ensures first-level maintenance of lifting equipment Meets strict safety standards to prevent any accidents Coordinates with ground teams to ensure efficient handling Monitors the status of loads and reports any anomalies Can train new operators in the safe use of overhead cranes

## Related Job Titles

- Gantry operator
- Gantry crane / Portico

## Activities and Skills

- Participate in regular safety training
- Use communications equipment
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Perform first level maintenance
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Carry out lifting and moving load operations according to the instructions of the ground teams
- Operate a mobile bridge
- Monitor weather conditions to adjust operations
- Analyze lifting needs on site
- Optimize load movement paths
- Suggest improvements to optimize security
- Ensure communication with ground teams
- Collaborate with maintenance teams
- Communicate instructions clearly
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Process information efficiently
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Areas of expertise

- Stress management in emergency situations
- Reading technical plans
- Updating mechanical knowledge
- Incident management

## Standards and processes

- Emergency and evacuation procedures
- Quality standards
- Visual inspection methods
- Principle of inertia of load balancing masses
- Technical documentation of equipment

## Professional techniques

- Use of remote control systems

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Handling a dangerous machine, equipment or tool
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Carrying and handling heavy or bulky loads
- On site
- Daytime work
- Work weekends and public holidays
