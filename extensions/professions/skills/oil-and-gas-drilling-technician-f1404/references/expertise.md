# Oil and gas drilling technician

## Scope

The oil and gas drilling technician participates in the implementation, monitoring and maintenance of drilling operations under the supervision of an engineer or site manager and can work on different types of equipment and processes. Prepares and installs drilling equipment, such as drill pipes, bits, and fluid circulation systems Monitors and controls drilling operations Performs preventive and corrective maintenance on drilling equipment Ensures compliance with safety procedures and environmental regulations on site Collaborates with other members of the drilling team, including engineers, specialist technicians, and machine operators May prepare and control drilling fluids
