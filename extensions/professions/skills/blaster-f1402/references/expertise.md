# Blaster

## Scope

The blaster carries out mining and slaughtering work allowing the production and exploitation of ores and rocks, carrying out earthworks, sanitation or underground work on public works sites, in quarries and mining operations. Controls the safety zone for loading shots Conducts loading and connection operations of the pyrotechnic chain (with possible assistance) Knows how to read a firing plan and implement it Masters the constraints (geological, environmental, etc.) Carries out a simple layout based on benchmarks Monitors and evaluates the effectiveness of explosions to optimize results Respects and applies environmental regulations during firing operations
