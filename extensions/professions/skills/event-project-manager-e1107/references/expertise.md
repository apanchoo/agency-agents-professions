# Event Project Manager

## Scope

Imagine and organize all or part of events, in order to serve the communication needs of your company or its clients. Ensures the success of each event (conferences, seminars, sporting and cultural events, festive evenings, inaugurations), taking into account the specifications and service providers. Coordinates all human, material, technical and logistical resources necessary for their smooth running. Can take care of communication around the project and look for possible partners, sponsors, patrons to be able to facilitate its implementation. Plans the tasks to be carried out and manages possible organizational and budgetary hazards throughout the project.
