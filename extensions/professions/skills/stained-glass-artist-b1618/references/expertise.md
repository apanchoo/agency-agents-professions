# Stained glass artist

## Scope

The stained glass artist is an artistic craftsman who creates stained glass windows, or restores stained glass windows, particularly those in religious buildings. Draws models which will serve as models for cutting the glass Works with the material, painting it if necessary, thus creating a sort of large-scale puzzle Crimps the glass pieces with lead rods

## Related Job Titles

- Stained glass restorer

- Determine the components of a hue, color
- Manually apply colors
- Assemble glass plates and panels
- Decorate glass
- Cutting glass

- Carry out crimping operations
- Carry out glass coloring

- Check the quality and aesthetics of the object after each intervention
- Monitor and check the supply of the crimping station with materials and consumables

- Clearly communicate artistic concepts to clients

- Check the feasibility of the order, define and agree with the customer the conditions of execution
