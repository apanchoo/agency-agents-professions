# Assistant / Assistant director

## Scope

The Assistant Director works closely with the director for the preparation and execution of live performance and cinematographic productions. Assists the director in the preparation and production of shows or filming Coordinates activities between the different artistic and technical departments Participates in rehearsing scenes to ensure compliance with the director's vision Manages props, costumes and sets according to the needs of the production Contributes to resolving logistical and technical problems during rehearsals and performances May assist in directing actors and coordinating movements on stage
