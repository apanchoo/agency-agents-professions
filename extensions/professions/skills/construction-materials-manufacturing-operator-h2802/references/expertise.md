# Construction materials manufacturing operator

## Scope

The construction materials manufacturing operator produces materials used in the construction sector on equipment or automated lines. Ensures first-level maintenance of machines to ensure their proper functioning Adjusts extrusion machines and rectifies manufacturing defects Controls the quality of materials produced while respecting safety standards and environmental protocols in force Collaborates with logistics teams to optimize the production chain
