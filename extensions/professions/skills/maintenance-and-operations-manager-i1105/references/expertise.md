# Maintenance and operations manager

## Scope

The maintenance and operations manager ensures the sustainability and efficiency of the infrastructure and/or equipment in the area entrusted to him. Supervises and coordinates equipment and infrastructure maintenance operations Plans preventive and corrective maintenance interventions Manages technical teams and organizes their work Ensures compliance of installations with safety and environmental standards Optimizes equipment costs and performance Develops strategies for continuous improvement of maintenance processes

## Related Job Titles

- Infrastructure project manager
- Head of Viability Security
- Infrastructure maintenance manager
- Electricity network operations manager
- Electricity operations manager
- Equipment and structure maintenance manager

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Train staff in the use of equipment
- Ensure technological monitoring to improve maintenance processes
- Define the infrastructure operating strategy
- Develop standardized maintenance procedures
- Optimize development and maintenance processes to reduce costs
- Propose continuous technical improvements
- Carry out infrastructure projects
- Organize infrastructure and building maintenance operations
- Ensure compliance of installations with current standards
- Design a maintenance plan
- Perform preventive maintenance of equipment
- Intervene in the event of a major breakdown to restore operation
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Control a maintenance operation
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Evaluate the risks linked to maintenance interventions
- Identify damage or incidents
- Monitor the general condition of equipment
- Ensure compliance of maintenance operations
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Manage spare parts stocks
- Write a report, an activity report
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Follow a contract, check compliance with contractual clauses
- Implement ecological solutions in maintenance practices
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Determine technical-economic solutions
- Evaluate the needs and operating requirements of an infrastructure
- Implement and anticipate the necessary actions to optimize the use of production means
- Implement performance indicators for equipment monitoring
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital tools
