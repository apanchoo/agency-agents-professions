# Head builder / Head set builder

## Scope

The Head Set Builder is in charge of the design and production of fixed and mobile set structures and elements for live performance, cinematographic or audiovisual productions. Designs, supervises, coordinates set construction activities according to the guidelines of the decoration team Manages the budgets and material resources necessary for the construction of sets Coordinates construction teams to meet production deadlines Ensures the quality and safety of constructed set structures Adapts sets according to filming or performance constraints

## Related Job Titles

- Head of set construction workshop

- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Organize and supervise the construction of the sets according to the specifications of the production designer
- Organize the work of a team
- Distribute the workload and tasks to be performed among team members
- Design sets and show props
- Create a sketch or model of sets and accessories
- Create an assembly plan
- Check the feasibility of a production
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Use modeling and simulation software

- Adapt the settings according to the scenario and its possible modifications

- Assemble a decor

- Implement decorative elements
- Assemble wooden structures
- Establish a schedule for carrying out the work
- Check the similarity of sets and props from the beginning to the end of the filming or performance
