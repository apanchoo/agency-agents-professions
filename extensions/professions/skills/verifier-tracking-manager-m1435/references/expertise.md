# Verifier tracking manager

## Scope

The tracking checker is a professional in the music industry responsible for controlling the use and distribution of works in order to monitor rights and exploitation. Verifies that the sums collected via SACEM correspond to the distribution of the works Records tours in France or abroad and verifies the collection by the corresponding management company Anticipates possible errors in reporting information (typo in the name, etc.)
