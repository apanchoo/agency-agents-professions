# Regional director of hypermarket or supermarket

## Scope

The regional hyper or supermarket manager is responsible for the management and development of stores in his region. Supervises and coordinates the activities of the various stores in the region Defines commercial objectives and ensures their achievement Ensures compliance with standards and regulations in force in all stores Optimizes management processes and store performance Manages relationships with suppliers and negotiates contracts Supervises and develops the skills of store managers

## Related Job Titles

- Regional Director / Regional Supermarket Director
- Regional Director / Regional Hypermarket Director

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Set individual and collective objectives for management teams
- Strengthen the leadership skills of managers
- Supervise and manage organizational, technical, human, financial, logistical and communication resources to achieve objectives
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Guarantee compliance with brand concepts and operational standards
- Optimize product flows to ensure stock availability
- Monitor sales figures and carry out associated reporting
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Ensure compliance with social, commercial and health legislation
- Implement ecological measures in store management
- Analyze market trends to anticipate customer needs
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and collect feedback
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Implement strategies to increase customer satisfaction
- Support the implementation of commercial and promotional policies
- Support store opening, renovation or restructuring projects
- Analyze performance indicators (turnover, margins, stocks, costs)
- Collaborate with external partners
- Define and deploy the commercial strategy in conjunction with national management
- Determine performance objectives, track achievements and identify corrective actions
- Evaluate the impact of pricing policies on sales
- Manage human, material and financial resources
- Optimize store organization and team productivity
- Manage the economic and financial performance of stores in the region
- Prospect a store location
- Supervise the management of a commercial entity
