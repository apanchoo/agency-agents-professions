# Shearer / Metallurgy shearer

## Scope

The Metal Shearer manufactures metal parts by cutting and shaping plates, bars or tubes using specialized machines, manual or automated. Intervenes at the start of the industrial process to prepare the elements necessary for production Monitors the machines and positions the parts according to the plans Analyzes the technical documents to prepare for cutting Performs routine maintenance on its equipment and reports any anomalies to guarantee optimal production flow
