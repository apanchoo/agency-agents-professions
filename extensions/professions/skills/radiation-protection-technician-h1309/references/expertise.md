# Radiation protection technician

## Scope

The Radiation Protection Technician plays a crucial role in protection against radiation and the safety of exposed environments. Monitors radiation levels and ensures the safety of exposed areas Conducts regular inspections and maintains protective equipment Trains staff in radiation safety procedures Conducts safety audits and writes detailed reports Responds to emergencies to limit accidental exposures Advises on improving safety protocols and preventative measures

## Related Job Titles

- Radiation protection agent
- Nuclear radiation protection agent
- Nuclear radiation protection technician
- Nuclear Radiation Protection Risk Prevention Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Provide technical support to quality, maintenance, methods, research and development services
- Analyze radiation protection data
- Ensuring the traceability of radioactive sources
- Carry out readings, measurements, samples, identify deviations (rate, threshold, etc.) and recommend corrective measures
- Analyze a malfunction or non-compliance
- Control radiological risk areas
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Monitor and regulate energy production equipment requiring authorization to operate a nuclear power plant (SN2, SN3, etc.)
- Monitor radiation exposure
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Develop exposure reduction strategies
- Implement security protocols
- Plan interventions in sensitive areas
- Propose solutions to improve safety at work
- Carry out or participate in carrying out risk studies (identification, census, evaluation, etc.)
- Check the operation of monitoring and treatment installations for discharges (water, smoke, etc.) or waste
- Ensure regulatory compliance in radiation protection
- Check the validity of qualification of means and people (authorization, authorization, approval)
- Control the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Prepare an accident and incident report
- Write a report, an activity report
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Participate in safety meetings
- Document radiation protection procedures
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing radiological emergencies
- Use digital tools
