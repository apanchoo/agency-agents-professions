## Scope

The Mail Liaison Officer ensures the efficient management and distribution of mail within organizations. Ensures the reception, sorting and distribution of internal and external mail Organizes mail distribution circuits to optimize delivery times Maintains regular communication with the various departments to guarantee smooth distribution Manages the sending of special mail and ensures the follow-up of important mailings May be required to manage administrative tasks related to mail, such as recording shipments and receipts Respects security and confidentiality procedures in handling mail

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Coordinate operations with other departments
- Use mail tracking software
- Proceed with recording, sorting and postage of mail
- Check the quality and condition of received mail
- Check the conformity of deliveries
- Encourage compliance with distribution deadlines
- Organize optimized delivery routes
- Organize a tour
