# Director of Connected Territories Projects

## Scope

As a project manager, orchestrates the management of innovative digital projects to energize territories and connect communities. Manages the project by coordinating the various actors and partners involved Assume responsibility for the project from the study phase to its completion Ensures compliance with the set objectives Provides technical responses to customer needs Analyzes the impacts of projects on the territory and proposes adjustments if necessary

## Related Job Titles

- Connected Territory Program Director

## Activities and Skills

- Promote exchange relationships and a climate of cooperation
- Plan and adapt, if necessary, the team's activity, assign tasks
- Carry out monitoring of an intervention team
- Supervise and manage organizational, technical, human, financial, logistical and communication resources to achieve objectives
- Ensure the interconnection of professional corporate communication systems to public networks
- Establish the schedule and establish the methods of execution of the site
- Monitor work progress schedules and make the necessary adjustments to meet deadlines and the committed budget
- Analyze information relating to the progress of the intervention taking into account execution plans and installation diagrams
- Monitor the work (facilitation of weekly monitoring meetings, writing of reports, quality management, cost, deadline, planning, hazard management, contradictory findings)
- Organize site personnel by ensuring reception, relations with staff representatives, interventions in the event of incidents or accidents
- Organize the operations to be carried out in order to respect deadlines and site constraints
- Organize workstations and define processes on the site in accordance with the safety, quality and budget standards provided for in the specifications
- Reassess the technical study, the forecast budget and develop the detailed budget for the site (volume of materials, necessary human and material resources, etc.)
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Coordinate the safety of a construction site
- Managing crises and unforeseen events in connected projects
- Supervise the implementation of digital solutions
- Identify equipment and material needs
- Ensure relationships and monitoring with the various project stakeholders (subcontractors, suppliers, end customers, communities, operators)
- Negotiate with suppliers and subcontractors
- Monitor and validate partners, suppliers, service providers participating in the project
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Record and process complaints from customers and beneficiaries
- Collect and define the needs of the company, customers and users in terms of smart systems (capacity, reliability, security, etc.)
- Ensure monitoring of customer satisfaction
- Design and promote a commercial offer
- Carry out technical monitoring of products and their evolution with customers
- Define digital development strategies
- Evaluate team performance using key indicators
- Promote connectivity initiatives
- Respect safety rules
- Develop your project management skills
