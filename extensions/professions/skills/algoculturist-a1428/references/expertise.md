# Algoculturist

## Scope

The Algoculteur (trice) is defined as the cultivator (trice) of the sea. Cultivates and harvests edible algae in a pond or in a marine environment according to environmental standards Selects cultivation sites adapted to the different species and monitors the growth of the algae Harvests the algae at maturity while minimizing the impacts on the marine ecosystem Can transform the harvested algae Can participate in the research and development of new cultivation techniques or in the design of new algae-based products

## Related Job Titles

- Seaweed producer
- Algae harvester

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Promote artisanal products on social networks
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Control the environmental parameters of algae ponds
- Install structures at sea such as floating networks, allowing the cultivation of algae
- Optimize algae growth conditions
- Harvesting algae sustainably
- Dry and prepare the seaweed for sale
- Transforming algae into consumer products (food supplements, cosmetics, for example)
- Adjust the level of a body of water
- Define the perimeter of an intervention zone
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Install prevention devices
- Analyze water quality for algae cultivation
- Monitor algae growth and health
- Packaging products
- Manage stocks of harvested algae
- Organize order processing
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Carry out accounting operations
- Promote sustainable aquaculture practices
- Carry out an environmental impact study
- Marketing the products of a farm
- Develop tourism marketing strategies
- Promote products to customers
- Promote seaweed products in the market
- Establish partnerships with distributors
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
