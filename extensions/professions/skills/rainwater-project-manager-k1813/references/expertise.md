# Rainwater project manager

## Scope

The Rainwater Project Manager supervises the management and implementation of policies and projects for the collection, treatment and use of rainwater. Develops and implements stormwater management strategies to prevent flooding and promote water reuse Collaborates with municipalities and government agencies to develop effective policies and regulations Conducts hydrological studies and diagnostics to assess stormwater management in a territory and the environmental impact Raise public and stakeholder awareness of the issue of sustainable rainwater management Implements rainwater collection and treatment systems with adjustments and improvements Ensures compliance with environmental standards and monitors current regulatory developments
