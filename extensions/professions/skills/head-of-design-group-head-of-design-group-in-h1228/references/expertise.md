# Head of design group / Head of design group in electricity and electronics

## Scope

The group leader designer in electricity and electronics supervises from the design to the implementation of projects in the electrical and electronic field. Collaborates in the production of electrical and electronic plans and diagrams according to current standards Coordinates and directs a team of designers and technicians to ensure the quality and conformity of projects Liaises with clients and other departments to ensure the accuracy of technical specifications Optimizes technical solutions according to budgetary constraints and project requirements Ensures the updating of the team's technical knowledge and the adoption of new technologies by monitoring corrections made to projects
