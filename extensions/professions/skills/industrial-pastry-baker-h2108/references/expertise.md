# Industrial pastry baker

## Scope

The industrial pastry baker creates large-scale bakery products according to hygiene and food safety rules. Manufactures different products Monitors the fermentation and cooking of dough Controls the quality of raw materials and finished products Optimizes production based on orders and sales forecasts Maintains the cleanliness of equipment and the workplace

## Related Job Titles

- Industrial baker
- Bakery operator
- Industrial bakery worker
- Kneader

## Activities and Skills

- Arguing about production choices
- Plan production operations according to orders
- Adjust machine settings as needed
- Make pastries
- Making pastries
- Develop new products or improve existing recipes
- Carry out the assembly and decoration of pastry products
- Master the different types of bread (traditional, sourdough, brioche, etc.) and their manufacturing methods
- Kneading bread and pastry dough
- Monitor fermentation of baked goods
- Act quickly in the event of a technical problem
- Ensure first level preventive maintenance of equipment
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Monitor the cooking of baked goods
- Ensure the traceability of production batches
- Carry out freshness control tests on food and preparations and take samples for the hygiene department
- Load, unload, handle products
- Packaging products
- Manage stocks of raw materials and finished products
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Establish production and incident reports
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Respect procedures, operating methods and instructions
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
