# Operator on hides and skins processing and finishing machine

## Scope

A Hides and Skins Processing and Finishing Machine Operator uses and monitors equipment to process, process and finish hides and skins, according to required specifications. Operates on specialized machines to process raw hides and skins into semi-finished or finished products Controls the quality of hides and skins at various stages of the processing process Makes adjustments to machines to ensure production accuracy and efficiency Monitors production parameters and adjusts processes according to specific material needs Maintains production equipment in good condition, performing regular cleaning and maintenance Follows strict safety and hygiene standards to ensure a safe working environment

## Activities and Skills

- Develop your skills by following training on new machines
- Adjust the thickness of skins and leathers depending on the final use of the item
- Adjust finishing machine parameters
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Driving a workstation
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine
- Prepare a product for processing
- Dry or frame skins and leathers according to defined stretching criteria
- Assemble, disassemble parts, machine tools
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Observe safety instructions when using machines
- Control the quality of treated hides and skins
- Load, unload, handle products
- Determine the allocation of skins and leathers according to use, customer orders, destination of products
- Group the skins according to their origin, size, thickness, with a view to their treatment
- Supervise the operation of materials processing and transformation facilities (control, adjustment, etc.)
