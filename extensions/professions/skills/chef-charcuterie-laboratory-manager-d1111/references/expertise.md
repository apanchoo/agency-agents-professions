# Chef/Charcuterie laboratory manager

## Scope

The charcuterie laboratory manager supervises the production of fine charcuterie with expertise and innovation. Supervises and coordinates production activities within the laboratory Controls the quality of raw materials and finished products Develops new recipes and improves manufacturing processes Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards Manages stocks of raw materials and finished products Trains and supervises laboratory staff

- Evaluate staff skills and performance
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Use production management software
- Plan production operations according to orders
- Know the recipes and manufacturing processes for different charcuterie products
- Develop new products or improve existing recipes
- Master the techniques for manufacturing charcuterie products (cutting, salting, cooking, smoking, etc.)
- Curing meat
- Smoking meat
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Supervise cleaning and disinfection operations
- Supervise production operations in charcuterie
- Monitor the condition of equipment and plan necessary maintenance
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Carry out tests and experiments to innovate and create new products
- Perform tests and analyzes to verify compliance with quality standards
- Identify and resolve quality issues
- Optimize production processes to reduce waste
- Supervise production to guarantee product quality and conformity
- Define supply needs
- Carry out the packaging in boxes, trays, trays, etc.
- Supervise inventory management

- Optimize production costs
