# Financial analyst

## Scope

The Financial Analyst evaluates the financial performance of companies to guide investment decisions. Analyzes financial statements and business reports to assess a company's financial health Develops financial models to forecast future results Assesses financial risks and suggests mitigation strategies Presents investment recommendations based on detailed analyzes Tracks market trends and investment performance Participates in the preparation of financial reports and communication of results to stakeholders

## Related Job Titles

- Derivatives Analyst
- Quantitative financial analyst
- Financial analysis and engineering manager
- Financial Advisor/Technical Advisor
- Financial engineer

- Integrate financial mathematical models into information systems

- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Establish an evolution curve for financial products

- Calculate financial ratios
- Develop instruments to assess the fragility and sensitivity of the company
- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Analyze the financial flows of a company
- Analyze a financial statement
- Check the reliability of financial statements
- Estimate the value of the company analyzed
- Establish a recommendation for the sale of financial assets
- Evaluate financial risks
- Evaluate and control the financial risks of an operation
- Prepare and present detailed financial reports to stakeholders
- Carry out a financial audit
- Analyze and identify trends and consumer preferences
- Analyze financial markets
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Carry out a market study, assess the potential of a product or service
- Monitor market trends to anticipate developments
- Monitor performance indicators, analyze and correct discrepancies
- Evaluate the financial impact of strategic decisions
- Expertise projects and implement merger-acquisition operations
