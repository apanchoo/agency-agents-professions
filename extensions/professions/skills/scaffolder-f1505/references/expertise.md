# Scaffolder

## Scope

The Scaffolder assembles, modifies and adapts all types of scalding to allow safe access to construction sites. Adapts the scaffolding to the specificities of the sites and the needs of the different trades to guarantee safety Regularly checks the condition of the scaffolding and carries out the necessary repairs Dismantles the installation at the end of the construction site Can collaborate with site managers to plan the assembly and dismantling of the installations Can train and supervise a team of scaffolding erectors

## Related Job Titles

- Scaffolder / Scaffolder in shipbuilding
- Scaffolding installer
- Scaffolder

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Communicate effectively with other trades
- Coordinate work with other trades
- Read and use the scaffolding installation diagram
- Close a construction site and clear the work area (machinery, materials, waste, etc.)
- Fix the elements of a metal structure
- Install metal structural elements
- Assemble or dismantle scaffolding
- Prepare the execution of the site
- Clean construction site tools and equipment
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Manage unforeseen events and adapt plans accordingly
- Put in place protections to secure the site
- Permanently respect established safety rules
- Check the operation of safety equipment
- Check the conformity of materials
- Load, unload, handle products
- Define a storage area
- Install and use mobile scaffolding in compliance with regulatory and safety conditions
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Handle material vertically at height, using winches or pulleys
- Store equipment
- Transport materials and equipment to the site
- Prepare site reports with the works manager or the client
- Attend site meetings
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Act quickly in the event of an incident
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Use digital tools
