# Adapted physical activity coordinator

## Scope

The adapted physical activity coordinator is responsible for adapted physical activity (APA) projects and provides his expertise within the cross-functional projects of the services in which he is located. Organizes APA support for APA teachers in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team and in agreement with the school project Plans, organizes and regulates the activity of teachers in adapted physical activity Ensures communication between the different stakeholders in the project Develops its network in sport - health Designs an educational tutoring project with a view to the transfer of skills and professional practices in its field of activity
