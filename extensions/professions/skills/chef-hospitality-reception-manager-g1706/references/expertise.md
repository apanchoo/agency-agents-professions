# Chef/Hospitality reception manager

## Scope

The Hospitality Reception Manager plays a key role in guaranteeing a welcome and quality service to the establishment's guests. Supervises and coordinates the activity of the hotel reception to ensure an optimal welcome of guests Manages reservations, arrivals and departures of guests, ensuring their satisfaction Trains and supervises the team of receptionists to maintain high quality service Resolves problems and complaints of guests to improve their experience Optimizes reception processes to improve efficiency and quality of service Collaborates with other departments of the hotel to ensure smooth and efficient management

## Related Job Titles

- Head of hotel reception brigade
- First / First reception in hotels
- Reception manager in outdoor hotels
- Hotel reception manager
- Responsible for hotel reservations

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Ensure coordination between the different hotel departments
- Evaluate and develop the skills of your employees
- Plan and organize the activity of staff under their responsibility
- Update customer information in the computer system
- Check access, locations (closure, presence of objects, people, etc.), equipment and security and prevention systems
- Analyze office supply needs and manage supplies
- Track orders, invoicing
- Track reservations
- Manage reservation schedules
- Liaise with other departments within the establishment
- Prepare financial and activity reports
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Monitor the safety of property and people within the hotel
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Inform, inform, guide a person on the services offered
- Optimize the organization of the reception
- Ensure monitoring of customer satisfaction
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Implement strategies to increase customer satisfaction
- Offer additional services to improve the stay
- Ensure the moderation of comments linked to the e-reputation of the structure or company
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
