# Stage Manager

## Scope

The Stage Manager manages and coordinates all technical activity on stage for shows or events. Coordinates the installation and dismantling of sets and stage equipment Ensures the technical management of shows while ensuring compliance with safety standards Supervises the technical teams and coordinates their activities during performances Maintains fluid communication between the different artistic and technical stakeholders Can participate in the design of stage plans and tour logistics

## Related Job Titles

- Rehearsal location manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Collaborate with lighting and sound teams
- Coordinate technical and artistic teams
- Manage team schedules
- Maintain team cohesion and motivation
- Ensure technological monitoring of new usable materials
- Create a sketch or model of sets and accessories
- Adjust technical details during rehearsals
- Develop and adapt the implementation plan for stage equipment
- Implement decorative elements
- Install stage structures
- Install stage lighting equipment
- Install sound equipment
- Optimize the use of stage space in your performance (lighting, sets, accessories, etc.)
- Supervise the assembly and dismantling of stage structures
- Ensure compliance of installations with safety standards
- Maintain stage equipment in good condition
- Plan preventive maintenance of equipment
- Resolve technical issues during performances
- Preventing professional risks
- Improve production processes
- Check the quality and reliability of the installation
- Coordinate the installation of pyrotechnic equipment
- Coordinate the transport of stage equipment
- Move set elements and equipment according to the movement of the actors
- Supervise the delivery and installation of logistics and signage at the venue of a show
- Handle the logistical, technical and administrative aspects related to the shows
- Manage a schedule
- Negotiate with stage equipment suppliers
- Provide solutions to different artistic and technical requests
- Ensure that activities are carried out in compliance with quality, cost and deadline objectives
- Communicate effectively with the team to ensure consistency of work
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Managing internal conflicts
- Manage unforeseen events, hazards and last minute changes
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect the deadlines and preparation times necessary for events, shows
- Respect rehearsal and performance schedules
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Know how to manage several projects or activities simultaneously

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes

- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
