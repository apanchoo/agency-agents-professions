# Heat treatment operator

## Scope

The heat treatment operator applies heat and thermochemical treatment in order to modify the mechanical and structural properties and characteristics of parts according to safety rules and production requirements (deadlines, quality, etc.). Can coordinate a team

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Carry out tests, experiments
- Complete the intervention documents
- Provide quality and production monitoring support
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Adapt, adjust an article or production according to need
- Adjust machine temperature settings
- Apply treatments adapted to the materials
- Remove incandescent parts, inductors and check their surface condition, appearance
- Position the parts (basket, grill, etc.), the heating equipment or place the parts in the oven
- Prepare the parts of thermal equipment
- Carry out heat treatment operations
- Carry out operations on tank, bath and crucible equipment
- Carry out an intervention requiring authorization
- Carry out a sanding, polishing, smoothing operation
- Adjust or control equipment, baths or treatment products (fluidity, temperature, etc.)
- Process plastic materials and steels
- Process minerals, glass, ceramics
- Transform and treat metals
- Check the conformity of the parts in relation to the manufacturing order
- Carry out the assembly/disassembly of preheating/heating tools (inductors, nozzles, etc.)
- Detect malfunctions then intervene within the limits of your skills or call on a professional who can remedy them
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Ensure self-checks of parts in relation to the manufacturing sheet
- Monitor the treatment (heating, cooling, bath, etc.), identify malfunctions and apply corrective measures
- Analyze the appearance of a product
- Analyze process quality
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Select or control heat treatment parameters and cycles
- Moving bulky, heavy and large products
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Foster a collaborative work environment
- Integrate into the team and show cooperation
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Respect the instructions relating to the discharge of fluids into the environment
