# Geotechnician

## Scope

The geotechnician, a field professional, studies the resistance of soils and subsoils (rocks) upstream of a project, in order to adapt the work to a given terrain and avoid natural risks. Carry out samples and soil surveys to assess geological risks Study geological and geophysical data on soils, subsoils, deposits and analyze their characteristics Carry out feasibility studies upstream of construction or drilling projects Develop technical files Collaborate with other engineers and construction or industry professionals (oil, mining, etc.)

## Related Job Titles

- Geological technician
- Geological probe technician
- Field geologist technician
- Technician / Study geologist
- Geological laboratory technician

## Activities and Skills

- Analyze solid, liquid, gaseous samples (water, sludge, waste) and record the results
- Collect samples for analysis
- Define compositions, geological models
- Determine technical solutions and recommendations
- Carry out feasibility studies for construction projects
- Carry out studies of foundations, settlement or stability of land (sliding, erosion, resistance)
- Analyze the geological characteristics of a site
- Analyze, exploit, structure data
- Map data
- Create technical documentation
- Establish analysis and measurement results
- Interpret geological surveys of soils, subsoils, rocks and study the characteristics (extents, compositions, geometry, etc.)
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Prospecting for extraction and drilling operations
- Carry out surveys, drilling, soil and subsoil pollution
- Process geotechnical data accurately
- Determining the density of bedrock
- Participate in the cleaning and maintenance of drilling machines and all equipment (truck, etc.)
- Assess geological risks in construction projects
- Optimize processes to reduce environmental risks
- Control the quality of a raw material, a resource
- Write detailed study reports
- Respect allocated deadlines and budgets
- Ensure compliance of projects with environmental standards
- Interpret and use scientific and economic information
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate study results to stakeholders
- Communicate, read and write technical documents, reports, notes in English
- Inform your superiors of the results during the different stages of the study
- Promote a proposal, a project
- Collect and analyze data, information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Monitor and update technical, economic, regulatory information, etc.
- Regularly update your knowledge
- Develop your technical skills

## Professional Approach
