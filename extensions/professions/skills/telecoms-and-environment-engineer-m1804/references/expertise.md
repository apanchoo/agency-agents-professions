# Telecoms and environment engineer

## Scope

The telecoms and environment engineer designs, optimizes and secures environmentally friendly telecoms networks. Designs, develops and optimizes architectures as well as technical solutions for telecommunications networks (telephony, multimedia, IT, etc.), integrating environmental issues and the digital strategy of the company or client Can coordinate a team or a project Takes charge of the security and technical aspects linked to telecoms work Organizes the execution, monitoring and carrying out of simple operating tests according to regulations

## Related Job Titles

- Telecom deployment engineer
- Engineer in telecoms architecture
- Engineer / Telecoms studies and support engineer
- Radio engineer
- Telecom network engineer
- Telecommunications Engineer
- Telecoms Engineer
- Telecom network validation engineer
- Network planner

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Write engineering documents (writing functional specifications and technical deliverables for solutions)
- Carry out tests, experiments
- Direct write access to machines and servers to configure
- Ensure the quality and updating of technical databases
- Ensure continuous monitoring of emerging technologies
- Design the design, specifications or configurations of a telecoms network
- Build a network development plan
- Establish the implementation plan
- Model and anticipate network uses
- Design and implement the technical and environmental strategy of the telecoms operator
- Determine the installation of relays, pylons and cables taking into account the environmental impact
- Study potential land
- Diagnose technical malfunctions
- Design and maintain a cybersecurity system
- Guarantee the proper functioning, availability and performance of network elements or software under your responsibility
- Apply environmental standards and regulations
- Evaluate and reduce the environmental footprint of infrastructure and equipment
- Integrate eco-design and energy optimization practices into telecom projects
- Provide technical assistance to teams
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Design and manage a project
- Monitor and negotiate the implementation of telecom network infrastructure (pylons, antennas, cables, etc.)
- Network and cooperate with diverse professional profiles
- Document technical procedures and configurations
