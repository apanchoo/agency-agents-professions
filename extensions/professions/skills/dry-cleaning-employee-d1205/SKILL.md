## Scope

The dry cleaner is a professional essential to the cleanliness and presentation of textiles. Treats textile items (clothing, draperies, etc.) to remove stains and clean them Uses specific products and techniques to stain and prepare items prior to cleaning Operates dry cleaning machines and other specialized equipment Follows health and safety standards in textile processing Advises customers on the proper treatment of their clothing and textile items May manage daily dry cleaning operations, including receiving and returning items to customers

## Skills to Apply

- Apply a treatment, a product
- Use and adjust textile devices and equipment
- Ensure routine maintenance or cleaning of equipment after use under the required health and safety conditions
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Ironing textiles (clothes, linen)
- Identify a stain or dirt
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
