# Dry cleaning employee

## Scope

The dry cleaner is a professional essential to the cleanliness and presentation of textiles. Treats textile items (clothing, draperies, etc.) to remove stains and clean them Uses specific products and techniques to stain and prepare items prior to cleaning Operates dry cleaning machines and other specialized equipment Follows health and safety standards in textile processing Advises customers on the proper treatment of their clothing and textile items May manage daily dry cleaning operations, including receiving and returning items to customers

## Related Job Titles

- Primer-detacher
- Detacher
- Dry cleaning receptionist
- Dry cleaner

## Activities and Skills

- Carry out delicate montages
- Apply a treatment, a product
- Distinguish between textile raw materials and their properties
- Detect assembly difficulties, non-conformities (aesthetics, falling clothing, fit) and provide technical solutions
- Implement product quality control procedures
- Prepare an item for ironing
- Carry out filling operations (hemming, topstitching, etc.) on textile items
- Use and adjust textile devices and equipment
- Carry out the zipper installation
- Ensure routine maintenance or cleaning of equipment after use under the required health and safety conditions
- Check the cleanliness of the laundry
- Remove tissue and skin
- Determine cleaning methods based on fabric types (dry wash, laundry, ironing, etc.)
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Ironing textiles (clothes, linen)
- Carry out a recoloring operation
- Practice safe handling gestures and postures
- Control the application of quality procedures
- Carry out control readings on the work sheets
- Identify a stain or dirt
- Check the quality of textile products (dimensions, appearance, seams, etc.)
- Load the laundry into the machines and start the program
- Control a supply
- Carry out collection and delivery of items to customers
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Apply rules and protocols aimed at limiting the effects of the activity on the environment
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Identify and process a customer request
- Return the items to the customer after checking cleaning, ironing
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Create a quote
- Organize the flow of clothes to be ironed to optimize time
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Know and interpret the safety pictograms on products and materials and deduce the associated usage precautions
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital tools
