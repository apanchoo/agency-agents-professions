# Gallery owner

## Scope

The gallery owner, art dealer, an enthusiast whose activity consists of buying and selling objects or works of art. Organizes and supervises art exhibitions to promote artists and their works Collaborates with artists and collectors to acquire or sell works of art Advises clients and collectors on potential acquisitions Manages the administrative and financial aspects of the gallery, including the negotiation of contracts Develops professional networks to increase the visibility of the gallery Participates in international art fairs and events to represent the gallery and its artists

## Related Job Titles

- Assistant / Gallery assistant
- Art gallery manager

## Activities and Skills

- Organize temporary or permanent exhibitions
- Organize thematic exhibitions
- Organize openings and meetings with artists
- Promote exhibitions via various communication channels
- Maintain a database of works and artists
- Use electronic payment equipment
- Ensure that works are properly displayed, maintained and presented
- Identify and choose works to exhibit and sell
- Plan and organize artist exhibitions in the gallery
- Supervise the installation of works in the gallery
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Managing the financial aspects of art sales
- Analyze the authenticity and provenance of works of art
- Study and evaluate works, pieces, collectibles
- Locate copies, reproductions of works of art
- Negotiate contracts with artists or lenders
- Welcome visitors and present the exhibited works
- Advise clients on acquisitions of works
- Analyze art market trends
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Prepare items for sale
- Present and promote a product or service
- Search for art, old or second-hand objects from professionals or individuals
- Develop partnerships with cultural institutions
- Promote the artists represented at dedicated events (exhibitions, openings, participation in art fairs, etc.)
- Search for works
