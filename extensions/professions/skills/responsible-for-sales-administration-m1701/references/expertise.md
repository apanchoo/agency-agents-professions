# Responsible for sales administration

## Scope

The sales administration manager organizes or manages sales contracts from receipt of orders to delivery of products to the customer. Supervises and coordinates all activities related to sales administration Ensures the management of orders, from receipt to invoicing and payment tracking Optimizes the flow of information between the different departments (sales, logistics, finance) Controls the compliance of sales contracts and manages possible disputes Analyzes sales performance and proposes improvements to increase efficiency Can participate in the definition of the commercial strategy and the implementation of new management tools
