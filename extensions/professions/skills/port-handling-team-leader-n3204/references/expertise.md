# Port Handling Team Leader

## Scope

The Port Handling Team Leader, a key role in the efficiency and safety of port operations. Supervises and coordinates loading and unloading operations of goods Ensures compliance with safety and quality standards during handling Organizes the team's work according to schedules and emergencies Trains new employees in procedures and the use of specific equipment Checks the condition of goods before and after handling, and reports any anomalies Optimizes storage spaces and ensures good management of goods flows

## Related Job Titles

- Port handling dock manager
- Deputy dock manager (transport)

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Encourage innovation within teams
- Ensure the integration of new recruits (presentation to teams, safety training, etc.)
- Develop training plans adapted to staff needs
- Provide activity monitoring materials
- Organize and optimize the physical storage of products and materials within its scope of activity
- Supervise and coordinate surveillance operations of a port site (fire protection, emergency plan, etc.) and develop emergency intervention plans
- Evaluate the risks linked to handling operations
- Promote occupational health and safety procedures
- Ensure monitoring of current environmental standards
- Load, unload, handle products
- Packaging products
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Use handling equipment safely
- Coordinate loading and unloading operations
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Ensure compliance with handling deadlines
- Analyze team performance and suggest improvements
- Develop strategies for managing peak activity
- Optimize work procedures to reduce waiting times
- Maintain clear and professional communication
- Document handling procedures for continuing education
- Managing internal conflicts
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate leadership
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Non-motorized handling equipment (pallet truck, hand truck, etc.)
- Lifting equipment

## Areas of expertise

- Advanced Trading Techniques
- Handling gestures and postures
- Optimization of goods flows

## Standards and processes

- Safety rules related to dangerous materials and products
- Regulations relating to the operation of handling equipment
- Safety standards

## Professional techniques

- Management of relations with port authorities

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- In the port area
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity

## Sectors

- Logistics and transportation
