## Scope

The logistics operator contributes to the efficiency and performance of the organization of logistics operations and which concern the flow of goods, within the organization which employs him, in coordination with several other services, sometimes not linked to logistics flows (e.g.: production chain). Receives, stores and prepares products for delivery to a given internal department or customer, in accordance with company procedures Ensures the loading and unloading of goods Participates in taking inventories to ensure stock accuracy Maintains the cleanliness and organization of the storage area (warehouse, store) to ensure a safe working environment May use material handling equipment such as industrial carts, automated or manual

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Work safely, for yourself and in relation to others
- Control a handling operation
- Load, unload, handle products
- Sort and distribute packages and goods according to instructions (coding, format, weight, number, etc.)
- Process information relating to orders and customer specifications
- Communicate effectively with carriers
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Comply with current safety standards and regulations
