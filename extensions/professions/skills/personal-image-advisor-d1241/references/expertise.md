# Personal Image Advisor

## Scope

The personal image consultant enhances the appearance and style of his clients to strengthen their self-confidence. Analyzes clients' needs and expectations in terms of image and style Offers personalized advice in colorimetry, hairstyle, morphology, makeup and clothing Supports clients in their transformation and monitoring of image by working on bodily and oral expression Organizes workshops and seminars on style, personal image and the sense of aesthetics Collaborates with fashion and beauty professionals to offer comprehensive services Maintains a watch on the latest fashion trends and advice in picture
