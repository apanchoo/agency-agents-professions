# Ornithologist

## Scope

Passionate about birds and their ecosystem, the ornithologist combines science and field to study, protect and raise awareness of avian biodiversity. Studies bird species (migration, reproduction, feeding) via observations, ringing or analyzes Participates in the preservation of natural areas (parks, reserves) and the restoration of ecosystems Carry out impact studies for development projects (wind turbines, urbanization) Conducts workshops, conferences or naturalist outings for the general public or schools Collects and analyzes data for scientific or regulatory reports Collaborates with different stakeholders: associations, communities or companies for conservation projects
