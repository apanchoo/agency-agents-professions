# Driver of shaping and routing machines

## Scope

The Finishing and Routing Machine Operator supervises automated printing processing and routing operations by optimizing production processes. Operates and monitors shaping and routing machines to ensure production quality Controls compliance of finished products with quality standards Provides first-level maintenance of machines to prevent breakdowns Coordinates with production teams to optimize workflows Respects current safety standards and environmental protocols
