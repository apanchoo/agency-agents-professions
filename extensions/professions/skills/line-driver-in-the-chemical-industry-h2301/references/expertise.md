# Line driver in the chemical industry

## Scope

A line operator in the chemical industry is responsible for the management and optimization of a production line in a chemical factory. Supervises and controls the proper functioning of chemical production facilities Ensures the start-up, monitoring and shutdown of production line machines Controls the quality of products during manufacture and adjusts parameters if necessary Performs first-level maintenance operations and reports anomalies Respects and enforces strict safety and environmental standards Documents production procedures and results to ensure traceability
