# Manager of automobile trading

## Scope

The automobile trading manager supervises the management and marketing of vehicles. Manages stocks of new and used vehicles Negotiates purchases and sales of vehicles Optimizes the commercial and marketing strategy of the business Supervises the sales team and ensures the training of new employees Ensures compliance of commercial practices with current legislation

## Related Job Titles

- Used vehicle dealer
- Trader of new vehicles

- Carry out operations related to human resources management
- Write a report, an activity report
