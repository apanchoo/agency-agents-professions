# Bus Controller

## Scope

The bus controller is at the heart of interaction with users, guaranteeing a quality transport service on buses. Welcomes and informs passengers about timetables, fares and regulations Ensures the sale of transport tickets and management of transactions Contributes to the safety of passengers and the proper conduct of operations Reports incidents and anomalies Participates in the promotion of transport services to users

## Related Job Titles

- Road passenger transport network controller

## Activities and Skills

- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Develop your crisis management and communication skills
- Implement emergency alert procedures and other predefined measures in the event of an incident
- Ensuring the safety and comfort of passengers during transport
- Proceed to cashout
- Use electronic ticketing systems and manage transactions
- Ensure compliance with passenger transportation laws
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Control access on board and check tickets
- Monitor passenger behavior to prevent incidents
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Collaborate with other team members to improve service quality
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Sell a service or product
- Communicate effectively with the control center in the event of an emergency
- Inform users of the security rules to follow
- Respect public transport standards and regulations

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate self-control
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Passenger transport vehicles

## Standards and processes

- Public transport emergency and safety procedures
- Transport ticket control procedures
- Safety rules in public spaces

## Professional techniques

- Managing difficult behavior

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Public sector employee
- In contact with the public
- Prolonged standing
- Work in staggered hours
- Daytime work
- Work weekends and public holidays
