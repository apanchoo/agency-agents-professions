# Army Super Heavy Duty Truck Driver

## Scope

The Army Super Heavy Duty Driver plays a crucial role in the transportation and logistics of military operations. Provides transportation of heavy equipment and troops across various terrain and weather conditions Operates specialized vehicles such as tank carriers and semi-trailers for specific missions Maintains vehicles in good operational condition, performing regular checks and minor repairs Follows strict security protocols to ensure the safety of cargo and personnel Participates in planned logistics missions and contingency deployments based on Army needs Collaborates with other military team members to ensure effective coordination during operations

## Related Job Titles

- Truck driver - machine carrier
- Army PFM Semi-Trailer Driver
