# Fabricator of decorative works in wood and associated materials

## Scope

The Fabricator of decorative works in wood and associated materials designs and produces pieces in wood and various materials according to specific customer requests. Selects the right wood and materials Shapes and finishes the pieces to guarantee aesthetic quality and durability Can restore or repair old works Can collaborate with designers and architects to create tailor-made elements Can lead a team and manage their own activity
