## Scope

The Fabricator of decorative works in wood and associated materials designs and produces pieces in wood and various materials according to specific customer requests. Selects the right wood and materials Shapes and finishes the pieces to guarantee aesthetic quality and durability Can restore or repair old works Can collaborate with designers and architects to create tailor-made elements Can lead a team and manage their own activity

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Apply finishing techniques to wood surfaces
- Make, make items
- Shape wooden elements on a traditional machine (flat top, flying top, scroll top, etc.) by manually guiding the tool or part
- Shape wooden elements on a numerically controlled or automated machine
- Select wood or composite materials according to the project
- Use hand tools to adjust pieces of wood
- Ensure first-level maintenance of your work tools
- Control the quality of materials and finished products
