# Meat processing operator

## Scope

Carries out the various meat processing operations (beef, sheep, pork). Bones and cuts meats until prepared and packaged into pieces and pieces. Controls its production by following QHSE instructions and rules.

## Related Job Titles

- Industrial butcher
- Deboner / Boning machine
- Boning trimmer
- Meat processing manufacturing operator
- Meat cutting worker
- Meat preparer in industry

## Activities and Skills

- Handle animals
- Perform anesthesia or euthanasia on an animal
- Respect animal welfare
- Slaughtering and cleaning animals
- Sharpen a tool, equipment
- Rid the skin of flesh, vessels and surface impurities
- Cutting, deboning, trimming meat
- Skinning an animal
- Identify types and cuts of meat
- Prepare materials for work
- Curing meat
- Carry out the calibration of a carcass
- Smoking meat
- Carry out a weighing
- Processing raw materials of animal origin
- Emptying and cleaning animals
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Identify the cause of an anomaly
- Analyze and prevent risks
- Determine corrective measures
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Guarantee the traceability of reagents, products, media and living materials
- Packaging products
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Carry out packaging of fresh produce
- Carry out the offal sorting operation
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Sorting pieces of meat
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Relay information
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
