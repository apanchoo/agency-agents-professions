## Scope

The cattle breeder ensures the care necessary for the management of breeding according to production objectives and with respect for animal welfare. Manages and monitors the health and development of cattle on a daily basis Ensures the animals are fed and hydrated according to their specific needs Carries out reproductive operations and monitors the development of calves Maintains livestock facilities and equipment in good condition Markets livestock products, such as meat and milk Can cultivate plants intended for animal feed (fodder, cereals, etc.) Can supervise a team and manage an activity
