# Statistician engineer

## Scope

Expert in data analysis and interpretation, you guide in making strategic decisions. Analyzes and interprets data to support decision-making in various industries Designs statistical models to predict and explain observed phenomena Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to integrate statistical analysis into projects Uses statistical software and data science tools to process and analyze data Presents results of analyzes in clear and detailed reports for stakeholders Stays up to date with the latest trends and techniques in statistics and data analysis
