# Patient partner / Patient partner in health

## Scope

The patient partner in health or caregiver partner mobilizes experiential knowledge from their experience of living with an illness, a disability, a health problem or the support of a loved one in order to collaborate with the people concerned, professionals, establishments and organizations. Contributes to the improvement of health and life pathways, the quality and safety of care and support, the experience of people, training, research, governance and the organization of the health, social and medico-social sectors. to the evaluation of training, information and awareness-raising actions intended for professionals, students, patients, relatives and users Contributes to the co-construction, implementation and evaluation of health pathways, professional practices, projects, services and organizations Supports people in the development of their power to act, their autonomy and their participation in decisions that concern them

## Related Job Titles

- Expert patient
- Patient intervener
- Patient partner
- Patient partner / Patient resource partner
- Patient partner researcher
- Patient training partner

## Activities and Skills

- Develop and manage a network of partners and service providers
- Promote exchange relationships and a climate of cooperation
- Optimize resources allocated to partnership projects
- Welcoming, reassuring, guiding, informing a person and those around them
- Analyze the experience, needs and expectations of those involved
- Lead, organize and adapt your educational action according to the needs of the person
- Ensure an interface function between the public and the health system
- Co-build pathways, projects, actions and organizations with the people concerned and professionals
- Promote patient expression and communication
- Promote the development of the power to act, through individual or collective projects
- Participate in training, information and awareness-raising actions by mobilizing your lived experience
- Mobilize your lived experience with your peers and professionals
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Adapt your posture by relying on new communication tools and uses
- Participate in consultation, governance and decision-making bodies in the health, social and medico-social sectors
- Contribute to research, innovation and evaluation projects in health and social sciences
- Participate in initiatives to improve quality, safety, certification and people's experience
- Participate in the creation or updating of regulations, codes of conduct or patient rights
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Contribute to the development of collaborative projects in partnership with the people concerned and organizations
- Identify and mobilize resources promoting the development of projects and partnership approaches
- Know how to work cross-functionally
- Adapt your professional posture to the contexts of intervention and the interlocutors
- Lead or co-lead groups, workshops or discussion spaces in an approach to sharing experience and peer support
- Contribute to the circulation and sharing of experiential knowledge within organizations
- Coordinate therapeutic education programs in line with the needs of beneficiaries
- Develop and maintain a relationship of trust in a health partnership and peer-help approach
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Organize your work within a multi-professional team
- Participate in the coordination of courses, projects or actions between the different stakeholders involved
- Reformulate comments to promote mutual understanding
- Adapt your communication to your interlocutors
- Adapt your behavior and your professional practice to situations
- Design or co-design supports promoting information, participation and partnership in health
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events
- Promote values of respect and tolerance
- Respect the rules of confidentiality, ethics and impartiality
- Use communication tools adapted to the needs of the person
- Use digital tools
- Regularly update your knowledge

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

- Writing professional writing

- Measurement of patient-reported outcomes and experience (PROMs, PREMs, etc.)
- Therapeutic patient education (ETP)
