## Scope

The climatologist, an expert in climatic phenomena, plays a crucial role in understanding climatic variations and their impacts on the environment. Analyzes and interprets weather data to study climates and their variations Models climate forecasts to anticipate changes and their consequences Contributes to research projects to improve the understanding of climate systems Collaborates with international scientists to exchange data and insights on climate Writes scientific reports and articles to share discoveries with the scientific community and the general public Participates in conferences to discuss the latest advances in climate science
