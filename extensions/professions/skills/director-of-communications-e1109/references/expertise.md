# Director of Communications

## Scope

The Communications Director supervises and coordinates all activities related to the company's internal and external communications. Defines and implements the communications strategy to promote the company's image Supervises the creation of content and manages relations with the media Coordinates digital and traditional communications campaigns Analyzes the impact of communications actions and adjusts strategies accordingly Manages and supervises the communications team Collaborates with other departments to ensure consistency of messages

## Activities and Skills

- Lead and supervise teams (communications officers, graphic designers, journalists, press officers, etc.)
- Train and develop team skills
- Provide an advisory role to general management on the evolution of the brand identity and positioning internally and externally
- Define the annual communication plan
- Define an editorial line
- Develop the event and public relations strategy
- Establish the strategic orientations of the company's communication in terms of image, visibility, message
- Manage communication crises in real time
- Master the different aspects of communication (corporate, external, internal, PR, crisis communication) and their constraints
- Organize a press conference
- Supervise the creation of materials intended for the company's various audiences, internal and external (brochures, brochures, kits, posters, press files and releases, newsletters, videos, podcasts, etc.)
- Process feedback to improve communication
- Validate the variations of the communication plan in its different aspects and supports: internal, external, corporate, digital communication
- Develop innovative means, networks, actions and tools to ensure the visibility of the company and maximize communication messages
- Analyze audience data to improve strategies
- Facilitate the integration of artificial intelligence solutions into existing projects
- Negotiate with communications service providers
- Develop, plan and manage the annual communications budget
- Monitor deviations in the communication budget compared to the forecast set at the start of the year and ensure reporting to general management
- Take into account resource constraints and new sustainability standards in projects
- Define the company's media, editorial and visual strategy (segmentation of message targets, choice of information channels, etc.)
- Manage relationships with advertising agencies
- Monitor market trends to adjust messages
- Define, implement and monitor a policy for evaluating communication actions through performance indicators
- Evaluate the social and environmental consequences of your decisions and act consistently with the company's values
- Implement a communications strategy
- Establish strategic partnerships
- Mobilize a strategic vision and anticipation
- Optimize or update existing processes in order to improve the functioning of the communication service
- Collect strategic information related to the development of the company from general management
- Ensure the strategic quality of communication actions and their consistency with the overall strategy
- Monitor and analyze the results of a communication campaign
- Using English in a professional context
- Act quickly in the event of a communications crisis
- Use digital communication tools
