# Leather and soft materials prototypist editor

## Scope

The Leather and Soft Materials Prototype Editor creates prototypes in leather and soft materials according to technical specifications. Designs and produces prototypes in leather and soft materials according to customer specifications Collaborates closely with designers to transform ideas into tangible products Makes adjustments and modifications to perfect the prototype using a variety of tools Participates in improving production processes by providing technical expertise Ensures the conformity of prototypes with quality and safety standards
