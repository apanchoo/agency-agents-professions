# Credit manager

## Scope

The credit manager manages the financial risks of a company in connection with customer credits, thus ensuring solvency and profitability by optimizing the management of customer accounts. Analyzes customer creditworthiness and assesses credit risks Optimizes outstanding credit while minimizing non-payment risks Negotiates payment terms and guarantees with customers Oversees customer account management and debt collection Collaborates with sales and finance departments to align credit strategies Implements procedures and tools to monitor credit performance

## Related Job Titles

- Credit controller

## Activities and Skills

- Provide training and support to sales teams on credit risks
- Issue management recommendations
- Train employees in credit management methods
- Calculate financial ratios
- Define processing and security procedures
- Managing a crisis situation
- Carry out internal control of banking transactions
- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Analyze the admissibility of a credit request
- Analyze financial data to anticipate risks
- Analyze key indicators (DSO - Days Sales Outstanding, non-payment rate, etc.)
- Define the credit conditions adapted to each customer (payment deadlines, maximum amounts, etc.)
- Establish cash flow reporting
- Establish a forecast cash flow
- Evaluate the solvency of a creditor and the risks
- Evaluate the solvency of companies
- Evaluate the financial risks of recovery files
- Manage cash flow, collection
- Manage invoices and collections in conjunction with accounting, legal and litigation departments
- Control counterparty, credit, market and operational risks
- Implement and monitor clearance plans in the event of customer financial difficulties
- Negotiate payment terms with customers
- Negotiate contractual provisions relating to credits with clients
- Prepare financial and activity reports
- Present financial reports to management for strategic decision making
- Make a decision on a credit application
- Carry out accounting operations
- Carry out credit studies based on financial documents (balance sheets, income statements, etc.)
- Monitor bank account management
- Process payment deadline requests
- Use scoring tools and specialized databases to assess risks
- Follow a credit policy
- Develop dashboards
- Establish financial performance indicators and adjust strategies accordingly
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Monitor legislative and regulatory developments in terms of credit and customer outstandings
