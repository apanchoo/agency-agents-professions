## Scope

The Fuel Delivery Driver plays a crucial role in the distribution and supply of fuel. Ensures the delivery of fuel to points of sale or vehicles Controls the quality and quantity of fuel delivered Ensures compliance with safety standards during loading and unloading Manages transport documents and associated administrative procedures Maintains vehicle in good working order Can advise customers on the different types of fuel available
