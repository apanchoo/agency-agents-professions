# Director of industrial production

## Scope

The industrial production manager ensures the manufacturing of products by optimizing processes and resources. Supervises and coordinates all production activities to achieve quality and cost objectives Analyzes production flows to improve productivity Ensures the quality of products manufactured in compliance with current standards Manages budgets and production costs Develops strategies to increase efficiency and reduce waste Collaborates with other departments to align production objectives with overall company objectives
