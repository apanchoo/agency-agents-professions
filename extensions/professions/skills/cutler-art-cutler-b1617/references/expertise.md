# Cutler / Art cutler

## Scope

Alone in his workshop or employee of a craft company, the cutler always carries out a large number of operations and technical gestures to create art knives. Draws a sketch Forms the metal hot Shapes the edge Makes the ornaments Assembles the parts of the knives Ensures the management of its activity (customer relations, administration, accounting, etc.)

## Related Job Titles

- Knifemaker-repairer

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Design metal and derivative works
- Design and create original graphic works
- Make engravings
- Make an artistic drawing
- Make a sculpture
- Make a sketch, a part drawing
- Cut out a duplicate of a sketch on a metal bar
- Cutting metal
- Define the intervention steps for shaping or decorating an object
- Shaping pieces of art
- Shaping missing or defective elements
- Lifting molten metal
- Proceed with shaping the cutting edge
- Carry out notching operations (microdenting on one or both sides)
- Carry out sharpening operations
- Carry out grinding operations (work on the entire cutting part of the knife up to the edge, on both sides of a blade)
- Carry out quenching (rapid heating and cooling)
- Collect and report dimensions
- Restoring decorative elements on instruments
- Carry out chisels
- Create decorations
- Create decorative effects and ornamentation
- Making sharp instruments
- Carry out polishing operations
- Carry out heat treatment operations
- Carry out forging operations
- Carry out assembly and assembly work
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Trace decorations on art or craft objects
- Assemble metal elements
- Carry out assembly, assembly and disassembly operations
- Controlling the melting of a metal
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Advisor in the context of a commercial relationship

## Professional Approach

- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Characteristics of materials of plant origin (amber, jet)
- Properties of metals

## Areas of expertise

- Fundamentals of metallurgy

## Professional techniques

- Soldering techniques
- Riveting techniques
- Metal Polishing Techniques
- Hammering Techniques
- Specialized bonding techniques for wood and metals
- Restoration techniques
- Weld polishing and finishing techniques

## Working Conditions

- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- Self-employed
- Fine motor activities (dexterity, etc.)
- In the workshop
- Exposure to high temperatures
- Handling a dangerous machine, equipment or tool
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Prolonged standing

## Sectors

- Art, audiovisual and entertainment crafts
