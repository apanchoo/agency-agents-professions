# Military pyrotechnics operator

## Scope

The military pyrotechnics operator carries out storage, assembly, distribution, preventive and corrective maintenance and elimination operations on all types of ammunition, pyrotechnic systems (powders, explosives, etc.) and associated. Be responsible for igniting pyrotechnic materials, such as explosive charges or fireworks. Respect the safety rules and procedures in force to guarantee the safety of people and property. Develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
