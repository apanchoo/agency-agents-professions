# Regular line coach driver

## Scope

The Coach Driver ensures the transport of people in conditions of optimal safety and comfort, in compliance with the highway code, regulations and company procedures, he drives on all types of routes, according to prescribed timetables and on routes with defined stopping points. Ensures the safety of passengers during the journey and may be required to manage incident situations Issues, collects and checks transport tickets and manages boarding and alighting of passengers Controls the operating status and carries out checks of the vehicle and equipment and reports any anomaly in coordination with operation and maintenance Welcomes, informs and informs passengers May carry out occasional (excursions, etc.) or specific school services, staff shuttles, freely organized regular services Represents the image of the company in its relations with users
