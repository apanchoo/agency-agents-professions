# Valet

## Scope

The Valet, an essential professional for a quality welcome and optimal management of vehicles. Greets customers and collects their vehicles at the entrance of a establishment Parks cars in dedicated areas, optimizing the available space Ensures the safety and supervision of parked vehicles Collects vehicles and returns them to customers within the promised timeframe May perform basic maintenance tasks such as checking tire pressure or oil levels Manages keys and ensures vehicles are ready to be returned in good condition

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Use electronic payment equipment
- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Manage vehicle keys
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Organize vehicle parking in the establishment's parking lot and park customers' vehicles there
- Monitor parking zones
- Manage conflicts in the event of a dispute with a customer
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Greet customers upon arrival and manage their departure
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Manage telephone reception
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Inform, inform, direct a person to the site
- Carry out shopping at the request of a customer or for the needs of the establishment
- Receive customers' luggage and clothing
- Collect a customer's opinion and comments
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Respect road safety rules
