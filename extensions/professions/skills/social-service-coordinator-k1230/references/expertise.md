# Social Service Coordinator

## Scope

The social service coordinator ensures the coordination and implementation of social services to effectively meet the needs of the communities. Organizes and supervises social assistance programs to ensure their effectiveness Coordinates multidisciplinary teams for comprehensive care of beneficiaries Develops partnerships with other organizations to improve the service offering Ensures monitoring and evaluation of actions carried out to adjust practices if necessary Trains and supports staff in the application of best practices in social intervention Manages the budgets allocated to the different programs to optimize available resources
