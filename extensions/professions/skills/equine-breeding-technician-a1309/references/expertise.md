# Equine breeding technician

## Scope

The Equine Breeding Technician, a professional passionate about the equestrian world, supervises and coordinates all activities of a horse breeding farm. Ensures health and nutritional monitoring of equines Manages the reproduction and well-being of horses Organizes and participates in horse training Contributes to the preparation of horses for competitions or sales May participate in the administrative and financial management of the breeding

## Related Job Titles

- Equine technician

## Activities and Skills

- Prepare the necessary equipment for equine training
- Promote and share good practices
- Advise an organization, a structure
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Act quickly in case of medical emergency for animals
- Determine food rations according to the physical and physiological needs of the animals
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Managing the well-being of equines on a daily basis
- Implement education or training actions for animals during daily activities
- Prepare equines for competition or events
- Carry out the breaking operation of a young horse
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Identify a sick animal and inform the stakeholders concerned (owner, veterinarian, etc.)
- Complete a breeding register
- Suggest improvements for livestock equipment
- Plan reproductive cycles
- Distribute the animals in breeding premises or outdoors
- Use specialized equipment for horse breeding
- Maintaining an animal's living space
- Maintain breeding facilities in good condition
- Clean and maintain riding equipment
- Arrange the transport of animals for sale or transfer
- Schedule veterinary check-up visits for animals
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage budgets allocated to breeding activities
- Negotiate sales or breeding contracts
- Promote sustainable breeding practices
- Marketing the products of a farm
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Respect health and safety standards
- Process information relating to equine performance
- Regularly update your knowledge
