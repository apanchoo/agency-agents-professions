# Forestry researcher

## Scope

The forestry researcher explores and studies forest ecosystems to better understand, protect and enhance them. Analyzes forest ecosystems and their dynamics Models ecological processes using specialized software Monitors the impact of human activities on forests

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Promote sustainable management practices to forest owners
- Advising researchers, institutions, companies on scientific questions
- Train students in research methods
- Collect samples for analysis
- Design experiments to test specific hypotheses
- Design theoretical models (calculation, simulation, modeling)
- Design and formalize research protocols and scientific studies
- Coordinate a research team
- Develop ecosystem resilience
- Sampling organisms for taxonomic and genetic studies
- Develop biodiversity conservation strategies
- Study the interactions between organisms and their environment
- Interpret data in a scientific context and develop theories
- Conduct research
- Conduct a complete exploration: learn about research already carried out on the subject and capitalize on existing knowledge
- Identify and collect quantitative and qualitative data on populations
- Seek solutions for the conservation and restoration of ecosystems
- Carry out impact studies and reports on the state of forest areas
- Ensure computer modeling of biological processes in order to propose a model for observed biological phenomena
- Integrate technological solutions into forest management
- Create maps of forest areas
- Managing the natural regeneration of forest areas
- Managing forest waste in an ecological way
- Manage the natural resources of the site to maintain the ecosystem
- Planning forestry operations
- Apply safety techniques in a forest environment
- Assess the risks associated with forestry operations
- Negotiate funding for research projects
- Analyze the health of a forest ecosystem
- Analyze environmental data to assess ecosystem health
- Arguing for sustainable solutions
- Evaluate the environmental impact of forestry activities
- Establish sustainable wood management systems
- Promote and implement sustainable forest management in order to optimize forest productivity
- Advocate for environmental measures
- Manage relationships with partners, investors, public bodies and regulatory authorities
- Interpret analysis results for strategic decisions
- Search for financing, investors
- Search for grants for conservation projects
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams for complex studies
- Inform and raise public awareness of the impacts of climate change
- Promote research projects to potential partners
- Write scientific publications (posters, abstracts, articles, protocols, analysis plans, final reports) and format them
- Maintain scientific and regulatory monitoring
