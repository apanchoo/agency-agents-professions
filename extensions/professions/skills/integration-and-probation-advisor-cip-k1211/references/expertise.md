# Integration and Probation Advisor (CIP)

## Scope

The Integration and Probation Advisor -CIP- plays a crucial role in the social and professional reintegration of people in difficulty. Evaluates the social and professional situation of individuals Proposes personalized integration plans Ensures the monitoring of legal measures Directs towards suitable structures Works in collaboration with social and judicial services Contributes to the prevention of recidivism through socio-educational support

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Supporting people in their professional integration journey
- Build a continuing professional development plan
- Teaching, developing skills
- Guide, support a person or family in their efforts
- Implement social reintegration initiatives
- Carry out a health and social diagnosis of a person
- Prevent psychosocial risks
- Facilitate internal and external communication
- Conduct an interview, an interview, an audition
- Analyze the life context of a person or family
- Regularly monitor the progress of individuals
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Write a report, an activity report
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Optimize communication with beneficiaries
- Collaborate with social and professional partners
- Design and manage a project
- Arguing in favor of case-specific solutions
- Inform and raise awareness of the themes of violence, drug addiction, sexuality, illiteracy
- Organize your work within a multi-professional team
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the confidentiality and rights of individuals
- Transmit and explain to people the social and civic rules during daily life activities
