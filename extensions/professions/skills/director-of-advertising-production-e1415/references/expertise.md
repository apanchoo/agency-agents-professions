# Director of advertising production

## Scope

The Director of Advertising Production coordinates and supervises all operations. Carries out strategic planning for advertising productions Ensures compliance with deadlines, budgets and quality standards Guarantees fluidity between the creative and production teams and external service providers Optimizes processes and tools to improve efficiency, reduce costs and margins Deploys a continuous improvement strategy

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Clearly communicate expectations to work teams
- Communicate effectively with teams to ensure coordination
- Coordinate the activity of a team
- Coordinate the intervention of multidisciplinary teams
- Develop and manage a network of partners and service providers
- Manage your team using performance indicators
- Organize and control team activity according to priorities and contingencies
- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Test the integration of innovation into production processes
- Provide and update activity monitoring tools (production dashboards, incident reports, quality, etc.)
- Managing crises and unforeseen events
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage relationships with customers and suppliers
- Launch and manage a call for tenders
- Negotiate preferential rates with service providers
- Ensure compliance with regulations related to advertising production
- Evaluate the environmental impact of production processes
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Check the feasibility of the order, define and agree with the customer the conditions of execution
- Ensure monitoring of performance indicators (KPI)
- Lead the production operations of an advertising campaign
- Implement the company's CSR strategy in production
- Define the production processes for advertising campaigns
- Determine performance objectives, track achievements and identify corrective actions
- Implement cost reduction strategies
- Negotiate with external service providers
- Manage production and operations management
- Plan production activity
- Coordinate external collaborators and partners
- Raise team awareness of the issues of Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR)
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
- Know how to manage several projects or activities simultaneously
