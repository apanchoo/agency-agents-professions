# Insurance risk expert

## Scope

The Insurance Risk Expert analyzes and manages the risks associated with insurance policies to protect the financial interests of insurers and policyholders according to the insurance regulations in the area of intervention. Analyzes claims files to determine liability and the extent of damage Proposes solutions adapted to the specific needs of clients in terms of multi-risk home, damage or civil liability Negotiates with stakeholders in the event of claims to establish fair compensation Follows regulatory and technical developments in the field of insurance to provide effective advice through awareness-raising actions Can manage disputes and represent the insurer during legal proceedings related to claims
