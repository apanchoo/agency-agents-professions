# Family assistant

## Scope

A family assistant, also called a "foster family", is a person or family who welcomes children placed by social services into their home. Temporarily welcomes children or adolescents who cannot live with their family of origin for various reasons Offers a secure, stable and loving family environment, ensuring the well-being, education and development of those welcomed Works closely with social services, health professionals and families of origin to ensure appropriate monitoring and support for those placed in its care. Plays an essential role in the lives of the people welcomed by providing them with a warm environment and helping them to overcome the difficulties they face
