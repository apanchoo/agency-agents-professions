# Meteorological Technician

## Scope

The Meteorological Technician analyzes and collects reliable weather data. Uses specialized instruments to measure parameters and carries out atmospheric surveys Contribute to the study of meteorological data for scientific and environmental work Writes detailed reports on weather forecasts Participates in meteorological observation campaigns and disseminates weather bulletins to different audiences Ensures the maintenance of measurement and data collection equipment
