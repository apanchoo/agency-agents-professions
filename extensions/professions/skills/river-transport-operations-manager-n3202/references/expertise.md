# River transport operations manager

## Scope

The River Transport Operations Manager ensures the smooth running of transport operations on the river routes. Supervises and coordinates passenger and freight river transportation activities Provides crew management and vessel maintenance Optimizes routes and schedules to improve efficiency Ensures compliance with safety and environmental standards Develops strategies to increase profitability of river operations Manages relationships with customers and business partners Manages transportation records for invoicing

## Related Job Titles

- Director of river tourism transport operations
- River freight transport operations manager
- Passenger river transport operations manager
- Sedentary river navigation manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Regularly assess crew skills and provide appropriate training
- Manage human resources
- Analyze navigation reports to improve future operations
- Create a transport file
- Check data compliance
- Coordinate the circuit of information on the functioning of a structure
- Ensure compliance with safety rules and the provision of protective equipment adapted to the products transported
- Control the progress of loading and unloading, the traceability of trips and determine corrective measures
- Organize passenger transport operations
- Organize the allocation of a river fleet according to operational requirements
- Organize order processing
- Organize the transport of goods
- Organize transportation routes to optimize travel times
- Follow an international river transport service
- Edit a statistical activity monitoring report
- Carry out administrative management of personnel
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Decide on the terms of purchase or sale of the transport service
- Develop a commercial strategy
- Implement the company's commercial strategy
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Determine changes in the organization and operation of a structure (productivity, profitability, reliability of operations) and monitor their implementation
- Managing an activity
- Arguing and defending operational decisions to stakeholders
- Communicate with port authorities
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events while respecting security protocols
- Integrate digital technologies to optimize operations management
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
