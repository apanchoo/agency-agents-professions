# Versatile agricultural worker

## Scope

Ensures the maintenance of pastures, meadows, crops and indoor and outdoor facilities on the farm. Works near animals and may be required to handle them as part of their activities. May occasionally carry out building maintenance work and first level repairs.

## Related Job Titles

- Agricultural clerk
- Agricultural worker
- Field crop agricultural worker
- Mixed crop and livestock worker

## Activities and Skills

- Respect animal welfare
- Carry out agricultural operations (sowing, harvesting)
- Maintaining an animal farm
- Plant a tree, a plant, a vegetable
- Prepare soils, plantations (spreading, sowing, etc.)
- Grouping animals together
- Adjust production machines to optimize product quality
- Adjusting the hydraulic systems of agricultural machines
- Distribute the animals in breeding premises or outdoors
- Monitor the condition of a plantation
- Carry out plant maintenance
- Maintain a green space
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain the structural elements of a building (fences, storage areas, etc.)
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Carry out a maintenance operation
- Load, unload, handle products
- Packaging products
- Store a product
- Sort, store products, raw materials
- Driving an agricultural vehicle
- Apply rules and protocols aimed at limiting the effects of the activity on the environment
- Evacuate animals, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Identify the presence of pests and note the nature and extent of the damage
- Inform, inform, direct a person to the site
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of cleaning equipment
- Operation of agricultural machinery
- Use of manual tools
- Use of mowing machines (self-propelled and self-propelled)

## Working Conditions

- Seasonal employment contract
- Private sector employee (CDI, CDD)
- In an allergenic environment
- Outdoors
- Handling a dangerous machine, equipment or tool
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Carrying and handling heavy or bulky loads
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity
- Agricultural exploitation
