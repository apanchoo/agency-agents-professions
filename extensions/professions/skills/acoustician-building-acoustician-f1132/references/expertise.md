# Acoustician / Building acoustician

## Scope

The Building Acoustician is an expert in sound environments and helps to improve the acoustic comfort of buildings. Evaluates and measures noise levels on construction sites and in buildings Designs and recommends solutions to improve acoustic insulation Collaborates with architects to integrate acoustic aspects from the design phase Conducts sound impact studies for new construction projects Ensures compliance of projects with current acoustic standards Advises clients on best practices in acoustics

## Related Job Titles

- Acoustics engineer
- Construction Acoustics Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Ensure continuous technological monitoring
- Design suitable insulation and acoustic correction solutions
- Inventory construction constraints (regulatory, technical, environmental)
- Carry out a technical and economic opportunity and feasibility study
- Analyze data from a study or project
- Create technical documentation
- Interpret the results of acoustic measurements
- Use modeling and simulation software
- Identify technical solutions for improving equipment and installations
- Carry out room measurements and surface area calculations
- Carry out sound measurements and diagnose the acoustic quality of spaces
- Check the compliance of installations with current standards
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Evaluate the effectiveness of acoustic solutions after installation
- Monitor the installation of sound insulation systems
- Estimate the costs and deadlines for an activity or service
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Respect allocated deadlines and budgets
- Promote the use of recycled or eco-friendly materials
- Advise on acoustic materials to use
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Ensure technical responses to calls for tenders
- Manage a construction study project (construction, work, development, etc.)
- Supervise and control the execution of studies, documents and detailed plans entrusted to design offices
- Collaborate with other professionals for specific projects
- Explain technical choices to customers
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
