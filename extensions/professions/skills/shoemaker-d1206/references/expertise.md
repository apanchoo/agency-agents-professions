# Shoemaker

## Scope

The shoemaker, an everyday artisan, preserves and beautifies your leather items. Repairs and restores various leather items, including shoes and bags Makes custom pieces and makes adjustments according to customer needs Offers customization services for various leather items Maintains an organized and safety-compliant workshop Advises customers on the care and conservation of their leather items May manage a retail business specializing in shoemaking and leather goods

## Related Job Titles

- Repairing shoemaker
- Shoemaker-bootmaker
- Shoe repair and multi-service operator
- Leather goods repairer
- Shoe repairer
- Leather goods repairer

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Adjust shoes (heightening, heel pads, etc.)
- Understanding the different types of leathers, textiles, rubbers and other materials
- Remove a part from its support
- Position reinforcing elements
- Create leather items at the customer's request
- Carry out assembly operations by nailing, stapling, sewing, on leather and skin parts
- Carry out assembly operations on items (stems, handles, soles, etc.)
- Create number plates and identification plates
- Make key reproductions
- Carry out the pampering of a leather goods item
- Use and adjust a printing or printing tool or machine
- Use a tool, machine, equipment, installation
- Determine repairs or modifications to the item
- Carry out a recoloring operation
- Repairing clothes
- Control the quality of shoe repairs
- Carry out product labeling
- Sort, store products, raw materials
- Proceed to cashout
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Negotiate with material suppliers
- Carry out accounting operations
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Master cleaning or repair techniques to delay the obsolescence of a product or object
- Optimize the use of raw materials and manage production drops
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
