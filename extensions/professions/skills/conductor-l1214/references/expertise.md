# Conductor

## Scope

The Conductor, maestro of musical coordination, directs and harmonizes the talents of each musician. Directs rehearsals and concerts, ensuring the quality and interpretation of musical works Interprets scores in detail to convey the composer's intentions Coordinates the various sections of the orchestra to ensure perfect harmony Works closely with soloists and other guest conductors May participate in the programming of musical seasons and the selection of works
