# Planner / Strategic planner

## Scope

The strategic planner designs, develops and supervises communications and marketing strategies to achieve client objectives. Analyzes market trends and consumer behaviors Develops appropriate communications and marketing strategies Collaborates with sales teams, consulting teams and creative teams to design relevant campaigns Presents and defends strategies to clients or internal teams Monitors the effectiveness of campaigns and adjusts strategies based on results May specialize in a specific industry or media type
