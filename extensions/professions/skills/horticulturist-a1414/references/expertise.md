# Horticulturist

## Scope

The horticulturist, specialist in flower cultivation, cultivates a variety of plants, in open fields, under tunnels or in greenhouses depending on the region, climate and production objectives. Cultivates and maintains ornamental plants, flowers and shrubs Monitors plant growth and applies necessary treatments to prevent diseases and pests Organizes and plans crop rotations to optimize productivity Advises clients on the choice of plants according to their needs Can manage a team and coordinate work on a horticultural operation
