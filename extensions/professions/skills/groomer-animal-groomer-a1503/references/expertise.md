# Groomer / Animal groomer

## Scope

Performs hygiene or maintenance care according to the needs and characteristics of the pet. Can advise and sell animal care products, accessories and food. May run a grooming salon.

## Related Job Titles

- Assistant groomer
- Bather-comber / Animal bather-comber
- Dog groomer
- Groomer / Dog groomer
- Clipper / Animal clipper

## Activities and Skills

- Advise, support a person
- Providing aesthetic care to an animal
- Detect traumatic, inflammatory, infectious contraindications and adapt treatment or refer to care partners
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Identify the reason for consultation, the degree of urgency and inform the animal owner of the treatment arrangements
- Handle animals
- Suggest appropriate treatment
- Carry out hygiene and comfort care for animals
- Raise awareness of animal welfare
- Select the equipment and products and prepare the animal for grooming by disinfection, shower, etc.
- Check the appearance of the animal and, if necessary, refer the owner to a veterinarian or a dog trainer.
- Complete the animal's follow-up file and offer further care
- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Manage inventory
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Manage a cash register
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Negotiate with suppliers for product purchases
- Carry out accounting operations
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Destroy waste
- Sort and direct waste according to its future treatment or recovery
- Greet the customer and install them
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Advise products, tools or services to customers based on their needs
- Identify the request (seasonal grooming, antiparasitic treatment, etc.) from the animal owner and plan the intervention
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Sell a service or product
- Identify intervention priorities (checks, surveys, etc.) based on annual planning and determine appropriate equipment
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use digital tools
