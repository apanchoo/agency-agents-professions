# Veterinarian

## Scope

The veterinarian prevents and diagnoses animal diseases and provides the necessary care while respecting animal well-being Conducts biological analyzes and functional exploration examinations Performs vaccinations and preventive checks Prescribes medicinal treatments Performs surgical interventions and ensures post-operative follow-up of animals Manages medical emergencies Advises owners or breeders on animal production (hygiene, feeding, reproduction, etc.) Can coordinate a team and direct a structure Can specialize in care domestic, farm or exotic animals

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Advise the owner on diet, exercise, environment, to maintain the health and well-being of the animal
- Teaching, developing skills
- Administer veterinary products
- Monitor the pregnancy of an animal
- Provide preventive or curative care to animals
- Carry out post-operative monitoring of an animal
- Evaluate an animal's behavior and health
- Managing veterinary emergencies
- Identify the reason for consultation, the degree of urgency and inform the animal owner of the treatment arrangements
- Inform the person responsible for the animal about therapeutic care (treatment, risks, cost, duration, etc.) or refer it to a specialized service (veterinary hospital center, etc.)
- Handle animals
- Implement a care and support program to promote an animal’s performance
- Prescribe treatments adapted to animals
- Carry out medical examinations on animals
- Perform anesthesia or euthanasia on an animal
- Perform and supervise medical imaging exams
- Performing surgery on an animal
- Perform biological analyzes (parasitology, bacteriology, virology, etc.)
- Maintain scientific monitoring of technical developments and veterinary care protocols
- Conduct research
- Complete the animal's follow-up file and offer further care
- Using telemedicine technologies to diagnose and treat animals remotely
- Handle medical equipment
- Clean and disinfect equipment after each use
- Carry out a risk analysis linked to medical care
- Manage stocks of medicines and pharmaceutical products
- Arrange scheduled appointments and veterinary emergencies
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Communicate with owners about the health of their animals
- Participate in public health programs
- Monitor the appearance of animal diseases which can have impacts on human health and alert the relevant services
- Maintain detailed medical records for each animal
- Collect information about the animal (problems, behavior, history, current treatments, etc.) from its companion or in the medical file
- Follow cleaning and disinfection protocols
- Use digital tools
