# Goldsmith turner pusher

## Scope

The goldsmith's pusher turner ensures the production of pieces using a pusher lathe, his expertise guarantees him a methodology adapted to all pieces and assembly of simple or complex shapes. Has technical mastery for the deformation and smoothing of materials Produces the tools necessary for the transformation of metals

## Related Job Titles

- Turner-repusher / Turner-repusher in goldsmithing

## Activities and Skills

- Create drawings or plans of the parts and tools to be produced
- Analyze the product manufacturing sheet to meet customer expectations (product range, expected level of finish, expected production times, etc.)
- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Define the intervention steps for shaping or decorating an object
- Identify defects in a part during adjustment, dent removal or polishing
- Create specific pieces of goldsmithing with given instructions or independently
- Organize your space and workstation, following the rules of cleanliness and safety
- Ensure first-level maintenance of your work tools
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Control quality and ensure adjustments to surface conditions
- Check the finishes
- Detect remaining defects, nuances to polish and adjustment constraints
- Send information to your manager
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Apply safety rules in practices and tasks
- Identify the characteristics of the product in order to mobilize the best possible preparation or assembly techniques
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
- Mobilize your concentration throughout a process

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show perseverance
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
