# Transport support agent

## Scope

The Transport Support Agent provides an essential service for the mobility of people. Accompanies and assists people when traveling by public or private transport Ensures the safety and comfort of people with reduced mobility Facilitates access to transport vehicles and the necessary facilities Informs passengers about transport routes and schedules Manages emergency situations and provides immediate assistance if necessary Maintains effective communication with other transport professionals to optimize service

## Related Job Titles

- Transport attendant for people with reduced mobility (TPMR)
- Airport support agent for passengers with reduced mobility

## Activities and Skills

- Promote and share good practices
- Handle emergency situations and provide first aid if necessary
- Use special equipment for mobility assistance
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Monitor and adjust temperature conditions inside the vehicle
- Organize transportation routes to optimize travel times
- Accompanying people with reduced mobility when traveling
- Respect scheduled times and routes
- Write a report, an activity report
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Control access to the vehicle to ensure safety
- Control vehicle access and manage transport tickets
- Monitor passenger behavior to prevent incidents
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Inform passengers and provide assistance to people with reduced mobility
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Ensure confidentiality and discretion regarding passengers
- Inform passengers of safety procedures on board

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Have a sense of service
- Listen, show empathy

## Areas of expertise

- Emergency and first aid procedures
- Continuing training on innovations in passenger transport
- Operational and Passenger Information Support Systems (SAEIV)

## Standards and processes

- Organization and operation of modes of transport
- Transportation regulations

## Professional techniques

- Non-verbal communication techniques
- Ability to manage stressful situations among passengers
