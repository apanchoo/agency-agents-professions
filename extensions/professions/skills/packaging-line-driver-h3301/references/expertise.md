# Packaging line driver

## Scope

The Packaging Line Operator plays a key role in the production process by ensuring the efficiency and quality of product packaging. Monitors and regulates an automated packaging line according to production requirements Makes adjustments necessary for the proper functioning of the machines Controls the quality of the packaged products and intervenes in the event of an anomaly Ensures first-level maintenance of the line's equipment Respects hygiene and safety standards in all operations Can coordinate a team and manage the logistics linked to packaging

## Related Job Titles

- Driver of stacker stackers
- Packaging line driver
- Machine or bottling line driver
- Industrial installation pilot

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate with the team to ensure smooth production
- Train new employees in operational procedures
- Document production procedures and compliances
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Make adjustments to machines and equipment with the aim of energy and environmental performance of the equipment
- Perform first level maintenance
- Determine corrective measures
- Monitor the supply of machines, facilities and the flow of materials or products
- Monitor the operation of the machine or packaging line and control devices and adjust in case of deviations
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Check the conformity of product packaging (tightness, weight, temperature, etc.)
- Packaging products
- Carry out product labeling
- Carry out vacuuming
- Carry out palletizing
- Optimize resource use to reduce waste
- Working in a group, in a network
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events on the production line
