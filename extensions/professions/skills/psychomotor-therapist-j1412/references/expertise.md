# Psychomotor therapist

## Scope

The psychomotor therapist is a professional essential to psycho-corporal well-being. Assesses a person's motor, tonic, affective and cognitive abilities Designs and leads a personalized care project Uses relaxation, bodily or artistic expression techniques Carry out rehabilitation and sensory stimulation activities Collaborates with other health professionals for multidisciplinary monitoring Informs and trains caregivers to support therapeutic intervention

## Related Job Titles

- Psychoreeducator
- Psychomotor rehabilitation therapist
- Psychomotor therapist

## Activities and Skills

- Organize appropriate therapeutic activities
- Identify the patient's environment (psychological, family), list their difficulties (state of acquisitions, cognitive disorders, attention) and inform them about the psychomotor approach
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Design a therapeutic project
- Evaluate patients' motor, perceptual and cognitive abilities
- Implement a therapeutic project
- Carry out psychomotor tests and analyze the information collected during the assessment (clinical manifestations, motivation, etc.)
- Respect personalized care protocols
- Carry out a psychomotor assessment
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Set up group workshops to stimulate social interaction
- Respect the confidentiality of patient or beneficiary information

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
