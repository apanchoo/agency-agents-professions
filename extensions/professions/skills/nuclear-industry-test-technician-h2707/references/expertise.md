# Nuclear industry test technician

## Scope

The nuclear industry test technician ensures the safety and efficiency of nuclear installations. Carries out tests and trials on nuclear equipment or installations to verify their proper functioning and safety Interprets test results to identify anomalies and failures Proposes improvements and solutions to optimize the performance and safety of installations Collaborates with engineers and technicians for the implementation of tests Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of test equipment and writes test reports Respects strict safety standards to prevent any risk of contamination or nuclear accident
