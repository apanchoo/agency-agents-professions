# Merger and acquisition advisor

## Scope

The merger and acquisition (M&A) advisor assists companies in their merger, acquisition, sale or reorganization operations, playing a strategic role in analyzing growth and synergy opportunities between the different entities. Evaluates target companies for mergers or acquisitions Negotiates terms and conditions of transactions Coordinates with legal and financial teams to finalize agreements Analyzes the financial and strategic impacts of operations Advises companies on the best growth strategies through acquisition Ensures post-acquisition monitoring to integrate operations and corporate cultures

## Related Job Titles

- Merger and acquisition manager

- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Analyze a financial statement

- Design a public purchase offer
- Conduct risk analyzes for merger projects

- Carry out a market study, assess the potential of a product or service
- Act as a mediator between stakeholders to facilitate negotiations
- Analyze market trends to identify merger opportunities
