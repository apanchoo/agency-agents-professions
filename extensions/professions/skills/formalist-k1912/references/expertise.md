# Formalist

## Scope

The formalist ensures the completion, monitoring and securing of administrative, legal and tax formalities linked to acts established by the notary office. Intervenes upstream and downstream of acts in order to guarantee their validity, their enforceability and their publication with the competent administrations. Contributes to the legal and fiscal compliance of acts and coordinates exchanges with internal and external contacts.
