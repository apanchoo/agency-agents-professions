## Scope

The Director of Administrative and Financial Services is a high-level professional, generally responsible for the overall financial strategy of the company; He is part of the management team and often reports directly to the CEO or the Board of Directors. Supervises the heads of administrative and financial departments and other financial and administrative functions. Optimizes administrative processes to improve operational efficiency Ensures regulatory compliance of financial operations Manages budgets, financial forecasts and activity reports Leads administrative and financial teams Negotiates with financial partners and supervises external audits

- Evaluate the financial impact of strategic decisions
