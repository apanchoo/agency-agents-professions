## Scope

A regional medical manager (RMR/MSL) in the pharmaceutical industry is a scientific expert who acts as a link between the laboratory and health professionals. Maintains scientific monitoring to stay up to date on innovations in the medical field Liaises between healthcare professionals and pharmaceutical companies to communicate the latest medical advances Organizes and participates in conferences and meetings with healthcare professionals to discuss new research and treatments Contributes to the continuing education of healthcare professionals on new products and therapies Monitors and analyzes regional medical data to guide public health strategies
