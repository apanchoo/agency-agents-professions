# Holiday center director

## Scope

The director of the holiday center manages all of the establishment's activities, from accommodation to the educational project designed for children and adolescents. Organizes and supervises leisure and educational activities for young people Ensures the daily management and security of accommodation sites Coordinates teams of facilitators and technical staff Develops educational programs adapted to different age groups Manages relations with parents and external partners Ensures compliance with the standards and regulations in force regarding the reception of young people

## Related Job Titles

- Director of leisure reception with accommodation
- Deputy director of leisure reception with accommodation
- Deputy director of holiday center
