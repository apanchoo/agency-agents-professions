## Scope

Operates shunting machines to organize and regulate rail traffic Ensures first-level maintenance of shunting machines Controls the compliance of shunting operations according to safety rules Coordinates with other teams to guarantee the flow of traffic Monitors and reports anomalies on the tracks and machines Participates in the training of new drivers
