# Driver of rail network maneuvering equipment

## Scope

The Rail Network Switching Engine Driver ensures the safety and efficiency of rail operations. Operates shunting machines to organize and regulate rail traffic Ensures first-level maintenance of shunting machines Controls the compliance of shunting operations according to safety rules Coordinates with other teams to guarantee the flow of traffic Monitors and reports anomalies on the tracks and machines Participates in the training of new drivers

## Related Job Titles

- Driver of rail traction equipment
