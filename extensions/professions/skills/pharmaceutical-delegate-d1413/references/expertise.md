# Pharmaceutical delegate

## Scope

The Pharmaceutical Representative plays an important role in liaising between pharmaceutical companies and healthcare professionals. Promotes pharmaceutical products to healthcare professionals Develops commercial partnerships and participates in the development of sectoral action plans Advises pharmacy teams on the merchandising plan Monitors activity, the achievement of objectives and implements corrective or optimization action plans Ensures competitive intelligence on the pharmaceutical products market

## Related Job Titles

- Pharmaceutical information advisor
- Medicopharmaceutical delegate
- Pharmaceutical Visitor

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Provide continuing training on new products
- Advise a pharmacist and his pharmacy team on product sales, stock management and merchandising
- Inform healthcare staff about pharmaceutical products
- Design communication and image supports
- Implement communication actions
- Use sales and activity analysis tools
- Manage stocks of medicines and pharmaceutical products
- Track orders, invoicing
- Organize and plan an activity
- Write a report, an activity report
- Argument the benefit of product exhibition and convince the pharmacy team
- Negotiate a contract
- Analyze customer needs for pharmaceutical products
- Act quickly in the event of complaints or problems
- Target visit priorities (identify, select pharmacists)
- Build lasting professional relationships with customers and suppliers
- Develop innovative sales techniques
- Read and understand activity dashboards and indicators
- Implement commercial and promotional actions
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Present and promote a product or service
- Be able to work in project mode
- Implement technical performance indicators
- Participate in professional events
- Know how to work cross-functionally
- Collaborate with healthcare professionals to optimize care
- Monitor and update technical, economic, regulatory information, etc.
- Use digital tools
- Develop and maintain a network
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of pharmaceutical regulations
