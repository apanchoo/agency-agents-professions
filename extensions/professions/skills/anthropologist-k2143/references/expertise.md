# Anthropologist

## Scope

The Anthropologist is interested in the study of humanity, under various aspects such as cultures, societies, languages, biological evolutions and human behavior, by specializing in one of these 4 branches, cultural (or social) anthropology, biological (or physical) anthropology, archeology and anthropological linguistics. Studies sociocultural aspects of human communities across time and space Analyzes behaviors, beliefs, social practices, and organizational structures Conducts fieldwork to collect data, often through participant observation Contributes to the preservation of intangible cultural heritage Participates in conferences and publishes articles to share findings Collaborates with academic institutions, NGOs, or government agencies
