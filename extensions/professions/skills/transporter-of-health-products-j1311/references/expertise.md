# Transporter of health products

## Scope

A healthcare product transporter is a professional responsible for the delivery and logistics of medical and pharmaceutical products. Ensures the transport and delivery of blood products between different health establishments Ensures compliance with temperature and safety conditions during the transport of blood products Controls and maintains the necessary documentation for the tracking and traceability of transported products Participates in continuing education to stay informed of the latest regulations regarding the transport of health products Collaborates with medical teams to guarantee effective communication and the correct receipt of products Performs regular checks of the vehicle to ensure its functionality and compliance with safety standards

## Related Job Titles

- Medicine transporter
- Blood transporter
- Organ transporter

## Activities and Skills

- Disinfect and decontaminate equipment
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain a tool or equipment
- Maintain transport equipment in good condition
- Check the operation of the medical vehicle equipment, identify malfunctions
- Assess the risks associated with the transport of specific goods
- Monitor and adjust temperature conditions inside the vehicle
- Inspect products at every stage of transportation to avoid damage or contamination
- Act quickly if there is a problem with deliveries
- Organize the transport of blood products
- Organize secure organ transport
- Ensure the traceability of transported products
- Deliver health products to healthcare facilities, pharmacies, or other distribution points
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Communicate with providers, healthcare facilities and other stakeholders
- Relay information
- Managing emergencies and unforeseen events in transport

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of refrigeration equipment for blood transport
- Use of secure transport equipment for biological substances

## Areas of expertise

- Use of preservation devices, such as refrigerated containers

## Standards and processes

- Quality and health safety standards
- Knowledge of local blood transport regulations
- Knowledge of standards for transporting biological materials
- Laws and regulations in force on the transport of health products
- Mastery of health standards for organ transport
- Secure Transportation Procedures
- Disinfection and safety procedures in medical transportation
- Packaging of health products

## Professional techniques

- Ability to follow strict procedures
- Organ preservation techniques during transport
- Loading and unloading techniques for sensitive products
- Time and route management
