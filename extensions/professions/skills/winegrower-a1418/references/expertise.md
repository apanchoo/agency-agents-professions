# Winegrower

## Scope

Participates in all vine cultivation and maintenance work (pruning, repair, tying, tillage) and grape harvesting depending on the growing cycle. May participate in winemaking and bottling operations. Can operate his own vineyard and supervise a team.

## Related Job Titles

- Vineyard foreman / forewoman
- Viticulture agricultural worker
- Viticulture worker
- Vineyard worker
- Employee / Vineyard employee
- Tailor / Vine pruner
- Handling winegrower

- Apply a treatment, a product

- Maintain a tree, a plantation
- Identify a disease, parasite or deficiency on a plant or tree
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine

- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions

- Caring for a tree, a plant
