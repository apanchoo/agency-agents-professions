## Scope

The rights and royalty manager is responsible for administering, protecting and monetizing the rights related to musical works on behalf of authors, composers or publishers. Monitors and controls the transcription of contractual data in royalty management systems Calculates royalties and establishes royalty statements due to authors, composers, performers and heirs, partner labels and publishers (as part of licensing or distribution contracts) Prepares and verifies the statements of accounts intended for authors, composers or performers Answers questions from rights holders relating to the statements
