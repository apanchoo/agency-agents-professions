# Sorter / Sorter of stones and pearls

## Scope

Sorts cut or raw stones, or pearls in order to constitute batches of stones or pearls according to precise specifications (color, purity, shape, etc.) After careful examination of the stones or pearls, evaluates their quality, their color, their purity, their size and their shape

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Evaluate the potential of a batch of stones or pearls based on sampling
- Carry out detailed analyzes of scrap goods
- Make batches of stones or pearls conforming to the given models, based on sampling
- Provide information on management software
- Use specific software
- Carry out measurements or stone measurements using suitable technological tools
- Carry out acid cleaning of stones in the laboratory
- Carry out the necessary verification weighings before sorting
- Select stones or beads intended for recutting to meet customer demand
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Control the quality of stones returned by the workshops after manufacturing
- Periodically check the quality of stones in stock
- Detect discrepancies following an inventory of stones and pearls
- Sort the goods, check fluorescence and dimensional criteria based on specifications
- Participate in an inventory
- Prepare customer orders by selecting the desired stones or beads from samples (single color, assortment of colors)
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Mobilize your concentration throughout a process

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
