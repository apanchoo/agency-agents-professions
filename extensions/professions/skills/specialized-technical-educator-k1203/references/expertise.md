# Specialized technical educator

## Scope

The specialized technical educator is a social actor who supports, trains and supervises people in difficulty to promote their social and professional integration. Develops and implements appropriate educational projects Conducts technical training and personal development workshops Promotes the development of individuals' social and professional skills Ensures individual monitoring and evaluates progress made Collaborates with other professionals for overall support

## Related Job Titles

- Educator / Technical educator

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise activities while ensuring the physical and psychological safety of others
- Promote personal development through structured activities
- Take into account the physical and psychological realities of different audiences
- Encourage active participation of people
- Evaluate individual training and integration needs
- Organize the work of a team
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Supporting people in their professional integration journey
- Adapt learning according to educational progress and difficulties encountered
- Facilitate technical training
- Assisting people in difficulty
- Design training programs adapted to specific needs
- Design and carry out educational activities for an individual or group
- Teaching, developing skills
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Facilitate social integration
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Maintain and facilitate the inclusion of supported people to avoid social isolation
- Participate in the design and monitoring of an individualized project
- Transmit theoretical bases, techniques and gestures by combining several teaching methods
- Follow up with families
- Intervene with people with disabilities or loss of autonomy
- Observe the state of mental and physical health of the person accompanied
- Observe the child's behavior and development and identify possible difficulties
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Ensure the safety of people in the workshop
- Ensure a welcoming and friendly atmosphere
- Lead a themed workshop
- Liaise with social services
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Promote the integration of all participants in the activities
- Organize and lead socio-behavioral skills development workshops
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Working in a group, in a network
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Apply safety rules in practices and tasks
- Be patient and keep your cool
- Managing an emergency situation
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Transmit and explain to people the social and civic rules during daily life activities
- Use digital tools
