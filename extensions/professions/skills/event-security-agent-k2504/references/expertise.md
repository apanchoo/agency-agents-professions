# Event security agent

## Scope

Carries out screening and public access control Orients people according to regulatory and contractual security procedures Participates in risk prevention (terrorists, accidents and crowd movements) Ensures the protection of people and property during major events

## Related Job Titles

- Screening security agent
- Security agent for major events
- Steward

## Activities and Skills

- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Detect and locate any individual or behavior presenting a threat to security or safety
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Write a report, an activity report
- Control access and movement of people
- Carry out visual and palpation checks at the entrance of visitors
- Carry out surveillance rounds
- Evacuate people, bring them to safety in the event of an accident or fire and guide emergency services
- Screening people or objects using screening devices
- Carry out identity checks
- Monitor and protect sensitive areas (locker rooms, parking lots, stands, playing area, stage, etc.)
- Check accreditations, entry fees, access permits
- Inform, inform, direct a person to the site
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Relay information
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Managing a conflict situation
- Inform users of the security rules to follow
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Use digital tools

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate self-control
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Connected tools

## Areas of expertise

- Foreign language practice

## Standards and processes

- Safety instructions in case of emergency
- Actions and techniques for prevention, safety and first aid

## Professional techniques

- Self-defense techniques
- Oral, written and digital communication techniques

## Working Conditions

- In contact with the public
- Outdoors
- Wearing professional attire or uniform
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Prolonged standing
- Night work
- On-call work
- Work in staggered hours
- Working split hours
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Shift work
