# Parking operations manager

## Scope

The Parking Operations Manager (RES) is the relay between the management of the company and the site(s) operated. Responsible for activities and staff management in terms of team leadership Manages monitoring of the budget, site attendance indicators (occupancy rate, user habits) and relationships with service providers and suppliers Guarantees compliance with the application of rules, safety instructions among its employees and users responsible for organizing the collection Carry out or supervise all administrative and accounting activities Can welcome, inform and assist customers and contribute to the implementation commercial actions and the processing of customer complaints Organizes the monitoring of equipment maintenance and establishes schedules for maintenance and cleaning work

## Activities and Skills

- Act in the face of conflict and/or dangerous situations
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Organize the parking operation activity
- Organize the control of collection activities
- Organize the control of security activities
- Organize the work of a team
