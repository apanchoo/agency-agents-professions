# Scientific and technical diver

## Scope

The scientific and technical diver explores and studies the seabed, thus contributing to the knowledge and preservation of the aquatic environment. Conducts technical dives for underwater research and data collection missions Operates specialized equipment for capturing images and data in underwater environments Analyzes samples and data collected to contribute to scientific studies Participates in writing reports and publishing scientific findings Ensures maintenance of diving equipment and measuring instruments Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams for research and conservation projects

## Related Job Titles

- Diver in underwater environment
- Autonomous scuba diver
- Diver / Underwater diver on board
- Underwater intervention technician
