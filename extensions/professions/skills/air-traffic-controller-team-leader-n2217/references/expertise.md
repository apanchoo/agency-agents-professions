# Air traffic controller team leader

## Scope

The Air Traffic Controller Team Leader orchestrates the safety and fluidity of air traffic. Supervises and coordinates the activities of a team of air traffic controllers to ensure flight safety Works closely with pilots to ensure safe landings and takeoffs Analyzes weather information and flight data to optimize trajectories Trains and mentors new air traffic controllers Ensures control procedures and navigation systems are updated Collaborates with other team leaders to continually improve air traffic control processes

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Optimize the use of technological resources in air traffic control
- Plan controller team rotations
- Strengthen the team's analytical capabilities
- Supervise the team of air traffic controllers
- Ensure continuing training for air traffic controllers
- Maintain aviation safety procedures up to date
- Analyze discrepancies between technical airspace data and parameters transmitted by pilots
- Check data compliance
- Identify intervention needs based on anomalies or incidents and implement alert procedures
- Promote safe work practices
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Coordinate air traffic control operations
- Identify the positioning of aircraft on control devices and monitor the conformity of their trajectory in the airspace
- Regulate air traffic and develop flight plan modifications based on emergencies
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Ensure compliance with aviation safety standards
- Analyze the performance of the control team
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Facilitate collaboration between different air services
- Relay information
- Implement operational decision methods
- Resolve operational issues in real time
