# Elevator

## Scope

The elevator engineer carries out maintenance, troubleshooting, monitoring or installation of equipment with mechanical, electrical, electronic, automatic, hydraulic devices, etc. (elevators, escalators, moving walks, freight elevators, etc.), according to safety rules and regulations. Detects and diagnoses breakdowns, then carries out necessary repairs (corrective maintenance) Controls, monitors and maintains equipment (preventive, predictive maintenance) Advises and trains users on the operation and maintenance of equipment Proposes solutions to improve the safety and performance of equipment
