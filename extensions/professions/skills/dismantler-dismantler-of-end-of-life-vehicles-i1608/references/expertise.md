# Dismantler / dismantler of end-of-life vehicles

## Scope

The Automotive Dismantler organizes and carries out the dismantling of an end-of-life vehicle (ELV). Ensures the safety and decontamination of the ELV Carry out the dismantling of parts and materials Manages and organizes the sorting of parts and materials Its activities are carried out in compliance with regulations relating to the environment, hygiene and safety

## Related Job Titles

- Dismantler / Automotive dismantler

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure traceability on computer support of the vehicle dismantling phases
- Provide information on a computerized medium about the status of dismantled parts
- Use inventory management software
- Apply standards relating to the handling of refrigerants
- Extract liquid and solid hazardous waste from an end-of-life vehicle
- Use a hydraulic press
- Carry out removal and disassembly operations
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Diagnose parts of the vehicle that are out of order before dismantling
- Apply regulations relating to automobile recycling
- Apply electrical safety standards
- Secure the end-of-life vehicle by identifying the different types of risks
- Optimize processes to reduce environmental risks
- Organize the sorting and storage of parts and materials
- Identify the administrative information of the end-of-life vehicle
- Organize the disposal of waste and materials

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Dangerous materials (gas, hydrocarbons, chemicals, etc.)
- Use of computerized traceability support
- Using diagnostic tools
- Using cutting tools
- Tire characteristics and approvals

## Areas of expertise

- Characteristics and operation of vehicle pyrotechnic systems
- Battery characteristics (lead, nickel/cadmium, lithium, ion, etc.)
- Inventory management software

## Standards and processes

- Safety rules related to the handling of refrigerants
- Safety rules related to dangerous materials and products
- Administrative categories of vehicles
- Methodologies related to battery extraction and storage
- Clearance procedures
- Electrical risks and different levels of authorization
- Interpretation of electrical or electronic diagrams
- Methodology for intervention on pyrotechnic systems (triggering, extraction, neutralization) and their storage method
- Typology of environmental and health risks
- Methods for checking the condition of parts
- Regulatory framework for approved automobile recycling centers

## Professional techniques

- Assessment of the condition of the parts
- Material recycling techniques
- Identification of reusable parts
- Specificities of interventions on electric, hybrid, LPG, CNG vehicles
- Techniques for removing and dismantling end-of-life vehicles
- Room Cleaning Techniques
- Waste management
