# Train escort agent

## Scope

The train escort agent ensures compliance with the rules and the safety of train passengers. Welcomes and informs travelers when accessing the train Informs and assists passengers Ensures the safety, security, assistance and comfort of travelers during journeys Checks the validity of transport tickets, draws up tickets and collects fines Contributes to the prevention of fraud and inappropriate behavior Checks the proper functioning of comfort and safety equipment Reports incidents and anomalies detected during the journey May be required, at the driver's request, to ensure the safety of the train in the event of an incident May participate in the training of new agents

## Activities and Skills

- Check data compliance
- Check the proper functioning and cleanliness of equipment
- Monitor passenger behavior during the journey
- Follow emergency evacuation procedures
- Secure travelers and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services in the event of an incident
- Check the presence of on-board materials and equipment (safety, alert), check their condition and report any anomaly or degradation
- Control the safety and comfort conditions of travelers
- Accompany passengers during the journey
- Guide passengers in case of difficulty
- Guide passengers to the platforms before the train leaves
- Inspect the wagons before the train leaves
- Check the condition of the toilets before the train leaves
- Check the raised position of the tables of each seat
- Control the accounting of fines collected
- Proceed to cashout
- Write a report, an activity report
- Use electronic ticketing systems and manage transactions
- Prepare a report
- Manage incidents and inform hierarchy
- Verbalize an offense
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Controlling passenger access to transport areas
- Greet travelers upon their arrival and manage their departure
- Inform travelers and provide them with assistance
- Ensure communication of travel information to passengers
- Relay information
- Working in a group, in a network
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Managing an emergency situation
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Comply with legal provisions related to swearing-in
- Use digital tools
