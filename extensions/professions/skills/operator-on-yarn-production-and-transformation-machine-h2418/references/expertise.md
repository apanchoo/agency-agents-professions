# Operator on yarn production and transformation machine

## Scope

The Yarn Production and Transformation Machine Operator adjusts and monitors the machines used to produce and transform textile yarns. Supervises and controls the yarn production and transformation process Ensures first-level maintenance of machines and equipment Optimizes machine settings to guarantee the quality of the yarns produced Controls the quality of the yarns and corrects any defects Documents the operations carried out and the results obtained Respects safety standards and environmental protocols

## Related Job Titles

- Yarn production and transformation machine operator
- Automated process operator on spinning machine
- Spinning worker

## Activities and Skills

- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine
- Pass the wires, wicks or ribbons through grids, bleeders, wire guides, check their guidance and winding on the spools
- Prepare materials for work
- Adjust spinning machine parameters
- Detect the causes of product anomalies and transmit to the maintenance department
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Monitor the continuous supply of wires to the installations, etc. and carry out knotting in the event of a break
- Check the operation of machines at the start of production and check measurement data during processing cycles
- Ensure training monitoring and trace it in the educational monitoring tools available
- Control the quality of yarns during production
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Remove non-compliant products
- Analyze production data and adjust processes
- Communicate effectively with supervisors about progress and issues
- Enforce occupational safety and health rules and make each employee responsible
- Manage emergencies and unforeseen events on the production line

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Wire characteristics

- Characteristics of inorganic or mineral fibers (carbon, glass, etc.)

- Use of production management software
- Characteristics of synthetic fibers
