# General agent / General insurance agent

## Scope

The General Insurance Agent distributes to a clientele of individuals or professionals the products of an insurance company that it represents, following its regulations and commercial strategy. Ensures a complete assessment of the new client's situation, assesses the risks to be covered and advises them on the most suitable products from their company. Examines the file in the event of a claim and ensures follow-up until compensation is paid. Develops and builds loyalty in its portfolio of policyholders and manages its agency. Can recruit employees, organize the work of his team and implement training activities. Implements marketing actions in partnership with its insurance company, surrounded by a team.
