# Socio-sporting facilitator

## Scope

Prepares, leads and supervises physical and sporting activities, particularly in groups, aimed at personal development, socialization, professional integration or even the transmission of values. By supervising these physical and sporting activities, generally contributes to a broader objective located on the social level within a diversity of structures such as sports, entertainment, integration, social and cultural associations. Mobilizes skills in pedagogy, social activities and psychology May supervise a sporting activity in a club as an initiator or working in conjunction with actors in the social sector, depending on their training or professional experience. This activity requires ensuring the safety of people and compliance with regulations

## Related Job Titles

- Educator / Educator integration through sport

## Activities and Skills

- Design the program of activities according to the specificities of the public
- Develop a sports activities project within an associative project
- Establish appropriate physical activity programs
- Explain the conditions for practicing a sporting activity
- Organize fun and educational activities during the journey
- Organize educational and cultural outings and stays
- Take into account the physical and psychological realities of different audiences
- Prepare the space and equipment needed for the activity session
- Present the exercise to practitioners and provide technical support
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Adapt a course or an educational sequence according to the level and progress of the learners
- Define a learning objective and the program of educational activities
- Teaching, developing skills
- Adapt your reception and support practices according to the specific needs of people
- Provide first aid in the event of an emergency
- Implement communication actions
- Organize sporting events
- Carry out continuous monitoring of game, animation and learning techniques
- Monitor equipment or sports equipment
- Define risk prevention measures
- Evaluate security risks in different situations
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Secure the public in the event of incidents and alert the relevant gendarmerie and fire services
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Organize and supervise the supply and logistics of an event
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Evaluate personal protective equipment
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Inform the public about the practice of the sporting discipline and the methods of organizing sessions
- Design an educational project
- Organize sporting activities as part of employment and integration policy projects
- Carry out an assessment of the educational or sports activities project and propose changes
- Develop and manage interpersonal relationships
- Network and cooperate with diverse professional profiles
- Transmit the values linked to the sporting discipline
