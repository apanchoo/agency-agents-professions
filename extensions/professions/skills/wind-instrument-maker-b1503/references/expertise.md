# Wind instrument maker

## Scope

The wind instrument maker makes or restores mouthpiece or reed instruments, depending on whether the instrument is made of metal or wood. Designs and manufactures wind instruments such as flutes, clarinets, saxophones, and trumpets Advises musicians on the selection and maintenance of their instruments Makes adjustments and repairs to improve the performance and playability of instruments May customize instruments at the request of musicians to meet specific needs
