# Cellar Master/Mistress

## Scope

The Cellar Master, an artisan of winemaking, supervises all stages of wine making in close collaboration with the oenologist. Supervises and coordinates vinification and wine aging operations Controls the quality of grapes, musts and wines Manages wine stocks and ensures their optimal conservation Directs cellar work and preparation of wines for bottling Collaborates with the oenologist for technical decisions and production choices May participate in the promotion of wines and the reception of visitors

## Related Job Titles

- Chef/Chief wine merchant
- Chef / Cellar manager

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate the activities of a cellar team
- Develop strategies to improve wine quality
- Carry out chemical control tests during the fermentation process and issue recommendations (additions of ingredients)
- Select a sample during production
- Train staff in winemaking techniques
- Manage the production schedule
- Plan the stages of wine production
- Adjust the dosage of alcohol ingredients
- Conduct and control a fermentation process
- Carry out and follow the steps of producing an alcohol
- Develop a blend or a vintage
- Managing the temperature of fermentation tanks
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Carry out winemaking operations (assembly, measurement of alcohol level, filtration, etc.)
- Drawing off a product after fermentation
- Supervise the aging of wines in vats or barrels, controlling temperature, humidity and aeration conditions
- Use and adjust agri-food machines or equipment
- Supervise the maintenance of cellar equipment
- Monitor wine storage conditions
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Ensure the traceability of production batches
- Control the clarity and limpidity of wines
- Check the quality of the grapes upon receipt
- Check product storage conditions
- Monitor wine stock levels
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Carry out the placing in barrels or bottles, the capping, the labeling
- Supervise the packaging of wine in bottles
- Organize order processing
- Optimize the energy consumption of systems
- Organize wine tasting events
- Present and promote a product or service
- Implement performance indicators for production monitoring
- Participate in company development projects
- Collaborate with development teams to improve products
- Promote sustainable development practices in production
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Use digital tools
- Develop your oenology skills
