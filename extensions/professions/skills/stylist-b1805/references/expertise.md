# Stylist

## Scope

The stylist creates and designs new lines of textile products, shoes and accessories taking into account the style and image of the brand, as well as technical and economic production requirements. Analyzes fashion trends and adapts its creations according to the target market Collaborates with production teams to create prototypes Selects materials, colors and patterns for each creation Can specialize in the styling of certain products Can organize the stages of distribution of the collection
