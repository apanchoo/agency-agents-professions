# Industrial lacquerer

## Scope

The industrial lacquerer applies layers of lacquer to various materials; this profession requires great precision and in-depth knowledge of lacquering techniques, the materials used and the equipment required; these professionals often find great satisfaction in the control of their production and the quality of the finishes they manage to obtain. Masters the different lacquering techniques Knows the materials and lacquering products Demonstrates precision and attention to detail Respects safety standards Works in a team Maintains good physical condition

## Activities and Skills

- Make an aesthetic or artistic appreciation
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Provide quality and production monitoring support
- Apply lacquer using pneumatic guns or brushes
- Identify the type of preparation to be carried out on the supports to adapt the operating method to the materials
- Handle dangerous or fragile products
- Assemble and adjust an installation, a machine
- Painting surfaces and visual supports
- Sand surfaces to prepare for paint application
- Sand, sand, level the surfaces between the different layers of lacquer or color
- Program the movements and trajectories of automated or robotic installations
- Prepare materials for work
- Prepare the surface of machined parts or assemblies being manufactured or restored by mechanical or manual abrasion
- Prepare the product (quantity, shade, mixture, etc.) and adjust the application equipment
- Prepare the surfaces (degreasing, masking, sanding, etc.) depending on the type of support
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Carry out a drying operation
- Trace and make markings, masks (pre-cut, adhesive, etc.) on the surface to be painted
- Use soaking baths or pads to apply lacquer
- Use a tool, machine, equipment, installation
- Organize your space and workstation, following the rules of cleanliness and safety
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Make corrections or repairs to a work
- Analyze the quality of a product
- Check the surface (deposit, appearance, thickness), identify non-conformities and carry out finishing touches
- Check stock status
- Define supply needs
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Reduce the environmental footprint of your activity
- Participate in continuous process improvement
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
