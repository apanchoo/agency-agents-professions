# Yield manager

## Scope

Specializing in optimizing pricing and sales capabilities to maximize a company's revenue, the Yield Manager dynamically adjusts pricing based on demand, competition and market trends. Analyzes market data to adjust pricing strategies Adapts and readjusts in real time the price of services in the hotel, transport (air, rail) or rental sectors Collaborates with sales and marketing teams to develop competitive offers Uses revenue management tools to forecast demand and adjust supply
