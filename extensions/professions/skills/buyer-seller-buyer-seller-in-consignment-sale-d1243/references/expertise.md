# Buyer seller / Buyer seller in consignment sale

## Scope

The consignment buyer seller is responsible for purchasing second-hand products which he resells to individuals or professionals. Evaluates and selects items to purchase or take into storage for resale, guaranteeing their condition Negotiates purchase and sale prices for their quality and to satisfy customer needs Organizes the sales area to maximize the attractiveness of products, monitors product arrivals Ensures the promotion of items available in store via various communication channels Advises customers on products, their origin and their maintenance Manages transactions and monitors stocks to maintain a balanced inventory
