# Disinfestation Agent

## Scope

The Pest Control Agent protects spaces by eradicating harmful insects in various environments. Inspects sites and applies treatments to eliminate infestations Advises clients on prevention measures using a range of methods and products Follows safety protocols for the use of chemicals Conducts regular checks to ensure the effectiveness of pest control techniques May participate in disinfection and rodent control missions

## Related Job Titles

- Insect killing agent
- Stray animal sensor
- Pest control disinfector
- Pest control
- Taupier / Taupière
- Rodent control technician

## Activities and Skills

- Inform the customer about infestation prevention procedures
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Apply integrated pest management methods
- Choose and prepare the equipment necessary for the intervention
- Diagnose the type of infestation and determine the method of treatment (disinfection, disinfestation, rodent control, fumigation, etc.)
- Disinfect and decontaminate sensitive premises
- Disinfect and sterilize pipes
- Install traps or products at infestation sites
- Maintain equipment in good working order
- Clean and disinfect equipment after each use
- Carry out disinfection by spraying or nebulization
- Secure the intervention perimeter
- Monitor the effectiveness of the treatments applied
- Identify the presence of pests and note the nature and extent of the damage
- Arguing about best prevention practices
- Collaborate with teams to optimize interventions
- Build a post-intervention follow-up plan
- Develop personalized intervention strategies
- Act quickly if a major infestation is discovered
- Document the procedures and interventions carried out
- Respect health safety protocols
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility
