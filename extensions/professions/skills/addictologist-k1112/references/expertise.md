# Addictologist

## Scope

The addictologist is a doctor specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of addictions. Evaluates and diagnoses addiction disorders in patients Proposes and implements personalized treatment plans Monitors patients to adjust treatments according to the evolution of their condition Participates in prevention and awareness-raising actions on the risks linked to addictions Conducts research to improve addiction treatment practices

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams for patient monitoring
- Coordinate actions with social and health services
- Participate in reflection and working hypotheses, in collaboration with doctors and the clinical study team
- Supporting people in their care journey
- Adapt treatments according to patient progress
- Encourage patients to express their concerns and questions
- Evaluate and validate the state of dependence
- Assess patient dependency
- Inform and advise patients on treatment options
- Intervening with people experiencing addiction
- Offer personalized advice on nutrition, physical exercise, stress management
- Organize support groups for rehabilitation
- Monitor the progress of patients' therapy
- Participate in clinical studies
- Managing crisis situations linked to addiction
- Work with other health professionals and community organizations to develop prevention programs
- Arguing therapeutic choices in front of a medical team
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Guarantee the confidentiality of information
- Inform and raise awareness of the themes of violence, drug addiction, sexuality, illiteracy
- Attend health conferences
- Raise public awareness
