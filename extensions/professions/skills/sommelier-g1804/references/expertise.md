# Sommelier

## Scope

The Sommelier, an expert in wines and spirits, guides customers' choices with passion and expertise. Advises customers on the choice of wines and spirits suitable for their meals Prepares wine and chooses the appropriate glassware to serve customers Manages the wine cellar, ensures optimal storage and conservation of bottles Collaborates with the kitchen team to harmonize menus and wine selections Trains restaurant staff on the characteristics of different wines May participate in the purchase and selection of wines and spirits for the establishment

## Related Job Titles

- Sommelier clerk
- Sommelier-wine merchant

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure staff training on wines
- Adapt the wine offerings to the seasons
- Design the wine and drinks list according to the type of cuisine, geographical location
- Participate in wine tasting events
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Monitor wine storage conditions
- Analyze the characteristics of wines for advice
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Managing a wine cellar
- Organize a cellar according to the characteristics of the bottles
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Carry out order management (placing, tracking, reception)
- Evaluate and select wine suppliers
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Select and buy wines from winegrowers, wholesalers, etc.
- Enforce alcohol consumption regulations
- Handle complaints professionally
- Evaluate the environmental impact of the wine list
- Integrate eco-responsibility into all dimensions of its activity
- Advise clients on food and wine pairings
- Prepare the selected wine (warming, decanting, etc.), choose the appropriate glassware and serve the customer
- Inform customers about the different characteristics of wines or spirits (grape variety, provenance)
- Analyze wine market trends
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Integrate organic wines into the offer
- Negotiate wine purchase prices with suppliers
- Promote local wines to customers
- Establish partnerships with local and international winegrowers
- Ensure clear and effective communication with the team
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Keep your knowledge up to date on new wines

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Characteristics of wines and spirits
- Types of grape varieties (ampelography)
- Vineyards and appellations

## Areas of expertise

- Organic and biodynamic wines
- Tasting techniques
- Wine and food pairings
- Knowledge of French wine regions
- Menu development in collaboration with the kitchen
- Wine storage specificities
- Sensory evaluation of wines
- Wine and cheese pairings
- Analysis of wine market trends

## Standards and processes

- Planning seasonal wine purchases
- Wine and spirits legislation
- Maintenance of cellar equipment

## Professional techniques

- Wine service in restaurants
- Business techniques
- Wine preservation methods

## Working Conditions

- In contact with the public
- Bar, Brasserie
- Traditional catering establishment

## Sectors

- Commerce and distribution
- Hospitality and catering
