# Neurologist

## Scope

The Neurologist is the nervous system expert who diagnoses and treats pathologies of the nervous and peripheral systems. Performs neurological examinations to evaluate sensory and motor functions Interprets the results of additional examinations such as MRI or brain scan Prescribes and adjusts drug treatments to optimize patient care Collaborates with a multidisciplinary team to provide advanced knowledge of the nervous system Monitors the progress of patients and adapts treatments according to progress or complications

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Evaluate patients' motor, perceptual and cognitive abilities
- Carry out Electroencephalography (EEC) examinations using radiographic techniques
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Monitor the progression of the disease and adjust treatments
- Monitor the evolution of neurological pathologies
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Conduct study and research work
