# Watchmaker

## Scope

The watchmaker designs, manufactures and repairs watches, pendulums, clocks and alarm clocks. Diagnoses malfunctions of objects to ensure repair Assembles and adjusts the various components of watch systems to guarantee their precision Restores old watch parts, thus preserving their value and functionality Advises customers on the maintenance and conservation of their watch goods Can create unique pieces on request, combining aesthetics and functionality
