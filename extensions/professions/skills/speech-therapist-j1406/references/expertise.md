# Speech therapist

## Scope

The speech therapist, an essential professional in the development and rehabilitation of language. Corrects or improves the oral and written communication of people with language, speech or voice disorders Can carry out speech therapy for people with specific disabilities (deafness, laryngectomy, etc.) Informs and advises families on best communication practices Collaborates with health professionals for multidisciplinary monitoring

## Related Job Titles

- Speech therapist
- Speech therapist

## Activities and Skills

- Advise families on communication strategies
- Encourage patient autonomy in managing their condition
- Teaching, developing skills
- Promoting the inclusion of people with communication disorders
- Providing support and advice to patients
- Identify the type of dysfunction (voice, oral language disorders), the difficulties (discomfort, manifestations) and the patient's environment
- Design the speech therapy session program and adapt the exercises according to the patient's condition
- Design the speech therapy project and decide on the intervention methods (progress, amount of personal work, etc.) according to the patient's situation (age, pathology, activity, etc.)
- Determine the need for speech therapy based on tests or advise the patient on additional follow-up (academic support, psychological support, etc.)
- Implement a care process
- Observe the state of mental and physical health of the person accompanied
- Organize the follow-up of medical files
- Perform speech therapy
- Carry out the end of rehabilitation assessment with the person and exchange information with the doctor, teacher, etc.
- Raise awareness of good hygiene and health practices
- Contribute to speech therapy research
- Use specific diagnostic tools
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Respect the ethical and legal standards in force
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Collaborate with teachers to support students
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data
- Respect the confidentiality of patient or beneficiary information

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Show perseverance
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of voice prostheses
- Use of aerophonoscope, metronome, dictaphone, etc.
