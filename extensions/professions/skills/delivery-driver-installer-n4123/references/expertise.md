# Delivery driver installer

## Scope

The Installer Delivery Driver / Installer Delivery Driver ensures efficient and secure delivery, while guaranteeing the proper installation of products at customers' premises. Ensures the delivery of products to homes or businesses according to agreed deadlines Installs and commissions products delivered to customers' premises, respecting safety standards Checks the condition of products before delivery to ensure their quality Plans routes optimally to maximize delivery efficiency Maintains clear and professional communication with customers to ensure quality service Manages delivery documents and necessary administrative formalities
