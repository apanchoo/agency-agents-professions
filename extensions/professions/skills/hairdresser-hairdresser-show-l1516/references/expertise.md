# Hairdresser / Hairdresser show

## Scope

The show hairstylist helps and supports the artists before, during and after the show to present hairstyles consistent with the artistic vision of the director and the artistic team. Creates and performs hairstyles appropriate to the characters and artistic performances Examines, maintains and repairs wigs, making quick modifications as needed Collaborates closely with directors and costume designers to harmonize styles Uses advanced wigmaking and postichery techniques for visual transformations Ensures the maintenance and quality of wigs and hair accessories May design complementary makeup to enhance the visual impact of the characters Manages the inventory of necessary supplies and equipment to show hairdressing
