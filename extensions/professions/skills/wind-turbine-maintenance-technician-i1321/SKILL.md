## Scope

The Wind Turbine Maintenance Technician is a link in the energy transition, guaranteeing the operation of wind turbine installations in order to optimize their production of electrical energy. Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of wind turbines to guarantee their performance Diagnoses and resolves mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdowns Performs regular inspections to prevent incidents and extend the life of equipment Participates in the installation and commissioning of new wind turbines Writes detailed reports on interventions and performance of wind turbines Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to continually improve maintenance processes

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Update maintenance log
- Identify faulty components and parts
- Carry out an operating diagnosis and check machines, installations and equipment
- Repair the installation by replacing and reconditioning electrical, electronic, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic devices
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Implement security measures
- Monitor equipment performance indicators
- Troubleshoot advanced technical issues

2. Prioritize possible causes and discriminating controls.
3. Prepare for intervention and recurrence prevention.
4. Define recommissioning and maintenance indicators.
