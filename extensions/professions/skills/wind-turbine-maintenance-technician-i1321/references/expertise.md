# Wind turbine maintenance technician

## Scope

The Wind Turbine Maintenance Technician is a link in the energy transition, guaranteeing the operation of wind turbine installations in order to optimize their production of electrical energy. Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of wind turbines to guarantee their performance Diagnoses and resolves mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdowns Performs regular inspections to prevent incidents and extend the life of equipment Participates in the installation and commissioning of new wind turbines Writes detailed reports on interventions and performance of wind turbines Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to continually improve maintenance processes
