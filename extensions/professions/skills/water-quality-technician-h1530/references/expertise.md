# Water quality technician

## Scope

The water quality technician ensures the safety and compliance of water treatment and distribution processes. Controls and analyzes water quality in different environments Takes water samples to measure physicochemical and microbiological parameters Interprets analysis results and writes detailed reports in the event of an anomaly or contamination Proposes solutions to improve water management and reduce pollution Ensures compliance of installations and processes with environmental standards Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams for the sustainable management of water resources

## Related Job Titles

- Water analysis laboratory technician
- Water quality measurement technician
- Water Sanitary Technician

## Activities and Skills

- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Analyze solid, liquid, gaseous samples (water, sludge, waste) and record the results
- Propose strategies for adaptation to climate change
- Receive samples, or take samples on construction sites or production sites, strictly respecting operating procedures and protocols
- Check the conformity of an analysis result
- Process analytics data to extract trends
- Design analysis protocols
- Organize the samples and analyzes to be carried out according to the construction site schedule or the production site schedule
- Prepare and check the conformity of products and measurement and analysis devices
- Regulate water treatments based on analyzes
- Carry out malfunction diagnostics of water treatment installations
- Assess the risks linked to climate change on water resources
- Participate in the establishment of safety standards for water treatment
- Analyze the microbiological quality of water
- Ensure the traceability of interventions
- Write clear and detailed expert reports
- Optimize the use of water resources
- Act quickly in the event of water contamination
- Evaluate groundwater resources
- Managing water resources for irrigation sustainably
- Suggest improvements for water treatment procedures
- Collaborate with teams for water resources management
- Educate guests about aquatic ecosystems and the importance of conserving natural resources
- Apply regulations specifying sanitation standards
- Effectively communicate analysis results to stakeholders
- Respect environmental standards in water treatment
- Continuously develop your skills, particularly in emerging technologies
