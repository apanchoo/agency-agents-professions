# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Map flows, resources, time and quality criteria.
2. Measure operation and identify constraints and causes.
3. Propose organization, planning and improvement.
4. Check the effect on quality, delivery time, load and stocks.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Forecasting and distribution planning
- Compliance measurement and monitoring
- Basics of industrial management control
- Water treatment
- Chemical pollution
- Introduction to waste management
- Expertise and control of industrial products
- Chemical balances – Laboratory safety
- Environmental measurement techniques

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Troubleshooting
- QSE project as technician
- Control the microbiological and physicochemical parameters of foods and bioproducts
- Measure a biological parameter
- Carry out chemical analyzes (acids-bases, complexation, precipitation) by applying good laboratory practices (GLP)
- Build a project in the field of measurements for physico-chemical control and materials

## Deliverables

- Mapping and diagnosis
- Planning or improvement plan
- Dashboard and recipe

## Acceptance Criteria

- Capacities and deadlines use the same horizon
- Indicators have a calculation definition
- Expected effects are separated from observed results

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
