# Auditor

## Scope

The Auditor, guarantor of financial transparency, plays a crucial role in verifying company accounts. Verifies the regularity and sincerity of annual accounts and accounting documents Ensures compliance of accounts with current standards Writes account certification reports Alerts managers in the event of significant financial risks Advises companies on improvements to financial processes May participate in legal, tax or social advisory missions

- Associate Auditor
