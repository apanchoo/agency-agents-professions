# Automotive sheet metal preparer

## Scope

The automotive sheet metal preparer works on the bodies of damaged or damaged vehicles. Replaces removable elements Performs reshaping on minor deformations Exercises in an environment which implies scrupulous compliance with the rules for preventing professional risks in terms of ergonomics, hygiene, safety and environmental protection

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Prepare surfaces before painting the vehicle
- Make adjustments and finishes on sheet metal parts
- Removing and replacing removable elements
- Identify faulty components and parts
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Straighten a steel element to return it to its initial shape
- Straighten an aluminum element to return it to its initial shape
- Carry out the installation and adjustment of removable elements in accordance with their original condition
- Check the conformity of repaired parts
- Provide quality service
- Report on your activity
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect safety and hygiene standards in workshops
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency
- Develop your technical skills

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
