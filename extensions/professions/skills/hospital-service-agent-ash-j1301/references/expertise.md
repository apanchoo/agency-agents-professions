# Hospital service agent (ASH)

## Scope

The hospital service agent contributes to the comfort of people in health, medico-social and social establishments by ensuring the maintenance, order and cleanliness of their environment. Carries out the cleaning of people's rooms, common areas and offices Respects hygiene rules and protocols related to biocleaning and the fight against nosocomial diseases Prepares and participates in serving meals under the supervision of the nursing staff Participates in the laundry service such as sorting and distributing clean linen Assists the nursing staff in the preparation and storage of medical equipment Contributes to waste management

## Related Job Titles

- Catering and hotel agent
- Hospital hotel agent
- Agent / Bio-cleaning agent

## Activities and Skills

- Supporting people in everyday activities
- Distribute meals and snacks to the patient or resident according to needs or medical prescription
- Apply the principles of biocleaning
- Stock a cleaning cart or laundry cart
- Ensuring the cleanliness of medical devices
- Change the sheets, make the bed (clean, re-lay) and replace the bathroom linen
- Handle cleaning and maintenance products
- Clean, disinfect and tidy the room and sanitary facility
- Sort dirty laundry
- Carry out cleaning of surfaces and floors in a hospital environment
- Trace the hygiene actions carried out
- Apply and respect the dosage recommendations of the different maintenance products
- Maintain a clean and secure environment
- Check the contents of the cleaning cart and prepare the cleaning solutions
- Monitor inventory and report needs
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Evacuate and sort waste, products
- Develop and manage interpersonal relationships
- Relay information
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Follow cleaning and disinfection protocols
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Know how to alert in the event of a problem or emergency

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Products, tools and materials

- Characteristics of cleaning products
