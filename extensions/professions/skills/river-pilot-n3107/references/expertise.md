# River pilot

## Scope

The River Pilot is responsible for the navigation of different types of boats (barges, passenger boats, freight boats, etc.) on rivers, canals and other inland waterways. Drives and maneuvers river transport vessels on rivers and canals Ensures the safety of crew, passengers and cargo Manages cargo loading and unloading operations Monitors weather conditions and water levels to navigate efficiently Performs basic maintenance and upkeep tasks on the vessel Ensures maneuvers comply with maritime regulations

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise and coordinate a team
- Use modern navigation equipment
- Carry out repair operations
- Maintain nautical equipment
- Maintain a boat
- Inspect the boat before and after each trip
- Carry out a maintenance operation
- Ensuring the safety of passengers and crew
- Manage routes to avoid risk areas
- Set up the river navigation convoy (number and type of barges, electrical, hydraulic and ventilation connections)
- Organize ship refueling operations (fuel, food, equipment, etc.)
- Organize the allocation of a river fleet according to operational requirements
- Plan stops for maintenance and refueling
- Coordinate with customers, shippers and transportation companies
- Carry out docking or undocking maneuvers for the boat
- Carry out river tourism transport
- Carry out river transport of heavy packages
- Carry out a river navigation
- Carry out navigation requiring authorization
- Control access on board and check tickets
- Coordinate activities on board the ship
- Ensure communication with river authorities
- Keep the logbook up to date
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Navigate while respecting river safety rules
- Respect environmental standards when refueling
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
