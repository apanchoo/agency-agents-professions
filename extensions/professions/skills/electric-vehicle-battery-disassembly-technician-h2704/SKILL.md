## Scope

The Electric Vehicle Battery Dismantling Technician carries out dismantling and storage operations specific to vehicle batteries with partial or total electric propulsion. In this context, he is responsible for the application of safety rules at the workstation and masters intervention protocols specific to batteries, on technical and organizational aspects. Checks the physical integrity of the battery (watertightness, degradation, etc.) and its traceability Establishes diagnostics Dismantles the battery and its components Secures, conditions and stores the battery Sorts and safely stores the electronic and electrical components of a vehicle with partial or total electric propulsion

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Diagnose the internal condition of the electrical and electronic components of the battery of an electric or hybrid vehicle
- Removing the battery pack from an electric or hybrid vehicle
- Removing the battery pack from an electric or hybrid vehicle
- Separate and store the internal components of the battery of an electric or hybrid vehicle
- Check the physical integrity of an electric or hybrid vehicle
- Plan the carrying out of live work safely and in compliance with regulations
- Condition the battery pack and its elements according to their specificities, their functional state and according to the existence and type of risks
- Store the battery pack and its elements according to their final destination
