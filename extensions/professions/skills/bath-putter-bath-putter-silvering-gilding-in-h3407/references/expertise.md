# Bath putter / Bath putter silvering - gilding in goldsmithing

## Scope

The Goldsmith's Bath Master carries out metallization operations (silvering/gilding) using electrolysis surface treatment equipment. Manages the baths ensuring the surface treatment of goldwork pieces (silvering/gilding) Carries out surface treatments with the aesthetic requirements of goldsmithing (silvering) Ensures fine manual cutting of varnishes for total or partial gilding (gilding) Executes operations with the aesthetic requirements of goldsmithing (gilding)
