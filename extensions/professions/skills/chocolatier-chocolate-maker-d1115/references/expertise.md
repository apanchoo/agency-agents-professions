# Chocolatier / Chocolate maker

## Scope

The chocolatier, an artisan of taste and precision, creates unique chocolate creations. Selects and prepares ingredients for making chocolates and confectionery Makes chocolates, truffles and other specialties according to traditional or innovative recipes Decorates and personalizes chocolate products for various occasions Ensures the quality and conservation of finished products Can sell and advise customers in a dedicated store Manages stocks of raw materials and finished products

## Related Job Titles

- Pastry chef-chocolatier / Pastry chef-chocolate maker

## Activities and Skills

- Making chocolates
- Decorating chocolates with fine techniques
- Proceed with the production of ganaches, pralines, truffles and other specialties
- Select cocoa types for specific creations
- Temper the chocolate to obtain a perfect texture
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Analyze the quality of cocoa beans
- Package chocolates while respecting conservation standards
- Respect product retention periods
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Define supply needs
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Ensure competitive intelligence on the chocolate market
- Collaborate with suppliers for the selection of raw materials
- Determining fair sales prices
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Evaluate chocolate market trends
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
- Understand the different types of chocolate, their origins and their characteristics
- Master additional ingredients, such as dried fruits, spices, flavorings

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials
