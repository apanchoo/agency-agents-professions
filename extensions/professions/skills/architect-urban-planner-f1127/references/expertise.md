# Architect-urban planner

## Scope

The architect-urban planner shapes urban spaces so that they are functional, harmonious and sustainable, thus helping to improve the quality of life of citizens. Designs and directs the implementation of urban development projects that integrate large-scale planning Analyzes the needs of populations and environmental constraints to propose suitable solutions Coordinates the various stakeholders in the project (engineers, designers, local authorities) Ensures compliance with urban planning standards and regulations in force Participates in impact studies and public consultations to integrate user opinions into projects Can specialize in urban renovation or sustainable development projects through urban diagnostics

- General urban planner / General urban architect of the State
