# Head of socio-educational service

## Scope

The head of the socio-educational department supervises the socio-educational teams and ensures the coordination of actions carried out within his department towards the people supported and their entourage. Ensures an interface role between the management of the structure and the teams he supervises Participates in the development of the service or establishment project as part of the development of his structure Supervises the teams and ensures the improvement of the skills of the employees Organizes, coordinates, supervises and ensures the quality of the socio-educational activities implemented within the service Develops partnerships in the territory to improve the service offering

## Related Job Titles

- Socio-educational supervisor
- Manager of socio-educational services
- Socio-educational manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Develop the team’s individual and collective skills
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Manage human resources on a daily basis
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Pay attention to the successful integration of each new employee
- Participate in the implementation of HR projects facilitating inclusion, equality and quality of life at work within the structure
- Support and develop autonomy in the use of digital tools and digital services
- Support professionals in taking a step back from the situations encountered
- Inform and train staff on health innovations
- Prevent psychosocial risks
- Implement diversity and inclusion policies
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Ensure monitoring of current regulations
- Facilitate teams’ appropriation of societal and legal developments as well as social policies
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Support the implementation of a socio-educational project by proposing areas of adaptation based on the reflective analysis of professional practices
- Analyze feedback to improve practices
- Ensure monitoring of performance indicators (KPI)
- Evaluate the implementation of a project, an action plan
- Plan, coordinate and set up events with stakeholders in the sector
- Facilitate inter-departmental communication for better coordination
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Report the information collected in the field to management
- Raise team awareness of the issues of Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR)
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Use digital tools
