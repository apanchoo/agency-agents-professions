# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Establish uses and consumption balance with their units.
2. Model heat flows and operating conditions.
3. Compare technical variants, costs and impacts.
4. Prepare the sizing, monitoring and verification of gains.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Solar thermal and photovoltaic
- Renewable energies
- Basics of Thermodynamics
- Production management and optimization
- Sustainable development
- Managing the impact of anthropogenic activities
- Project management 3: Lead a professional project
- Data sources
- Norms, standards and risk analysis

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Optimization of the energy and environmental performance of a building, a site or an installation as part of a design project or an energy audit
- Assessment of an organization’s greenhouse gas emissions and development of a low carbon strategy
- Organize and implement an animal experimentation procedure within the framework of current regulations
- Monitoring a treatment process and its impact on the environment
- Carry out a technological watch
- Implement an IP telephony solution according to customer needs

## Deliverables

- Energy balance
- Comparison of solutions
- Measurement and monitoring plan

## Acceptance Criteria

- The perimeters and climatic conditions are explained
- Yields and conversion factors are sourced
- Estimated gains are separated from measured gains

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
