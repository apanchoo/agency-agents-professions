# Silo Agent

## Scope

The Silo Agent manages the storage and preservation of stocks of agricultural raw materials before their processing or distribution. Manages reception, storage and conservation operations for grains or other products Controls product quality and conservation conditions Coordinates product loading and unloading activities Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Maintains the cleanliness and safety of storage facilities

## Related Job Titles

- Cereal silo driver
- Silo driver in the food industry
- Silo driver in the dairy industry
- Automated silo driver

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Collect samples for analysis
- Carry out technical or technological monitoring to anticipate developments
- Update maintenance log
- Use inventory management software
- Monitor silo temperature and humidity parameters
- Use and adjust agri-food machines or equipment
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Carry out cleaning operations on silos and equipment
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Carry out preventive maintenance operations on silo machines
- Analyze the risks of contamination
- Check the quality of cereals upon reception
- Check product storage conditions
- Ensure the traceability of stored batches of cereals
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Collaborate with logistics teams to coordinate raw material flows and optimize loading and unloading operations
- Packaging products
- Move and store products and materials according to quality and safety standards
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Carry out regular reporting to the hierarchy
- Report on your activity
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect safety and hygiene standards within the silo enclosure
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Develop your skills by following continuing training

## Professional Approach
