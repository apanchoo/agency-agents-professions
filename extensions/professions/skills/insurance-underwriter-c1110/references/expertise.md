# Insurance underwriter

## Scope

The Insurance Underwriter defines and implements the subscription rules and pricing levels for insurance products. Determines coverage conditions and rates for insurance policies based on assessed risks Collaborates with insurance agents and brokers to provide advice and solutions tailored to customer needs Follows market trends and regulations to adapt insurance offerings Manages customer files and monitors current policies
