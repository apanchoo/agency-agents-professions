# Operating room nurse (IBODE)

## Scope

Organizes the care of patients in the operating room and provides technical assistance to the practitioner throughout the procedure according to the surgical protocol and the rules of hygiene and asepsis. Implements health and safety measures taking into account the risks inherent to the nature of the interventions and the specificity of the patients, working in protected areas and the use of specific medical devices. Can coordinate a team.
