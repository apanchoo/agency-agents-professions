# Guardian of protected adults

## Scope

The guardian of protected adults ensures the interests of vulnerable people. Manages the property and rights of persons placed under guardianship or curatorship Ensures the legal representation of protected adults in acts of civil life Develops a forecast budget and ensures its monitoring Reports regularly to the guardianship judge on the management carried out Can intervene in medical or educational decisions in collaboration with other professionals Supports protected adults in the realization of their life projects

## Related Job Titles

- Supervisory manager
- Tutor for social benefits
- Curator Tutor

## Activities and Skills

- Assess a person's specific needs
- Facilitate the resolution of questions and administrative procedures
- Guide, support a person or family in their efforts
- Observe the state of mental and physical health of the person accompanied
- Protect the interests and rights of vulnerable people while respecting the legal framework
- Emotionally supporting individuals in difficulty
- Take inventory of a person's resources
- Monitor the evolution of the situation of individuals
- Anticipate financial, social or legal risks to secure the protected person’s journey
- Manage the financial, property and administrative resources of protected adults
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Support people in managing their budget
- Apply legal protection measures in accordance with court decisions
- Report on management to judicial and administrative authorities
- Conduct social investigations upon judicial request
- Protect people and property
- Evaluate the personal, social and financial situation of protected persons
- Liaise with social services
- Communicate with those close to the person being supported
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported

## Professional Approach
