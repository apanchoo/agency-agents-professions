## Scope

The electrical network installer is responsible for the implementation, operation, maintenance and optimization of electricity transmission and distribution networks. Carries out installation operations for overhead or underground cabling of distribution networks (electricity, signals, etc.) according to the safety rules and regulations in force Ensures the monitoring and operation of electricity distribution networks to guarantee their operation and safety Carries out preventive and corrective maintenance interventions on electrical lines, transformers, and associated equipment Diagnoses breakdowns or anomalies on the electrical network and implements the necessary actions to remedy them Manages power outages and coordination of interventions to restore them quickly Monitors network performance indicators and proposes improvement solutions

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Disable and log an electrical system
- Commission an electric meter, boxes and panels, electrical systems
- Act quickly in the event of a breakdown to minimize interruptions
- Diagnose electrical network malfunctions
- Maintain electrical installations and equipment
- Anticipate dangers, problems or material and technical needs
- Check the quality of electrical installations
