# Forestry Works Driver

## Scope

The forestry work manager orchestrates the management of forestry work, combining technical expertise and respect for the environment to preserve and enhance the natural heritage. Manage an operational team and implement operational objectives Opens, monitors and accepts a site Ensures compliance with environmental and safety standards during forestry interventions Manages the budget and material resources necessary for projects (equipment, vehicles, premises) Can participate in research and development projects on sustainable forest management

## Related Job Titles

- Forest exploitation supervisory agent
- Forestry site manager
- Head of forest district
- Sector manager on forestry sites

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate team activities in the field
- Encourage innovation and creativity within the team
- Organize the work of a team
- Ensure personnel management
- Train staff in forest safety standards
- Organize training for teams in the field
- Promote the integration of innovative technologies
- Carry out impact studies and reports on the state of forest areas
- Analyze geographic data for site planning
- Integrate technological solutions into forest management
- Monitor work progress schedules and make the necessary adjustments to meet deadlines and the committed budget
- Monitor the condition of a plantation
- Establish traceability systems for cut timber
- Identify the trees on an operating site
- Identify trees to be felled or pruned
- Carry out development work
- Organize meetings and site visits
- Planning forestry operations
- Monitor all work, ensure compliance with quality and deadlines and control pre-established budgets
- Check the quality and cleanliness of equipment or materials
- Evaluate the performance of forestry equipment
- Assess the risks associated with forestry operations
- Oversee the safety of forestry operations
- Supervise tree cutting and processing operations
- Supervise maintenance operations
- Supervise forest infrastructure work
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Define supply needs
- Manage the equipment fleet, vehicles and premises
- Organize order processing
- Prepare work progress reports
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Monitor the application of environmental standards
- Supervise and control the implementation of work safety procedures and instructions
- Analyze the health of trees and plants
- Evaluate the environmental impact of forestry activities
- Optimize the use of natural resources
- Manage relations with local authorities
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Design biodiversity conservation programs
- Develop partnerships with environmental NGOs, schools, citizen groups and other stakeholders
- Communicate with internal and external contacts
- Raise environmental awareness and promote sustainable development
- Document standard operating procedures
- Managing internal conflicts
