# Garden center salesperson

## Scope

The Seller garden center advice, an expert in greenery and personalized advice for any green space. Advises customers on the choice of plants, products and equipment adapted to their needs and their environment Sells plants, gardening products and equipment Organizes and maintains the sales area to guarantee the quality and visibility of the products Can produce garden layout plans for customers Manages stocks and product orders Monitors market trends to offer innovative and attractive products

- Nursery salesperson
- Agricultural sector salesperson
