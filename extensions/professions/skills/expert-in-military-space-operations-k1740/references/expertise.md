# Expert in military space operations

## Scope

The Military Space Operations expert is responsible for the planning, programming, execution and supervision of military space operations. Ensures the coordination of satellite systems, data management and communications Leads, optimizes and coordinates the use of military space capabilities made available to defense Orients and participates in the reflections of the doctrinaire corpus accordingly Measures their effectiveness with respect to the operational objectives set and coordinates the activities of RETEX in the use of space capabilities by the armies Participates in the preparation of scenarios and the conduct of space exercises
