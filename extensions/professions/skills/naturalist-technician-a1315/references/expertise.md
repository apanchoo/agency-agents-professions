# Naturalist technician

## Scope

The naturalist technician is a specialist in fauna or flora or fungi, as well as where applicable in geological heritage, often in a particular group such as botany (flora), ornithology (birds), entomology (insects), chiropterology (bats), fauna, etc. Carry out naturalistic monitoring through inventories by collecting information and samples in the natural environment according to a precise protocol Carry out naturalistic studies through the choice of the inventory method, the collection of existing data, the synthesis of information and the definition of the appropriate protocols Conducts inventories in the field (species, habitats and functions) following established protocols (naturalistic monitoring) Produces reports, banks the data and models them Contributes to carrying out diagnostics ecological, and impact studies, in particular the evaluation of the state of conservation of species and natural habitats and the proposal of avoidance, reduction or compensation measures
