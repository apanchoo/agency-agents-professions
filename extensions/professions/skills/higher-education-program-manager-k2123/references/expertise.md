# Higher education program manager

## Scope

The Higher Education Program Manager works on the design, development and improvement of academic teaching programs within higher education establishments, such as universities, business schools, engineering schools, and other institutions. Designs and develops higher education programs adapted to the needs of the labor market and academic standards Supervises and coordinates the activities of teachers and stakeholders Ensures the quality and continuous updating of teaching programs Develops partnerships with academic and professional institutions Evaluates the effectiveness of programs through feedback from students and teachers May participate in conferences and seminars to represent the institution and its programs

## Related Job Titles

- Head of Department of Higher Education
- Higher education sector manager
- Higher education subject manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate activities between different departments
- Coordinate with professionals and develop the appropriate network of stakeholders
- Ensure constant monitoring of the program to improve the educational experience and student outcomes
- Design training engineering and educational sequences
- Coordinate external stakeholders for training
- Coordinate educational programs according to institutional guidelines
- Define educational resources
- Define a learning objective and the program of educational activities
- Develop and structure course content in collaboration with teachers and experts
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Facilitate communication between the different stakeholders (students, teachers, administration)
- Update programs according to academic and professional developments
- Organize continuing training for staff
- Plan and organize courses, seminars, and other educational activities
- Promote innovative teaching methods
- Promote international study programs and participate in the development of academic partnerships
- Chair or participate in an examination board
- Collect feedback from students and teachers to improve courses
- Ensure the quality and relevance of educational content, in line with academic standards and the needs of the job market
- Inform students and teachers about educational projects
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Evaluate and improve teaching methods
- Analyze labor market trends
- Design and manage a project
- Develop partnerships with other educational structures
- Develop partnerships with institutions
- Manage relationships with external stakeholders (partners, companies)
- Prepare educational projects and activities with teachers and supervise them
- Know how to manage several projects or activities simultaneously
