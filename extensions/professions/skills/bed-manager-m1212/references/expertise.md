# Bed Manager

## Scope

The Bed Manager collects and analyzes information on a daily basis to anticipate and optimize the allocation of hospitalized patients and the management of beds and places within the practice area. Optimizes bed occupancy to ensure efficient management of hospital resources Coordinates with different departments to ensure adequate bed allocation Tracks admissions and discharges to adjust bed availability in real time Participates in the continuous improvement of bed management processes to increase operational efficiency Uses hospital management software to keep bed information up to date Ensures communication between medical and administrative teams to optimize bed utilization

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams for patient monitoring
- Optimize staffing, adequacy and allocation of resources
- Organize the distribution of beds in a health establishment
- Train and develop team skills
- Coordinate the circuit of information on the functioning of a structure
- Update available bed information
- Optimize information dissemination processes
- Assign the most suitable bed to patients in compliance with established rules
- Archive documents allowing traceability of exchanges with clinical services (cancellations, etc.)
- Permanently monitor the status of patients to be hospitalized and hospitalized in the bed management software
- Control the consistency of data and information
- Coordinate patient admissions and discharges
- Communicate the availability of beds to administrative and nursing staff
- Receive, control and process accommodation requests and implement solutions for accommodation outside the perimeter managed by the bed management unit
- Write security reports
- Enter data relating to scheduled or unscheduled entrances and exits and update movements (programming, unprogramming, non-arrival) in the bed management software
- Ensure monitoring of performance indicators (KPI)
- Implement procedures to improve operational efficiency
- Optimize organizational performance
- Ensure the interface between the different members of the team and staff
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Effectively communicate needs and emergencies
- Convince by arguing with one or more interlocutors
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Establish a relationship of trust with the medical, nursing and paramedical profession
- Promote communication between different stakeholders
- Identify, analyze errors in communication or processing of information and seek their causes
- Collect oral and written information relating to forecasts and patient flows (entrances, transfers and exits)
- Relaunch clinical services for completeness of the information necessary for bed management (reason for entry, etc.)
- Alert about incidents, malfunctions and risks relating to bed management
- Deciding on the best solution between several options
- Identify, analyze, prioritize and synthesize information relating to your field of activity
- Prioritize tasks based on emergencies and needs
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
