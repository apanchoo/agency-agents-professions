# Steward / Train hostess

## Scope

The train steward or hostess mainly sells fast food while providing friendly, quality service. Informs passengers about timetables, stops and connections Serves drinks, snacks and meals to passengers in a courteous and professional manner Manages stocks of catering products and ensures their restocking Follows cash register procedures and manages financial transactions Manages emergency situations and ensures compliance with safety procedures

## Related Job Titles

- Commercial / Commercial on board trains

## Activities and Skills

- Use specific software
- Prepare hot or cold drinks
- Prepare or reheat breads and pastries
- Prepare or reheat catering dishes
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Ensuring passenger safety when stopping and starting
- Ensuring the safety and comfort of passengers during transport
- Proceed to cashout
- Control access on board the train
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Arrange products at the point of sale
- Clearly communicate times and stops to passengers
- Communicate effectively with passengers and/or their attendants
- Demonstrate diplomacy and strength of conviction
- Working in a group, in a network
- Inform passengers of safety procedures on board
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Be proactive
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Types of catering products
- Types of sandwiches and snacking products
- Use of cleaning equipment
- Basic maintenance of train interior

## Areas of expertise

- Rail routes
- Emergency and first aid procedures
- Services on board

## Standards and processes

- Rail safety rules
- Knowledge of onboard cleaning procedures

## Professional techniques

- Reception and orientation of passengers
- Management of passenger complaints and requests
- Management of reservations and ticketing
- Preparation of travel reports
- Interpersonal communication techniques

- National zone
- Business travel
- In contact with the public
- Wearing professional attire or uniform
