# Luthier / Violin maker

## Scope

The luthier manufactures, repairs and maintains plucked string instruments such as guitars, and bowed string instruments of the quartet, such as violins. Designs and manufactures stringed musical instruments in an artisanal or semi-industrial manner Selects quality materials for the construction and repair of instruments Makes adjustments and adjustments to optimize the sound of instruments Collaborates with musicians to adapt instruments to their specific needs May specialize in one type of instrument, such as electric guitar or cello
