# Charter group leader

## Scope

The charter group leader manages and coordinates the charter activity, optimizing its quality and profitability; he can carry out this activity on all modes of transport. Organizes and supervises charter operations Negotiates and establishes charter contracts with carriers and customers Negotiates the purchase and sale of services in compliance with budgetary objectives Guarantees compliance with commercial commitments Develops and retains the charter customer portfolio Develops the network of subcontractors Optimizes flows with a view to profitability Coordinates the service in conjunction with the operations and logistics teams Analyzes the performance of charter operations Proposes improvements Leads and supervises the charter team

## Related Job Titles

- Head of charter department
- Charter service manager

## Activities and Skills

- Contribute to maintaining the team's negotiation skills
- Supervise a team of charterers
- Supervise and evaluate team performance
- Apply processing to data to analyze and interpret it (with a spreadsheet, processing software, a calculation query in a database, etc.)
- Check data compliance
- Develop digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects, AI, platforms) adapted to the activity and develop their use
- Update operations management tool settings
- Optimize operational activity
- Select and monitor service providers and stakeholders, in order to guarantee quality of service
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Organize the national and international transport chain
- Check the conformity of transport operations for specific products
- Coordinate multimodal freight transport operations
- Coordinate operations to improve logistics processes
- Analyze budget and cost variances and propose adjustments
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Evaluate and measure the performance of subcontractors (including their regulatory compliance) obligations, registers, contributions
- Monitor activity performance indicators
- Pilot actions that take sustainable development into account in operations (optimization of loading capacity, selection of carriers, etc.)
- Decide on the terms of purchase or sale of the transport service
- Develop the charter customer portfolio
- Establish commercial proposals, write price offers, prepare quotes
- Contribute to the sourcing of new service providers, partners, networks
- Develop the network of subcontractors to enrich the transport offer
- Develop dashboards
- Establish commercial charter offers in line with the company's strategy
- Identify and activate business growth levers (new lines, partnerships, diversification)
- Respond to national European and international transport tenders
- Clearly communicate decisions, arbitrations and instructions
- Listen to customer needs
- Adapt your communication to your interlocutors
- Monitor the application of regulatory and safety standards
- Enforce safety procedures
- Manage crisis situations (accidental factors, geopolitics, etc.)
- Managing emergencies and unforeseen events in transport
- Maintain regulatory, technical and competitive monitoring
- Use digital tools
