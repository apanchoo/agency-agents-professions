# Service Quality Director

## Scope

The Service Quality Director ensures the supervision and continuous improvement of quality standards within the service company. Defines and implements the company's quality policy in accordance with the overall strategy Supervises internal and external audits to ensure compliance with quality standards Analyzes performance data to identify areas for improvement Trains and supervises teams in quality procedures Collaborates with the different departments to integrate quality requirements into all activities Manages relations with certification bodies and monitors the certifications obtained

## Related Job Titles

- Service quality auditor
- Service quality manager
