# Technical agent for supplementary pension and provident schemes

## Scope

The Technical Agent for supplementary pension and provident schemes is involved in the management of pension rights and provident cover for members. Calculates retirement rights, verifies the accuracy of information and monitors beneficiaries' accounts Responds to clients' questions and provides advice on their retirement and pension situation Handles complaints from affiliates by providing clear and precise information in an effort to improve procedures Collaborates with other services to optimize the management of pension and pension plans
