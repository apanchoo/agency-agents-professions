# Decorator / Interior decorator

## Scope

The Interior Designer designs and creates aesthetic and functional arrangements to optimize interior spaces. Designs decoration and interior space development projects according to the client's needs Chooses materials, colors and furniture to create aesthetic harmony Produces plans and 3D models to visualize the final project Coordinates with craftsmen and suppliers for the implementation of the project Monitors the construction site to ensure compliance with deadlines and the quality of the work Advises clients on decoration and interior design

## Related Job Titles

- Decorator
- Decorator / Consulting decorator
- Decorator/Furnishing consultant
- Decoration and layout manager

## Activities and Skills

- Make an aesthetic or artistic appreciation
- Design structures and decorative elements
- Design layout or layout plans using a digital tool
- Design interior layouts and layouts
- Create plans and sketches in PO3D
- Adapt proposals based on customer feedback
- Define the colored harmonies, technical and decorative lighting and materials (fabrics, tiles, walls, etc.)
- Participate in the choices and proposals of subjects
- Select materials with the client based on budget and aesthetics
- Choose suitable materials for decorations
- Design interior designs with project managers
- Optimize interior space and functionality
- Monitor all work, ensure compliance with quality and deadlines and control pre-established budgets
- Ensure style consistency across rooms
- Ensure relations and monitoring of the project with subcontractors
- Manage files in their entirety, from welcoming the client to delivery of the site
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Negotiate with suppliers of decorative elements
- Respect the deadlines set for the completion of the project
- Manage the budget allocated to decoration
- Integrate ecological solutions into projects
- Providing tailor-made and ergonomic solutions to customers
- Assist the client in site reception
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Create presentation folders
- Propose, according to customer files, decoration coaching
- Prepare or establish commercial proposals, write price offers, develop quotes
- Identify trends and decoration atmospheres
- Design and manage a project
- Monitor the progress of an interior decoration project
- Respect environmental standards in the use of products and materials
