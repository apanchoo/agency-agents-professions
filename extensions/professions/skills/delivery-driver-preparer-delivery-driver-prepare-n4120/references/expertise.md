# Delivery driver preparer / Delivery driver prepare orders

## Scope

The Preparing Delivery Driver / Preparing Delivery Driver is essential to ensure logistics and customer satisfaction through efficient order distribution. Prepares orders and arranges delivery routes to optimize lead times Drives a light vehicle to deliver goods to customers in assigned geographies Loads and unloads goods, ensuring the safety and integrity of products being transported Verifies purchase orders and invoices, ensuring deliveries are complete and accurate Maintains vehicle in good condition, performing regular checks and reporting maintenance needs May manage merchandise returns and resolve delivery issues directly with customers

## Related Job Titles

- Store driver

- Check the operating condition of the vehicle
- Perform basic vehicle maintenance
- Maintain the vehicle in good clean and working condition
- Check the accuracy and conformity of goods transport documents
- Load, unload, handle products
- Use handling equipment safely
- Ensure the safety of transported goods and the vehicle
- Driving a road vehicle
- Deliver an order
- Organize optimized delivery routes
- Organize vehicle loading according to the tour plan
- Track orders, invoicing
- Update delivery or collection tracking data on digital or handwritten media
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Present and promote a product or service
- Maintain clear and professional communication
- Adapt your driving according to weather conditions
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Contact the center for any questions or problems during delivery
- Respect the environmental standards in force during deliveries
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
