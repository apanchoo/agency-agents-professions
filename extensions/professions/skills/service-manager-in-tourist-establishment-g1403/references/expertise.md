# Service manager in tourist establishment

## Scope

The Service Manager in a tourist establishment organizes, coordinates and supervises the activities of a service (accommodation, catering, reception, maintenance, etc.) in order to guarantee customer satisfaction and service production objectives. Ensures team management, staff training and compliance with service standards Analyzes the establishment's performance and proposes operational improvements Develops strategies to improve the customer experience Ensures coordination and communication with the establishment's various departments
