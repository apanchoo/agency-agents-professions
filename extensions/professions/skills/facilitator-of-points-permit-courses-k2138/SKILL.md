## Scope

The Points License Internship Facilitator raises awareness of road safety. Co-leads sessions with a psychologist facilitator Co-leads road safety awareness sessions for drivers who have lost points on their license Uses educational materials to facilitate participants' understanding and learning Assesses participants' knowledge at the start and end of the session to measure the impact of the training Encourages exchanges and discussions between participants to share experiences and reinforce key messages Regularly updates knowledge of road regulations to provide precise information Ensures administrative monitoring of courses and the issuance of certificates of participation

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Provide a training program aimed at initiating a process of change in attitudes and behavior among offending drivers
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Inform trainees about road safety rules, the effects of alcohol and drugs on driving, driving fatigue, etc.
- Raise awareness of the causes and consequences of road accidents to avoid repeat offenses
- Stimulate exchanges and discussions between participants to promote engagement and individual awareness
- Evaluate the effectiveness of courses on driver behavior
- Ensure administrative follow-up of trainees (certificates of participation, transmission of information to prefectures, etc.)
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
