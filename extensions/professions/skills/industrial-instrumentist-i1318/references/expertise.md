# Industrial Instrumentist

## Scope

The industrial instrumentist measures and controls instruments in industrial environments. Ensures the installation, calibration and maintenance of measurement and control instruments Optimizes regulation systems to guarantee efficient and secure production Performs diagnostics and resolves technical problems with equipment Collaborates with engineering teams to improve industrial processes Participates in the training of new technicians on specific equipment Ensures compliance with safety and environmental standards in the performance of his duties

## Related Job Titles

- Instrumentalist
- Electronic device instrumentalist
- Instrumentist on automatic analyzers
- Electrician fitter / Electrician fitter in control and regulation installations
- Instrument adjuster
- Measuring and regulating equipment maintenance technician

## Activities and Skills

- Define and implement technical measurement solutions to get to the root cause of a malfunction
- Check data compliance
- Configure an automation program for a programmable controller
- Measure electrical characteristics
- Record, control, adjust measurements and dosages
- Install and adjust hydraulic power elements (cylinders, motors, etc.)
- Install electronic equipment
- Carry out mechanical construction and maintenance work
- Ensure the maintenance of measuring equipment
- Ensure basic preventive maintenance of equipment
- Check the conformity of equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Repair equipment, a machine, an installation
- Check the quality of electrical installations
- Determine corrective measures
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Check the tightness
- Produce inspection and diagnostic reports for anomalies or risks and transmit them to the production, maintenance and security departments.
- Mobilize eco-gestures
- Promote sustainable practices
- Advocate for environmental measures
- Communicate technical issues effectively
- Apply a safety instruction
- Ensure machining production in compliance with safety objectives and rules
- Check compliance with the rules for using equipment and materials and organize their maintenance
- Manage technical emergencies on equipment
- Meet production deadlines to meet customer requests
- Comply with current safety standards

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
