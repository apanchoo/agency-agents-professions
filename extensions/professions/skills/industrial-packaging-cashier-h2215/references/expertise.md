# Industrial packaging cashier

## Scope

The industrial packaging cashier is responsible for manufacturing the box in compliance with the rules and operating procedures. Uses different types of machines and portable tools to manufacture pieces of wood Assembles the pieces and controls the quality of production Ensures the maintenance of the equipment and diagnoses possible malfunctions Cleans its workstation and premises

## Related Job Titles

- Box Packer

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Record activities in a log
- Organize your space and workstation in line with a production program
- Cut, point, cut, drill large pieces of wood
- Cut and shape the pieces of wood according to the required dimensions
- Start or stop a machine, a line
- Determine the wood volumes (type, length, section, etc.)
- Equip the workstation with the tools, supplies, materials adapted and verified according to the activity to be carried out
- Shape wooden elements on a numerically controlled or automated machine
- Identify manufacturing operations from the manufacturing file
- Mark finished products to guarantee their good transport and conservation conditions
- Select wood or composite materials according to the project
- Trace cuts in pieces of wood, assemblies
- Use and adjust machines or equipment elements of the wood industry
- Use hand and portable tools (hammers, chasers, planes, cutter meters, etc.)
- Assemble wooden structures
- Detect and describe equipment malfunction
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Maintain cleanliness of equipment and workspace
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Act quickly in the event of a manufacturing defect detected
- Control the quality of wood and assemblies
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Carry out control readings on the work sheets
- Check the conformity of the mounting and assembly
- Optimize production flows and completed tasks with the aim of saving materials and reducing waste
- Check the conformity of an achievement with specifications
- Ensure that activities are carried out in compliance with quality, cost and deadline objectives
- Cooperate with other teams and external partners
- Identify and take into account the constraints and needs of other teams
- Relay information
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Transmit information and ensure understanding by using appropriate language to internal and external stakeholders
- Act quickly in the event of a breakdown to minimize production stoppages
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
