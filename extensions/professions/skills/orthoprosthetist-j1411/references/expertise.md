# Orthoprosthetist

## Scope

The orthoprosthetist is a craftsman of rehabilitation and autonomy, he designs tailor-made medical devices. Designs and produces personalized orthopedic devices to improve patient mobility Adapts prostheses and orthoses according to the specific needs of users Performs adjustments and repairs on existing devices for optimal comfort Advises patients on the use and maintenance of their orthopedic equipment Collaborates with medical teams to offer solutions adapted to each case Participates in continuing training to master the latest technologies and materials in orthopedics

## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Identify medical, psychological factors and the patient's environment, examine the area to be fitted and analyze posture (walking test, grip test, etc.)
- Advise the person (use, adaptation, maintenance) and care partners
- Determine the possibilities of equipment according to pathological and medical constraints and make the choice with the person
- Identify the person's needs and inform them about the treatment (number of sessions, impression taking methods, type of equipment, etc.)
- Carry out or control hygiene and comfort care and provide assistance to the patient
- Innovate in prosthetics techniques
- Analyze a medical prescription
- Manage technical documentation for equipment
- Design limb orthoses
- Design prosthetic limbs
- Cut, shape the materials and assemble the elements of the equipment (orthosis, prosthesis, etc.)
- Take an impression (prosthesis molding, orthosis)
- Analyze specific prosthetic needs
- Try the device on the person
- Proceed with the definitive installation of a prosthesis or the replacement of an orthosis
- Carry out the aesthetic shaping of a device (ocular prosthesis, facial prosthesis, etc.)
- Carry out the assembly of articulation elements (mechanical, electronic, etc.) on equipment (myoelectric prosthesis, etc.)
- Maintain prosthesis manufacturing equipment in good condition
- Control the quality of the materials used
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Carry out financial transactions in cash, bank cards
- Arguing about technical choices before medical authorities
- Collaborate with medical teams for personalized solutions
- Communicate with patients and their families
- Act quickly in the event of equipment faults
- Respect workshop safety standards
- Participate in continuing education sessions
