# Fruit picker

## Scope

The fruit picker carries out a job in the heart of nature, he is an essential player in fruit farms. Harvests different types of fruits depending on the season Ensures the selection of fruits according to their maturity to guarantee quality Transports the harvested fruits to storage areas Can participate in the maintenance of fruit trees and plantations Can sort and package the fruits

## Related Job Titles

- Cherry picker
- Peach picker
- Pear picker
- Apple picker
- Carrier / Carrier of hoods
- Grape harvester

## Activities and Skills

- Check the maturity of a fruit
- Maintain a tree, a plantation
- Handle fragile products gently to avoid damaging them
- Prepare materials for work
- Harvest a product at maturity and according to the calibration instructions
- Selecting fruits for direct sale
- Use specific picking equipment
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Organize picking areas
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Package products in crates or containers to facilitate transport without damaging them
- Receive and ship orders
- Sort, store products, raw materials
- Transport goods
- Manage picking waste in an ecological way
- Monitor trees and plants for signs of disease or pests and notify agricultural officials if necessary
- Respect the picking times set according to weather conditions
- Follow safety procedures when handling loads
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit

- Demonstrate rigor and precision

## Working Conditions

- Seasonal employment contract
- In difficult climatic environment
- Outdoors
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Carrying and handling heavy or bulky loads
- Prolonged standing
- Work during school holidays
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Seasonal work
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity
- Agricultural exploitation
