# Electronic installation and maintenance technician

## Scope

The Electronic Installation and Maintenance Technician installs and ensures corrective and preventive maintenance of devices, equipment, installations or systems with electronic components. Sets up and configures electronic systems according to technical specifications Performs performance and safety tests on electronic equipment Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of electronic installations Advises and trains users on the handling of electronic equipment Manages stocks of spare parts and orders new necessary components
