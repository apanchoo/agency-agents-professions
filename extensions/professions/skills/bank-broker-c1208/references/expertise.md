# Bank broker

## Scope

The bank broker facilitates financial transactions, particularly in terms of loans, credits and financing between clients and banks. Supports clients in all stages of the loan process, from initial application to signature Ensures follow-up of client files and updates of financial information Negotiates with banking establishments to obtain the best loan conditions Develops and maintains a network of financial partners to diversify loan offers Advises clients on the different loan restructuring and investment options

## Related Job Titles

- Brokerage account manager
- Credit broker
- Broker in wealth management
- Loan broker
- Real estate loan broker

## Activities and Skills

- Support in validating the file and signing the file
- Update a file, a database
- Complete administrative and regulatory formalities
- Collect all customer supporting documents
- Compare offers from several banks to find financing adapted to the property loan agreed with the client
- Advise on the choice of bank based on the rates and rewards offered (rate, insurance, maturity, etc.)
- Negotiate financing terms with banks
- Search for different banks likely to accept the financing file established by the broker
- Help upgrade the file to send to the banks
- Analyze and manage financial risks
- Analyze a client's financial situation: checking their income, debts, legal arrangements and repayment capacity
- Design complex financial arrangements
- Create real estate loan or loan repurchase files
- Create and decide on a financing file
- Create a credit file
- Reassess the client's project taking into account the financial analysis carried out
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Follow a contract, check compliance with contractual clauses
- Support the client with the validation of the file by the bank
- Advise the client during negotiations with the bank
- Advise on loans (rates, maturities, volume)
- Take stock with the client after acceptance of the file by the different banks
- Develop the financing file adapted to your client’s situation
- Collect and analyze customer needs
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop a customer and prospect portfolio
- Design and manage a project
- Build a network of professionals in the field (real estate agents, notaries, etc.) to access various information and guide clients
- Search for financing, investors
- Convince, negotiate
- Listen to customer needs
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Ensure constant monitoring of legislative developments around finance, interest rates, loan assistance and real estate purchases
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures
- Respect the confidentiality of information
