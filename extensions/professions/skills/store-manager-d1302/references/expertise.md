# Store manager

## Scope

The store manager manages the daily life of a sales space to maximize its attractiveness and performance. Manages the daily activity of the store to ensure customer satisfaction and achieve sales objectives Supervises and trains a sales team to optimize customer service Implements merchandising strategies to maximize the visual impact of products Analyzes performance indicators to adjust commercial strategies Ensures inventory and supply management to meet customer needs Maintains an excellent presentation of the store and ensures compliance with brand standards
