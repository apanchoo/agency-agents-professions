# Employee / Au pair

## Scope

The Au Pair Employee, an essential role to support families in the daily management of their household. Assists parents with childcare and educational activities Participates in light household chores and meal preparation Accompanies children on their school and extra-curricular trips Supports the learning of new languages ​​or skills according to the needs of the family Ensures the well-being and safety of children in the absence of parents Promotes cultural and social integration according to the family context

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Organize and lead fun and awareness activities
- Helping the child with homework
- Supporting the development, autonomy and awakening of the child
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Support hygiene, nutrition and development of children under 3 years old
- Adapt your practices to support children with disabilities
- Promote the child's personal development
- Observe the child's behavior and development and identify possible difficulties
- Carry out the bathing and care of the child
- Prepare and give a meal or drink to the child
- Maintain a clean and secure environment
- Ensure child safety at home and outside
- Build lasting professional relationships with customers and suppliers
- Communicate orally in a professional environment

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Child and adolescent psychology

## Standards and processes

- Home Safety Rules
- Knowledge of the stages of cognitive development
- Hygiene and cleanliness standards
- Hygiene and food safety rules

## Professional techniques

- Ability to plan educational excursions
- Meal preparation techniques for children
- Conflict resolution techniques
- Ability to manage daily routines
- Ability to monitor homework and school tasks
- Knowledge of positive education methods
- First aid techniques specific to children
- Relaxation and well-being techniques
- Ability to provide a safe and secure environment

## Working Conditions

- Adults
- Children
