# River Transport Operations Assistant

## Scope

The River Transport Operations Assistant contributes to the smooth running of river operations. Organizes and supervises loading and unloading operations of goods Plans routes taking into account river conditions and regulatory requirements Ensures coordination between the different actors of river transport (pilots, boatmen, port authorities, etc.) Controls the conformity of transport documents and safety procedures Optimizes transport costs and times according to the company's objectives May participate in the training of seagoing personnel and the improvement of operational procedures

## Related Job Titles

- River freight transport operations technician
- Passenger river transport operations technician

## Activities and Skills

- Manage human resources
- Provide staff training in first aid
- Create a transport file
- Check the conformity of documents with the standards in force
- Coordinate the circuit of information on the functioning of a structure
- Control the traceability of trips and determine corrective measures
- Organize passenger transport operations
- Organize the allocation of a river fleet according to operational requirements
- Organize the transport of goods
- Follow an international river transport service
- Edit a statistical activity monitoring report
- Carry out administrative management of personnel
- Optimize costs and completion times
- Ensuring the safety of people and property
- Analyze incidents to improve procedures
- Ensure communication with river authorities
- Liaise with port authorities when necessary
- Inform pilots of changes in conditions or routes
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Comply with regulatory requirements related to your area of expertise
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of software specific to river transport
- Navigation and geolocation systems (professional GPS, mobile apps)

## Areas of expertise

- Types of river boats
- Geography of rivers, canals and works on waterways
- Analysis of river transport costs

## Standards and processes

- Regulation of river navigation
- Regulation of the transport of dangerous materials and products
- Risk management in river transport
- Port regulations

## Professional techniques

- Strategic planning of river routes
- Emergency procedures in the event of a river accident
- Incident reporting procedures
