# Ironer in the textile and clothing industry

## Scope

The Ironer in the textile and clothing industry works and enhances the finishes of clothing. Processes and irons garments to remove wrinkles and improve final appearance Uses various industrial ironing machines to process different types of fabrics Controls the quality of garments after ironing to ensure they meet standards May specialize in ironing certain types of garments such as suits or dresses Maintains ironing equipment in good condition, performing regular checks and maintenance Collaborates with other textile industry professionals to optimize finishing processes
