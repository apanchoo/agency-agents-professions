# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Define targeted properties, requests and end of life.
2. Compare families of materials and processes.
3. Prepare characterization tests and material choice.
4. Check properties, transformation and recycling constraints.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Composite materials 1
- Composite materials 3
- Composite materials 2
- Mechanics and Materials 5
- Mechanics and Materials 2
- Mechanics and Materials 4
- Sample preparation
- Relationship structures/formulas and properties of formulated materials and/or products
- Durability of materials and/or formulated products

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Adaptation and optimization of a manufacturing process
- Industrial materials project
- Implement a maintenance action
- Prevent risks
- Risk analysis
- Management and optimization of a process

## Deliverables

- Material choice matrix
- Characterization plan
- Process and end of life file

## Acceptance Criteria

- The properties are attached to a nuance and conditions
- Aging assumptions are indicated
- The choice considers manufacturing and use

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
