# Engineer in materials engineering

## Scope

The Materials Engineering Engineer is involved in the research, development and optimization of materials used in industrial sectors. Uses advanced techniques to analyze the structure and properties of materials Improves existing materials to increase their durability, strength and efficiency Designs and tests new materials to meet specific needs Works with multidisciplinary teams and ensures material compliance with industry standards
