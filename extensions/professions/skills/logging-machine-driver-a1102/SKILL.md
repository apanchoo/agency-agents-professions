## Scope

Carries out mechanized silviculture and logging work. Operates advanced logging machines to carry out felling, limbing, cross-cutting and hauling work. Felling pre-identified trees while preserving the stand. Performs first level upkeep and maintenance of machines.

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Cut down a tree

- Mount forestry machine accessories
- Clearing a plantation and its access paths
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Driving an agricultural vehicle
- Process collected meteorological information
- Cut a tree according to its use
