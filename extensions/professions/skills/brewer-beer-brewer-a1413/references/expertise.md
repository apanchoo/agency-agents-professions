# Brewer / Beer brewer

## Scope

The beer brewer, artisan of flavor and tradition, designs and produces beers according to specific recipes and processes. Controls the quality of raw materials and finished products Manages stocks of raw materials and finished products Ensures first-level maintenance of brewery equipment Complies with hygiene and food safety standards Can develop new beer recipes Can lead a team and manage the company's activity

## Related Job Titles

- Brewer-maltster

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Advising clients on food and drink pairings
- Train new employees on brewing techniques
- Carry out chemical control tests during the fermentation process and issue recommendations (additions of ingredients)
- Follow trends and innovations in the beer industry
- Plan production based on demand
- Adjust the dosage of alcohol ingredients
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Mix raw materials
- Carry out and follow the steps of producing an alcohol
- Develop recipes
- Develop a blend or a vintage
- Managing the temperature of fermentation tanks
- Make a taste, smell or visual assessment
- Take, weigh and dose materials or products (ingredients, colorings, grains, etc.) on scales, weighing hoppers, etc.
- Respect brewing recipes
- Transforming raw materials of plant origin
- Act quickly in the event of a technical problem
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Clean and maintain equipment and machines used
- Organize or monitor manufacturing phases
- Analyze the quality of the finished beer
- Analyze the quality and conformity of raw materials
- Ensure regulatory compliance of production processes
- Ensure product traceability
- Control the quality of the conservation conditions of raw materials
- Check product storage conditions
- Ensure real-time inventory tracking
- Packaging products
- Define supply needs
- Carry out the placing in barrels or bottles, the capping, the labeling
- Organize order processing
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Order products and raw materials from different suppliers and farmers in your region
- Evaluate the environmental impact of production processes
- Managing production waste in an environmentally friendly way
- Marketing food products
- Develop marketing strategies to promote products and services
- Develop innovative sales techniques
- Promote hygiene and cleanliness in the workshop
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
