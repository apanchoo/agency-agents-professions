# Legal department manager

## Scope

The Legal Department Manager directs the company's legal strategy. Oversees compliance of company operations with applicable laws Advises management on legal aspects of projects and contracts Manages legal risks and prevents disputes Negotiates and drafts contracts and agreements Directs and trains members of its legal team Represents the company in legal proceedings if necessary

## Related Job Titles

- Head of legal department
- Legal Director
- Legal manager

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Develop training plans adapted to staff needs
- Provide legal training to teams
- Advise an organization, a structure
- Teaching, developing skills
- Define and implement security protocols
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Carry out internal legal audits
- Analyze the legal risks linked to decisions or activities
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Provide advice on dispute resolution procedures
- Advise management on strategic legal aspects
- Defend the interests of a company during financial and legal transactions
- Provide legal advice
- Manage relationships with lawyers and external counsel
- Implement compliance procedures
- Negotiate a contract
- Participate in the negotiation of complex contracts
- Supervise compliance of company practices with current legislation
- Supervise the drafting of internal legal documents
- Ensure compliance with the Data Protection Act, manage the list of personal data processing operations, interface with the CNIL
- Ensuring the legal security of a company
- Integrate sustainability and cooperation into contracts and legal documents
- Develop partnerships with legal institutions
- Optimize legal processes to gain efficiency
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Participate in multidisciplinary meetings
- Ensure legal and regulatory monitoring
