# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Qualify the facts, the parties and the question to be addressed.
2. Search for the texts and documents applicable to the date concerned.
3. Compare qualifications and options with their consequences.
4. Prepare the act or note and the necessary professional verifications.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Judicial organization
- Commercial law
- Contract law

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Audit and advice related to the practices and procedures of an organization or service
- Bringing an organization into compliance with its legal, economic and social environment

## Deliverables

- Timeline and Parts Inventory
- Sourced legal note
- Draft act or verification grid

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each rule is linked to a source and a date
- Uncertain facts remain reported
- The document distinguishes project, opinion and validated act

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
