# Workshop instructor in a protected workplace

## Scope

The Workshop Monitor in a protected workplace supervises people with disabilities in adapted professional activities, promoting their autonomy and social integration. Supervises and leads groups of people with disabilities in production activities Organizes and supervises daily activities to ensure the proper functioning of the workshop Adapts workstations according to the specific needs of the people supported Ensures monitoring of production and the quality of the products produced Participates in the training and professional integration of people with disabilities Ensures compliance with safety and hygiene standards within the workshop

## Related Job Titles

- Workshop instructor in a specialized establishment

- Encourage active participation of people
- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Organize the work of a team
- Promote the personal expression of individuals
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Distribute the workload and tasks to be performed among team members
- Take into account the skills of employees in the distribution of tasks
- Apply principles of equity in group management
- Foster an inclusive work environment
- Integrate an employee with a disability
- Supporting people in their professional integration journey
- Support the development and implementation of a career guidance project
- Supporting the development of a person’s autonomy
- Support people in carrying out their daily activities
- Provide technical support to the multidisciplinary team
- Advise on the adaptation of workstations for employees with disabilities or medical restrictions
- Develop professional integration strategies
- Teaching, developing skills
- Evaluate learning, training or experience acquired
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Maintain and facilitate the inclusion of supported people to avoid social isolation
- Organize training in risk prevention
- Participate in the design and monitoring of an individualized project
- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Ensure a presence and a safe environment for those accompanied
- Intervene with people with disabilities or loss of autonomy
- Observe the state of mental and physical health of the person accompanied
- Supporting those supported in managing their stress and anxiety
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Propose solutions to improve safety at work
- Prevent risks and the safety of the person accompanied
- Control the quality of a raw material, a resource
- Control the quality and conformity of a product
- Maintain a record of operations carried out
- Plan the necessary resources
- Ensure the safety of people in the workshop
- Communicate effectively with the team to ensure consistency of work
- Communicate instructions effectively
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
