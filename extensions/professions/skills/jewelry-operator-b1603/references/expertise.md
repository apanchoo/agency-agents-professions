# Jewelry operator

## Scope

According to industrial manufacturing processes, the operator takes pieces of jewelry. It carries out assembly operations and assembly operations. Checks the quality of his work Ensures the application of safety rules

## Related Job Titles

- Chain maker
- Goldsmith and jewelry operator

## Activities and Skills

- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Analyze a production file, a manufacturing sheet, a production range, an operating procedure
- Understanding the value chain and the interconnection of professions
- Equip the workstation with the tools, supplies, materials adapted and verified according to the activity to be carried out
- Identify the operations to be carried out
- Implement processes and technical operating methods
- Carry out assembly and assembly work
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Know how to rework cast iron on a simple part
- Know how to saw an injection rod or excess stamping
- Know how to grind simple parts
- Organize your space and workstation
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Detect defects, nuances, alloy of materials and determine the constraints of the part
- Carry out checks at the end of the project, ensuring compliance with the specifications
- Send information to your manager
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Alert, request support or arbitration
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Respect the rules and instructions for storing and cleaning your workstation
- Consult and use work documentation on a digital platform

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Products, tools and materials

- Use of piercing, cutting or pointed tools

## Areas of expertise

- Fundamentals of metallurgy
- Reading technical drawings

## Professional techniques

- Flame soldering techniques
- Laser welding techniques

## Working Conditions

- Fine motor activities (dexterity, etc.)
- In the workshop
- In line or production island
- Exposure to high temperatures
- Wearing professional attire or uniform
- Extended sitting position
- Repetitive work or imposed cadence
