# Trainer

## Scope

The Trainer designs and carries out the transmission of theoretical, practical and specialized professional knowledge, generally to employees or job seekers. Designs and leads training sessions adapted to the needs of learners Evaluates participants' prior learning and adjusts programs based on results Uses various educational tools to facilitate learning and maintain engagement Supports learners in putting their new skills into practice Regularly updates knowledge to ensure the relevance of the training provided Can specialize in various fields such as languages, first aid, or IT to meet specific needs
