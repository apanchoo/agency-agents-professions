# Bike mobility facilitator

## Scope

The Bicycle Mobility Facilitator promotes the use of the bicycle as a sustainable means of transport for everyone. Raise awareness among different audiences about the use of bicycles for daily travel Organize and lead introductory or educational sessions on bicycle mobility Learn about the highway code, safety rules and sharing the road Develop partnerships with schools, businesses and communities and participate in communication events to promote soft mobility

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Adapt the material or equipment according to the supervised audiences
- Provide supervision for the child or a group of children
- Plan the routes according to the abilities of the participants
- Present the exercise to practitioners and provide technical support
- Facilitate cycling learning workshops (balance, circulation, safety)
- Design training materials
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Being the contact person for external partners (families, professionals, administrations and local authorities)
- Implement communication actions
- Organize events to promote bicycle mobility (bicycle festival, challenges, discovery day or outing, etc.)
- Ensure the safety of participants during activities
- Share individual and collective safety rules in order to prevent incidents
- Evaluate learner progress
- Implement the material and logistical organization of an event
- Manage registrations for different activities
- Organize your schedule of activities and appointments
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Promote soft mobility and eco-responsible transport solutions
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Explain how to maintain and carry out simple repairs on a bicycle (punctures, chains, brakes)
- Identify the public's expectations, inform them about the entertainment activity(ies) and organizational arrangements
- Provide information on assistance systems for purchasing or repairing bicycles
- Develop and manage a network of partner organizations
- Supervise an audience
- Encourage the participation of each member of the group
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Adapt to your interlocutor
- Raise awareness among road users of road safety rules
- Respect road safety rules
- Monitor compliance with road safety rules
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of new cycle technologies
