## Scope

The Line Manager in the paper industry supervises and controls automated machines guaranteeing the quality of the paper produced. Provides first-level maintenance of production line equipment Optimizes machine settings to ensure production quality and quantity by making adjustments as necessary Coordinates with the team to ensure smooth and efficient production that meets customer demand Complies with current safety standards and environmental protocols
