# Pediatrician

## Scope

The pediatrician is a specialist dedicated to the health and well-being of children. Diagnoses and treats children's illnesses from birth to adolescence Monitors children's physical and psychological development Vaccinates and prevents common childhood illnesses Advises parents on nutrition, safety and appropriate behaviors Conducts regular exams to monitor children's growth and development

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Supporting parents in their educational and parental role
- Advising parents on child nutrition
- Teaching, developing skills
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Evaluate the child's psychomotor development
- Managing pediatric emergencies
- Inform, support a patient
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Monitoring a child's health
- Monitor reactions to medications and adjust treatments
- Update the patient's medical record
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Ensure the administrative management of an activity
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness
