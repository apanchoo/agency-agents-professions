# Methods and Validation

## Workflow

1. Define the objective of analysis, synthesis or formulation.
2. Identify samples, methods, conditions and risks.
3. Prepare the protocol, calculations and quality controls.
4. Interpret the results provided and document gaps and limitations.

## Technical Areas

Select techniques that fit the task and available resources.

- Chemical risks and GLP - GMP
- Sample preparation
- Experimental analyzes of formulated materials and/or products
- Chemical balances – Laboratory safety
- Atomic and molecular structures
- Culture and communication 4
- Simulation

## Validation Scenarios

Choose a scenario suited to the deliverable and specify inputs, constraints, expected results and checks.

- Verification of a given characteristic of a material or formulated product
- Study and development of a material and/or a formulated product
- Carry out chemical analyzes (acids-bases, complexation, precipitation) by applying good laboratory practices (GLP)
- Characterize and interpret sample analysis results according to the measurement chain used
- Use virtual and/or augmented reality to anticipate and correct problems in real situations
- Design of a safety room

## Deliverables

- Protocol and preparatory calculations
- Analytical control plan
- Interpretation report

## Acceptance Criteria

- Concentrations, units and material balances are consistent
- Blanks and references are provided
- No experimental results are invented

Retain assumptions, units, inputs and intermediate results needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish observed results from checks that still need to be performed.
