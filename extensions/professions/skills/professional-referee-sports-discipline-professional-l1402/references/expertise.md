# Professional referee / Sports discipline professional

## Scope

The professional referee ensures the application of the rules and the smooth running of sports competitions. Ensures compliance with the rules of the game and fairness between players Communicates effectively with players, coaches and other officials Makes rapid and impartial decisions with complete objectivity and neutrality, possibly relying on a technical assistant
