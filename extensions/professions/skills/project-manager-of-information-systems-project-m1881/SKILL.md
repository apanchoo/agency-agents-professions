## Scope

The Information Systems Project Manager (MOA) ensures the coordination and management of IT projects. Defines user needs and develops specifications Ensures the interface between end users and technical teams in achieving objectives Supervises the smooth running of the project and guarantees compliance with deadlines and budget Validates deliverables and ensures their compliance with initial requirements Organizes and leads project monitoring and coordination meetings Manages project risks and proposes solutions to mitigate them
