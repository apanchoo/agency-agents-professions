# Project Manager of Information Systems Project Management (MOA)

## Scope

The Information Systems Project Manager (MOA) ensures the coordination and management of IT projects. Defines user needs and develops specifications Ensures the interface between end users and technical teams in achieving objectives Supervises the smooth running of the project and guarantees compliance with deadlines and budget Validates deliverables and ensures their compliance with initial requirements Organizes and leads project monitoring and coordination meetings Manages project risks and proposes solutions to mitigate them

## Related Job Titles

- Head of project management of information systems
- Project Management Coordinator (MOA)

## Activities and Skills

- Allocate and organize project resources according to needs and constraints
- Lead, coordinate a team
- Coordinate development or production teams to meet deadlines
- Manage communications between project stakeholders
- Implement the transformation actions necessary for changes
- Write engineering documents (writing functional specifications and technical deliverables for solutions)
- Write specifications and technical specifications
- Analyze user needs for system design
- Analyze data to make strategic decisions
- Participate in the implementation of an Integrated Management Software (PGI)
- Use project management software
- Optimize development and maintenance processes to reduce costs
- Lead risk management
- Ensure project compliance with legal requirements
- Participate in defining the schedule, overall budget and deadlines
- Write a report, an activity report
- Negotiate with IT and security solution providers
- Negotiate with suppliers for technical aspects of the project
- Present developments in the information system to the sponsor
- Design and manage a project
- Define the information systems strategy
- Evaluate the impact of new systems on current operations
- Optimize project management processes
- Monitor the progress of projects and adjust plans if necessary
- Communicate with internal and external contacts
- Organize coordination meetings with clients and teams
- Prepare and lead a meeting, a working group, a workshop
- Structuring, synthesizing information

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit

- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
