# Building works manager

## Scope

The Building Works Manager supervises one or more construction or renovation sites, coordinating the organization, quality and safety until the work is completed. Defines and organizes the means of production (teams, equipment, materials) Plans and supervises the different stages of the construction site Coordinates the work of the teams and subcontractors Ensures compliance with safety standards and regulations in force Optimizes the costs and deadlines for completing the work Manage one or more teams and manages communication between the different stakeholders in the project Controls the quality of the work carried out

## Activities and Skills

- Ensure the financial management of construction sites (optimization of the execution margin of construction sites)
- Coordinate the intervention of multidisciplinary teams
- Supervise the teams under his responsibility (works supervisors, site managers, supervisors, workers, etc.)
- Encourage innovation and adoption of new technologies
- Carry out building renovation projects
- Adapt the implementation and supervision of construction sites according to materials, energy systems and construction methods
- Attend the reception of the work and ensure the lifting of any reservations
- Calculate, analyze and justify the results of the site
- Monitor construction sites and ensure the progress of the various lots (structural work, technical trades, etc.)
- Establish the forecast schedule, the budget and compare the reality of the site to the market file
- Organize meetings and site visits
- Organize workstations and define processes on the site in accordance with the safety, quality and budget standards provided for in the specifications
- Plan site operations
- Reassess the technical study, the forecast budget and develop the detailed budget for the site (volume of materials, necessary human and material resources, etc.)
- Define and implement security protocols
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Supervise execution and detail plans from a 3D digital model
- Ensure the proper application of safety and hygiene procedures in conjunction with the SPS coordinator (safety, health protection)
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Control the production and quality of the as-built plans (DOE: documents of the works executed; DIUO: documents of subsequent interventions on the work, etc.) and participate in their updating
- Detect additional intervention needs with the project owner
- Ensure compliance of the work and site with quality standards and participate in the quality audit phase
- Organize the storage of materials, structural elements and prefabricated elements
- Order materials and materials
- Prepare site reports with the works manager or the client
- Prepare a site report
- Organize and plan an activity
- Ensure the interface with the various stakeholders in the work project (client, public administration, design office, architectural firms, control bodies, etc.)
- Control the execution and costs of a service
- Evaluate the services offered and participate in the choice of suppliers specialized in each area
- Receive and study the elements of the technical file (architect's plans, study report, forecast budget)
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Monitor the budget for the construction site
- Use information from the digital model to track expenses and ensure data consistency
- Follow a contract, check compliance with contractual clauses
- Ensure legal compliance of the site
- Quantify, analyze and ensure the traceability of material flows, materials and equipment for deconstruction, total or partial
- Assist the works manager in the response phases to calls for tenders (construction, rehabilitation, development)
- Coordinate responses to calls for tenders
- Maintain commercial relationships with public or private clients with a view to identifying future opportunities
- Estimate the cost of work and construction sites
- Negotiate with suppliers for the purchase of materials and services
- Propose continuous improvements to BIM practices based on feedback
- Prepare and present application files for calls for tenders
- Set objectives for site managers: team formation, progress points, performance indicators
- Lead work monitoring meetings
- Communicate with internal and external contacts
- Present a site file to the site manager and stakeholders
- Identify technical building documents
