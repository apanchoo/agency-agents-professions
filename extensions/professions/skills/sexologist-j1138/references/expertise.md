# sexologist

## Scope

The sexologist is a health professional specializing in disorders and questions related to sexuality. Assesses and treats sexual dysfunction in individuals or couples Provides counseling and tailored therapies to improve sexual health Conducts sex education sessions for different age groups Collaborates with other health professionals to provide comprehensive support May speak at conferences or specialist publications to raise public awareness

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Facilitate open discussions about sexuality
- Support a couple or family in formalizing the marital or family agreement project
- Develop appropriate sex education programs
- Promoting sex education in diverse contexts
- Adapt treatments according to patient progress
- Providing support and advice to patients
- Advise on safe sex practices
- Educate patients on managing their health
- Assess clients’ emotional and sexual needs
- Managing stressful situations among patients
- Inform patients about gynecological disease prevention, safe sexual practices, and healthy lifestyle habits
- Suggest appropriate treatment
- Carry out a diagnosis or paramedical assessment
- Manage calendars and schedule appointments
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Greet, orient and provide information to a patient
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Create a relationship of trust with patients
- Organize collective information on the theme of sexuality
- Raise public awareness
- Working in a group, in a network
- Manage the confidentiality of consultations
- Manage patient records
- Respect ethical and confidentiality standards
- Processing sensitive information
