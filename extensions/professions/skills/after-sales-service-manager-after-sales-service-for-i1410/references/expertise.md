# After-sales service manager - after-sales service - for household appliances

## Scope

The After-Sales Service Manager for household appliances manages the service dealing with post-sales interventions in the repair, maintenance and monitoring of household appliances. Manages customer complaints and after-sales service requests Organizes and supervises field technician interventions Ensures ongoing training of technical teams to ensure quality service Optimizes service processes to improve operational efficiency Maintains clear and effective communication with customers to ensure customer satisfaction Analyzes service data to identify trends and improve services
