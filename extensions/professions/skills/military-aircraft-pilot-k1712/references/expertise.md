# Military aircraft pilot

## Scope

The military aircraft pilot is assigned to combat, troop or equipment transport, reconnaissance, in-flight refueling, or rescue missions. Is able to make quick and accurate decisions under pressure. Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out his profession

## Related Job Titles

- High-level expert navigator officer weapons systems transport
- High-level transport pilot expert
- High-level expert in-flight refueling pilot
- Specialist navigator officer weapons systems transport
- Airplane pilot specialist
- Transport pilot specialist
- In-flight refueling pilot specialist

## Activities and Skills

- Working with civil and military authorities
- Maintain staffing equipment
- Anticipate a danger or constraint
- Control the aircraft
- Managing stress and reactions in combat situations
- Implement security measures
- Ensure in-flight refueling
- Perform combat air missions
- Perform the paratrooper drop
- Carry out a mission in instrument flight mode
- Manage on-board equipment and systems constituting a cockpit
- Master aeronautical phraseology
- Implement personnel or equipment release procedures
- Implement air navigation procedures
- Implement reactions to external threats on board an aircraft
- Pilot or participate in the operation of the aircraft, particularly in a hostile environment
- Pilot an aircraft and use onboard subsystems
- Prepare the ground mission
- Prepare for an air mission
- Carry out transport, supply, surveillance, rescue, intelligence or attack missions
- Transport troops in external operations
- Communicate effectively with other members of the unit
- Communicate by radio means in combat situations
- Being projected into a military operation on national territory and in a conflict zone
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Make quick decisions under pressure
- Acquire and develop sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out your profession
- Be trained in the dropping of personnel and equipment

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate self-control
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Areas of expertise

- Maintaining equipment in condition
- Foreign language practice
- Interallied or NATO structures and procedures
- Topography

## Standards and processes

- Airland Doctrines and Procedures
- Emergency procedures on board an aircraft
- Respect for the laws of war

## Professional techniques

- Survival techniques in hostile environments
- Air navigation techniques
- Communication in combat situations
- Use of night vision devices
- Ability to work as a team in stressful situations

## Working Conditions

- Military
- Working with international organizations or partners
- International zone
- National zone
- At altitude
- In a noisy environment
- In cramped or confined environments
- Carrying weapons and ammunition
- Wearing professional attire or uniform
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Extended sitting position
- Working in hostile and dangerous environments
- Annual schedules
- Can be mobilized in an emergency
- Military organization

## Sectors

- Public service, defense and security
