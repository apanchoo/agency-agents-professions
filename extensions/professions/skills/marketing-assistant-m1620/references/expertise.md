# Marketing assistant

## Scope

The marketing assistant supports the marketing manager or team in the implementation of a company's commercial and communication strategy. Analyzes the market and competition in order to identify opportunities and trends Participates in the implementation of communication campaigns (digital and traditional) Coordinates the creation of marketing materials (brochures, newsletters, web content, social networks) Monitors and evaluates the performance of marketing actions using key indicators Manages the customer database and updates monitoring tools (CRM, dashboards) Assists the sales team in the preparation of promotional operations and events
