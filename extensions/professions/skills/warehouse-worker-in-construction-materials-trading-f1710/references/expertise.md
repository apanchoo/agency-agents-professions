# Warehouse worker in construction materials trading

## Scope

The Building Materials Warehouse handles the products from entry to exit from the agency's stocks, while welcoming the customer at the point of sale. Receives, moves, loads, unloads, arranges and stores construction materials Controls the proper functioning of the machines used Prepares orders and shipments of materials Welcomes and advises customers Can ensure the sale of materials and manage the organization of transport

## Related Job Titles

- Warehousekeeper / Depot warehouse worker in the construction industry

## Activities and Skills

- Design packaging and product packaging
- Use inventory management software
- Optimize storage space
- Ensure first level maintenance of storage equipment
- Ensure the cleanliness of the work area
- Organize and optimize the physical storage of products and materials within its scope of activity
- Ensuring cargo safety during transportation
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Check product storage conditions
- Load, unload, handle products
- Take an inventory of products or materials available in reserve
- Define a storage area
- Identify product unavailability or stock-out risks and report them
- Handle handling or construction equipment
- Plan stock rotations
- Use protective equipment to handle heavy loads
- Check the conformity of deliveries
- Assist delivery drivers or customers when loading and unloading vehicles
- Prepare orders and shipments of construction materials
- Evacuate and sort waste and construction materials
- Propose solutions to minimize waste
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Analyze customer needs
- Arguing about the choice of materials with customers
- Propose solutions adapted to specific customer needs
- Communicate with internal and external contacts
- Communicate orally in a professional environment
- Network and cooperate with diverse professional profiles
- Adapt and optimize your practice to the context and professional risks (gestures, postures, ergonomics)
- Follow safety procedures when handling loads
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Use digital tools

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Show responsiveness
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
