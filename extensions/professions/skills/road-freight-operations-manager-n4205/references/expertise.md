# Road freight operations manager

## Scope

The operations manager supervises the activity of a team of operators and drivers. Optimizes and researches the resources and means at its disposal Ensures the monitoring of performance within the framework of the cost, deadlines, quality and safety objectives set by the company and in compliance with the regulations in force Participates in the development of responses to calls for tenders in support of salespeople Guarantees the maintenance of a social climate of quality and team development Guarantees the fulfillment of service commitments to customers and contribute to their satisfaction

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Ensure the training and integration of new entrants (operating agents and drivers)
- Coordinate actions to be taken in an emergency situation
- Supervise a team of operations agents and drivers
- Regularly evaluate the technical skills of the team
- Maintain team cohesion and motivation
- Prevent and support teams in managing conflict situations
- Contributes to the recruitment process
- Adapt your communication according to the interlocutors and the media used
- Use digital tools (communication, applications, connected objects)
- Apply processing to data to analyze and interpret it (with a spreadsheet, processing software, a calculation query in a database, etc.)
- Check data compliance
- Update Operations Management Tools (TMS) variable settings
- Optimize dematerialized transport documentation processes
- Control the implementation of operating procedures and operating rules in force within the operation
- Organize logistics activities taking into account available resources, budget and regulations
- Design a transportation plan
- Effectively manage own and subcontracted resources to minimize costs and maximize efficiency
- Optimize transport and customs costs
- Manage and optimize the operating budget
- Analyze budget and cost variances and propose adjustments
- Develop, monitor and manage a budget
- Establish a forecast cost for your scope
- Present a budget
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Ensure compliance of services with regulations
- Supervise the management of mandatory training and driver authorizations
- Master the regulations in force related to your sector of activity
- Establish and enforce a framework integrating regulatory and environmental developments
- Propose adaptations of services to improve the quality of service
- Analyze and formalize responses to calls for tenders for the operation activity
- Analyze customer feedback to improve products or services
- Calculate the margins, profitability of a product or activity
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Raise customer needs internally to adapt the offer to emerging issues
- Negotiate with service providers and subcontractors
- Participate in continuous process improvement
- Prepare or establish commercial proposals, write price offers, develop quotes
- Check the technical, regulatory and financial feasibility of commercial proposals
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Negotiate with external service providers
- Clearly communicate decisions, arbitrations and instructions
- Implement a monitoring system (sources and elements of price monitoring, standards, environment, etc.)
