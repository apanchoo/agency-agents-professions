# Driller

## Scope

The driller, an essential professional in the exploration and exploitation of underground natural resources. Carries out drilling operations to extract resources such as water, oil or minerals Controls and maintains drilling equipment to ensure a safe and efficient operation Analyzes soil or rock samples to determine the composition and structure of the terrain Collaborates with geologists and other specialists to plan drilling operations Follows safety and environmental standards to minimize impact on the environment
