# Road transport driver of dangerous goods

## Scope

The Road Transport Driver of dangerous goods safely drives a heavy group vehicle requiring category C, C1 or CE, C1E licenses, autonomously and optimized in the commercial context of the company, to transport goods classified as dangerous nationally or internationally from a collection point to a delivery point. Participates in the protection of the environment, by adopting the principles of eco-driving Respects deadlines, the regulations in force particularly in terms of driving and rest times, road regulations, on safety, on the transport of dangerous goods and company procedures Prepares and organizes its mission Checks the operating state of the vehicle and its equipment, the presence and validity of mandatory documents and reports any anomalies. Loads and unloads goods or supervises handling operations while ensuring the prevention of risks to people, property and the environment Completes or arranges for transport documents to be completed Maintains regular communication with the operations department to, among other things, report any incident Represents the company's brand image in its relations with shippers and recipients
