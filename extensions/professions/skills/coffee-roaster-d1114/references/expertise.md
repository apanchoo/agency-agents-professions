# Coffee roaster

## Scope

The roaster, artisan of taste, reveals the aromas of the coffee. Selects and purchases green coffee beans according to quality criteria Controls the roasting process to ensure the flavor of the final product Cools, packages and stores roasted beans to preserve their quality Advises customers on coffee selection and preparation methods May manage the sale and promotion of coffee products

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Innovate in roasting techniques
- Create and adjust roast profiles for different types of coffee
- Develop unique coffee blends
- Promoting sustainable roasting practices
- Quickly cool roasted beans to stop the roasting process
- Adjust roasting machines
- Ensure first level maintenance of equipment
- Choose beans based on their origin, variety and quality to ensure superior coffee quality
- Examine and test coffee beans before and after roasting
- Monitor the roasting process
- Define supply needs
- Packing roasted coffee
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Identify and select suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Inform customers and partners about different types of coffee and roasting methods
- Analyze coffee market trends
- Negotiate commercial terms
- Sell roasted coffee to consumers, cafes, grocery stores or online
- Optimize production costs
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
