# Specialist in the protection of State forces, installations or sensitive equipment

## Scope

The specialist in the protection of State forces, installations or sensitive materials ensures the security of sites, premises, equipment, techniques, people and authorization of access to sites or installations only to authorized or authorized persons. Gives the alert in the event of an abnormal event Identifies, analyzes and evaluates potential risks for forces, installations or sensitive equipment. Develops sufficient physical and mental condition to carry out his profession

- Maritime defense and interdiction expert
