# Phonographic publishing project manager

## Scope

The phonographic publishing project manager is a professional in the music publishing sector responsible for managing the production and promotion of sound projects (albums, singles, compilations, reissues, catalogs) from their conception to their distribution. Pilots the marketing, communication and commercial strategy of a musical project from the post-production phase until its distribution on the market Is responsible for the promotion of the artistic project, the management of the artist's image, and the overall coherence of the promotion and distribution actions, while respecting the allocated budget Defines and implements the marketing strategy of a project in consultation with the artist, the artistic direction and the management of the label Pilots the commercial launch actions (release date, formats, partners, etc.) and coordinates all internal and external stakeholders (promotion, distribution, media relations, artistic direction) Supervises the production of promotional content (clips, visuals, teasers) and monitors performance indicators (listenings, sales), and adjusts the strategy if necessary Represents the project and advises the artist on the consistency of its image and the positioning of its releases Manages the marketing and promotional budget, ensuring the balance between artistic ambitions and economic constraints and can contribute to the valorization of the catalog

## Activities and Skills

- Supervise artistic and technical production

- Advise an artist on his image
- Understand the uses, limits and legal issues of AI/AGI in the processing and generation of content

- Manage and stick to a budget
- Adapt launch strategies according to broadcast formats and promotional channels (radio, television, press, platforms)

- Design, implement and monitor multi-channel marketing actions
- Pitch a musical project to the media
- Carry out market intelligence, competitive intelligence
- Write marketing content (arguments, newsletters, press releases, product sheets)
- Develop a business development strategy
- Manage a project and coordinate the stakeholders necessary for the production of a work
- Coordinate the different actors (artists, producers, studios, distributors)
- Present and defend a project to internal and external partners
