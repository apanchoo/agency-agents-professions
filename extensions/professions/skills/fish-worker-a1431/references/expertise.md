# Fish worker

## Scope

The fish farm worker participates in the breeding of seawater or freshwater fish while respecting the environment and the well-being of the fish. Monitors and maintains ponds and aquaculture equipment Feeds fish and monitors their health Monitors stocks and prepares orders May participate in the marketing of products resulting from production

## Related Job Titles

- Fish farming agent
- Fish farming help

## Activities and Skills

- Inform the public about sustainable fish farming techniques
- Provide preventive or curative care to animals
- Determine food rations according to the physical and physiological needs of the animals
- Prepare and distribute food rations to animals
- Respect animal welfare
- Monitor fish growth and health
- Take a sample
- Analyze fish growth data
- Prepare materials for work
- Harvest the product of breeding
- Adjust the level of a body of water
- Immerse a pool or pond
- Optimize aquatic life space
- Prepare ponds for fish breeding
- Install rainwater harvesting systems
- Perform first level maintenance of tools and equipment
- Clean ponds and fish farming equipment
- Solve technical problems related to pools
- Ensuring safety at work
- Control water quality
- Carry out fish packaging operations: washing, sorting, packaging, labeling
- Manage stocks of food and other supplies
- Prepare orders following procedures
- Managing natural resources sustainably
- Marketing harvested or fished products
- Adapt working methods to the environment
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
