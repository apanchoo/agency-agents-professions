# Construction equipment and public works mechanic

## Scope

The construction equipment and public works mechanic maintains and repairs the equipment and machines used on construction sites: hydraulic excavators, dozers, graders, loaders, compactors, finishers, etc. Assembles and dismantles all machines thanks to its perfect knowledge of their operation Carry out preventive maintenance interventions in the workshop Carry out a fault diagnosis and carry out curative maintenance operations on site

## Related Job Titles

- Workshop and site mechanic in Building and Public Works equipment (BTP)
- Mechanic / Site mechanic
- Mechanic for servicing and maintenance of construction and public works machinery
- Repair mechanic / Repair mechanic of construction machinery
- Repair mechanic / Repair mechanic of construction equipment
- Construction maintenance technician
- Construction equipment maintenance technician
- Public works site equipment maintenance technician

## Activities and Skills

- Carry out the necessary recording actions and arrange your workstation to operate safely
- Welcome new arrivals to the site and support them in their progress
- Find ideas (gestures, postures, equipment, etc.) to improve safety and working conditions or optimize the operation of the machine
- Read and use manufacturers' operating instructions and instructions using digital tools
- Keep the maintenance logbook up to date by completing daily intervention sheets and parts tracking sheets (to feed stock management)
- Analyze information relating to the progress of the intervention taking into account execution plans and installation diagrams
- Organize the operations to be carried out in order to respect deadlines and site constraints
- Check the proper functioning of equipment and repaired machines before making or making them available in order to guarantee the quality of the intervention carried out
- Listen to and question drivers and machine operators in order to identify perceived anomalies
- Make adjustments to machines and equipment with the aim of energy and environmental performance of the equipment
- Use data from machine sensors to detect possible malfunctions
- Interpret the information communicated by the diagnostic tools using the digital tools available
- Materialize the handling area to work safely while respecting the instructions during work on site
- Identify the cause of the breakdown or malfunction and identify the technical interventions to be carried out, the parts and elements to be replaced
- Carry out tests and measurements to locate malfunctions
- Carry out cleaning and adjustment operations on materials and equipment
- Carry out on-site repairs or towing a machine (construction machine, agricultural machine, etc.)
- Repair faulty mechanical components
- Monitor the condition of equipment and report any malfunctions
- Act to protect yourself or others from imminent danger and warn your superiors
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Know how to explain a breakdown and get remote assistance (by an expert or the manufacturer)
- Control repairs
- Order the supplies necessary for machine maintenance, in discussion with your manager, to guarantee the start of maintenance operations within the planned deadlines
- Manage spare parts stocks
- Identify dysfunctions and propose improvements
- Adapt your activity to hazards while taking into account quality and deadline objectives
- Reformulate instructions and rules (safety, procedures, etc.)
- Respect safety standards during interventions
- Master basic digital skills (writing, reading an email, using computer equipment, etc.)

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
