# Remote monitoring operator

## Scope

The remote surveillance operator ensures the security of property and people remotely. Monitors and controls electronic and video security systems Responds to alarms to take necessary actions according to established procedures Writes incident reports to document anomalies and interventions Communicates with law enforcement and emergency services when necessary Handles incoming telephone calls for emergencies and information

## Related Job Titles

- Video surveillance security agent
- Remote monitoring agent
- Video protection operator
- Central monitoring station technician
- Video surveillance technician for games

## Activities and Skills

- Identify anomalies, incidents and inform law enforcement and customers
- Carry out remote monitoring
- Apply safety procedures in case of emergency
- Trigger emergency measures in the event of an incident
- Detect and locate any individual or behavior presenting a threat to security or safety
- Detect suspicious behavior
- Identify risky behaviors or situations
- Securing people, places and property with a video protection system
- Secure equipment, a perimeter
- Document security incidents for audit reports
- Prepare an accident and incident report
- Complete the control and report materials
- Protect people and property
- Act quickly in the event of an accident or unforeseen event
- Explain and enforce rules and procedures
- Manage crisis or emergency situations
- Manage alarms and responses to security incidents
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Managing a conflict situation
- Respect rules, instructions, standards and operational procedures

## Professional Approach

- Demonstrate autonomy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Show responsiveness

## Standards and processes

- Management of access control systems
- Actions and techniques for prevention, safety and first aid

## Professional techniques

- Secure communication protocols
- Management of monitoring interfaces
- Rapid response security techniques
- Use of surveillance equipment
- Behavioral analysis via video surveillance
- Use of alarm systems
- Digital video archiving techniques
- Knowledge of motion detection systems
- Interpretation of alarm signals
- Monitoring data management
