# Doctor responsible for Documentation and Medical Information (Doctor responsible DIM)

## Scope

The Doctor responsible for documentation and medical information plays a crucial role in the management of health data. Collects and analyzes patient information to ensure accurate and complete documentation Ensures the confidentiality and security of medical data Participates in the improvement of hospital information systems to optimize data processing Collaborates with medical teams to provide detailed reports and statistics Contributes to medical research by providing reliable data for clinical studies Trains hospital staff in the use of medical information systems

## Related Job Titles

- Physician Director / Physician Director of Medical Information

## Activities and Skills

- Train and raise awareness among medical staff on good medical documentation practices
- Provide support and guidance on medical information management systems used by the facility
- Organize and manage scientific and medical communication with health professionals, patients and internal services
- Analyze health data
- Check the quality of recorded data
- Process data
- Complete medical-administrative documents
- Manage medical databases
- Ensure that clinical documentation is complete, accurate and complies with current standards and regulations
- Oversee the recording, management and preservation of patient medical records
- Ensure the accuracy and relevance of information recorded in medical records
- Analyze medical data to identify trends, gaps and opportunities for improvement
- Define the documentation policy based on the analysis of the internal needs of the teams
- Relay information
- Ensuring the confidentiality of medical data
- Ensure the confidentiality of exchanges and information
- Carry out documentary monitoring
