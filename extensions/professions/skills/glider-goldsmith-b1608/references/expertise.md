# Glider goldsmith

## Scope

The glider goldsmith ensures the creation of pieces of goldsmithing using the techniques of shrinking and flat working with a hammer. Assembles simple or complex shapes using a methodology adapted to all parts Has technical mastery for the deformation of materials Produces the tools necessary for the transformation of metals

## Related Job Titles

- Planer / Planer by hand in goldsmithing

## Activities and Skills

- Create drawings or plans of the parts and tools to be produced
- Be a force for suggestions on the design of shapes
- Analyze the product manufacturing sheet to understand the requirements of turned parts
- Record the start and end of a step on a digital production order
- Use a graphical interface (tablet, smartphone, numerical control machine, etc.)
- Analyze, prepare and organize the work of creating a piece of goldsmithing by planing based on the instructions (drawing or technical sheet)
- Shaping missing or defective elements
- Carry out part shaping operations
- Create specific pieces of goldsmithing with given instructions or independently
- Carry out the finishing of a product, an article, a work
- Carry out precision work requiring a sense of detail
- Select accessories, products, tools
- Organize your space and workstation
- Ensure first-level maintenance of your work tools
- Implement preventive management of professional risks
- Detect defects, nuances, alloy of materials and determine the constraints of the part
- Carry out checks at the end of the project, ensuring compliance with the specifications
- Promote understanding and collaboration
- Report your action, risks or anomalies to the manager
- Alert colleagues if there are defects so that the part can be reworked for adjustment or polishing
- Know and respect current safety protocols
- Identify the characteristics of the product in order to mobilize the best possible preparation or assembly techniques
- Respect workshop safety standards
- Mobilize your concentration and motivation
