# Handler

## Scope

The handler ensures the handling and movement of goods efficiently and carefully. Loads and unloads goods in storage areas and means of transport Sorts and classifies products according to warehouse or customer specifications Moves or stores goods, possibly using suitable equipment (example: manual or electrically assisted pallet jack) Follows safety standards and operational procedures to prevent accidents and damage to goods May participate in stock inventory and report any discrepancies May carry out conditioning and packaging tasks for products before shipment

## Activities and Skills

- Apply security protocols
- Identify the risks of breakdowns, incidents or accidents
- Practice safe handling gestures and postures
- Ensure that your equipment is in good working order
- Check product storage conditions
- Visually check the quality of packaging
- Ensure the nature of the products handled
- Apply safe lifting and handling techniques
- Load, unload, handle products
- Moving bulky, heavy and large products
- Move and store products and materials according to quality and safety standards
- Participate in an inventory
- Store products or goods according to their expiry dates and storage conditions
- Sort and distribute packages and goods according to instructions (coding, format, weight, number, etc.)
- Use handling equipment safely
- Contribute to order processing and shipment preparation
- Complete the goods traceability monitoring tools
- Process parcel tracking logistics information
- Sort defective products and direct them to the relevant areas or services (repair, recycling, evacuation, etc.)
- Communicate effectively with drivers
- Inform your hierarchy of reception anomalies during deliveries
- Ensure the transmission of information to superiors as quickly as possible
- Integrate into the team and show cooperation
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Report incidents and anomalies encountered
- Comply with current safety standards and regulations
- Follow safety procedures when loading and unloading
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
