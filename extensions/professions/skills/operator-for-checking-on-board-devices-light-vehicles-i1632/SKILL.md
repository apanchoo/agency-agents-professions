## Scope

The light vehicle on-board device verification operator carries out periodic inspections and checks of immobilizer breathalyzers and taximeters. Identifies non-conformities and anomalies Returns these devices to conformity Can replace defective elements of the metrological chain Certifies their conformity All of these activities are carried out in accordance with the regulations in force, in particular those relating to safety, and within the framework of the predefined procedures of a quality system

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.

## Skills to Apply

- Carry out the regulatory operations to receive the Anti-Start Breathalyzer (EAD)
- Carry out tests of taximeter equipment to verify compliance
- Check the regulatory operation of the taximeter equipment
- Complete regulatory documents related to compliance control and intervention traceability
- Check the regulatory operation of the immobilizer breathalyzer (EAD)
- Carry out the administrative procedures for receiving the immobilizer breathalyzer (EAD)
- Identify and report anomalies, non-conformities or defective elements of the EAD (judicial)
- Identify and report anomalies, non-conformities or defective elements of taximeter equipment
