# Wellness area manager

## Scope

A wellness area manager manages the services and equipment dedicated to well-being in the establishment (spa, balneotherapy, massage, beauty salons, hairdressing, etc.). Welcomes and advises customers on the different treatments and products available Carry out body and facial treatments adapted to customer needs Ensures the preparation and cleanliness of treatment rooms Ensures compliance with hygiene and safety standards Contributes to customer loyalty and the promotion of Spa offers Can supervise a team, train new employees and manage treatment schedules as a Spa Manager

## Related Job Titles

- Spa Manager
- Spa Practitioner

## Activities and Skills

- Enforce Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE) rules
- Prevent and resolve conflicts
- Ensure continuing training of staff on new care techniques
- Teaching, developing skills
- Master different massage techniques to meet the varied needs of clients
- Practice draining, relaxing, firming and stimulating manual techniques
- Providing body and beauty care
- Use customer relationship management (CRM) software
- Use inventory management software
- Optimize the layout of the care space for client comfort
- Check the cleanliness of a place
- Ensure the conformity of machines and treatments for UV, spa, sauna, hammam sessions
- Check compliance of the site, installations and equipment with regulatory standards (ERP, hygiene, fire safety, PMR accessibility)
- Monitor the operating parameters of Spa equipment
- Control the quality of service and customer reception
- Ensure order tracking and inventory management
- Define supply needs
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Manage your department's budget (revenue, expenses, turnover, etc.)
- Ensure monitoring of accounting, invoicing and pay slips (alone or in conjunction with external consultants)
- Manage complaints and disputes
- Greet customers and identify their specific needs
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Calculate the margins, profitability of a product or activity
- Design aesthetic care services adapted to current safety standards and rules
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop a range of services or merchandising adapted to customers
- Present and promote a product or service
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Ensuring the confidentiality of customer information
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
