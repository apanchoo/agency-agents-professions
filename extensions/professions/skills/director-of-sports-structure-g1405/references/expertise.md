# Director of sports structure

## Scope

Defines, in conjunction with the shareholders or the management team, the extra-sporting project of the structure, is the guarantor of the general good functioning of the structure on the extra-sporting level Is in supervision of all extra-sporting activities of the activities, while relying on specialized internal and external resources (development of the sports sector, human resources, legal, accounting, finance, public relations) Can carry out, mainly in associative clubs, complementary activities of communication, events, management of public relations, or even activities to supervise physical and sporting practice in complementarity with sports educators
