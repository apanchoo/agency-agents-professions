# Technical-sales / Technical-sales for the nautical industry and services

## Scope

The technical salesperson in the nautical industry and services works for a shipchandler or on a boatyard to support a customer in choosing the options to install on the boat. Advises and sells boats and nautical equipment adapted to customer needs Ensures technical and commercial monitoring of the products sold Develops and maintains lasting relationships with a clientele of individuals and professionals Ensures inventory management of nautical equipment (fittings, ropes, etc.) Analyzes the market to anticipate trends and adjust the product offering Can manage the collection of sales (quotes, invoices)
