# Mobile network maintenance technician

## Scope

The Mobile Network Maintenance Technician ensures preventive and corrective maintenance operations on mobile networks (4G - 5G) Diagnoses and resolves faults on telecommunications sites and equipment Performs software and hardware updates to optimize network performance Carries out tests and measurements to guarantee the quality of the signal and network coverage

## Related Job Titles

- Mobile network maintenance manager
- Telecommunications maintenance technician
- Telecommunications network maintenance technician
- Telecom operations technician
- Network and telecommunications intervention technician

## Activities and Skills

- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Train users in new technologies
- Organize a technical assistance service
- Analyze a signal
- Configure or configure telecommunications equipment, low currents
- Read and use a plan, a diagram
- Optimize network coverage and system capacity
- Complete the intervention documents
- Update and upgrade telecommunications or low current equipment
- Establish recommendations and recommendations for use, commissioning, dismantling or maintenance
- Take measurements for the creation of the optical equipment and establish the work execution sheet
- Prepare and use the equipment, materials and tools provided for the task
- Analyze the instructions while appreciating the difficulties of the site
- Assemble the elements of the electrical or electronic product (components, wiring)
- Connect electronic equipment
- Connect a junction box
- Wiring equipment
- Install the equipment on site and connect it to external networks
- Connecting low voltage elements
- Detect installation malfunctions, determine the causes of breakdowns and establish maintenance intervention requests
- Perform software update
- Find the origin of breakdowns and identify repair solutions
- Carry out maintenance operations of the technical environment (electrical cabinet, locksmith, green spaces, etc.)
- Carry out a breakdown or installation malfunction diagnosis
- Check safety equipment or materials (PPE, harness, etc.)
- Identify and assess potential risks linked to the environment (variation of climatic conditions)
- Respect safety rules including wearing PPE and appropriate clothing
- Identify the characteristics of a site
- Determine solutions and technical recommendations and communicate them to customers
- Adapt working methods to the level of risks in electrical environments
- Adapt working methods to the level of risks of working at height
- Continuously develop your skills, particularly in emerging technologies
