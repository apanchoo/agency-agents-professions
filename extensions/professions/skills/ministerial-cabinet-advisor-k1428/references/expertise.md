# Ministerial Cabinet Advisor

## Scope

The ministerial cabinet advisor is the right arm of a minister, on a specific thematic scope; it constitutes the link between the minister and the stakeholders in the file: central administration, other cabinets, Parliament and interest representatives.

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Advise political power on methods of action in crisis management
- Analyze legal documents to prepare cases
- Analyze text projects
- Write framework notes
- Ensure follow-up of current affairs and files
- Develop decision support systems
- Interpret legal texts
- Prepare draft laws or regulations for the Government and develop presentation documents for Parliament
- Assist a Minister in the orientation and development of public policy and in the conduct of the affairs of the Ministry
- Establish technical arguments to arbitrate and make strategic choices consistent with the political orientations of elected officials (management methods, missions, projects, resources, action plans)
- Evaluate the economic impact of new policies
- Prepare files on complex themes
- Examine the questions submitted to the minister
- Implement public policies
- Anticipate political issues and the impacts of public decisions
- Ensure the interface with external stakeholders (interest representatives, associations, professional federations or experts)
- Coordinate cross-functional strategic files
- Discerning the issues
- Establish forecasts, evaluations, recommendations, perspectives
- Establish a strategic diagnosis
- Identify the circuits and key players in a decision-making process
- Interpret analysis results for strategic decisions
- Write decision support reports, taking into account the entire ecosystem, all stakeholders and their interests
- Ensure interministerial coordination
- Be a source of proposals for the integration and support of innovative methods and techniques
- Prepare technical files for meetings
- Anticipate blocking points and possible counter-arguments
- Filter and synthesize information produced by central administration

## Professional Approach

- Be open to changes
- Demonstrate leadership
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
