# General housekeeper / General housekeeper in hotels

## Scope

A strategic manager, the General Manager in the hotel industry contributes to developing the quality and brand image of the hotel. Supervises all room services and sometimes other related services (laundry, light maintenance, etc.). Manages the activity and budget of the hotel's maintenance and cleaning department Ensures the recruitment and training of floor staff teams Organizes and monitors the daily activities of the entire department Collaborates with hotel management to define and improve quality standards Participates in strategic decisions (renovations, equipment purchases, etc.) of the establishment
