# Baker

## Scope

The Baker, an essential craftsman in our daily lives, transforms and enhances raw materials to delight our taste buds. Prepares and produces bakery and pastries products according to food hygiene and safety rules Designs new bread and pastries recipes to diversify the offering Manages stocks of raw materials and orders the necessary supplies Ensures optimal cooking of each product to guarantee the best quality Can sell bakery and pastries products Can manage a food retail business (bakery, bakery-pastry shop, etc.)

## Related Job Titles

- Baker's helper
- Baker-pastry chef
- Baker-pastry chef-caterer
- Baker-caterer
- Baker-viennoisier / Boulangère-viennoisière
- Baker worker

## Activities and Skills

- Pass on a technique, a know-how
- Innovate in creating new recipes
- Rolling and rolling or shaping the dough pieces
- Making pastries
- Bake pieces of pastries
- Cook dough pieces
- Unpacking breads and pastries
- Master the different types of bread (traditional, sourdough, brioche, etc.) and their manufacturing methods
- Take, weigh and dose materials or products (ingredients, colorings, grains, etc.) on scales, weighing hoppers, etc.
- Knead dough manually or mechanically
- Adjust cooking machine settings
- Act quickly in the event of a breakdown to minimize interruptions
- Maintain equipment, a machine, an installation
- Maintain, clean a space, a place, a premises
- Define supply needs
- Receive products, raw materials, check the conformity of a delivery
- Check raw material stocks
- Welcome, inform and guide customers
- Take a customer order
- Meet a customer's expectations
- Collaborate with suppliers for the selection of raw materials
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop the image and reputation of the structure
- Organize, set up a sales space
- Present and promote a product or service
- Maintain a clean and organized work environment
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
