# Head baker

## Scope

The Head Baker, a key artisan of French gastronomy, guarantor of tradition and innovation in bread making. Directs and coordinates the activity of a bakery, supervising the production and quality of products Develops new recipes and techniques to improve the quality of bread and derived products Manages stocks of raw materials and ensures order management to optimize costs Supervises and trains teams of bakers, passing on their know-how and ensuring compliance with hygiene standards Can participate in professional competitions to promote their know-how and that of their bakery Develops customers and builds loyalty by offering quality products and regularly innovating
