# Sports activities development manager

## Scope

Develops, coordinates and supports development projects for the structure or network of sports structures to strengthen or diversify the existing offer, increase the number of licensees Builds and leads partnerships, seeks financing, ensures the promotion of the structure or sports activity Manages the administrative and financial aspect of the projects carried out and their evaluation May have a level of responsibility and autonomy of a manager level depending on the size of the company
