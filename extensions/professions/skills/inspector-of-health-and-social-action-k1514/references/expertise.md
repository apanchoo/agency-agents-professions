# Inspector of health and social action

## Scope

Guarantor of the quality and safety of health and social establishments, the health and social action inspector ensures compliance with standards and the protection of users. Evaluates the quality of care and services offered to users Controls compliance with hygiene and safety standards in establishments Inspects premises and equipment to verify their compliance Advises establishments on good practices and improvements to be made Participates in the development of public health and social policies

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Promote a respectful and safe work environment
- Advise establishments on good practices and improvements to be made
- Promote good practices in medical information management
- Promote public health programs
- Monitor the implementation of corrective actions
- Identify anomalies and provide corrective solutions
- Analyze professional practices to assess their compliance with regulations
- Monitor compliance with health and safety standards in establishments
- Evaluate the quality of care and services offered to users
- Identify non-conformities
- Inspect premises and equipment to verify their compliance
- Implement corrective actions in the event of non-compliance
- Write detailed reports on the checks carried out
- Write detailed reports on observations and non-conformities
- Investigate reports of dysfunction or abuse
- Ensuring transparency and fairness in inspection processes
- Adapt the recommendations according to the specificities of the establishments
- Ensure the application of user rights and their protection
- Participate in the development of public health and social policies
- Coordinate actions aimed at improving access to care and the health of populations
- Collaborate with other state services and local authorities
- Communicate critical information to relevant authorities
- Discuss with professionals to understand the difficulties encountered
- Promote good hygiene practices at work
- Raise awareness among different stakeholders about public health policy, prevention and health promotion
- Ensure regulatory and legislative monitoring
- Maintain regulatory monitoring in matters of public safety

## Professional Approach

- Have a sense of service
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Demonstrate a sense of responsibility

## Areas of expertise

- Institutional systems and actors in the health sector (ARS, associative actors, health professionals, etc.)

## Standards and processes

- Institutional, administrative and legal framework of health, social and penitentiary structures
- Rights of users of the health system
- Health data security standards

## Professional techniques

- Interdisciplinary health communication

## Working Conditions

- Healthcare professionals
- Business travel
- Public health
- Work at an irregular pace and peaks in activity
- Health authorities

## Sectors

- Public service, defense and security
