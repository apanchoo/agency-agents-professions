## Scope

The gastroenterologist is a doctor specializing in disorders of the digestive system with very in-depth knowledge of anatomy. Diagnoses and treats illnesses, such as ulcers, gastroesophageal reflux disease, and inflammatory bowel disease Performs endoscopies to examine the inside of the digestive tract and perform biopsies or remove polyps Interprets imaging studies and laboratory tests to make accurate diagnoses Prescribes drug treatments and advises on dietary modifications needed to improve gastrointestinal health Collaborates with other medical specialists to manage cases complex requiring a multidisciplinary approach Regularly monitors patients to evaluate the effectiveness of treatments and adjust care plans according to the evolution of symptoms
