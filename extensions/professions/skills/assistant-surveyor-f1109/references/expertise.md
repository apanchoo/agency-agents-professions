# Assistant surveyor

## Scope

The assistant surveyor assists the surveyor or the expert surveyor in the missions of measurement, survey and processing of topographical data, participates in field operations and contributes to the production of plans and technical documents. Prepares and uses measuring instruments (theodolite, GPS, total station, etc.) Performs data readings in the field (demarcation, leveling, implementation) Enters and processes this data and participates in the writing of reports and plans

## Related Job Titles

- Land surveyor-topographer assistant
- Surveyor
- Operator / Surveyor operator
- Surveyor-topographer operator
- Picketer
- Restoration technician / Photogrammetry restoration technician

## Activities and Skills

- Inventory construction constraints (regulatory, technical, environmental)
- Map roads, residential areas, rivers, bodies of water
- Understand, interpret data and technical documents
- Create a technical drawing, plan, or map
- Update various infrastructure and network plans
- Carry out the digital mapping of a cadastral plan and a Local Urban Planning Plan (PLU)
- Draw topographic and hydrographic maps
- Inventory the easements relating to land
- Materialize the identification by guaranteeing the required precision, stipulated in the specifications
- Prepare materials for work
- Carry out topographical land surveys
- Carry out land studies or documents and establish architectural plans (plot division, land development, etc.)
- Carry out an interior and facade survey of an existing structure and determine the perimeter and surface area
- Use and adjust geology and topography machines or equipment
- Carry out surveys of the implementation of existing distribution networks (electrical, telecommunications, etc.)
- Check and analyze the anomaly noted
- Check the conformity of the implementation of an achievement
- Check the conformity of the product to the order
- Pilot a drone for data capture, topographical data, transport of loads, etc.
- Write a report, an activity report
- Estimate the value of a good or a product
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Communicate in writing appropriately
- Participate in carrying out a study
- Relay information
- Working in a group, in a network
- Digitizing terrain data for a geographic information system
