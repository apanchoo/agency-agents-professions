## Scope

The designer makes the garments made from different materials by hand or by machine, to measure or in small batches. Chooses appropriate fabrics, colors and patterns for designs Makes alterations and adjustments as necessary for a perfect fit Collaborates with designers and clients to develop new styles Manages inventory of materials and supplies needed for tailoring May supervise a team of tailors in an atelier or fashion business
