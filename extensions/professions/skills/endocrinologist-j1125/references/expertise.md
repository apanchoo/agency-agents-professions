# Endocrinologist

## Scope

The endocrinologist, specialist in hormones, diagnoses and treats pathologies of the endocrine glands. Identifies and treats diseases linked to hormonal imbalances Conducts hormonal assessments to assess endocrine functions Prescribes and adjusts hormonal treatments to balance deficiencies or excesses Follows patients over the long term to adjust treatments according to the evolution of symptoms Advises patients on hormonal disorders and the impacts on their general health Collaborates with other specialists for multidisciplinary management of complex pathologies
