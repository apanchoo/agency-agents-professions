# Manufacturing operator in the leather, skin and associated materials industry

## Scope

A Manufacturing Operator in the leathers, skins and materials industry transforms raw materials into finished products. Processes hides and skins into finished products according to technical specifications and quality standards Uses specialized machines to cut, assemble and finish leather products Controls the quality of raw materials and finished products to ensure conformity Makes adjustments to machines to optimize production and minimize waste Collaborates with other departments (design, quality) to improve manufacturing processes Maintains a clean and safe working environment in accordance with regulations
