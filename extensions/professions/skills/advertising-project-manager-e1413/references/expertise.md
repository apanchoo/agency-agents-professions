# Advertising Project Manager

## Scope

The Project Manager manages the design, monitoring and delivery of advertising projects or events, coordinating internal teams and service providers. Ensures the management of a campaign at each stage, by being the main contact for the client Pilots a project by developing a schedule and coordinating the various stakeholders Ensures the adequacy of the creative and technical production with the client brief, its graphic charter, its expectations Monitors the performance of a campaign and participates in the implementation of corrective measures
