# Driver of exceptional convoy protection vehicle

## Scope

The exceptional convoy protection vehicle driver accompanies an exceptional transport or convoy, sometimes with a guide vehicle driver. Reports the presence of one or more vehicles whose dimensions and/or mass exceed the regulatory limits defined by the Highway Code so that the convoy circulates in optimal safety conditions Drives a category B vehicle, released from the probationary period Ensures the convoy's guiding operations while ensuring the prevention of road risks in circulation and the stopping of people, property and the environment Apply and respect the regulations relating to exceptional transport Indicates to other users the specific traffic rules for crossing difficult points Prepares and follows the route Maintains regular communication with all those involved in the convoy Indicates to other users the specific traffic rules for crossing difficult points Prepares and follows the route Maintains regular communication with all those involved in the convoy
