# IoT Architect - Internet of Things

## Scope

The IoT - Internet of Things Architect plays a key role in the digital transformation of businesses. Designs and develops IoT solutions adapted to specific customer needs Ensures the integration of IoT systems with existing IT infrastructures Evaluates and implements security protocols for networks of connected objects Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams for the implementation of IoT projects Conducts technology monitoring to stay at the forefront of innovations in the Internet of Things Can train and advise customers on the optimal use of IoT technologies
