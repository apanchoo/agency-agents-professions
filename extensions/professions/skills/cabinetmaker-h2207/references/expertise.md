# Cabinetmaker

## Scope

The cabinetmaker manufactures and restores furniture with art and precision, thus enhancing the furniture heritage. Designs and produces custom or mass-produced furniture Restores old furniture while preserving its authenticity and value Selects wood and other materials suitable for creating or restoring furniture
