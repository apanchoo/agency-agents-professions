# General Practitioner

## Scope

The general practitioner provides personalized medical monitoring for each patient. Diagnoses and treats common illnesses and various pathologies Ensures medical monitoring of patients of all ages, from birth to old age Refers patients to specialists when necessary Carries out preventive and screening actions to maintain public health Can provide specific care Manages medical emergencies and ensures continuity of care

## Activities and Skills

- Teaching, developing skills
- Analyze medical test results
- Advise the patient on preventive health practices
- Determine therapeutic needs and provide medical care
- Managing medical emergencies
- Organize post-treatment follow-up
- Identify symptoms, dysfunctions, identify the patient's living environment and carry out the clinical examination
- Carry out the medical prescription, explain the treatment methods to the patient and advise them on lifestyle
- Carry out a medical diagnosis
- Represent your structure or service during an event or to decision-making bodies
- Conduct study and research work
- Create procedures for collecting medical information
- Update the patient's medical record
- Check data compliance
- Provide information and update technical documentation
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Ensure the administrative and financial management of an activity
- Manage the relationship with suppliers, subcontractors, service providers
- Manage medical databases
- Carry out accounting operations
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals

## Professional Approach

- Listen, show empathy
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
