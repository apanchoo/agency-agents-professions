# Receptionist

## Scope

The Receptionist ensures the first contact with customers within the establishment and provides a warm and professional welcome. Greets and registers guests upon arrival Handles reservations and cancellations Informs guests about property services and local attractions Handles specific guest requests and complaints Coordinates with other hotel departments to optimize the guest experience Maintains a clean and organized work environment
