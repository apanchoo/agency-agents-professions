# Financial Markets Operations Manager

## Scope

The Financial Market Operations Manager organizes the monitoring and recording of operations carried out on the financial markets Ensures the accounting treatment of operations by reconciling the results with management data Validates transactions, contracts with the legal department Can define procedures for processing market operations Processes complaints, payment or receipt incidents Manages recovery and litigation procedures
