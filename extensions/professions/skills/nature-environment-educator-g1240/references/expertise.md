# Nature environment educator

## Scope

The nature environment facilitator raises public awareness of the knowledge and protection of nature and biodiversity. Designs and leads educational activities in a natural environment Organizes discovery and observation outings of fauna and flora Develops environmental education programs for different audiences Collaborates with local organizations to promote environmental conservation Produces communication and information materials on nature and the environment

## Related Job Titles

- Biodiversity facilitator
- Ecomuseum facilitator
- Nature initiation facilitator
- Environmental facilitator
- Nature environment facilitator
- Eco interpreter
