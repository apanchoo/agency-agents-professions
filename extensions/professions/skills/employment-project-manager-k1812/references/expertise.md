# Employment project manager

## Scope

The Employment Project Manager is a dynamic player involved in professional integration and the labor market. Identifies the employment and skills needs of local companies Implements professional integration and training projects aimed at facilitating access to employment Ensures liaison between companies and job seekers by targeting job offers adapted to each profile Organizes networking events and skills development workshops expected by employers Monitors the evolution of employment projects by collaborating with partners and measures their impact on the territory Develops actions and projects linked to public employment and social policies training

- Professional integration project manager
- Manager / Corporate Relations Manager
- Employment Training Coordinator
