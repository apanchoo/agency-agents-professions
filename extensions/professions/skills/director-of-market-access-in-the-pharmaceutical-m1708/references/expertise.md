# Director of Market Access in the Pharmaceutical Industry

## Scope

The Director of Market Access plays a crucial role in the economic and medico-economic strategy of the product portfolio in the market. Defines and implements market access strategy to maximize product penetration Negotiates with stakeholders to ensure optimal presence in target markets Analyzes market trends to adapt strategies accordingly Collaborates with marketing and promotion teams to align objectives and strategies Monitors product performance in the market and adjusts action plans Ensures compliance with local and international regulations in the go-to-market process
