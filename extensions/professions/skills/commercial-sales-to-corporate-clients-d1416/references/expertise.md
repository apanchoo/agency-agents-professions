# Commercial / Sales to corporate clients

## Scope

A business salesperson (often called a B2B salesperson) is someone who sells products or services to businesses rather than individuals. Develops and maintains business relationships with existing and potential customers Identifies customer needs and proposes appropriate solutions Negotiates contracts and terms of sale to maximize profits Follows up with customers to ensure satisfaction and resolve potential issues Participates in trade shows and network meetings to promote the company's products and services

## Related Job Titles

- Sales associate / Sales associate for corporate clients
- Sales representative / Sales representative providing services to businesses
- Sales / Sales in services to companies
- Sales advisor / Sales advisor to corporate clients
- Sales advisor / Sales advisor in business services

## Activities and Skills

- Train new employees in sales techniques
- Implement communication actions
- Organize networking events to meet potential clients
- Organize and participate in various entertainment events (breakfasts, trade fairs, conferences, etc.) and various commercial meetings
- Update customer information in the computer system
- Monitor sales figures and carry out associated reporting
- Establish a customer visit and follow-up report
- Manage a schedule
- Write a report, an activity report
- Ensure compliance of business practices
- Manage complaints and ensure quality follow-up
- Draft commercial contracts in compliance with legal standards
- Analyze customer needs to offer appropriate services
- Ensure customer satisfaction
- Conduct commercial negotiations
- Conduct commercial negotiations: prices, deadlines, promotions and conclude the sale (purchase orders, sales contracts, etc.)
- Develop and maintain customer relationships
- Develop a commercial strategy
- Develop and adapt a commercial proposal
- Establish a prospecting tour plan (targeting, contacts, preparation of technical files)
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and collect feedback
- Prospect new customers, new markets
- Analyze sales data to identify growth opportunities
- Carry out regular monitoring of commercial activity: number of customers met, commercial proposals written, presented, sold, follow-ups carried out, etc.
- Convince by arguing with one or more interlocutors
- Use digital tools
- Develop interpersonal communication skills
