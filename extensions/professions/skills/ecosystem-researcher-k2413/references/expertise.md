# Ecosystem researcher

## Scope

The ecosystem researcher studies the complex interactions between living organisms and their environment to understand and preserve biodiversity. Collection and analysis of ecological and environmental data Studies the impacts of climate change on ecosystems Proposes strategies for conservation and management of ecosystems

## Related Job Titles

- Ecotoxicologist
- Scientific research naturalist
- Scientific Research Photochemist
- Scientific Research Radiochemist
- Scientific Research Toxicologist

## Activities and Skills

- Advising researchers, institutions, companies on scientific questions
- Acting on unexpected results in research
- Collect samples for analysis
- Design and coordinate a program, a research project
- Design and formalize research protocols and scientific studies
- Coordinate a research team
- Direct research work (thesis, study dissertation, etc.)
- Develop innovative research methods
- Develop ecosystem resilience
- Sampling organisms for taxonomic and genetic studies
- Conduct in-depth academic research
- Develop biodiversity conservation strategies
- Formulate hypotheses and test their reliability
- Manage research projects while respecting deadlines and budgets
- Conduct a complete exploration: learn about research already carried out on the subject and capitalize on existing knowledge
- Seek solutions for the conservation and restoration of ecosystems
- Manage a digital database
- Use modeling and simulation software
- Analyze the ecosystem to identify endangered species
- Advocate for environmental measures
- Manage relationships with partners, investors, public bodies and regulatory authorities
- Interpret analysis results for strategic decisions
- Search for financing, investors
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams for complex studies
- Present research results to diverse audiences
- Write scientific publications (posters, abstracts, articles, protocols, analysis plans, final reports) and format them
- Regularly update your knowledge

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate rigor and precision
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
