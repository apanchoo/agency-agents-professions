# Host / Hostess of welcome and cruise entertainment

## Scope

The Cruise Welcome and Entertainment Host or Hostess creates a welcoming and entertaining atmosphere for passengers. Welcomes and orients passengers on board the ship Conducts entertainment activities and events on board Liaises between passengers and crew to respond to specific requests Ensures compliance with passenger safety and comfort standards May participate in the management of reservations and boardings Contributes to the promotion of services and excursions offered during the cruise

## Related Job Titles

- River cruise host/hostess host
- Host host / hostess hostess for maritime cruises

## Activities and Skills

- Lead fun and informative activities on board
- Facilitate communication between passengers and crew
- Organize a reception area
- Ensuring the safety of passengers during the cruise
- Control access on board and check tickets
- Accompany passengers and respond to their requests
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Regularly assess passengers’ needs and expectations
- Provide tourist and local information to customers
- Inform the client about the tourist and cultural environment
- Optimize the customer experience through personalized service
- Promote the services and products available on the boat
- Collect a customer's opinion and comments
- Liaise with port authorities when necessary
- Write one or more foreign languages
- Speak one or more foreign languages
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Comply with maritime regulations

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Listen, show empathy
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Products, tools and materials

- Knowledge of leisure facilities on board

## Areas of expertise

- Eco-friendly practices on a cruise
- Tourist activities

## Standards and processes

- Passenger boarding and disembarkation procedures
- Maritime safety rules

## Professional techniques

- Rescue techniques and first aid
- Reception and customer service techniques
- Group facilitation techniques
- Management of emergency situations at sea
- Management of logistics of on-board activities
