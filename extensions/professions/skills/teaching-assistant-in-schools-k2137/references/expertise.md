# Teaching assistant in schools

## Scope

The mission of the educational assistant in schools is to support teachers and students in order to promote the conditions for learning and academic success, in a daily support and mediation role. Monitors and supervises students during class hours and between classes Assists teachers in the preparation and implementation of educational activities Contributes to the organization of educational activities and school outings Participates in the application of rules of conduct and the prevention of conflicts between students Provides light administrative support for teaching staff Can intervene in homework help and academic support

## Related Job Titles

- Teaching assistant in charge of education

## Activities and Skills

- Ensuring the safety of students during interclasses
- Supervise students during school outings
- Promote the social integration of young people
- Manage classroom behaviors to maintain an environment conducive to learning
- Participate in extracurricular activities (outings, workshops, sports activities, etc.)
- Participate in projects and educational, cultural and sporting outings organized by the school
- Participate in the implementation of educational projects
- Monitor students
- Monitor students in common areas, constantly, during exams and outings
- Support students in learning working methods
- Adapt learning according to educational progress and difficulties encountered
- Provide individualized help or in small groups to facilitate understanding of lessons and completion of homework
- Encourage and motivate students to achieve their academic goals
- Demonstrate pedagogy to facilitate understanding
- Promote student autonomy in learning
- Respect and participate in the implementation of the educational project of the establishment
- Provide academic support (completion/correction of exercises, homework, etc.)
- Support students in solving personal problems
- Work in close collaboration with teachers, teaching teams, school psychologists and families to ensure personalized monitoring of students
- Use digital educational tools
- Support the child in learning the gestures of daily life and the rules of community life
- Support students in organizing their personal work
- Evaluate the child's autonomy and physical, cognitive and relational abilities in their daily life
- Ensure the maintenance of educational and administrative materials
- Contribute to the organization of school events
- Supervise the administrative monitoring of students
- Facilitate the integration of new students
- Provide moral and psychological support to students in difficulty, in cooperation with educational advisors
- Promote collaboration between students for group projects
- Participate in the preparation of educational materials and course materials
- Promote mutual respect between students
- Understand and respect the rules of collective life
- Managing individual and group behavior in class
- Managing conflicts between students
- Manage incidents and apply internal regulations
- Respect and enforce the rules of conduct in class
- Respect the confidentiality of information
- Monitor punctuality, student attendance at classes, check supporting documents
