# Methanization operations technician

## Scope

The Methanization Operations Technician combines technical skills and commitment to renewable and local energy, serving the energy transition and the circular economy. Supervises and controls the operation of biogas production facilities from organic materials Manages incoming and outgoing materials Ensures preventive and corrective maintenance of equipment Performs regular analyzes to guarantee the quality of the biogas produced Complies with operating and safety instructions linked to the activity

## Related Job Titles



## Activities and Skills

- Collect, test, analyze samples, materials
- Take biomass or digestate samples and carry out the first analyzes
- Analyze data to make strategic decisions
- Develop processes and technical operating methods
- Supply a machine with raw material or product
- Monitor inputs (organic waste) and outputs (biogas, digestates)
- Ensure the cleanliness of the premises
- Monitoring the safety of methanization installations
- Carry out or supervise preventive and corrective maintenance interventions
- Repair and replace defective parts
- Define and implement security protocols
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Plan maintenance interventions
- Plan the shutdown of equipment
- Monitor equipment performance indicators
- Check the operation of equipment and installations, identify anomalies and preventive or corrective actions
- Analyze the quality of a product
- Control the quality of inputs (urban or agricultural bio-waste)
- Control the quality and compliance of processes
- Manage a quality approach, a continuous improvement process
- Handle handling equipment safely
- Organize the storage and evacuation of digestate
- Supervise the loading of a truck
- Maintain a record of operations carried out
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage incidents and inform hierarchy
- Communicate effectively with customers and suppliers
- Manage production and operations management
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams
- Raise public awareness of the benefits of renewable energies
- Manage stress and make quick decisions in emergency situations
- Respect safety standards
- Respect the rules of Quality, Hygiene, Safety, Health and Environment (QHSSE)
- React to the unexpected and be proactive in the event of a malfunction
- Use digital tools
