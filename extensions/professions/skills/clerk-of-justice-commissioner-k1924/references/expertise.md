# Clerk of Justice Commissioner

## Scope

The clerk of the justice commissioner is the right arm of the justice commissioner, formerly called a bailiff; it plays an essential role in the legal field, ensuring rigorous and methodical management of files. Ensures the drafting and service of judicial and extrajudicial documents Manages the amicable or judicial recovery of debts Organizes and monitors execution procedures Advises and assists clients throughout the procedures Carries out findings and inventories under judicial mandate Can coordinate the activity of a team of clerks within the study

## Related Job Titles

- Bailiff's Clerk
- Clerk sworn bailiff
- Bailiff's associate in charge / Bailiff's associate in charge of recovery
- Principal Bailiff Clerk

- Provide administrative support during auctions

- Receive and record a deposit of gaming regulations (sports lotto, lottery, etc.)
- Negotiate payment arrangements with debtors
- Carry out observations on the ground in the presence of the bailiff
- Apply a legal or regulatory framework
- Conduct legal research to support cases handled
- Monitor customer files and current procedures
- Prepare a report

- Collaborate with other clerks and staff
- Ensuring data confidentiality
