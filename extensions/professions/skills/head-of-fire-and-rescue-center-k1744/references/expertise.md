# Head of fire and rescue center

## Scope

The Fire and Rescue Center Manager has a demanding job where leadership, responsiveness and a sense of public service save lives on a daily basis. Supervises firefighters and coordinates human and material resources Develops emergency plans Participates in safety exercises

## Related Job Titles

- Head of emergency center
- Head of relief column
- Head of rescue group
- Fire and rescue site manager

## Activities and Skills

- Coordinate human and material resources
- Coordinate administrative tasks
- Coordinate emergency response teams
- Leading a team in a crisis situation
- Optimize resource management
- Organize interventions on fires, accidents, rescue of victims
- Supervise fire brigade teams
- Evaluate staff training needs
- Develop or apply a safety organization plan in the event of a fire or crisis situation
- Plan the management of premises and equipment
- Analyze crisis situations and make rapid decisions
- Communicate safety procedures effectively
- Check the validity of the verification and calibration certificates of the equipment used
- Plan equipment maintenance operations
- Define schedules and holidays for your teams
- Readjust the schedule according to contingencies and priorities
- Manage budgets and control expenses
- Ensure compliance of practices with current regulations
- Coordinate an assistance and rescue operation for people
- Develop contingency plans
- Organize a relief logistics operation
- Participate in safety exercises
- Participate in and secure official and protocol visits
- Liaise with local authorities
- Communicate with health authorities
- Clearly communicate technical procedures to teams
- Working in collaboration with the Prefectures
- Work in collaboration with town halls
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Ensure compliance with procedures by all employees

## Professional Approach

- Have team spirit
- Demonstrate leadership
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives

## Areas of expertise

- Design of security protocols
- Ability to track project budgets
- Coordination with other healthcare professionals
- Development of security policies
- Team coordination

## Standards and processes

- Regulations on property and casualty insurance (Fire, Accidents, Miscellaneous Risks)
- Fire safety regulations
- Risks linked to road traffic, fires and weather conditions
- Forest fire prevention
- Safety standards for the maintenance of natural spaces

## Professional techniques

- Coordination with service personnel
- Firefighting techniques

## Working Conditions

- Departmental zone
- Wearing personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, shoes, helmet, hearing protection
- Working in hostile and dangerous environments
- Night work
- On-call work
- Work weekends and public holidays
- Territorial communities

## Sectors

- Public service, defense and security
