## Scope

The job of Inspector of Competition, Consumption and Fraud Repression (commonly known as DGCCRF - General Directorate of Competition, Consumer Affairs and Fraud Repression) is central to ensuring market regulation, consumer protection and compliance with commercial laws. Controls and monitors commercial practices to prevent fraud and infringements Conducts field investigations to collect evidence and verify the conformity of products and services Writes detailed reports following inspections and proposes corrective measures Participates in training and information activities for professionals and consumers Collaborates with other institutions and authorities to coordinate market regulation efforts May initiate legal proceedings in the event of serious and repeated breaches

Check that the request fits the required skills and expected outcome. Consult the [activities, knowledge and working contexts](references/expertise.md) as needed.
