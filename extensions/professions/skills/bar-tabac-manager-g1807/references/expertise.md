# Bar-tabac manager

## Scope

Passionate about customer service and with management expertise, the tobacco bar manager makes his establishment a friendly and warm place. Manages the daily activity of the tobacco bar, including stock and order management Ensures a warm welcome and quality service to customers Supervises and trains staff to guarantee efficient and courteous service Maintains the cleanliness and organization of the establishment to provide a pleasant environment Respects and enforces regulations relating to the sale of tobacco and alcohol
