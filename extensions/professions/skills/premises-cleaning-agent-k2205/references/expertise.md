# Premises cleaning agent

## Scope

A cleaning professional, the agent ensures every day to provide impeccable and pleasant spaces, thus contributing to a healthy and welcoming environment. Ensures the cleaning and disinfection of surfaces and premises according to current protocols Uses specific cleaning products for different types of surfaces Ensures the proper functioning and maintenance of cleaning equipment Respects strict safety and hygiene standards when carrying out their tasks Can intervene for special cleaning in the event of incidents Manages the supply and storage of cleaning products
