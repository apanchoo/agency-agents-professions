# General food store manager

## Scope

The general food store manager orchestrates the daily management and overall performance of the store Supervises the organization of the store and the layout of products Ensures compliance with hygiene and food safety standards Recruits and supervises a sales team by planning daily activities Develops sales strategies to achieve commercial objectives and satisfy customers Manages inventory and supplies to guarantee product availability Maintains a quality relationship with suppliers and negotiates purchases
