# Human Resources Assistant (HR)

## Scope

The Human Resources (HR) Assistant assists managers and optimizes the administrative and operational management processes of human resources within a company or organization. Carries out the administrative monitoring of personnel management (contracts, absences, medical examinations, declarations to social organizations, etc.) Participates in the recruitment process, from the publication of job offers to the integration of new employees Implements the continuing professional training plan and ensures its monitoring Ensures the coordination and administrative support of relations between the employer and the staff representative bodies Answers questions from employees concerning the various aspects linked to human resources Can collect and verify the information necessary for the preparation of pay slips and carry out monitoring payroll operations in conjunction with the accounting department
