# Director of green spaces and biodiversity

## Scope

The director of green spaces and biodiversity orchestrates the management and development of green spaces to promote urban biodiversity. Supervises the design, development and maintenance of parks, gardens and green spaces Develops projects aimed at improving the biodiversity and ecological appeal of urban areas Collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to integrate sustainable practices in the management of green spaces Ensures compliance with environmental regulations and sustainable development goals Manages the budgets, human resources and equipment necessary for the maintenance of green spaces
