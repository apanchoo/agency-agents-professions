# Dietitian

## Scope

The dietician is a health professional specializing in nutrition who advises and supports individuals to balance their eating habits and improve their health. Develops personalized nutritional plans to meet specific needs Advises clients on healthy food choices by integrating the psychological, socio-economic dimension Supervises and adjusts dietary plans according to changing needs Participates in nutrition awareness and education campaigns and collaborates with other professionals

## Related Job Titles

- Dietitian / Consulting dietician
- Dietitian / Dietitian nutritionist

## Activities and Skills

- Support people in implementing the nutritional program
- Help prepare balanced meals
- Identify people’s eating habits, medical history, treatments and determine their nutritional needs
- Conduct a health education and prevention approach
- Assess individual nutritional needs
- Implement a care process
- Offer personalized advice on nutrition, physical exercise, stress management
- Schedule health checkups
- Practice dietary monitoring
- Carry out dietary care
- Carry out a diagnosis or paramedical assessment
- Raise awareness of mental and physical well-being
- Raise awareness of good hygiene and health practices
- Monitor the evolution of patients' health
- Follow a suitable nutrition program
- Adapt menus according to the needs of a structure, budgetary requirements and the specificities of the public
- Adapt recipes for specific needs (allergies, diets, preferences)
- Design a menu according to a medical prescription
- Offer personalized meal plans
- Prepare balanced meals while respecting specific diets
- Detect risky situations (eating behavior disorders, malnutrition, etc.) and refer them to the competent people
- Organize a food circuit according to food hygiene regulations
- Produce detailed reports on client progress
- Monitor regulatory developments in food safety
- Greet customers and identify their specific needs
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Design and implement nutritional education actions (taste initiation workshops, theme week)
- Create a relationship of trust
- Develop and manage interpersonal relationships
- Establish a relationship of trust and facilitate communication with the person being supported
- Participate in public health programs
- Promote healthy eating practices
- Respect hygiene and food safety rules
