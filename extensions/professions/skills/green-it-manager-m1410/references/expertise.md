# Green IT Manager

## Scope

The Green IT Manager, a key player for sustainable and responsible IT. Optimizes IT resources to reduce the ecological footprint Advises on best environmental practices in IT projects Implements eco-responsible solutions in information systems Raises awareness and trains teams on Green IT issues Supervises compliance of IT projects with environmental standards Evaluates and continually improves the environmental impacts of the technologies used

## Related Job Titles

- Responsible digital officer
- Green IT Project Manager
- Green IT consultant
- Director of responsible digital uses
- Responsible digital manager

## Activities and Skills

- Supporting change among employees
- Implement the transformation actions necessary for changes
- Analyze green technology needs
- Carry out studies on the organization and evolution of information systems
- Write a report, an activity report
- Define and deploy a sustainable development policy
- Develop solutions to reduce the energy consumption of buildings
- Incorporate sustainability criteria into technological choices
- Optimize IT processes to reduce the ecological footprint
- Design and manage a project
- Lead and manage a whole, a structure, an organization
- Evaluate the effectiveness of the Green IT solutions implemented
- Train teams in the responsible use of technologies
- Manage transition projects towards greener solutions
- Implement technical performance indicators
- Raise stakeholders’ awareness of the importance of Green IT
- Ensure compliance with environmental regulations
- Maintain continuous information monitoring
- Use digital tools

## Professional Approach

- Be proactive
- Show creativity, inventiveness
- Demonstrate leadership
- Organize your work according to priorities and objectives
