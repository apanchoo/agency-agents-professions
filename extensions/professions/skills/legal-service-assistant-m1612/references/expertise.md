# Legal service assistant

## Scope

The Legal Department Assistant is responsible for supporting attorneys, counsel, or other members of a legal department, by performing administrative tasks and legal document preparation work. Conducts research in legal databases Writes, prepares documents and updates legal files Provides administrative support for his or her managers Manages communication between the different stakeholders (clients, lawyers, internal company services, courts)

- Legal Secretary
