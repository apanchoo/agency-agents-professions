# Yacht captain

## Scope

The yacht captain is responsible for the maritime expedition; he orchestrates navigation and ensures the safety of his crew and passengers on board. Navigates and maneuvers the yacht according to weather conditions, maritime safety regulations Ensures compliance with regulations relating to the protection of the marine environment Supervises the upkeep and maintenance of the yacht to ensure its proper functioning Manages the crew, distributes tasks and ensures compliance with procedures Ensures communication with ports, maritime authorities and other vessels Plans itineraries taking into account the wishes of passengers and sailing conditions May participate in yachting competitions and represent the yacht at nautical events

## Related Job Titles

- Motor Pleasure Patron (PPM)
- Sailing Pleasure Patron (PPV)
