# Municipal policeman / Municipal policewoman

## Scope

The municipal police officer is a local agent responsible for ensuring respect for security, tranquility and the rules of daily life in his municipality; it intervenes 24/7 close to residents by patrolling by car, on foot or by bike, to ensure security, calm neighborhoods and ensure respect for the sharing of public space. Carries out the execution of the mayor's police orders in matters of prevention and surveillance, security and public health (traffic, town planning, neighborhoods, etc.) Verifies compliance with the application of legislation (municipal orders and regulations) on the territory of the municipality Can coordinate a team

## Activities and Skills

- Lead, coordinate a team
- Provide mediation
- Raise awareness of the causes and consequences of road accidents to avoid repeat offenses
- Administer a survey, a poll
- Carry out remote monitoring
- Monitor municipal property (buildings, schools, public transport, parks, cemeteries, etc.), events and public places
- Ensuring the safety of property and people
- Design and implement actions to prevent incivility, violence and degradation among students
- Manage capture and impoundment operations for stray or dangerous animals
- Identify and report anomalies concerning roads, public lighting, faulty signs
- Supervise a vehicle removal operation
- Securing people, places and property with a video protection system
- Secure pedestrian crossings near schools
- Prepare periodic safety reports
- Write inspection reports and minutes in the event of an infraction
- Write detailed intervention reports
- Apply administrative police measures under the authority of the mayor
- Liaise with police and justice services
- Record violations of orders concerning the circulation, stopping and parking of vehicles in station yards
- Monitor and enforce compliance with legal and regulatory provisions
- Monitor compliance with municipal bylaws and the highway code
- Prepare a report
- Carry out all observations on the advertising policy, signs and pre-signs
- Search for and note violations of the rules relating to the fight against neighborhood noise
- Record in writing violations of health regulations relating to roads and the cleanliness of public spaces
- Report an infraction
- Respect the rules of ethics and professional conduct
- Draft legal documents and procedures
- Sanction infractions and assign appropriate penalties
- Participate in road safety and school safety missions
- Preventing public order disturbances
- Prevent disturbances to public peace by islanding, patrolling, and resolving conflicts
- Carry out interventions to combat disturbances to peace and public order (maintenance of order, fight against delinquency, crime, etc.)
- Carry out a public order mission
- Monitor public spaces and establishments open to the public (ERP)
- Welcome, guide, inform an audience
- Adapt your intervention according to the audience (young people, traders, festival-goers, etc.)
- Assist municipal services during public events
- Collaborate with other police services
- Collaborate with national law enforcement (police, gendarmerie) if necessary
- Create a relationship of trust
- Embody the values of the State and Public Service
- Maintain a relationship of trust with residents and elected officials
- Raise citizens' awareness of safety and civic rules
- Act quickly in an emergency situation
- Manage emergency situations and apply appropriate procedures
- Handle emergency or unforeseen situations calmly and efficiently
- Negotiate in the event of conflict to defuse tensions
- Comply with local regulations
- Use digital tools
