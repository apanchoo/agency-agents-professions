# Occupational and prevention doctor

## Scope

The occupational physician plays an important role in promoting health at work and preventing occupational risks. Monitors the health of employees based on the risks linked to their activity and their working environment Advises the company on the organization of work and working conditions Conducts regular medical examinations to prevent occupational illnesses Participates in prevention and training actions on safety at work Collaborates with human resources services to adapt positions, techniques and working rhythms Intervenes in the event of a workplace accident to assess disabilities and recommend adjustments

## Related Job Titles

- Prevention doctor
- Preventive health doctor
- Occupational doctor
- Regional Labor and Manpower Inspector Doctor -MIRTMO-

## Activities and Skills

- Identify the necessary adjustments to a workstation
- Advise on the adaptation of workstations for employees with disabilities or medical restrictions
- Teaching, developing skills
- Be the medical referent for occupational health issues within the company
- Analyze patients' health needs
- Provide informed medical opinions on requests for professional reclassification or incapacity
- Identify the person's environment, carry out a health examination (visual, hearing test) and establish a fitness assessment
- Conduct follow-up examinations after an illness or work accident
- Determine the need for additional medical examination and refer the person to a treating physician or specialist
- Carry out specific health checks based on company-specific risks (noise, toxic substances, etc.)
- Carry out and participate in vaccination campaigns
- Implement medical monitoring protocols
- Organize vaccination campaigns
- Schedule health checkups
- Carry out periodic medical examinations to assess fitness for the job
- Carry out emergency care or organize arrangements for hospitalization, decontamination, etc. during accident situations, intoxication, etc.
- Monitor the evolution of patients' health
- Monitor a person's health
- Monitor the evolution of patients' health status
- Interact with external bodies such as the Labor Inspectorate, Social Security and public health services
- Work in collaboration with staff representatives, CHSCT (Hygiene, Safety and Working Conditions Committees), and other bodies
- Develop prevention actions or rules
- Identify and assess professional risks in the company
- Implement health prevention programs
- Implement and coordinate emergency procedures in the event of a workplace accident
- Carry out regular visits to premises and workshops to identify potential risks and assess working conditions
- Study working conditions to propose ergonomic improvements
- Write a report, an activity report
- Manage medical records and ensure their confidentiality
- Manage medical databases
- Evaluate the nuisance factors in the school or professional environment and propose improvements
- Identify and identify health and environmental risks
- Collaborate with other healthcare professionals
- Participate in a multidisciplinary project
- Promote good hygiene practices at work
- Follow cleaning and disinfection protocols
- Participate in continuing education sessions and professional conferences
