# Commercial advisor / Sales advisor to individuals

## Scope

The commercial advisor to individuals sells products and services and adapts his offers to best meet the needs of his customers. Identifies customer needs to offer appropriate solutions Develops a customer portfolio and builds customer loyalty Negotiates commercial conditions while respecting sales objectives Ensures follow-up of customer files and resolves any problems Keeps informed of market developments to adjust its commercial strategy Participates in events and trade shows to represent your company
