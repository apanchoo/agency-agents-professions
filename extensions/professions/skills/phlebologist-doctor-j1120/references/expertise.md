# Phlebologist doctor

## Scope

The phlebologist is a specialist in venous health who provides the diagnosis and treatment of venous diseases. Diagnoses and treats venous pathologies such as varicose veins and phlebitis Performs ultrasound examinations to assess the condition of veins and arteries Prescribes drug treatments or surgical interventions if necessary Advises patients on the prevention of venous diseases Monitors the progress of treatments and adjusts prescriptions as necessary Collaborates with other specialists to provide comprehensive care
