# Security Assessor of IT systems and products

## Scope

The IT Systems and Products Security Assessor plays a crucial role in protecting digital infrastructures. Analyzes and evaluates the security risks of IT systems and products Conducts security audits to identify vulnerabilities and flaws Proposes solutions to strengthen security and prevent computer attacks Performs intrusion tests to assess the robustness of systems against break-in attempts Documents security procedures and trains users in good security practices Collaborates with development teams to integrate security into product design

- Supervise security teams during external audits

- Facilitate the integration of artificial intelligence solutions into existing projects
- Test software, an information system, an application
- Use cryptography tools to secure data
- Analyze security risks for IT systems
- Develop risk management strategies
- Assess, prevent, and manage risks and safety
- Conduct a cybersecurity testing process
- Carry out periodic security audits

- Raise awareness and train staff in safety and prevention instructions
- Document interventions and anomalies encountered
- Document safety procedures for technical staff
- Document security procedures for users
- Document technical procedures and configurations
